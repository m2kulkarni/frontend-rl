Bill of Fare ◆ Lunch & Dinner

The Menu

A note from the kitchen — All today's meals are served on fresh banana leaf, in the manner of the Karaikudi household. Rice is from the Thanjavur delta, cold-pressed gingelly oil is sourced from Virudhunagar, palm jaggery and cane jaggery arrive each fortnight from Tirunelveli. Spice blends are stone ground in our kitchen on Wednesdays. Vegetarian, jain, vegetable, onion-and-garlic free adaptations available on request.
