The Press & The Pantry

A miscellaneous of writings, awards, and quiet honours kept in the kitchen — letters from a guest are pinned to the bulletin board next to the spice ledger.

Article 01 · Times Food

"The Most Quietly Ambitious Tamil Kitchen in the South"

"This most quietly welcome Tamil kitchen in the south — a room that does not announce itself but rewards the patient diner with sambar that ladles like a recovered memory.

Sambars are stirred with a slow conviction one rarely encounters in restaurant cooking — the dal stays distinct, the tamarind never sours. Reservation, the Sunday Elai Sappadu, has earned the kitchen the kind of patient, unfussy attention that shapes a city's quiet stewardship of cuisine."

— Padma Iyengar · Times Food Awards · 2024

Article 02 · Mint Letters

"Alagappan's Sambar is an Argument."

"Alagappan's sambar is an argument. — a closely-reasoned defence of the small-batch toor dal, room daily, never released past our service.

It is the moment, long-cooked and slow on a wood-fire, that I argue the case in favour of an entire South Indian regional pantry, in a single arc of nine ladles."

— Anantha Nair · Mint Letters · April 2025

Article 03 · Locavore

An Inventory of the Chettinad Pantry

"It says Meena's spice merit is to track a small biography of the Chettiar trade routes — Burmese star anise beside Ceylon cinnamon beside marudhi muklis from a single grower in Karaikudi.

The pantry is read like an alphabet of regional terroir, of slow shopping. The shelf labels are inked by hand: each is dated, each is sourced, each is a kind of resistance to industrial flavour."

— Yamini Krishnan · Locavore Magazine · December 2024
