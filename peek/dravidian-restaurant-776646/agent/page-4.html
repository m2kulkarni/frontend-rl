Arriving

Parking & Auto

Valet parking from ₹220 at the side gate on 12th Cross. Two cars per family, please. Auto-rickshaws may stop directly at the brass-listed door — the lane is wide enough. Nammma Metro: alight at Indiranagar (Purple Line); six minutes on foot via 12th Main Road.

A Villager's Room

Private Dining

The Mandapam Room seats twelve at a single rosewood table, lit by a brass-cradle lamp and ringed in gilded teak panels rescued from a Karaikudi mansion. Reserved for engagements, naming days, and quiet birthdays. Set menu from ₹3,400 per guest (vegetarian) or ₹5,200 (Chettinad mutton-and-rice). Two-week notice. 10% deposit.

From the Kitchen

Dietary Notes

The kitchen is pure vegetarian on Friday lunch in keeping with the household traditions; no meat, fish, or egg crosses the threshold between 11.30 and 15.00 that day. All sambars are toor-and-shallots; chutney via the Sunday list — no Banner lentil, sourced direct from a single farm in Mandya district. Jain, vegan, and gluten-free meals are honestly tracked. Notice required: tell us at the time of booking; the rasam stock will be set aside for you.
