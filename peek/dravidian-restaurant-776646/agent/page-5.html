The Calendar Marked ◆ Special Meals

Feast Days & Seasons

Four times a year the temple kitchen at Kalasha bends to the planets. Harvest sun, river flood, nine nights of the goddess, and the long lamps of Karthigai — each calls for a different rice, a different kuzhambu, a different way of laying the leaf. Bookings open six weeks in advance.

The Four Feast Days

The Harvest Lunch · Pongal

Pongal Thali ₹950

Saturday, 11 January & Wednesday, 15 January 2026 · noon to 15.00 and 19.30
  • Sakkarai pongal cooked at the table over a clay stove, jaggery from Erode
  • Ven pongal with whole black pepper, cashew, twelve curry leaves
  • Seven vegetable koota, raw turmeric pickle, gingelly oil
  • Sugarcane, banana, betel leaf — served on a fresh plantain leaf

Fixed thali at one price. Whole table reservations only — minimum two, maximum eight per leaf.

The Aadi Festival · Aadi 18

Aadi Perukku ₹1,250

Saturday, 1 August & Sunday, 2 August 2026 · evening only, 19.00 PM
  • The Aadi paruppu rice — coconut, sesame, tamarind, curd, cooked & presented in banana leaf
  • Five seasonal periyals (vazhakkai, kovakkai, kothavarangai)
  • Paruppu payasam with palm jaggery, served warm from copper
  • Tender mango pickle, three day aged, from the kitchen's clay jar

Carry-out parcels available for the river-bank ritual — order three days ahead. Dining-room visits a la carte.

Nine Nights · Sundal Counter

Navaratri Sundal ₹180 / cone

22 September – 1 October 2026 · 17.00 – 21.00 PM. Sundal counter only.
  • Nine days, nine legumes — chana, kabuli, peanut, green gram, mochai, karamani, thatta payaru, white peas, horse gram
  • Tempered with mustard, asafoetida, fresh coconut, raw mango
  • Served in palm-leaf cones with kosvaalli salt and small payasam glass

Open dropping in. Walk to the door – we will fill the cones. The kitchen sends a bowl of yellow rice with each cone, on the ninth night only.

The Festival of Lamps · Karthigai Deepam

Lamp Supper ₹1,650

Wednesday, 4 December 2026 · evening only · 19.30 onwards · single sitting, 36 covers
  • Eight hundred clay agarbati lamps lit, the entrance lit dimly on arrival
  • The dinner served on copper-and-clay plates from Athangudi
  • Pori urundai and pori appam at the close, after
  • Slow cooked country chicken kuzhambu with marudham spice, kal dosai

A Lamp Supper is a single seating, fixed forty-eight covers, served in a single sitting from 7.30 to 9.30 only.

A Slow Society · Monthly Sitting · Members Only

The Kuzhambu Club

One long table. Twelve cuves. Two old kuzhambus, every season.

When

Last Saturday of every month

Doors at 7.30 PM. The first course is served at 8.00 PM, sharp.

How it works

A single signed-it limited menu. Twelve cuves, twelve at a single bench — sit beside whoever the kitchen places you next to. The card is dealt at first ladle.
