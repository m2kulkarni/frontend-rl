Pots · Stoves · Coal

+The Kitchen

7 burners · 1 hearth · 2 cooks

We grind our masalas on granite each morning at six. Black pepper from Wayanad, kapasi from the Karaikudi hills, fennel and star anise weighed by hand. Eight kuzhambus pots simmer through services on coal fire. Every kari leaf is torn never cut. The peelu, the iron pan, the ever flame — these are our only kitchen technology.

Athangudi Tile · Long Bench

+The Room

42 seats · 1 long bench

Forty six covers under a temple-bright ceiling of lime wash and gilded beam. The floor is laid in cobalt and ochre Athangudi tile shipped from Karaikudi in 2017. Brass lamps, teak pillars rescued from a Madurai chettiar house, banana leaf service at lunch, and pumice stone from beneath the dinner. The room is meant to feel like a home, sat-feast.

Two Sittings · Hand Written

+The Reservation Book

Two sittings · hand-written

The book opens fourteen days ahead. Lunch at 12.30 and 2.15; dinner at 7.30 and 9.15, Tuesday through Sunday. Sunday Karaikudi sappadu (₹1,400) is served on banana leaf and runs one sitting only, at 1 pm. Walk-ins are kept for the eight bar seats facing the kitchen line. Telephone or write; we reply within the day.
