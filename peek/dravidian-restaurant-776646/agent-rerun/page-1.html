Pots, Stones & Coal

The Kitchen

— Where Chettinad Begins

We grind our masalas on granite each morning at six. Black pepper from Wayanad, kalpasi from the Kanji-apatty hills, fennel and star anise weighed by hand. Eight kuzhambus pots simmer through service on coal fire. Every kari kari is born, never cut. The pestle, the iron pan, the slow flame — these are our only kitchen technology.

Athangudi Tile · Long Bench

The Room

— Athangudi Tile, Long Bench

Forty-six covers under a temple-bright ceiling of lime wash and gilded beam. The floor is laid in cobalt and ochre Athangudi tile shipped from Karaikudi in 2017. Brass lamps, teak pillars rescued from a Madurai chettiar house, banana-leaf service at lunch, and peacock-blue linen at dinner. The room is meant to feel like a home, mid-feast.

The Reservation Book

— Two Sittings, Hand-Written

The book opens fourteen days ahead. Lunch at 12.30 and 2.15; dinner at 7.30 and 9.15, Tuesday through Sunday. Sunday Karaikudi sappadu (₹1,650) is served on banana leaf and runs one sitting only at 1 pm. Walk-ins are kept for the eight bar seats facing the kitchen line. Telephone or write; we reply within the day.
