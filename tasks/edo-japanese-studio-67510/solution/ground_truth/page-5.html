所 在 地 ・ A D D R E S S

Taishō-dōri 3-7-2, Taitō-ku

The bench occupies the ground floor of a two-storey wood-frame house three minutes east of the Sumida embankment. A blue noren marked with the paulownia kamon hangs at the entrance from morning until close. There is no signboard; the noren is the sign.

Visitors are received by appointment only, Tuesday through Saturday. We ask that letters of introduction precede a first visit by no fewer than seven days, so that work in progress may be set aside without strain on the joint.

The studio is closed during O-Bon (mid-August) and the New Year week (28 December — 4 January), and on the third Wednesday of each month for tool maintenance.

Annual Closures

O-Bon · New Year · Maintenance

Closed 13 — 17 August for O-Bon, and 28 December — 4 January for the New Year week. Letters received during these periods are answered upon return.

Second Saturday · 鉋 削 り の 日

Planing Day.

On the second Saturday of each month, between 13:00 and 16:00, the bench is opened to those who wish to observe the dressing of a board with a Japanese plane. No demonstration is staged for its own sake — work proceeds as it would on any other day, and visitors are asked to remain quiet, seated on the matted step, while the shaving runs.

Attendance is by prior letter only. We accept no more than four guests on a given day. Children above the age of ten are welcome if accompanied; younger children, regrettably, cannot be received, as the floor is hung with edged tools.

To request a place, please write at least three weeks in advance, naming the date you hope to attend and a brief reason for your interest. A reply will be sent by return post.

— Iwakura Tōru, joiner-in-charge

By foot from Kuramae station

The nearest station is Kuramae on the Toei Asakusa line — exit A2, then four minutes on foot, walking east toward the river. From the Ōedo line concourse, the same exit is reached by an underground passage of roughly ninety paces.

Visitors arriving by bicycle may leave their machines along the wall opposite the noren; please do not lean them against the lattice. There is no parking for motor vehicles.

The studio sits between a dry-goods seller and a small printing works. If you reach the rice-cracker shop on the corner, you have walked one house too far.
