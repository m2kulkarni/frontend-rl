Commissions.

A working register of recent works delivered from the bench at Taishō-dōri. Each piece is built to a written specification, signed inside the carcass in sumi, and accompanied by an inventory of joints employed. Wood species named below were milled within the Kantō region unless otherwise stated.

Reiwa 5 — 6 · Six pieces of record · Compiled by Kondō Hiroaki, joiner
壹 — Recent Works
壹 · No. 01

Tansu for the Yanaka Shōkōkai

Wood — Aizu paulownia (kiri), Bunsei-period stock

Year — Reiwa 5 (2023) · delivered 11.iii

Joinery — arigata-tsugi dovetails throughout the carcass; kanawa-tsugi at the plinth scarf; hand-cut shōji-mortice for drawer runners.

A four-tier merchant's chest commissioned by the Yanaka Shōkōkai as a reception piece for their Sakura-dōri offices. The kiri was reclaimed from a 1827 storehouse beam donated by the Itō family of Sendagi; the grain runs continuous across the three lower drawers. Hardware in hand-forged iron by Murata Kinzōku-ten, Asakusa.

Recorded value · ¥1,840,000
貳 · No. 02

Cha-dōgu-bako for Kondō-sensei

Wood — Quartersawn kiri (paulownia), unfinished

Year — Reiwa 5 (2023) · delivered 22.vii

Joinery — hako-zashi mitred frame, blind kakushi-arigata corners; lid registered with single 3.2 mm cherrywood pin.

A storage box for a set of four chasen and a single chashaku, made for Kondō Sōkō-sensei of the Urasenke Tōkyō branch. The interior is left raw to absorb humidity; the exterior given two passes of tonoko-polish only. The lid lifts with the breath of a closed shōji — slow, even, no pop. Signed beneath the floor in pale sumi.

Recorded value · ¥285,000
参 · No. 03

Pair of Shōji Frames, Asakusa Engei Hall

Wood — Kiso sugi (Japanese cedar), 60-yr stand

Year — Reiwa 4 (2022) · delivered 09.xi

Joinery — kumiko lattice in asa-no-ha pattern; through-tenons wedged with cherry; sashimono frame in cogged dadō-tsugi.

A matched pair fitted to the green-room of the Asakusa Engei Hall, where rakugo is performed nightly. The kumiko stand at 9 mm × 4 mm with no metal fastenings; the asanoha figure was selected by the proprietor Tachibana-san for its association with healthy growth in children. Paper hung by Sekiyama Hyōgu-ten across the river in Mukōjima.

Recorded value · ¥520,000 (the pair)
肆 · No. 04

Bun-tsukue (writing desk) in Keyaki Burl

Wood — Tama-mokume keyaki (zelkova burl), single board

Year — Reiwa 5 (2023) · delivered 04.ii

Joinery — shiho-zashi four-way frame; sliding kō-arigata drawer behind a hidden release; legs joined with hozo-sashi-komi and bamboo pin.

A low writing-desk for a private collector in Yushima, requesting a piece that would not require iron. The top is a single 920 × 460 board of figured keyaki sourced from a felled tree at Tōfuku-ji's Tōkyō branch (storm of Shōwa 34). The drawer pull is a finger-pocket only; the desk weighs 14.6 kg.

Recorded value · ¥1,420,000
伍 · No. 05

Sanbō Offering-Stand, Mishima Inari

Wood — Kiso hinoki (Japanese cypress), unwaxed

Year — Reiwa 4 (2022) · delivered 28.ix

Joinery — kumi-shiguchi corner; pierced trefoil (hōju) windows by chisel only; no glue, no nails — disassembles for purification.

An offering-stand for the autumn harvest rite at Mishima Inari Jinja, two streets east of the workshop. The piece is built per Engi-shiki convention: hinoki only, planed in a single direction, joined dry. It will be replaced after twenty years and the old wood ritually burnt. Built to a measured drawing held by the shrine since the rebuild of Shōwa 24.

Recorded · gratis (奉納 / hōnō)
陸 · No. 06

Restoration of an Edo Merchant's Chest

Wood — Original sugi carcass; replacement kiri drawer-fronts

Year — Reiwa 5 (2023) · returned 17.v

Joinery — reinforcement of failing arigata-tsugi with sashi-mochi splines; new kanawa scarf at the lower rail; iron-work re-tempered at Murata's forge.

A four-drawer chest dated to Bunsei 8 (1825), brought in by the Hayashi family of Negishi. The carcass was sound; three drawer-bottoms had cupped beyond use and the front-left dovetails were lost in a kitchen fire of 1947. The work followed the principle of fukugen — restoration to the maker's intention, not to mint condition. The original sumi inscription has been preserved, untouched, on the back panel.

Recorded value · ¥640,000 (labour)
餘白 — Ma

On the entry of new commissions

The bench accepts no more than nine substantial pieces a year. A first letter — by post or by hand at the workshop door — opens a conversation; a measured drawing follows after a visit. Wood is selected together; price is fixed only after the drawing is signed.

Estimates given here are recorded values at the date of delivery and are noted for the historical record only. Current commissions are quoted afresh.
