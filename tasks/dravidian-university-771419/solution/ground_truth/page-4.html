Pravartana · Projects in Progress

Three Current Initiatives

Field, archive, and laboratory projects supported by the Indian Council of Historical Research and the Tamil Nadu State Department of Archaeology.

Project No. 04/2024 · ICHR

Photogrammetric Survey
of Chola Bronzes

A high-resolution structured-light survey of forty-seven 11th-century Chola bronzes housed in the Tanjavur Art Gallery and the Sri Sailapati Devasthanam. Each Naṭarāja, Somāskanda, and Tripurāntaka image is recorded at 0.04 mm per voxel for iconometric study against the Mānasāra and Śilparatna canons.

Principal
Dr. K. Saraswathi Iyer
Period
2023 – 2026
Sites
Tanjavur · Darasuram
Grant
₹ 84,00,000
Project No. 11/2023 · CIIL Mysuru

Digital Concordance
of the Tirumurai

A searchable, lemmatised concordance of the twelve books of the Tirumurai, comprising 18,464 hymns of the Nāyaṉār canon. Built on Unicode-Tamil with parallel Roman IAST and a sandhi-aware token index, the corpus is being aligned to Umāpati Sivam's 14th-century commentary tradition.

Principal
Prof. M. Velliangiri
Period
2022 – 2025
Tokens
2.4 million
Grant
₹ 46,50,000
Project No. 18/2024 · ASI Hampi

Pillared Mandapas
at Hampi

A finite-element structural analysis of the kalyāṇa-maṇḍapas of the Vitthala and Achyutaraya temples, in collaboration with IIT Madras Civil Engineering. The study models the load behaviour of monolithic granite colonettes and saptasvara pillars under seismic input matched to the 1843 Bellary event.

Principal
Dr. P. Anantharaman
