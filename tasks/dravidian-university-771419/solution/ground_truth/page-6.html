How to Reach the Campus

The Tanjavur campus lies on the banks of the Vadavāru, an easy walk from the Bṛhadīśvara Temple complex.

By Rail

2.4 km · 7 min by auto

Tanjavur Junction (TPJ) is served by the Rockfort Express, Cholan Express, and direct services from Chennai Egmore, Madurai, and Tiruchirappalli. Pre-paid auto-rickshaws to the institute gate cost approximately ₹80. Cycle-rickshaws are available outside the eastern exit for ₹40.

By Air

58 km · 75 min by road

Tiruchirappalli International Airport (TRZ) receives daily services from Chennai, Bengaluru, Colombo, Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, and Sharjah. Pre-arranged institute cars meet visiting fellows by appointment (₹2,400 one-way). Government taxis charge a metered ₹1,800 to Tanjavur.

By Road

NH-67 · Tanjavur ring road

The campus gate opens onto Sivaganga Road, three minutes east of the Tanjavur–Pudukkottai junction. State Express buses from Chennai, Madurai, and Coimbatore arrive at the New Bus Stand (4 km). Visitor parking for thirty vehicles is provided beside the Pillared Hall guesthouse.

For International Scholars

The institute maintains a year-round guesthouse for visiting fellows, sabbatical researchers, and partner-university faculty.

॥ Pillared Hall ॥
Visiting-Fellow Guesthouse

The Pillared Hall

Set within the south-east quadrant of the campus, the Pillared Hall is a restored 1908 chettinad-style residence with twenty-eight teakwood-pillared rooms arranged around an inner courtyard. Each room offers a private veranda, study desk, and overhead fan; eight rooms include air-conditioning. Vegetarian meals are served thrice daily in the common dining hall, and a quiet reading-room with a small reference library remains open until 22:30.

The Pillared Hall is open to international fellows, sabbatical scholars, partner-university faculty, and invited speakers year-round, except during Pongal week (13–20 January), when the institute observes its full festival closure. Bookings must be confirmed in writing at least four weeks in advance through the Office of the Registrar.
