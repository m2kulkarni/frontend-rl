Sri Rajagopuram Institute of Temple Arts & Sciences

यतो धर्मस्ततो जयः

Where Stone Speaks, Knowledge Endures.

Founded in 1967 on the banks of the Kāveri at Tanjavur, the Institute upholds the unbroken transmission of sthapatya-veda, āgama, and Dravidian classical arts — taught alongside contemporary disciplines of conservation science, archaeometry, and digital humanities.

Welcome from the Vice-Chancellor

VICE-CHANCELLOR

"The temple is our laboratory, the chisel our pen."

Prof. Kalyanasundaram Iyer · 14th Vice-Chancellor

For fifty-seven years, this Institute has stood beside the Bṛhadīśvara as both pupil and custodian. We measure granite plinths and verse-feet of the Tirukkuṟaḷ with the same patience, believing that no civilisation survives without its grammar of stone, sound, and breath.

Our 2024 cohort welcomes 1,847 students across three campuses — Tanjavur, Madurai, and Srirangam — joining 312 sthapatis-in-training, 218 Carnatic vocalists, and a new wave of doctoral scholars in agamic computation and conservation chemistry. We invite you to walk our pillared courtyards and find a path that is both ancient and new.

— Kalyanasundaram
