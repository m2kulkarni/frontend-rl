Schools & Departments

Seven foundational schools where stone, sound, syllable, and equation are taught as a single inheritance — under the seal of the seventeen acharyas of the Cauvery delta.

The Seven Schools

Each school is governed by a senior acharya, awards degrees at three tiers — Bachelor (B.A.), Master (M.A.), and Doctorate (Ph.D.) — and maintains an annexed atelier or workshop on the Tanjavur campus.

School I · Sthapatya

Sthapatya-Veda & Temple Architecture

Department of the Builders
Acharya
Dr. Muthuswami Sthapati, B.Arch. (Anna), Ph.D. (BHU)
Faculty
34 professors · 9 visiting sthapatis
Degrees
B.A.M.A.Ph.D.

The original school, founded 1962 by the Mayamata circle. Students learn the six classical treatises — Manasara, Mayamata, Silpa Prakasa, Kasyapa Silpa, Vishvakarmiyam, and the Tantrasamuchaya — alongside on-site survey of the Brihadeeswarar and Airavatesvara temples. The fifth-year capstone is the construction of a working three-tier vimana in the campus atelier.

School II · Āgama

Agama & Sanskrit Studies

Department of Ritual Texts
Acharya
Prof. Venkateswara Sarma Dikshitar, M.A., Ph.D. (Madras)
Faculty
28 professors · 7 visiting acharyas
Degrees
B.A.M.A.Ph.D.

Custodian of the twenty-eight Saiva Agamas and the Pancharatra and Vaikhanasa traditions. Pāṇinian grammar is mandatory through the third year; advanced students translate unpublished palm-leaf manuscripts from the Sarasvati Mahal collection. The department's critical edition of the Suprabhedagama, in eight volumes, was completed in 2019.

School III · Tamiḻ

Tamil & Sangam Literature

Department of the Mother Tongue
Acharya
Dr. Selvi Ponnuthai Karunanidhi, M.A., Ph.D. (Madurai Kamaraj)
Faculty
31 professors · 6 visiting pulavars
Degrees
B.A.M.A.Ph.D.

Curriculum opens with the Tolkappiyam and proceeds through the eight Eṭṭuttokai anthologies, the ten Pattuppāṭṭu, and the moral epigrams of the Tirukkuṟaḷ. The school administers a working printing-press of the kind used by U. V. Swaminatha Iyer, and its annual Bhakti-literature seminar draws scholars from across the four southern states.

School IV · Saṅgīta

Carnatic Music & Bharatanatyam

Department of the Performing Arts
Acharya
Vidwan T. R. Subramanian, Sangita Kalanidhi (2017)
Faculty
29 professors · 11 visiting vidwans
Degrees
B.A.M.A.Ph.D.

Vocal and instrumental tracks (vīṇā, mridangam, violin, nadaswaram) follow the seventy-two melakarta system as codified by Venkatamakhi. The dance wing teaches the Tanjore Quartet repertoire under the guru-shishya parampara, with weekly arangam practice in the campus's stone-floored Natya Mandapa, modelled on the Chidambaram nrtta sabha.

School V · Śilpa

Iconography & Stone Sculpture

Department of the Image
Acharya
Dr. Ganapathi Sthapati Rajagopalan, D.Litt. (hon., Mahabalipuram)
Faculty
22 professors · 5 visiting silpis
Degrees
B.A.M.A.Ph.D.

Concentrates on the granite and Krishnashila traditions of the Pallava–Chola corridor. The iconometric canon — tāla, aṅgula, navatāla — is taught with calipers and chisel. Doctoral candidates work in the bronze-casting yard at Swamimalai, producing solid-cast Naṭarāja and Pārvatī icons under hereditary stapathi mentorship.

School VI · Vāstu Gaṇita

Vastu Mathematics

Department of Sacred Geometry
Acharya
Prof. Kalyanasundaram Iyengar, M.Sc. (IISc), Ph.D. (TIFR)
Faculty
19 professors · 4 visiting researchers
Degrees
B.A.M.A.Ph.D.

The Sulba-sutras of Baudhayana and Apastamba meet contemporary metric geometry. Coursework treats the 81-pada and 64-pada vastu-purusha-mandala as constraint-satisfaction problems; computational laboratories simulate sun-path, acoustic resonance, and structural load on temple plans drawn from the Kanchi and Tanjavur traditions.

School VII · Vijñāna

Modern Sciences

Department of Conservation, Materials & Computational Heritage
Acharya
Dr. Ramanathan Krishnamoorthy, Ph.D. (IIT-Madras), MIT post-doc
Faculty
21 professors · 5 visiting researchers
Wings
Conservation Science · Petrology · Heritage Computing · Photogrammetry
Degrees
B.A.M.A.Ph.D.

Founded 2004 to pair the older six schools with empirical methods. Laboratories handle XRF analysis of patinated bronzes, structural finite-element modelling of heritage gopurams, terrestrial-laser scanning of inscriptions, and machine-readable transcription of Grantha and Vatteluttu palm-leaves. Joint-supervision agreements are in force with the École française d'Extrême-Orient and the Smithsonian's MCI.
