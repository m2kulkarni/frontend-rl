Academic Year ◆ 2024 — 2025 ◆ Intake Opens 1 February

Admissions & Application

Become a śiṣya of the sthapati and the agamic master. Applications for the academic year commencing 12 July 2024 are reviewed in three rounds across our Tanjavur, Madurai and Srirangam centres.

Application Timeline

Key Dates for the 2024–25 Cycle Tanjavur · Madurai · Srirangam

  1. Stage I 1 February 2024

    Online and paper applications open. Application form available at all three campus offices and via post on written request.

  2. Stage II 15 April 2024

    Last date for receipt of completed applications, demand draft, and self-attested transcripts.

  3. Stage III 9 May 2024

    Common Entrance Examination conducted simultaneously at the Tanjavur and Madurai centres, 09:30–13:00 IST.

  4. Stage IV 28 May – 14 June 2024

    Aptitude interviews and practical demonstrations (śilpa, vādya, vācana). Candidates summoned in batches of forty.

  5. Stage V 22 June 2024

    Provisional admission lists published. Counselling and seat acceptance held at the North Wing, Tanjavur Campus.

  6. Stage VI 12 July 2024

    Academic year begins with the traditional Vidyārambham ceremony at the Rajagopuram Hall.

Application Fee

₹ 850 for general applicants; ₹ 425 for SC/ST, EWS, and hereditary temple-service candidates. Payable by demand draft favouring "Sri Rajagopuram Institute" on a Tanjavur-clearing bank.

Hostel & Mess

Optional residential charges of ₹ 32,000 per annum (twin-share in the Brihadeeshwara Hall). Daily sāttvic meals included; no separate caution deposit for shastric scholars.

Reservation Policy

15% of seats reserved for hereditary śilpi and arccaka families; 22.5% for SC/ST; 7.5% for differently-abled candidates. All quotas verified at counselling.

◆ Vidyā Dadāti Vinayam ◆

Scholarships Awarded on merit & need

Rajaraja Chola Merit Award

₹ 60,000 / year · 12 seats

Full tuition waiver for the top three rank-holders in each undergraduate programme. Continued each year subject to a CGPA of 8.0 and an annual essay on Cōḻa-period architecture.

Andal Tamil Scholarship

₹ 35,000 / year · 24 seats

For women candidates pursuing Tamil Śaiva literature, Bharatanāṭyam, or vocal music. Open to applicants from Tamil-medium schools across Tamil Nadu and northern Sri Lanka.

Vishvakarma Sthapati Stipend

₹ 4,000 / month · 18 seats

Monthly maintenance stipend for hereditary śilpi and stone-carving diploma students. Includes a free toolkit and lodging at the Tanjavur workshop guesthouse.
