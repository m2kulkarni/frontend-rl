A Table Beneath The Iwan

Reservations & Private Dining

Bookings at Chehel Sotoun are kept by the maître d' in a single ledger, opened thirty days before each service. Reserve a place at the long table, claim the Cobalt Iwan for your own evening, or arrive without notice and take a seat at the bar.

The Ledger

— kept by Mr. Pirouzi, opened daily at the eleventh hour —

A reply by telephone arrives within two working days. The ledger holds no place until that reply is given.

Thirty Days Hence

· XXX ·

The ledger opens at the eleventh hour each morning, accepting bookings for the same date thirty days forward. A request lodged today for the fourteenth of next month is the earliest hand the kitchen will take.

The Cobalt Iwan

£145 per guest

Our private room, faced in cobalt and turquoise tilework, accepts bookings of eight or more for an eleven-course tasting drawn from the courtly cuisine of seventeenth-century Isfahan. Wine pairings, £75. One service nightly.

The Bar Counter

— walk-in —

Twelve stools at the brick counter, kept always for those without booking. Mast-o-musir, shirazi salad, kashk-e bademjan and warm taftoon are served from six in the evening until half past midnight, six nights of the week.

Terms of the House

— cancellation, deposit, and the courtesy of notice —

A reservation for the long table or the garden niche is held against a card; no charge is taken at the time of booking. Should plans change, we ask forty-eight hours of notice — given by telephone or by reply to the confirming letter — and the card is released without fee. Cancellation within forty-eight hours, or absence at the appointed hour, is settled at £35 per guest; this sum goes to the kitchen, in fairness to the produce already brought in for your table.

Bookings of the Cobalt Iwan require a deposit of £40 per guest at the time the date is confirmed, redeemed against the final account. Cancellations of private bookings made more than seven days ahead are refunded in full; within seven days, the deposit is retained. We do not levy a service charge — gratuity, if offered, is divided in equal hand among the kitchen and the floor.

Children dining before nine in the evening are most welcome; a children's portion of chelow with saffron-glazed lamb is laid at £14. We regret that, after nine, the dining room is reserved for guests above the age of twelve. Dogs are received in the courtyard only, where a bowl of water and a piece of dried lamb await.
