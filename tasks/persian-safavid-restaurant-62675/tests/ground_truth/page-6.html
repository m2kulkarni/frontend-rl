Finding the Iwan

A narrow lane off Hanover Square, a turquoise lantern at the door, and the courtly table of seventeenth-century Isfahan, four minutes from the underground.

On Dress

Considered, unfussed. We have no jacket rule and no list of forbidden things; we ask only that what you wear feels like care taken. The room is candlelit and tiled — most guests dress for the room rather than against it.

On Children

Welcome at lunch on Friday and Saturday, where they tend to be happiest among the platters of rice and herbs. At dinner, by quiet arrangement only — please mention them when you reserve, and we will set the corner banquette aside.
