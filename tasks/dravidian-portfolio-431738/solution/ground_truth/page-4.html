XXIV PLATES · CARTRIDGE · INK

These plates are the working record of the studio's drawing-board, kept in tall pigeon-holes against the south wall of the workshop on Big Street, Kumbakonam, where the morning light falls flat across the boards. Each sheet is drawn in carbon ink upon ninety-grams-per-square-metre cartridge of Indian manufacture, ruled with a Faber 3H pencil for the under-construction and inked with crow-quill and reservoir pen for the finished line. Elevations are drawn at one-to-forty-eight; plans, by long custom of this studio, at one-to-ninety-six.

Where the plate records a measurement taken in the field, the dateline gives the Tamil month and the year of survey, and the source line names the monument, the elevation studied, and the assistant who held the rod. The convention is severe: no shading, no rendering of texture, only the line. Stone is shown by its profile alone. Ornament — lotus, kuḍu-arch, vyāḷa — is set down at the same scale as the masonry, that the proportion may be read at a glance.

I. Gopuram Elevations

After the southern gate-towers of the Cōḻa-Pāṇḍya succession — seven and nine tiers

PLATE I

South Gopuram, Tiruvarur

Aani 2008 · ink, 1 : 48

Eleven-bay base; seven receding talas. Pratima niches on every pāda of the second through fifth tier. Kalaśa count: thirteen, in three sizes.

After measurement of the southern gopuram, Tyāgarāja-svāmi temple, Tiruvārūr; Aani 2008. Rod held by S. Murugan.

PLATE II

East Gopuram, Chidambaram

Maasi 2009 · ink, 1 : 48

Nine-tier elevation, the great east gate. Niche programme reads the hundred and eight karaṇas in successive bays.

After measurement of the east gopuram, Naṭarāja temple, Chidambaram; Maasi 2009. Surveyors V. Periyasami, K. Anbu.

PLATE III

Rajagopuram, Srirangam

Karthigai 2011 · ink, 1 : 48

The unfinished tower as completed in 1987, drawn at full elevation; eleven talas above the dvāra-koṣṭha.

After photographs and partial site survey, Raṅganātha temple, Srirangam; Karthigai 2011.

PLATE IV

West Gopuram, Madurai

Vaikasi 2012 · ink, 1 : 48

Nine-tier elevation, with central kuḍu-band study. Note the projection of the karṇa-kūṭas on the third tala.

After measurement of the west gopuram, Mīnākṣi-Sundareśvara temple, Madurai; Vaikasi 2012.

PLATE V

North Gopuram, Tiruvannamalai

Aippasi 2013 · ink, 1 : 48

Seven talas, the proportions of which are notably squat; comparative scale-bar set against Plate I for ratio study.

After site visit, Aruṇācaleśvara temple, Tiruvannamalai; Aippasi 2013.

PLATE VI

Composite Elevation Study

Margazhi 2014 · ink, 1 : 48

Three gate-towers of the Cōḻa, late-Cōḻa, and Vijayanagara periods drawn at one scale, that the development of the silhouette may be compared.

Compiled in studio from Plates I, III, and field notebooks; Margazhi 2014.

II. Prākāra Plans

Concentric enclosures — the Srirangam manner of seven and the Cōḻa manner of three

PLATE VII

Seven Prākāras, Srirangam

Panguni 2009 · ink, 1 : 96

The seven concentric prākāras with their gopuras placed along the cardinals; eccentric outer-court extension to the south recorded.

After plotted survey of the Raṅganātha temple, Srirangam; Panguni 2009. Reduced from field plot at 1 : 24.

PLATE VIII

Three Prākāras, Darasuram

Aadi 2010 · ink, 1 : 96

Late-Cōḻa plan; the inner sanctum offset eastward by one rod from the outer enclosure's centre.

After ground survey of the Airāvateśvara temple, Darasuram; Aadi 2010. With S. Murugan.

PLATE IX

Inner Prākāra, Tiruvarur

Maasi 2010 · ink, 1 : 96

The inner court only, with the Tyāgarāja shrine and the maṇḍapa-of-six-pillars indicated.

After measurement of the inner enclosure, Tyāgarāja temple, Tiruvārūr; Maasi 2010.

PLATE X

Outer Court, Chidambaram

Chittirai 2011 · ink, 1 : 96

The Śivagaṅgā tank and the thousand-pillared maṇḍapa located within the second enclosure; gates on three cardinals.

After plotted survey, Naṭarāja temple, Chidambaram; Chittirai 2011.

PLATE XI

Vimana & Cella, Tanjavur

Aippasi 2012 · ink, 1 : 96

Plan of the Bṛhadīśvara cella with circumambulatory; ardhamaṇḍapa, mahāmaṇḍapa, and entry porch all to the same scale.

After Archaeological Survey plates, recompiled and re-measured by the studio; Aippasi 2012.

PLATE XII

Comparative Prākāra Plans

Karthigai 2013 · ink, 1 : 96

Four temple plans — Pallava, early Cōḻa, late Cōḻa, and Vijayanagara — ordered by century, that the multiplication of enclosures may be read.

Studio compilation from field plates VII through XI; Karthigai 2013.

III. Lotus-Rosette Ceiling Studies

Studies in the rotation of the eight-petalled lotus — ceiling-slab geometry

PLATE XIII

Eight-Petalled Lotus, Darasuram

Vaikasi 2014 · ink, 1 : 12

The standard rosette; eight petals upon a circular kalyāṇa-medallion. Petal width to length is in the ratio of three to seven.

After ceiling-slab in the mahāmaṇḍapa, Airāvateśvara temple; Vaikasi 2014.

PLATE XIV

Sixteen Petals, Tribhuvanam

Aani 2014 · ink, 1 : 12

Sixteen-petal rotation, the inner ring offset by half a petal from the outer; rare in late-Cōḻa work, perhaps unique to this site.

After ceiling of the Kampahareśvara temple, Tribhuvanam; Aani 2014.

PLATE XV

Nested Rosette, Patteeswaram

Aadi 2015 · ink, 1 : 12

The eight-and-eight rosette: the inner ring of small petals counter-rotated through half a division.

After the maṇḍapa ceiling, Patteeswaram; Aadi 2015. See Folio III for the case-study.

PLATE XVI

Square-on-Circle, Gangaikondacholapuram

Avani 2015 · ink, 1 : 12

The geometric construction underlying the rosette — the square inscribed in the square inscribed in the square — from which all the petal-arcs are struck.

Reconstructed from ceiling rubbing, Bṛhadīśvara temple, Gangaikondacholapuram; Avani 2015.

PLATE XVII

Rotation Sequence, Six Plates

Purattasi 2016 · ink, 1 : 24

Five steps of fifteen-degree rotation, with the assembled lotus at the foot. Drawn for the apprentices.

Studio teaching plate; Purattasi 2016.

PLATE XVIII

Setting-Out for the Carver

Aippasi 2016 · ink, 1 : 6

The setting-out plate: the construction-circle, the petal-circle, the centre, with chase-lines marked in oxblood for the chisel.

Studio plate, prepared for V. Sundaram, carver; Aippasi 2016.

IV. Kalaśa Proportioning & Detail Sheets

The finial-pot studied for stone, copper-clad, and bronze cast

PLATE XIX

Stone Kalaśa, Five Cubits

Margazhi 2017 · ink, 1 : 6

The standard five-cubit kalaśa: pot-belly to neck in the ratio four to one; lip projection one-twelfth of width.

After kalaśas in stock at the studio yard; Margazhi 2017.

PLATE XX

Bronze Kalaśa with Pendant Beads

Thai 2018 · ink, 1 : 4

Cast-bronze finial of the dvajastambha-cap variety; the three pendant pearls indicate Vaiṣṇava attribution.

After original in the bronze gallery, Tanjore Art Gallery, by permission; Thai 2018.

PLATE XXI

Kuḍu-Arch & Vyāḷa

Panguni 2018 · ink, 1 : 8

At left, the kuḍu-arch (gavākṣa) with face emergent; at right, the vyāḷa-yāḷi rampant against a foliate ground.

After detail of the south wall, Naṭarāja temple, Chidambaram; Panguni 2018.

PLATE XXII

Pillar Capital — Fluted Order

Vaikasi 2019 · ink, 1 : 6

The fluted-shaft order with bell, abacus, and corbel; the corbel taken at the proportion of nine to four.

After pillar in the maṇḍapa-of-six, Tiruvārūr; Vaikasi 2019.

PLATE XXIII

Door-Jamb & Threshold

Aani 2020 · ink, 1 : 6

Four-banded door-jamb with lalāṭa-bimba; the threshold-block (uttara-pīṭha) shown in elevation and section.

After the western entry of the inner enclosure, Patteeswaram; Aani 2020.

PLATE XXIV

Index Sheet & Scale-Bar

Margazhi 2023 · ink, 1 : 1

The scale-bar at full size; the studio's title-cartouche; the standing key to all twenty-four plates of the present folio.

Studio plate, perpetual; first ruled Margazhi 2023, kept as the master-sheet.
