The Practice

This is a workshop of temple drawing, vimana measurement, and figural carving in the southern manner of the Cholas and the Nayaks. The granite is local; the chisels are forged at Swamimalai; the rule is the cubit of twenty-four angulas, as set down in the Mayamata and the Manasara.

Every commission begins with a measured plan in graphite and red ochre on tracing linen, advances through clay bommai in three scales, and ends only when the stone has answered to the hand of three generations of carvers in this house.

Mudra of the

The Studio

R. Kalyanasundaram took apprenticeship in 1962 under his father, V. Ramaswamy Sthapati of the Vishwakarma lineage of Tiruvarur, and opened the present workshop on Big Street, Kumbakonam, in the spring of 1978 — the same year the gopuram of Sri Sarangapani Temple was last regilt.

The studio accepts three commissions a year, no more. Drawings are issued in two copies on hand-laid Pondicherry linen, signed in vermilion and bound in palm-leaf cord. The workshop is closed on Amavasya and on Vijayadashami.

Three Works in Hand

— accepted by the studio for the year Krodhi, Vikrama Samvat 2081 —

Commission · I

Granite Mandapa
for Sri Patteeswaram

Patteeswaram Trust · commenced Aippasi, 2023 · for completion Vaikasi, 2025

A sixteen-pillared ardha-mandapa in black Kanchipuram granite, raised before the southern entry of the Sri Thenupureeswarar shrine. Each pillar bears a yali in raised relief, with the central pair worked as Bhairava and Durga after the Chola precedent at Darasuram.

Foundation stone laid by the trustees on 11 November 2023, the day of Karthikai Deepam. Stone supplied from the Pallavaram quarries; carving in progress at the workshop yard, Big Street.

Commission  ·  ₹ 47,80,000
Stone  ·  184 cu. ft. granite
Hands  ·  7 carvers, 2 draughtsmen

Commission · II

Bronze Kalasha Set
for a Thanjavur Shrine

Private patron, Thanjavur · cast Maasi, 2024 · to be installed Aani, 2024

A set of nine kalashas in pancha-loha bronze for the rooftop finials of a private Vishnu shrine in West Main Street, Thanjavur. Cast at the Swamimalai foundry of T. Muthuswamy Sthapati to drawings issued by this studio in February.

Tallest finial 27 inches; smallest 11. Each vessel is incised at the throat with the Sri-vatsa and the patron's house mark. Patina developed by the traditional turmeric-and-tamarind bath over fourteen days.

Commission  ·  ₹ 8,65,000
Metal  ·  142 kg pancha-loha
Casting  ·  lost-wax, single pour

Commission · III

Conservation Drawings
for a 14th-c. Prakara

State Department of Archaeology · Phase I, 2024 · ongoing consultancy

Measured plans, sections, and elevation studies of the outer prakara wall of the Sri Vaitheeswaran Koil — a Vijayanagara-period work of 1387 — undertaken in advisory partnership with the Tamil Nadu State Department of Archaeology, Madras circle.

Forty-two sheets in graphite, ink, and oxide red on cotton tracing, drawn at scales of 1:50 and 1:20. Three watercolour reconstructions show the lost southwestern bastion as it must have stood before the storm of 1819.

Consultancy  ·  ₹ 2,40,000 / phase
Drawings  ·  42 sheets, 2 portfolios
Field days  ·  31, March–July 2024
