Selected Works

— Stone, Line, and the Measured Plan, 2009 – 2024 —

Nine commissions chosen from thirty-six years of work in granite, sandstone, bronze, and ink — undertaken for temple trusts, devasthanams, and private patrons across the Kaveri delta and beyond. Every piece carries its own measure: a foot-rule of the old tāḷamāna system, a date in the Vikari or Pingala year, and the name of the donor by whose grace the stone was cut.

Folio I · Granite

Pillared Mandapa,
Sri Patteeswaram

Granite · Tanjavur District · 2019 – 2022

A sixteen-pillared mukha-maṇḍapa raised before the southern shrine, each shaft cut from a single block of black Krishnashila and dressed with yāli, hamsa, and lotus-cushion capitals after the late Chola manner. Commissioned by the temple Devasthanam under the trusteeship of Sri V. Ramanathan

Patteeswaram Devasthanam ₹ 84,00,000
Folio II · Sandstone

Lotus-Band Base Course,
Tiruvidaimarudur Shrine

Sandstone · Mahamaham month · 2017

Restoration of the padma-bandha course running the full perimeter of the Mahalingaswamy vimāna, replacing twenty-three weather-eaten panels with new sandstone carved to match the surviving 12th-century band. Drawings prepared from rubbings taken in monsoon

Mahalingaswamy Trust ₹ 19,40,000
Folio III · Stone

Yāli Corner-Bracket,
Private Collection

Black granite · Madurai · 2015

A free-standing corner bracket — yāli rampant over a crouching elephant — cut for the inner courtyard of the Sundaram family residence. Eighty-four inches high, finished by hand with iron chisel and emery; signed and dated on the underside in Tamil

Sundaram Family, Madurai ₹ 6,75,000
Folio IV · Drawing

Twenty-One Elevations
of a Nine-Tier Gopuram

Ink on cartridge paper · 2013

A measured drawing-set comprising twenty-one sheets at 1:48 scale: front, side, and quarter-rear elevations, plus eighteen sectional plates through each tala of a proposed nine-tier gopuram for the Tiruvarur eastern entrance. Drawn in walnut ink with a ruling pen

Tiruvarur Temple Office ₹ 2,25,000
Folio V · Bronze

Kalasha & Stūpi Finial,
Tiruchirapalli

Cast bronze · Vrishabha month · 2011

A nine-pot crowning kalaśa and stūpi finial cast in single-pour bronze for the south prākāra shrine at Tiruchirapalli, eleven feet six inches in total height. Lost-wax model carved in the atelier; chasing and gilding completed on site

Sri Ranganathaswamy Annexe ₹ 11,80,000
Folio VI · Granite

Dvārapālaka Pair,
Swamimalai New Shrine

Krishnashila granite · 2020

Two door-guardians, each seven feet tall, flanking the eastern gateway of the new Subrahmanya shrine at Swamimalai. Iconography fixed in consultation with the chief āgama-paṇḍita Sri T. Ananthakrishna Sastrigal; consecrated on the morning of Krittika

Swamimalai Temple Trust ₹ 22,60,000
Folio VII · Wood

Painted Ratha Panels,
Kumbakonam Adi-Kumbeswarar

Teak with mineral pigment · 2009

Six narrative panels for the temple chariot recounting the Kumbeśvara sthala-purāṇa, drawn from the Tamil text of Kāḷahasti Kavi. Carved in seasoned Nilambur teak and painted in vermilion, peacock blue, indigo, and gold leaf over a gesso ground

Adi-Kumbeswarar Trust ₹ 4,90,000
Folio VIII · Drawing

Measured Plan,
Darasuram Airāvateśvara

Pencil & ink wash · 2016

Twelve-sheet measured survey of the Airāvateśvara temple at Darasuram undertaken on commission for the Archaeological Survey, Chennai Circle. Ground plan, longitudinal section, and details of the chariot-wheel plinth recorded over forty-one site days

ASI · Chennai Circle ₹ 3,40,000
Folio IX · Stone

Nāgabandha Threshold,
Tirunallar Saneeswaran

Black basalt · 2024

A two-piece threshold stone bearing twin nāga-knots in low relief, fitted at the western gate of the Saneeswara shrine. Cut from a single basalt block at Pudukkottai quarry; laid on the auspicious day of Sani-pradosha after invocation by the temple archaka

Tirunallar Devasthanam ₹ 2,15,000
