Of Drawings & Site Visits

a long-standing convention of the studio, not lightly set aside

It is the firm practice of this atelier that no drawing, plan, or elevation is dispatched until the Sthapati has himself walked the site, taken the soil in hand, and noted the orientation by sun and pole-star. A measured site-visit is therefore the necessary preliminary to all commissions, whether for a new mandapa, the restoration of a vimana, or the carving of a single dvarapala.

Site visits are arranged for the second and fourth Tuesdays of each month. The journey, lodging, and a modest honorarium of ₹4,500 per day are borne by the trust or patron. Within the Cauvery delta the visit ordinarily occupies one day; for sites beyond Madurai or Tirunelveli, three days should be reckoned.

Drawings are rendered upon hand-laid Wadhwan paper, in india ink with vermilion notation, and are delivered rolled in a cloth-bound bamboo cylinder by registered post within ninety days of the site visit.

Direction from the Junction

for visiting trustees alighting at Kumbakonam railway station

From the Kumbakonam Junction (Southern Railway, Mayiladuthurai — Tiruchirappalli line), proceed on foot or by cycle-rickshaw eastward along Railway Feeder Road for some four hundred yards, until the road meets the Sarangapani temple chariot street. Turn south, passing the eastern gopuram of the Sarangapani temple on your right.

Continue for two short blocks. Where the chariot-street narrows and the brass-merchants' stalls begin, Big Street (Periya Theru) opens to the left. The fourteenth doorway, marked by a vermilion-painted threshold and a small inscribed lintel reading SILPASTHANAM, is the workshop.

Total distance from the railway platform: approximately one kilometre. The walk is shaded for the greater part of the year. Auto-rickshaws will not enter the lane; trustees arriving by motor-car may set down at the chariot-street corner.

Three Resthouses for Visiting Trustees

all within walking distance of the workshop, listed in the order of the studio's preference

  1. Pandian Hotel & Choultry 52, Sarangapani East Sannadhi Street · Kumbakonam 612 001 · Tel. (0435) 242 0397.
    A modest establishment of fourteen rooms above the proprietor's brass shop. Vegetarian meals are served on plantain leaf at half past eleven and at eight; the upper rooms look directly onto the Sarangapani temple gopuram. Single room ₹850 per night; trustees' suite (two beds, writing-desk, attached bath) ₹1,400. Telegraphic address: PANDIAN.
  2. Sri Mahamaham Lodge 7, T.S.R. Big Street · Kumbakonam 612 001 · Tel. (0435) 243 1156.
    Recommended for parties of three or more. The proprietor, Sri V. Subramaniam, is himself a trustee of the Adi Kumbeshwarar temple and is well acquainted with the customs of visiting devasthanam delegations. Filtered coffee is brought to the room at six in the morning without request. Rooms ₹1,100; family suite ₹1,950.
  3. The Raya's Bungalow Guest House Sannadhi Street, opposite Nageswaran temple tank · Kumbakonam 612 001 · Tel. (0435) 242 8821.
    A converted nineteenth-century zamindari residence with six rooms set about a tiled inner court. Quieter than the foregoing two, and favoured by scholars from the École française d'Extrême-Orient and the Archaeological Survey. ₹1,650 per night, including a simple south-Indian breakfast. Advance booking by post is essential during the Mahamaham year.
