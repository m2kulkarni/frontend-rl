Accessibility

Step-free access to every public gallery is provided by a discreet ramp through the north iwan, opening directly into the central courtyard. Wheelchairs are available without charge at the porter's lodge; large-print exhibition labels and a tactile tile-fragment cabinet stand beside Gallery III.

Companions of disabled visitors enter without charge. Service animals are welcome throughout, save for the Kitābkhāna manuscript room where humidity is regulated.

A quiet sanctuary, lined with cobalt felt, sits behind the muqarnas vestibule for visitors who require rest from the dense ornament of the main halls.

Guided Tours

Docents — drawn from the curatorial staff and senior students of the Isfahan University faculty of restoration — lead small groups of no more than twelve through the permanent collection. Tours convene at the gold star inlaid in the courtyard pavement.

The Courtyard Tea-Room

Rosewater sharbat beneath the plane tree

Set in the south-eastern corner of the museum's chahār-bāgh courtyard, the tea-room serves chilled rosewater sharbat, saffron-perfumed tea, and almond nān-e nokhodchī from late morning until closing. Seating extends onto the tiled hawz terrace whenever the Isfahan weather obliges; in winter the room retreats indoors beneath a small painted dome.
