The Madrasa Found

— address & approach from the Qaisariyya gate —

Where We Stand

✦ ✦ ✦

The Madrasa occupies the southern arcade of the Maydān-i Naqsh-i Jahān, two bays east of the Masjid-i Shāh and four bays west of the Qaisariyya gate. Visitors arriving from the bazaar emerge directly beneath our painted timber portico; those approaching from the Royal Mosque should keep the great square upon their right hand and walk the length of seven shop-fronts.

The institute has remained at this address since its re-foundation in MMVII, occupying buildings continuously associated with the Safavid royal workshops since 1031 AH / 1622 CE.

The Approach

✦ ✦ ✦

Most scholars enter the Madrasa by the Qaisariyya gate, that monumental tile-clad portal at the northern head of the square. From its threshold the path is direct, ceremonial, and entirely within the great Safavid maydan — a route designed for processions of the Shah and now retraced daily by our students.

  1. Pass beneath the Qaisariyya gate; note the painted muqarnas vault overhead.
  2. Bear south along the eastern arcade for some 280 paces, keeping the central pool to your right.
  3. At the Lutfullah mosque, cross the maydan diagonally toward the Masjid-i Shāh.
  4. Our gate is the seventh ogee bay east of the Royal Mosque, marked by a turquoise tile lozenge bearing the Madrasa seal.

Of Carriages, Caravans & Trains

— how the visitor may compose his journey to Isfahan —

From abroad, scholars most often arrive by air at the Shahid Beheshti International Airport, eighteen kilometres east of the maydan; from Tehran the daily express train of the Rāh-Āhan deposits travellers at the Sufiyan station, whence a metro line of the second order reaches Naqsh-i Jahān in twenty-two minutes.

Within the city itself we counsel the abandonment of taxicabs once the bazaar has been entered; the maydan is closed to vehicular traffic from sunrise until ten of the evening, and the Madrasa keeps no carriage gate.

  1. Tehran — Isfahan express train · 6 h 20 m · daily 07:25 & 22:40
  2. Airport coach · half-hourly · alight at Maydān-i Imām
  3. Isfahan metro Line 1 · stop "Naqsh-i Jahān" · two minutes' walk
  4. By foot from the Si-o-se Bridge · twenty-eight minutes north

Of Modest Dress

All visitors are asked to enter the courtyard with arms covered to the wrist; women are kindly requested to bring a light scarf for the iwan and the prayer hall. Loaner garments are kept by the gatehouse without charge.

Photography

Photography is permitted in the courtyard and exhibition iwan without flash. The library, the manuscript vault, and the small Lutfullah-style chamber are reserved from all lenses, on penalty of forfeit of the visit.

Group Visits

Parties exceeding nine in number are received by appointment only, addressed to the Registrar fourteen days in advance. The Madrasa may at its discretion divide such parties between two consecutive tours.
