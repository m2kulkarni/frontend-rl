· Bismillāh · Ann. Domini MMVII ·

The Royal Square
of Learning

Founded in the spirit of the royal square of Shah Abbas, our institute trains scholars in the arts, sciences, and architecture of Safavid Iran (1501–1736). Within these cobalt walls the lessons of the Maydān endure: that geometry is argument, that calligraphy is breath, and that the spandrel is never silent.

— A residential madrasa under the patronage of the University of Isfahan Studies, granting the degrees of Mubtadī, Mustanṣir, and Mudarris.

EST. · ISFAHAN · 1429 A.H.

The Three Faculties

· Three Pavilions Around the Court ·

Architecture
& Tile

· ʿImāra wa Kāshī ·

Mastery of the iwan, the muqarnas, and the seven-fold tile. Students measure the courts of the Maydān-i Naqsh-i Jahān, draft the squinches of the Shaykh Luṭfullāh, and fire haft-rang glazes in the institute's own kiln yard.

Five-year programme leading to the Ijāza of the Builder, with summer at the Masjid-i Shāh.

42 SCHOLARS · KILN OPEN MON–THU

Manuscript
& Calligraphy

· Khaṭṭ wa Taṣwīr ·

From the reed pen of Mīr ʿImād to the gold-leaf margins of the Shahnama-yi Shah Tahmasp. Pupils learn nastaʿlīq under the niche-light of the scriptorium, then bind their copybooks in oxblood morocco at week's end.

Three-year course; entrance by examination of the alif and a single sūra in shikasta.

28 SCHOLARS · SCRIPTORIUM DAILY

Astronomy
& Mathematics

· Hayʾa wa Riyāḍiyyāt ·

Inheriting the geometry of Bahāʾ al-Dīn al-ʿĀmilī, the faculty trains students in the astrolabe, qibla-finding, and the calendar of the Jalālī era. The roof observatory, fitted with a brass quadrant of 1632, is open on clear nights.

Four-year course; a thesis of original observation is required for graduation.

19 SCHOLARS · OBSERVATORY NIGHTLY
· I ·

The Court

Daily lessons under the open iwan of the central courtyard, from dawn prayer until the call of ʿaṣr.

· II ·

The Pen

Forty hours of calligraphy and design weekly, regardless of faculty — the discipline of the line for all.

· III ·

The Compass

Every scholar must demonstrate mastery of the seven-fold and twelve-fold girih before graduation.

· IV ·

The Tongue

Persian, Arabic, and Ottoman Turkish are read in original; lectures alternate weekly between them.
