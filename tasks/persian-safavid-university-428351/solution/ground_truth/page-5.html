An Invitation to Study

— Maydān-i Naqsh-i Jahān, Isfahan —

Twice each year the Madrasa receives candidates into its long-form programmes; the autumn cohort, opening at the close of summer 1404, admits to all six faculties, including those whose work is conducted within the tile-yards and observatories rather than the lecture-halls. The application process is deliberate, slow, and exacting. We do not require eloquence so much as evidence of patience: a sustained capacity to read, to draft, to copy, and to look. The pages below set out what we ask of those who wish to join us.

Candidates are admitted on the strength of their dossier alone. There is no entrance examination, no interview by panel; instead, the Registrar and two senior fellows from the relevant faculty consider each application against the standards described herein. Notice of admission is given by registered post, never by telephone, and never before the deadlines below have closed.

Required of Every Applicant

  1. A Research Statement of 1,500 Words

    Composed in English, French, or Persian, addressing a single problem the candidate proposes to take up under the supervision of a named fellow. Footnotes and bibliography fall outside the word-count; transliteration follows the Library of Congress system without diacritics.

  2. Two Academic References

    One must come from the candidate's most recent supervisor of record. The second may be from any scholar familiar with the candidate's written work. Both letters should arrive sealed, by post or via institutional electronic dispatch, addressed to the Office of the Registrar.

  3. Evidence of Language Proficiency

    Certified competence in classical Persian to the level of unaided reading of Safavid chancery hands; or, for candidates working chiefly in European sources, certified reading knowledge of one of: French, Italian, or Latin. Evidence may take the form of transcripts, certificates, or signed attestation by a previous instructor.

  4. A Portfolio — Tile Mosaic Faculty Only

    Applicants to the Faculty of Kāshī-kārī must submit a portfolio of fifteen plates: ten of completed work in cut-tile, glazed brick, or related media, and five of measured drawings. Plates are to be presented at A3, unbound, and shipped flat to the address given below.

Deadlines for the 1404 Cohort

— Reckoned by the Solar Hijrī calendar; postmark, not receipt, governs —
International Applicants
30 Dey 1404
(20 January 2026)

Dossiers from candidates resident outside the Islamic Republic of Iran must reach the Registrar's office by this date. Allowance is given for postal transit; courier-tracked envelopes are recommended.

Domestic Applicants
15 Esfand 1404
(6 March 2026)

Candidates resident within Iran may submit by post or in person at the Office of the Registrar, situated in the eastern īwān of the Madrasa, Tuesday through Saturday, eight to four.

Concerning Fee Waivers

The application fee of 2,400,000 rials is waived in full for candidates whose registered place of birth, or whose continuous residence of not less than seven years, falls within the historic provinces of artisanal practice from which the Madrasa draws much of its inheritance. Documentation of provincial residence — a national identity card or its certified equivalent — must accompany the dossier.
