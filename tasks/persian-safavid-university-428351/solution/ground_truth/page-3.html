Principal Fellows in Residence

Three medallions of those scholars whose research currently shapes the Madrasa's published programme for the year 1446 H.

Prof. Mahin Daneshvar

Chair of Tile Mosaic & Architectural Ceramics
✦ Faculty of Ornament ✦

Professor Daneshvar leads a four-year reconstruction of the cuerda-seca repertoire of the Sheikh Lotfollah dome, working from in-situ tracings and the workshop fragments preserved in the Madrasa's kiln archive. Her atelier produces both critical drawings and fired test panels at 1:1.

In Residence Since 2011 · Office: Iwan-i Sharqī, Room 4

Dr. Hossein Aghazadeh

Reader in Codicology & Manuscript Studies
✦ Faculty of the Book ✦

Dr. Aghazadeh is preparing a critical edition of the 1602 Shahnameh of Shah Abbas I, collating the Tehran codex against fragments held at the Chester Beatty and the Topkapı. The first three fascicles, comprising the Kayanid cycle, will appear in 2026 from the Madrasa Press.

In Residence Since 2017 · Office: Maktab-khāna, Room 11

Dr. Eleanor Whitcombe

Visiting Fellow · Warburg Institute, London
✦ Hilāl Visiting Chair · 2024–2025 ✦

Dr. Whitcombe arrives from the Warburg Institute to complete her monograph on Sebastiano Serlio's reception of Islamic ornament — tracing how Venetian woodcuts of Cairene and Tabrizi pattern entered the Libro Quarto of 1537 and onwards into the printed grammar of European architecture.

In Residence Sept. 2024 — June 2025 · Guest Wing, Cell 3
