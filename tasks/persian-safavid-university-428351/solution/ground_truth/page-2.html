Faculties & Programs

Four courses of study under the dome of the Naqsh-i Jahān

The Madrasa offers four sustained courses of study, each shaped by the conviction that the geometric, calligraphic, and architectural sciences of Safavid Persia remain a living discipline. Instruction is conducted in the workshops of the Maydān, in the manuscript rooms of the library, and on the scaffolding of Isfahan's monuments themselves.

Degrees of the House

— Four scholarly tracks, taught by master and apprentice —

Master in Tile Mosaic Conservation

✦ M.A. · Two Academic Years ✦

Conducted within the restoration ateliers adjoining the Masjid-i Shāh, this degree trains conservators in the haft-rang and muʿarraq techniques: the cutting, glazing, and re-bedding of cobalt and turquoise tile-work as practised by the kāshī-tarāsh masters of the seventeenth century. Students spend the second year on a sanctioned restoration site under the Cultural Heritage Organisation.

Duration24 months
LanguagePersian & English
CycleAdmits September

Doctorate in Persian Codicology

✦ Ph.D. · Four to Six Years ✦

Devoted to the material study of Safavid manuscripts: paper-fibres, gallnut inks, gold-flecked illumination, and the binding traditions of Tabrīz, Qazvīn and Iṣfahān. Doctoral candidates work directly with codices in the Naqsh-i Jahān library, including the celebrated Shāhnāma fragments of 1614, and present an original critical edition as their final defence.

Duration48–72 months
LanguagePersian, English, Arabic
CycleAdmits February

Diploma in Geometric Construction

✦ Girih · One Academic Year ✦

A practical diploma in girih: the line-and-compass construction of the five-, seven-, ten- and twelvefold star polygons that govern Safavid ornament. Pupils proceed from straightedge drafting through cardboard tessellation to a final cartoon executed at full architectural scale. No prior mathematical training is presumed beyond Euclidean geometry.

Duration10 months
LanguagePersian & English
CycleAdmits September & January

Sorbonne–Isfahan Certificate

✦ Safavid Urbanism · Joint Programme ✦

A joint certificate co-conferred with the Université Paris-Sorbonne, examining the Maydān-i Naqsh-i Jahān as a paradigmatic act of imperial urbanism: the alignment of mosque, palace, bazaar and caravanserai under ʿAbbās I. Candidates spend one term in Paris and two in Isfahan, with field studies in Qazvīn and Mashhad on the via regia.

Duration18 months
LanguageFrench, Persian, English
CycleAdmits October (biennial)

Faculty Leads & Their Pedagogy

— Three masters who hold the seal of each programme —

Prof. Mahin Daneshvar

Chair, Tile Mosaic Conservation

"We do not teach the restoration of tile as the patching of an injured surface, but as the slow re-learning of a craft language. Each student cuts a thousand tesserae before lifting one from the wall — for only the hand that has made the mosaic is fit to repair it."

Dr. Hossein Aghazadeh

Director, Persian Codicology

"A manuscript is a body. Before reading its words we read its skin — the burnish of paper, the grain of the gallnut, the tremor of a scribe's wrist on a winter morning in Tabrīz. Doctoral work here begins where philology ends: in the materials themselves."

Prof. Eleanor Whitcombe

Co-Chair, Sorbonne–Isfahan Urbanism

"The Maydān is not a square but an argument — drawn in stone between sovereign, merchant, and worshipper. Our students learn to read this argument as a Parisian reads Haussmann: with the same rigour, but with an ear for a different polity and a different sky."
