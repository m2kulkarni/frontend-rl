Notices to Readers  ·  No. Ⅵ — terms, hours, and submissions —

Annual Subscription

Four issues, posted by the third day of each season.

2,400 mon — or ¥4,800 in modern coin
Issues
Spring (立春), Summer (立夏), Autumn (立秋), Winter (立冬). Dispatched within three days of each season's commencement.
Within
The Three Capitals (Kyōto, Edo, Ōsaka): postage included. Posted by the second day where the rider permits.
Beyond
Provinces along the Tōkaidō and Nakasendō: add 240 mon for the road. Hokkaidō and Kyūshū: add 480 mon.
Payment
By postal coin, bank draft on Kyōto, or by arrangement with the office during open hours.
Renewal
Renewed at the winter solstice. A reminder sheet, sealed with the crane mark, is sent in the eleventh month.
Lapsed
Back issues from Bunsei 6 onward are held in the print room and may be requested at 800 mon each, while supply lasts.

On Submitting Essays

The Gazette reads submissions between issues, in the slack weeks following each printing. We welcome essays on Edo-period print culture, textile and dye, the traveling roads, and the small material disciplines of machiya life — provided they are written with a particular eye and a willingness to be edited.

Manuscripts may be delivered in person at the office, or posted to the address opposite. The editor, Andō Rinpeireads through the season and replies, in his own hand, before the next issue closes.

  1. One essay only, 1,800 — 4,800 characters, on a single subject, treated patiently.
  2. Brushed or printed copy. Footnotes welcomed; bibliographies preferred over assertions.
  3. Include a cover sheet with name, place of residence, and a sentence on why the subject was chosen.
  4. Reading takes between thirty and sixty days. A reply is sent regardless of decision.
  5. Accepted pieces are paid at the rate of one tan of fine paper, or 1,200 mon per thousand characters.

Andō Rinpei — 安藤 林平
