The Founding

Editor  ·  Founder

Anjana Krishnan

A Mylapore-born art historian trained at Stella Maris and the SOAS, Anjana founded The Prakara Review in the monsoon of 2019, working from a single first-floor room above a flower-seller's stall on Kutchery Road. Her editorial line is severe in matters of accuracy and generous in matters of reverence.

Editor since Issue I, Margazhi 2019

Co-Founder  ·  Architectural Historian

P. Sundaresan

Author of three monographs on Pallava and early Chola sanctum-planning, Sundaresan oversees the Review's drawing office and its programme of measured surveys. He divides his year between Chennai, Kanchipuram, and the Kaveri delta, where his notebooks accumulate at a rate of one per village.

Architectural Editor since 2019

The Printing of the Quarterly

Printer & Bindery

Each issue is printed by Sivakasi Litho Press, Sivakasi, on offset sheets sewn and bound by hand at their Old Bazaar Street works. Plate-making is supervised on press by Sundaresan in person twice a year — for the Aadi and Margazhi issues — to ensure the registration of the colour rules.

The Review goes to bed three weeks before the first day of each named month.

Paper, Type & Binding

Text pages are run on 90 gsm cream wove, milled at Tamil Nadu Newsprint & Papers, Kagithapuram. Plate sections are tipped in on 130 gsm matte. The case is quarter-bound in vermilion cloth over ivory boards.

Set in Fraunces for display and small caps; Inter for running text and captions; ornamental borders re-drawn from late-Chola woodblocks held in the office library.

The Editorial Office

Address for Letters & Manuscripts
The Prakara Review
14 Kutchery Road
Mylapore, Chennai 600 004
Tamil Nadu, India
Open Tuesday to Saturday, 10.30 — 17.30. Closed on temple festival days.
+91 44 2461 1904  ·  office@prakarareview.in

The office occupies the upper floor of a 1928 Madras-tiled house a short walk from the Kapaleeshwarar tank. Visitors are welcome to consult the bound run of back issues and the architectural-drawing archive, by prior appointment with the deputy editor.

"We are a small house. We answer every letter, and we read every manuscript twice — once for what it says, once for what its temple would say."

Manuscripts should be submitted in hard copy, double-spaced, with measured drawings supplied as separate sheets at not less than 1:200. Photographs are to be cleared with the photographer before submission. Permission queries are received at the office address above.
