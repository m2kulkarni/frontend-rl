A Note from the Editor

On the Review

Acta Diurna was founded in the autumn of 2012, in two rented rooms above a bookbinder on Brattle Street, on the conviction that there remained a serious and curious readership for slow writing about the ancient world. We have not, in the years since, much altered that founding article of faith.

Antiquity is not a museum. It is a long quarrel still in progress, and we mean to take part. — L. Aurelian Pratt, Inaugural Editorial, Volume I

The review appears four times a year — at the equinoxes and the solstices — and is printed by the Stinehour Press of Lunenburg in two-colour letterpress on Mohawk Superfine. Each issue runs to roughly one hundred and forty-four pages. We accept unsolicited essays of between three and ten thousand words, addressed to the Managing Editor at the postal address opposite. Reply, when forthcoming, may take eight weeks.
