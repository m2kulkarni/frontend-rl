Dispatches
from the Field

Six brief notices from trenches, archives, and conservation laboratories — read by candle, ledger, and X-ray fluorescence — gathered between November and March.

— Brevia Archaeologica —
Herculaneum · Campania

The Charred Library Yields Plato

At the Officina dei Papiri, the Vesuvius Challenge team confirms a passage from the Academy in scroll PHerc. 1691 — read without unrolling.

Filed by F. Vitale14 March 2024 · Naples
Oplontis · Torre Annunziata

A Fresco Quietly Reattributed

In the so-called Villa Poppaea, panel north-3 — long credited to a Pompeian II workshop — has been reassigned by Soprintendenza analysts to the hand of the Boscoreale Master.

By M. Carbone2 February 2024 · Torre Annunziata
Vindolanda · Northumberland

An Inscription Dated 122 CE

A limestone slab unearthed beside the granary mentions one Crispinus, centurio, and the consular year of Aviola — a near-perfect synchrony with Hadrian's first northern progress.

By A. Birley27 November 2023 · Hexham
Mérida · Extremadura

Forum Mosaic Beneath a Car Park

Workers extending Calle Almendralejo have laid bare 38 m² of polychrome opus tessellatum — a marine cortège — under what was, until October, parking space 47.

By I. Méndez-Ríos9 January 2024 · Augusta Emerita
Aquileia · Friuli

A Patriarchal Lead Seal Surfaces

Dredging in the Natissa river has turned up a bulla plumbea bearing the name of Bishop Maximus, last attested in correspondence with Ambrose of Milan in 381 CE.

By G. Tagliaferri22 December 2023 · Aquileia
Bath · Aquae Sulis

A Curse Tablet, Newly Read

Conservation under raking light has resolved twelve fresh lines of defixio Tab. Sulis 94 — a stolen cloak, a litany of suspects, and a goddess called to redress.

By P. Hartwell5 March 2024 · Somerset
