I.Civic
Works

Civic Works

Opera Civilia

The department of Civic Works receives commissions for basilicas, courts, libraries, and public porticoes. Each project begins with a measured study of the site and proceeds through orthographic plates drawn at the board, in graphite and Indian ink, before any model is begun. We hold that the public building must outlive its commissioning council, and we draw accordingly.

II.Domestic
Interiors

Domestic Interiors

Domus et Cubicula

For the private house, villa, and apartment we conceive interiors after the manner of the Pompeian schemes, with stuccoed friezes, coffered ceilings, and floors of opus sectile or cement-tile. Each room is treated as a small architectural composition: walls articulated by pilasters, doors framed by entablatures. We work in marble, fresco, oak, and bronze, and decline plastic substitutes.

  • Director · L. Albertini
  • Studio · Sala dei Disegni II
  • Fee · From €22,000

Commemorative Monuments

Monumenta Memoriae

Triumphal arches, funerary stelae, civic columns, and inscribed cenotaphs are conceived in this department. We collaborate with letter-cutters in Carrara and bronze-founders in Vicenza; every inscription is set in Roman square capitals composed by hand, never by machine. The monument is the slowest of buildings, and we accept no commission of less than four years' patience.

  • Director · P. Falconieri
  • Studio · Sala dei Disegni III
  • Fee · From €74,000

Recent Commissions

Opera Recentiora · MMXXII — MMXXIII

The work of the atelier proceeds in the slow measure proper to building. We do not advertise; commissions reach us by recommendation of the magistrates of the cities in which we have already built. In the past two seasons our boards have been principally occupied with two works of public consequence, both undertaken at the invitation of the Soprintendenza and recorded here for the citizen who would know our hand.

Basilica Aurelia · Rebuild Study

Ravenna · MMXXII

A measured proposal for the restoration of the lost Basilica Aurelia upon its sixth-century footprint, with new apse, ambo, and a roof of cedar trusses on Corinthian colonnade. Drawings deposited with the Diocesan Archive, March MMXXIII.

Forum of Aquileia · Visitor Pavilion

Aquileia · MMXXIII

A small tetrastyle pavilion in Istrian limestone serving the western edge of the archaeological forum, completed for the patronal festival of June MMXXIII. Roof in lead-sheathed timber; floor in opus signinum after the antique.
