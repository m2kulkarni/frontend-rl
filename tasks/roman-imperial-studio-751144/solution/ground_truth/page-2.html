Liber Operum · MMXIX – MMXXIV

Selected Commissions

Eight works drawn from the books of the studio: dwellings, civic chambers, places of remembrance, and restorations of the antique. Each is recorded here with its date, its place, the materials of its making, and a brief account of the principles by which it was composed.

Catalogus Operum · MMXIX – MMXXIV
OpusI

Villa Serena

Lake Como · MMXIX

Anno MMXIX✦Como, Lombardia✦Domus Privata

A summer residence raised upon a terraced lakeshore, composed about a square peristyle of twelve unfluted Tuscan columns enclosing a shallow impluvium of Carrara marble. The triclinium opens northward through a screen of three arches toward the water; its barrel vault, twelve metres in span, is coffered in stucco and bears at its centre a gilt rosette. Domestic axes follow the Vitruvian sequence: vestibule, atrium, tablinum, peristyle, garden — each chamber proportioned in the ratio of three to five.

Travertino Romano · Carrara · Stucco Veneziano

Commission · € 1,840,000

OpusII

Curia Minor

Aquileia · MMXXI

Anno MMXXI✦Aquileia, Friuli✦Aedes Civica

A municipal council chamber for forty-eight magistrates, built upon the foundations of a mediaeval guild hall. The plan is a single rectangle, two squares long, terminated by an apsidal tribune raised three steps above the floor. Above the assembly rises a coffered vault of nine-by-five caissons, each bearing a painted oak-leaf in vermilion and Pompeian red. The Doric order, set against piers of Istrian stone, governs the elevation in five intercolumniations of equal measure.

Pietra d'Istria · Cipollino · Cassettoni dorati

Commission · € 3,120,000

OpusIII

Mausoleum Albani

Frascati · MMXXII

Anno MMXXII✦Frascati, Lazio✦Sepulchrum

A funerary chamber for the Albani family, set within a cypress grove upon the slopes of Tusculum. The exterior is a square cella of nine metres, surmounted by a hemispherical dome clad in lead and crowned by a small bronze acroterion. Within, four niches of porphyry receive the sarcophagi; the floor is a labyrinth of black-and-white marble in the manner of the Pantheon. A single oculus, two cubits in diameter, admits the noonday sun upon the central altar.

Travertino · Porfido Imperiale · Piombo

Commission · € 980,000

OpusIV

Thermae Baianae

Baia · MMXXIII

Anno MMXXIII✦Baia, Campania✦Restitutio

A study and partial restoration of the Severan thermae upon the bay of Baia, undertaken with the Soprintendenza Archeologica della Campania. The work comprised the consolidation of two hypocaust chambers, the recasting of nine columns of cipollino verde, and the reconstitution of a fresco cycle depicting the labours of Hercules in the third Pompeian style. The atelier furnished measured drawings, opus-by-opus, totalling some four hundred and twenty plates at scale 1:20.

Cipollino Verde · Affresco Pompeiano · Opus Reticulatum

Commission · € 410,000

OpusV

Bibliotheca Cornelia

Bologna · MMXX

Anno MMXX✦Bologna, Emilia✦Bibliotheca

A private library of fourteen thousand volumes, contained within a long basilical hall of five bays. The order is Ionic, of seven diameters, set upon a continuous stylobate of grey pietra serena. Above the cornice runs a frieze of carved oak-leaves, and a clerestory of nine round-arched windows admits the northern light without direct insolation. The reading-tables, of solid walnut, are arranged in transverse pairs along the central axis, after the manner of the Laurentian.

Pietra Serena · Noce Massello · Stucco a Calce

Commission · € 1,260,000

OpusVI

Fons Clitumni

Spoleto · MMXXI

Anno MMXXI✦Spoleto, Umbria✦Nymphaeum

A monumental fountain at the head of a vineyard, conceived in the manner of Pliny the Younger's letter on the springs of Clitumnus. A semicircular exedra of seven niches, each containing a herm of Carrara marble, frames a basin of polished granite five metres in diameter. Water descends in three registers, the lowest scalloped after the antique pattern. The whole is enclosed by a balustrade of vase-baluster form, repeated thirty-six times along the curve.

Carrara Statuario · Granito Sardo · Bronzo Patinato

Commission · € 540,000

OpusVII

Porta Aureliana

Tarquinia · MMXXIV

Anno MMXXIV✦Tarquinia, Lazio✦Porta Urbis

A new ceremonial gate marking the western entry to the historic precinct, raised upon the line of the late-antique walls. The composition is a triumphal arch of one large central opening flanked by two minor passages, in the proportions of the Arch of Constantine reduced by one third. Six engaged columns of the Composite order rise from a high attic, which is inscribed with the names of the consuls of the comune in tracked square capitals fifteen centimetres tall.

Travertino di Tivoli · Marmo Bianco P · Bronzo Fuso

Commission · € 2,250,000

OpusVIII

Sacellum Sanctae Agnetis

Anagni · MMXXII

Anno MMXXII✦Anagni, Lazio✦Sacellum

A small votive chapel of twenty-two square metres, set within the cloister garden of a thirteenth-century Benedictine house. The plan is a Greek cross inscribed in a square; the four arms are barrel-vaulted and meet at a low pendentive dome, twelve palms in diameter, frescoed with a starred firmament in lapis and gold. The Corinthian pilasters, of half-relief, are gilded only upon the abaci and the volutes — a discipline of restraint after the example of San Pietro in Montorio.

Marmo Bianco · Lapislazzuli · Foglia d'Oro Zecchino

Commission · € 320,000
