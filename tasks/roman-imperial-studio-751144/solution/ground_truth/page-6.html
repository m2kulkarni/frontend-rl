The Address

The atelier occupies the piano nobile of a sixteenth-century palazzetto, three minutes on foot from the Piazza Navona and the river.

Visitors arriving on foot are advised to approach by the Via dei Coronari from the Ponte Sant'Angelo; the entrance is set within a travertine portal flanked by two Tuscan pilasters, marked by a bronze plaque bearing the studio device. The portinaio receives callers between the tenth and sixteenth hours.

For matters of construction, surveying, or the inspection of works in progress, callers may address the office of the capomastro directly. The studio does not transact business by electronic mail; we hold that the considered letter, signed by hand, remains the proper instrument of an architectural correspondence.

De
Mandatis

On the Receiving of Commissions

Commissions are received in person; preliminary letters welcomed.

The studio undertakes the design of civic buildings, monuments, sacred works, private villae, and considered interiors in the Roman classical tradition. We do not solicit commissions through publication or competition; rather, the patron is invited to call upon the atelier, that the matter may be discussed across the drafting table in the manner of our predecessors.

A first interview ordinarily occupies one full hour. The patron is asked to bring such drawings, surveys, or photographic plates as may pertain to the site, and to articulate the programme in the plainest language available. No fee attaches to this preliminary conversation; the studio reserves, however, the liberty to decline a commission whose nature is held to be inconsistent with the principles of the house.
