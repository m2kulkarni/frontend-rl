Find Us in Madurai
Beneath the North Gopuram

The Sthapati Workshop occupies a restored merchant's house on East Avani Moola Street, three minutes' walk from the Meenakshi Amman temple's northern tower. The studio receives visitors by appointment, Tuesday through Saturday.

Tier II — A Note on Commissions

WHAT WE TAKE ON

The studio accepts between three and five new architectural commissions in any given calendar year, in addition to ongoing exhibition and print work. We allocate carefully: each project is led by a principal and held to the slow, drawn pace our research demands.

Preference is given to public buildings, religious foundations, and cultural-institution work — sites where the Dravidian vocabulary can serve a civic congregation rather than a private interior. We are happy to discuss residential and commercial briefs but will rarely take them.

Tier III — Practical Notes

BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

Festival Closures

The studio is closed for Pongal (14–17 January), Tamil New Year, Aadi Pooram, and the ten days of the Chithirai festival in April–May, when the surrounding streets are given over to processions.

Please confirm dates by telephone if planning a journey around these weeks.

What to Bring

If you are visiting on commission business, please bring a brief, the site address with cadastral details where available, and any heritage or planning documentation already in hand. Tea is provided; lunch can be arranged on a day's notice.

Photography & Press

Photography of working drawings and unbuilt models is restricted. Press visits are coordinated by Lakshmi Raghavan; a one-page brief sent in advance allows us to prepare the relevant material from the archive.
