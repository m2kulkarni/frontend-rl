Stone, Lime, Pigment, Plan

Selected commissions from the Workshop's eleven-year practice — civic mandapas, restored chettiar houses, libraries laid out as concentric prakara, and exhibition design that returns Nayak vocabulary to the gallery wall. Each project below records the year of completion, the seat of the work, the commissioning party, and a short note on the architectural argument.

Works are arranged in reverse chronology of completion. Two are presently on the drawing board; one closes this year.

Nine Built & Ongoing Works

PROJECT — 01

Tiruchendur Visitor Pavilion

Year
2023
Place
Tiruchendur, Tamil Nadu
Client
Tamil Nadu Tourism Development Corporation
Scale
1,180 sq.m, ₹3.4 Cr

A forty-metre granite mandapa receiving pilgrims at the seaward approach to the Murugan temple. Twelve yali-base columns carry a flat lime-and-shellac ceiling; a kolam-incised plinth marks the line between footwear and bare foot. Built with stonework from Kanchipuram and Mamallapuram ateliers.

★ Completed
PROJECT — 02

Vermilion & Gold — Exhibition Design

Year
2022
Place
Kala Bhavan, Chennai
Client
Tamil Nadu Department of Art & Culture
Scale
620 sq.m, four halls

A 14-week exhibition on Nayak-period painted ceilings. The plan unfolded as a miniature temple: outer prakara of object-cases, inner mandapa of lit panels, garbha-griha of a single restored ceiling fragment. Walls finished in unpolished red-oxide; cases trimmed in beaten brass.

★ Completed
PROJECT — 03

Chettinad House Revival

Year
2021
Place
Karaikudi, Sivaganga district
Client
Private — Meenakshi & R. Alagappan
Scale
2,400 sq.m residence

A century-old Chettiar mansion brought back from collapse: Athangudi tile floors relaid by the original family of makers, Burma-teak columns scarfed and re-pegged, and the rear thinnai opened as a reading court. The work strictly avoided new structural intervention behind the street façade.

★ Completed
PROJECT — 04

Sangam Library

Year
2019
Place
West Masi Street, Madurai
Client
Madurai District Literary Trust
Scale
1,800 sq.m on three floors

A reading house on the temple's outer street, planned in three concentric prakara: the periphery for periodicals and children, a middle ring for the Tamil canon, and a central rotunda holding palm-leaf and early printed manuscripts. A high clerestory in carved teak admits indirect north light to the manuscript core.

★ Completed
PROJECT — 05

Brindavan Temple Garden

Year
2025 (in progress)
Place
Outskirts of Mysore, Karnataka
Client
Sri Brindavan Seva Samiti
Scale
3.6 ha walled garden

A walled landscape framing a small Krishna shrine: nine quadrants laid on a tank-and-channel kolam, planted with tulasi, parijata and red hibiscus. The compound wall is built in laterite with a granite coping; gopuram-form gateway on the eastern axis is presently under stonecutting.

⟡ Site work, May 2025
PROJECT — 06

Aazhi Mandapa, Rameswaram

Year
2024
Place
Agni Theertham foreshore
Client
Hindu Religious & Charitable Endowments Dept.
Scale
320 sq.m open hall

An ablution mandapa on the eastern beach, set on a stepped granite plinth that takes the morning tide. Six pillars in black charnockite, lotus-bracketed; salt-resistant lime plaster developed with the Tamil Nadu Building Centre. Used by some 4,000 pilgrims daily during the Aadi season.

★ Completed
PROJECT — 07

Kolam — Small-Press Imprint

Year
2020
Place
Madurai & Pondicherry
Client
Cre-A: Publishers, Chennai
Scale
14-title imprint, ongoing

The Workshop's print arm: a fourteen-title series on temple craft, set in metal Tamil and Latin types and printed letterpress in Sivakasi. Bindings in marbled paper from Auroville; covers blocked in vermilion and gold-leaf to reference the Nayak ceiling palette.

★ Five titles in print
PROJECT — 08

Srivilliputhur School Wing

Year
2022
Place
Srivilliputhur, Virudhunagar
Client
Andal Memorial Higher Secondary School
Scale
14 classrooms, 1,950 sq.m

A new wing for an existing 1962 campus, set behind a colonnaded verandah on the model of the temple's outer mandapa. Classrooms are paired across a shaded central court; roofs in country-tile over teak rafters, clear-storey ventilation drawn from temple section drawings.

★ Completed
PROJECT — 09

Thanjavur Bronze Atelier

Year
2024 (closing 2025)
Place
Swamimalai road, Thanjavur
Client
Sthapati family of Sri Devasenapathy
Scale
540 sq.m foundry & museum

A working bronze foundry for a sixth-generation icon-casting family, with a small public gallery showing the lost-wax sequence step by step. The plan separates the wax-room from the casting yard with a thick laterite wall pierced by a single ornamented opening — a working version of an antarala.

⟡ Closing November 2025

To Commission a Work

The Workshop accepts a small number of new commissions each year — chiefly civic, devotional, and cultural projects in Tamil Nadu and the southern states. Initial enquiries should describe site, programme, and the patron body, and may be directed to the Madurai studio.
