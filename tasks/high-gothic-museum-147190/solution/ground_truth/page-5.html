Practical Information

Plan your visit to the priory

The cloister-wing of the former Benedictine priory of Saint-Évroult opens its doors six days a week to receive visitors among the surviving capitals, fragments, and stained-glass panels assembled there since 1894. The information below — hours, ticketing, travel by rail, parking, and accessibility — is intended to make your visit straightforward.

Reservations are required only for guided tours and groups of ten persons or more. Individual visitors may purchase tickets at the porter's lodge upon arrival. Last admission is thirty minutes before closing.

Reaching the Priory

The most direct approach is by rail. Trains from Paris-Montparnasse reach the Gare de L'Aigle in approximately one hour and forty minutes, with onward connections via the regional line to L'Aigle station, the nearest stop to the priory.

From the Gare de L'Aigle, the museum is a fifteen-minute taxi journey east along the D930 toward La Ferté-en-Ouche; a seasonal shuttle (May to October, weekends only) departs the station forecourt at 10h15 and 14h15.

By road from Paris, take the A13 to Évreux, then the N13 and D930 — approximately two hours' drive. Free public parking is available at the place de l'Église, two minutes' walk from the porter's lodge. Coach parking is signposted along rue de la Chapelle.

Access & Mobility

The medieval fabric of the priory imposes certain limits on accessibility. The principal galleries and the historic chapter house remain open to all visitors without obstacle. Where stairs cannot be avoided, attendants are present.

  • The south walk of the cloister is fully step-free and paved in flat limestone.
  • The chapter house and lapidary gallery are accessible at ground level.
  • Wheelchairs are available on loan from the porter's lodge, without charge.
  • An accessible WC is located adjacent to the western entrance.
  • The upper dorter is accessible only by a turning stair of forty-two steps.
  • The bell-tower platform is closed to visitors with reduced mobility.

Service animals are welcome throughout. Large-print guides in French and English are available at reception, as are induction loops for guided tours.

Groups & Scholars

Reservations for Group Visits

Parties of ten or more, schools, and academic seminars must reserve in advance. Please indicate the date, the size of the party, the language of the tour, and any access requirements when writing to the curatorial office.

Write to reservation@cloitres-st-evroult.fr
