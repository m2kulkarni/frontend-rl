Folio II · The Catalogue

The Collection

Fifteen works drawn from the royal kitābkhāna, the tile-yards of Naqsh-i Jahān, and the courtly textile workshops of Yazd, Kāshān, and Iṣfahān — assembled here as a tessellated catalogue, ordered by medium and date.

Holdings 1,142 objects On view 287 Period 1501 – 1736
All Media ✦ Tilework ✦ Manuscripts ✦ Metalwork ✦ Textiles ✦ Arms & Standards
i · Tilework & Architectural Revetment
Acc. 1971.04.112

Cuerda-Seca Tile Panel

Iṣfahān · c. 1612 (1021 AH)

Medium Stonepaste, polychrome glazes within manganese outline
Dimensions 124 × 198 cm, mounted in seven sections

Recovered from a courtyard wall of the Masjid-i Shāh. Cobalt grounds the saz leaves; turquoise threads bind the cloud-bands. The maker's brand survives at lower-left.

Acc. 1984.11.027

Mosaic Spandrel Fragment

Iṣfahān · c. 1602

Medium Cut tile mosaic (muʿarraq kāshī)
Dimensions 86 × 64 cm

A spandrel from a portal of the Lutfullāh complex, its arabesque set against a deep ultramarine. The fitting marks of the original masons remain incised on the verso.

Acc. 1969.01.003

Star & Cross Tile Set

Kāshān · early 17th c.

Medium Lustre over opaque white glaze, gold reflect
Dimensions Eight-pointed stars, 21 cm; crosses, 14 cm

A sequence of nine stars and eight cross-tiles from a domestic dado, each star bearing a couplet from Saʿdī's Bostān in nastaʿlīq.

ii · The Royal Kitābkhāna
Acc. 1976.22.041

Rustam Mourns Suhrāb

Tabrīz · c. 1535, kitābkhāna of Shāh Tahmāsp

Medium Opaque watercolour, ink, gold on paper
Dimensions Folio 47 × 31.8 cm; image 28 × 19 cm

A leaf from the dispersed Shāhnāma of Shāh Tahmāsp. The hero's grief unfolds beneath a tent-pole that splits the composition along its sacred axis.

Acc. 1991.18.114

Khamsa of Niẓāmī, Single Folio

Qazvīn · c. 1576, attrib. Ṣādiqī Beg

Medium Opaque watercolour and gold on burnished paper
Dimensions 32.4 × 22.1 cm

Khusraw glimpses Shīrīn at her bath — a scene rendered in the late-Qazvīn manner, with figures elongated and the rocks edged in oxblood.

Acc. 2003.07.002

Album Page, Calligraphic Quatrain

Iṣfahān · c. 1620, signed Mīr ʿImād al-Ḥasanī

Medium Black ink in nastaʿlīq, gold sprinkle, marbled border
Dimensions 24 × 14 cm

Four lines of a quatrain by Hāfiẓ, set diagonally. Mīr ʿImād's hand is at its most disciplined — the kashīda cuts like a drawn bow.

Acc. 1988.09.061

Riẓā ʿAbbāsī, Seated Youth with Cup

Iṣfahān · c. 1627

Medium Tinted drawing on paper
Dimensions 17.8 × 10.4 cm

A single figure rendered in supple line. The pleated sash, drawn with a single sweep, is the master's signature gesture, dated by his autograph at lower-right.

Acc. 1995.31.018

Qurʾān Juzʾ, Illuminated Frontispiece

Shīrāz · c. 1550

Medium Ink, opaque pigment, gold and lapis on paper, leather binding
Dimensions 31 × 21 cm; 42 folios

The opening shamsa bristles with nine concentric registers of arabesque. Endpapers are abrī — Persian marbled paper of the highest grade.

Acc. 1972.14.090

Lacquer Bookbinding

Iṣfahān · c. 1680

Medium Pasteboard, painted and varnished; doublure of cut leather
Dimensions 26.5 × 17 cm

A nightingale and rose set within a four-fold cartouche. The varnish has clarified with age; the gilt rule is original to the binding.

iii · Metalwork, Instruments & Standards
Acc. 1970.06.011

Planispheric Astrolabe

Iṣfahān · 1659/60 (1070 AH), Muḥammad Mahdī al-Yazdī

Medium Brass, engraved and pierced, silver inlay on rete
Dimensions Diameter 14.6 cm; six plates plus rete

Signed beneath the throne. Plates serve latitudes from Mecca to Samarqand; the rete carries thirty-one named stars in kūfī.

Acc. 1981.02.244

ʿAlam Standard Finial

Qazvīn · late 16th c.

Medium Pierced and chiselled steel, traces of gold koftgari
Dimensions 78 × 36 cm

Bearing the names of the Fourteen Pure Ones in thuluth. Carried in Muḥarram processions; the pierced field reads as light made legible.

Acc. 1973.05.077

Tinned Copper Bowl with Verses

Iṣfahān · c. 1645

Medium Tinned copper, engraved cartouches
Dimensions Diameter 22 cm; height 9 cm

Six cartouches carry verses by Ṣāʾib of Tabrīz. The interior bears a single sun-rosette, hammered from beneath.

Acc. 1999.13.058

Watered-Steel Shamshīr & Scabbard

Iṣfahān · early 17th c., blade signed Asadullāh

Medium Crucible steel (jowhar), gold koftgari; fish-skin scabbard with silver mounts
Dimensions Blade 78.4 cm; total length 92 cm

The watered figure runs in flowing parallels — the so-called kirk-narduban or forty-rung ladder. The cartouche cites the Nād-i ʿAlī.

iv · The Textile Workshops
Acc. 1978.20.005

Silk Lampas, Lotus & Cloud-Band

Yazd · late 16th c.

Medium Silk lampas, ground in cream, pattern in cobalt and ruby
Dimensions Loom-width 67 cm; preserved length 218 cm

The repeat advances by a half-drop of the chīnī cloud-bands; lotuses turn each on a different stem. A robe-length, never cut.

Acc. 1986.04.222

Vase-Technique Carpet, Fragment

Kirmān · c. 1620

Medium Wool pile on cotton foundation, asymmetric knot
Dimensions 2.34 × 1.18 m (preserved field)

Three offset lattices interlock — palmette, cloud, and vase. The reds derive from madder and cochineal; the blues are pure indigo.
