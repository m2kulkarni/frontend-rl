Commission Enquiries

a careful letter is the beginning of a careful panel
Enquiries may be addressed to the studio in Persian, English, or French. A useful first letter names the surface to be tiled, the orientation, the desired register of ornament — geometric, vegetal, or mixed — and the season by which the work is to be installed.

Lead Times & Scope

Two Registers of Commission

Architectural Work

minimum nine months

Spandrels, mihrab niches, dado registers, courtyard fountains and full façade programmes executed in mosaic-faïence (mu‘arraq kāshī). Site survey, full-scale cartoon, kiln tests on local clay, and on-site installation by the atelier's two journeymen are included. A signed cartoon is delivered to the patron at the close of the third month.

from 14,500 € per square metre

Movable Panels

minimum three months

Free-standing haft-rang panels, table tops, framed roundels, and devotional plaques to a maximum dimension of 90 × 120 cm. Six pigment campaigns are fired in sequence on the studio's small reduction kiln. Each piece is signed on the verso, dated by the Hijri solar calendar, and accompanied by a one-page conservation note.

from 2,800 € per panel

What a First Letter Should Carry

Brevity is welcome. Photographs are welcome. The atelier replies, in the order received, within fourteen days of a written enquiry.

  • i.the surface — wall, niche, panel, vault
  • ii.approximate dimensions in centimetres
  • iii.orientation & light at midday
  • iv.register sought: geometric / arabesque / mixed
  • v.desired completion month & year
  • vi.whether installation by the atelier is required
An Open Door

To Scholars & Restorers

The Reference Library

Scholars, conservators and graduate restorers are warmly welcomed to request access to the atelier's reference library, which presently holds 312 historical tile fragments drawn from the workshops of Isfahan, Yazd, Kashan, Ardestan and Tabriz, with the earliest sherd attributed to the late Ilkhanid period and the most recent to the Qajar revival of 1881.

The collection is consulted at a long ivory table beneath the courtyard's north iwan. A working session of half a day may be reserved by writing to the librarian; a small selection of pieces is brought up from the cabinets according to the visitor's stated theme — haft-rang palette ratios, girih joinery, glaze losses, or under-glaze pencilling.

The atelier asks only that no fragment be photographed against a coloured ground, and that the catalogue card accompany each piece back to its drawer.

— D. Farahani, written at Isfahan, the 14th of Tir
