Portfolio — II

Selected Works

fourteen years of cut tile, cobalt, and patient geometry

Fourteen years of work, ordered by site rather than by date — for I have come to believe a tile remembers the wall it was set against more readily than the year it was cut. What follows is a register of nine projects between 2014 and 2024: hammam spandrels, mihrabs, dome interiors, garden pavilions, a private library, two exterior courtyard cycles, and a memorial niche.

Each was made in the workshop on Chahar Bagh Lane, Isfahan, in either haft-rang (the seven-colour overglaze technique), moarraq kāshi (cut mosaic-faïence), or cuerda seca, and installed on site by the studio.
