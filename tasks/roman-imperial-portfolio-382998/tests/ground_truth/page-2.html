Opus Selectum · MMXIII – MMXXIV

Selected Works

Twelve commissions completed over the last decade, surveyed in the order of their tabulation in the studio's libri operum. Each project is conceived in the Roman manner — from parti to entablature — drawn by hand at the atelier in Trastevere, then translated into stone, brick, lime, and bronze with named collaborators credited below.
