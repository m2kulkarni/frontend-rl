The Studio & Its Method

The Atelier was founded in Rome in 2011 by Gaius Vedrani, principal architect, to advance a living classicism rooted in Vitruvian principle and the lessons of the Eternal City. Working in stone, brick, lime, and bronze, the practice undertakes villas, libraries, civic buildings, and ecclesial commissions for clients who value permanence over novelty.

From a studio at Via dei Coronari 142 in Rome and a second drafting room at Lafayette Street 88 in New York, a team of nine architects, draftsmen, and stonecutters develops each project from typological survey through measured drawing, model, and on-site direction of the trades.
