Inspectiones Mercatuum

Three principal markets under the daily eye of the curule aediles & their lictors

Macellum Magnum

The Great Provision Market

Standing east of the Forum on the Decumanus Maximus, the Macellum houses one hundred and twelve stalls of grain, oil, garum, smoked meat, and Campanian cheese. Inspectors of the aediles attend at the third hour of every day excepting the Kalends.

Forum Piscarium

Fish Stalls at the Tiber Mouth

Beneath the warehouses of the Horrea Epagathiana, the fish-mongers' tables stretch one hundred paces along the river quay. Catch from the Tyrrhenian and the lagoons of Portus is sold from dawn until the sixth hour, after which all unsold flesh is removed under penalty of confiscation.

Macellum Lanarium

The Wool Market on the Decumanus

Held in the porticus opposite the Baths of Mithras, the wool-mongers gather thrice in the month: on the Nones, the Ides, and the day before the Kalends. Fleeces from Tarentum, Pollentia, and the Apennine flocks are weighed by the public statera before sale is permitted.

Edictum · Affixum in Foro

De Mensura Falsa

Penalties for Fraudulent Weight or Measure
  1. Mulcta Pecuniaria Upon a first conviction before the aediles, the offender shall pay a fine of one hundred sesterces (HS C) into the treasury of the temple of Ceres. Sums are receipted by the quaestor mercatus and posted in the public ledger before sundown.
  2. Confiscatio Mercium The whole stock present at the offending stall — grain, oil, fish, wool, or wine — shall be confiscated by the lictors of the magistracy and either distributed to the corn-dole or destroyed, according to its nature.
  3. Affixio Publica The name of the offender, his trade, and the nature of his fraud shall be inscribed upon a wooden tabula and affixed for thirty days at the rostra of the Forum, that all citizens and visitors of Ostia may know him.
  4. Iteratum Crimen Should the offence be repeated within one year, the fine is doubled to two hundred sesterces, the licence to trade revoked for the remainder of the magistrates' term, and the offender excluded from all three markets named above.
Sealed by the order of the Curule Aediles · L. Vibius Sabinus & Q. Calpurnius Verus
