Senatus Populusque Ostiensis

Officium Aedilium
Coloniae Ostiensis

Convened in the Forum of the Corporations beside the Tiber's mouth, this Office attends the daily welfare of the colony — its grain, its waters, its baths, its stones, its measures, and its peace. Citizens, freedmen, and incolae are heard here in the morning hours, sub colonnade.

Anno Conss. L. Veri et Q. Servilii Pudentis · A.U.C. DCCCCXIX

Forum Vinarium

De vino, oleo, et mensuris

Oversight of the wine-market behind the Decumanus is restored to this office by decree of the duoviri. The standard amphora is fixed at XXVI sextarii, and all measures are sealed each kalends with the lead stamp of the colony.

Three assayers are appointed to test the new vintage from Setia and Falernum, and adulterators are fined no less than CCL sesterces per cask, the sum entered in the public ledger.

Thermae Neptuni

Inspectio balnearum publicarum

Quarterly inspection of the Baths of Neptune is now in progress. The hypocaust beneath the tepidarium is to be relined in the second half of Maius, and the mosaic of the sea-god is being washed with vinegar and lye by the conductor balnei, T. Flavius Hilarus.

Entrance is reduced for the duration to one quadrans for citizens, free for veterans of the classis Misenensis, and barred to dogs after the sixth hour.

Aqua Traiana

Cura aquarum et castelli

Maintenance of the Ostian branch of the Aqua Traiana, drawn from the Lacus Sabatinus, proceeds at the castellum aquae beside the Porta Romana. Six fistulae of fixed bore feed the public fountains; private taps in excess of one quinaria are forfeit.

The aquarii of the familia publica are stationed at the watch-tower from the third hour of night, and any breach of the specus is to be reported within one watch under penalty of HS M.

Edictum Aedilicium · Standing Notice

De Frumento Et Pondere

— promulgated by M. Caecilius Rufinus, Aedilis Maior —

Whereas certain dealers in the Macellum near the Capitolium have been found employing modii of short measure, and whereas the price of bread has risen above two asses for the loaf of one libra, the senior aedile, by authority granted in the lex coloniae, decrees as follows.

All grain-merchants, pistores, and bakers shall present their measures at the Office of the Aediles before the next nundinae for re-stamping in lead. The standard modius castrensis is fixed at sixteen sextarii, and the libra at twelve unciae as marked on the bronze table of the forum.

Any merchant convicted of false weight shall pay a fine of HS DC to the public chest, and his stall shall be closed for the duration of one nundinum. A second offence brings loss of licence and posting of the name upon the album in the basilica.

This notice shall remain affixed beneath the portico of the curia until the kalends of Iunius, and shall be read aloud by the praeco at each market-day.
