第五 — Visiting Notes

Visit.
Hours, tickets,
and the lane in.

The museum keeps the unhurried pace of a Yanaka backstreet. Allow ninety minutes to wander the machiya, the print room, and the small kura beyond the courtyard. Tea is poured in the eaves at three.

Hours

The galleries are open five days a week, with a single afternoon closing. Last entry is half an hour before closing so that visitors may take the full circuit through the reconstructed townhouse without being hurried.

Quiet Notes

Photography is permitted in the courtyard but not in the print room, where light levels are kept at fifty lux. Footwear is removed at the genkan; slippers are provided.

The reconstructed second floor is reached by a steep wooden stair with a single rope rail. A ground-floor circuit is available for visitors who prefer level passage.

An umbrella stand and small lockers are at the entrance. Strollers may be left in the genkan.

The lane in.

From Yanaka Ginza, walk east along the slope, past the cooper and the persimmon tree, then take the second narrow turning. The museum sits behind a low cedar fence, marked by a single paper lantern.

Accessibility

› A ramp at the rear gate gives level access to the courtyard and the ground-floor galleries. Please write ahead so that the cedar gate is opened.

School Visits

› Groups of fifteen or more receive guided tours by request. Reservations open three months in advance; correspond by post or telephone.

Tea on the Eaves

› Sencha is served at three o'clock in the engawa, weather permitting, at no charge to ticket-holders. A small confection is included.
