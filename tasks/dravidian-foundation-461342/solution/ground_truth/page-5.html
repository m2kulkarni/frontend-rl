II · The Named-Endowment Ladder

Perpetual Chairs & Funds

A named endowment becomes a permanent line in the Trust's books, drawing only from interest. The principal is held in three-year cumulative deposits at Indian Bank under the supervision of the Investment Sub-Committee. Each chair is dedicated by formal Sankalpa at Thiruvalanjuli on Aippasi Pournami.

The first named chair was settled in 1971 by Smt. Padmavati Iyer in memory of her grandfather, the temple-architect Sri. Subbaraya Stapathi of Kumbakonam. It still funds a single mason at the Thiru Nageswaram mandapa today.

A chair is not a sum of money. It is a vow that the chisel, in this discipline, will never fall silent for want of patronage.

— from the 1962 Trust Deed, clause IX

Endowments may be settled in life or by codicil. The Trust's solicitors, M/s. Iyer & Krishnaswami of Mylapore, draft the deed of settlement at no cost to the donor. Foreign-currency endowments are received only after FCRA clearance is on record.

Overseas donors

FCRA Status

The Trust holds standing registration under the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010, and may receive contributions from foreign sources only into the designated FCRA account at the State Bank of India, New Delhi Main Branch.

Overseas donors are requested to write to the Treasurer prior to remittance, so that the FCRA-compliant routing memo can be sent under separate cover.

FCRA Registration Number 075910442 Validity renewed 14 March 2024
Tax Eligibility

80G & 35AC

Domestic donors are entitled to deduction under Section 80G of the Income-tax Act, 1961, at the rate of fifty per cent of the qualifying sum, the Trust's certificate having been renewed by the CIT (Exemptions), Chennai on 02 August 2023, valid through assessment year 2028–29.

Corporate donors funding capital conservation works are further eligible for the residuary benefits previously available under Section 35AC, retained by special notification for protected-monument projects.
