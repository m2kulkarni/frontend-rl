Small sums · Long horizons · Local guild leadership
Principle the First

Modest in Sum

A grant from the Trust is rarely large. We disburse in increments of two to fifty lakhs, paid in tranches against milestones the silpi himself sets. We have learned, over forty-six years, that conservation suffers more from sudden plenty than from steady patience.

Principle the Second

Long in Horizon

No programme is funded for less than three years. The lime-plaster ceilings of Lepakshi, the kalasha of Tiruvannamalai, the palm-leaf treatises of the Saraswathi Mahal — none can be hurried. Our trustees commit, where conditions warrant, for windows of seven, ten, even twelve years.

Principle the Third

Local in Hand

Every programme is led by a stapathi, silpi, or pundit drawn from the locality and its hereditary guild. The Trust does not import expertise; it pays for the expertise already present, and it pays well. Our role is the cheque, the auditor, and the silence in which work proceeds.

The Nine Active Programmes · Year M.M.XXIV

Field Programmes & Disbursements

Each programme below is governed by a written compact between the Trust and the principal silpi or conservator. Sums shown reflect disbursement to date within the current funding window; arrears, where they exist, are settled at the close of the work-season.

I Re-Gilding · Architectural

The Kalasha at Tiruvannamalai

Site
Sri Arunachaleswarar, Rajagopuram apex
Principal
Sri V. Muthukumaraswamy Stapathi
Window
2023 — 2025

Removal, re-leafing, and remounting of the eleven copper finials of the eastern gopuram, with consecration to follow the Krittika asterism of December 2025. The principal supervises a guild of nine, four of them under apprenticeship.

₹46.2L Disbursed
II Lime-Plaster · Mural Stabilisation

The Lepakshi Ceiling Fragments

Site
Veerabhadra Temple, Anantapur
Principal
Smt. Padmaja Rangaswamy, Conservator
Window
2022 — 2027

Slow-curing lime stabilisation of seventeen detached panels of the great Vijayanagara ceiling. Pigment consolidation by hide-glue tissue, executed at micro-climatic dew-points only. No synthetic resin enters the precinct.

₹62.8L Disbursed
III Manuscript · Cataloguing

The Saraswathi Mahal Palm-Leaf Treatises

Site
Saraswathi Mahal Library, Thanjavur
Principal
Dr. K. Subramanian, Pundit-Curator
Window
2021 — 2030

Cataloguing, citraka-oil treatment, and digital surrogacy of 11,400 palm-leaf bundles of the Maratha collection — Ayurveda, Natyashastra, and Sanskrit grammar. Three pundits and six junior scribes are in residence.

₹84.5L Disbursed
IV Stonework · Bronze Recasting

Chola Bronzes — Swamimalai Guild

Site
Periya Stapathi Vidu, Swamimalai
Principal
Sri R. Devasenapathy Stapathi (V gen.)
Window
2024 — 2031

Lost-wax recasting in the panchaloha tradition of fourteen icons commissioned by village trusts whose originals were lost or stolen between 1962 and 1981. Each work is iconographically certified by the Sthapati Sangam.

₹38.0L Disbursed
V Granite · Reset & Re-Cut

The Brihadeeswara Plinth Survey

Site
Brihadeeswara, Gangaikonda Cholapuram
Principal
Sri A. Selvanathan, Senior Stapathi
Window
2023 — 2026

Photogrammetric survey, displacement-mapping, and selective re-setting of forty-three displaced ashlars on the southern adhishthana. The Trust co-funds with the Tamil Nadu HR&CE; no quarry-stone is introduced.

₹29.6L Disbursed
VI Wood-Carving · Ratha

The Tiruchendur Ratha Re-Build

Site
Subrahmanya Swamy Temple, Tuticorin
Principal
Sri T. Murugaiyan Asari & Sons
Window
2022 — 2026

Reconstruction of the great wooden temple-car after the marine-borer infestation of 2019, in seasoned vagai, teak, and jackwood. Forty-eight figural panels carved to the patterns of the elder Asari's grandfather.

₹52.4L Disbursed
VII Mural · Pigment Recovery

The Mattancherry Palace Murals

Site
Mattancherry Palace, Kochi
Principal
Smt. Lalitha Pillai, Pigment Conservator
Window
2024 — 2028

Recovery of the Ramayana cycle in the bedchamber wing — saturation testing, salt-efflorescence reversal, and re-binding of flaking ochres. The Trust contributes alongside the Archaeological Survey of India.

₹19.7L Disbursed
VIII Tank · Hydraulic Restoration

The Madurai Theppakulam Surveys

Site
Vandiyur Mariamman Theppakulam
Principal
Er. P. Chidambaram, Hydraulic Engineer
Window
2023 — 2027

Restoration of the inflow channel from the Vaigai, with re-lining of the tank's rubble pavement and consecratory dredging. The mandapa at the islet's centre receives parallel stonework under sub-grant.

₹41.0L Disbursed
IX Guild Stipend · Living Tradition

The Kaveri Delta Silpi Stipends

Site
Kumbakonam, Mayiladuthurai, Thiruvarur
Principal
The Trust's Standing Sthapati Council
Window
2019 — Open-Ended

A monthly retainer of ₹18,000 paid to thirty-two senior craftsmen — stone, bronze, and wood — without project condition, in recognition that the slack year is precisely when a guild requires support, not the busy one.
