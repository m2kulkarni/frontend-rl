光 琳 木 工 Kōrin · Mokkō

Joiners, paper-makers, and lacquer-finishers, working by hand for the houses and temples of Kyōto.

We make shoji, ranma, paper lanterns, and small chests of kiri and zelkova, fitted to the rooms they will occupy and to the seasons that will pass through them.

Restoration of machiya joinery is undertaken upon written commission. New work is accepted from the third month of the year.

光琳
壱 The House
Founded Bunsei 4 (1821) in the Nishijin ward of Kyōto, two blocks south of the Imamiya shrine. Six artisans, one apprentice, one cat. The forge for our chisels is shared with the smith Watanabe of Murasakino; our paper is drawn from the Kurotani vats by hand each winter.
