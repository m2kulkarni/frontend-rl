Visit ・ 案内

To call upon the workshop, write ahead — and walk the six minutes from Imadegawa.

The atelier is a converted machiya on a quiet lane in Kamigyō, between the Horikawa and the Kamo rivers. We keep regular hours Tuesday through Saturday, but receive visitors only by prior arrangement so that no plane is set down mid-stroke. A letter, a postcard, or a telephone call — any of these will reach us.

Finding the door · 道順

Six minutes west, then a quiet turn into Daikoku-chō.

The simplest approach is to leave Imadegawa subway station by Exit 6, cross Karasuma-dōri, and walk west along Imadegawa-dōri keeping the Nishijin quarter on your right. The atelier is two streets south of the textile museum, behind a noren of unbleached hemp.

  1. Exit 6 of Imadegawa Station; walk west on Imadegawa-dōri for three minutes.
  2. At the second small crossing past the bus stop, turn south.
  3. Pass the Nishijin Textile Museum on your right, continue 80 metres.
  4. Look for a kuro-shikkui wall & an indigo noren — the workshop is the second machiya.

Writing Ahead

A request before the visit — 御来訪に先立ちて

So that the work may be set down with care, we ask all visitors to send a brief note one or two weeks in advance. Mention the date you would prefer, the number in your party, and — if you are coming with a commission in mind — a few lines on the room or the object you imagine.

Replies are written by hand and posted within four working days.
