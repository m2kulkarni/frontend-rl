Public Inspection Notice · Water & Sanitation Division

Annual Cistern Inspection Schedule for the Maydān & Bāzār Precincts

The Bureau hereby publishes the inspection rota for the eleven Safavid-era subterranean cisterns (āb-anbār) within the protected perimeter. Inspections shall commence at first light on 9 Tir and conclude at the closing of the bāzār on 18 Tir 1403. Affected merchants and residents along Sepah, Hātef, and Ostāndāri thoroughfares are advised that potable supply from the Madreseh-Chāhārbāgh standpipe will be intermittent on the dates listed below.

Inspectors from the Bureau, accompanied by a delegate of the Provincial Office of Public Health, shall verify the integrity of the qanāt feeders, the lime-plaster lining, and the seasonal silt deposit. Findings will be entered into the Register on 22 Tir.

Public Tender · Atelier of Tiles & Glazes

Open Call: Cobalt Tile Firing for the South Īwān of Masjid-i Sheikh Lotfollāh

The Bureau invites sealed proposals from licensed kiln-masters of the Province for the firing of two thousand four hundred (2,400) hexagonal cuerda-seca tiles, dimension 21 × 21 cm, employing the cobalt-and-turquoise palette specified in the 1387 SH conservation memorandum. Tiles shall be fired to a single bisque at 980 °C and a glaze firing not exceeding 1,040 °C, in accordance with the historical recipe attested in the workshop ledgers of Master Reza Esfahāni (1031 AH).

Submission packets must include a fired sample of one tile, a kiln-curve diagram, and a sworn declaration of provenance for all metallic oxides. Estimated ceiling: 8,400,000,000 IRR. The award shall be announced before the Council on 28 Tir.

Permit Disposition · Façade Restoration

Granted: Restoration of the Plaster Reliefs at Khāneh-yi Sheikh-ol-Eslām (Permit 1403-FR-118)

Following review by the Sub-Committee on Domestic Architecture, the Bureau has granted, with conditions, the application of the Sheikh-ol-Eslām family trust for the restoration of the seven stucco panels of the andarūni courtyard. Works shall employ traditional gypsum-and-straw render only; no Portland-cement intrusions are permitted. A Bureau monitor shall attend the site each Sunday and Wednesday between 9 Mordād and 30 Shahrivar 1403.

Objections, if any, may be lodged with the Office of the Steward within fourteen (14) days of the present notice. Failure to commence works by 1 Mehr 1403 shall void the present permit.

Abridged Minutes · Bureau Council

Session of the Twenty-Second of Ordibehesht

Twelfth Ordinary Sitting · 22 Ordibehesht 1403 / 11 May 2024 · Convened 16:00, Adjourned 19:42

The Council assembled in the Chamber of the Forty Columns (Tālār-i Chehel-Sotūn), the Chair being held by the Honourable Steward-General Dr. Roxānā Mīrzā-Hosseini. A quorum of nine of the eleven seated members was confirmed by the Recording Clerk, Mr. Kāmrān Sa'idi. The agenda, as circulated on 18 Ordibehesht under file 1403-AG-12, was approved without amendment.

The principal matter of the sitting concerned the proposal, tabled by the Office of Urban Mobility, for the partial pedestrianization of Sepah Street between the western gate of Naqsh-i Jahān and the intersection with Tohid Boulevard, a stretch of approximately 1,180 metres. The proposal calls for the closure of the carriageway to private motor vehicles between the hours of 07:00 and 22:00 daily, with continued passage permitted to emergency services, sanitation vehicles, and the bāzār freight permit-holders.

Counsel for the bāzāri guilds, Mr. Hādi Tabrizi, addressed the Council for thirty-two minutes, advancing the position that any restriction of vehicular access shall be undertaken only after a binding consultation with the seventeen guilds whose warehouses lie along the affected stretch. He further noted that the freight rota of 1397 SH, presently in force, was negotiated under markedly different traffic conditions and would require renegotiation.

The Steward of Public Works, Eng. Farāmarz Soltāni, presented findings from the pilot closure conducted on the Fridays of Esfand 1402, recording an average reduction of 64% in particulate matter at the Maydān gauge and a 41% increase in foot traffic to the bāzār entrances during the trial. Three of the eight surveyed shopkeepers reported a net increase in custom; two reported decline; three reported no measurable change.

Concerns regarding the cooling of the south flank of Masjid-i Shāh, raised by the Steward of Monuments, Bānū Mo'tamedi, were taken under advisement; she noted that the loss of vehicular shading from delivery awnings could elevate stone-surface temperatures by an estimated 3 — 4 °C in summer afternoons, with attendant implications for the long-term preservation of the lower revetment courses.

Resolutions Carried

  1. That a binding consultation with the seventeen affected guilds shall be opened by the Office of the Steward not later than 15 Khordād 1403, the consultation to remain open for forty-five days.
  2. That the Office of Urban Mobility shall prepare, by 1 Shahrivar 1403, a revised freight rota proposal accommodating the early-morning provisioning windows of the bāzāri.
  3. That the Bureau shall commission, at a cost not exceeding IRR 1,200,000,000, an independent thermographic survey of the south flank of Masjid-i Shāh during the Tir-Mordād interval.
  4. That the matter of pedestrianization shall be re-tabled at the Ordinary Sitting of 4 Ābān 1403, at which time a binding vote shall be taken.
  5. That the present minutes shall be entered into the Public Register and posted upon the door of the Bureau within three days of approval.
