Visit the Bureau

The doors of the Bureau open onto a small courtyard set back from the boulevard, shaded by two old plane trees and signposted in cobalt enamel. All citizens, scholars, and craft-trade representatives are welcome to call during public hours, or to write ahead in Persian or English.

The Bureau Resides At No. 12 Ostandari Street
Isfahan, 81746-73441 · Islamic Republic of Iran
32°39′24″ N · 51°40′47″ E · Plot Reference IS-OST-012-A
I

Reception & Records

Ground Floor · East Iwan

Walk-in queries, document lodgement, and copies of public registers are handled at the Reception Desk in the eastern iwan, immediately past the courtyard ramp. No appointment is needed for short enquiries.

II

Permits Counter

Upper Hall · Northern Wing

Restoration permits, façade interventions, and craftspersons' registration are managed in the Upper Hall. Submitted dossiers are read into the Council ledger on the same working day before 13:00.

III

Reading Room

Courtyard Pavilion · By Card

The Reading Room holds 1,840 catalogued plans, surveys, and tilework studies dating from 1308 SH onward. Researchers should request a one-day card from Reception, presenting one form of photographic identification.

Article II

Public Hours & Closures

The public counters are open from Saturday through Wednesday, between 08:30 and 14:00 local time, with the records desk closing fifteen minutes earlier to allow ledgers to be sealed and returned to the safe. Thursday mornings are reserved for internal Council deliberations and are not open to the public; on these mornings, written submissions left in the brass slot beside the gatehouse will be received and date-stamped at 09:00.

The Bureau observes all national holidays of the Islamic Republic of Iran, and additionally closes for the Bureau's own founding observance on 7 Mehr each year. Visitors travelling from outside the province are encouraged to telephone Reception in advance to confirm that no unscheduled closure is in force.

The Reading Room operates on a narrower window, 09:00–13:00, Saturday through Tuesday; Wednesday afternoons are devoted to reshelving and the conservator's weekly humidity inspection.

Article III

Accessibility & the Courtyard

A stone-and-bronze ramp, installed in 2022 to replace the original three-step threshold, runs the full eastern flank of the courtyard at a 1:14 gradient. The ramp is laid in dressed sandstone with a cobalt enamel guide-strip and is wide enough for two abreast.

  • Step-free access from Ostandari Street through the gatehouse to all ground-floor counters.
  • One accessible W.C. adjacent to Reception, signed in Persian, English, and pictogram.
  • The Upper Hall is reached by a small lift (capacity 320 kg); on lift outage, an officer will receive papers downstairs.
  • Two reserved parking bays on Kucheh Bagh-e Vali, fifty metres west of the gatehouse.
  • Assistance dogs are welcomed; please notify the gatekeeper on arrival.
Article IV

Transit from Imam Hossein Square

The Bureau is most easily reached by the Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) line which terminates a short walk from the gatehouse. The journey from Imam Hossein Square takes approximately eleven minutes outside peak hours.

  1. Board BRT Line 1 (Naghshe-Jahan corridor) at Imam Hossein Square, southbound platform, towards Soffeh.
  2. Travel four stops to Ostandari station; the carriage announces stops in Persian and English.
  3. Alight on the western side and walk 220 metres along Ostandari Street, keeping the iron railings to your left.
  4. The Bureau gatehouse stands beneath two plane trees, opposite the Public Works Annex.
  5. Single fare 15,000 rials; concession card holders board free before 10:00.

A Note to Correspondents

The Bureau receives written correspondence in either Persian or English, and considers both equally official under Article 11 of the founding ordinance. Letters may be addressed to the Records Desk and should bear, where possible, the parcel number or street segment in question. Replies are issued within fourteen working days; matters touching on permits in active review may be acknowledged the same week.

— Bureau of Heritage Stewardship, Ostandari Quarter
