From the gateway of the Qaysariyya to the river-walks of Si-o-se-pol, the Civic Bureau coordinates restoration, public works, and the orderly conduct of council across the historic precincts of Isfahan.

Public Services of the Bureau

I

Heritage Permits

Issued at the South Iwan · Sun–Wed

Applications for facade restoration, plasterwork, and the repair of muqarnas vaulting within the Naqsh-e Jahan precinct are received in person at Window 4. Standard review concludes within 21 working days; petitions concerning load-bearing brick or qanāt access require additional inspection by the Bureau's masonry warden.

Schedule of fees: 480,000 IRR for residential facades; 1,250,000 IRR for commercial frontage on Chahar Bagh.

II

Public Works Notices

Posted Thursdays · 06:00 Local

The Bureau publishes weekly notices of street closures, drainage works, and bazaar repairs. From 7 to 19 Tir, the eastern arcade of the Qaysariyya will undergo cleaning of its tilework; pedestrian flow is redirected through the Sheikh Lotfollah court. Vehicular access along Ostandari Avenue is restricted between 22:00 and 05:00 for tram-rail levelling.

Citizens may register objections within ten days of posting at the Bureau's reception.

III

Council Sessions

Convened Mondays · 09:30

The Council of Forty meets in the upper hall of the Bureau under the chairmanship of Chief Steward Mahsa Daryāyi. The 14 Khordād session will hear petitions on the lighting of the Khaju Bridge, a proposal to register seven coffeehouses of the Jolfa quarter, and the budget for the autumn restoration of the Ali Qapu mural cycle.

Sessions are open to citizens of the precinct; minutes are published within seven days.

Ordinance 47 / 1402 · Sealed in Council

On the Restoration of Facades along Chahar Bāgh Avenue

Be it known to all proprietors, guildsmen, and residents along the avenue of Chahar Bāgh that, by unanimous resolve of the Council of Forty, the rendering of new facades shall conform to the surveyed datum of 1308 SH; that no aluminium fenestration shall be permitted between Hasht Behesht and the southern gate of the Madar-i Shah madrasa; and that the Bureau shall extend, until 30 Mehr 1403, a restoration grant of up to 65% of approved works for residences of registered character.

The complete instrument, with schedules of brick, plaster, and tile, is held in the Bureau's registry under file Ch.B./1402/47 and may be consulted on any working day between 08:30 and 14:00.

Signed
Mahsa Daryāyi
Chief Steward
