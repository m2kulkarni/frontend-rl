Issue XXVII · Summer–Autumn 1403

The Bureau opens its courtyard, council chamber, and partnered monuments to the public throughout the warm months, in keeping with the long Isfahani custom of maydān-gardī — the leisurely civic walk through public space. Each entry below has been published with the consent of the Office of the Mayor, the Heritage Council, and the relevant guild masters. Capacities are firm. Walk-ins are welcomed at all open council sessions; ticketed lectures and workshops require advance enrolment by post or in person at the Receiving Hall, Window 3.

On Enrolment & Civic Conduct

شرایط ثبت‌نام و آدابِ حضور

Enrolment for ticketed engagements opens at the Receiving Hall (Window 3) ten days prior to each entry, between 08:30 and 13:00 on civic working days. A nominal courtyard contribution of 25,000 tomans is requested for craft demonstrations and lectures; this sum is waived for pupils, retired civil servants, and registered residents of the historic precinct upon presentation of identification. Council sessions are without charge. Citizens are asked to keep silence during the procession of the Council and to refrain from photography within the lecture hall while a master is speaking. Children under seven must be accompanied at all times. Modest dress is requested at all monument tours; head-covering is provided at the gate of the Jāmeʿ Mosque.
