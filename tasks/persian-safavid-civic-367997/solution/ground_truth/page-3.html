Bureau Service · Section III

Heritage Permits &
Restoration

Authority and procedure for facade, tile, and stucco works within the protected precincts of Isfahan — from the spandrels of the Imām Square to the lime-rendered lanes of Jolfā and Hakim. All works affecting visible historic fabric require a precinct permit issued by the Bureau before commencement.

Ordinance 1402/14 ✦ Effective 21 Farvardīn 1403 ✦ Reviewed Quarterly
Schedule of Works

The Five Permit Classes

Every intervention upon historic fabric is classified by visible scope and material sensitivity. Applicants must select the lowest class consistent with the proposed work; the Bureau may reclassify upward following site inspection.

I

Surface Cleaning & Repointing

Class A

Routine wash-down of stone and brick courses, lime repointing of mortar joints up to 4 m² per facade, and removal of biological growth using approved poultices. No scaffold above 6 m.

Fee · 4,200,000 rials
II

Lime-Plaster & Stucco

Class B

Refinishing of lime-render walls, replacement of decayed gypsum stucco, and consolidation of muqarnas plaster up to 12 m². Requires accredited mu'ammar (master plasterer) on record.

Fee · 9,800,000 rials
III

Tile Re-laying & Mosaic

Class C

Removal, documentation, and reinstatement of haft-rang tile work or mu'arraq mosaic. Each replacement tile must be matched to the Bureau archive of glaze samples (ref. Cabinet IV-B) before firing.

Fee · 18,500,000 rials
IV

Structural & Vault Work

Class D

Stabilisation of arches, vaults, and load-bearing piers; replacement of timber tie-beams; underpinning of foundations within the protected precinct. Engineer's seal and seismic dossier required.

Fee · 42,000,000 rials
V

Adaptive Reuse & Major Works

Class E

Conversion of historic structures to new civic, cultural, or hospitality use; new openings in historic walls; reconstruction of lost elements from documentary evidence. Council resolution required.

Fee · 78,000,000 rials
Submission & Review

Required Documentation

For All Classes

Every application is submitted to the Office of the Precinct Inspector at the Bureau's ground floor (Counter 3) between 08:30 and 12:30, Saturday through Wednesday. Digital submissions are accepted through the registered cadastral portal but must be followed within seven days by a physical dossier bearing the owner's wet seal and the residential block number.

  1. Title deed or notarised tenancy authorising the works, copied twice.
  2. Cadastral extract (block, parcel, and precinct designation) issued no earlier than 90 days before submission.
  3. Photographic survey: minimum twelve plates documenting all elevations affected, including raking-light images of any decorated surface.
  4. Measured drawings at 1:50, with a separate 1:10 sheet for any tile, stucco, or carved-stone field exceeding one square metre.
  5. Method statement specifying mortars, glazes, and binders by source and provenance — Yazd lime and Mey­baud cobalt frit are presumed acceptable; all others require sample approval.
  6. For Classes C through E, a conservator's report signed by a Bureau-accredited specialist (the current roster is posted in the vestibule and issued every Nowrūz).

The Twenty-One Day Review Window

Upon receipt of a complete dossier, the Inspector issues a stamped receipt bearing the file number and the calendar date on which the review window opens. Within twenty-one days the Bureau shall: conduct a site visit; circulate the dossier to the relevant sub-committee (Tile, Plaster, or Structure); and return a written determination — approval, conditional approval with remediation, or refusal with grounds.

Silence at the close of the twenty-first day constitutes provisional approval for Classes A and B only, and only where the precinct is not within the immediate buffer of the Imām Square or the Chehel Sotūn gardens. For all other works, no commencement is lawful before written determination.

On-Site Conduct

Approved works must display the Bureau's blue placard at street level for the duration of the works. The placard bears the file number, the permit class, the name of the responsible mu'ammar or engineer, and the closing date of the authorisation. Works extending beyond the authorised window require a renewal application, lodged at least fourteen days before expiry.

The Bureau's inspectors may enter any approved site between 07:00 and 17:00 without prior notice. Concealment of works behind unrequested hoarding, or deviation from the approved method statement, is grounds for a stop-notice and forfeiture of the bond paid at submission.

The Review Pathway

1

Lodge Dossier

Counter 3, Bureau ground floor. Stamp issued on receipt of complete file.

2

Site Inspection

Within seven days. Inspector photographs all affected fabric and verifies measured drawings.

3

Sub-Committee

Dossier circulated to Tile, Plaster, or Structure committee. Convened weekly on Mondays.

4

Determination

Written ruling on or before day twenty-one. Approval bears the gold-foil seal of the Council.
