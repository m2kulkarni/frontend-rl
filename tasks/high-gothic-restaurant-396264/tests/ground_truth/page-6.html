The Supper, in Brief

92 € per guest
— wine pairing 56 €, by the chef's hand —

Maison Saint-Vrain serves a single fixed-price supper of nine courses, drawn from the long table-traditions of medieval Picardy and reset each fortnight by chef Aurélien Brescot. The room seats sixteen guests, and only sixteen; service begins at twenty hours and concludes well after midnight.

Reservations are taken by telephone or written request only. We keep no online form, no booking ledger but our own.

Of Dietary Concerns

The kitchen will gladly accommodate allergies, vegetarian guests, and observances of religious or medical custom — provided we are warned at least three full days before the supper. Our nine-course sequence is composed each fortnight from what the market and the season offer; substitutions made under pressure are seldom worthy of the table.

For coeliac and severe allergies, please confirm by telephone. A written note alone is not sufficient.
