— V — Visit & Quarter —

Hours, Address,
& the Quarter

A long supper kept slowly, behind a low door on the rue des Drapiers — eight minutes south-east of the cathedral, four centuries from the boulevard.

First Column

The Calendar of the Table

Opening Nights

The dining room admits twenty-two guests at a single seating. Doors open at 19h00; supper is served at 19h30 sharp, and runs until midnight or thereabouts.

  • Sun — WedClosed — kitchen at rest
  • Thursday19h30 — 23h30
  • Friday19h30 — 24h00
  • Saturday19h30 — 24h00

Closure Dates

  • August Recess From Saturday, the 2nd of August through Tuesday, the 2nd of September. Chef Brescot returns to the orchards of the Somme; the cellar is rebuilt in his absence.
  • Christmas Week From the 22nd of December through the 2nd of January. The hearth is doused on the eve of the Nativity and re-lit at Epiphany.
  • Toussaint Closed Friday the 31st of October and Saturday the 1st of November.
A word on the cobbles The rue des Drapiers is closed to vehicles after 18h00. Taxis and private cars must set down at the corner of the rue des Trois-Cailloux; the remaining sixty paces are on foot, over cobbles. Stout shoes are kindly suggested when the weather turns.

Second Column

Approaching the House

From the Cathédrale Notre-Dame

I
Quit the West Front With the great rose window at your back, take the rue Robert de Luzarches eastward, past the chapter-house wall.
II
Cross the Place Saint-Michel Bear south. The square narrows into the rue des Trois-Cailloux; keep to the shaded side.
III
Turn at the iron lantern A black wrought lantern marks the mouth of the rue des Drapiers. Twenty paces in, on the left, beneath a carved oak lintel — Maison Saint-Vrain.

Eight minutes on foot, at an unhurried pace.

Taxi Rank

The nearest stand is on place Gambetta, a 200-metre walk from our door. Taxis Bleus d'Amiens may be summoned at 03 22 91 30 03, and arrive within ten minutes most evenings. After supper, our maître d'hôtel will gladly telephone on your behalf.

Before Supper — The Quarter

The cloth-merchants' quarter has, against the run of the centuries, kept its trade. Within three streets of the house you will find Atelier Cousu-Main at no. 14 rue des Drapiers (hand-stitched linen and shrouds for table) open until 18h30; the bookbinder Périlleux et Fils on the rue de la Hotoie, who works in calf and gold-leaf as his great-grandfather did; and the chandler Cire d'Abeille, whose tapers light our own tables. The wine merchant Caves Madeleine, on the corner of the place du Don, opens his vaulted cellar to visitors who knock politely before 19h00.
