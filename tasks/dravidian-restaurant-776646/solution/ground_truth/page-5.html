The Kalasha Calendar · MMXXIV–MMXXV

Feast Days & Seasons

Four times a year the temple kitchen at Kalasha bends to the planets. Harvest sun, river flood, nine nights of the goddess, and the long lamps of Karthigai — each calls for a different rice, a different kuzhambu, a different way of laying the leaf. Bookings open six weeks in advance.

The Four Feast Days

The Harvest Lunch · Thai Month

Pongal Thali ₹950

Saturday 11 January & Wednesday 15 January 2025 · seatings 12:30 and 2:30 PM

  • Sakkarai pongal cooked at the table over a clay stove, jaggery from Erode
  • Ven pongal with whole black pepper, cashew, twelve curry leaves
  • Seven-vegetable kootu, raw turmeric pickle, gingelly-oil avial
  • Sugarcane, banana, betel leaf — served on a fresh plantain leaf

BookingFixed thali, no à la carte. Whole-table reservations only — minimum two, maximum eight per leaf. Pre-payment confirms.

The River Festival · Aadi 18

Aadi Perukku ₹1,250

Saturday 2 August & Sunday 3 August 2025 · single seating, 1:00 PM

  • Five flavoured rices — coconut, lemon, tamarind, curd, sesame — packed in banana leaf
  • Vazhaipoo vadai, sundakkai vatha kuzhambu, kovakkai poriyal
  • Paruppu payasam with palm jaggery, served warm from copper
  • Tender mango pickle, three-day aged, from the kitchen's clay jar

BookingCarry-out parcels available for the river-bank ritual — order three days ahead. Dining-room seats forty.

Nine Nights · Sundal Counter

Navaratri Sundal ₹180 / cone

22 September – 1 October 2025 · 5:00 to 9:00 PM, courtyard counter

  • Nine days, nine legumes — chana, kabuli, peanut, green gram, mochai, karamani, thatta payaru, white peas, horse gram
  • Tempered with mustard, asafoetida, fresh coconut, raw mango on alternate evenings
  • Served in palm-leaf cones with kosumalli salad and a small payasam
  • Free golu darshan in the entrance bay — bring the children

BookingWalk-in counter, no reservation required. Whole-platter pre-orders for kolu visits accepted till 4 PM same day.

The Oil-Lamp Dinner · Karthigai Deepam

Lamp Supper ₹1,650

Wednesday 3 December 2025 · single seating, 7:30 PM, lamps lit at 6:45

  • Eight hundred clay agal lamps along the entrance, lit by guests on arrival
  • Pori urundai and nei appam at the door, by hand, still warm
  • Slow-cooked country-chicken kuzhambu with marudhani spice, kal dosai
  • Adai-aviyal, rice-flour kozhukattai, payasam of sago and coconut milk

BookingForty-eight seats only. Vegetarian table set apart on request at booking. Children below ten at half-cover.

The Standing Supper Series · Since 2019

The Kuzhambu Club

One long table. Twelve seats. The chef cooks one kuzhambu, three ways.

When

Last Saturday
of every month

Doors at 7:30 PM. The first course is served at 8:00 sharp; latecomers join at the next ladle. The evening closes by 10:45.

How It Works

A single regional kuzhambu — meen, kara, vatha, ennai, or pulikuzhambu — is taken apart through the meal. First poured over rice, then over dosai, then sipped from a tumbler. The chef sits down for the third course.

Cover & Booking

₹2,400 per head

Includes a welcome rasam, three kuzhambu courses, two rices, four side dishes, dessert, filter coffee. Wine and toddy at additional cost. Pre-payment is required to hold a seat.

Upcoming dates — 25 January · 22 February · 29 March · 26 April 2025. To book, write club@kalasha.in or call the kitchen line directly. Members of the club receive the year's twelve dates by post in early January.
