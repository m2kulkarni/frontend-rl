Bill of Fare  ·  Lunch & Dinner

The Menu

A Note from the Kitchen — All midday meals are served on fresh banana leaf, in the manner of Karaikudi households. Rice is from the Thanjavur delta; cold-pressed gingelly oil is sourced from Virudhunagar; palm jaggery and cane jaggery arrive each fortnight from Tirunelveli. Spice blends are stone-ground in our kitchen on Wednesdays. Vegetarian, jain, and gluten-conscious adaptations available on request.

II

Meals · Sappadu

Served on Banana Leaf · Lunch Only

Karaikudi Virundhu Sappadu

₹680

Heritage Plate · Eleven Items

A weekday feast in the Karaikudi tradition — ponni rice, vatha kuzhambu, more kuzhambu, two poriyals, kootu, urulai varuval, appalam, fresh curd, mango pickle, and paal payasam to close. Refills of rice, sambar, rasam, curd.

Hand-pounded ponni rice from Mannargudi · banana leaf from Theni.

Sunday Elai Sappadu

₹820

Sundays Only · Fifteen Items

Our elaborate Sunday spread — kollu rasam, paruppu urundai kuzhambu, three seasonal poriyals, ennai kathirikai, vazhaipoo vadai, mor kuzhambu, beetroot pachadi, adhirasam, and paal payasam. Served twelve to three.

Heirloom millets from Sivagangai · jaggery from Tirunelveli pans.

“In Chettinad, a meal is a procession — rice arrives first, then thirteen small dignities, each with its own moment on the leaf.”

Kuzhambu & Varuval

Slow-Cooked · À La Carte

Chettinad Kozhi Varuval

₹520

Pepper-Roast Chicken · Dry

Country chicken stir-fried with our signature varuval masala — black pepper, fennel, kalpasi stone-flower, and curry leaf — until each piece is dark, glossy, and clinging with spice.

₹540

Tamarind Fish Curry · Coastal

Seer fish simmered in a clay pot with tamarind, fenugreek, shallot, and gingelly oil. The gravy is intentionally thin, stained terracotta-red — best the next day, sharper the morning after.

Day-boat seer from Rameswaram · tamarind from Tirunelveli groves.

Stuffed Brinjal · Gingelly-Oil Braise

Mutton Sukka Varuval
