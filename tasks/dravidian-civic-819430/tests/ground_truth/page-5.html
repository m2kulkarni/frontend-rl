Public Notices & Tenders

All statutory notices issued by this office under the Tamil Nadu District Municipalities Act, the Town and Country Planning Act, and the Tamil Nadu Local Fund Audit Act are published here on the day of issue and remain on record for the period prescribed by law. Original signed copies are displayed at the Commissioner's office and at the notice board of each ward office.

Type Tender Call Dated 24 June 2024 Ward All (SWM Division) File No. TWN/SWM/2024-25/03

Supply of Three (3) Compactor Vehicles for Solid-Waste CollectionOpen

The Commissioner, Tiruvanaikoil Town Panchayat invites sealed competitive tenders, in two-cover system, from manufacturers and authorised dealers registered with the Government of Tamil Nadu for the supply, delivery and ninety-day commissioning of three (3) hydraulic-compactor refuse-collection vehicles (8 m³ capacity, BS-VI compliant) for door-to-door solid-waste collection across the eighteen wards of the town.

Tender documents may be obtained in person from the Engineering Section, Room 4, between 10.30 a.m. and 4.00 p.m. on working days, or downloaded from the Tamil Nadu e-Procurement portal under reference TN/MAWS/TVK/2024/SWM/03. Pre-bid clarification meeting will be held on 10 July 2024 at 11.00 a.m. in the Council Hall.

Bidders must satisfy the qualification criteria stated in Clause 4 of the tender document, including minimum annual turnover of Rs. 3.50 crore in any of the last three financial years and prior supply of at least five similar vehicles to a local body in Tamil Nadu, Karnataka or Kerala.

Type Public Objection Dated 18 June 2024 Ward 1 – 18 (Town-wide) File No. TWN/PLN/MP-REV/2024

Draft Revision of the Tiruvanaikoil Master Plan, 2024–2044Under Review

Notice is hereby given under Section 32 of the Tamil Nadu Town and Country Planning Act, 1971 that the Tiruvanaikoil Town Panchayat, in concurrence with the Directorate of Town and Country Planning, has prepared a draft revision of the Master Plan governing land use, building height regulation, river-margin protection along the Kollidam, and the temple-precinct heritage buffer of 250 metres around the Jambukeswarar Temple complex.

Copies of the draft Master Plan, together with the supporting maps at scale 1:5000, are available for public inspection at the Commissioner's office on all working days between 10.00 a.m. and 5.00 p.m., and at the Block Development Office, Srirangam, until the closing date below.

Any person, body of persons or local authority affected by, or interested in, any provision of the draft Plan may submit objections or suggestions in writing, in Tamil or English, to the undersigned. Submissions received after the closing date will not be entertained except for reasons recorded in writing under sub-section (3).

Type Audit Notice Dated 12 June 2024 Quarter Q1 · F.Y. 2024-25 File No. DLFA/TRY/TVK/Q1/24-25

Quarterly Audit of Town Panchayat Accounts by the Director of Local Fund AuditScheduled

In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 5 of the Tamil Nadu Local Fund Audit Act, 2014, the Director of Local Fund Audit, Chennai, has instructed the District Audit Officer, Tiruchirappalli, to undertake the quarterly audit of receipts, expenditure and grant utilisation of this Town Panchayat for the period 1 April 2024 to 30 June 2024.

The audit team will commence work on 5 August 2024 and is expected to conclude by 23 August 2024. All Section Heads, Revenue Inspectors, the Sanitary Officer, and the Manager (Accounts) are required to keep their cash books, register of demand-collection-balance, contractor bills, and stores ledgers ready for production.

Members of the public who wish to draw the attention of the auditor to any specific transaction of public interest may submit a signed representation, with documentary evidence, on or before 2 August 2024. Anonymous representations will not be acted upon, in keeping with Rule 17(2) of the Audit Manual.

How to Engage with a Notice

Filing Objections

Written objections to any notice must carry the file number, the objector's full address and signature, and be submitted in duplicate at the Inward Counter (Room 1) before the closing time. A dated acknowledgement is issued at the counter and serves as proof of receipt.

Inspection of Documents

Tender documents, draft plans, and audit registers connected to current notices may be inspected free of charge during office hours. Photocopies are issued on payment of Rs. 2 per page under the Right to Information Act, 2005.

  • Inward Counter · 10.00 a.m. – 5.30 p.m.
  • Lunch Recess · 1.30 – 2.15 p.m.
  • Closed · 2nd Saturday & Public Holidays

Languages of Notice

All statutory notices are issued in Tamil as the primary language, with an English rendering for reference. In the event of any divergence between the two texts, the Tamil text shall prevail, in accordance with G.O. (Ms.) No. 145 of the Tamil Development & Information Department.
