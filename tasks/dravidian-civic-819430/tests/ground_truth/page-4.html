The Eighteen Wards of Tiruvanaikoil

The municipal area is divided into eighteen wards, drawn around the four temple-streets, the Cauvery bund colonies, and the eastern shandy quarter. Each ward returns one elected member to the Council; each ward is also assigned a Bill Collector who attends a fixed local hour daily for tax collection, certificate intake and grievance receipt.

Reading the Map

The temple-precinct (★) anchors the four Mada Veethi wards. Tank cells (≈) mark the Cauvery channel and the Sandhai-pettai municipal pond. Wards 13–18 lie east of the channel.
