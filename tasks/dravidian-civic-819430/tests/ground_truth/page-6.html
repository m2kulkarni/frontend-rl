Visit the Panchayat Office

The Office of the Municipal Commissioner welcomes citizens, applicants and ward delegates on every working day at 14 Sannathi Street, opposite the eastern gateway of the temple precinct. This page records the office address, public hours, statutory grievance-redressal procedure under the Tamil Nadu Public Services Delivery Guarantee Act, and travel directions from Tiruchirappalli Junction.

Grievance Redressal under the Tamil Nadu Public Services Delivery Guarantee Act

Statutory NoticeAct 26 of 2011File: TVK/GRV/SOP/05

Every notified citizen service — birth/death certificate, building plan approval, trade licence renewal, property-tax assessment and water connection — is delivered within a fixed time-limit. Where the designated officer fails to deliver within the stipulated period, an aggrieved person may proceed under the procedure prescribed below. The full schedule of services and stipulated days is on display in Hall A.

  1. First Appeal — Commissioner

    File Form A at counter 6 within thirty (30) days of the lapse. Decision communicated within fifteen working days.

  2. Second Appeal — District Collector

    If unsatisfied, prefer Form B before the Tiruchirappalli Collectorate within sixty (60) days.

  3. Final Appeal — Tamil Nadu Public Services Commission

    Submitted via post or e-portal within ninety (90) days of the second-appeal order.

  4. Penalty & Compensation

    A penalty of ₹500 to ₹5,000 is leviable on the defaulting officer; compensation may be awarded to the citizen.

Accessibility & Language Assistance

The Panchayat Office is wheelchair-accessible at the eastern entrance; a ramp of 1:12 gradient leads to Hall A. A Tamil-English interpreter is available at counter 2 between 10:30 and 16:30; sign-language assistance may be requested in advance on 1800-425-1900. Drinking water, seating and a lactation room are provided on the ground floor. Service animals are permitted throughout the building.
