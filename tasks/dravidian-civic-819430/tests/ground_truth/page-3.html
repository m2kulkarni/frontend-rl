Resolution 47 / 2024 · Sitting of 12 January 2024

Sanction of Rs. 18.60 Lakh for Repair of the South Mada Street Stormwater Drain

The Council resolved to sanction expenditure of Rupees Eighteen Lakh Sixty Thousand only towards reconstruction of the masonry stormwater drain along South Mada Street between the Tank Bund junction and the Old Bus Stand, comprising 412 running metres, under the State Finance Commission Devolution Grant 2023–24. Tendering to be completed before 28 February 2024.

Resolution 84 / 2024 · Sitting of 11 April 2024

Approval of the Annual Budget Estimate for the Year 2024–25

The Council approved the Budget Estimate placing the total receipts at Rs. 9.42 crore and the total expenditure at Rs. 9.18 crore, leaving a closing balance of Rs. 24.06 lakh. Heads of major outlay: street lighting Rs. 1.62 cr, water supply Rs. 2.34 cr, sanitation Rs. 1.86 cr, road maintenance Rs. 1.47 cr, and the Periya Kulam desilting reserve Rs. 38.50 lakh.

Resolution 96 / 2024 · Sitting of 09 May 2024

Sanction for Augmentation of the Cauvery Combined Water Supply Scheme

Resolved to accept the technical estimate of Rs. 2.34 crore prepared by the Tamil Nadu Water Supply and Drainage Board for the augmentation of the existing Cauvery Combined Water Supply Scheme, comprising one additional 2.5-lakh-litre overhead tank at Therkku Theru and 6.8 km of distribution mains, and to remit the panchayat share of 25 per cent (Rs. 58.50 lakh) in three instalments.
