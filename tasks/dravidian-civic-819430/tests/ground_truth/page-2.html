Catalogue of Citizen Services and Statutory Certificates

The Office of the Municipal Commissioner administers fifty-three statutory services for residents of Tiruvanaikoil Town Panchayat. The six services listed below account for over 84% of all counter footfall recorded during the financial year 2023–2024. Each service is governed by a section of the Tamil Nadu District Municipalities Act 1920 or rules framed thereunder, and each carries a fixed counter, fee, and timeline.

Walk-in applicants are advised to collect a token at the Reception Hall by 11:00. Online tracking is available through the Tamil Nadu Urban Local Body portal using the file number printed on every receipt issued at the Sannathi Street office.

The Six Principal Services

Statutory Catalogue 2024 – 2025

№ 01

Birth Registration — Form 1

Registration of Births & Deaths Act 1969 · §8 r/w TN Rules 2000

Recording of every live birth occurring within the panchayat limits. Mandatory within 21 days of occurrence. Late registration up to 30 days requires an explanation in writing; beyond one year, an order from the Revenue Divisional Officer is required.

SectionPublic Health
CounterCounter 03 · Hall A
Timeline7 working days
Fee₹ 50 per copy
№ 02

Death Registration — Form 2

Registration of Births & Deaths Act 1969 · §8(1)(e)

Issue of statutory death certificate. Hospital deaths are forwarded by the institution within 21 days. Domestic deaths are reported by the head of the household, with cremation receipt from the Kallanai Road or Cantonment burning ghat.

SectionPublic Health
CounterCounter 03 · Hall A
Timeline5 working days
Fee₹ 50 per copy
№ 03

Trade Licence Renewal

TN District Municipalities Act 1920 · §249-A · Sched. V

Annual renewal of D&O (Dangerous & Offensive) trade licences for hotels, lodges, provision stores, timber depots, and twenty-eight other enumerated trades operating along Sannathi Street, Cauvery Bridge Road, and ward markets 1 through 18.

SectionRevenue · Trade Cell
CounterCounter 07 · Hall B
Timeline15 working days
Fee₹ 500 – ₹ 12,000
№ 04

Building Plan Approval

TN Combined Development & Building Rules 2019 · Rule 14

Sanction of plans for residential plots up to 300 m² and commercial plots up to 750 m². Plans exceeding these thresholds are forwarded to the Directorate of Town & Country Planning. FSI, parking, set-back, and street-line scrutiny conducted at this counter.

SectionTown Planning
CounterCounter 11 · Hall C
Timeline30 working days
Fee₹ 18 per m² built area
№ 05

Water-Tap New Connection

TN Water Supply & Drainage Board Act 1970 · §33

Application for a new domestic ½″ tap connection to the Cauvery combined water-supply scheme. Includes site inspection by the Junior Engineer (Water), road-cut deposit, and meter sealing. Bulk and commercial connections are processed at the Engineering Wing.

SectionEngineering · Water
CounterCounter 09 · Hall B
Timeline21 working days
Fee₹ 4,500 (deposit ₹ 2,000)
№ 06

Property-Tax Payment

TN District Municipalities Act 1920 · §81 to §86

Half-yearly remittance of property tax based on Annual Rental Value (ARV) as revised in 2022. Rebate of 5% available if both half-years are paid before 30 April. Receipts include the ward number, door number, and nine-digit assessment number.

SectionRevenue · Tax Cell
CounterCounters 01 & 02 · Hall A
TimelineSame day (instant)
FeeAs per ARV slab

Walk-in Procedure

Four Steps to Receive a Certificate

1

Token at Reception

Collect a numbered token from the Reception Hall between 09:15 and 11:00. Carry one government-issued photo identity and the relevant application form.

2

Counter Submission

Present the application at the designated counter. The clerk will verify enclosures, generate a 9-digit file number, and stamp acknowledgement on the duplicate.

3

Fee Remittance

Pay the prescribed fee at the Cash Counter (Hall A). Demand drafts must be drawn in favour of The Commissioner, Tiruvanaikoil Town Panchayat, payable at Trichy.

4

Collection or Despatch

Collect the certificate within the statutory timeline at the same counter, or opt for India Post Speed-Post despatch by paying an additional ₹ 35.

Notice to Applicants — Right to Service Act 2010

Any officer who fails to deliver a service within the statutory timeline above is liable for penalty under §3 of the Tamil Nadu Right to Service Act 2010. Applicants may file an appeal before the First Appellate Officer (Asst. Commissioner) within thirty days of the lapsed timeline. The appeal counter is located at Hall C, ground floor, opposite Counter № 11.

Accessibility

The Sannathi Street office is fully ramp-accessible at the eastern entrance. A wheelchair, a sign-language assistant (Wed & Fri), and large-print forms are available on request at Reception.

Helpline for persons with disabilities: 0431 — 226 1971.

Language · மொழி
