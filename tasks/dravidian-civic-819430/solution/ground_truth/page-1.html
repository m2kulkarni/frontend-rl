A Word from the Office

Welcome to the Commissioner's Office

Tiruvanaikoil — temple-town on the northern bank of the Cauvery — has served its citizens through a constituted municipal body since 1888. This is the official portal of the office charged with its day-to-day administration.

Tmt. R. Kalaivani, I.A.S.
Municipal Commissioner

Vanakkam, fellow citizens.

Since assuming charge on 14 March 2024, this office has worked to bring our 18 wards under a single, transparent service window. The financial year 2024–25 carries three commitments that I have placed before the Council and to which my staff and I are accountable: the desilting of the Cauvery North Bund before the north-east monsoon, the conversion of all 4,612 streetlights to LED before December, and a citizen-friendly property-tax self-assessment window from 1 April to 30 June.

The Panchayat office at Big Street, opposite the Mariamman Tank, is open to walk-in petitioners every working day from 09:30 to 17:45 hrs, and a Saturday grievance camp operates between 10:00 and 13:00. Where you can be served online, this portal will serve you online. Where you must be heard in person, my chamber is open.

— R. Kalaivani, I.A.S.
Commissioner · Order Ref. G.O.(Ms) No. 142, MA & WS Dept., 11.03.2024
Council Priorities · Fiscal 2024–25

Three Works Before the Monsoon

Each priority is supervised by a named officer and reported quarterly to the Council. Citizens may track progress through the published resolutions.

I.
Flood Mitigation

Cauvery Bund Desilting Programme

Mechanical desilting of 6.4 km of the North Bund between Sengulam Sluice and Anaikarai Bridge, with reinforcement of three sluice gates. Tendered under TUFIDCO scheme TPC/2024/B-09 at ₹ 4.62 crore.

Target: 30 September 2024 · Officer i/c: A.E. (Civil)
II.
Energy Transition

Streetlight Conversion to LED

4,612 sodium-vapour fixtures across all 18 wards being replaced with 60W and 90W LED units under the EESL aggregated tender. Estimated annual saving: 38.4 lakh kWh.

Status: 2,107 of 4,612 done · Officer i/c: J.E. (Electrical)
III.
Revenue Reform

Property-Tax Self-Assessment

Revised plinth-area-based assessment open from 1 April – 30 June 2025. Online filing through the Citizen Services portal; rebates of 5% for early payment before 30 April. Counter help-desk at Big Street office.

Window: 01.04 – 30.06.2025 · Officer i/c: Revenue Inspector
