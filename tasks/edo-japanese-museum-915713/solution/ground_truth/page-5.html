Hours 拝観のご案内

The museum occupies a restored Meiji-era machiya tucked along Aneyakōji-dōri in central Kyoto. Galleries, the inner garden and the workshop floor are open year-round to visitors arriving on foot from Karasuma-Oike subway.

By subway

Karasuma-Oike Station (Karasuma & Tōzai lines) — exit 2, then eight minutes on foot southward along Karasuma-dōri, turning west onto Aneyakōji.

By bus

City Bus 15 or 51 to Karasuma-Oike; the museum sits a short block west of the stop.

On foot from Nijō-jō

A pleasant fifteen-minute walk east through the merchant lanes of Nakagyō.

A note on the entrance

Enter through the noren-curtained doorway, not the side gate.

The machiya presents two openings to the street. Visitors should pass beneath the indigo noren hung at the principal genkan — recognisable by the small crane seal on its left panel. The narrow side gate, set further down the lane behind a slatted lattice, leads only to the staff entrance and the kura storehouse and is kept closed during public hours.
