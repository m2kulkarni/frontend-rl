Undergraduate Admissions  ·  Academic Year 2026

Entry by
letter, ink,
and resolve.

Office of the Registrar  ·  Established Meiji 14

Rikuō welcomes a single intake each spring. Candidates apply along one of three tracks below; each is read by a panel of three faculty members from the relevant school. We accept a deliberately small cohort — approximately one hundred and forty undergraduates per year — so that every entrant can be known by name.

— I.

Three
Deadlines

Each track is independent. An applicant may stand for one only. All times are Japan Standard Time, end of day.

Track 一 · Recommendation

Recommendation
Track

10 December 2025

For candidates put forward by an affiliated upper secondary or by a practicing master in a recognised craft. Interview held on campus the following January.

Track 二 · International

International
Application

15 January 2026

Open to applicants schooled outside Japan. Submitted online with certified transcripts. Conducted in English or Japanese; no preference is given to either.

Track 三 · Domestic

Domestic
Common Test

25 February 2026

Standard route for graduates of Japanese senior high schools. Common Test results are read alongside the personal statement and any submitted portfolio.

— II.

Required
Documents

All applicants, regardless of track, submit the following. Files are uploaded to the admissions portal; physical portfolios are couriered to the Yanaka campus.

  1. 一
    Personal Statement
    A continuous essay of approximately 1,200 words on a chosen prompt issued each October. Written in either Japanese or English.
  2. 二
    Two Letters of Reference
    From two persons who have taught or supervised the candidate for at least one academic year. Submitted directly through the portal.
  3. 三
    Portfolio Fine Crafts only
    Eight to twelve works in any medium pursued by the candidate. Documentation in photographs is acceptable; original objects need not be sent.
  4. 四
    Calligraphy Sample Encouraged
    A single sheet, free composition. Read not for technical mastery but for attention. Optional in every track.
— III.

Tuition &
Support

¥640,000

Per Annum  ·  AY 2026

The Tachibana Foundation Scholarship

Each year the Tachibana Foundation, established in 1962 by the late Tachibana Yoshinori, awards twelve full-tuition scholarships to incoming undergraduates. Recipients are nominated by faculty after admission; no separate application is required.

The scholarship is renewable for the full four years of study, contingent on academic standing.

To stand for entry is, in itself, the first lesson.

The portal opens 1 October 2025. Candidates with questions may write to the Office of the Registrar at any time; replies are sent within ten working days.
