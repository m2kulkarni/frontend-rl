No. 一 — From the President

A welcome from President Tachibana Sōen

We remain a small university by deliberate choice. Our halls in Yanaka are old, our enrolment modest, our presses still set in metal type for the annual review. To those who come to study with us — in literature, in lacquer, in the histories of the Eastern Capital — I extend a quiet welcome and the same promise the founders made: attention without haste, scholarship without vanity.

— 立花 宗淵
Tachibana Sōen, President

No. 三 — From the Charter

A line from the founding charter, 1868

“Let learning be cultivated as one cultivates a garden in the city — enclosed by walls, but open above to the same weather as the rest of the world.”
— Charter of Rikuō, Meiji 元年 · 1868
