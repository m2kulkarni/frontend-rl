Of the Workshop
at rue au Lin

Founded in the year of our Lord two thousand and eight, in a courtyard off the rue au Lin in the shadow of the cathedral of Notre-Dame de Chartres, the atelier of Guillaume de Sancerre keeps to the manner of work set down by the master glaziers of the twelfth and thirteenth centuries. Every panel that leaves these benches has been cut from pot-metal glass blown to our specification at the verrerie of La Rochère, painted in iron-oxide grisaille, fired thrice in a brick-built kiln, and assembled with milled lead came of an H-section true to the medieval profile.

The workshop is small by intention: the master, two compagnons, and a single apprentice in his fourth of seven years. We accept no more than three commissions in any given season, and we keep a written record, in the old hand, of every cartoon drawn and every kiln-firing made — a discipline learned from the surviving pattern-books of Reims and from the fabric accounts of the cathedral chapter, MS. lat. 14410, which the master copied in his student years.

We work for chapels, for parish churches, for private oratories, and for the conservancy of the old glass wherever the bishops will have us. We do not undertake secular ornament, nor sandblasted glass, nor any process that would dishonour the lead.

— Guillaume de Sancerre, mag. vitr. —

Recent Commissions

Anno MMXXII — MMXXIV · Three Works of the Workshop

The Lady Chapel,
Saint-Pierre de Beauvais

Five lancets of the south Lady Chapel, replacing nineteenth-century glazing of poor ferment. The central light bears the Coronation of the Virgin in cobalt and madder upon a ground of grisaille trellis; the flanking lights, four prophets named in our chapter accounts of October MMXXII. Twelve panels, 4,840 pieces of pot-metal cut by hand. Installed Whitsuntide MMXXIV.

Comm. of the Diocese MMXXII — MMXXIV

A Private Oratory
at Reims

Single pointed-arch window, six feet by sixteen, for the oratory of the Maison Lavergne at Reims. The Tree of Jesse rendered in eleven medallions upon a deep-cobalt field; flesh tones in two firings of grisaille, the haloes back-painted with silver-stain and fired at six hundred degrees. Cartoons drawn from a Champenois book of hours, c. 1280, by gracious permission of the family.

Private Commission MMXXIII

North-Aisle Lancets,
Sainte-Radegonde

Restoration of the three north-aisle lancets of the parish church of Sainte-Radegonde, dating in their original ferment to the fourth quarter of the thirteenth century. Storm damage of February MMXXI required the dismantling of fourteen panels; eighty-three lights were releaded with new H-came drawn to the original profile, and forty-one missing pieces were reblown and repainted in our manner. Re-set Michaelmas MMXXIII.
