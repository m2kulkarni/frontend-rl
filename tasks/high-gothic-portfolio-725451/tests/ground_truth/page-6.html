Groups of more than four persons are received par rendez-vous only.

Visitors are welcome to climb to the gallery above the studio floor, whence the cutting of pot-metal and the painting of grisaille upon the bench may be observed without disturbing the hand of the workman. The gallery is reached by a stair of seventeen steps; a bench is provided for those who wish to remain.

The atelier lies four streets distant from the south porch of the cathedral, in the lower town between the Place des Halles and the river Eure. The nearest railway station is Chartres, whence the walk is some twelve minutes by way of the rue du Bois-Merrain.

Those who would observe a particular operation — the firing of a panel, the leading of a finished cartoon — are advised to write beforehand, that the day of their coming may be matched to the work then upon the bench.
