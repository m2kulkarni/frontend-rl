Scholarship
& Library

Bibliotheca Traiani

The Bibliotheca Traiani exists to serve scholars, curators, and qualified students engaged in the close study of the Roman Empire. Founded as the museum's research arm in MCMXXIII, the library now combines a substantial reference collection, a digital squeeze archive of Latin and Greek inscriptions, an annual fellowship programme, and the editorial seat of the journal Acta Antiquaria. Reading-rooms occupy the museum's upper west wing and may be entered by appointment.

Holdings & Resources

Reference Library

Bibliotheca Manuscripta

18,000

Bound Volumes

The reference collection comprises approximately eighteen thousand volumes covering Latin and Greek epigraphy, Roman provincial archaeology, numismatics, the early Imperial historians, and the architectural treatises of Vitruvius, Serlio, and Palladio. Holdings are non-circulating; consultation is by appointment in the Sala Traianea.

Squeeze Archive

Tabularium Inscriptionum

3,200

Digital Inscriptions

The museum's epigraphic squeeze archive holds three thousand two hundred high-resolution digital impressions of Roman inscriptions, drawn principally from the Forum Traiani, Ostia Antica, and the African provinces. Each entry is catalogued by CIL number, find-site, and metric profile; access is granted to credentialed researchers.

Fellowship Programme

Sodalitas Annua

III

Resident Fellows · MMXXIV

Three competitive fellowships are awarded each year to scholars of established standing or advanced doctoral candidates. Fellows reside in Rome for terms of four to nine months, hold reading privileges throughout the museum, and contribute a public lecture and a paper to Acta Antiquaria. The next call for applications closes on the Kalends of March.

Acta Antiquaria & Correspondence

The Annual Journal

Acta Antiquaria · Founded MCMXXIII

Acta Antiquaria is the museum's peer-reviewed annual journal, in continuous publication since 1923. The volume comprises eight to ten studies of original scholarship in Roman archaeology, epigraphy, history, and material culture, with a standing section devoted to short editions of unpublished inscriptions in the museum's keeping.

Founded
A.D. MCMXXIII (1923)
Frequency
One volume yearly, issued at the Ides of October
Languages
Italian, English, French, German, Latin
Length
Articles 6,000–12,000 words; notes 1,500–3,000

Submission terms. Manuscripts are received on a rolling basis until the Kalends of February for the volume of that year. Authors should submit a single PDF, double-spaced, conforming to the journal's house style (available on request) and a separate anonymised version for double-blind review. Photographs of inscriptions must be accompanied by squeeze impressions where possible. The editorial committee notifies authors of decisions before the Kalends of June.

  1. Address submissions to the Editorial Secretary, marked Acta Antiquaria · Vol. CII.
  2. Include institutional affiliation and a 150-word abstract in English and Italian.
  3. Authors of accepted papers receive twenty offprints and one bound volume.
