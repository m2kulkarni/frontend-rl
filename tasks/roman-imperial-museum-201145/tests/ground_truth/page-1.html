Memoria Vrbis Aeternae

· Salvete · Welcome to the Antiquarium ·

The Antiquarium Traianum stands upon the southern flank of the Forum of Trajan, founded by royal decree in 1907 to gather and conserve the lapidary, bronze, and architectural patrimony unearthed during the systematic excavation of the imperial fora. For more than a century our galleries have offered scholars and citizens a measured passage from the Augustan settlement of 27 B.C. through the closing chapter of the Severan dynasty.

Our holdings comprise four thousand two hundred and twelve catalogued objects: dedicatory inscriptions to emperors and consuls, fragmentary equestrian bronzes, sculptural revetments from the Basilica Ulpia, fistulae bearing the names of imperial freedmen, and the celebrated wall of marble portraits drawn from the necropoleis of the Via Latina. A reading room and the Bibliotheca Traiani complete the institution's scholarly apparatus.

The museum is open Tuesday through Sunday from nine in the morning to seven in the evening, and is reached on foot from the Via dei Fori Imperiali. Admission to the permanent galleries is twelve euro; concessions, school groups, and citizens of Rome enter at reduced rate. We invite you to walk among the stones, and to read what the Empire wrote of itself.
