Access for All

De Aditu Universali

The Antiquarium is committed to making the material culture of the Empire legible to every visitor. The four floors of the museum are reached by a lift situated to the right of the atrium, with controls in raised characters. Wheelchairs are available at the cloakroom without charge.

Tactile reproductions of three principal portraits — Augustus of Prima Porta, Trajan in armour, and the empress Iulia Domna — are mounted at convenient height in the central rotunda and may be handled freely.
