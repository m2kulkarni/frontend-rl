A plate room of twelve architectural studies, drawn from the choirs and tribunes of northern France between MCXLV and MCCLXXX.

Engraved · Measured · Annotated

Each leaf is rendered to half-elevation, with measurement bars in pieds-du-roi; initials beneath the rule denote the draughtsman of the studio.

After Viollet-le-Duc · Lassus · Boeswillwald

The Twelve Plates · MMXXIV

The plates collected here are working drawings, not finished engravings: each was prepared on the desk in the shadow of the choir, measured from scaffolding or copied from earlier surveys, and inked in the studio of the Quarterly between Easter MMXXIII and Lent MMXXIV. Reproductions are sold by the press at twelve livres the dozen.
