Subscribe & Correspond

Four issues per annum, posted at the four canonical quarter-days. Letters to the editor, manuscript proposals, and plates received by post or by hand.

The Quarterly is a small subscription review, set in lead-by-hand at our desk in the shadow of the choir, and printed each quarter on Munken Pure paper at the press of Saint-Séverin. We carry no advertising and no corporate underwriting; the journal subsists upon its readers and upon the Auerbach-Holcombe Fund, which has supported our editorial independence since 2021. The pages that follow record the terms of subscription, the manner of correspondence, and the colophon by which we account for ourselves.

Colophon

The Lancet Quarterly is edited by Reuben Auerbach and Marguerite Holcombe at a single desk in the rue du Cloître Notre-Dame, with editorial assistance from Sœur Madeleine Coquillard of the cathedral archive.

The Quarterly does not run advertising of any kind, and accepts no commercial sponsorship. Since the spring of 2021 it has been supported, in whole and without editorial condition, by the Auerbach-Holcombe Fund for the study of medieval architecture.

Plates are reproduced by offset lithography at four colours; text is set in lead and printed letterpress on Munken Pure 120 g/m². Bindings are sewn in linen thread by Atelier Pellerin.
