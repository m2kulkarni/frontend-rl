Seated in the northern arcade of the Maydan-e Naqsh-e Jahan, between the Qaysarieh Gate and the Mosque of Sheikh Lotfollah, this office records the edicts of His Majesty Shah ‘Abbas, the prices of the bazaar, and the labours undertaken upon the river and the canals for the welfare of the city and her people.

Year 1037 A.H. · 1628 C.E.

From the Royal Court

Edict of His Majesty
Shah ‘Abbas

Be it known to all merchants, caravan-masters, and travellers passing through the Maydan that the new caravanserai at Madar-i Shah, erected by royal command, shall open its gates to lodgers from the first of Jumada-al-Awwal next.

Tariffs upon the silk of Gilan, raw and woven, are by the same hand reduced by one part in ten, that the trade of His Majesty's domains may flourish.

Sealed · Royal Diwan

Public Works

Notice — Zayandeh-Rud
Canal Works

The Master of Waters announces that, beginning the 20th of this month, the dredging and re-lining of the madi of Faradan shall commence, with labour drawn from the wards of Joubareh and Dardasht.

Watering of orchards along the southern channel shall be permitted only between the second prayer and the call of sunset for the duration of forty days.

Any obstruction of the works, or diverting of water without leave, shall be answered before the Dīvānbegi.

— Mirza Taqi, Mir-Ab of Isfahan
