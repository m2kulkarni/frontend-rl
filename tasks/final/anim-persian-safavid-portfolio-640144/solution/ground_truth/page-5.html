Folio V · Recorded Notices

Press & Honours

A chronicle of writings, mentions and distinctions concerning the atelier's tilework — set in cartouches, in the order the world received them.

]>
In Chronological Order
  1. 2024 London
    Book · Anthology Entry

    Persian Tile in the Twenty-First Century

    Thames & Hudson, hardcover, 312 pp. — atelier featured in chapter VII, “The Mosaicist's Hand,” with photographic plates of the Jolfa workshop and the Mashhad mihrab commission.

    Edited by Sussan Babaie · ISBN 978-0-500-02463-1
  2. ]>
  3. 2023 March
    Periodical · Feature Essay

    A Living Tilework

    Ten-page profile in Apollo Magazine tracing the cutting of a single cobalt star from raw frit to its setting in the Khaju courtyard restoration. Photography by Newsha Tavakolian.

    Apollo · Vol. CXCVII, No. 712 · pp. 64–73
  4. ]>
  5. 2021 July
    Periodical · Cover Story

    The Last Mosaicists of Isfahan

    Cover feature in Aramco World on the surviving practitioners of moʿarraq-kāri, with extended interview on the inheritance of cartoon-drawing from the Tabrizi lineage.

    Aramco World · Vol. 72, No. 4 · pp. 12–27
  6. ]>
  7. 2020 Paris
    Honour · International Nomination

    UNESCO Living Human Treasure

    Nominated by the Iranian Cultural Heritage, Handicrafts and Tourism Organisation under the Programme for the Safeguarding of Intangible Cultural Heritage — citation for sustained transmission of Safavid tile-mosaic technique.

    UNESCO · Dossier IR-2020-LHT-014
  8. ]>
  9. 2018 Tehran
    Honour · Gold Medal

    Tehran Triennial of Traditional Crafts

    Awarded the gold medal in the architectural ornament category for the spandrel panel Eight Pointed Star with Tendrils — fired earth on lime mortar, 1.4 × 1.4 m. Jury chaired by Faryar Javaherian.

    Niavaran Cultural Centre · 18–28 Mehr 1397
