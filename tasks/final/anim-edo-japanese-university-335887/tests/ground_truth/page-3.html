三Admissions · 入学案内

入学のご案内 Apply to
Hokurin.

Hokurin admits a small undergraduate cohort and a smaller graduate institute each year — roughly 320 entering students across the machiya campus. The academic calendar offers three intake windows for AY 2025–2026, with rolling review for international applicants. We read each application by hand, in committee, and reply in the season we received it.

一

Three Intake Windows

Application Calendar · 2025–2026
Autumn intake · 秋入試

Standard Domestic Cycle

1 September 2025

Application portal opens 1 July. Documents due 1 September. Written examination 12 October at the Sanjō campus. Decisions issued by 15 November for January matriculation.

Spring intake · 春入試

Late Cycle & Transfers

15 January 2026

Documents due 15 January. Examination 8 February. Limited to faculties of Letters, Environmental Humanities, and the Craft Sciences research track. Decisions by 6 March.

International · 留学生

Rolling Review

Ongoing

Reviewed within 30 days of receipt. JLPT N2 or equivalent recommended; written exam waivable for applicants holding completed degree-track Japanese coursework. Visa support letter issued upon offer.

Tuition & Fee Waivers

学費および減免制度

A small public university charges what is needed and not more — and it asks the prefecture for the rest.

Hokurin's published tuition is set annually by the Board of Regents and approved by the Kyoto Prefectural Assembly. The figures below cover AY 2025–2026 and exclude the one-time admission fee of ¥282,000, which is waived for graduates of partner secondary schools and for the Tōrin Bunko apprenticeship track.

三

Documents & Examination

出願書類および入学試験
Required of every applicant

Documents

  1. Application form 入学願書 Completed in Japanese or English, signed in ink. Available from the admissions portal or by post.
  2. Secondary transcript 成績証明書 Original sealed transcript covering all years of upper-secondary study, with official translation if not in Japanese.
  3. Statement of intent 志望理由書 1,200–1,600 characters (Japanese) or 600–800 words (English) addressing one chosen faculty.
  4. Two letters of reference 推薦状 At least one from a teacher of record. For graduate applicants, both must be academic.
  5. Photograph & identity copy 写真・身分証 Recent 30×40mm photograph and copy of passport or residence card.
  6. Examination fee receipt 検定料領収書 ¥17,000 undergraduate · ¥30,000 graduate. Waivable under Tier I and II tuition relief.
Held at the Sanjō campus

Entrance Examination

Required papers · 必須科目
Japanese & English
120 minutes each. Japanese paper includes a short literary commentary (modern or classical, applicant's choice) and a précis. English paper combines reading comprehension with a 400-word analytical response.
Elective paper · 選択科目
Mathematics or World History
90 minutes. Mathematics covers the standard upper-secondary curriculum through Math II/B. World History favours interpretation of primary sources (Edo, late Qing, early modern Mediterranean). Choose one at registration.
Interview · 面接
Twenty-Minute Conversation
Two faculty readers. Conducted in Japanese (English available for international cycle). The conversation begins from the statement of intent; there are no trick questions and no formal dress requirement.
志は遠く、歩みは近く
Distant aim, near step
Hokurin's admissions seal is affixed by hand to each offer letter at the close of the cycle. The paulownia is the formal mark of the university registrar; you will see it once on your envelope, and again on your diploma four years later.
