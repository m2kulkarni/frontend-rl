Grant inquiries — whether for the conservation of inscriptions, the survey of aqueducts and cisterns, or the stabilisation of civic mosaics in situ — are convened and reviewed by the College of Curators quarterly, on the Kalends of January, April, July, and October. Submissions received between sittings are docketed and held until the next Kalend. Decisions are returned by sealed letter within thirty days of the sitting.
