Programmes & Active Projects under the seal of the Foundation, anno MMXXIV

Of the seventy-eight applications received during the last quadrennium, the Council of Curators has elected to underwrite the three undertakings recorded below. Each programme is bound by deed of patronage to a named lead conservator, a fixed term in years, and a sum committed in euros from the endowment. Reports are deposited semi-annually at the Foundation's office in Rome and at the relevant superintendency.

Programma· I ·

Inscriptiones Insulae Sacrae

In partnership with the Soprintendenza Speciale Archeologia, Belle Arti e Paesaggio di Roma — Sede di Ostia Antica

Cataloguing, photogrammetric recording, and critical edition of four hundred and twelve unpublished funerary inscriptions recovered during the 1928–1939 and 1988–1994 campaigns at the necropolis of Isola Sacra, between Portus and Ostia. The corpus, presently held in the depositi at Via dei Romagnoli, contains stelae, columellae, and tabulae of freedmen, navicularii, and members of the collegia of the Tiber mouth. The Foundation underwrites the appointment of two full-time epigraphers, the digitisation of squeezes preserved at the Istituto di Epigrafia, and the publication of the resulting volumes in the series Tituli Portuenses Inediti, to appear with the Quasar press of Rome.

Programma· II ·

Arcus Aquae Claudiae

Structural survey of the Aqua Claudia arcade at chilometro 14,2 of the Via Latina, between the localities of Capannelle and Roma Vecchia

Across a stretch of nine hundred and forty metres, sixty-three piers of the Claudian arcade — completed under the emperor in 52 of the Christian era and restored in part by Vespasian and Titus — present advancing micro-fissuration of the tufo and lime mortar core. The Foundation funds a photogrammetric and laser-scan campaign at sub-millimetre resolution, supplemented by ground-penetrating radar of the foundation pads, with the object of producing a structural atlas usable by the comune di Roma for any future stabilisation. No physical intervention upon the fabric is undertaken: this is a programme of measurement, drawing, and counsel only. Findings will be deposited with the Parco Archeologico dell'Appia Antica.

Programma· III ·

Triclinium Neptuni

In-situ consolidation of the polychrome mosaic of the Triclinium of Neptune — Insula V, Herculaneum, under licence of the Parco Archeologico di Ercolano

The pavement, depicting Neptune charioted by hippocampi amid a frieze of marine thiasos, was uncovered in the 1932 campaign and has since lost approximately seventeen per cent of its tessellated surface to thermal cycling and saline efflorescence drawn from the buried tuff substrate. The Foundation's grant covers the construction of a microclimatic shelter of weathering steel and lime-rendered timber, the lifting and re-bedding of nine displaced panels onto a vapour-permeable lime-pozzolana matrix, and a five-year monitoring covenant. No tessera shall be replaced; only those already separated from the bed shall be re-set, and all interventions shall remain reversible per the Carta del Restauro of 1972.

· Concerning the Selection of Works ·

The Council convenes twice per annum, on the Ides of March and the Ides of October, to deliberate upon submitted dossiers. Preference is given to projects with explicit superintendency partnership, peer-reviewable methodology, and an obligation of open publication. Direct correspondence with the Secretary may be opened by writing to the office in Rome; see Scribe Nobis.
