Established at Rome in the consulship of two private citizens, MMIX, for the safeguarding of stone, water, and inscription across the former provinces of the Empire.

Tres Operae

— The three programmes of the Foundation —

Programma I
· I ·

Epigraphia

Lapidary inscriptions are the spine of historical record. Our epigraphic programme funds the cleaning, consolidation, and photogrammetric recording of stone-cut texts from Hadrian's Wall to Lepcis Magna — from the boundary cippi of Aquileia to the tabulae of Volubilis. Each season we underwrite four field campaigns and the publication of one fascicle in the Corpus Aurelium Inscriptionum.

Programma II
· II ·

Hydraulica

Aqueducts, cisterns, nymphaea, bath-house calidaria — the hydraulic legacy of Rome remains the most fragile of her bequests. Our hydraulic programme presently supports works at the Aqua Claudia near Capannelle, the cisterna of Ptolemais in Cyrenaica, and the nymphaeum of Sagalassos. Average grant per site: thirty-eight thousand euro, awarded over three consecutive seasons.

Programma III
· III ·

Musivum

Civic mosaics — the floors of basilicas, the thresholds of guild-halls, the marine pavements of provincial baths — survive chiefly by the patient labour of conservators. We support the in-situ stabilisation of polychrome floors at Piazza Armerina, the Baths of Antoninus at Carthage, and the synagogue pavement of Hammam Lif. Annual disbursement to the mosaic chair: one hundred forty-two thousand euro.
