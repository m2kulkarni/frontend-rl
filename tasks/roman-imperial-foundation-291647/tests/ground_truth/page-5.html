Acta

Publications, technical reports, and field notebooks issued by the Fundatio Aurelia for the conservation of Roman epigraphy, hydraulic infrastructure, and civic mosaics.

MMXII — MMXXIV

The Reading Room

Each volume of the Acta gathers the season's surveys, transcriptions, and conservation memoranda. Printed in octavo on Magnani laid paper at the Tipografia Sandron of Rome; bound in quarter-vellum over travertine boards. Issued one volume per civil year.

VOL · XIII

Acta XIII

Pavimenta Civica: Conservation of the Mosaic Floors at Aquileia, Insula VI

MMXXIV Editor: Dott.ssa Livia Fontana 284 pp · 47 plates

The thirteenth volume documents three campaigns of consolidation conducted upon the marine-themed pavements of Insula VI at Aquileia, recording for the first time the full polychrome sequence of the Triton emblema after lifting and re-bedding. Appended are tessera inventories, lacuna maps at 1:20, and a revised chronology placing the workshop activity between A.D. 178 and 215.

VOL · XII

Acta XII

Rilievo idrologico dell'Aqua Marcia, settore suburbano

MMXXIII Editor: Ing. Marco Severini 312 pp · 22 fold-out plates

A complete hydrological survey of a 4.7 km suburban segment of the Aqua Marcia between Capannelle and Tor Fiscale, with manometric readings, calcareous deposit thickness measurements taken at 142 stations, and the first photogrammetric record of the opus quadratum specus interior. The volume concludes with conservation priorities for the four arch-bays at imminent structural risk.

Request Reading Copy · EUR 56

VOL · XI

Acta XI

Tabulae Castrenses: Garrison Inscriptions of Lambaesis Reconsidered

MMXXII Editor: Prof. Hadrien Boucher 248 pp · 31 plates

A re-edition of seventy-three military inscriptions from the principia and adjacent groma at Lambaesis, with new RTI imagery permitting the recovery of fourteen previously illegible cognomina and three centurial marks. Comparative tables align the corpus with the Numerus Hemesenorum diplomas of A.D. 167, prompting a revised garrison chronology.

Request Reading Copy · EUR 44

VOL · X

Acta X

Index Decennalis: A Cumulative Index to Acta I–IX

MMXXI Editor: Dott. Paolo Renzi 196 pp

A decennial index — onomastic, topographic, and material — to the first nine volumes of the Acta, prepared upon the occasion of the foundation's tenth anniversary. The work consolidates 4,712 epigraphic citations and provides concordances with the CIL, AE, and EDR databases for every inscription edited by foundation fellows since MMXII.

Request Reading Copy · EUR 36

VOL · IX

Acta IX

Tre iscrizioni inedite dalla Via Appia antica

MMXX Editor: Dott.ssa Chiara Veroli 208 pp · 18 plates

The principal contribution of this volume is the first publication of three funerary inscriptions recovered during drainage works at the IV milestone of the Via Appia in November MMXIX, including a previously unknown dedication to a freedwoman of the Caecilii Metelli. Supplementary essays treat the laser-cleaning protocol applied to two of the stones and the orthographic peculiarities of the cutter.

VOL · VIII

Acta VIII

Castellum Aquae: The Distribution Tank at Pompeii, Region VII

MMXIX Editor: Prof.ssa Renata Polverini 264 pp · 39 plates

A monographic treatment of the Porta Vesuvio castellum aquae, presenting fresh measurements of the three cisterns and recalibrated flow estimates that revise downward by 22 percent the supply figures advanced by Eschebach in 1979. The volume includes the first cross-section drawings to register the Roman repair phase identifiable from the Severan fistula stamps.

Request Reading Copy · EUR 48

VOL · VII

Acta VII

Inscriptiones Africae Proconsularis: Field Notebooks from Dougga and Bulla Regia

MMXVIII Editor: Dott. Étienne Larrieu 296 pp · 54 plates

Notebooks compiled across two seasons of survey at Dougga and Bulla Regia, transcribing 117 inscriptions encountered in situ, the great majority funerary, with squeezes deposited at the École française de Rome. A concluding chapter establishes a stone-by-stone protocol for biocidal treatment in the local microclimate.

Request Reading Copy · EUR 52

VOL · VI

Acta VI

Mosaici di Piazza Armerina: Two Conservation Memoranda

MMXVII Editor: Dott.ssa Anna Ricci 224 pp · 41 plates

Two memoranda issued at the request of the Soprintendenza of Enna, addressing salt efflorescence on the Great Hunt corridor and proposing a reversible re-fixing protocol for forty-three lifted tessellatum panels in the southern apsidal hall. The volume publishes the calibration data underlying the new humidity-control regime adopted in MMXVII.

Request Reading Copy · EUR 44

VOL · V

Acta V

De Aquaeductibus Hispaniae: Segovia, Tarragona, Mérida

MMXVI Editor: Prof. Joan Ribas i Albareda 340 pp · 28 fold-out plates

Three Iberian aqueducts surveyed under a single methodology of laser scanning combined with traditional theodolite control, allowing direct comparison of pier deformation rates over the past forty years. The Segovia chapter publishes the first complete elevation drawing accurate to ±4 mm across all 167 surviving arches.

Request Reading Copy · EUR 58

VOL · IV

Acta IV

Tituli Sepulcrales Salonitani: Funerary Inscriptions from Salona

MMXV Editor: Prof. Tomislav Brajić 252 pp · 33 plates

Sixty-eight funerary stones from the cemeteries of Manastirine and Marusinac are presented with squeeze-derived photographs, palaeographic dating, and reconstructed family stemmata. The volume corrects six readings transmitted since the Salona III fascicles of 2000 and proposes the identification of two stones long thought lost.

Request Reading Copy · EUR 42

VOL · III

Acta III

De Foro Iulii Rudiarum: A Civic Forum Re-examined

MMXIV Editor: Dott. Stefano Della Croce 188 pp · 21 plates

A reassessment of the forum at Cividale del Friuli following two seasons of geophysical prospection, identifying the curia and a hitherto-unrecognised honorific arch in the south portico. The argument rests in part on the reading of three travertine fragments first published here, two of them bearing the consular date L · CEIONIO · COMMODO · COS.

Request Reading Copy · EUR 38

VOL · II

Acta II

Tegulae signatae: A Catalogue of Stamped Roof-tiles from the Tiber Workshops

MMXIII Editor: Dott.ssa Maria Grazia Furlani 176 pp · 26 plates

A catalogue of 412 stamped tegulae and bipedales recovered from the Trastevere kiln-sites and from secondary deposition along the lower Tiber, refined by petrographic analysis and arranged by the chronology of imperial estate administration. The introduction restates the case for a Domitianic origin for the figlinae Caepionianae.

Request Reading Copy · EUR 34

VOL · I

Acta I

Liber Inauguralis: Charter, Statutes, and the Programme of the Foundation

MMXII Editor: Conte Giovanni-Battista Aurelio 144 pp · 8 plates

The inaugural volume sets out the charter of the Fundatio Aurelia, ratified at Rome on the kalends of March MMXII, together with the founding statutes governing fellowships, conservation grants, and editorial policy. An appendix reproduces the four manifesto lectures delivered by the founder before the Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei in the same year.

De Libris Petendis

On the Procurement of Printed Copies

Printed copies of any volume of the Acta may be requested by written correspondence addressed to the Office of the Editor at the foundation seat in Rome. Copies are dispatched by registered post within thirty days of receipt of payment; orders from institutional libraries enjoy a courtesy reduction of twenty percent.

Subscribers receive each new volume upon publication, accompanied by an offprint of the editor's preface bound in pompeian-red wrappers. The complete run from Acta I to Acta XIII may be acquired as a set in matched quarter-vellum binding for EUR 540, postage included within the European Union.
