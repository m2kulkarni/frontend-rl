De Hereditate

Sub umbra legis · Ente Morale · D.P.R. CCCXXII / MCMLXI

Bequests to the Fundatio Aurelia are received under the Italian framework of the ente morale di diritto privato, recognised by Decree of the President of the Republic on the 14th of March, MCMLXI, and registered with the Prefettura di Roma Capitale at register number IV-1882. The Foundation may accept testamentary dispositions, donations inter vivos, life-tenancy transfers of real property, and gifts of cultural objects, in accordance with articles 14–35 of the Italian Civil Code and the provisions of Law 222/1985.

A standard testamentary clause is available in Italian, English, French, and German upon written request to the Cancelleria. All bequests above €25.000 are entered into the present album in perpetuum; testators may elect anonymity, dedication in memoriam of a named person, or association with a particular field campaign — the aqueduct of Aspendos, the mosaics of Piazza Armerina, or the Severan epigraphic survey at Lepcis Magna.

Notarial enquiries are answered by Dott.ssa Marcella Quintili and Avv. Aulus Pomponius the younger, in office at the Foundation's Roman seat, every Tuesday and Thursday from the third hour after dawn until the eleventh.
