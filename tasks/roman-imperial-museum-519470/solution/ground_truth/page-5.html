The Annual Journal

Acta Traiani

Vol. XXVII · 2024 · edited by Marco Renzetti

Published each November since 1997, Acta Traiani gathers peer-reviewed studies on the archaeology, epigraphy, and material culture of the Imperial Fora and the broader topography of monumental Rome. The 2024 volume devotes its principal section to the Basilica Ulpia and its pavement, with twelve contributions, three excavation reports, and an extended catalogue of newly conserved inscriptions from the Forum of Trajan.

The volume is co-published with Edizioni Quasar and distributed in print and open-access digital editions. Annual subscriptions for institutions are €68; single issues €34. The editorial board welcomes submissions in Italian, English, French, and German until 15 March each year.

Porphyry & Power
in the Antonine Age

Claudia Lorenzini · xviii + 384 pp.

A long-awaited study of the imperial uses of Egyptian porphyry from Hadrian to Commodus, drawing on quarry surveys at Mons Porphyrites, unpublished archive photographs from the 1932 Fori Imperiali campaign, and the porphyry torsi held in the Antiquarium's reserve collection.

Cloth · €58

Autumn 2025 · Edizioni Quasar

The Markets of Trajan
Reconsidered

Andrea Pisani · xii + 296 pp.

A reassessment of the so-called Markets, drawing together the results of three campaigns of architectural recording (2018–2023) by the museum's laboratory together with the Soprintendenza Speciale di Roma. Eighty-four new measured drawings; appendices on brickstamps and clamp typology.

Cloth · €72

Spring 2025 · Brepols

Inscriptions of the
Forum of Nerva

F. de Angelis · viii + 212 pp.

A complete catalogue of the 137 inscribed marble fragments excavated or held in the museum store, with full epigraphic apparatus, photogrammetric plates, and a concordance with the Corpus Inscriptionum Latinarum. The first comprehensive treatment of the corpus since Lanciani.

Current Research Fellowships

Sodales Antiquarii

Three residential fellowships are awarded annually by the Curatorium of the Antiquarium Traianum.

Senior Fellowship

The Plotina Fellowship

12 months · €34,800 stipend

Awarded to one senior scholar of established reputation working on the topography or material culture of the Imperial Fora. The Fellow holds a study in the Palazzo Roccagiovine, full library access, and is expected to deliver three public lectures and a contribution to Acta Traiani.

Apply by 31 January 2025

Postdoctoral

The Apollodorus Fellowship

9 months · €22,400 stipend

For early-career scholars within five years of the doctorate, working on Roman architecture, building craft, or the history of construction. The 2024–25 holder is Dr. Sabine Vollmer (Humboldt-Universität), preparing a study of the timber centerings of the Markets of Trajan.

Apply by 15 February 2025

Doctoral

The Renzetti Studentship

6 months · €9,600 stipend

Open to doctoral candidates writing on any aspect of the late-Republican to late-antique city of Rome. Studentship includes a desk in the laboratory annexe, archive access, and accommodation in the Casa dei Borgognoni. Held twice yearly, with cohorts of two.

Apply by 1 March & 1 September

Officina Conservandi

The Conservation Laboratory

Since 2019 the Antiquarium's conservation laboratory, directed by chief conservator Beatrice Salviati, has worked in continuous partnership with the Soprintendenza Speciale di Roma on the Basilica Ulpia pavement campaign — the most ambitious lifting and re-laying of an ancient marble floor undertaken in the city since the postwar period. To date 412 square metres of giallo antico, pavonazzetto, africano and grey granite paving have been documented in situ, lifted in 1,184 numbered fragments, cleaned with controlled laser ablation, and stabilised on aerated lime mortar beds in the laboratory's east hall.

Public viewing of the campaign is permitted on Wednesday and Saturday mornings (10.00–13.00) by appointment with the laboratory office. Conservators from partner institutions — the Opificio delle Pietre Dure in Florence, the British School at Rome, and the École française de Rome — rotate through the laboratory on residencies of three to six weeks. A full technical report on the first phase of the campaign will appear as a supplement to Acta Traiani XXVIII in November 2025.
