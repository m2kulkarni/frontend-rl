Accessibility

Aditus Omnibus Patens

Step-Free Entry

Step-free admission is provided at the Largo Corrado Ricci entrance, where a ramped approach leads directly into the museum's lower atrium and to the lift serving every gallery floor. Wheelchair loan is offered without charge at the ticket desk on presentation of identification, and assistance dogs are welcome throughout the building. Tactile reproductions of selected sculpture and large-print gallery notes are available upon request from the welcome staff; induction loops are installed at every information point and within the auditorium of the Sala Ulpia. Visitors requiring sign-language interpretation may book a guided tour at least seven days in advance by writing to the visitor services office.
