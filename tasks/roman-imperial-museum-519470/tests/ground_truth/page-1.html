A Memory of Empire

Set above the foundations of the Forum of Trajan, the Antiquarium gathers marble, mosaic and inscription drawn from a century of excavation along the Via dei Fori Imperiali. Here the civic monuments of Rome — portrait and pediment, frieze and dedication — are returned, fragment by fragment, to public sight.

Special Exhibition

Marble of the Forum

Trajan to Hadrian, 98–138 CE

Forty-two carvings, drawn from the Trajanic and Hadrianic phases of the Forum, are gathered for the first time beneath a single roof — capitals from the Basilica Ulpia, a colossal foot from the equestrian Trajan, and the rediscovered cornice block known as the “frieze of the eagles.”

The exhibition follows a chronological line from the dedication of 112 to the great building works of Hadrian, with attention to workshop hands, marble provenance, and the slow drift of imperial taste from civic gravitas to Hellenizing grace.

Permanent Galleries

Twelve Rooms, One Forum

Republic to Late Antiquity

Twelve rooms set out the long story of the Imperial Forum: the Forum of Caesar and its temple of Venus Genetrix; the Augustan precinct of Mars Ultor; the bronze and marble portrait gallery of the Julio-Claudians; and the late mosaics lifted from the basilica floor in 1932.

Each room is anchored by a single monumental object, with epigraphic panels in Latin and Italian. Audio guides in English, French, German, Japanese and Spanish are issued at the atrium without charge.

Featured Object

Antinous in Porphyry

Fragmentary portrait, c. 130–138 CE

Recovered in October 1998 from a drainage trench beside the apse of the Basilica Ulpia, this porphyry head — broken at the neck, missing the right ear — is among the few imperial portraits in the imperial purple stone to survive in Rome itself.

The crystalline working of the brow and the deep undercutting of the curls place the piece firmly within the Hadrianic court workshops. It is shown beside a Pentelic marble bust of the same youth, lent on long-term deposit by the Soprintendenza Speciale di Roma.
