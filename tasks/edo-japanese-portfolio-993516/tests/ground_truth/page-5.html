Commissions & Editions 巻之五 · folio v

注文と版 On commissioning a block.

The studio accepts a small number of original commissions each year, alongside continuing editions struck from blocks already cut. Every piece — whether a chūban from the print drawer or a bespoke ōban for a private interior — passes through the same hands at the same workbench on Murasakino-dōri.

This page sets out tiers, timelines, and the customs by which we work. Read it as a colophon as much as a price list.

Tiered Pricing — 価格表

Process & Timeline — 経過

委嘱の道The patron's path.

A bespoke commission unfolds in five movements, each marked by correspondence and, where possible, a visit to the studio. Deposits are taken at signing; the balance falls due on completion of the keyblock proof.

壱

Letter of intent

weeks 1–3 · no charge

Written enquiry by post or in person. We reply with provisional sketches, a discussion of subject and scale, and a costed proposal. No obligation until the proposal is countersigned.

弐

Agreement & deposit

week 4 · 50% on signing

Half of the agreed fee is taken on signature; the studio orders cherry blocks (yamazakura) from the workshop in Tōno and reserves paper from Iwano Ichibei in Echizen. From this point the schedule is fixed.

参

Drawing & block cutting

months 2–8

The hanshita-e is drawn, pasted to the keyblock, and cut. Colour blocks follow. Patron is sent two photographs at this stage — never the proofs themselves — and may attend a single studio visit by appointment.

肆

Proofing & approval

months 9–11

A trial pull (shiken-zuri) is sent for sign-off. Adjustments to colour saturation and bokashi are made within reason. Once approved in writing, the edition is printed across consecutive working days.

伍

Sealing & delivery

months 12–14 · balance due

Each sheet is signed, numbered, and sealed. Blocks are then retired (see policy below). Delivery follows by paulownia-tube post or in-person collection at the Nishijin studio.

Shipping

発送

All prints ship rolled in handmade paulownia (kiri) tubes, interleaved with acid-free glassine and tied with hemp cord. Domestic delivery within Japan by Yamato Bin; overseas by EMS, fully insured. Allow 5–10 working days for in-stock chūban; longer for ōban during humid summer months when paper must rest before tubing.

Framing

表装

We recommend acid-free four-ply mat, hinged with Japanese mulberry tape — never adhered. Glazing should be UV-filtering, ideally museum acrylic for sheets larger than chūban. We do not frame in-house but can refer patrons to two trusted hyōgu-shi: Hosokawa Hyōgu (Teramachi) and Mizuno-dō (Kawaramachi-Marutamachi).

Reprints

再版なし

Blocks are retired at the close of every edition and are never restruck. The keyblock is marked across the face with a single sumi line and stored in the studio's wood archive; colour blocks are released to firewood or, if the patron requests, returned. There are no second printings, no posthumous editions, no licensed reproductions.

注文の方法To place an order.

Orders are taken by post or in person at the studio — never online, and not by telephone. A handwritten letter is sufficient; include subject of interest, intended scale, and a return address. Replies are sent within ten working days.

For existing editions, half the price is requested at confirmation; the balance falls due on shipment. For bespoke commissions, fifty percent is required on agreement and the remainder on the patron's approval of the trial pull.
