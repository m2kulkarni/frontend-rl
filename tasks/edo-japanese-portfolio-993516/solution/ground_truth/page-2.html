Acquiring a print
求印之事

Each impression is signed in pencil at the lower-left margin and embossed at the lower-right with the eighth-generation Yashiro seal. The colophon block (oku-zuke) on the verso records the edition number, paper-mill, and date of pulling. Editions sold out in the studio occasionally re-emerge through Tokyo Bunsendō and Kyoto Hyōgu Sasaki — please write for current trace.

Standard editions ship rolled in paulownia tubes; collector impressions on hōsho are flat-shipped between cherry boards. Domestic delivery within Japan is included; international shipping is quoted at cost.

All prices in Japanese yen, exclusive of consumption tax. Studio inquiries answered in the order received — current backlog approximately three weeks.
