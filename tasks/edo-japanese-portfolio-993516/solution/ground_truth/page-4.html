— About the Studio

Eight Generations 八代の系譜

From a Nishijin machiya whose roof tiles still bear the kiri crest of the second proprietor, the Yashiro family has cut, inked and pulled woodblock prints without interruption since the second year of Bunkyū. Six masters, two mistresses, one workshop.

— Proprietor

Yashiro Tomé,
Eighth of the Line

Born in the spring of Shōwa 51 (1976) above the workshop she now directs, Yashiro Tomé took up the smaller carving knives at fourteen under her father, the late seventh master Yashiro Kanjirō (1934 — 2018). For nine years she cut only key blocks, never colour, before being permitted to register a print of her own.

She received certification from the Kyoto Hanga Association in 2003, the year her father retired from active carving but continued at the printing bench. She assumed the family name as eighth proprietor at his death in the summer of Heisei 30 (2018), keeping the shop's two presses, four baren and the Bunkyū-era cherry-block plane that her ancestor brought from Edo.

Tomé works in the meisho-e tradition of her line, but the studio under her direction has expanded the catalogue to include night-scenes of the Kamogawa, fan-shape editions, and small private commissions of family crests — for which the shop has, since 1862, kept a register of more than four thousand kamon and their authorised cuttings.

— Recognition

Awards & Honours

  • 2017 Kyoto Prefecture Traditional Craft Recognition (京都府伝統工芸認定) — for sustained contribution to the woodblock-print craft of the Nishijin district.
  • 2009 Japan Print Association Newcomer Prize (日本版画協会新人賞) — awarded for the four-sheet edition Kamogawa, Twelve Months.
  • 2003 Certification, Kyoto Hanga Association.
— In Residence

Apprentice

Mori Hisako · fourth year of training

Mori-san entered the workshop in the autumn of Reiwa 2 (2020) following six years at the Kyoto City University of Arts. She is presently entrusted with secondary colour blocks and is registered with the Hanga Association as an apprentice in good standing.

Her training-piece, a single-block sumi-zuri view of Kitano shrine in winter, will be released as a limited edition of forty in spring 2026.
