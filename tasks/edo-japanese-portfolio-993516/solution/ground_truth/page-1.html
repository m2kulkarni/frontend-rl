Studio Note 序

Yashiro Tomé carves and prints in the Edo hanga manner — cherry block, sumi ink, hand-pulled bokashi gradients — from a machiya on Sengen-dōri in Kyoto's Nishijin quarter. The shop has stood at the same address since the second year of Bunkyū, when Yashiro Kichigorō took the signboard from his teacher and hung it above the entrance facing south.

The work is slow. A single sheet of Echizen kōzo passes the baren between five and twenty-two times, depending on the design; pigments are ground each morning in a stone suribachi; the cherry blocks come from a grove in Yoshino that the studio has used since the third generation. Visitors are received by appointment and asked to write ahead by post.

— 八代 留, eighth-generation proprietor, autumn 2024

Kamigyō Twelve Bridges

Reiwa 6 · pulled Ōkimi 14, 2024

A set of twelve ōban-yoko prints following the small bridges of the Horikawa and Kamiya canals from Kuramaguchi south to Marutamachi. Sumi keyblock with two bokashi colours — indigo for water, sayagata-cinnabar for the bridge planks. Edition of forty-eight, signed and sealed; numbered 1/48 through 48/48 with three artist's proofs retained by the studio.

Open Studio Day

The fifteenth, by appointment

15 December, Reiwa 6 · 13:00 — 17:00
