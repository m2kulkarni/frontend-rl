The Wine Programme

Curated by Anahita Vossoughi

Our cellar follows a single instinct: the wine must answer the spice. Anahita Vossoughi — sommelière, returned from seven years between Tbilisi and the Northern Rhône — has built a list of forty-eight bottles around three poles.

From the slopes above Shiraz, where the vine itself first carried the city’s name, we pour Iranian-style fortifieds and Australian Barossa Syrah descended from those cuttings. From the clay qvevri of Kakheti, amber and skin-contact whites that meet the saffron and barberry head-on. From Cornas, Côte-Rôtie, and Châteauneuf, dark, structured Syrah and Grenache that hold their own against pomegranate-glazed lamb.

Glasses from $14. Bottles from $58. Corkage, by appointment, $35.

From the Samovar

The Tea List

All teas are drawn from a hand-thrown copper samovar set on the cellar landing — charcoal-warmed, never re-boiled. Served in tulip-waisted estekan glasses with rock sugar (nabat) and a dish of dates.
