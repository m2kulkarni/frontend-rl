The House on Ash Lane

Royal-kitchen recipes, set beneath a tiled vault.

Naqsh-e Jahan opened on a quiet evening in October 2019, when chef Darius Mehrabani lit the first charcoal under a copper pot at 14 Ash Lane and served seventeen guests a single tasting drawn from his grandmother's transcriptions of the Safavid royal kitchens of Shah Abbas. The room is small — twenty-eight seats, one long communal table, a dome of cobalt and turquoise tile overhead.

What we cook is a careful, modern reading of the manuscripts: lamb with quince and barberry, saffron rice steamed in muslin, sour-cherry sherbet poured tableside, dishes finished with rosewater and sumac in the way Isfahani cooks have done for four hundred years. We change the menu with the season and the harvest, and we keep the candles low.

— Darius Mehrabani, Chef & Proprietor
