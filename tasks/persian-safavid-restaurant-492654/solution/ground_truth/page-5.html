— Reservations —

A Table Beneath the Vault

Eight seatings each evening, candles caught in the cobalt tilework. We hold every booking with care; please arrive a few minutes before your hour so the rosewater may be brought.

Book a Table

— Parties of one to eight —
— I —

Parties & Groups

Tables for one to eight guests are booked online through the panel above. For parties of nine or more — wedding lunches, corporate hosting, or festive Nowruz gatherings — please write to bookings@naqsh-saffron.co.uk with your preferred date and we will reserve a private section of the dining room.

— II —

Dietary Accommodation

Vegetarian, vegan, halal, gluten-free and nut-free menus are prepared by Chef Mehrabani when noted in your booking. Many Safavid recipes are naturally suited to such requests. Please give us 24 hours' notice for full substitution of the tasting menu.

— III —

Cancellation Window

We hold tables for fifteen minutes past the booked hour. Cancellations made within 24 hours of the sitting are charged at £25 per guest, the cost of the produce already drawn from the larder. Outside that window, cancellation is without fee.

A walk through the royal kitchens of Shah Abbas — saffron-cured trout, lamb with sour cherry, the celebrated fesenjān of pomegranate and walnut, finishing with rosewater sorbet beneath gold leaf. Wine pairing £55 supplement.

The Chef's Table

For Six · By Request

Set within the tile alcove beside the open kitchen, our chef's table seats six in the company of Chef Mehrabani himself. A bespoke nine-course menu is prepared, drawn from his archive of Isfahani manuscripts, served from copper trays at the table.

Reserve via bookings@naqsh-saffron.co.uk — three weeks ahead.
