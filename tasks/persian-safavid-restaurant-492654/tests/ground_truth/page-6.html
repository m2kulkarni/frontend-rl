Access & Welcome

Naqsh-e Jahan was designed to be as open as the courtyards that inspired it. The threshold from Ash Lane is step-free, and our staff are happy to assist any guest who would like a quieter table or a hand to the cellar. If we can prepare anything in advance for your visit, please write ahead.

Step-free Entry

A level threshold from Ash Lane leads directly into the dining room and the courtyard fountain.

Accessible WC

A fully accessible washroom is located on the ground floor beside the tea room passage.

Induction Loop

An induction loop is fitted at the bar; please ask any host to switch to the seat above it.

Finding the Door

Ash Lane runs north from Guilford Street, three minutes on foot from Russell Square station. Look for the cobalt tile above number 41 — a single eight-pointed star marks the threshold.

The 59, 68 and 168 buses stop at Russell Square; the 91 and 168 stop at Southampton Row. Bicycle parking is available at the corner of Lamb's Conduit Street.

For taxis, ask the driver for the corner of Ash Lane and Guilford Street; the lane itself is pedestrian after six in the evening.
