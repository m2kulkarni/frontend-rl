The Carte · Spring & Summer 1403

The Menu of the
Royal Kitchens

Chef Darius Mehrabani's reading of the Safavid table — sourced from the recipe codices of Shah Abbas's pastry-keeper and reinterpreted under our tiled vault. Service is à la carte; a five-course tasting (£74) follows the same axis from mezze to sweets.

Mezze & Cold Plates

Mokhalafat · Pīsh-ghazā

Mast-o Khiar

£9

Strained sheep's-milk yoghurt folded with cucumber from the Vale of Evesham, dried Damask rose petal, walnut, and a thread of mountain mint.

ماست و خیار

Kashk-e Bademjān

£12

Smoked aubergine pounded with whey-curd kashk, caramelised shallot, dried mint bloomed in saffron oil. Served warm with sangak.

کشک بادمجان

Borāni Esfenāj

£10

Wilted spinach with garlic confit and yoghurt, after the version recorded in the kitchen rolls of Mirza Khan, 1597.

بورانی اسفناج

Salad-e Shirazi

£8

Diced cucumber, tomato, and red onion dressed with verjuice and lime-omani; finished with summer purslane and crushed sumac.

سالاد شیرازی

Nān-o Panir-o Sabzi

£11

A board of warm sangak, aged Lighvan brine cheese, walnuts, basil, tarragon, radish, and a single confit fig from Yazd.

نان و پنیر و سبزی

Mirzā Ghāsemi

£11

Caspian-style smoked aubergine with crushed garlic, tomato, free-range egg, and a slick of saffron-infused butter.

میرزا قاسمی

From the Hearth

Az Ātash · Kabāb-khāneh

Jujeh Kabāb-e Za'farāni

£24

Corn-fed poussin, marinated forty-eight hours in saffron, lime, and yoghurt; grilled over charcoal of Persian oak. Served with grilled tomato and barberry-butter rice.

جوجه کباب زعفرانی

Kabāb-e Barg

£32

Aberdeen Angus fillet, hand-pounded thin, marinated with onion juice and saffron. Cooked four minutes a side. Sumac on the rim of the plate.

کباب برگ

Māhi-ye Sefid

£29

Whole Cornish sea bass roasted in a salt crust scented with dried lime; lifted at the table and served with herb-rice and a green walnut relish.

ماهی سفید

Kabāb Koobideh

£22

Two skewers of hand-minced Welsh lamb shoulder and grass-fed beef, pressed onto flat skewers and grilled over white charcoal.

کباب کوبیده

Shishlik-e Shāndizi

£34

A rack of milk-fed lamb from the Brecon Beacons, marinated in saffron and onion, finished with sour-cherry molasses. After the Khorasani manner.

شیشلیک شاندیزی

Kabāb-e Bakhtiyāri

£27

Skewered chicken thigh and beef fillet, alternated and basted with saffron butter. The "tribesman's skewer" of Isfahan's western tablelands.

کباب بختیاری

Rice & Stews

Polō · Khoresht

Fesenjān of Norfolk Duck

£28

Slow-braised wild Norfolk duck leg in a six-hour sauce of toasted walnut and reduced pomegranate molasses from Saveh. Served with chelō and saffron-soaked tahdig.

فسنجان اردک

Tahchin of Saffron Rice

£26

A cake of saffron-yoghurt rice baked over confit chicken thigh, turned out at the table to reveal a crisp golden crust strewn with barberries and slivered almond.

ته‌چین

Khoresht-e Gheymeh

£23

Diced lamb shoulder and yellow split pea simmered with dried lime and tomato; crowned with shoestring potatoes fried in clarified butter.

خورشت قیمه

Khoresht-e Ghormeh Sabzi

£24

Lamb neck simmered with red kidney beans and a fistful of fenugreek, parsley, coriander, and chive — sautéed until almost black, in the old Tabrizi method.

قورمه سبزی

Bāqalā Polō bā Māhicheh

£29

Dill and broad bean rice with a slow-roasted lamb shank, the bone glazed in its own saffron jus. The dish of Nowruz, served all year.

Zereshk Polō

£21

Long-grain rice scattered with sugar-glazed barberries and saffron, served with a bone-in chicken leg poached in cardamom and rose.
