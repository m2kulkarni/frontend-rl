Reaching Kumbakonam

Kumbakonam lies on the old Tanjore–Tiruchi line, watered by the Kāveri. Trains from Chennai Egmore halt here twice each evening; buses run continually from the Tanjore stand. Three principal approaches:

  • From Tanjore· 40 km southwest ·
    One hour by road along NH-36 via Papanasam. Town buses depart the Tanjore New Bus Stand every twenty minutes between 5 a.m. and 9 p.m. Hire-cars from the railway junction quote ₹900–1,200 one way. The river is crossed twice.
  • From Tiruchirapalli· 90 km west ·
    Two hours by road via Lalgudi and Thiruvaiyāru, or by passenger train on the Tiruchi–Mayiladuthurai section, alighting at Kumbakonam Junction. From the station, an auto-rickshaw to Sannidhi Street is ₹60; ten minutes' walk through the bazaar suffices for the unhurried.
  • From Chennai· 280 km via Villupuram ·
    Six hours by the Cholan Express or the overnight Tiruchendur Express, both from Egmore via the Villupuram line. By road, the East Coast highway as far as Pondicherry, then inland through Chidambaram and Mayiladuthurai. Alight at Kumbakonam Junction; the studio is a five-minute walk west.

A Note on Correspondence

The proprietor does not keep electronic mail. Patrons, scholars, and temple committees are asked to write by post, or to telephone in the working hours given above. Letters are answered in the proprietor's own hand on the workshop's letter-paper, sealed with the Atelier's vermilion stamp. Drawings, when requested, follow under separate cover by registered post.
