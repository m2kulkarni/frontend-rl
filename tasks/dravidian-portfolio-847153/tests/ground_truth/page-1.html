Drawings, maquettes, and stone for temple trusts across Tamil Nadu and the Kaveri delta.

· The Studio ·

Atelier Sthapati is the working name of the workshop of R. Kalyanasundaram Sthapati, fourth-generation of the Tiruvidaimarudur lineage of temple craftsmen — a house that has set chisel to stone for the trustees of the Kaveri delta since the latter days of the Tanjore principality. From a yard at 14, Sannidhi Street in Kumbakonam, the studio prepares measured drawings, palmwood and clay maquettes, and finished granite and Krishna-shila work in the Chola–Vijayanagara idiom, in accordance with the Mānasāra and the Mayamata, for sthala-temples, maṭhas, and private patrons.

The Proprietor

R. Kalyanasundaram Sthapati
Fourth of the Tiruvidaimarudur Lineage

Born to the late Sri R. Rāmaswāmi Sthapati in the Tamil month of Maśi, 1957, the proprietor took up the chisel under his father at the age of fourteen and the rule and pencil under Sri V. Ganapati Sthapati at the Government College of Architecture and Sculpture, Mahābalipuram, between 1975 and 1978.

He has since served as principal śilpi to the Hindu Religious & Charitable Endowments Department on works at Tiruvārūr, Tirubhuvanam, and Swāmimalai, and consults privately for trusts at Madurai, Coimbatore, and Pondicherry. He keeps a single apprentice and three full-time stonecutters, and accepts no more than four large commissions in a calendar year.
