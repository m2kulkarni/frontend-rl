Folio II · Karma-Patrika

Selected Works · 1994 — 2024

Three decades of drafting, supervision, and execution in the Chola–Vijayanagara idiom — temples, mandapas, gopurams, and stand-alone sculpture, recorded by Tamil-month and patron, with notes on stone, plaster, and pigment.

Twelve Karma · Granite · Plaster · Pigment · Brass-pin

D — measured drawings, iconographic plates, paper templates supplied to a third-party śilpī.
Drafting & Supervision
D+S — drawings plus weekly site visits, mason corrections, dimension-stone approval.
Full Execution
F — chisel-to-consecration: studio carves on-site, kalasha set under our hand.
Patron-Inscribed
★ — the patron's name is recorded in the completed inscription panel.
