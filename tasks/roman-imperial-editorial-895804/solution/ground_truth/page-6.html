Subscribe

Acta Diurna is read by classicists, archaeologists, museum trustees, and private students of antiquity in twenty-eight countries. Our quarterly is sustained almost entirely by subscribers and patrons. Choose a tier below; your name shall be entered into the colophon of issue XLI.

— I —

Reader

$48/yr
payable in one annual instalment
  • Four print issues per year, mailed in linen-wrap (Spring · Summer · Autumn · Saturnalia)
  • Letterpress index sheet for the year, suitable for binding
  • Reader's email despatch with corrigenda & reading list
  • Access to the public Forum web archive

Patron

most chosen · best value for scholars
  • All Reader benefits, with print copies upgraded to numbered Patron edition
  • Full digital archive of issues I–XL, searchable, with downloadable PDFs of every essay
  • Annual portfolio print: a hand-pulled intaglio plate by a guest engraver, mailed each December
  • Patron's correspondence — quarterly letter from the editors, not published online
  • Pre-publication access to long reads two weeks before press
