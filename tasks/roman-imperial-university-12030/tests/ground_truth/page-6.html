Aula Magna

The Great Hall · Coffered Vault MCMXXVIII

The principal ceremonial chamber of the University, completed in 1928 by the architect Marcello Severi after the model of the Pantheon. Its coffered vault, gilded oculus, and marble floor of opus sectile remain among the finest examples of Italian neoclassical interior in Rome.

Tours depart from the Porta Aquilana and proceed through the Cortile dei Filosofi to the Hall, returning by way of the Loggia Vitruviana.

Directions to the Campus

I.

From Roma Termini

Take Metro Line B (Laurentina direction) two stops to Cavour. From Cavour, proceed on foot south-west along Via dei Serpenti and Via dei Fori Imperiali for approximately twelve minutes to Via dei Fori Severiani.

Approx. 18 min · €1.50

II.

From Fiumicino Airport

Board the Leonardo Express to Roma Termini (32 min, hourly), then connect as above. Alternatively, the FL1 regional service to Roma Tiburtina permits a direct Metro B connection to Cavour.

Approx. 70 min · €14.00

III.

By Foot from the Forum

From the Arch of Titus, follow Via di San Bonaventura eastward to Largo Corrado Ricci; cross into Via dei Fori Severiani. The Porta Aquilana is the iron gate marked by twin marble eagles at number XIV.

Approx. 8 min on foot

Sedes · The Address

The Porta Aquilana

The campus of the Universitas Aquilana occupies a single contiguous block bounded by the Via dei Fori Severiani to the north, the ancient wall of the Suburra to the east, and the gardens of the Palazzo Cornaro to the south. Visitors arrive at the Porta Aquilana, an iron gate flanked by twin Carrara-marble eagles sculpted by Pietro Canonica in 1931.

The Porter's Lodge, immediately within the gate, offers printed maps, audio devices in six languages, and complimentary cloakroom service. A small caffè under the eastern colonnade serves espresso and pastries from 07:30 until 19:00 on weekdays.

Accessus · Accessibility

Provisions for Visitors

The University is committed to welcoming all guests. The principal historic buildings have been adapted with discretion, preserving their architectural integrity while providing dignified access for visitors with reduced mobility.

  • Step-free entrance via the Porta Carracci on the western flank, opened upon request at the Porter's Lodge.
  • Lifts to all floors of the Aula Magna and the Library of the Pantheon; tactile maps available at reception.
  • Induction loops fitted in the Aula Magna and the Sala dei Codici; large-print itineraries on request.
  • Service animals welcomed throughout the campus, including reading rooms.
  • Reserved seating for guided tours; please notify the Office of Public Engagement at least 48 hours in advance.

Closures & Festal Days

The campus is closed to public visitors on the Feast of Saints Peter and Paul (29 June), Ferragosto (15 August), and from 23 December to 6 January inclusive. Tours do not operate during graduation week (typically the second week of July, dates announced annually). Confirmation by post or telephone is recommended for travellers from afar.
