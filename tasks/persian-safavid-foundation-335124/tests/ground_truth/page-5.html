Programmes Annual Cycle 1403 / 2024–25

Grants & Fellowships
for the Conservation of Safavid Arts

Three competitive awards supporting tile conservators, manuscript scholars, and field researchers working within the dominions of the old Isfahan school.

An Endowment in Service of the Persian Arts

Since 1998 the Foundation has administered three annual programmes intended to renew the disciplines of Safavid material conservation. Awards are conferred by an independent jury and disbursed through the Trustees' office in Isfahan. All applications for the 1403 cycle must be received not later than 30 November.

The Sotoudeh Fellowship

€18,000

For early-career tile conservators undertaking a twelve-month residency on a Safavid-era haft-rang or kāshī-i muʿarraq programme. Two fellowships awarded annually.

Eligibility
Practitioners ≤ 7 years post-qualification
Term
1 March 1403 – 28 February 1404
Deadline
30 November (postmark)
Host sites
Masjid-i Shaykh Lutfullah · Imamzadeh Harun-i Vilayat

The Whitcombe–Pirzad Manuscript Travel Award

€7,500

Awarded to scholars consulting illuminated manuscripts of the Isfahan and Qazvin schools held in libraries of Tehran, Mashhad, Dublin, and Istanbul. Five awards annually.

Eligibility
Doctoral candidates & independent scholars
Disciplines
Codicology · paleography · pigment analysis
Deadline
30 November
Notification
15 February (each cycle)

The Maidan Field Grant

€12,000

For site-based research and survey work in Isfahan province — courtyards, qanats, garden pavilions, and the Chaharbāgh corridor. Three grants annually, including stipend & travel.

Eligibility
Architectural historians · landscape archaeologists
Term
Up to 9 months in country
Deadline
30 November
Co-funded with
Isfahan Cultural Heritage Office

The Judging Panel Chaired by Prof. Layla Mostofi, Tehran University

The independent jury convenes each January at the Foundation's office on the Chaharbāgh-e Bālā. All decisions are final; the Trustees do not enter into correspondence concerning unsuccessful applications. Members serve in rotating three-year terms.

Application Checklist

All materials in PDF, in English or Persian, posted or e-mailed to the Trustees by 30 November.

Required Documents

  1. Letter of intention, ≤ 1,200 words, signed.addressed to the Secretary, Bagh-e Fin Foundation
  2. Curriculum vitae with full dates of training and posts held.
  3. Two letters of reference from senior practitioners or supervisors.
  4. Project outline (max. 8 pages) with methodology and timetable.
  5. Detailed budget itemised in Euros, including indirect costs.
  6. Copy of academic credentials or guild certification.

Supplementary Materials

  1. Portfolio of recent conservation work (10–15 plates).Sotoudeh Fellowship applicants only
  2. List of manuscripts to be consulted, with shelfmarks.Whitcombe–Pirzad Award applicants only
  3. Letter of permission from site authority or library.
  4. Statement on ethical handling and reproduction rights.
  5. Plan for dissemination — lecture, paper, or report.
  6. Signed declaration of no conflicting funding above 30%.

Inquiries should be directed to the Secretary's office. The Foundation regrets that, owing to the volume of submissions, individual feedback cannot be furnished.
