“The Foundation shall acquire only those objects whose passage from the workshop, the shrine, or the bazaar can be traced with documentary certainty; preference is given to fragments orphaned by the restorations of 1932 and 1958, returned here to be studied, stabilised, and shown without ornament of their loss.”

— Acquisitions Policy, ratified 14 Mehr 1368 / 6 October 1989

Access & Reading-Room Procedures

The full study collection comprises forty-eight registered objects. Folios, textiles, and cartoons are held in a controlled-environment vault at the Foundation's Isfahan office and may be consulted by appointment in the Bagh-e Fin reading room, Tuesdays through Thursdays, 9.00–13.00. Tile fragments BFF-2003-014 through BFF-2008-058 are on permanent display in the south gallery; high-resolution facsimiles of all manuscript folios are available without restriction.

Endowment Notice

The Foundation operates upon a perpetual endowment established 1989 and replenished by the Hosseini-Bahrami bequest of 2004. No object in the study collection is held for sale or trade. Conservation services are offered without fee to public shrines of the three named provinces.
