Plan Your Visit

Three short notes for those who would call upon the reading room

By Appointment

Readers, scholars, and conservation colleagues are received in the reading room only after written request. Please write at least fourteen days in advance, naming the manuscripts, tile fragments, or archival folders you wish to consult.

How to Arrive

The reading room sits two minutes' walk south-east of the Si-o-se-pol bridge, behind the small courtyard at the head of Khaqani Street. Look for the cobalt-tiled lintel and the brass plate marked simply Bagh-e Fin.

In the Reading Room

Pencils only; soft cotton gloves are provided for tile and manuscript handling. Photography is permitted without flash. The garden tea-room beyond the iwan is open to readers between sittings, with cardamom tea served at eleven and three.

Press & Loan Inquiries

For exhibition loans, image rights to the tile fragments held at Ardabil, and press, please write to Mme. Shirin Mortazavi-Khanjani, Registrar, at registrar@baghefin-foundation.ir. Replies are issued within ten working days.

Practical Notes for Visitors

Nearest Stop

Si-o-se-pol metro station, Line 1, exit B. From the bridge, walk south-east along the embankment for two minutes.

Wheelchair Access

The ground-floor reading room and tea-room are level. The upper tile study is reached only by stair; please notify us in advance and we will bring folios down.

Languages

Reading-room staff converse in Persian, English, and French. German correspondence is answered through the London office.

Donations & Endowments

Gifts to the conservation programme may be directed to the Trustees through Coutts & Co., London. Receipts are issued by the Registrar.

Endowment Notice

The Foundation is sustained by the Bagh-e Fin Endowment, established 1998 in Isfahan, and by gifts from the Hartshorn Trust of London. Audited accounts are deposited annually with the Iranian Cultural Heritage Organisation and the Charity Commission for England and Wales.
