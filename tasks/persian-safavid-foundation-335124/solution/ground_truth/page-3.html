Field Register · 1403 / 2024–25

Conservation Projects

A working catalogue of six active interventions undertaken by foundation conservators and partner ateliers across the cities of Isfahan, Kashan, and Ardabil — each entry comprises locality, lead conservator, commencement, and a brief scope of works.

Six Sites · Three Cities · Four Disciplines

Active Fieldwork, Cycle of 1403

Each project below is overseen by a foundation-appointed lead conservator working alongside the Iranian Cultural Heritage, Handicrafts and Tourism Organisation. Reports are filed quarterly to the Trustees in Isfahan.

· Project No. I ·

Sheikh Lotfollah Inner-Dome Stabilization

Naqsh-e Jahan Square, Isfahan
Lead
Mehrnoush Tabrizi
Began
March 2023

A campaign of micro-grouting and seismic monitoring addresses hairline fissures along the dome's lemon-yellow lozenge field, identified during the 2022 ground-survey. Phase II will reseat thirty-seven displaced haft-rang tiles using lime-pozzolan mortars cured to nineteenth-century specification.

· Project No. II ·

Imam Mosque Iwan Tile Re-Cutting

Masjed-e Shah, Isfahan
Lead
Paolo Sereni
Began
September 2022

The southern iwan's spandrel mosaics, weathered by sixty winters of frost-cycling, are being matched and replaced piece-by-piece by the foundation's Kashan kiln using cobalt and turquoise glazes formulated from period recipes. Each replacement bears a discreet incised mark on its reverse to distinguish modern intervention from Safavid original.

· Project No. III ·

Chehel Sotoun Mirror-Ceiling Cleaning

Chehel Sotoun Pavilion, Isfahan
Lead
Ladan Esfandiari
Began
January 2024

Conservators are removing nineteenth-century shellac varnishes that have darkened the audience-hall's faceted mirror vault, working in eighty-centimetre grids using cotton swabs and a buffered solvent gel. The treatment recovers the original silver flash that animated wall-paintings of the Shah Abbas court receptions below.

· Project No. IV ·

Ardabil Shrine Carpet Documentation

Sheikh Safi al-Din Khānegāh, Ardabil
Lead
Reza Khorrami-Manesh
Began
May 2023

A non-invasive photogrammetric record is being made of the surviving knot-by-knot fragments housed in the shrine's museum wing, complementing the V&A and LACMA holdings of the dispersed pair. Fibre samples taken with curatorial consent are being analysed at the Tehran Polymer Institute for dye-source identification.

· Project No. V ·

Ali Qapu Music-Room Muqarnas Survey

Ali Qapu Palace, Upper Storey, Isfahan
Lead
Catherine Verhaegen
Began
October 2023

A laser-scan and stratigraphic study of the celebrated stucco music-room records the niches and instrument-shaped cut-outs ahead of any consolidation. Findings will be published jointly with the Isfahan Higher Institute of Architecture in autumn 1404, and will inform the long-debated question of polychrome reinstatement.

· Project No. VI ·

Bagh-e Fin Hydraulic Restoration

Bagh-e Fin Garden, Kashan
Lead
Hossein Mokhtari-Yazdi
Began
April 2022

The garden's qanāt-fed channels and pressurised cascades — original to Shah Tahmasp's commission of 1574 — are being relined in traditional saruj rather than concrete, restoring the audible register of the runnels. Work follows a hydrological model commissioned from Sharif University and is now in its concluding cypress-allée phase.

Endowment Notice

The Foundation operates upon a perpetual endowment established 2007 by private bequest, augmented by the Aga Khan Trust for Culture and the Barakat Foundation. No state monies are received; all conservation works are undertaken at the invitation of site custodians.
