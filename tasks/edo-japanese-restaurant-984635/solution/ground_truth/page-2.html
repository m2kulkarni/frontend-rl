令和六年 神無月 十二日 Reiwa 6 · Kannazuki · 12
Saturday, October 12
Hand-set by the okami at dawn.

本日のお品書 Honjitsu no
O-shinagaki.

Seven courses, set out in the order the kitchen serves them, drawn from this morning's market at Tsukiji and the Tama river fishermen who come to our gate before five. The list changes by the day; what is written here will be served tonight only. Reservations close at four in the afternoon.

On Reading the List

Each course is named first in kanji as it appears on the kitchen slip, then in roman letters for our guests from abroad. A one-line note records the river or hill from which the principal ingredient was taken this morning.

Sake pairings (three pours, 2,800 yen) are chosen by the okami after the second course and brought without announcement.

Total set price · 22,400 yen
Service · 10%
Tax included
一

Sakizuke 先附

Vinegared kohada with kikurage, dressed in a thin tosa-zu and bedded on a single shiso leaf.

— Kohada landed before dawn at Tsukiji; kikurage from the Chichibu hills.

2,200 円
二

Suimono 吸物

Clear matsutake broth, with a single yuzu peel and a sliver of seasonal hamo.

— Matsutake from Tamba; first picking of the season, arrived October 9.

2,800 円
三

Mukōzuke 向附

Tai sashimi, four slices, with fresh-grated wasabi and a drop of murasaki shōyu.

— Tai from the Sagami bay; wasabi grated to order from Izu, Amagi-yama.

3,400 円
四

Takiawase 炊合せ

Simmered satoimo and ginnan, finished with a brushing of yuzu and a thread of katsuo.

— Satoimo from Saitama, Kawagoe; ginnan gathered at Yushima Tenjin.

2,600 円
五

Yakimono 焼物

Salt-grilled ayu, whole, set on a bamboo leaf, with tade-zu vinegar in a small dish.

— Ayu from the Tama river above Ōme; brought to the gate at half past four.

3,800 円
六

Gohan 御飯

New-rice with chestnut, simmered in donabe, served with red miso shiru and three pickles.

— Shinmai from Niigata, Uonuma; chestnuts from Tanba, peeled by hand at noon.

2,400 円
七

Mizumono 水物

Persimmon and kuzukiri, with a thin black-sugar syrup and a leaf of mint.

— Persimmon from Gifu, fuyu variety; kuzu from Yoshino, Nara.

2,200 円
