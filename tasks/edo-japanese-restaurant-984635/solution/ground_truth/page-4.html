Shun no Kondate

旬の献立

Four kaiseki courses, set to the four seasons of the Kanda riverbank. Each menu is composed weekly from the recipe books of the Kiritsubo house — handed down since the Bunsei era — and adjusted to what the morning's deliveries allow.

The dishes named below are those presently in rotation. Substitutions are made silently when the river or the weather requires.

¥18,000 — ¥32,000 / per person

春Haru · Spring

Sakizuke & Mukōzuke

  • 先附Wakatake-ni — fresh takenoko from Rakusai, Kyōto, simmered with wakame in first-pressed dashi
  • 向附Sakura-tai no kobujime — Akashi sea bream cured between sheets of Rausu kombu, plum vinegar
  • 椀Hamaguri to nanohana no suimono — clam and rape-blossom clear broth
  • 焼物Sawara no saikyō-yaki — Spanish mackerel in white miso of Saikyō, charcoal-grilled
  • 飯Sakura-meshi — rice steamed with cherry blossoms preserved in salt
Served 3rd Day, 3rd Month — 25th Day, 4th Month
(Mar 3 — Apr 25)

¥22,000per guest, ten courses

  • 水菓子Kuzu-kiri — arrowroot ribbons in dark kuromitsu

A note on the kondate

Each course is composed by the third-generation proprietor, Tachibana Mosuke, in the manner set down in the Kiritsubo recipe book of Bunsei 8 (1825) and continued, with revisions, into the Meiji period. Where the old text calls for fish no longer drawn from the Kanda river, we substitute from coastal markets — Toyosu in the dawn, Kanda by the breakfast bell.

Allergies, dietary observances, and abstentions are accommodated when notice is given at the time of reservation. Please see Reservations & Private Zashiki.
