Kiritsubo stands a short walk from Awajichō, on the south bank of the Kanda river, where a single paper lantern marks the lane after dusk.

Kanda-Sudachō · Chiyoda-ku · Tōkyō

2-chōme 14-7,
Kanda-Sudachō — the south-bank lane.

Our gate is set back from the street by twelve paces, marked only by a noren of indigo linen and the small lantern that hangs from the eave. There is no signboard at the road; if you reach the bridge, you have walked one house too far west.

The river is loud here in summer and altogether silent in winter. We keep the lattice door slid open from a quarter-hour before each service so that guests may step in at their leisure.

Michi-annai · From Awajichō Station

Four minutes along the south bank.

  1. Take Exit A3. From the Marunouchi Line at Awajichō, ascend through Exit A3. You will emerge facing the willow at the corner of Sotobori-dōri.
  2. Turn east toward the river. Walk two short blocks along the south side of Sotobori-dōri. The cooper's shop with the red shutter is your landmark; pass it on your left.
  3. Follow the south bank. At the second crossing, turn left onto the narrow lane that traces the Kanda river. Maples line the embankment for some sixty paces.
  4. Stop at the plum tree. Where the lane bends, an old white plum overhangs the fence. Our small lantern hangs in its shadow. Slide the lattice door to your right.
提

“Follow the lantern past the plum tree.”

— inscribed on the small andon at our entrance, in the hand of the fourth proprietor, ca. Meiji 12.
