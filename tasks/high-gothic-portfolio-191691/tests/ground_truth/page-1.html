Guillaume & his Lodge
cutters of stone · leaders of glass · gilders of iron

In the workshop at Sens, by the grace of God and the favour of the Chapter, we cut stone, lead glass, and gild iron for the cathedrals of Champagne and the Île-de-France since the year of Our Lord MCCLXXXVII. Our compass and square pass from master to apprentice in unbroken line; our hand answers to the bishop, the chapter, and the saints alone.

The Three Portals

·MCCXCIV·
Works in Stone

Tracery for the chevet of Saint-Étienne, vault-bosses for the Lady Chapel at Provins, and the rose-armature of Notre-Dame de Sens — three-and-thirty commissions in limestone of Vernon and tufa of Champagne, each cut to the master's drawing upon the tracing-floor.

·MCCCI·
Works in Glass

Cobalt struck from the kilns of Saint-Denis, madder boiled in the abbey vats, and lead drawn at our own bench — the windows of the south transept at Troyes and four lancets for the bishop's oratory, each came-line set by hand and fired to the seventh hour.

enter the glaziers' bench
·MCCCVIII·
The Master's Hand

Drawings from the tracing-floor in red ochre and silverpoint: the geometry of pointed arches, the setting-out of quatrefoils, the proportions of the lodge after Villard de Honnecourt — kept under lock in the cedar chest, here shown by leave of the Chapter.

enter the tracing-floor

Of the Lodge at Sens

The atelier is housed against the north flank of the cathedral close at Sens, between the canons' garden and the rue de la Fonderie. Within its compass labour seven journeymen, three apprentices, a glazier of Champagne, and the master himself — God preserve him.
