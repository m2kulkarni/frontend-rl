Works in Stone

Tracery, Vaulting, & Bar-work — Anno Domini MCCXC – MCCC

Herein are inscribed the six commissions undertaken by this lodge in cut stone, from the lancets of Saint-Étienne de Sens to the vault-ribs of lesser parishes upon the Yonne. Each work is sealed by the year of its erection, with mention of patron, quarry, and the moulding-profiles employed.

MCCXCI

The North Transept Lancets

at Saint-Étienne de Sens

Three lancets of unequal height pierce the north wall of the transept, the central rising to nine toises and crowned by a sixfoil oculus. The bar-tracery was set in February of the Year of Our Lord twelve hundred ninety and one, after a winter spent in the cutting-shed at the foot of the apse. Each light is divided by a slender mullion of three-quarter round, the cusps of the trefoiled heads meeting at a small carved bossage of vine-leaf.

Patron
Chapter of Saint-Étienne, Archbishop Estienne Bécard presiding
Year
MCCXCI (set Feb. – Lent)
Stone
Tonnerre limestone, fine bed; quoins in Conflans chalk
Fee
iiij c lvij livres tournois, payable in three terms

Technique — Cusping cut to the half-round with sunk spandrel; outer moulding a triple roll-and-fillet, inner a double ogee. The mullion-bases rest upon a continuous chamfered string, the better to shed rain into the gutter of the transept roof.

MCCXCIV

The Triforium Screen

at Notre-Dame de Soissons

Eleven bays of triforium were cut, each comprising two pointed lights beneath a quatrefoil, the whole bound by a horizontal string-course of dog-tooth and small carved heads at the springers. Travel was made from Sens to Soissons in the autumn of the Year of Grace twelve hundred ninety-three, and the screen was raised in the months of June and July following. The work supplanted an earlier wooden balustrade thought beyond reasonable repair.

Patron
Bishop Gérard de Montcornet & the Cathedral Chapter
Year
MCCXCIV (raised summer)
Stone
Conflans chalk for the screen, Tonnerre for the colonnettes
Fee
vij c xx livres parisis, with carriage

Technique — Cusping pierced clear through, after the new manner; mouldings reduced to a single keel-and-hollow on the chamber side, that the eye be not confounded when read against the dimmer gallery beyond. Each colonnette is en délit, of one stone with its capital.

MCCXCVIII

Bar Tracery for the Chapter House

at the Abbey of Pontigny

A central rose of twelve foils, set within a square frame of pierced quatrefoils, lights the eastern wall of the chapter house. Below it, four tall lancets of equal height carry the daily light to the brethren's stalls. The work was undertaken at the request of Abbot Pierre de Toucy and concluded on the Vigil of Saint Martin, MCCXCVIII, with the tracery-cutter himself attending the placement of the central boss.

Patron
Abbot Pierre de Toucy, Cistercian community of Pontigny
Year
MCCXCVIII (set autumn, completed XI Nov.)
Stone
Tonnerre limestone, hard upper-bed selected
Fee
v c lxxxiv livres tournois; ten muids of grain in lieu of carriage

Technique — The rose is bar-cut, not plate; cusps formed by chiselling away the spandrels after assembly, that the foils show clean against the southern light. Mouldings are restrained, as befits the Order: a single hollow chamfer on the outer face, a wave moulding within.

MCCXCV

Rib-Vault of the South Aisle

Église Saint-Pierre-le-Vif, Villeneuve

Four bays of quadripartite vaulting were raised over the south aisle of this small parish, replacing a wooden ceiling lost to fire on the Eve of the Annunciation, MCCXCIV. The ribs spring from corbels carved with the heads of the Four Evangelists, each in its own attitude — Mark with his book half-closed, Luke with the ox at his shoulder. The keystones are of the simplest sort, plain rounds with painted star-fields.

Patron
Curé Jehan Bouchart and the parish of Villeneuve-l'Archevêque
Year
MCCXCV (centring struck at Whitsun)
Stone
Conflans chalk for webbing, Tonnerre for ribs & corbels
Fee
ij c xij livres tournois, with masons' lodgings provided

Technique — Diagonal ribs of the keel-and-fillet profile, transverse arches plain chamfered. The webbing laid in courses parallel to the wall-rib, after the manner taught by the elder masons of Auxerre. No tas-de-charge employed; the ribs spring independently from each corbel.

MCCXCVI

West Window of Saint-Savinien

parish church beyond the walls of Sens

Two pointed lancets surmounted by a circle of six foils, set within a single great pointed arch — the simplest of the geometric fashion now coming up from the Île-de-France. The window was paid for by the bequest of Dame Aalis de Marsangis, late of this parish, and her arms (a chevron between three escallops) are carved upon the spandrel within. The glazing was executed separately by the master Renaud le Verrier of Auxerre.

Patron
Bequest of Dame Aalis de Marsangis (d. MCCXCV)
Year
MCCXCVI (consecrated XV August)
Stone
Tonnerre limestone throughout
Fee
lxxij livres tournois, plus xij sous for the carved arms

Technique — Plate-tracery in the ancient manner has been wholly abandoned; the foils are cut from bars set to the radius of the circle. Mouldings of two orders only, the outer a roll-and-hollow, the inner a single bowtell — restraint suited to a parish work.

MCCXCIX

Sedilia & Piscina, Notre-Dame-du-Pré

at Joigny upon the Yonne

Three sedilia of graduated height, with a piscina to the south, were cut into the chancel wall of this small church on the river. Each seat is recessed beneath its own crocketed gable, the gables linked by a continuous string of ball-flower. The work occupied the lodge through the late autumn of MCCXCIX, the masons sheltering against early frost beneath canvas stretched between the choir and the temporary roof.

Patron
Sire Guillaume de Joigny, lord of the manor; with the curé Robert Beaufils
Year
MCCXCIX (concluded eve of St. Andrew)
Stone
Conflans chalk, soft enough for fine crocketing
Fee
xxxviij livres tournois & six aunes of fustian

Technique — Crockets of the early curled-leaf type, not yet the writhing form coming into fashion. Cusping of the seat-heads is deeply undercut, that the shadow read clearly from the body of the church. The piscina drain is cut through a single block, set with a leaden pipe.
