Guillaume de Sens, the Younger

— born at Vézelay circa Anno Domini MCCLXII, freed of the Lodge of Saint-Denis in the year MCCLXXXIV, master at Sens since the feast of Saint-Étienne, MCCLXXXVII.

Guillaume the younger was born in the shadow of the Madeleine at Vézelay, in the second year of the reign of Louis the Saint, to a father who cut voussoirs for the abbey choir and to a mother who was a vintner's daughter of the Cure valley. Of his eleventh year he was set to the tracing-floor at Saint-Denis under Master Eudes le Breton, successor in the lodge to Pierre de Montreuil, and there he learned the Parisian manner of the fenestrage — the lancets thinning, the cusps multiplying, the mullions running unbroken from sill to apex.

He was freed of his apprenticeship in the spring of MCCLXXXIV, marked upon the cheek with the cross of the lodge and given the small bronze square that he wears yet upon his belt. Three years he journeyed: to Reims for the chevet of the Coronation Church, to Strasbourg under Master Erwin's kinsmen, and at last to Troyes where he cut the rose-jambs of Saint-Urbain in the manner that has since been called rayonnant.

In the year MCCLXXXVII the Chapter of Sens, having need of a master upon the death of Master Renaud le Vieux, called him by deed sealed at the feast of Saint-Étienne. He took the lodge above the south transept, where it stands to this day, and in the seventeen years since has set the tracing-floor with no fewer than forty-one chantiers — chapels, vault-keys, glass, monstrances, and one chasse of gilded copper for the bones of Saint Loup.

The Journeymen & Apprentices of the Lodge

— presently sworn upon the square at Sens, in the Year of Our Lord MCCCIV —

MCCXCI
Aymeric
The Glazier

Born at Chartres of a family of verriers seven generations deep, Aymeric came to the lodge in MCCXCI, bringing with him the cobalt of Saint-Denis and the secret of the madder pot which he sets only at the new moon. He cuts the came with the iron tooth, leads the saints, and binds the panels with the iron armatures forged by Pierre Crochet of the rue des Cordeliers.

Verrier · since A.D. MCCXCI
MCCXCV
Renaud
Of Troyes

Renaud of Troyes, third son of a master-mason of Saint-Urbain, was sent to Sens in the year MCCXCV that he might learn the Sénonais manner of the rib-vault. He has now the freedom of the lodge and cuts the springers and corbels for the chapter, and is besides the lodge's keeper of the parchment-roll and of the brass dividers that were Master Eudes' own.

Tailleur de pierre · since A.D. MCCXCV
MCCC
Jehan
Le Petit

Jehan, called le Petit for he is small of stature though he is now of his fifteenth year, was bound apprentice in the year of the Jubilee. He is the son of a Senonois weaver and was given to the lodge by his uncle, a sub-deacon of the cathedral. He sweeps the tracing-floor, fetches the iron, grinds the colours of azurite and orpiment, and is taught the rudiments of the geometry by Renaud upon Saturday eves.

Apprenti · since A.D. MCCC

The Four Crowned Martyrs

— Sancti Quattuor Coronati, patroni lapicidarum et fenestrariorum —

Before these four the workshop keeps its candle. They were Christian masons and sculptors of the Pannonian quarries under the emperor Diocletian, who suffered death rather than carve the figure of Aesculapius for his pagan altar. Their relics rest at Rome upon the Caelian; their feast is the eighth of November; their patronage is held by every lodge of stone from Compostela to Cologne.

Upon the south jamb of the lodge door there is a small niche, cut by Master Renaud le Vieux in MCCLIX, wherein four candles of beeswax are kept burning by Jehan in turn — one for each Crowned Martyr, that the chisel may not slip and that the keystone, when set, may bear its load until the trumpet of the Last Day.

Chronicle of the Master

A.D. MCCLXII
Born at Vézelay, on the eve of Saint Bernard.
A.D. MCCLXXIII
Bound apprentice at the lodge of Saint-Denis under Master Eudes le Breton.
A.D. MCCLXXXIV
Freed of the lodge upon the feast of the Ascension; cuts his first chef-d'œuvre, a corbel of the prophet Habakkuk.
A.D. MCCLXXXV–LXXXVII
Journeyman at Reims, Strasbourg, and Troyes; cuts the rose-jambs of Saint-Urbain.
A.D. MCCLXXXVII
Called master at Sens by deed of the Cathedral Chapter; takes the lodge above the south transept.
A.D. MCCXCI
Receives Aymeric the glazier into the lodge.
A.D. MCCC
Binds Jehan le Petit apprentice in the year of the Jubilee.

The Oath of the Lodge

I swear upon the Crowned Four, upon the iron compasses of my master, upon the keystone yet to be set: that I shall cut the stone true to the trace; that I shall not betray the secret of the geometry to him who has not been freed; that I shall keep faith with my fellows of the chisel and of the came; and that the work of my hands shall stand until the day when all stone shall be made one with the heavenly Jerusalem.

And if I break this bond, may my chisel turn upon me, may my mortar fail at the spring, may my name be struck from the lodge-roll and forgotten of the brethren, here and in the life to come.

— Guillaume, Master at Sens
