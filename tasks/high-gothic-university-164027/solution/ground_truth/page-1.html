Michaelmas Term · Anno Domini MMXXIV

A Welcome from the Chancellor Dr. Aliénor de Châtillon, D.Phil. (Oxon.)

It is with the deepest gladness that I greet the Michaelmas cohort of 2024 to the hill of Vézelay, where since the year 1148 successive generations of clerks, scholars, and pilgrims have ascended the rue Saint-Pierre toward the basilica's western tympanum and the cloistered courts beyond. Our four faculties — Theology, the Liberal Arts, Civil & Canon Law, and Natural Philosophy — convene this term in unbroken communion with that long inheritance.

You arrive in a season of particular grace. The Bibliothèque Saint-Aubain has welcomed a fragment of Vincent of Beauvais into its shelves; the chapel choir has resumed the Sarum use at Vespers; and the Lent matriculation window will open shortly to those discerning a vocation of study. May your reading be steadfast, your disputations charitable, and your hours upon the hill fruitful.

Given at the Chancellor's Lodging,
the Feast of Saint Michael and All Angels,
Aliénor de Châtillon

A New Acquisition

Vincent of Beauvais Recovered

The Bibliothèque Saint-Aubain announces the acquisition of a hitherto unrecorded thirteenth-century fragment of the Speculum Naturale of Vincent of Beauvais — six leaves of vellum in a Northern French hand, c. 1264, with rubrication in vermilion and lapis. The fragment will be placed on view in the Salle des Manuscrits from 14 November.

Lent 2025 Matriculation

The Application Window

Applications for matriculation in the Lent 2025 cohort will be received from Monday 6 January to Friday 7 February 2025. Candidates submit a Latin petitio, two scholarly testimonia, and may be summoned to viva voce examination at the Hôtel des Études.

Tuition is set at €4,820 per annum; bursaries of the Saint-Aubain Foundation cover one applicant in three.
