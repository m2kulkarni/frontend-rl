Three Principal Lectures
January · Januarii
XVI
Thursday — Feria Quinta

Boethius and the Music of the Spheres

Prof. Inge Holm — University of Uppsala

Inscribed in the calendar as the term's inaugural reading, this lecture traces the lineage of Boethian musica mundana from the De Institutione Musica to the cosmologies of the Chartrian masters, with a digression upon the silent harmonies legible in Carolingian psaltery diagrams.
18h00 · Salle Capitulaire
Faculty of Liberal Arts · Open to Townspeople
February · Februarii
IV
Tuesday — Feria Tertia

Disputatio Quodlibetalis: Whether memory is a passion of the soul

Faculty of Theology contra Faculty of Liberal Arts

The annual quodlibetal disputation, held in the high choir of the Aula Magna, places the Theologians (respondents) against the Artists (opponents) upon the question whether memoria be properly a passio animae, after the manner set forth in Aristotle's De Memoria et Reminiscentia and the corresponding question of Aquinas. Magister Jean-Baptiste Roussel will preside; refutations are heard in Latin, with a vernacular gloss provided.
15h00 — 18h30 · Aula Magna
Faculties of Theology & Liberal Arts
February · Februarii
XXVII
Thursday — Feria Quinta

The Annual Lecture on Civil Law

Prof. Anouk Veldkamp — University of Leiden

Delivered each year on the Thursday before the first Sunday of Lent, this is the principal address of the Faculty of Law. Prof. Veldkamp will treat the survival of Justinianic forms in the customary law of Burgundy, drawing on charters from the Vézelay scriptorium and from the comital archives of Mâcon.
17h30 · Salle des Décrets
Faculty of Civil & Canon Law

Ordo Lectionum · Hilary 2025

Beyond the three principal occasions above, the studium keeps its ordinary readings throughout Hilary Term. The townspeople of Vézelay and pilgrims passing toward Saint-Mary-Magdalene are received without ticket; benches are reserved for the matriculated until the half hour before each reading, after which all places are common.

The full ordinary calendar of disputations, ferial readings, and the Lenten exercises of the four faculties is posted upon the great north door of the Aula Magna, and copied weekly into the porter's register.
