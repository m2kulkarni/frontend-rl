A Note from the Chief Curator

Dott.ssa Livia Carnelos

Three hundred and forty-two objects rest within these four halls — every one of them recovered, conserved, or bequeathed within the past century. We arrange them not by reign or by emperor's hand, but by the four registers of imperial life: the face, the inscribed word, the painted wall, and the coined treasury. Together, they describe a republic that learned to wear a crown.

Our most recent acquisition, a fragment of the Acta Diurna in carved limestone, joined Sala II in March of this year. It is displayed beside its conservator's notebook.

« Roma non perit; Roma se recordatur. »
SALA · I

Sculpture & Portraiture

Marble and bronze likenesses spanning the principate from Augustus to the Severans, including civic dedications recovered from Ostia, Tibur, and the Esquiline.

INV. MA-1138

Portrait Bust of Antoninus Pius

Material
Parian marble, traces of pigment
Provenance
Ostia Antica, Decumanus Maximus
Date
c. 150 CE

Recovered 1923 during the drainage works of the Tiber estuary. The hairline retains the diadem-channel typical of late Hadrianic workshops.

INV. MA-0421

Cuirassed Statue of a Magistrate

Material
Luna marble, restored left arm
Provenance
Tibur, Villa of Q. Hortensius
Date
c. 75 CE

The breastplate bears a relief of two griffins flanking a candelabrum — an iconography rare outside the Flavian period.

INV. MA-0907

Bronze Head of a Youth (Genius?)

Material
Cast bronze, copper inlay
Provenance
Esquiline, Horti Lamiani
Date
c. 30 BCE — 14 CE

Eyes hollowed for paste inlay, now lost. Compare to the Vatican Genius Augusti for stylistic kinship.

INV. MA-1402

Funerary Relief of the Vibii

Material
Travertine
Provenance
Via Latina, third milestone
Date
c. 40 BCE

Three freedmen of the Vibia gens: Lucius the baker, Marcus the weaver, and the matron Vibia Prima. Names retained in original cutting.

INV. MA-1660

Veiled Head of a Vestal

Material
Pentelic marble
Provenance
Forum Romanum, Atrium Vestae
Date
c. 200 CE

The infula is carved with unusual depth; the back of the head is unfinished, suggesting placement against a wall niche.

INV. MA-2011

Equestrian Fragment — Hoof & Plinth

Material
Gilt bronze on travertine
Provenance
Capitoline slope, sondage 4
Date
c. 175 CE

All that remains of a colossal equestrian honorific — likely Marcus Aurelius or a kindred ruler. The plinth retains an iron tang for fixing.

SALA · II

Inscriptions & Epigraphy

Stone-cut testimonies of decree, dedication, and daily commerce. Texts are presented with squeezes and letter-by-letter transcription.

INV. MA-0033

Decree of the Aventine Curia

Material
Carrara marble tabula
Provenance
Aventinus, Templum Cereris
Date
19 March 78 CE

Records a grain ration for the plebs urbana under Vespasian. Twenty-three lines, complete; the bronze fixing pins survive in situ.

INV. MA-0512

Boundary Stone of the Pomerium

Material
Tufa
Provenance
Via Salaria, near Porta Pinciana
Date
c. 49 CE

Inscribed: « CLAVDIVS · CAESAR · POMERIVM · AMPLIAVIT ». One of seven cippi from Claudius's enlargement of the sacred boundary.

INV. MA-0844

Funerary Stele of Aurelia Crispina

Material
Limestone
Provenance
Via Appia, sixth milestone
Date
c. 215 CE

Aurelia Crispina, freedwoman, lived twenty-two years, three months. The dedication is by her husband, the wine-merchant Crescens.

INV. MA-1029

Fragment of the Acta Diurna

Material
Travertine
Provenance
Forum, near the Curia Iulia
Date
c. 60 CE

Acquired March 2025. Reads: « HODIE · DICTATA · LEX · DE · ANNONA · POPVLVS · ASSENTITVR ». A rare epigraphic echo of the daily gazette.

INV. MA-1181

Bronze Military Diploma

Material
Bronze, two leaves bound
Provenance
Pannonia Inferior (gift, 1962)
Date
7 January 138 CE

Grant of citizenship to Tiberius Claudius Dossenus, auxiliary cavalry, upon honorable discharge. Witnessed by seven citizens of Rome.

INV. MA-1597

Tabella Defixionis (Curse Tablet)

Material
Lead, rolled and pierced
Provenance
Anio, Sanctuary of Anna Perenna
Date
c. 110 CE

The petitioner Septimia binds the tongue and the hand of one Caecilius before the courts. Letters cut in mirror script.

SALA · III

Domestic Frescoes

Wall paintings detached and restored from Pompeii, Herculaneum, and the suburban villas of Latium. Works are displayed in muted light and at original elevation.

INV. MA-0277

Garden Wall — House of the Pomegranates

Material
Fresco on lime plaster
Provenance
Pompeii, Regio I, Insula 9
Date
c. 50 CE

A continuous viridarium with pomegranate, oleander, and hooded songbirds. Detached 1957 in a single panel of 3.10 metres.

INV. MA-0633

Cubiculum Panel — Cupid & Psyche

Material
Fourth-style fresco
Provenance
Herculaneum, Casa del Bicentenario
Date
c. 65 CE

A small mythological vignette set within an architectural aedicula. The pigment yellow ochre was sourced from Sinope.

INV. MA-0951

Dining Frieze with Theatre Masks

Material
Fresco fragment
Provenance
Boscoreale, Villa of P. Fannius
Date
c. 30 BCE

Tragic and comic masks alternate above garlands of laurel. Cinnabar red is preserved with rare luminosity.

INV. MA-1156

Lararium Panel of the Praetorian

Material
Fresco on tufa-mortar
Provenance
Rome, Caelian Hill (Castra Nova)
Date
c. 200 CE

Genius of the household flanked by twin lares. Inscribed beneath: « GENIO · LOCI · SACRVM ».

INV. MA-1340

Black-Ground Panel with Egret

Material
Third-style fresco
Provenance
Stabiae, Villa Arianna
Date
c. 20 CE

A solitary white egret upon a velvet-black field. The most-requested image in our 2024 study-room reservation log.

INV. MA-1818

Ceiling Coffer with Rosette

Material
Stucco & fresco composite
Provenance
Rome, Domus Transitoria
Date
c. 64 CE

A single coffer recovered from the ruins beneath Nero's Domus Aurea. Gilt traces remain in the rosette's central boss.

SALA · IV

Coins & Cameos

The cabinet of small treasures: sestertii, denarii, glass paste, and carved sardonyx. Magnification stands are provided at every case.

INV. MA-N-0114

Aureus of Augustus — Capricorn Reverse

Material
Gold, 7.92 g
Provenance
Lugdunum mint (acq. 1971)
Date
15 — 13 BCE

Obverse: bare head of Augustus. Reverse: Capricorn with cornucopia and globe — the natal sign of the princeps.

INV. MA-N-0388

Sestertius of Nero — Port of Ostia

Material
Orichalcum, 26.4 g
Provenance
Rome mint
Date
c. 64 CE

Reverse: a bird's-eye view of the harbour with seven ships and a colossal Neptune. One of the finest known specimens.

INV. MA-N-0541

Denarius of the Liberators

Material
Silver, 3.71 g
Provenance
Travelling mint, Greece
Date
42 BCE

Brutus on the obverse; « EID · MAR » beneath two daggers and the cap of liberty on the reverse. Fewer than one hundred examples are recorded.

INV. MA-G-0042

Sardonyx Cameo — Livia as Ceres

Material
Three-layer sardonyx, 41 mm
Provenance
Bequest, Carnelos collection
Date
c. 14 — 29 CE

Profile bust crowned with wheat. The reverse retains the antique gold setting prepared for a finger ring.

INV. MA-G-0129

Glass Paste Intaglio — Eagle & Standard

Material
Cobalt-blue glass
Provenance
Aquileia (acq. 1939)
Date
c. 90 CE

A legionary aquila clutching thunderbolts between two vexilla. Probably a soldier's seal-stone.

INV. MA-N-0772

Antoninianus of Gallienus — Soli Invicto

Material
Billon, 3.10 g
Provenance
Rome mint
Date
c. 265 CE

Radiate bust of Gallienus. Reverse: Sol standing left, raising hand, with whip — a coin of the crisis decades.

Object Study by Appointment

Researchers, students, and conservators may request handling sessions in the Sala di Studio, third floor. Applications are received through the registrar at studio@mvseo-aureliano.it; permission is generally granted within fourteen days. A magnifying lamp, white-cotton gloves, and a graphite pencil are provided; ink is forbidden.
