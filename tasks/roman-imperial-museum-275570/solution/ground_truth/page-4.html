Finding the Museum

Via di Santa Sabina, 27

00153 · Roma

The Museo occupies a sixth-century basilica converted in 2007, set within the cypress gardens of the Aventine. The principal façade faces the Tiber; the step-free Clivo dei Publicii entrance lies on the southern flank.

Arriving

  • Metro Circo Massimo · Line B · 7 min walk
  • Bus Lines 75, 175, 280 · Aventino stop
  • Tram Line 3 · Piramide
  • By Foot 12 min from Bocca della Verità
  • Cycle Racks at the Clivo gate
  • Parking Limited; accessible bays on Via di Santa Sabina
Tickets timed at thirty-minute intervals; we recommend booking three days in advance for weekend visits.
Cloakroom and audio guides (Italian, English, Latin) are included with admission.
