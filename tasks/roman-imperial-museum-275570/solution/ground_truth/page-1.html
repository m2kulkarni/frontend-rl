The Travertine Basilica

Housed in a converted travertine basilica on the southern flank of the Aventine, the Museo Aureliano holds 4,200 catalogued objects spanning the years 30 BCE to 312 CE — from the principate of Augustus to the eve of Constantine's accession. Its galleries trace not only the imperial portrait and the public monument, but the quiet civic instruments of Roman life: the boundary stone, the funerary altar, the household lar, the wax tablet.

Acquired piece by piece since the museum's founding, the collection privileges inscription and provenance over spectacle. Each object is presented with its findspot, its dedicator, and, where legible, its hand.
