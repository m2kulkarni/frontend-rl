The Research Programme

The Centro di Studi Aureliani sustains a programme of original research in Roman epigraphy, sculpture, and the social fabric of the Imperial city. Three publication series, an annual journal, and a forthcoming open-access digital corpus carry that work to colleagues and the public.

Acta Aureliana

The annual peer-reviewed journal of the museum, published since 1998. Each volume gathers original studies on Roman art, civic ritual, and inscriptions, with extended catalogue entries from the permanent collection.

Vol. XXVII · 2024 · 312 pp.

Studia Epigraphica

A monograph series in two strands — Romana and Provincialia — devoted to the close edition of inscriptions, dedications, and funerary texts. Eighteen volumes have appeared since 2006, distributed by L'Erma di Bretschneider.

XVIII vols. · since 2006

Corpus Digitale

A forthcoming digital edition of 1,860 inscriptions held in the Aventine collections, encoded in EpiDoc TEI XML with high-resolution photogrammetry, RTI imaging, and full apparatus criticus. Free at launch.

Public release · Spring 2026

The 2026 Visiting Fellowship

Submission Guidelines

The Museo Aureliano awards three Visiting Fellowships each year to scholars of any nationality working on Roman Imperial art, epigraphy, or the social topography of the city of Rome between 27 BCE and 337 CE. Fellows reside on the Aventine for three uninterrupted months and are expected to deliver one public lecture and to deposit a working paper in the museum's open repository.

Applications must be submitted in a single PDF, in English, Italian, or German, and comprise: a curriculum vitae of no more than four pages; a research proposal of 2,500 words inclusive of bibliography; one published writing sample; and the names of two referees willing to write directly to the Director by the deadline.

Address all correspondence to Segreteria Scientifica, Museo Aureliano, Via di Santa Sabina 14, 00153 Roma, or by electronic submission to fellowships [at] museoaureliano.it.
