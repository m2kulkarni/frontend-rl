Chartered in the year of our Lord · MCCXLVII

The Foundation of
Saint-Gervais
of the Glasswrights

A perpetual charity of bread, lodging, and the craft of coloured glass

Be it known to all who pass beneath this arch that the Foundation of Saint-Gervais of the Glasswrights was chartered in the year of our Lord one thousand two hundred forty and seven, at Chartres, by the hand of Maître Étienne de Bonneval, glazier to the cathedral chapter, that there might be provided, in perpetuity, bread, lodging, and instruction unto apprentice glaziers and unto the widows of journeymen of the craft.

The Almshouse

⚜

Twelve cells under one slate roof at the rue des Changes, with hearths kept against the winter and a refectory wherein bread and pottage are served twice each day to widows of the craft and to brothers fallen ill. The bell rings at lauds and at vespers.

The Apprentice Roll

⚜

Forty-and-three boys are presently bound under articles of seven years to the masters of the cathedral chapter. Their names, their parishes, and the panels they have set within the windows of the choir are entered, year by year, into the great Book of Saint-Gervais.

→ To the Apprentice Roll

Schedule of Benefactions

⚜

From the Countess Adèle of Blois, four livres tournois yearly; from the Confraternity of Saint-Lubin, six measures of wheat at Michaelmas; from divers burgesses of Chartres, candles, lead, and coloured glass. The full schedule, with bequests and obits, is herein recorded.

→ To the Benefactions

The seat of the Foundation is at the Maison de Verre,
rue des Changes, in the parish of Saint-Hilaire, hard by the cathedral of Notre-Dame de Chartres.
