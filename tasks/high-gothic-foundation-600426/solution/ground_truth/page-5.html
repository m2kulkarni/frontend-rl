Liber Beneficiorum · Folium V

Schedule of
Benefactions
& Bequests

Wherein are recorded the principal gifts, rents, and pious legacies received by the Foundation of Saint-Gervais of the Glasswrights, from the year of Our Lord MCCXLVII unto the present day.

— folio v —

Let it be known unto all who shall read this register that every gift, however slight, is set down here in good order, that the souls of donors may be remembered at the altar of Saint-Gervais and the chantry of the glaziers, and that no penny of charity be lost from the reckoning.

The Principal Benefactions

⚜
MCCXLVII

F Of the Most Reverend Chapter of Notre-Dame of Chartres, upon the day of the chartering of this Foundation, forty livres parisis in coin, sealed in three purses of stamped leather and delivered into the hands of our first procurator, Master Aymon le Verrier. Of this sum, twelve livres were laid out for the purchase of the corner-house in the rue des Changes; the remainder placed in the chest beneath the chapel of Saint-Gervais, against the establishment of the almshouse and the apprentices' school.

MCCLXII

Granted by Sieur Geoffroi de Berchères, knight, from his lands of Berchères-les-Pierres lying east of the cathedral quarry, a yearly rent of four muids of wheat, to be carted to our oven at the feast of Saint-Michel and rendered without diminution by the bailiff of his estate, in perpetuity, for the bread of the poor brethren of the maison and for the masters of glass who shall be lodged therein. Confirmed by his seal of red wax pendant on a silken cord.

MCCLXXVIII

Of Dame Constance de Pontgouin, widow of the late Sire Renaud, twelve écus of gold, given upon the vigil of the Assumption, expressly for the gilding of the chapel screen which divides the chantry of the glaziers from the nave of Saint-Gervais. The work was put to Maître Hugues l'Orfèvre and finished within the twelvemonth, with the remainder of three écus laid out in fine ultramarine for the painting of the rood-beam.

MCCLXXXVI

From the guild of mercers of the rue au Lait, a perpetual gift of two pounds of beeswax at every feast of Saint-Lucie, for the candles of the Lady Chapel and the night-lamp burning before the statue of Saint-Gervais, patron of the glasswrights. The first taper was set alight by the warden Adam Boutillier in the year aforesaid.

MCCXCIV

Bequest of Maître Hervé l'Aîné, master glazier of this town, by his last will read before the procurator and two witnesses upon the eve of his death: a stone house at the corner of the rue au Lait and the petite rue des Lombards, together with its workshop, two glazing-tables, the great kiln, his stock of cobalt and ruby pot-metal, and the lesser tools of his craft, to be held by the Foundation for the lodging of two apprentices and one journeyman of the school. Requiescat in pace.

To those who would Bequeath

⚜

Whosoever, moved by piety toward Almighty God and by charity toward the poor masters and apprentices of the glaziers' craft, shall be pleased to add unto this Schedule any gift, rent, vestment, or pious legacy — whether in coin of the realm, in wheat, in candle-wax, in tools of the craft, or in lands and tenements — let him address himself, in person or by letter sealed, unto the procurator of this Foundation at the chapter-house of the cathedral of Chartres, adjacent the cloister of the canons.

Bequests by testament shall be witnessed by two clerks of the chapter and entered into the present register by the warden's hand. The names of all benefactors are read at the altar at the Mass of the Holy Cross upon every Friday of the year, and the souls of those departed are commended to God in the chantry of the glaziers, the candle of Saint-Gervais burning before them.

Given under the seal of the Foundation, this twenty-third day of November, anno gratiæ MCCXCV.
— Frère Eustache de Lèves, Procurator
