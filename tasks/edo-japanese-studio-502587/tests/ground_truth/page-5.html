Vol. V — Notes from the Workshop

Sawdust, gampi,
and the slow turn of the year.

About this journal Brief, dated entries kept by the studio principals between visits to clients and suppliers. We post when something is worth noting — a sample arriving, a jig being cut, a week closed for festival. No commentary, no photography, no hurry.
08   entries · 2023 — 2024
All · Workshop · Materials
14 March 2024

The Kamigyō ranma is too dense; reducing kumiko from 144 to 96.

First mock-up arrived on the bench yesterday and the screen reads as a wall — light cannot pass. Counted the cells and we were at 144, twice what the original 1962 drawing called for. We will retool the jig tonight and try 96, with the asanoha pattern loosened at the upper register only. The client is patient; the room, north-facing, is not.

№ 008 Workshop
02 February 2024

Hinoki billets from Kiso arrived — the grain is closer than last spring's lot.

Three bundles, 38 pieces total, milled to 24 × 24 × 1820 mm by Tanihata Lumber. Moisture meter reads 11.4% on average; we will rest them on the south wall for six weeks before any cutting. The pale band near the sapwood is wider than I would have liked — likely a younger stand. Suitable for interior frames, not for the andon shoulders.

№ 007 Materials
11 January 2024

Hatsu-shigoto: the first cut of the year is a small box for Mrs. Yoshikawa.

Tradition holds that the first work of the year sets the year's tone, so we keep it small and exact. A walnut document box, 280 × 190 × 60 mm, mitred with hidden splines, finished in two coats of raw lacquer. Delivered on foot to Teramachi by mid-afternoon. The lid closes with the soft sound one hopes for.

№ 006 Workshop
2023
02 December 2023

Iwano-san sent the gampi sheets for the andon shades.

Forty-eight sheets, 630 × 940 mm, hand-couched at the Echizen workshop. The winter batch is paler than autumn's, as expected — the bark stripped in October still carries a touch of the leaf colour. We will use the lighter sheets for the upper panels of the four floor lanterns destined for the Nishijin teahouse, and reserve the warmer ones for the lower registers.

№ 005 Materials
28 October 2023

Visited Nakagawa Mokkougei in Sakyō to discuss the kiri tansu commission.

Three hours over tea looking at drawer fronts. Their position on book-matched paulownia is firm and persuasive; we will take their counsel and abandon the quartersawn proposal. The client's drawing of his grandmother's cabinet is on the wall above my bench. We are not copying it; we are answering it.

№ 004 Workshop
19 August 2023

Closed for obon week.

The studio is shut from 13 to 20 August. We are at the family graves in Ōhara. Correspondence will resume on the 21st; nothing in the workshop is on a clamp.

№ 003 Studio
06 June 2023

The Murin-an commission is delivered and installed.

Two folding screens of kumiko backed with usuyō washi, each 1700 × 900 mm, completed over fourteen months and 312 logged workshop hours. The garden side carries an asanoha; the room side, a plain goma-gara. Final fee: ¥2,840,000 inclusive of installation. The screens will outlast everyone who saw them today.

№ 002 Workshop
22 April 2023

A new apprentice: Sora Hayashida joins us from the Takayama programme.

Two-year placement, beginning Monday. She has good hands and a steady eye on the marking knife — both are rarer than they should be. Her first task will be sharpening the studio's twenty-three chisels in sequence, a week's work, after which she will rebuild the planing beam. We pay ¥220,000 monthly with lodging in the Imadegawa annex.
