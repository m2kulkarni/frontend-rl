Calling at the
atelier in Kamigyō.

The workshop sits a short walk north of Shōkokuji's south gate, behind a single-board cedar door painted black. We keep the front room ordered for visitors; the planing benches are through the noren at the back.

Commission II  —  Four Steps
  1. 01

    Enquiry letter

    A short note by post or email describing the room, the use, and any drawings or photographs you have to hand. We reply within ten working days.

  2. 02

    Site visit

    Aiko or Junpei travels to the site to measure, observe light, and discuss the brief in person. Within Kansai there is no charge; further afield, travel is billed at cost.

  3. 03

    Fee proposal

    A written proposal follows in three to four weeks: scope, materials, milestones, and a fixed fee. Studio commissions begin at ¥480,000; full interiors at ¥3,200,000.

  4. 04

    Build window

    Work proceeds in the workshop on a six- to eighteen-month window depending on scale, joinery complexity, and the seasoning of the timber chosen for the piece.

The spring open studio falls on the weekend of 12 — 13 April 2025, when the umé in the courtyard is at its second flush and the long planks of kiri are turned out from the drying rack into the front room.

Visitors are warmly welcomed but please write to Junpei a week ahead so the benches can be cleared. Tea is served at three. The lane is narrow; we recommend the bus from Imadegawa station and a short walk through the temple grounds.
