Catalogue 02 — Selected Commissions

Twelve works,
across ten years
of joinery and paper.

A chronological record of completed commissions between 2014 and 2024. Each entry names the principal materials and the carpenter, lacquerer, or paper-maker with whom the studio collaborated.

No. 01 / 2024

Kitano Tenmangū Reading Pavilion

A small hinoki-framed reading pavilion built within a private courtyard adjoining the shrine grounds, with sliding shōji of double-layered kōzo paper.

2024 · Hinoki · Itō family, Kamigyō-ku

with Yamamoto Daiku, Kameoka

No. 02 / 2023

Kamigyō Tea Room

A four-and-a-half mat chashitsu inserted into a renovated machiya, joined entirely without metal fasteners. Walls in earthen plaster; tokonoma post in unstripped akamatsu.

2023 · Hinoki, washi, akamatsu · Private, Kyoto

with Tsuji Sashimono, Sakyō-ku

No. 03 / 2022

Andon Series No. 7 — for Sasayuri

Edition of nine floor lanterns commissioned for the ryokan Sasayuri-ann in Nara. Frame of jointed sugi; double shade of hand-pulled kōzo with a single indigo line.

2022 · Sugi, kōzo paper · Sasayuri-ann, Nara

paper by Hatano Wataru, Ayabe

No. 04 / 2022

Honke Owariya Counter Rebuild

Replacement of the kitchen-side counter at the 550-year-old soba house, salvaging two original keyaki boards and rejoining them with butterfly keys.

2022 · Keyaki (reclaimed) · Honke Owariya, Nakagyō

with Inaba-gumi carpenters

No. 05 / 2021

Mori Residence Kumiko Screen

A 2.4-metre kumiko screen of asanoha pattern, dividing the genkan from the living room. Cut from a single yakusugi board, recovered from the 2010 thinning programme.

2021 · Yakusugi · Mori family, Ōhara

with Kumiko Ichihara, Hikone

No. 06 / 2020

Gion Bunkō — Archive Cabinetry

Twelve full-height cabinets in kiri, designed to store woodblock-printed ephemera under stable humidity. Drawers run on traditional wedged dovetails.

2020 · Kiri (paulownia) · Gion Bunkō Foundation

with Nakagawa Mokkōgei, Kamigyō

No. 07 / 2019

Sobaya Tsuruya — Pontochō Counter

A nine-seat counter for the soba house Tsuruya, single-board hinoki finished only with a planed surface; no varnish. Renewed annually with rice-bran cloth.

2019 · Hinoki · Sobaya Tsuruya, Pontochō

with Yamamoto Daiku, Kameoka

No. 08 / 2018

Andon Series No. 4 — Wall Edition

A run of fourteen wall-mounted andon for a hotel corridor in Hakone. Frame in finger-jointed sugi; shade of gampi tissue layered over kōzo, lit by 2 W LED.

2018 · Sugi, gampi, kōzo · Gōra Kadan, Hakone

paper by Awagami Factory, Tokushima

No. 09 / 2017

Tsuwano Bookshop Fixtures

A complete fit-out for an independent bookshop in the castle town of Tsuwano: shelves, counter, low display tables, and a single suspended andon over the entrance.

2017 · Sugi, kōzo paper · Hon-no-mori, Tsuwano

with Iwami Mokkōsha, Shimane

No. 10 / 2016

Ōkubo Atelier — Workbench Series

A pair of joinery workbenches for the painter Ōkubo Aiko, designed around her left-handed working stance. Each bench joined with through-tenons and oak wedges.

2016 · Mizunara oak · Ōkubo studio, Iwakura

with Tsuji Sashimono, Sakyō-ku

No. 11 / 2015

Higashiyama Guesthouse Interior

Full interior commission for a six-room guesthouse: shōji, ranma, tokonoma, fixed lighting, and a six-mat dining table milled from a single felled keyaki.

2015 · Hinoki, keyaki, kōzo · Yamashita-tei, Higashiyama

with Inaba-gumi carpenters

No. 12 / 2014

Hakubai Atelier — Studio Counter

The studio's own working counter, made on commencement of practice. Two-board hinoki top with a butterfly key in cinnabar urushi; the only marked work in the catalogue.

2014 · Hinoki, urushi · Hakubai Atelier, Kamigyō

made in-house, signed by all three founders

Index — A

Materials drawn upon, 2014 — 2024.

Six woods and two papers carry nearly the full body of work. Counts indicate the number of catalogued commissions in which each material appears as a principal element.

檜

Hinoki

Japanese cypress, fragrant and pale. Used for tea-room timbers, counters, and the studio's own bench. Sourced from Kiso and Kameoka thinnings.

In 05 works

杉

Sugi

Cryptomeria. The structural wood of the andon frames; cut on the quarter, finger-jointed, finished with raw lacquer.

In 03 works

屋久杉

Yakusugi

Old-growth sugi from Yakushima, only obtained through licensed thinning recovery. Reserved for kumiko and tokonoma posts.

In 01 work

桐

Kiri

Paulownia. Light, dimensionally stable, suited to archival cabinetry and instrument cases. Acquired from Aizu cooperatives.

In 01 work

楮

Kōzo

Paper-mulberry fibre, the principal washi used in our shōji and lantern shades. Hand-beaten and laid on bamboo screens.

In 06 works

雁皮

Gampi

A finer, translucent fibre, layered over kōzo where a softer light is wanted. Reserved for inner shade leaves and tea-room ranma.
