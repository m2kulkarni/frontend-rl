白梅工房
HAKUBAI ATELIER

A studio in Nishijin, Kyoto. Joinery, washi, and the architecture of small rooms. Founded 1998 by Aiko Tachibana and Junpei Mori.

Current Works · Spring 2024
No. 一

Tea-Room Renovation, Kamigyō

A four-and-a-half mat tea room reset within a Meiji-era machiya. New tokonoma in figured keyaki; replastered earthen walls in Jurakudai ochre; a single shoji rebuilt with hand-pulled Echizen washi.

2024 · Keyaki, Jurakudai earth · Private commission
No. 二

Andon Lamps for Ryokan Sasayuri

A run of fourteen cedar floor lanterns for the corridor and entry of Sasayuri, an inn on the Kumano Kodō. Yoshino sugi frames with kōzo washi, oil-wax finish, low 2700K LED warm to the eye.

2023–24 · Yoshino sugi, kōzo washi · Ryokan Sasayuri
No. 三

Kumiko Screen, Mori Residence

A 2,400 × 1,800 mm asa-no-ha kumiko partition for a Setagaya residence by architect K. Hasegawa. 1,184 hand-cut hinoki pieces, no fasteners; installed February in a single day on site.

2024 · Kiso hinoki · Mori family, Setagaya
