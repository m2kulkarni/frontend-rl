Bunkyū 2nd Year · 3rd Month · 1862

The civic office charged with the registration of the machi wards, the upkeep of the conduits running from the Sumida to Nihonbashi, and the coordination of the fire-watch towers of the eight-hundred-and-eight machi.

公示 Public Notices · Three Postings Posted at the Bureau Gate · Hongokuchō

Notice № I · Watercourses

Dredging Order: Kanda-gawa, Spring Quarter

Issued · Bunkyū 2 / 3rd month / 4th day Reach · Suidōbashi to Yanagibashi Wards Levied · 17 machi

Be it known that the silting at the Suidōbashi reach of the Kanda-gawa has risen to obstruct the passage of timber rafts bound for the lumberyards at Saruyachō. The seventeen wards bordering this stretch are summoned to provide labour parties of three men per chōnai for the duration of fifteen days.

Stipend: rice three shō and copper sixteen mon per labourer per day, drawn upon the Bureau treasury at Hongokuchō.

— Mizu-bugyō Inoue Tadayoshi

Notice № II · Register

Posting of New Ward Elders, Nihonbashi-Hongokuchō

Issued · Bunkyū 2 / 3rd month / 11th day Ward · Hongokuchō Itchōme through Yonchōme Term · Three years from this posting

Upon the retirement of the elder Sawaya Kichibei, aged seventy-three, the following residents are hereby entered onto the Register as Toshiyori of their respective chōnai: Komatsuya Jirōbei (1-chōme), Echigoya Sōshichi (2-chōme), Tsuruya Hanzaemon (3-chōme), and Iseya Tokubei (4-chōme).

Their seals shall be impressed upon the ward register at the Bureau hall on the morning of the 18th day, in the presence of the city magistrate's deputy.

— Machi-bugyō Sakai Tadayuki

Notice № III · Fire-Watch

Proclamation for the Dry-Wind Season

Issued · Bunkyū 2 / 3rd month / 19th day Effect · From this day until the 5th month Penalty · Forfeit of three kanmon

With the northwesterly winds now prevailing across the townscape, all hearths shall be banked at the hour of the Dog. Bath-houses are to extinguish their furnaces by the eighth bell. Lanterns hung from eaves must be shielded with paper of the heavier weight, and no firework, however small, is to be discharged within the city wards.

The hikeshi of the Yo-gumi, I-gumi, and Ro-gumi companies shall keep doubled watch upon the towers of Asakusa, Kanda, and Kyōbashi until the wind shall turn.

— Hikeshi-yoriki Ōkubo Magoshirō
