On the Reading of This Register

Hearth counts reflect the registry as last reconciled at the close of the Tenth Month. Wards in which a ward elder is presently absent — through illness, mourning, or removal to country residence — are governed in the interim by the deputy elder (kumigashira), whose name is held on the unabridged scroll at the bureau office and is not reproduced here.

Petitions concerning the boundaries of any ward, the legitimacy of an elder's standing, or the assessment of hearths shall be lodged in writing at the bureau gate during the third through seventh hour of the day, accompanied by the seal of two householders of standing within the ward concerned.
