Fire-Watch Towers & Hikeshi Stations.

Compiled by the Bureau
Lower City Office, Nihonbashi
Bunsei 9 · Spring
緒言 — Preamble

Of the yagura watchtowers and the townsmen's brigades that keep them.

By edict of the magistrate's office, every district of the lower city maintains a timber yagura of three storeys, surmounted by a hangarane bell of brass and a wooden hyōshigi clapper. The watchman ascends at the hour of the dog and descends at the hour of the rabbit; in the dry-wind months he does not descend at all.

Forty-eight machi-bikeshi companies are registered with this Bureau, lettered after the iroha and grouped by river-quarter. Their captains, addresses, and standard motifs are published below for the benefit of householders, innkeepers, and the wardens of the seven gates.

Standing Ordersduring the Karakaze months

Issued from the eleventh day of the tenth month to the last day of the second month, when the dry winds descend from the west and the city is most apt to burn. Householders shall observe these orders without exception.

  1. Cisterns full at sundown Every wooden cistern in the ward shall be drawn full from the conduit by the closing of the gates. The water-runner of the ward will inspect at lamp-lighting.
  2. No fires beyond the hour of the dog Charcoal braziers must be banked, hibachi lids closed, and lanterns of paper extinguished save those required by the watchman of the gate.
  3. Roof-buckets readied Households of two storeys shall keep two buckets of sand and one of water on the upper roof-line; tenement landlords are answerable for the same.
  4. Matoi standards displayed Each company's standard shall stand at the door of the captain's house from dusk to dawn, that runners may find it without inquiry.
  5. Bell-signal observed Three short strokes — distant fire. Six strokes — within the ward. A continuous peal — abandon the block and gather at the appointed open ground.
  6. Roof-breakers given right of way Tobi-ninsoku of the brigades shall pass freely through any door, and may break any wall to halt a fire, without warrant or fee. Damages claimed at this Bureau within thirty days.
