The Six Schools of the Institute

Schools & Faculties

Beneath the gilded vimanam of our central mandapa, the Institute teaches the disciplines that sustained a thousand years of South Indian sacred building. Six constituent schools — Sthapatya, Silpa, Sangita-Nritya, Bhasha, Samrakshana, and Pratima-Vidya — together confer the Bachelor's, Master's, M.Phil. and Doctoral degrees recognised by the University Grants Commission.

Estd. 1978·UGC NAAC A++·Trimester System·1,142 Resident Scholars

The Six Constituent Schools

◆ Six Prakaras of Learning ◆
I · STHAPATYA

School of SthapatyaTemple Architecture & Sacred Geometry

Dean: Prof. Dr. Veerappan Muthuswami, M.Arch (IIT-M), Ph.D.
Faculty: 18 · Studios: 6 · Founded: 1978
B.A. (5 yr)M.A. (2 yr)M.Phil.Ph.D.

Heir to the Mayamatam and Manasara traditions, the School of Sthapatya trains scholar-architects in the four-fold proportional grammar — pada-vinyasa, ayadi-shadvarga, mana, and tala — that governs the Dravida vimana. Students measure surviving temples of the Chola heartland, draft palm-leaf-derived karṇa-bhitti diagrams, and complete a final mandapa-design thesis under stone-yard supervision.

II · SILPA

School of SilpaStone & Bronze Craft

Dean: Prof. Sthapati Ramanathan Achari, Hereditary Silpi (Swamimalai), D.Litt. (h.c.)
Faculty: 14 · Foundries: 2 · Stone-yard: 1.4 acre
B.A. (5 yr)M.A. (2 yr)M.Phil.Ph.D.

The hands-led School of Silpa preserves the lost-wax madhuchchhishta-vidhana method of bronze casting and the granite ulipi-and-chisel craft of the Pallava-Chola lineage. Each cohort apprentices under a hereditary stapathi for two trimesters, then advances to figural commissions — Nataraja, Somaskanda, Bhairava — measured against the talamana of Sariputra.

III · SANGITA-NRITYA

School of Classical Music & DanceCarnatic Sangita & Bharatanatyam

Dean: Prof. Smt. Lalitha Krishnamurthy, Sangita Kalanidhi, Ph.D. (Madras)
Faculty: 22 · Performance halls: 3 · Annual concerts: 84
B.A. (4 yr)M.A. (2 yr)M.Phil.Ph.D.

Faithful to the seventy-two melakarta system codified by Venkatamakhin, this School cultivates voice, veena, mridangam, and the natya-vadya ensemble alongside the Tanjore quartet's Bharatanatyam repertoire. The Margazhi cohort presents the full margam — alarippu through tillana — at the Brihadisvara open-air stage as the public viva of the Master's degree.

IV · BHASHA

School of Sanskrit & Tamil LettersSastra, Sangam & Bhakti Literature

Dean: Prof. Dr. Arul Chelvan Pillai, M.A. (Sanskrit), M.A. (Tamil), Ph.D. (Pondicherry)
Faculty: 20 · Manuscript holdings: 9,400 palm-leaf bundles
B.A. (3 yr)M.A. (2 yr)M.Phil.Ph.D.

The Bhasha School trains philologists in Panini's Ashtadhyayi and Tolkappiyam in equal measure, and reads the Tevaram, Tiruvasagam, Tiruvaymoli, the Silappadikaram, and the Saiva Siddhanta corpus alongside the Agama-sastras. Doctoral candidates contribute to the Institute's ongoing critical edition of the Karandai Tamil Sangam plates.

V · SAMRAKSHANA

School of Heritage ConservationStone, Mural & Site Conservation

Dean: Prof. Dr. Devanathan Iyer, M.Sc. (Chemistry), M.A. (Archaeology), Ph.D. (ICOMOS Fellow)
Faculty: 12 · Field labs: 4 · Active sites: 27
B.A. (3 yr)M.A. (2 yr)M.Phil.Ph.D.

Established in 1996 in collaboration with the Archaeological Survey of India, Samrakshana trains conservators in lime-mortar petrography, biocide-free granite cleaning, and the in-situ stabilisation of Nayaka-period murals at Tiruvarur and Chidambaram. The School's mobile diagnostic laboratory has surveyed forty-one heritage temples across the Kaveri delta since 2014.

VI · PRATIMA-VIDYA

School of Iconography & Art HistoryMurti-Sastra & Comparative Forms

Dean: Prof. Dr. Padmaja Subrahmanian, Ph.D. (SOAS, London), Padma Shri 2019
Faculty: 11 · Slide library: 38,200 plates · Founded: 1984
B.A. (3 yr)M.A. (2 yr)M.Phil.Ph.D.

Pratima-Vidya treats the icon as a doctrinal document: students read the Vishnudharmottara, Silparatna and Kashyapa-Silpa; analyse Pallava, Chola and Vijayanagara stylistic horizons; and compare Dravidian forms with Pala, Hoysala and South-East Asian counterparts at Polonnaruwa and Prambanan. The School curates the Institute's Bronze Gallery of 312 catalogued works.

Pramana

Credit Framework

The Institute follows the UGC Choice-Based Credit System adapted to a three-trimester academic year. One credit is reckoned at fifteen contact-hours of theory or thirty hours of studio / fieldwork.

  • B.A. Programme132 credits
  • M.A. Programme88 credits
  • M.Phil. Pre-Doctoral36 credits
  • Ph.D. Coursework24 credits
  • Studio / Fieldwork share≥ 40 %
  • Sanskrit / Tamil floor12 credits
  • Open electives8 credits
  • Min. attendance75 %

Kala-cakra

Trimester Calendar

The academic year is partitioned by the Tamil month-cycle into three trimesters, each of fourteen instructional weeks framed by a temple-festival recess.

Aippasi
18 Oct 2024 — 24 Jan 2025
Opens with Saraswati Puja and the Annaabhishekam fortnight. Foundational coursework, agamic primers, fresh studio inductions.
Thai
03 Feb 2025 — 09 May 2025
Heaviest of the three. Mid-term juries, Pongal field visits to Gangaikondacholapuram, intensive bronze-casting weeks.
Vaikasi
26 May 2025 — 22 Aug 2025
Thesis trimester. Final viva-voce, Vaikasi Visakam recital, public exhibition at the Institute Mandapa, dissertation submission.
