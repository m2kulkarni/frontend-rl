Create an aspirant profile on the SRITAS portal. Application fee ₹1,200 (₹600 for SC/ST/PwD). Upload Class XII transcripts and a 500-word statement of intent.

due 22 May

Portfolio Requirements

For craft-track aspirants only

Submit a single bound dossier (A3, max 24 leaves) by speed-post to the Registrar, postmarked on or before 22 May 2024. Digital copies are accepted in parallel.

  • Eight original works — drawn, modelled, or carved within the last 24 months. At least three from observation.
  • One technical study demonstrating proportional drawing of a Chola-period mūrti following the Mānasāra talaman.
  • One process sheet documenting a single project from conception to finishing — sketches, measurements, photographs.
  • A signed declaration of authorship countersigned by a school teacher or studio guru.
  • Stone & Bronze applicants additionally submit one small relief or wax model (max 30×30 cm).
  • Tanjore Painting applicants submit one finished panel showing gold-leaf application.

~ Frequent Inquiries ~

Aspirants Often Ask

May I apply to two programmes simultaneously?

Yes. An aspirant may file up to two applications in a single cycle, paying the form fee for each. The viva-voce panels are however independent, and on selection, only one programme may be accepted.

I have a Class XII certificate from another country — am I eligible?

Foreign qualifications are evaluated by the Office of International Affairs against the Association of Indian Universities equivalence list. Candidates from SAARC nations may apply directly; others should write to intl@srirajaraja.edu.in by 15 March 2024.

Is hostel accommodation guaranteed?

The Institute operates four hostels — Tirumala Bhavan and Andal Nilayam for women, and Karuvūr and Kāvēri Bhavan for men — with a combined capacity of 612 berths. First-year students receive priority allocation; rooms are confirmed at the time of fee payment in July.

What is the medium of instruction?

English is the principal medium across all degree programmes. Source-language instruction in Tamil, Sanskrit, or Telugu is provided in the relevant courses; bilingual handouts accompany every theory paper.

Can I defer admission for one year?

Deferment is granted in writing for medical, military, or compassionate grounds, subject to the approval of the Dean of Admissions. The aspirant must reconfirm their seat by 31 March of the deferred year and pay a re-registration fee of ₹3,000.

How do I claim the SRITAS travel stipend for the viva?

Candidates traveling more than 400 km to Tanjore for the viva are reimbursed AC three-tier rail fare on submission of original tickets at the Registrar's office on the day of interview. The reimbursement is credited within 10 working days.
