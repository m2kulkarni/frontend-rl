Established 1987 · Thanjavur · Deemed University Sri Rajaraja Institute of Temple Arts & Sciences श्री राजराज शिल्प-कला महाविद्यालय

Founded in 1987 within sight of the Brihadisvara temple, the Institute is a deemed university devoted to the rigorous study of Dravidian heritage — sacred geometry and Silpa Sastra, granite craft and bronze casting, Bharatanatyam, Karnatic music, Tamil epigraphy, and the conservation sciences that keep these traditions whole. Three thousand four hundred students. Twenty-seven schools. One thousand-year horizon.

Living Traditions

Practice within the temple year

The Institute is not a museum. Our students perform at the Brihadisvara & Airavatesvara temples through the festival calendar, under master-teachers (asans) who are themselves descendants of the Cholan workshops at Swamimalai and Kumbakonam.

  • Annual Margazhi residency at Tiruvaiyaru
  • Granite-cutting yard at the Karuvur campus
  • Bronze foundry & wax-modelling studios
  • Kuravanji & Kuchipudi exchange weeks

A Note from the Vice-Chancellor

"The chisel is older than the alphabet."

Friends, when I joined this Institute as a student of epigraphy in 1991, the Brihadisvara was approaching its thousandth winter. We were taught to read its stone-cut Tamil aloud, slowly, as though the temple itself was correcting our pronunciation. That habit — listening before interpreting, copying before composing — is what we still teach. The coming year brings two new chairs in conservation science and a long- deferred Centre for Pallava Bronze Studies. I invite you to study with us, or simply to walk our prakaras when the Kaveri is high.
Prof. Kalyani Subramaniam Vice-Chancellor · Chair, Tamil Epigraphy

From the Mandapa

14 Mar2024

Granite-Conservation MoU Signed with ASI Chennai Circle

The Institute has formalised a five-year memorandum with the Archaeological Survey of India (Chennai Circle) for joint stone-conservation work at six early-Chola sites, including Pullamangai and Kilaiyur. Twelve PG students will be assigned to field stations from June.

02 Mar2024

Mandapa Library Inaugurated by the Hon'ble Governor

The new Mandapa Library — 18,000 sq.ft of granite-pillared reading halls housing 92,000 volumes and 4,200 palm-leaf manuscripts — was inaugurated on Maha Sivaratri. The east wing holds the donated Sambamurthy collection of Karnatic notations.

19 Feb2024

Sthapati S. Ganapati Awarded Honorary Doctorate

Master sculptor S. Ganapati Sthapati of Mahabalipuram was conferred the degree of D.Litt. (honoris causa) at the 31st Convocation, in recognition of fifty years of pedagogy and the rebuilding of the Tiruvarur ratha.
