A Note on Photography in the Workshops

The stone-craft and bronze workshops are working spaces in which apprentices chant ślokas before each first cut. Visitors are welcome to photograph completed pieces in the courtyard, but please refrain from photographing artisans at work, half-finished icons, or measured drawings. No flash, no tripods, and no audio recording within the foundry. A short written request, addressed to the workshop master, will usually obtain a portrait sitting at the close of the working day. We thank you for honouring the discipline of the craft.
