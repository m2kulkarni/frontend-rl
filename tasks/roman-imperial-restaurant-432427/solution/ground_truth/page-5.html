Triclinium Rubrum

The Red Dining Room
XII Lecti · IX Hospites

Our innermost room, frescoed throughout in cinnabar and panelled with garlands after the Villa of Mysteries. Twelve couches arrayed about three low tables in the proper Roman fashion, accommodating nine reclining guests. The seat of formal dinners; service is by lamp-light only.

Hire · €1,400 per evening

Atrium

The Reception Hall
Stantibus · XL Hospites

The marble-paved entrance hall, opened beneath an impluvium of running water and ringed by a colonnade of six fluted columns. Suited to standing receptions for as many as forty guests, with passed gustatio of olives, libum, and oysters, and a long table of cold meats and bread.

Hire · €1,800 per evening

Hortus

The Walled Garden
Aestate · XV Hospites

A small enclosed garden behind the house, set with a vine pergola, a stone fountain, and a long table of cypress wood. Available from May to September only, by reason of weather; supper is taken beneath the leaves with the city beyond the wall. Fifteen guests, seated in the modern manner.

Hire · €1,100 per evening

De Lyra et Tibia

Musicians of the lyre and the double-flute, by arrangement

For any private convivium, the house can summon a small conventus musicorum: a lyre-player and a tibicen of the double-reed pipe, both trained in the Roman modes by Maestro Lupo Cassini of the Conservatorio di Santa Cecilia. They will play during the gustatio and through the secundae mensae, and will fall silent for any toast or address. A third voice — soprano, in Latin — may be added for nuptial and triumphal banquets.

A fortnight's notice is required. The musicians take their meal in the kitchen, after the last course is cleared.

Duo · €420 per evening  ·  Trio · €620 per evening
