Triclinium Aureum

Cenae More Romano · Suppers in the Roman Manner

Upon the slope of the Clivus Publicius on the Aventine, a frescoed supper-room revives the cookery of Apicius for a modern table. Here are dressed garum and honeyed dormice, suckling kid in laser, and the dark Falernian poured warm against the November rain. The proprietor, Lucius Varro Severus, receives guests from the hora undecima — the eleventh hour, by our reckoning half past four — until the secunda vigilia, the second watch of the night.
