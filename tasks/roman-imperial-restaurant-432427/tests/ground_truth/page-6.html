Dies Solis · SundayClausum

The doors are opened at the eleventh hour of the Roman day (XVII:00). The last couches are dressed at the first watch of night (XXII:00). The house keeps silence on the Kalends of every month and on the public feriae — Saturnalia, Lupercalia, and the Feast of the Vestalia.

Ascend from the Tiber at the foot of the Aventine, follow the Clivus Publicius past the rose garden of the Roseto Comunale, and turn at the basilica of Santa Sabina. Our oaken door bears a single bronze laurel — knock, and Septimius will open.
