Tabula II · Pars Cenae

The Carta

Bill of fare for the evening, set forth in three courses according to the custom of the Imperial table — that the convivium may proceed in due order, from gustatio to mensae secundae.

Gustatio

— the appetiser, taken with mulsum —
Ova Spongia ex Lacte VI Den.
Milk-soaked egg sponge, crowned with Hyblaean honey
Beaten farm eggs cooked in warmed ewe's milk, golden upon the platter, finished with thyme honey from the slopes of Mons Albanus and a turn of black pepper.
Glires Melle ac Papavere XII Den.
Honeyed dormice, rolled in poppy
Apicius IXThe signature dish of the house: dormice fattened on chestnut, glazed with honey and crusted with toasted poppy seed. Served upon a small terracotta tile.
Moretum Aventinense IV Den.
Aventine herb-cheese with rustic bread
Fresh sheep's cheese pounded in the mortar with garlic, rue, coriander and aged vinegar. Served with hot panis quadratus from our wood oven.
Olivae Columellae III Den.
Cured olives after Columella
Picene and Sabine olives cracked and steeped with fennel seed, lentisk and a thread of garum. A small dish to whet the appetite.
Cucurbitae Alexandrinae V Den.
Alexandrian gourd, cold
Boiled gourd dressed with cumin, mint, asafoetida and a vinegar of dates — a dish brought from the eastern provinces by our cook.

Prima Mensa

— the main board, served upon silver —
Pullum Numidicum XVI Den.
Numidian chicken with dates & asafoetida
Apicius VI · viiA young hen of the Campagna, roasted whole then finished in a sauce of dates from Jericho, asafoetida, pepper, cumin and garum. Served with toasted pine nuts.
Porcellus Flaccianus XXII Den.
Suckling pig in the manner of Flaccus
Whole suckling pig stuffed with fig, leek and crumbled wheat, roasted slowly upon the spit and basted with passum. Carved at the table.
Lepus in sua Aqua XVIII Den.
Hare braised in its own broth
Hill-country hare cooked with pepper, lovage, savory, raisin wine and a splash of garum, served with ash-baked turnips.
Mullus Rubescens XIV Den.
Red mullet of Antium, on bay leaves
Whole red mullet line-caught off Antium, grilled upon fresh bay leaves and finished with oenogarum — wine, garum and a turn of rue.
Lenticula ex Castaneis VIII Den.
Lentils with chestnut & coriander
Egyptian lentils slowly stewed with whole chestnuts, leek, coriander, mint and a finishing thread of vinegar. A dish for the longer courses.

Secunda Mensa

— sweet courses, with Falernian wine —
Patina de Piris VII Den.
Pear & cumin custard, baked in the dish
Apicius IV · iiCrescentine pears poached in passum, then folded with eggs, honey, pepper and a whisper of cumin and baked until just set. Served warm with a dusting of pepper.
Tiropatinam V Den.
Honeyed milk & egg pudding
A bowl of warmed milk thickened with eggs and Hymettian honey, finished beneath the lid with cracked black pepper.
Dulcia Domestica VI Den.
House sweetmeats — dates & nuts
Soft Jericho dates stoned and stuffed with pine kernel, pepper and salt, then sealed in honey. Served with toasted almonds of Damascus.
Mala Cydonia in Melle VI Den.
Quinces preserved in honey & defrutum
Quinces from our orchard, slowly cooked down with honey, defrutum and a single bay leaf, dark and fragrant. A spoonful suffices.
Caseus cum Favo IV Den.
Fresh sheep's cheese with the comb
A simple offering: a slab of fresh ricotta-style cheese set beside a piece of honeycomb from the bee-keeper Tullia of Tibur.

De Allergiis — Garum (fermented fish sauce) appears in nearly every savoury dish; please advise the steward of any allergy to fish, sesame, mustard, gluten or tree-nut. A vegetarian carta upon request, prepared after the manner of the Pythagorean households.

De Origine — Olive oil pressed at the Sabine farm of our proprietor; bread baked daily on the Aventine; fish landed at Ostia each dawn; honey from Tibur and the Alban Hills; pepper, cumin and asafoetida brought by sea from the eastern markets of Alexandria.

De Tempore — The full carta of three courses requires not less than two hours and a half. Convivia begin at the eleventh hour (V post meridiem) and close at the second hour of night.
