Sacred Commissions

№ I · MMXIV

Chartreuse de Champmol
Bell-Tower Repointing

Diocèse de Dijon · Côte-d'Or · sacred fabric

Full repointing of the fifteenth-century campanile in hydraulic lime, with replacement of forty-six damaged limestone voussoirs from the Comblanchien quarry. Iron cramps were drawn out, decorroded, and reset in lead. The bell frame was conserved in situ to retain the original oak resonance.

Client · Conseil Départemental€ 1 240 000

№ II · MMXVIII

Saint-Maclou Rose-Window Leadwork Survey

Église Saint-Maclou · Rouen · glazing report

A condition survey of the western rose, comprising photogrammetric capture of every cames profile and a phased proposal for releading without disassembly of the original fourteenth-century grisaille. Recommendations addressed wind-load on the tracery and the failing ferramenta saddle-bars.

Client · DRAC Normandie€ 184 000

№ III · MCMXCVIII

Chapelle Saint-Bénigne, Vézelay Sacristy

Abbaye de la Madeleine · Yonne · new build

A small sacristy added against the south transept, in coursed Tonnerre limestone with a single lancet of unleaded antique glass. The roof structure copies the chevron-rafter geometry of the surviving twelfth-century outbuildings; floor of waxed terracotta hexagons drawn from a fragment found beneath the porch.

Client · Communauté Monastique€ 412 000

№ IV · MMVI

Notre-Dame de l'Épine
South Aisle Vault Stabilisation

Marne · Champagne-Ardenne · structural

Stitch-grouting of three bays of quadripartite vaulting whose ribs had separated from the webs by up to 27 mm. Stainless armatures were threaded along the ridge, masked behind original mortar joints. The gilded boss of the central bay was lowered, conserved in our atelier, and reset.

Client · Monuments Historiques€ 760 000

№ V · MMXXII

Cathédrale de Laon
North Tower Crockets

Aisne · Hauts-de-France · stone replacement

Eighty-four crockets and three pinnacles re-carved in Saint-Maximin limestone, working from drawings we made in 2009 before frost cycles had removed the last legible profiles. Each piece was banker-marked with the date and the carver's monogram, after the practice of the original masters.

Client · Ville de Laon€ 2 080 000

№ VI · MMXXIV · in progress

Saint-Étienne de Beauvais
Crossing Tower Survey

Oise · Picardie · ongoing campaign

A three-year programme of dimensional survey, mortar sampling and selective dismantling of the lantern tower whose flying buttresses have begun to lean outward by twelve millimetres a decade. The first season's findings will be issued as a printed dossier in the autumn of 2025.

Client · Diocèse de Beauvais€ 940 000 / phase I

Civic Commissions

— ouvrages publics · libraries, hospices, town halls —

№ VII · MMIX

Hôtel-Dieu de Beaune
Visitor Circulation

Côte-d'Or · Bourgogne · museum design

A reordering of the visitor route through the fifteenth-century hospice, replacing a 1970s mezzanine with a low oak gallery cut clear of the painted polychrome rafters. New thresholds of slate were introduced at room boundaries; nothing fixed to the historic surfaces. Visitor capacity rose forty per cent.

Client · Hospices Civils€ 1 660 000

№ VIII · MMXX

Bibliothèque Municipale de Senlis · North-Aisle Infill

Oise · Picardie · contemporary infill

A reading room of 240 m² inserted into the abandoned north aisle of an unfinished Gothic chapel, glazed against the medieval arcade with a single sheet of low-iron glass. Stacks in oiled oak; a mezzanine of fair-faced concrete poured against fabric formwork to soften its meeting with the stone.

Client · Ville de Senlis€ 2 380 000

№ IX · MCMXCV

Mairie de Provins
Council Chamber

Seine-et-Marne · Île-de-France · interior

Restoration of the painted timber ceiling discovered behind a 1953 plaster soffit, and a new oak bench-table for thirty-one councillors. The chamber's lighting was hung from concealed cables drawn through the original beam channels — no fixings into the painted surfaces, the only condition the heritage office imposed.

Client · Ville de Provins€ 540 000

№ X · MMXVI

Tribunal de Grande Instance Annexe · Reims

Marne · adjacent to the cathedral close

A 1 100 m² annexe wedged between two protected façades, its own elevation a screen of pre-cast Savonnières limestone in narrow vertical fins. The fins step in plan to acknowledge the Romanesque rhythm of the eastern neighbour; courtrooms are lit only from clerestories above the public street.

Client · Ministère de la Justice€ 4 120 000

Conservation Reports

— études et rapports · published surveys —

№ XI · MCMXCI

Mortar Atlas of the Champagne Cathedrals

Reims · Châlons · Troyes · field study

The studio's first commission: a four-volume sampling and petrographic analysis of mortars across three cathedral fabrics, undertaken with the École de Chaillot. Two hundred and seventeen samples were drilled and catalogued; the resulting reference matches are still in use by regional contractors today.

Client · Ministère de la Culture€ 96 000

№ XII · MMXIII

Ferramenta & Saddle-Bar Corrosion in Picard Glass

Picardie · joint with CRMH Amiens

A two-year inventory of iron armatures supporting medieval glazing across forty-one churches, from Noyon to Saint-Riquier. Each armature was photographed, weighed where removable, and chemically tested for chloride contamination. Findings prompted a regional protocol for sacrificial-anode replacement in stained-glass conservation.

Client · DRAC Hauts-de-France€ 142 000
