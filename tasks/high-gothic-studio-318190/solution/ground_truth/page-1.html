Of the
Practice

For thirty-eight years our drawings have been measured against limestone, against memory, and against the patient arithmetic of the vault. The Atelier maintains three departments under a single roof in Reims, each charged with a distinct register of work yet bound to a shared discipline of slow building.

We accept perhaps nine commissions in any given year — chapels, libraries, civic interiors, the occasional new house — and we conduct each as a conversation between mason, draughtsman, and place. Stone is never asked to act as veneer.

  • i.Sacred Fabric Conservation of liturgical interiors, vault stabilisation, and the repair of historic glass under the direction of M. Aubertin.
  • ii.Civic Stone Mairies, libraries, and small museums — masonry-led civic commissions, often in collaboration with municipal preservation offices.
  • iii.New Build Ground-up architecture in the long-span tradition: chapels, reading pavilions, and private commissions of liturgical character.

Featured Commissions

MMXXII – MMXXIV
— No. I —

Reims Chapter House Re-roofing

2022 · Reims · Sacred Fabric

Replacement of the thirteenth-century timber roof above the canons' chapter, executed in seasoned French oak under traditional draw-bored joinery. Lead returned to the spire flèches; rainwater redirected from buttress crowns.

Completed
— No. II —

Cluny Reading Pavilion

2023 · Saône-et-Loire · New Build

A small civic library set into the ruined precinct of the abbey: a single tall nave of Bourgogne limestone, eleven oak desks, clerestory glazing in mouth-blown antique. Commissioned by the Commune de Cluny.

Opened May ’24
— No. III —

Saint-Léger-de-Soissons Nave Vault

Ongoing · Aisne · Sacred Fabric

Stabilisation of the twelfth-century quadripartite vaulting above the nave, following structural movement first recorded in 1893. Scaffold campaign in three phases; new tie-irons forged in Laon by the Pellerin forge.
