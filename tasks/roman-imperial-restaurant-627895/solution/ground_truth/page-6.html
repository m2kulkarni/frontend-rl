De Consuetudine — House Policy

Cancellation. Tables may be released without charge until twenty-four hours before the appointed seating. Within that window a courtesy of forty euro per guest is retained, payable upon arrival or by bank transfer; we ask this both to honour the kitchen's market shopping at Testaccio and to offer the seat to the next guest on the list.

Children. Children of all ages are warmly welcomed at the early seating from 19:30 to 21:00. Half-portions of cacio e pepe and rigatoni al pomodoro are prepared on request, without surcharge.

Attire. Smart, unfussy. The Aventino is a hill of cypresses and orange gardens; come as one comes to a long Sunday lunch.

Menù del Console — six courses · €85 per guest · 48 hours' notice

Alia Via — Other Means

Per chi preferisce la voce. Should the tablet above prove uncongenial, our reservations desk is staffed from eleven in the morning until eleven at night.
