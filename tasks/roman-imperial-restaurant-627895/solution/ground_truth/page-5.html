Travel & Arrival

three notes for the prudent guest

M

By Metro

Linea B serves the Aventine at Circo Massimo, the nearest station, six minutes on foot from our door. Exit toward Via del Circo Massimo, cross beneath the umbrella pines, and ascend Clivo dei Publicii. The climb is gentle and ends at the Giardino degli Aranci.

Trains run from 05:30 until 23:30 on weeknights, until 01:30 on Friday and Saturday. A single B.I.T. ticket at €1,50 covers the journey from Termini in under nine minutes.

P

By Carriage

Parking on the Aventine is residents-only along Via di Santa Sabina and the surrounding viali. Visitors are best served by the paid garage at Lungotevere Aventino, beside the Bocca della Verità, a level walk of nine minutes.

Taxi standsits at Piazza Albania and at the foot of Via Marmorata. We are pleased to telephone a return car after dinner; ask any member of the dining-room staff and a vehicle is rarely longer than ten minutes away.

A

Accessibility

The dining room sits at street level, entered by a single 4 cm threshold. The interior is one continuous floor and accommodates wheelchairs at four tables; please mention your needs at booking and we will reserve accordingly.

An accessible water-closet is provided. Guide and assistance dogs are welcome throughout the house. For guests of limited mobility we suggest arrival by taxi to the door, as the surrounding streets are cobbled in sampietrini.
