I

La Cucina

Chef Lucia Ferri

In the kitchen,Chef Lucia Ferri commands a brigade of seven, cooking the four pillars of cucina romana — cacio e pepe, gricia, amatriciana, carbonara — alongside the slow trades of coda alla vaccinara and abbacchio scottadito. Pasta is rolled by hand each morning at five, the guanciale cured upstairs, and Tuesday brings the only quinto-quarto tasting in the rione. Lucia trained at Checchino dal 1887 and at Da Cesare al Casaletto before bringing her ledger of nonna recipes to the Aventine in 2014.

Tonnarelli, rigatoni, fettuccine — fatti a mano Daily · from 17:00
II

Il Cellario

Lazio & Campania

Below the dining room,a barrel-vaulted cellar holds two hundred and forty references, weighted unapologetically toward the natural growers of Lazio and Campania. Cesanese del Piglio from Damiano Ciolli, Frascati Superiore from Casale Marchese, Aglianico del Vulture from Cantine del Notaio, and the long, salty Fianos of Pierluigi Zampaglione. Sommelier Marco Devoto pours fourteen bottles by the glass each evening, none above twelve euro, and keeps a standing list of the house amari beside.

240 references · 14 by the glass Cellar tours · Saturdays 16:30
III

La Sala

Via di Santa Sabina, 14

Built into the hill,the room is a single travertine vault discovered behind a plaster wall during the 2013 restoration — likely a fragment of a third-century horreum once attached to the basilica above. Forty-two seats, oil-lamp sconces, white linen, and a long communal table beneath the keystone. Above ground, six tables on a quiet terrace look across to the orange grove of the Savello and, on clear evenings, to the dome of Saint Peter's beyond the Tiber.
