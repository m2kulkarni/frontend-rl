The Thali

An offering of seven compartments arranged upon the banana leaf, after the manner of the Chola court — sambhar, kuzhambu, vadai, masiyal, avial, pachadi, and the closing payasam. Served at noon and at dusk; rice, ghee and buttermilk poured at table without measure.

❋SATVIK · TEMPLE-PURE · NO ONION, NO GARLIC WHERE MARKED❋

Upon The Leaf

— seven compartments, in the order of serving —

Sambhar of Three Lentils mūnru paruppu sāmbhār

₹ 95

Tuvar, moong and chana simmered with drumstick, brinjal and shallot; tempered in cold-pressed gingelly oil with curry leaf, fenugreek and a pinch of asafoetida from Karaikudi.

SATVIK
❦ ❋ ❦

Mor Kuzhambu mōr kuḻampu

₹ 110

Sour buttermilk thickened with soaked tuvar, coconut and green chilli, and crowned with whole ash-gourd cubes. A kitchen heirloom from the Kaveri delta, gentle on the noon stomach.

SATVIK
❦ ❋ ❦

Vazhaipoo Vadai vāḻaippū vaḍai

₹ 80

Banana-blossom hand-chopped fine, bound with chana dal, fennel and dried red chilli; pressed by thumb into rounds and fried in groundnut oil till the edges crackle. Two pieces to a serving.

SATVIK
❦ ❋ ❦

Keerai Masiyal kīrai masiyāl

₹ 70

Country amaranth and arai-keerai mashed with a wooden ladle, finished with one whole green chilli, salt and a tear of gingelly oil. The plainest dish on the leaf, and the most measured.

SATVIK
❦ ❋ ❦

Avial in Coconut-Curd aviyal

₹ 130

Yam, raw banana, snake-gourd, ash-gourd and drumstick cut to the length of a finger and warmed in a paste of fresh coconut, cumin and curd. Coconut oil drizzled in at the last moment.

SATVIK
❦ ❋ ❦

Pachadi of Mango & Ginger māṅkāy iñci pacaṭi

₹ 85

Half-ripe Salem mango grated coarse, set whole-curd, fresh ginger root and split mustard. A cold, bright corner of the leaf to cut the ghee. Served in a small dipped katori.

SATVIK
❦ ❋ ❦

Payasam of Jaggery & Ghee paruppu pradhamam

₹ 140

Yellow moong dal roasted dark, simmered in coconut milk and Marayoor jaggery; broken cashew and raisin browned in ghee from the temple-cow herd at Tiruvaiyaru. The closing sweet, ladled over the centre of the leaf.

SATVIK · RITUAL SWEET

The Set Thalis three feasts, after the courts

Chola ₹ 420

— eleven courses on the leaf —

Steamed Ponni rice, two ghees, sambhar, rasam, mor kuzhambu, two poriyals, keerai masiyal, pachadi, appalam, butter, and paruppu payasam. The everyday thali, served from 12.00 to 15.00 and 19.00 to 22.00.

Pandya ₹ 560

— fifteen courses, with vadai and ghee-rice —

All of the Chola, with vazhaipoo vadai, kootu of black-eyed bean, avial, ven pongal with pepper-ghee, beetroot pachadi, and a second sweet — semiya payasam alongside the paruppu. The midday choice of the temple street.

Vijayanagara ₹ 780

— twenty-one courses · weekend only —

The festival leaf: kalkandu sadam, puliyodharai, curd-rice with pomegranate, mango thokku, three vadais, three poriyals, mor kuzhambu, vatha kuzhambu, milagu rasam, plain rasam, two payasams, jangiri, and Mysore pak fried that morning.

SAT · SUN · 11.30–15.30

Each set thali includes unlimited rice, ghee, sambhar, rasam, buttermilk and a betel-leaf with fennel at close. Served upon a fresh banana leaf cut at dawn from the Mylapore garden.
