The temple-town calendar marks our kitchen as much as the harvest does. Through Pongal smoke, the cardamom of Vishu, the sundal-rice of Navaratri, and the pre-dawn Margazhi tiffin, the mandapa hall holds open its doors. Reserve a seat at the leaf, name your hall, and arrive in time for the first conch.

The Year at the Leaf

— Four Vishesham Days of the Tanjore Calendar —

Pongal Vishesham

Mid-January · 14th–17th · Harvest

The new rice arrives from Thanjavur fields and is boiled to the brim of a brass panai in the courtyard, sugarcane bound at the rim. Our six-course harvest meal opens with sakkarai pongal ladled onto the leaf by the head cook, followed by ven pongal, vatha kuzhambu, avial, mor kuzhambu, and elai vadam, closing with a wedge of jaggery and fresh ginger.

Seatings at 11:30, 13:15, and 15:00. The first leaf is laid only after the milk has overflowed.

₹ 1,250 per leaf · children under ten ₹ 600

Tamil New Year Sadhya

14th April · Puthandu

A twenty-four item Chola-court sadhya laid on a full plantain leaf, opening with the auspicious maanga pachadi — six tastes in one spoon: raw mango, neem flower, jaggery, salt, chilli, tamarind — the year's full bouquet. Followed by kootu, poriyal, kalan, olan, pulissery, sambar, rasam, and three payasams: paruppu, semiya, and ada-pradhaman.

Single seating, 12:30 sharp. Ritual lamp lit at 12:15.

₹ 1,650 per leaf · advance booking only
❀ ❁ ❀

Navaratri Sundal Counter

October · Nine Nights · 17:00–21:00

For the nine nights of the Goddess, the courtyard becomes a counter of nine sundals — one legume to each night: kondakadalai, pattani, mochai, karamani, payaru, thattaipayaru, peanut, chickpea-coconut, and the closing ellu sundal of black sesame. Served in folded jackfruit-leaf cups with tender coconut and a paper packet of murukku. Golu doll-display on the upper landing through all nine evenings.

Walk-in counter; no reservation required.

₹ 180 per cup · ₹ 1,200 nine-night pass
❀ ❁ ❀

Margazhi Tiffin at Dawn

December–January · 04:30–07:00

Through the music season, the kitchen lights at four. By the time the kutcheris of the sabhas across Mylapore release their first ragams, our tables are set with idli & molagai podi, ven pongal in lotus-leaf, kanchipuram idli, rava kesari, filter coffee in dabarah-tumblers, and a small brass cup of panakam. The hall stays silent until 06:00 for those returning from the Tiruppavai recital at Kapaleeswarar.

Seasonal: 16th December – 14th January.

₹ 480 per person · group of six ₹ 2,600
