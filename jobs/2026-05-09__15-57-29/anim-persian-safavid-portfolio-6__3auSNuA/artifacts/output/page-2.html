◆ 2007 ◆

FEATHERS OF THE HOUSE OF SHEIKH HASAN

Restoration of the iwan-soffit in cut tiling — green, turquoise and ochre forming the long-axis cartouche identifying the patron and his lineage.
◆ 2009 ◆

QAṢR-E SHAHBĀZ MAQBARA

A new garden-pavilion ceiling cut in seven-color rosette and starwork, taking its proportion from a single bay of the original timberwork.
◆ 2011 ◆

CHEHEL-SOTUN MIRROR PANEL REPLACEMENT

A 3.4-square-metre patterned glass mirror replacement panel set into the lateral hall, coloured to dye-bath records of the previous wholly transparent mirror layouts.
◆ 2013 ◆

PRIVATE GARDEN PAVILION, KASHĀN

A six-metre dome programme set with seven-colour bands in carmine, lapis and gold-leaf with an inscription cartouche on a serpentine ribbon, with the stalactites and a trace lining issued for the entrance gate.
◆ 2016 ◆

SARQAT MAḌRASA FOUNTAIN SURROUND

A six-pointed eight-leaf rosette surrounding the central fountain, in the manner of the Madrasa-ye Chahār Bāgh, with the half-section repeats taken in the inner hall.
◆ 2018 ◆

BORZUJI ÅBADI READING NOOK

An inset of the cyrene as a half-section in cobalt & lapis on the north-facing wall — fixed in plate, set inside an opening framed of the same period as the lateral wall.
◆ 2021 ◆

EMBASSY OF OMAN RECEPTION HALL

A diplomatic-residence reception programme with a stalactite vault in the manner of Sheikh Lotfollāh, with seven-colour banding for the cornice.
◆ 2024 ◆

JOLFĀIAN PRESS RESTORATION, JOLFĀ

Reconstruction of the seventeenth-century printing-press doorway, with cobalt cornice, finely-traced cartouches in the historical tradition of the Tabrīz manuscript ateliers.

ON COMMISSIONS

The atelier accepts no more than three to four works in any calendar year, drawn from international archival commissions only. All preparatory drawings are issued by hand in atelier sequence.
