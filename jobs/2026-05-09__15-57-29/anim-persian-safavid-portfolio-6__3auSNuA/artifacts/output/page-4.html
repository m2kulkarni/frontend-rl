A BRIEF NOTE

Born in Tabriz, 1973, into a family of carpet-knotters and copper-chasers, Mirza Reza Tabrizi has spent thirty years answering a single question — how the cobalt and turquoise of a haft-rang loop their ways across four centuries — and answers it again each morning, in the rite, the workmanship of a small atelier in Jolfā.

He early on apprenticed to the late Aboulhassan Sadeghi, was for a time a copper apprentice in the Bazaar Tabriz, and was inducted, at twenty-two, to the Ḥassan-i Tarrāḥ workshop where he held the chisel for nine years. The hand-rolled bordeh-tile of his master Ḥaqqī Khurāsānī, master of cut-tile mosaic of the Sheikh Lotfollāh, founded the atelier program: tile in the Sheikh Lotfollāh manner, drawn directly to the master's lineage, in cobalt & lapis with the gold cartouche field of the late period of the masters.

The atelier was founded by him in 1386 H.S. — 2007 — under the patronage of Master Hadžī Sadr Mirza Reza, to consolidate the master's lineage. He works after his master in the patronage of new mosques and madrasas in the Persian-speaking diaspora — and unfolded a study of seven-colour tilework and cobalt cartouches with two of the masters of his lineage. The studio takes private commissions of new mosques and madrasa, with new gathering halls and ceiling-bound dome carving — for the people of his lineage.

CHRONOLOGY

1973
BORN AT TABRIZ
Born in Tabriz to the Tabrīzī family of Tabriz. Apprenticed to copper at twelve, with the lineage's beginning to the Tabriz copper-bazaar's intermediate practice.
1986–1992
EARLY YEARS · TRADITIONAL ARTS
Diploma in tile and architectural ornament with parallel two-month tutorship in calligraphy and the binding of paper-paste cartouche manuals. First master in calligraphy of the Iranian period bound master.
1995–2006
APPRENTICESHIP · ARTHUR LOTFOLLĀH CONFRATERNITY
Tutored on the dye-stone in cobalt for the Sheikh Lotfollāh in Sa'di Khan-e Tabrīz; eight years' attention to the language of the cobalt cartouche of the Sheikh Lotfollāh in cobalt-bound binding.
2007
ATELIER FOUNDED · MIRZA REZA TABRIZI CONFIGURATION
Atelier formally founded in Jolfā Isfahan after the lineage of Tabrizi; the first commission is awarded by the Sheikh-Lotfollāh-confraternity for restoration of the western iwan-soffit, and the lineage of seven-colour set as the principal practice of the atelier.
2013
REVĀQ-KĀRĪ MAKHẒAN COMMISSION
Awarded the patronage of Sheikh Lotfollāh in calligraphy of the cobalt cartouche on the iwan-soffit, dedicated by the western patronage of the Iranian house.
2018
NATIONAL LIVING TREASURE CITATION
Cited by the Cultural Heritage Organization of Iran as a National Living Treasure for the language of cobalt cartouche of the Iranian master, with the inscription on the lineage of Tabrīzī.
2024
INAUGURATED ATELIER A "VAQIDA — MOOR" DUE
Awarded a one-year commission for the cobalt cartouche-pavilion of the new Tabriz cultural pavilion, dedicated by the Sheikh Lotfollāh of Iran in cobalt cartouche-bound binding.
