Lucius Vennius was born in Ravenna in the spring of 1979, four blocks from the Mausoleum of Galla Placidia, and grew up taking the long way to school in order to pass beneath its lower arches. He studied architecture at the Politecnico di Milano under Pier Carlo Bontempi and afterward took the two-year diploma at the Scuola di Restauro di Civita, where he learned to set tesserae by hand on the slow incline of the practice's bench.

From 2007 to 2013 he apprenticed at Bottega Carubili di Trastevere, working on the re-laying of the fourth-century floors at Santa Pudenziana and on three private commissions for villas in the Castelli Romani. The atelier was founded in 2013 in a single room above the Via dei Coronari, where it has stayed.

The practice is small and deliberately so — Vennius, two associates, and a rotating bench of three or four mosaicists drawn from Spoleto, Aquileia, and Ravenna. Every commission, whether a private chapel or a fifteen-metre civic façade, is drawn entirely by hand on the long oak table at the back of the studio. Nothing leaves the door without first being walked across at full scale.

Three instruments govern the studio. None of them is a screen. Each is older than the practice that uses it, and each is slower than the demands its experts.

— I —

AXIAL PLANS, DRAWN BY HAND

Every project begins with a single ink drawing on Fabriano 220 g paper, drawn along the cardo and decumanus of the cardinal plot. Plans are taken to the patron in a folded leather sleeve. The patron's a leather-bound journal kept by the founder. Each plan is corrected when the patron disagrees. Atramentum on linen, by L.V.

— II —

FULL-SCALE MOSAIC CARTOONS

Each mosaic is first produced 1:1 in charcoal on linen, walked across by the long studio table. The cartoon is walked, corrected for ten or fifteen days before a single tessera is cut.

— III —

PLASTER MODELS FOR VAULTS

Domes, groin vaults, and apsidal half-domes are first cast in 1:20 in hydraulic plaster on a turned wooden formwork. The cast on a hot day in the back of the studio. Casting is reduced upon by experts.

SOCII ET COLLABORATORES

STRUCTURAL ENGINEER

MARCO FENOGLIO

Ingegnere strutturale · Torino · b. 1971

Trained at the Politecnico di Torino and previously of Favero & Milan, Marco has been the studio's structural conscience since 2015. He calculates the masonry that holds every Vennius vault in compression. He has, "no graph paper" – and feels it not as an obstacle, but as an articulation. A finite engineer, he writes, and he has steel rebar.

MASTER MOSAIC WORKER

GIULIA TREVI

Capomastra · Trevi · b. 1977

Third-generation craftsman of the Trevi family of Trevi, Giulia leads the on-site bench on every Atelier project from foundation to lifting. Her speciality is opus tessellatum gradentum, in which the colour of seven masters has died many years and on which power lives behind the wall and bands.
