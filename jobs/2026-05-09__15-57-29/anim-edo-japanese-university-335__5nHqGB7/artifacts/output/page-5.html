五CAMPUS & VISITING·校舎・来訪のしおり

Four buildings,
one quiet block of
Higashiyama.

The Hokurin campus occupies a single Higashiyama-ward street block of Sanjō-dōri, four 1762 machiya buildings around a central courtyard. Visitors enter from the Sanjō Gate, ten minutes on foot from Higashiyama Station; we ask, gently, that the courtyard be kept open for residents.

一THE FOUR BUILDINGS本部・人文・工芸・環境
建物 一 · HOMBU

Sanjō Gate & Office

Provost office, registrar, and the visitors' genkan. The original 1762 storefront, restored in 1989.

建物 二 · BUNGAKUKAN

Humanities House

Three reading rooms above the courtyard; the Tōrin Bunko occupies the second floor.

建物 三 · KŌGEIKAN

Craft Sciences

Two atelier benches, a wet-bench room, and the dye archive. Restored separately in 2003.

建物 四 · KANKYŌKAN

Environmental Studies

Soil-science labs and the field-trip outfitting room. Built 2011 in the kura style.

二WALKING DIRECTIONS徒歩のしるべ

Two routes by foot

一
From Higashiyama Station

Exit 2, walk west on Sanjō-dōri 0.6 km. The Sanjō Gate is on the south side, between the lacquerware shop and the public bath.

二
From Sanjō Keihan

Cross the Kamo and walk east on Sanjō-dōri 1.2 km, past Yasaka. About 14 minutes; cross the small bridge by the corner.

三
By city bus

Routes 100 / 206 stop at Higashiyama Sanjō. Two minutes' walk south.

三OPEN-CAMPUS DAYS学校公開日

Twice a year the campus opens its rooms. The visitors' studio shall be the lecture itself, the courtyard's bricks, the conservation studio, and two book carts. No registration is required; printed walking guides in Japanese, English, and French are offered at the Higashiyama genkan.

18

Saturday at Hokurin Spring Open Day. Doors open 10:00; tours 11:00 and 14:00.

26

October — Hokurin Field Visit Day. Field exhibits open the entire ward.

"Twice a year the campus opens its rooms — A Hokurin should be made for the summer alone."
