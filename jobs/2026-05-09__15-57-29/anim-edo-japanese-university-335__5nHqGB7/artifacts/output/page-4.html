四RESEARCH & LIBRARY·研究と図書

Slow scholarship,

The Tōrin Bunko occupies the original 1762 machiya, with three reading rooms, two atelier benches, and a shelf-list of 14,210 woodblock impressions, fascicle-bound and indexed.

四VISITING SCHOLARS客員研究員

The Tōrin Bunko hosts six visiting scholars each academic year, drawn equally from working conservators and university researchers. The residence covers a sleeping room above the genkan, full reading-room access, and an honorarium against the Sanjō field-stipend fund. Applications close 1 May for the following academic year.
