Heritage Precinct & Conservation

The Authority's Heritage Cell, established under G.O. 14 of 1962, exercises civic and customary jurisdiction over the precinct of the Sri Brihannayaki Temple — comprising the four mada veethis, the eastern bathing tank, and one hundred and seventeen subsidiary structures inventoried in the 1968 Stones Register.

Mandate II · Custody

Custody, Use & Custodial Limits

The Authority does not own the temple stones. It is, by the 1962 instrument, the custodian — the steward against weather, ill-use and the slow erosion of an inattentive city.

The Stones Register
Address by the Heritage Cell · 1962

Of stones that remember the village's earliest years.

The 1968 Register lists one hundred and seventeen "named stones" — pillar bases, threshold lintels, processional bollards and the great inscribed stones of the western mada veethi — each catalogued by donor, date and the script in which the donor's name was incised.

Among the oldest is the threshold of the southern prakara, on which a Chola merchant — one Pottiraman of Mannargudi — set a date that reads (under reasonable allowance) AD 1024.
