Section II · Wards & Administration

Twenty-Seven Wards of
Thiruvanam

The municipal limits of Thiruvanam are divided into twenty-seven elected wards across four administrative zones — Sannidhi, Kaveri, Pillaiyar Koil, and Periya Theru. Each ward returns one councillor to the General Council, which sits on the second Tuesday of every month at the Chola Hall, Town Hall complex.

Committee Structure & Zonal Command

Council · Estd. 1947

Two Houses of the General Council

The Authority's Charter (T.N. Government Order 1124-MA) provides for two deliberative houses — the Council in Plenary (all twenty-seven councillors, sitting in the Chola Hall) and the Estimates Committee (eight councillors, drawn by lot at each fiscal turn).

Standing Committees For Public Works

  • · Sanitation & Health — chair Smt R. Lakshmi Devi
  • · Heritage & Antiquities — chair Tmt T. Mangaiyarkarasi
  • · Markets & Levies — chair Thiru G. Murugesan
  • · Estimates — drawn by lot, three years

"The Heritage & Antiquities committee was constituted by G.O. No. 14 of 1962 following the floods, with original mandate to inventory the temple stones of the precinct after the storm."
