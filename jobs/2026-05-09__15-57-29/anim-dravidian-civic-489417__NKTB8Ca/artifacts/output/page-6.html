Visit the Town Hall at 14 Sannidhi Veethi

Approach · Hours · Etiquette

A genteel, well-served walk of 800 metres from the Mufussil Bus Stand. Visitors and petitioners are received by the Reader (Hall 1) immediately on entering, and may consult any office on the floors above.

Approach · Halls

Finding the Right Door

  • Mahomet's Hall (Hall 1) — visitors are received by the Reader. Tokens are issued. All matters of provenance begin here.
  • Pillared Hall (Hall 2) — Grievance Darbar, public hearings, contested-tender openings, and any sitting open to the public.
  • Chola Hall (Hall 3) — Council in Plenary on the second Monday of every month at 10:30 IST. Witness gallery 24 seats.
  • Estimates Hall (Hall 4) — Cashier sittings, Estimates Committee.
  • Reading Wing — public viewing of the registers, drawings, the 1968 Stones Register, and back-bulletins of the Notices.

A Foot Walk · 12 minutes

Walking from the Mufussil Bus Stand

  1. 1Disembark at Mufussil Bus Stand.The bus stand sits 800 metres west of the Town Hall, fronting the Big Bazaar Road.
  2. 2Walk eastward along Big Bazaar Road for 350 metres until you cross the small bridge over the Annaikara channel.
  3. 3Bear right at the bridge, onto Sannidhi Veethi.The street narrows. Vehicular traffic abates. The temple gopuram becomes visible to the south.
  4. 4Continue 350 metres along Sannidhi Veethi.Fourteen on the left side — the columned facade in dressed sandstone, two-storied, set back from the road behind a low railing.
  5. 5Enter through the western gate; the Reader will receive you in Hall 1.
Bearings · For All Halls

From the Reading Hall · For Every Visitor

The 1872 reading hall is the first chamber on the right as one enters from the western gate. Petitioners with appointments will be directed by the Reader to the appropriate floor.

  • Visitors · Please carry photo identification (Aadhaar / driving licence / voter ID).
  • Photography · Permitted in the Reading Wing and the open ground; not in chambered sittings.
  • Refreshments · The lower-floor canteen serves tea, filter coffee and tiffin between 10:00 and 16:30.
  • Accessibility · A ramp adjoins the western gate. The lift is on the southern wing.

"To know a town's care for its citizens, attend its town hall on a Tuesday morning. The patience of the queue, and the courtesy of the Reader, will tell you what its laws cannot."

Need a better appointment?
