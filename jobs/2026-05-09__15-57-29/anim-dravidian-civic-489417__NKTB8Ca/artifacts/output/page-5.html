Services for Residents

The Resident Services wing, headquartered on the ground floor of the Town Hall and devolved across six revenue circles, administers the everyday compact between the township and its citizens — from the registration of a newborn at Counter 4 to the night-soiler cartulation that keeps the Sannidhi Veethi clear by dawn.

Registry · Vital Statistics

Birth & Death Registration

Counter 4 · Town Hall · 1st Floor

Births and deaths occurring within the limits of Thiruvanam Town Panchayat must be reported within 21 days under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969. Hospital intimations are recorded daily by the Registrar.

  • · Standard certificate (first issue)
  • · Additional copies · ₹40 per certified extract
  • · Late registration (21–365 days)
  • · Aadhaar-linked entries (post 1947)

Commerce · Establishments

Trade Licensing

Counter 6 · Mondays Apr 1 – Mar 31

Every establishment offering goods or services within ward limits must hold a current D&O ("Dangerous & Offensive") Trade Licence under Section 249 of the Tamil Nadu District Municipalities Act. Annual renewal sought before the 31st of March.

  • · Petty trader/d-licensee · ₹600 / annum
  • · Eating houses, lodges, mess · ₹2,400 / annum
  • · Inspection by Sanitary Inspector following clean inspection

Public Works · Utilities

Water Connection Applications

Counter 7 · Mon–Sat 10:00 – 17:30

Domestic and commercial connections to the Cauvery Trunk Main are administered jointly with TWAD Board. Site verification by the Junior Engineer (Water) is undertaken within seven working days of fee remittance.

  • · Domestic 1/2" connection · deposit ₹14,500, monthly tariff ₹125
  • · Commercial 3/4" connection · deposit ₹24,000, monthly tariff ₹450
  • · Bulk meter (apartments / 3-units) · prosperity ₹38 / KL
  • · Re-connection after disconnection · ₹250 + tariff arrear
HoursMon–Sat 10:00 – 17:30

Public Health · Built Environment

Building Plan Approvals

Counter 9 · Heritage Cell concur reqd.

Applications are processed under the Tamil Nadu Combined Development and Building Rules, 2019. Plans within the Heritage Precinct (Wards 1–6, & 8–11) require additional clearance from the Heritage Conservation Committee.

  • · Domestic plot ≤ 4,500 sqft · scrutiny ₹4,500 · 30-day disposal
  • · Multi-storey (above G+2) · scrutiny ₹14,500 · 60-day disposal
  • · Heritage Precinct mandatory facade review, six councillor
  • · Completion certificate sought after occupation

Token at Reception

Tokens are issued from 09:30. Limited concession queues for senior citizens and pregnant petitioners.

Counter Filing

Counters operate sectional. Each window admits one petitioner at a time, with documents in original.

Cashier · Hall 4
