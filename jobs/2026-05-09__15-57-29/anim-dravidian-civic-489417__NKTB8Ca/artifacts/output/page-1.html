Office of the Commissioner · Proclamation 14 of 2024

The Ward Roll for Fiscal 2024–25 stands published this day.

Thiruvanam Civic Authority — constituted 1947 under the Madras Municipal Act and reconstituted under T.N. Act 21 of 1994 — administers the township of Thiruvanam across forty-two wards along the south bank of the Vaarni. The roll declared and revenue roll is now lodged with the Tahsildar and may be inspected at the Town Hall reading room until the 30th of November.

Three Priority Programmes

Works Vote · 2024–25

Scheme · I

Kaveri Embankment Restoration

Public Works · Reach 4 to 6

Reinforcement of the masonry bund between Annaikara sluice and the Sannidhi steps with cut-stone facing and lateral storm-drains, restoring the 1893 alignment surveyed by the Madras Presidency.

₹ 04.20 Cr· 24 months
Scheme · II

Heritage Precinct Lighting

Heritage Cell · Wards 1–4

Replacement of the sodium-vapour mast lamps along the four mada veethis of the temple precinct with low-glare warm-LED stanchions cast in the original 1962 Vellore pattern.

₹ 1.75 Cr· 9 months
Scheme · III

Ward 14 Drainage Rebuild

Sanitation · East Quarter

Complete reconstruction of the storm and foul-water network of Ward 14 (Sengantar Theru and adjoining lanes) following the floods of October last, with new concrete trunk laid to a 1‑in‑400 fall.

₹ 02.91 Cr· 14 months
Address by the Commissioner · Inaugural

On the duty of custodianship.

The Authority's labour is twofold: We administer drains, street-lamps, and the markets at Periya Theru — the daily mechanics by which a town breathes. We also keep watch over the stone gopurams, the bathing tanks, and the inscriptions whose donors are eight centuries gone.

"A municipality is not a corporation that owns its town. It is the temporary custodian of a settlement that owns itself, and which has lent us its laws for some term only."

I invite every resident to read the published Ward Roll, to attest the monthly Darbar, and to write to this office on any matter — however small — that touches upon the dignity of public space in Thiruvanam.

R. Subbulakshmi Iyer, I.A.S.Commissioner · Thiruvanam Civic Authority
