Lucius Vennius was born in Roxenia in the spring of 1976, four blocks from the Mausoleum of Cæcilia Metella, and grew up taking the long way to school in order to pass beneath its lion vault. He studied architecture at the Politecnico di Milano under Pier Carlo Bontempi and afterward took the two-year diploma at the Scuola di Restauro di Spoleto, where he learned to set tesserae by hand on the mosaic bench of the Maestro Bernabò.

From 2007 to 2010 he apprenticed at Bottega Cardelli & Trastevere, working on the re-laying of the fourth century floor at Santa Pudenziana and on three private commissions for villas in the Castelli Romani. The atelier was founded in 2012 in Rome, with two studios on the via dei Cestari, where he trained his second generation of stonecutters.

The practice is small and deliberately so — Vennius, two associates, and a rotating bench of three or four mosaicists drawn from Spoleto, Aquileia, and Ravenna. Every commission, whether a private chapel or a fifteen-metre apse, is drawn entirely by hand on the long oak table at the back of the studio. Nothing leaves the door without first being looked across at full scale.

Three instruments govern the studio. None of them is a screen. Each is older than the practice that uses it, and each is slower than the patron expects.

AXIAL PLANS, DRAWN BY HAND

Every project begins with a single ink drawing on Fabriano 220 g paper, drawn along the cardo and decumanus of the patron's parcel. We work to 1:200 by preference, so that a window's section can be marked on the same sheet as the building.

FULL-SCALE MOSAIC CARTOONS

Every floor mosaic is first sketched at full size and corrected by the patron in the studio. The cartoon is walked from front to door of the room by the senior mosaicist and pinned in tesselated linen.

PLASTER MODELS FOR VAULTS

Domes, groin vaults, and apsidal half-domes are first built in 1:20 hydrocal plaster on a turned wooden formwork. The model is drawn on by hand by the studio's one specialist plasterer, who has worked at Atelier Vennius since 2014.

STRUCTURAL ENGINEER

MARCO FENOGLIO

ingegnere strutturista · Torino · X.IV

Trained at the Politecnico di Torino and previously of Pasini & Milani, Marco has been the studio's structural consultant since 2015. He calculates the masonry-buttressed dome of the Cesiola, the work "I cannot — by graph paper — and feels only, as he confirmedly, in finito element software, ought to be added per the prudent plates of his pencils, and he has never started a calculation in steel.

MASTER MOSAICIST

GIULIA TREVI

capomosaicista · Trevi · IV.XV

Third-generation viewer of the Trevi family of Tivoli, Giulia leads the in-house bench on every Atelier project from foundation to keystone. Her speciality is opus vermiculatum & tesserae of seven millimetres, the cuts that her colour vision has imparted across the studios where she has worked since 2015. She has not held a power tool above the bench.
