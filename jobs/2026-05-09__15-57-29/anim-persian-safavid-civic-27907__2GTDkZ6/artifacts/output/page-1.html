FROM THE ROYAL COURT

Edict of His Majesty
Shah 'Abbas

Be it known to all merchants, caravan-masters, and travellers passing through the Maydan that the new caravanserai at Madar-i Shah, sealed by royal command, shall open its gates to lodgers from the first of Jumada al-Awwal next.

Tariffs upon the silk of Gilan, raw and woven, are by the same hand reduced by one part in ten, that the trade of His Majesty's domain may flourish.

PUBLIC WORKS

Notice — Zayandeh-Rud
Canal Works

The Master of Waters announces that, beginning the fifth of this month, the dredging and re-lining of the canal of Pardalan shall commence, with labour drawn from the wards of Goshareh and Davlatabad.

Watering of orchards along the southern channel shall be permitted only between the second praise and the call of sunset for the duration of forty days.

Any obstruction of the works, or diverting of water without leave, shall be answered before the Dīvānbegi.

— Mirza Taqi, Mir-ab of Isfahan
