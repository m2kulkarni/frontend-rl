A Word of Thanks unto the Quarter of Jolfa

The Dīwān records, with the gratitude of the city and the favour of His Majesty, the gift of seven seven hundred toman from the merchants and free citizens of the Armenian quarter to the rebuilding of the Pol-e Khwāju after the spring inundations. Their care for the bridge, which lies upon the road that bears the name of the Master of Builders, has earned the fixed esteem of the people, and as request the master who shall enroll their gracious names within the public ministry. Requested oratorian shall be permitted, by the worship of the season, to offer thanks at the new mihrāb. May the great Lord render unto them their reward in the courts of the merciful.
