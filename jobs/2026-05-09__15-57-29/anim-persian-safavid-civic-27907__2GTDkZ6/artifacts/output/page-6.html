The Path Through the Maydan

Step by Step

  1. Enter the maydan by the Qaysarieh gate, beneath the great inscription.
  2. Pass the rope-makers' arcade upon the left, the carpet-sellers upon the right.
  3. At the second pillar of striped marble, turn toward the Mosque of Shaikh Lutfullah.
  4. The doorway of the Dīvān stands beneath the carved horseshoe arch.
  5. Present yourself to the porter; declare the matter of your visit.
  6. Wait upon the bench in the outer courtyard until summoned.

Plan of the Maydan

Conduct Within the Courtyard

Observed by all who enter, that order may be preserved

You Must & You May

  • Remove your slippers in the outer hall, and leave with the porter.
  • Approach the bench only when called by name.
  • Address the Dīvānbegi as "Janāb"; the clerks as "Mirzā".
  • Bring witnesses or representatives if matter requires.
  • Take a copy of the receipt-stamp before departing.

You Must & Forbear

  • Bear no weapon, even of small blade. The porter shall hold it until your leaving.
  • Speak not over the voice of the clerk; await your appointed turn.
  • Smoke the qalyān only within the eastern arcade.
  • Bring no animal save service-dogs; mounts to the rear hitching-post.
  • Touch not the seal upon the door, nor the registers laid upon the bench.

By the seal of the Dīvānbegi,
and the welfare of the city of Isfahan, may all who come depart in peace.
