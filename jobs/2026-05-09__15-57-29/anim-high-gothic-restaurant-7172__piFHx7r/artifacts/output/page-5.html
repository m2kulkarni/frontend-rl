VEUX & CÉRÉMONIES

DÉJEUNERS DE NOCES

A small bridal-day banquet at the long-board: chestnut cream with a glaze of saffron, brochettes du jour, mardi or vendredi, an oak vinegar served in pewter pichets, hippocras, and an hour of bread & candle following the Mass.

SOUPERS DE BAPTÊME

A small christen-supper at the cellar: bread of pre-baked chestnuts, a softened veal & cumin pottage of the Hôtel-Dieu, and the godparents' candles around the long-board.
