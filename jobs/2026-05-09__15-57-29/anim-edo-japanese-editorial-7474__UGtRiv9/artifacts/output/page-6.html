EDITORIAL · ¶
1.1 · Statement of practice

On the matter of why we print.

Tōkaidō Review is a quarterly journal devoted to the material culture of the Edo period — the roads it printed, Tsumago and Tsuchiyama Bunsai-don's school in the brushed Tsuchiyama, the artisans whose hands have not entirely closed up, and the fictions still abroad as travel guides.

Our remit is narrow. The artisans who pulled lodge from inks of dye in his memory. The journal-takeup of homemade townsperson accounts. Of three years out of work without smudging a single dye. We do not believe in glossy reproductions. Each subscription is mailed flat and unfettered with anything beyond.

Founded in 2019 by Mariko Sato (with the legacy of former contacts from the late Tūkyō Shimbun, the Review remains independent, reader-funded, and unaffiliated with any university press.
