Museum & Society

Founded 1977, with the gardens of Chahar Bagh, when a small society of Isfahani scholars, conservators, and surveying engineers of the city's heritage gathered, in the wake of the city's restoration, to give the city its proper hall.

A Brief Chronicle of the Museum

1977
Founded by a small society of Isfahani scholars and conservators in the gardens of Chahar Bagh; the museum opens with three halls and a single conservator.
1983
Re-opens after the renovation of the east wing. The Royal Atelier Library is established, with seed gifts from the Mostofi and Ekbatan estates.
2008
The Reconstructed Iwān, a half-scale replica of the south-facing Iwān of the Mosque-e Shah, is opened in Hall VII after four winters of work.

Charter of the Museum

The Museum of Isfahan exists, by its charter, to gather, to conserve, and to interpret the arts of the late Safavid court — the painting and manuscript, the silken weave, the ornament of the mosque — and to make these as available to the citizen, the student, and the scholar as their fragility allows.

It exists also to keep open a slow corner of the city, in which the lover of pattern, of the Persian carpet, of the line, the calligrapher's nib, may sit and look — and to this end the museum opens its doors at little cost, six days of the seven, to all comers.

— Introduced & Sealed by Esmaeil Pakari ·

Office of the Director

Dr. Hossein Mostofi

DIRECTOR

Director since 2014. Before, head of research at the Tehran Carpet Institute. Has written widely on the late Safavid carpet and on the conservation of dye in silk. He may be addressed by appointment at the Office of the Director.

Dr. Pardis Keramati

DEPUTY DIRECTOR · HEAD OF RESEARCH

Deputy since 2018. Before, conservator at the Bibi-Khanum mosque restoration. She is the museum's principal contact for symposium proposals, fellowships, and the visiting scholar programme.
