Chef's note — Wanmono Course

For the soup course — usually a clear dashi of matsutake in autumn or hamaguri in spring — chef Sōzō prefers a gently warmed junmai, drawn to 42 °C (nuru-kan), never hotter.

Heat lifts the rice's quiet sweetness without scattering its grain; a Yamahai or kimoto from Akishika or Tengumai answers the broth more honestly than any chilled daiginjō. Ask the counter — we will pour a small cup alongside.
