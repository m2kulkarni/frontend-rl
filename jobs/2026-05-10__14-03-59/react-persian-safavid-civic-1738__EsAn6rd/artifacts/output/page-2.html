Edicts & Public Notices

From the Bureau of the Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān, Iṣfahān

By the grace of the Pādishāh and under the seal of the Dīvān-i Shahr, the following ordinances and proclamations were affixed to the gates of the Qaysariyya, the Masjid-i Jāmiʿ, and the four caravanserais of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, beginning the fourteenth day of Rabīʿ al-Awwal in the year one thousand one hundred and eight of the Hijra. Criers of the Edicts, Garlands ʿAbdul-Vahab and Riḍāyull qasimī are charged to attend their reading.

01 RABĪʿ AL-THĀNĪ 1108 / 14 October 1696

On the Standard Weight of the Common Loaf (nān-i sangak)

Issued by Mīrzā ʿAbd al-Bāqī, Mukhassib of the Royal Maydān

Be it known to every baker (nānvā) of the four quarters that, by the command of the Dīvān, the loaf of sangak shall not weigh less than nine-and-a-half (9½) misqāl-i Iṣfahānī when drawn fresh from the oven. The price is fixed at one shāhī and a half. Any baker found light by the inspector's scale shall forfeit one day's flour to the poor of the Masjid-i Jāmiʿ.

07 RABĪʿ AL-THĀNĪ 1108 / 20 October 1696

Repair of the Sapphire Fountain (saqqā-khāna) in the Jūbāra Quarter

Issued by Ḥājjī Luṭf-ʿAlī, Inspector of Waters

The spout-fed fountain at the head of the Kūcha-yi Yaʿqūbī Beg, having fallen into ruin since the floods of Muḥarram, is hereby ordered restored. Master mason Ustād Jaʿfar of Ardistān is appointed; the cost of forty-two tūmāns shall be drawn from the waqf of the late Mīrzā Tāqī. Residents of Jūbāra are forbidden to draw upon the cistern of the Madrasa-yi Chahār Bāgh in the interim.

12 RABĪʿ AL-THĀNĪ 1108 / 25 October 1696

Prohibition of Grazing within the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān

Issued by Mullā Ḥasan, Imām of the Quarter

No man shall lead sheep, goat, mule, nor camel upon the gravel of the Royal Square between the Qaysariyya gate and the Masjid-i Shāh. The harshness of the cobblers' villages shall pass instead by the way of Darvāza-yi Dasta-ābād. A first transgression carries a fine of two dīnārs; the second, the confiscation of the beast for the royal stables.

16 RABĪʿ AL-THĀNĪ 1108 / 29 October 1696

On the Sweeping of the Bāzār-i Buzurg before the Friday Prayer

Issued by Āqā Riḍā, Warden of the Quarters

Each shopkeeper of the long bāzār, from his door post to the line of the coppersmiths, shall sweep his threshold and water the dust thereof one hour before the noon call of every Friday. Inspectors of this office shall pass at the third pang, whose fail in this charge shall be cited as the office's rebuke's response.

23 RABĪʿ AL-THĀNĪ 1108 / 05 November 1696

Concerning the Quality of Indigo Sold in the Sarā-yi Cubbān

Issued by Mīrzā Bāqir, Examiner of Dyes

Merchants importing indigo of Bandar and of Sarāfaq shall present each batch to the dye-examiner before opening sale. Adulteration with charcoal or with the dust of pomegranate is forbidden, the penalty being the burning of the parcel in the maydān and a fine of ten tūmāns to the treasury of the Dīvān.
