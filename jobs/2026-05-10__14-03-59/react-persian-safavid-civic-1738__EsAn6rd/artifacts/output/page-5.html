The Four Principal Mādīs

Of the divisions of the Zāyande-rūd unto the gardens and canals of the city

Mādī Nāṣirī

First turn of the Zāyande-rūd; gardens of Jūlfā and the dye-courts of Bīd-ābād. Audited each Friday.

Mādī Niyāsarm

The middle ditch of the Bridge of Pul-i Ḥasanābād; serves four orchards and the bath of Mīrzā Salīm.

Mādī Faraḥābād

Ditch of the southern gardens; serves the great pomegranate orchards of Saʿādatābād.

Mādī Šāhsavanī

Eastern ditch of the brickyards; the cattle-troughs and the courtyards of the Madrasa-yi Saljūqī.
