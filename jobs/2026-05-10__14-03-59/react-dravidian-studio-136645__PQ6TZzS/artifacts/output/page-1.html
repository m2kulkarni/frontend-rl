A fifth-generation atelier of Sthapatis on East Chitra Veedhi, Madurai — founded by Sthapati Vaidvarutha Ramaswamy in 1887 within sight of the Meenakshi Amman gopurams. We draw, consult, and carve in the Dravida tradition: vastu for new mandapas, polychromy for restored gopurams, and granite figuration cut by hand.

The Threefold Practice

From the drafting hall on East Chitra Veedhi the workshop attends to three disciplines, each governed by the Mayamata and the Manasara silpa-sastra, each priced by hand-day and carried in Krishnagiri black granite or Sadarahalli grey.

I · Vastu Consultation
New Mandapas
Site orientation, axial calculation, and full ground-plan drawings for kalyana mandapas, vivaaha shrines, and village avanam temples. Each commission begins with a three-day visit to read the soil, the water-table, and the cardinal axes. Drawings are inked on cotton paper at 1:40 and accompanied by a written vastu-sutra prayoga from the table.
From ₹1,80,000
II · Gopuram Restoration
Polychromy & Stucco
Survey, condition report, and full-scale colour cartoon for the suvla, sikha, and stucco figures of storied gopurams. We work in lime and jaggery render and the twelve mineral pigments — vermilion, peacock, orpiment, ultramarine — fixed without acrylics. Scaffold engineering and bell-metal kalasha replacement are carried out with allied workshops in Pannimadai.
From ₹4,40,000
III · Granite Carving
Figurative Commissions
Hand-carved moolavar and utsava figures, pillared yali brackets, and door-jamb dwarapalakas, cut to silpa-shastra proportion for marquee craftsmen. Murugan and his five apprentices. Standard size for a moolavar Ganesha in Krishnagiri black is thirty-six angula, delivery to fourteen months from the day of stone selection.
From ₹6,25,000

Current Commissions

Three works are presently under the chisel and the brush, each accepted with the blessing of the temple trustees and the family priest.

I
Rajagopuram Repainting · Tiruvannamalai
Eleven-tier polychrome renewal for the western Rajagopuram of the Jambukeswarar temple — 1,488 stucco figures inventoried, pigments mixed on site in lime and jaggery, scaffold raised through the monsoon shadow.

II
Sixteen-Pillared Kalyana Mandapa · Kumbakonam
A new shoulara-mandapa madhya beside the Sarangapani tank, in Sadarahalli grey granite. Sixteen yali-bracketed pillars, a central lotus ceiling medallion, and four cornice dwarapalakas drawn from our 1923 pattern-book.
Carving

III
Private Navaratri Mandapa · Chettinad
A nine-stepped kola-mandapa for the Aalagappa family of Karaikudi — teak frame on a black granite plinth, with lime-stucco kudaarchais and gold-leaf vimanas on the cornice. Consecration set for Panguni 2026.
