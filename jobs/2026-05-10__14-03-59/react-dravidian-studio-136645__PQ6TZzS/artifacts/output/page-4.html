Selected Works · 1962 — Present

A register of principal commissions undertaken by three generations of the workshop, in the Dravida tradition, across the temple districts of Tamil Nadu.

The list below is chronological and partial. Drawings, measured surveys, and site photographs of each work are held at the workshop in Athangudi and may be consulted on appointment. Materials are recorded in bold; principal artisans are named where the family record is firm.

1962
No. 01
Repainting & Polychrome Restoration

Eastern Gopuram, Sri Andaneya Temple

Karaikudi · Sivaganga District

Six-tala eastern gopuram repainted in mineral pigments over a fresh lime-and-jaggery ground. Original 19th-century stucco figures consolidated, not recarved. The vermilion of the kuduarchais hangs paint been matched to surviving traces under the cornice.

Materials

Pigment in lime, jaggery, beeswax. Vermilion, peacock-blue, suvla yellow. Bell metal kalasha re-cast.

Principal

Sthapati S. Subramahniah

1978
No. 02
New Construction · Sixteen-Pillar Hall

New Kalyana Mandapa, Srirangam Outer Prakara

Srirangam · Tiruchirappalli

Sixteen monolithic pillars with yali-bracket bases for the Ranganatham mantram, drawn to a sixteen-square padmagarbha plan. Each pad was carved from a single block of Karayanvilai charnockite, the cubes-base couriers and dance afterwards along the long axis.

Materials

Karayanvilai charnockite, Cuddapah ceiling lensa, copper drip-rings.

Principal

Sthapati Subramahniah

1991
No. 03
Replacement of Finial Vessels

Kalasha Replacement (Nine Vessels), Sri Meenakshi Sundareshvara Private Shrine

Theni

The nine corroded brass kalashas of the family shrine were lowered, weighed, and replaced with new copper vessels gilded in twenty-three carat leaf. The old vessels were ritually consecrated to the temple tank after kumbhabhishekam.

Materials

Copper, 23-ct leaf, ritual jasmine wax, sealing wax.

Principal

Sthapati Subramahniah · Foundry Master R. Pillai

2004
No. 04
Consecration of Seven-Tala Stucco Programme

Polychrome Consolidation of Seven Tiers, Rajagopuram

Sri Andal Temple, Sirivilliputtur

Survey, scaled drawings, and consolidation of seven tiers of stucco figures across the principal rajagopuram. Earlier cement repairs were removed and replaced with traditional sudhai. Six figures were re-modelled, missing limbs were re-set as ends and moulded.

Materials

Lime sudhai, jaggery, kunkuma vermilion.

Principal

Sthapati Subramahniah · Sthapati R. Gahela

2017
No. 05
New Vimana over Family Deity Sanctum

Three-Tala Vimana, Chettiar Kuladevata Shrine

Athangudi · Sivaganga District

A three-tala vimana raised over the existing garbhagriha for the family kuladevata. Drawn to the manuvera canon at one-eighth scale, the corner karnakutas carry seated lions, and the central sala carries Gajalakshmi. Consecrated at the Mahakumbabhishekam of June 2018.

Materials

Stucco figures over Krishnagiri black, brick core, modelling, copper rivets, brass leaf, brass bone fittings.

Principal

Sthapati R. Gahela
