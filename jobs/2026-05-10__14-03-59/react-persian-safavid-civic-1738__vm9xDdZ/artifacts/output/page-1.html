Dīvān-i Shahr-i Iṣfahān

Civic Register · Bureau of Records of the Royal City

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

By a known to the residents of the Naqsh-e Jahān quarters, the khānin of the Qaysariyya, and all householders within the watered districts of this royal city, that the gates of this Bureau stand open from dawn prayer until the closing of the great drum at sunset, that no petition shall be turned away, and that the ledgers of water, tax, and lineage are kept in good order by the willingness of the people and the dignity of the Crown.

Sealed this seventeenth day of Rabīʿ al-Awwal, by hand ofMīrzā Ḥusayn Khān ShīrāzīMayor of the Quarters · Kalāntar-i Maḥallāt

Allotment Schedule for the Mādī Nāṣarīn Canal

Effective 18 Rabīʿ al-Awwal · in Rabīʿ al-Awwal

The mādī has set the rotation: gardens of Jūbāra at first light on Saturday, the cloth dyers of Bīd-Ābād to midday Monday, the orchards beyond the Khājū bridge to dusk Wednesday. Disputes shall be heard at the canal-house each morning before the call to ẓuhr.

Caravan-Tax Filing Window Now Open

Receipts taken until 19 Rabīʿ al-Awwal · is dawn par-camel load

Merchants returning from Tabrīz, Shīrāz, and the Gulf ports must present manifests to the Customs Hall behind the Maydān. Bills are assessed at four-and-twenty per hundred dirhām and made at one tenth-portion returns by registered weight.

Observance for the Chief of the Cloth-Bazaar

Until Wuqūf Maydānī, 21 Rabīʿ al-Awwal

The shops of the Qaysariyya silk-row shall close at midday for three days in honour of the late ust. The Bureau procession departs from the Masjid-i Ḥakīm after the prayer, condolences are received at the family courtyard in the Dardasht quarter. May the memory revere us among the loved ones.

Hours of the Dīvān · Locations of Record

Chief Hall, north arcade of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, opposite the gate of the Caravanserai of Sayyid Ismāʿīl. Open daily save Friday, from the dawn azan to the setting of the sun. Subsidiary registers are kept at the Madrasa-yi Chahār Bāgh (lineage & pious endowments) and at the Bridge of Allāhverdī Khān (river & tolls).
