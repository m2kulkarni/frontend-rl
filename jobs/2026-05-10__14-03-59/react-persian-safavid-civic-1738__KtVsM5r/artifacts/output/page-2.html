Edicts & Public Notices

From the bureau of the Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān, Iṣfahān

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

y the grace of the Shāhanshāh and under the seal of the Dīvān-i Shahr, the following firmāns and bayanāt proclamations were affixed to the gates of the Qaysariyya, the Masjid-i Jāmiʿ, and the four caravanserais of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, beginning the fourteenth day of Rabīʿ al-Awwal in the one thousand one-hundred-and-eight of the Hijra. Citizens of the Jūbāra, Dardasht, ʿAbbās-ābād, and Bīdābād quarters are charged to attend their reading.

Index of FirmānsAḥkām (47)Ittilāʿāt (29)Iʿlānāt (53)Customs & Excise (12)Common to Register IV (8)
[motif: islamic-star]FIRMĀN №01

12 Rabīʿ AL-AWWAL · 1108 (16 October 1696)

By Mīr ʿAbd al-Bāqī, Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān

On the Standard Weight of the Common Loaf (nān-i sangak)

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

Be it known to every baker (nānvāʾī) of the four quarters that, by command of the Dīvān, the loaf of sangak shall not weigh less than nine (9 ½) of a Tabrīzī when drawn fresh from the oven. The price is fixed at one shāhī and a half. Any baker found light by the inspector shall be fined for the first offense, and double for the second. Any thrice shall forfeit his stall.

[motif: islamic-star]FIRMĀN №02

15 Rabīʿ AL-AWWAL · 1108 (19 October 1696)

Issued by Ḥājjī Ismāʿīl, Inspector of Waters

Repair of the Public Fountain (saqqā-khāna) in the Jūbāra Quarter

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

The qanāt-fed fountain at the head of the Kūcha-yi Khwājagān-bābādī, having fallen into ruin since the floods of Muḥarram, is hereby ordered restored. Master mason ʿUmīd Khān of Ardistān is appointed; the cost of forty-two tomans shall be drawn from the waqf of the late Mīrzā Saqī. Residents of Jūbāra are forbidden to draw upon the cistern of the Madrasa-yi Chahār-Bāgh in the interim.

[motif: islamic-star]FIRMĀN №03

18 Rabīʿ AL-AWWAL · 1108 (22 October 1696)

Issued by the Dīvān of the Qaysariyya

Prohibition of Grazing within the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

No man shall lead sheep, goat, mule, nor camel upon the grounds of the Royal Square between the Qaysariyya gate and the Masjid-i Shāh. The keepers of the southern villages shall pass beyond by the way of Darvāza-yi Ḥasan-ābād. A first transgression carries a fine of two ʿabbāsīs; the second, the confiscation of the beast for the royal stables.

[motif: islamic-star]FIRMĀN №04

21 Rabīʿ AL-AWWAL · 1108 (25 October 1696)

Āqā Ridā, Warden of the Qaysariyya

On the Sweeping of the Bāzār-i Buzurg before the Friday Prayer

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

Each shopkeeper of the long bāzār, from the spice court to the head of Mīnā Saqī unto the gate of the coppersmiths, shall sweep his threshold and water the dust thereof one hour before the noon call of every Friday. Inspection of the office shall pass at the third gong; whose fall to be cleared shall be cured to the river at the offender's expense.

[motif: islamic-star]FIRMĀN №05

23 Rabīʿ AL-AWWAL · 1108 (27 November 1696)

By Mīrzā Bāqir, Examiner of Dyes

Concerning the Quality of Indigo Sold in the Sarā-yi Cubhan

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

Merchants importing indigo of Bardasīr and Sarābāj shall present each bale to the dye examiner before opening sale. Adulteration with charcoal or with the lees of pomegranate is forbidden, the penalty being the burning of the parcel in the maydān and a fine of five tomans to the treasury of the Dīvān.

[motif: islamic-star]

Copied truly from the great register of the Dīvān-i Shahr, this fair sheet is bound for the wall of the Qaysariyya gate, that no man of the city may plead ignorance of its command.— Mīrzā Yaḥyā, scribe of the third desk
