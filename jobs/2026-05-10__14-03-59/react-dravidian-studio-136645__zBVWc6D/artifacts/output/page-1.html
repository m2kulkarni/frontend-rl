The Threefold Practice

From the drafting hall on East Chitra Veedhi the workshop attends to three disciplines, each governed by the Mayamata and the Mānasāra śilpa-śāstras, each prized by hand-day and carried in Krishnagiri black granite or Sadarahalli grey.

I · VĀSTU CONSULTATION
New Mandapas
Site orientation, axial calculation, and full ground-plan drawings for kalyāṇa maṇḍapas, navagraha shrines, and village utsavan temples. Each commission begins with a three-day visit to read the soil, the water-table, and the cardinal stars.

Drawings are inked on cotton paper at 1:48 and accompanied by a written vāstu mantra proportion table.
FROM₹ 1,80,000
II · GOPURAM RESTORATION
Polychromy & Stucco
Survey, condition report, and full-scale colour cartoon for the śuka, niḷā, and stucco figures of storied gopurams. We work in lime and jaggery render and the twelve mineral pigments — vermilion, peacock, orpiment, ultramarine — fixed without acrylics.

Scaffold engineering and bell-metal kalaśa replacement are carried out with allied workshops in Swamimalai.
FROM₹ 4,40,000
III · GRANITE CARVING
Figurative Commissions
Hand-carved vimāna and utsava figures, pillared yāḷi-brackets, and door-jamb dvārapālakas, cut to uchavu sūtra proportion by master craftsmen K. Murugesan and his five apprentices.

Standard size for a seated Gaṇeśa in Krishnagiri black is thirty-six aṅgula, delivery in fourteen months from the day of stone selection.
FROM₹ 6,25,000

· Current Commissions ·

Three works are presently under the chisel and the brush, each accepted with the blessing of the temple trustees and the family priest.

I
Rājagopuram Repainting · Tiruvanaikāval
Eleven-tier polychromy renewal for the western Rājagopuram of the Jambukeshwarar temple — 1,890 stucco figures inventoried, pigments mixed on site in lime and jaggery, scaffold raised through the monsoon shadow.
IN PROGRESS2024 — 2026
II
Sixteen-Pillared Kalyāṇa Maṇḍapa · Kumbakonam
A new akṣaja-vimāna kalyāṇa maṇḍapa beside the Sarangapani tank, in Sadarahalli grey granite. Sixteen yāḷi-bracketed pillars, a central lotus ceiling medallion, and four corner dvārapālakas drawn from our 1923 pattern-book.
CARVING2025 — 2027
III
Private Navarātri Maṇḍapa · Chettinad
A nine-stepped lola-maṇḍapa for the Aṇṇāgūḍi family of Karaikudi — teak frame on a black granite plinth, with lime stucco kuttumalas and gold-leaf coursing on the cornice. Consecration set for Navarātri 2026.
