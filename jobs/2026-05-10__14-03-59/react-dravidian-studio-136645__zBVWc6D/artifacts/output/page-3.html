Practice & Disciplines

One hundred and thirty-seven years from the four śilpa-skandhas handed down from Vaidyanātha Sthapati. Each is summarised below, followed by the principal craftsman who attends.

I. Vāstu-Puruṣa Maṇḍala Layout
I

If a new shrine and gopuram-mound are to be marked, the workshop attends to the layout ceremony first by the orientation of the gopuram in relation to the gnomon. We pace the perimeter in 64 paramāṇu-padās, set the angle pakshachatuṣṭaya, and assignment of the deities of the directions to their plinth squares. Twelve mantra-painted granite stakes — driven on the morning of the new moon — fix the diagram. The principal and the apprentice sleep one night on the marked ground before the first pickaxe is lifted from the cart.

PRINCIPAL · K. SABESH RAMASWAMY Two-day Vāstu reading 14 paramāṇu sticks Granite blazing 4 wooden stakes
II. Tāḷamāna & Gopuram Tier Design
II

A design supersession from the modest 64-tāḷa three-storied form of a village agrahāra to the nine-tier rājagopurama of a dynastic-foundation, finds itself confronted by the orders of proportion. Each tier is calculated in 8-tāḷa rules, with the column rule taken from the third chapter of the Mānasāra (Pūjyapāda's redaction), and the height of the antarāḷa from sthāna-bhāga's 9-bhuva proportion. We attend the carving of the kalaśa with cinder.

TĀḶAMĀNA · MĀNASĀRA · SUKANĀSIKA Three to nine seasons 11-tier max in our hand 6-month modelling Lime stucco rendering
III. Polychromy Restoration & Conservation
III

Aṣṭa-figures on gopuram, vining murals on the maḍapas, and the Pulvāṇgika column-staff: the chronologies of stucco have been organized by the bonded craftsmen of the workshop in a 1948 register that documents the seven mineral pigments — vermilion, peacock, orpiment, indigo, smalt, ultramarine, and brick-red. Lime is fixed by the master Iyer in white-of-jaggery slurry; cartoons are inked at one-eighth scale and worked in fresco-buon by Tarun and Murugesan.

PIGMENTS · SHILPI YOGAṆ NĀRĀYAṆAN Twelve mineral pigments Lime + jaggery slurry 3-month cure cycle 1948 ledger maintained
IV. Granite & Krishnagiri Idol Carving
IV

On every yard at Tirumalai-quarry grey granite for structural members — pillars, beams, adhiṣṭāna courses — and the prized black Krishnagiri 4-rim for figural work. The asphalt-and-iron yard, established in 1962, has the only fluted-blade saw in this district. Forty-three blocks lie in seasoned rest year-on-year, the oldest dressed in 1996. Each block is consecrated with mantras and a basil leaf burned in the eastern fire-pit before the chisel.

STONE & SEASONING · K. MURUGESAN Sadarahalli & Krishnagiri Seasoned 4–6 years Fluted-blade saw 43 stocked blocks
V. Drawing-Board & Consultation
V

Incoming kalyāṇa-pālā — the pre-shaped board that sits at the apex of every renovation and gopuram — is, in our family, a passive duty. The senior most son in attendance must show the new drawing on the projection-board to the principal. We hear by the noon-bell only the proportion contests and rectification, and gauge the proper inscription of the cipher of the workshop's mark into the corner. The matter then descends to the three apprentices.

BOARD · SUBRAMANIAM SHILPI Pencil & ink Cotton-paper, 1:48 scale Third mark cipher Filed with the principal
