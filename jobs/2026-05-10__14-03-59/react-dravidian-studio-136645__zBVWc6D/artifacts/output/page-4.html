Selected Works · 1962 — Present

A register of principal commissions undertaken by three generations of the workshop, in the Drāviḍa tradition, across the temple districts of Tamil Nadu.
The list below is chronological and partial. Drawings, measured surveys, and site photographs of each work are held at the workshop in Madurai and may be consulted on appointment. Materials are recorded as built; principal artisans are named where the family record is firm.
1962No. 01
RENOVATION & POLYCHROME RESTORATION
Eastern Gopuram, Sri Anjaneya Temple
Karaikudi, Sivagangai District
Six-tier eastern gopuram repainted in mineral pigments over a fresh lime-and-jaggery ground. Original 19th-century stucco figures consolidated, not recarved. The vermilion of the kuttumalas-parapet band was matched to surviving traces under the cornice.
Particulars
  • Drāviḍa S.
  • Renovation
  • Lime stucco
  • Jaggery binder
  • Six-tier kalaśa
  • Mantra-painted
  • Indigo, smalt, brick-red, ochre & ultramarine
1978No. 12
NEW CONSTRUCTION · SIXTEEN-PILLAR HALL
New Kalyāṇa Maṇḍapa, Sriraṅgam Outer Prākāra
Sriraṅgam, Tiruchirappalli
Sixteen monolithic pillars with yāḷi-bases in the Vijayanagara manner, drawn to a sixteen-square padmagarbha plan. Each yāḷi was carved from a single block of Kanjamalai charnockite; the riders bear the conch and discus alternately along the long axis.
Particulars
  • Vijayanagara
  • Kanjamalai stone
  • 16-square plan
  • Cotton scaffold
  • Hand-cut riders
  • Eight craftsmen
  • Consecrated by Śri Tiruvenkaḍam Iyer
1991No. 24
REPLACEMENT OF FIGURAL STUCCO
Kalaśa Replacement (Nine Vessels), Sri Meenakshi Sundareshwarar Private Shrine
Theni
The nine corroded brass kalaśas of the family shrine were lowered, weighed, and replaced with new copper vessels gilded in twenty-three-carat leaf. The old vessels were ritually consigned to the temple tank after kumbhābhiṣekam.
Particulars
  • Drāviḍa S.
  • Re-consecration
  • Brass to copper
  • 23ct leaf
  • 9 vessels
  • Tank-consigned
  • Pradosha rites observed
2004No. 38
CONSOLIDATION OF SEVEN-TIER STUCCO PROGRAMME
Polychrome Consolidation of Seven Tiers, Rajagopuram
Sri Andal Temple, Srivilliputtur
Survey, scaled drawings, and consolidation of seven tiers of stucco figures across the principal rajagopuram. Earlier cement repairs were removed and replaced with traditional valluvar. No figures were re-modelled; missing limbs were left as voids and recorded.
Particulars
  • Drāviḍa S.
  • Conservation
  • Cement removal
  • Lime + valluvar render
  • Andal lime-shell
  • Twelve craftsmen
  • Seven tiers each side
2017No. 52
NEW VIMĀNA FOR FAMILY DEITY SANCTUM
Three-Tāḷa Vimāna, Chettiar Kuladevatā Shrine
Athaṅguḍi, Sivagangai District
A three-tāḷa vimāna raised over the existing garbhagṛha for the family kuladevata. Drawn to the maumāra canon at one-eighth scale; the corner kuṭas-niche were carried into and the central sūkṭas-pillar centred. Consecrated at the Mahākumbhābhiṣekam of June 2018.
Particulars
  • Drāviḍa S.
  • New construction
  • Three tāḷas
  • Sadarahalli stone
  • Brick over copper
  • Family-deity
  • Coral, basil-leaf, śatamāna
