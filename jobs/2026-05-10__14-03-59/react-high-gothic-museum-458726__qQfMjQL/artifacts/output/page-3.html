Current Exhibitions

Three temporary exhibitions on view through Candlemas, the second day of February in the year of our Lord two thousand and twenty-five.

[motif: quatrefoil]
[motif: arch-pointed]
Exhibition · I

The Cobalt of Chartres

North Transept Gallery · Through 2 February

A close study of the pigment chemistry behind the famed bleu de Chartres, presented alongside side-by-side panels drawn from the Bulle Verriere, with cross-section microphotography and trace-element analysis from the Bullet Lavin.

Curator
Dr. Hélène Mousse
Panels
14 stained-glass leaves, c. 1180–1230
Admission
Included with general entry
Audio Guide
FR · EN · DE · LATIN, 45 min
[motif: arch-pointed]
Exhibition · II

Hand of the Master: the Reims Sketchbooks

South Cloister · Through 3 February

Thirty-eight silverpoint drawings attributed to the workshop of Villard de Honnecourt, presented for the first time in their bound order. Architectural elevations, line studies, and a remarkable diagram of a self-winding water clock.

Curator
Prof. Aldair Tousine-Réamur
Drawings
38 leaves, silverpoint on prepared vellum
Vitrines
6 oak frames, low-iron glass
Workshops
Tuesday vespers, 30 minutes
[motif: arch-pointed]
Exhibition · III

Gold and Lapis: a Book of Hours from Bourges

Reliquary Chamber · Through 2 February

A private collection manuscript opened to the consecration miniatures, with its leaf turned each Friday at the eleventh hour by conservator Frère Anselme Vandresel, who will speak briefly before each turning.

Origin
Bourges atelier, c. 1410–1420
Pigment
Gold leaf, lapis lazuli, vermillion
Page Turning
Fridays, XIIth bell, free
Capacity
24 visitors per turning
