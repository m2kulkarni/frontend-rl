SELECTED WORKS · 1962 — PRESENT

A register of principal commissions undertaken by three generations of the workshop, in the Dravida tradition, across the temple districts of Tamil Nadu.

The list below is chronological and partial. Drawings, measured surveys, and site photographs of each work are held at the workshop in Athangudi and may be consulted on appointment. Materials are recorded as built; principal artisans are named where the family record is firm.
1962No. 03
Repainting & Polychrome Restoration
Eastern Gopuram, Sri Anjaneya Temple
Karaikudi, Sivaganga District
Six-tala eastern gopuram repainted in mineral pigments over a fresh lime-and-jaggery ground. Original 19th-century stucco figures consolidated, not recarved. The vermilion of the kumkuma-panjara band was matched to surviving traces under the cornice.

Principal

Sthapati S. Ramaswamy

Materials

Lime mortar, jaggery, kumkuma decoction, mineral vermilion, peacock blue indigo, gold leaf at kalasha-finial

Duration

Eighteen months, four monsoon seasons

1978No. 07
New Construction · Sixteen-Pillar Hall
New Kalyana Mandapa, Sriranjam Outer Prakara
Srirangam, Tiruchirappalli
Sixteen monolithic pillars with yali bases in the Vijayanagara manner, drawn to a sixteen-square padmagrabha plan. Each yali was carved from a single block of Karatuvelan charnockite; the riders bear conch and danas alternating along the long axis.

Principal

Sthapati P. Ramaswamy with junior K. Krishnan, S. Murugappa

Materials

Karatuvelan charnockite, jaggery-lime mortar, gold-leaf at hood and finial

Duration

Forty months, three monsoon seasons

1991No. 14
Replacement of Finial Vessels
Kalasha Replacement (Nine Vessels), Sri Meenakshi Sundareshwarar Private Shrine
Theni
The nine-corroded brass kalashas of the family shrine were lowered, weighed, and replaced with new copper vessels gilded to twenty-three carat leaf. The old vessels were ritually committed to the temple tank after kumbhabhishekam.

Principal

Sthapati R. Ramaswamy (the present master), assisted by foundry-stalwart B. Periyasamy

Materials

Copper alloy 11% tin, gold leaf, sealed copper plate

Duration

Twenty-three months, single tools

2004No. 21
Consecration of Seven-Tier Stucco Programme
Polychrome Consolidation of Seven Tiers, Rajagopuram
Sri Andal Temple, Srivilliputhur
Survey, scaled drawings, and consolidation of seven tiers of stucco figures across the principal rajagopuram. Earlier cement repairs were removed and replaced with traditional sudhai. No figures were re-modelled; missing limbs were left as voids and recorded.

Principal

Sthapati R. Ramaswamy

Materials

Sudhai (lime-shell, jaggery, palmyra), mineral pigments, gold leaf

Duration

Six years, two monsoon-cycle pauses

2017No. 28
New Vimana, New Family Deity Sanctum
Three-Tala Vimana, Chettiar Kuladevata Shrine
Athangudi, Sivaganga District
A three-tala vimana raised over the existing garbhagriha for the family kuladevata. Drawn to the manasara canon at one-eighth scale; the corner karnakutas carry seated lions, and the central sala carries Gajalakshmi. Consecrated at the Mahakumbhabhishekam of June 2018.

Principal

Sthapati R. Ramaswamy with K. Krishnan, principal

Materials

Brick over sudhai modelling, copper kalasha, wax-bronze door frames

Duration

Forty-three months
