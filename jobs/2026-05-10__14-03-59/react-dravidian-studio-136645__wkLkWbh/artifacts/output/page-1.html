The Threefold Practice

From the drafting hall on East Chitra Veedhi the workshop attends to three disciplines, each governed by the Mānasara and the Mānasāra śilpa-śāstra, each priced by hand-day and carved in Krishnagiri black granite or Sadarahalli grey.

I · Vastu Consultation
New Mandapas
Site orientation, sanctum calculation, and full ground-plan drawings for kalyana mandapas, vasantha shrines, and village sthana temples. Each consultation begins with a three-day visit to read the soil, the water-table, and the cardinal axes. Drawings are inked on cotton paper at 1:40 and accompanied by a written sūla-mana proportion table.
From₹ 1,80,000
II · Gopuram Restoration
Polychromy & Stucco
Survey, condition report, and full stucco colour cartoon for the śudai, śilai, and stucco figures of storied gopurams. We work in lime and jaggery render and the twelve-mineral pigments — vermilion, peacock, orpiment, ultramarine — fixed without acrylics. Scaffold engineering and bell-metal kalasha replacement are carried out with allied workshops in Swamimalai.
From₹ 4,40,000
III · Granite Carving
Figurative Commissions
Hand-carved mandapas and utsava figures, pillared yali brackets, and door-jamb dvarapalakas, cut to vāstu-mana proportion by master craftsman K. Murugesan and his five apprentices. Standard size for a seated Ganesha in Krishnagiri black is thirty-six aṅgula, delivery in fourteen months from the day of stone selection.
From₹ 6,15,000

Current Commissions

Three works are presently under the chisel and the brush, each accepted with the blessing of the temple trustees and the family priest.

I
Rajagopuram Repainting · Tiruvannamalai
Eleven-tier polychromy renewal for the western Rajagopuram of the Jambukeswarar temple — 1,406 stucco figures inventoried, pigments mixed on site in lime and jaggery; scaffold raised through the monsoon shadow.
In Progress2024 — 2026
II
Sixteen-Pillared Kalyana Mandapa · Kumbakonam
A new chodaiya-mandapa adjoining the Sarangapani sanctum: in Sadarahalli grey granite. Sixteen yali-bracketed pillars, a central lotus ceiling medallion, and four corner dvarapalakas drawn from our 1933 pattern book.
Carving2025 — 2027
III
Private Navaratri Mandapa · Chettinad
A nine-stepped śāla-mandapa for the Aalagappa family of Karaikudi — each frame on a black granite plinth, with lime stucco kuthiradesa and gold-leaf vimanas on the cornice. Consecration set for Navaratri 2026.
