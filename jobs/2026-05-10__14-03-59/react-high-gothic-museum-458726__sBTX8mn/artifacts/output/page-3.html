CURRENT EXHIBITIONS

Three temporary exhibitions on view through Candlemas, the second day of February in the year of our Lord two thousand and twenty-five.

[motif: cross-flory]
[motif: arch-pointed]
— Exhibition I —

The Cobalt of Chartres

North Transept Gallery · Through 2 February

A close study of the pigment chemistry behind the famed Bleu de Chartres — presented alongside side-by-side panels drawn from the Bobi Verrière, with cross-section microphotography and a trace-element analysis from the Atelier Lorin.

Curator
Dr. Hélène Moreau
Panels
14 stained glass works · c. 1180 – 1230
Admission
Included with general entry
Schedule
Fri · 10h – 16h · 60 min.
[motif: arch-pointed]
— Exhibition II —

Hand of the Master:
The Reims Sketchbooks

South Cloister · Through 2 February

Thirty-eight silverpoint drawings attributed to the workshop of Villard de Honnecourt, presented for the first time in their bound order. Architectural elevations, foliate studies, and a remarkable diagram of a self-winding water-clock.

Curator
Prof. Aldric Vasseur-Roche
Folios
38 leaves; silverpoint on prepared vellum
Admission
€4 over general entry
Sittings
Timed entry, every 20 minutes
[motif: arch-pointed]
— Exhibition III —

Gold and Lapis: A Book of Hours from Bourges

Reliquary Chamber · Through 2 February

A private collection manuscript opened to the Annunciation miniature, with its leaf turned each Friday at the eleventh hour by conservator Frère Anselme Vaudreuil, who will speak briefly before each turning.

Origin
Bourges atelier, c. 1430 – 1455
Folio
Gold leaf, lapis lazuli, vermilion
Page Turning
Fridays, 16h00 sharp
Capacity
14 visitors per turning
