The Michaelmas Lecture

A signal event in the museum's autumn calendar
14
November · MMXXIV
19h00

Plate Tracery and the Geometry of the Quatrefoil

Delivered by Dr. Hélène Macquard, Maître de conférences, Université Paris-Sorbonne

Drawing on a decade of fieldwork at Chartres, Reims, and the lesser-known chapter-house of Saint-Quentin, Dr. Macquard traces the evolution of the quatrefoil from its origins as a pierced circle in plate tracery to its full flowering as the governing geometry of the rose window. The lecture will be illustrated with original-site photography and reconstructed compass diagrams.

A reception of mulled wine and almond cakes will follow in the West Cloister.

For Apprentices & Adepts

Workshops in the medieval crafts, for the young and the not-so-young

A MASON'S APPRENTICESHIP

Eight Saturdays · 09h00 – 16h30

Children take up the chisel and the set-square in the hands-on workshop. They will dress a small block of soft limestone, learn to read a mason's mark, and assemble a tooled portail and entryway by some traditional methods inherited from the great cathedrals.

For Children · €240 the term

THE SCRIPTORIUM WORKSHOP

Wednesdays · 18h Vespers

Under the guidance of conservator Béatrice Amabel Wittenwald, participants prepare gum (eg) base leaf gold for vellum, and burnish to a mirror finish using an agate tool. The course concludes with the gilding of a personal initial in the manner of a thirteenth-century Beatus page. All materials.

Of Five Sittings · Materials Provided

THE GLAZIER'S HAND

Half-term Sundays · By Application

A three-day introduction to the medieval glazier's craft. Students cut coloured glass with diamond and tungsten pointer, lead-up the panel using H-section calmes, and bind a small lancet by the laying-in of fired enamel. The course closes with a class concord-pace in the cloister.

For Students of Eighteen Years &amp; Above
