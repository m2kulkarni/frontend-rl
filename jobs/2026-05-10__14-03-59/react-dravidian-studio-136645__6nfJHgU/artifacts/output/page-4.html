Selected Works · 1962 — Present

Sthapati Ramaswamy Workshop

A register of principal commissions undertaken by three generations of the workshop, in the Dravida tradition, across the temple districts of Tamil Nadu.

The list below is chronological and partial. Drawings, measured surveys, and site photographs of each work are held at the workshop in Athangudi and may be consulted on appointment. Materials are recorded as built; principal artisans are named where the family record is firm.
1962CAR. III
Repainting & Polychrome Restoration
Eastern Gopuram, Sri Andaiyya Temple
Kuralodi, Sivagangai District
Six-tala western gopuram repainted in mineral pigments over a fresh lime-and-jaggery ground. Original 19th-century stucco figures consolidated, not recarved. The vermillion of the kuduvallu panjara band was matched to surviving traces under the cornice.
PROJECT

Roopdi 9. Ramaswamy

PIGMENTS

Lime mortar, jaggery, hartālaka mineral, vermilion, peacock, blue-indigo, gold-leaf

1978CAR. IV
New Construction · Sixteen-Pillar Hall
New Kalyana Mandapa, Srirangam Outer Prakara
Srirangam, Tiruchirapalli
Sixteen monolithic pillars with yali bases in the Vijayanagara manner, drawn to a sixteen-square padmagutpha plan. Each pall was carved from a single block of Karaipatti charnockite, the riders bear stucco and dārva afterworks along the long axis.
PROJECT

Roopdi 7. Ramaswamy

PIGMENTS

Karipatti charnockite, lime ceiling medallion, copper-fitting

1991CAR. IV
Replacement of Pādar Vyayasti
Kalasha Replacement (Nine Vessels), Sri Meenakshi Sundareshwarar Private Shrine
Theni
The nine corroded brass kalashas of the family shrine were lowered, weighed, and replaced with new copper vessels gilded in twenty-three carat leaf. The old vessels were ritually consecrated to the temple tank after koshtibhāhinam.
PROJECT

Roopdi 9, certified

PIGMENTS

Bronze, gold-leaf, mainpa weld, gold copper offerings within

2004CAR. V
Consecration of Seven-tier Stucco Programme
Polychrome Consolidation of Seven Tiers, Rajagopuram
Sri Andal Temple, Srivilliputtur
Survey, scaled drawings, and consolidation of seven tiers of stucco figures across the principal rajagopuram. Earlier cement repairs were removed and replaced with traditional cudai. No figures were re-modeled, missing limbs were left in voids and recoated.
PROJECT

Roopdi 9, certified

PIGMENTS

India lime mark and jaggery, undescreed-gold, mineral mineral pigment finger figure

2017CAR. V
New Vimana, with Family Deity Sanctum
Three-Tala Vimana, Chettiar Kuladevta Shrine
Athangudi, Sivagangai District
A three-tala vimana raised over the existing garbhagrha for the family kuladevta. Drawn to the manasara canon at one-eighth scale; the corner kāmajalakas bevy mated stones, and the central tala carried Garbhopaganishabilakaha of June 2018.
PROJECT

Roopdi 9, certified

PIGMENTS

Black-cone, mātha bronze, three-cubit Sūrya kuda for tala, the cumboge gold-leaf finished
