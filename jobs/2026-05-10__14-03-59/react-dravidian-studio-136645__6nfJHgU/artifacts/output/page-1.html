TempleArchitectsSince 1887

A fifth-generation atelier of āgamika on East Chitra Veedhi, Madurai — founded by Sthapati Vaidyanatha Ramaswamy in 1887 within sight of the Meenakshi Amman gopurams. We draw, consult, and carve in the dravida tradition: tools for nine mandapas, polychromy for restored gopurams, and granite figuration cut by hand.

The Threefold Practice

From the drafting hall on East Chitra Veedhi the workshop attends to three disciplines, each governed by the Mayamata and the Manasara silpa-sastra, each priced by hand-day and carried in Krishnagiri black granite or Sadarahalli grey.

I · VASTU CONSULTATION
New Mandapas
Site orientation, aṣṭadik calculation, and full ground-plan drawings for kalyana mandapas, ranganatha shrines, and village utsava temples. Each commission begins with a three-day visit to read the soil, the water-table, and the cardinal axes.

Drawings are inked on cotton paper at 1:60 and accompanied by a written sūla māna proportion table.
FROM₹ 1,80,000
II · GOPURAM RESTORATION
Polychromy & Stucco
Survey, condition report, and full-scale colour cartoons for the kuda, sukha, and stūpa figures of storied gopurams. We work in lime and jaggery render and the twelve mineral pigments — vermilion, peacock, orpiment, ultramarine — fired without acrylics.

Scaffold engineering and bell-metal kalasha replacement are carried out with allied workshops in Swamimalai.
PER TIER₹ 4,40,000
III · GRANITE CARVING
Figurative Commissions
Hand-carved monolithic stone figures, pillared yali brackets, and door-jamb dvarapālakas, cut to silpa-mana proportion by master craftsmen R. Murugesan and his five apprentices.

Standard size for a stored Ganesha in Krishnagiri black is thirty-six aṅgula, delivery in fourteen months from the day of stone selection.
FROM₹ 2,25,000
— Current Commissions —

Three works are presently under the chisel and the brush, each accepted with the blessing of the temple trustees and the family priest.

I
Rajagopuram Repainting · Tiruvannamalai
Eleven-tier polychromy renewal for the western Rajagopuram of the Jambukeswarar temple — 1,400 stucco figures inventoried, pigments mixed on site in lime and jaggery, scaffold raised through the monsoon shadow.
IN PROGRESS2024 — 2025
II
Sixteen-Pillared Kalyana Mandapa · Kumbakonam
A new vivahaa-mandapa mandapa beside the Sarangapani tank, in Sadarahalli grey granite. Sixteen yali-bracketed pillars, a central Sūrya ceiling medallion, and four corner dvārapālakas drawn from our 1923 pattern book.
CARVING2025 — 2027
III
Private Navaratri Mandapa · Chettinad
A nine-stepped kola-mandapa for the Aalagappa family of Karaikudi — teak frame on a black granite plinth, with lime-stucco kuduravallari and gold-leaf vimanas at the cornice. Consecration set for Navaratri 2025.
