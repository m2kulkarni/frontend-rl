The Michaelmas Lecture

A signal event in the museum's autumn calendar

14
November · MMXIV
19:00

Delivered by Dr. Hélène Mestat-Aurel, Maître de conférences, Université Saint-Sulpice

Plate Tracery and the Geometry of the Quatrefoil

Drawing on a decade of fieldwork at Chartres, Reims, and the lesser-known chapter-house of Saint-Quentin, Dr. Mestat-Aurel traces the evolution of the quatrefoil from its origins as a pierced circle to plate tracery to its full flowering as the governing geometry of the rose-window. The lecture will be illustrated with original site photographs and reconstruction compass diagrams.

A reception of mulled wine and almond cakes will follow in the West Cloister.

For Apprentices & Adepts

Workshops in the medieval crafts, for the young and the not-so-young

A Mason's Apprenticeship

Saturdays · MMXIV–MMXX, ages 11–14

Children take up the chisel and the set-square in this hands-on workshop. They will dress a small block of soft limestone, learn to read a mason's mark, and assemble a model portal arch using the same wedge-and-keystone principles that raised the great cathedrals. Each apprentice receives a leather-bound logbook and a numbered mason's mark of their own.

Six places · €60 · Tools provided

The Scriptorium Workshop

Autumn term, six Saturdays · adult

Under the guidance of conservator Frère Anselme Whitcombe, participants prepare goose-, hen-, and swan-feather quills, mix burnish red and gum-arabic ink, and bound copper-iron oak-gall. Two scripts in three nibs: the Carolingian minuscule, the textura quadrata, and a final flourish — the trial Lombardic — at the appointed hour. Including 24 leaves laid into a buckram-bound sheaf for one's own keeping.

Six places · €240 · Materials included

The Glazier's Hand

Half-term Mondays · 24 – 28 October · ages 16+

A three-day introduction to the medieval glazier's craft. Students cut coloured glass with a grozing iron, paint trace-lines with iron-oxide pigment, and lead a small panel of their own design. The conserv-laudatorium in a kiln-fired round-and-rectangular contest furnace.

Eight places · €310 · Furnace and tools
