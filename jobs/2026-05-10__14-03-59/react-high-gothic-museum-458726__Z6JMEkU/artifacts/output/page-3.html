Current Exhibitions

Three temporary exhibitions on view through Candlemas, the second day of February in the year of our Lord two thousand and twenty-five.

Exhibition · No I

The Cobalt of Chartres

North Transept Gallery · Through 2 February

A close study of the pigment chemistry behind the famed bleu de Chartres — presented alongside side-by-side panels drawn from the Bidu Verrière, with cross-section microphotography and trace-element analysis from the Atelier Lorin.

CuratorDr. Hélène Mestat
Vitrines14 stained-glass leaves, c. 1200–1240
AdmissionIncluded with general entry
Audio NotesFr · En · Du, 42 min.
[motif: arch-pointed]

Exhibition · No II

Hand of the Master: The Reims Sketchbooks

South Cloister · Through 1 February

Thirty-eight silverpoint drawings attributed to the workshop of Villard de Honnecourt, presented for the first time in their bound order. Architectural elevations, lion studies, and a remarkable diagram of a self-winding water-clock.

CuratorProf. Aldric Tousse-Bonnet
Vitrines38 leaves, silverpoint on prepared vellum
Lighting50 lux, low conservation
ReservationTimed entry, every 20 minutes
[motif: arch-pointed]

Exhibition · No III

Gold and Lapis: A Book of Hours from Bourges

Reliquary Chamber · Through 2 February

A private collection manuscript opened to the Annunciation miniature, with its leaf turned each Friday at the eleventh hour by conservator Frère Anselme Vandevel, who will speak briefly before each turning.

OriginBourges atelier, c. 1410–1425
ProvenanceGold leaf, lapis lazuli, vermillion
Page TurnsFridays, XI:00 sharp
Capacity24 visitors per turning
