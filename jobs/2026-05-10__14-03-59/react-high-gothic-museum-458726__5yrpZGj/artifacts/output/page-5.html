The Michaelmas Lecture

A signal event in the museum's autumn calendar

14
November
MMXXV

Plate Tracery and the Geometry of the Quatrefoil

Delivered by Dr Hélène Marquand, Maître de conférence, Université Paris Sorbonne

Drawing on a decade of fieldwork at Chartres, Reims, and her lesser-known chapter house at Saint Quentin, Dr Marquand traces the evolution of the quatrefoil from its origins as a pierced circle to plate tracery to its full flowering at the prevening galleries of the rose window. The lecture will be illustrated with original site photography and reconstructed compass diagrams.

A reception of mulled wine and almond cakes will follow in the West Cloister.

For Apprentices & Adepts

Workshops in the medieval crafts, for the young and the not-so-young

A Mason's Apprenticeship

Saturdays · 10h00 – 13h00 · Ages 8 – 12

Children take up the chisel and the riser-square in the lapidary court workshop. They will draw a small block of soft limestone, learn to read a mason's mark, and assemble a model portal arch using the same wedge-and-keystone principles that raised the great cathedrals. Each apprentice receives a leather-bound logbook and a numbered mason's mark of their own.

£25 per child · Term subscription

The Scriptorium Workshop

Autumn term · 5th Sundays · Ages 12+

Under the guidance of conservator Brother Anselm Whitcombe, participants prepare gum, lay loose-leaf gold in vellum, and burnish to a mirror finish using an agate tooth. The course concludes with the gilding of a personal initial in the manner of a thirteenth-century Beatus page. All materials — including 23-karat leaf — are supplied.

£35 each · Materials Included

The Glazier's Hand

Half-term retreats · 10 – 18 October · Ages 16+

A three-day introduction to the medieval glazier's craft. Students cut coloured glass with a grozing iron, paint trace lines with iron oxide pigment, and assemble a small panel with lead came and gold of fine wire designs. The teaching culminates in a kiln-fired enamel and participant-cut frame.
