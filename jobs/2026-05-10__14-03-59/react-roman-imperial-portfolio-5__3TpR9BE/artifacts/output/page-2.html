CATALOGUS · OPERUM · MMXVI–MMXXIV

SELECTED WORKS

Seven completed commissions of Atelier Vesperia, set in chronological order from the first atrium in Trastevere to the household aedicula of the present year. Each is described in two registers — site and method — and divided by the Greek key.

[motif: meander-greek-key]
  1. I

    TABULARIUM READING ROOM

    SOPRINTENDENZA SPECIALE DI ROMA · CAPITOLINE HILL, ROME · MMXIX — 2018

    Insertion of a scholarly reading room within the lower vaults of the Tabularium, threading travertine reading desks beneath the original tufa arches. The intervention draws daylight down through a narrow oculus aligned with the Forum axis, leaving the antique surface untouched.

    [motif: meander-greek-key]
  2. II

    MOSAIC FLOOR AT VILLA DEI QUINTILI

    PARCO ARCHEOLOGICO DELL'APPIA ANTICA · VIA APPIA ANTICA, ROME · MMXXI — 2021

    Reconstruction in opus tessellatum of a black-and-white guilloche field for the eastern triclinium, sized to the surviving threshold and laid with hand-cut basalt and Carrara cubes. The pattern resumes the second-century rhythm without imitating its losses.

    [motif: meander-greek-key]
  3. III

    TEPIDARIUM PAVILION

    COMUNE DI TIVOLI · BAGNI DI TIVOLI · BAGNI DI TIVOLI, LAZIO · MMXXII — 2023

    A small bathing pavilion of new travertine beside the sulphur spring, vaulted in unreinforced brick on a cruciform plan. Warm water enters along a continuous travertine sill, returning the room to the older Roman idea of architecture as a climate.

    [motif: meander-greek-key]
  4. IV

    ATRIUM OF THE CASA MARCIANA

    PRIVATE COMMISSION, FAMIGLIA PETRUCCI · TRASTEVERE, ROME · MMXVI · 2016

    A four-column impluvium atrium reconstructed within a nineteenth-century shell, its compluvium glazed with cast prismatic glass. The first built work of the studio and the source of its planning vocabulary.

    [motif: meander-greek-key]
  5. V

    CABINET OF ANTIQUE GLASS

    FONDAZIONE TORLONIA · PALAZZO TORLONIA, ROME · MMXX — 2020

    A single-room display of Roman cage cups and millefiori, set within a coffered niche of stained pearwood and bronze. Lighting is delivered indirectly from above, reading the vessels as objects of geometry rather than relics.

    [motif: meander-greek-key]
  6. VI

    CARDO PEDESTRIAN RESTORATION

    SOPRINTENDENZA ARCHEOLOGICA DI OSTIA · OSTIA ANTICA · MMXXII — 2022

    Re-laying of one hundred and forty metres of basalt paving along the Cardo Maximus, with a parallel drainage channel of Tiburtine travertine. The new joints follow the original cart-rut spacing recorded in nineteenth-century surveys.

    [motif: meander-greek-key]
  7. VII

    AEDICULA FOR A DOMESTIC LARARIUM

    COMMISSIONED, STUDIO VESPERIA · STUDIO, VIA DEI CORONARI, ROME · MMXXIII — 2024

    A miniature temple-front of marmo statuario and gilded bronze, designed to hold three household bronzes from a private collection. The pediment carries an oak-wreath roundel cut at one-to-one with the studio drawing.

[motif: olive-wreath-circle]
