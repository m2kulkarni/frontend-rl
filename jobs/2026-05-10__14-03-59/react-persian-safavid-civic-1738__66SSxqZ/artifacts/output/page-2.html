Edicts & Public Notices

From the bureau of the Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān, Iṣfahān

By the grace of the Muḥtasibate and under the seal of the Dīvān-i Shahr, the following maktūbāt and iṣdārāt proclamations were affixed to the gates of the Qayṣariyya, the Masjid-i Jāmiʿ, and the four caravanserais of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, beginning the fourteenth day of Rabīʿ al-Awwal in the year one thousand one hundred and eight of the Hijra. Citizens of the Dīvān, customs of the bazaars, edicts and qawānīn are charged to attend their reading.

[motif: islamic-star]Firmān 491

15 Rabīʿ al-Awwal · 19 October 1696

On the Standard Weight of the Common Loaf (nān-i sangak)

Issued by Mīrzā ʿAbd al-Bāqī, Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān

Be it known to every baker (nānvā) of the four-quarter that, by command of the Dīvān, the loaf of sangak shall not weigh less than ratl-i 6½ of Tabrīz when drawn from the oven. Any baker found light by the inspector's scale shall forfeit one day's flour to the post of the Masjid's Jāmiʿ.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
[motif: islamic-star]Firmān 492

17 Rabīʿ al-Awwal · 21 October 1696

Repair of the Fountain (saqqā-khāna) in the Jūbāra Quarter

Issued by Hājj Luṭf-ʿAlī, Inspector of Waters

The qanāt-fed fountain at the head of the Bāzār-i Qayṣariyya Khājū-i Jūbāra, having fallen into ruin since the floods of Muḥarram, is hereby ordered restored. Master mason ʿUstād Salār of Iṣfahān is appointed; the cost of forty-two tomans shall be drawn from the waqf of the late Ḥājjī Mīrzā Riḍā of Khāju, that no draws may be taken from the cisterns of the Madrasa-i Chahār-Bāgh in the meantime.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
[motif: islamic-star]Firmān 493

18 Rabīʿ al-Awwal · 22 October 1696

Prohibition of Grazing within the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān

Issued by the Dīvān of the Quarters

No man shall lead sheep, goat, mule, nor camel upon the gravel of the Royal Square between the Qayṣariyya gate and the Masjid's bāb. The horsemen of the courtiers' village shall pass instead by the Bridge of Khwājū. First transgression carries a fine of two dīnārs; the second, the confiscation of the beast for the royal stables.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
[motif: islamic-star]Firmān 494

19 Rabīʿ al-Awwal · 23 October 1696

On the Sweeping of the Bāzār-i Buzurg before the Friday Prayer

Issued by Āqā Kāẓim, Warden of the Qayṣariyya

Each shopkeeper of the long bāzār, from the spice-vault of the iqlīm to the Bāzār-i Mīsī Yaft unto the gate of the coppersmiths, shall sweep his threshold and water the dust thereof one hour before the noon call of every Friday. Inspectors of this office shall pass at the third pang, whose lode in the channel shall be carried to the river at the offender's expense.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
[motif: islamic-star]Firmān 495

22 Rabīʿ al-Awwal · 26 October 1696

Concerning the Quality of Indigo Sold in the Sarā-yi Gulshan

Issued by Mīrzā Bāqir, Examiner of Dyes

Merchants importing indigo of Sind and the Indian-Yard shall present each bale to the dye-examiner before opening sale. Adulteration with charcoal or with the dross of pomegranate is forbidden, the penalty being the burning of the parcel in the maydān and a fine of ten dīnārs to the treasury of the Dīvān.
