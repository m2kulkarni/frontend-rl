Quarters, Canals & Bazaars

Being a true register of the twelve administrative quarters of Iṣfahān, the four principal mahallāt within them, and the eight-and-thirty bazaar guilds that gather under the gates of the Dīvān-i Maydān.

Compiled in the Month of Ṣafar · Sanā 1108[motif: islamic-star]Filed under: V · Quarters

Within the precinct walls and across the four watered districts of Iṣfahān, the dīvān keeps the names and bounds of the quarters, half their breadth in mile, the houses of merchants and craftsmen, the lodgings of the schools, and the four guilds united on the heart-bench. With these followings of names and quoted gauging the city is most truly known, and the eastern abutments of the Khāju-Iṣfahān bridge.
