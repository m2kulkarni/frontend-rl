Dīvān-i Shahr-i Iṣfahān

Civic Register · Bazaar Inspectorate · Records of the Royal City

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

By the grace of the Almighty and the favour of the Sublime Shāh, this office stands as the registrar of houses, persons and trades within the great Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān. Here are kept the rolls of the bazaars, the chronicles of edicts and the orderly affairs of every quarter, that no thread of the city's weft may pass beyond the dignity of the Crown.

Issued this seventeenth day of Rabīʿ al-Awwal, by the hand of

Mīrzā Ḥusayn Khān Shīrāzī

Mayor of the Quarters · Kalāntar-i Maḥallāt

Effective · 14 Rabīʿ al-Awwal — 21 Rabīʿ al-Awwal

Allotment Schedule for the Mādī Nāṣirīn Canal

The mādī shall run for fourteen gardens of Khāju at Saturday, the sixth share of Bīd-ābād to midday Monday, the orchards beyond the Khāju bridge to dusk Wednesday. Disputes shall be heard at the canal-house each morning before the call to ẓuhr.

Read the Roll
[motif: islamic-star]

Filing · 18 Rabīʿ al-Awwal — 4 Rabīʿ al-Thānī

Caravan-Tax Filing Window Now Open

Receipts taken until 18 Rabīʿ al-Awwal · 4 dīnārs per camel-load. Merchants returning from Tabrīz, Bīrūn, and the Gulf ports must present manifests at the Customs Hall behind the Maydān. Files are assessed in turn and every person bearing the imperial stamp is dismissed in one sitting.

[motif: islamic-star]

Convocation · 22 Rabīʿ al-Awwal · Forenoon

Observance for the Chief of the Cloth-Bazaar

Ustād Yaʿqūb Khorāsānī, h. 1078 · d. 1106. The shops of the Qayṣariyya silk-row shall close at midday for three days in honour of the late ustād. The bureau procession departs from the Masjid's Dālān after ʿaṣr prayers; condolences are received at the family courtyard in the Dardasht quarter. May his memory weave on among the looms.

Sign the Register

Hours of the Dīvān · Locations of Record

Chief Scribe-of-week attends the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, opposite the gates of the Caravanserai of Jaʿid Ismāʿīl-Qulī, daily save Friday, from the dawn azān to the ringing of the noon. Subsidiary registrars are kept at the Madrasa-i Chahār-Bāgh khānaqā in green undertawn and at the Bridge of Allāhverdī Khān (rivers & tolls).
