On the Lodging of an ʿArīḍa

Procedure observed at the eastern arcade of the Dīvān

By order of His Excellency the Kalāntar of Iṣfahān, every resident of the Royal City — whether of Maydān-i Buzurg, Maydān-i Sūk, Jūbāra, Dardasht, or the immemorial environs of Junbān — may bring forward a written petition before the city secretariat. Audiences are held in the columned arcades-side of the Bureau, on the morning of Saturday and Tuesday, between the second and fifth hours after sunrise. Petitioners shall present themselves with two witnesses of good standing and with a sheet of stamped Khurāsān paper before drafted, and the same week from the office of the Mawjūd al-Maʿaltik.

NotePetitions delivered without the correct seals + chancery stamp shall be set aside and not read. Tea-money is drawn each Friday after the noon prayer.

Days & Hours of Audience

The columned rotunda is opened by the porter (darbān) at the call of the second hour. Petitioners are received in the order of their arrival, modified only by the precedence noted below.

Saturday (Shanba)2nd – 5th hour
Tuesday (Sih-shanba)2nd – 5th hour
Friday— closed —
Festival days— closed —

Where to Present Yourself

The Bureau of Records occupies the eastern wing of the dīvān-khāna, behind the cypress court of the Naqsh-e Jahān. Enter by the lesser gate (dar-i kūchak) facing the workshops of the gilders, ascend the seven steps of grey marble, and present your stamped paper to the door-keeper. Your name, quarter, and guild affiliation will be entered into the day register (ruzāmcha) before you are admitted to the rotunda.

Drafting Conventions

  1. Open with the formula Hawa al-ḥaqq, the bismillāh in the hand of the kātib.
  2. Name yourself, your father, your quarter, and your guild — in that order.
  3. State the matter plainly, in not more than seven lines of cursive nastaʿlīq.
  4. Avoid figurative speech and complaint against named persons of higher rank.
  5. Close with the date in the Hijrī reckoning and your seal, or, lacking a seal, the impress of your right thumb in lampblack.
  6. Leave a margin of three finger-breadth at the foot of the folio for the secretariat's endorsement.

Order of Precedence

  1. Sayyids and ʿulamāʾ of the Friday Mosque, and elders of the four colors of Iṣfahān.
  2. Guild of the qaylānbāffis (carpetiers) and the silk merchants of the Qaysariyya bāzār.
  3. Tilers, masons, and gilders of the royal workshops, on matters of wages and apprenticeship.
  4. Residents of Jūbāra and Dardasht, in matters of water-rights and the qanāt division.
  5. Armenians most charge of Julfā, by their kalāntar, in commercial and inheritance affairs.
  6. All other residents of the city in the order of their arrival at the gate.
