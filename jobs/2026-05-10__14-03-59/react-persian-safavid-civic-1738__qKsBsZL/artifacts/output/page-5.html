Folio V ✶ From the City

Quarters, Canals & Bazaars

Being a true register of the twelve administrative quarters of Iṣfahān, the four principal madīs that water the city, and the eight-and-thirty bazaar guilds confirmed by the office of the Muḥtasib.

Composed in the reign of Shāh ʿAbbās II Year 1067 A.H. · Iṣfahān
[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

Within nine miles before the Dīvān, or would by petition concerning water-share, market-stall, or boundary of quarter, shall consult these tables. Names of the kalāntār-khānūmān (quarter-elders), schedule (water-watchers), and similar (guild-masters) are set forth herein, with their audiences days at the Qaysariyya Gate; the books along the Chahār Bāgh, and the western almsmen of the Khwājū Bridge.
