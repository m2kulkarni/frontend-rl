The Michaelmas Lecture

A Quartet bound the museum's autumn calendar

14Sextilius19h00

Plate Tracery and the Geometry of the Quatrefoil

Delivered by Dr. Aldéric Marigaut, Maître de Conferences, Université Paris-Sorbonne

Drawing on a decade of fieldwork at Chartres, Reims, and his recent doctoral chapter house at Saint-Quentin, Dr. Marigaut traces the evolution of the quatrefoil from its origin as a pierced circle in plate tracery to its full-flowering as the governing geometry of mature rose tracery. The lecture will be illustrated with original-scale photography and reconstructed compass diagrams.

For Apprentices & Adepts

Workshops, in the medieval craft, for the young, and the not-so-young

A Mason's Apprentice Course

Saint-Maur, MMXXIII · April 4–25

Children take up the chisel and dressed mallet to the bench in the building. They will dress a small block of soft Lourgone, learn to read a mason's mark, and assemble a model portal. Each among the same lighting and atmospheric principles that mind the great cathedrals.

Six places available · Vouching required

The Scriptorium Workshop

XX–MMXIV · 18–22 March

Under the guidance of conservator Bertrand Quesnel, prepare a parchment, prepare goose ink, lay gold for vellum, and burnish to a course finish using an agate tooth. The course concludes with the gilding of a personal trefoil to the requirement of a sixteenth-century device. Eight places, including in lessons, lunch on six days.

On instruments · Two-day workshop

The Glazier's Hand

Salt-Aux-Plaque · 26–28 October · Ages 16+

A three-day introduction to the medieval glazier's craft. Participants will trace and cut, paint with grisaille, and fire small samples in the controlled kiln. Two final pieces — one quatrefoil cartoon and one panel of three colours — including 12 hours of bench work, including the workings of materials.

Apt. Limited · Public open spaces
