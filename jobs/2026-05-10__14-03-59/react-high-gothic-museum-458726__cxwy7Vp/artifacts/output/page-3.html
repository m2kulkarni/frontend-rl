Current Exhibitions

Three temporary exhibitions on view through Candlemas, the second day of February in the year of our Lord two thousand and twenty-five.

[motif: quatrefoil]
[motif: arch-pointed]

Exhibition I

The Cobalt of Chartres

North Transept Gallery · Through 02 February

A close study of the pigment chemistry behind the famed Bleu de Chartres, presented alongside side-by-side panels drawn from the Belle-Verrière, with cross-section microphotography and trace-element analysis from the Berlin-Lorin.

Curator
Dr. Hélène Moreau
Panels
14 stained-glass leaves, c. 1180–1220
Admission
Included with general entry
Audio Tour
Fr · En · It · 26 min.
[motif: arch-pointed]

Exhibition II

Hand of the Master: the Reims Sketchbooks

South Cloister · Through 17 February

Thirty-eight silverpoint drawings, attributed to the workshop of Villard de Honnecourt, presented for the first time in their bound order. Architectural elevations, lion studies, and a remarkable diagram of a self-winding stone-clock.

Curator
Prof. Aldric Sautier-Bernet
Drawings
38 leaves, silverpoint on prepared vellum
Provenance
Reims–Frankfurt
Sessions
Timed entry, every 30 minutes
[motif: arch-pointed]

Exhibition III

Gold and Lapis: a Book of Hours from Bourges

Reliquary Chamber · Through 1 February

A private collection manuscript, opened to the Annunciation miniature, with its leaf turned each Friday at the eleventh hour by conservator Frère Anselme Vaudeval, who will speak briefly before each turning.

Origin
Bourges atelier, c. 1418–1425
Pigments
Gold leaf, lapis lazuli, vermilion
Page Turning
Fridays, 11h00 sharp
Capacity
24 visitors per turning
