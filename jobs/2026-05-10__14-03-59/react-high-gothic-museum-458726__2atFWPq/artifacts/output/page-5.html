The Michaelmas Lecture

A signal event in the museum's autumn calendar

14
November · MMXXV
19h30

Plate Tracery and the Geometry of the Quatrefoil

Delivered by Dr. Hélène Marquand, Maître de conférences, Université Paris-Sorbonne

Drawing on a decade of fieldwork at Chartres, Reims, and the lesser-known chapter-house of Saint-Quentin, Dr. Marquand traces the evolution of the quatrefoil from its origin as a pierced circle in plate tracery to its full flowering as the governing geometry of the rose window. The lecture will be illustrated with original-site photography and reconstructed compass diagrams.

A reception of mulled wine and almond cakes will follow in the West Cloister.

For Apprentices & Adepts

Workshops in the medieval crafts, for the young and the not-so-young

A Mason's Apprenticeship

Saturdays · MMXXV-MMXXVI · Ages 8–14

Children take up the chisel and the rule rasper in this hands-on workshop. They will dress a small block of soft limestone, learn to read a mason's mark, and assemble a model portal arch using the same wedge and keystone principles that raised the great cathedrals. Each apprentice receives a leather-bound logbook and a numbered mason's mark of their own.

€45 per apprentice · including materials

The Scriptorium Workshop

Twelve hours · 16 Sundays · Adults

Under the guidance of conservator Frère Anselme Vaudreuil, participants prepare gum-Arabic ink, gilt on vellum, and burnish to a mirror finish using an agate tooth. The course concludes with the gilding of a personal initial in the manner of a thirteenth-century Beatus page. All materials — including 23-karat leaf — are provided.

€280 full course · materials included

The Glazier's Hand

Half-Day Saturday · 22 – 24 October · Ages 14+

A three-day introduction to the medieval glazier's craft. Students cut coloured glass with a grozing iron, paint trace lines with iron-oxide pigment, and lead a small panel of their own design. The character culminates in a kiln-firing session and each participant carries home a finished panel of their own work.

€165 · limited to twelve places
