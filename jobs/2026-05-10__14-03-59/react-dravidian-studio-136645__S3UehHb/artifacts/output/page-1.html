A fifth-generation atelier of sthapatis on East Chitra Veedhi, Madurai — founded by Sthapati Vaidyanatha Ramaswamy in 1887 within sight of the Meenakshi Amman gopurams. We draw, consult, and carve in the Dravida tradition: tools for the new mandapas, polychromy for restored gopurams, and granite figuration cut by hand.

The Threefold Practice

From the drafting hall on East Chitra Veedhi, the workshop attends to three disciplines, each governed by the Manasara and the Manasara silpa-sastra, each priced by hand-day and carried in Krishnagiri black granite or Kadambadi grey.

I · Vastu Consultation
[motif: lotus-flower]

New Mandapas

Site orientation, axial calculation, and full ground-plan drawings for kalyana mandapas, vasantha shrines, and village stupika temples. Each commission begins with a three-day visit to read the soil, the water-table, and the cardinal axes.

Drawings are inked on cotton paper at 1:48 and accompanied by a written sila-saaya properties table.

FROM₹ 1,80,000
II · Gopuram Restoration
[motif: floral-headpiece]

Polychromy & Stucco

Survey, condition report, and full-scale colour cartoon for the kudu, sikhi, and stucco figures of storied gopurams. We work in lime and jaggery render and the twelve mineral pigments — vermilion, peacock, orpiment, ultramarine — fixed without acrylic.

Scaffold engineering and bell-metal kalasha replacement are carried out with allied workshops in Swamimalai.

FOR TIER₹ 4,40,000
III · Granite Carving
[motif: conch-shell]

Figurative Commissions

Hand-carved mukhas and utsava figures, pillared yali brackets, and door-jamb dvarapalakas, cut to silvasa-sutra proportion by master craftsman R. Murugesan and his five apprentices.

Standard size for a seated Ganesha in Krishnagiri black is thirty-six angula, delivery in fourteen months from the day of stone selection.

FROM₹ 2,25,000

Current Commissions

Rajagopuram Repainting · Tiruvanmaiyalai

Eleven-tier polychromy renewal for the western Rajagopuram of the Jambukeswarar temple — 1,890 stucco figures inventoried, pigments mixed on site in lime and jaggery, scaffold raised through the monsoon shadow.

Sixteen-Pillared Kalyana Mandapa · Kumbakonam

A new sthalam vimanika mandapa beside the Sarangapani tank, in Sadasivkalli grey granite. Sixteen yali-bracketed pillars, a central lotus ceiling medallion, and four corner Dvarapalakas drawn from our 1933 pattern-book.
