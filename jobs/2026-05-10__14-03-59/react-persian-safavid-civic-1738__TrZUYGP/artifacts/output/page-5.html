Register V ✶ Maḥallāt va Bāzāra

Quarters, Canals & Bazaars

Being a true register of the twelve administrative quarters of Iṣfahān, the four principal water-channels by the year 1108 of the Hijra, and the eight-and-thirty bazaar guilds reckoned by the office of the Mīrshahrī.

Compass of the Royal City[motif: islamic-star]1108 A.H. · 1696 A.D.

Wherever the river of Zayandah-rūd binds its sash about the south of the city, twelve maḥallāt of Iṣfahān are reckoned upon the rolls of this Bureau. Names of the householders represent which masters of the sūk-i furūshān are reckoned along with the warders of the rural-side maydāns, the apportionment of bridges, the order of guildmasters, and the allotment of madī-water, all set forth in this folio after the manner of the Master of Records.

The Twelve Quarters of the City

As tabled by the Master of Quarters

Northern & Eastern Quarters

BīdānOlive trees and vine-tracks; the fortified watch.
DardashtQuarter of saddlers and dyers; mosque of Sayyid Muḥammad.
Bāgh-i QalʿaWalled gardens beyond the carpenters' lane.

Southern & Western Quarters

SaghdābādīBeyond the Khājū Bridge; market of the qatī-makers.
JūbāraOld Jewish quarter; spice-market of the Sarā-yi Yusūf.
Tūl-i KhājūLong quarter beside the eastern bridge.
Khūrshīd-i JunūbīSun-quarter; orchards of citron and quince.

The Four Principal Mādīs

As reckoned for the year 1108 of the Hijra

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

Mādī Faʿūn

Mādī Faʿūn the elder

Threading the wool quarters; reckoned to fill on Saturday from dawn till the noon adhān.

Mādī Nāṣirīm

Mādī of the merchants

Crosses the Maydān beneath the silk-row; full on Sunday and the first half of Wednesday.

Mādī Saʿīd

Mādī of the Vizier Saʿīd

Encloses the great gardens of the Chahār Bāgh; runs each Monday from the second hour.

Mādī Khājū

Mādī of the eastern bridge

Serves the orchards of the Khājū belt; Tuesday rotation, with overflow on Friday.
