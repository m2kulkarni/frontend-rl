[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

By a firmān to the residents of the Naqsh-e Jahān quarters, the kalāntīr of the Quarters and all householders within the maximal districts of this royal city, that the gate of this Bureau stand open from dawn-prayer until the closing of the great doors at sunset, that no petition shall be turned away, and that the bidings of water, tax, and lineage are kept in good order for the welfare of the people and the dignity of the Crown.

Sealed this seventeenth day of Rabīʿ al-Awwal, by the hand of

Mīrzā Ḥusayn Khān Shīrāzī

Mayor of the Quarters · Kalāntar-i Maḥallāt

Allotment Schedule for the Mādī Niāṣarm Canal

Effective 18 Rabī‘ al-Awwal · in Rabī‘ al-Awwal

The mādī has set the rotation: gardens of Jūlfā at first light on Saturday; the cloth dyers of Bīd Ābād at midday Monday; the orchards beyond the Khājū bridge at dusk Wednesday. Disputes shall be heard at the canal-house each morning before the call to prayer.

Caravan-Tax Filing Window Now Open

Receipts taken until 28 Rabī‘ al-Awwal · A door per camel load

Merchants returning from Tabrīz, Shīrāz, and the Gulf ports must present manifests to the Counting Hall behind the Maydān. Silks are assessed at four-and-twenty per hundred dīnār; indigo and madder at one tenth; precious stones by separate inspection. Bring two witnesses and the seal of your caravan bāshī.

Observance for the Chief of the Cloth-Bazaar

Until Sirjab Khwāndmīr · 4 Rabī‘ al-Ākhar

The shops of the Qaysariyya silk row shall close at midday for three days in honour of the late ostāḍ. The bazaar procession departs from the Masjid-i Shāh after ẓuhr prayers; condolences are received at the family courtyard by the Darūshāh quarter. May his memory wear, as among the Just.

Hours of the Dīvān · Locations of Record

Chief Scribe's arcade of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, opposite the gate of the Caravanserai of Sayyid Yūsuf. Open daily, from the dawn-call to the setting of the sun. Subsidiary registers are kept at the Madrasa-i Chahār Bāgh (foreign assessors-residence) and at the Bridge of Allāhverdī Khān (twin yikāl).
