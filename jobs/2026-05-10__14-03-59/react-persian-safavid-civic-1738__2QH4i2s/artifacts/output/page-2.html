Register II · Sahīfa-i Firmān

Edicts & Public Notices

From the Bureau of the Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān, Iṣfahān

By the grace of the Mubārak-niyāt and under the seal of the Dīvān-i Shahr, the following firmāns and bāzār proclamations were affixed to the gates of the Qaysariyya, the Masjid-i Jāmiʿ, and the four caravanserais of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān; beginning the fourteenth day of Rabīʿ al-Awwal in the one-thousand-one-hundred-and-eighth of the Hijra. Citizens of the Jūlfa, Darāzīt, ʿAbbās-Ābād, and Bīdābād quarters are charged to attend their reading.

FARMĀN 405
15 RABĪʿ AL-AWWAL 1108· FARMĀN 405

On the Standard Weight of the Common Loaf (nān-i sangak)

— Sealed by Mīrʿā Abū al-Bashar, Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān

Be it known to every baker (nānvā) of the four quarters that, by command of the Dīvān, the loaf of sangak shall not weigh less than nine-of-ten ʿabbāsī minted from fresh wheat. The loaf, when struck, shall sound a clear, dry note; its hue shall be that of the Maydān fired-clay tile.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
FARMĀN 406
17 RABĪʿ AL-AWWAL 1108· FARMĀN 406

Repair of the Public Fountain (saqqā-khāna) in the Jūbāra Quarter

— Sealed by Ūdjī ʿAlī Aḳā, Inspector of Waters

The qanāt-fed fountain at the head of the Bāzār-i Bozorg-i Naqsh-Sayfī, having fallen into ruin since the floods of Muḥarram, is hereby ordered restored. Master mason Ustād Jaʿfar of Iṣfahān is appointed; the cost of forty-two tūmān shall be drawn from the waqf of the Imām Maydān. Repair of jūbāras and pebble-roads on the courses of the Khūsh-i Chahār Bāgh in the meantime.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
FARMĀN 407
19 RABĪʿ AL-THĀNĪ 1108· FARMĀN 407

Prohibition of Grazing within the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān

— Sealed by Hājj Riḍā, Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān

No man shall lead sheep, goat, mule, nor camel upon the gravels of the Royal Square between the Qaysariyya gate and the Masjid-i Shāh. The herdsmen of the southern villages shall pass instead by the way of Darvāza-i Ḥasan-Ābād. A first-transgression carries a fine of two ʿabbāsī; the second, the confiscation of the beast for the royal stables.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
FARMĀN 408
23 RABĪʿ AL-THĀNĪ 1108· FARMĀN 408

On the Sweeping of the Bāzār-i Buzurg before the Friday Prayer

— Sealed by Aḳā Riḍā, Warden of the Qaysariyya

Each shopkeeper of the long bāzār, from the spice souk of the foot-of-Bīṭāl Yāqā to the gate of the coppersmiths, shall sweep his threshold and water the dust thereof one hour before the noon-call of every Friday. Inspectors of this office shall pass at the third gong; refuse left in the channel shall be carried to the river at the offender&apos;s expense.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
FARMĀN 409
02 RABĪʿ AL-THĀNĪ 1108· FARMĀN 409

Concerning the Quality of Indigo Sold in the Sarā-yi Gulshan

— Sealed by Mīrzā Bāqī, Examiner of Dyes

Merchants importing indigo of Bayāna and of Saʿīdiyya shall present each bale to the dye-examiner before opening sale. Adulteration with charcoal or with the lees of pomegranate is forbidden; the penalty being the burning of the parcel in the maydān and a fine of five tūmāns to the treasury of the Dīvān.
