Plate Tracery and the Geometry of the Quatrefoil

Delivered by Dr. Hélène Marquand, Maître de conférences, Université Saint-Andoche

Drawing on a decade of fieldwork at Chartres, Reims, and the lesser-known chapter-house of Saint-Quentin, Dr. Marquand traces the evolution of the quatrefoil from its origin as a pierced circle in plate tracery to its full flowering as the governing geometry of the rose window. The lecture will be illustrated with original-size photography and reconstructed compass diagrams.

For Apprentices & Adepts

Workshops in the medieval crafts, for the young and the not-so-young

A Mason's Apprenticeship

Saturdays · MMXXV–MMXXVI · Ages 11–17

Children take up the chisel and the cut-square in this hands-on workshop. They will dress a small block of soft limestone, learn to read a mason's mark, and assemble a model portal arch using the same wedge-and-keystone principles their forebears knew. Each apprentice receives a leather-bound logbook and a numbered mason's mark of their own.

Six Places · Term Inquiries

The Scriptorium Workshop

Autumn Term · Six Sundays · Adults

Under the guidance of conservator Frère Anselme Whittembre, participants prepare goose, lay-brush leaf gold on vellum, and burnish to a mirror finish using an agate tooth. The course concludes with the gilding of a personal initial in the manner of a thirteenth-century Iberian psalm. All materials — including 24 carat leaf — are supplied.

Six Places · Materials Provided

The Glazier's Hand

Half-term Intensive · 24–28 October · Ages 16+

A three-day introduction to the medieval glazier's craft. Students cut coloured glass with a grozing iron, paint trace lines with iron-oxide pigment, and lead a small panel of their own design. The intensive culminates in a kiln-fired roundel each participant carries home.
