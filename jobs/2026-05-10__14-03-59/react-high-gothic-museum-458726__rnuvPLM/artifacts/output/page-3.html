Current Exhibitions

Three temporary exhibitions on view through Candlemas, the second day of February in the year of our Lord two thousand and twenty-five.

Exhibition · I

The Cobalt of Chartres

North Transept Gallery · Through 2 February

A close study of the pigment chemistry behind the famed bleu de Chartres, presented alongside side-by-side panels drawn from the Bois Verriers, with cross-section microphotography and trace-element analysis from the Atelier Lecot.

Curator
Dr. Hélène Mason
Panels
14 stained-glass leaves, c. 1180 – 1240
Lighting
Included with general entry
Audio Tour
FR · EN · DE · 42 min
[motif: arch-pointed]

Exhibition · II

Hand of the Master: The Reims Sketchbooks

South Cloister · Through 5 February

Thirty-eight silverpoint drawings attributed to the workshop of Villard de Honnecourt, presented for the first time in their bound order. Architectural elevations, lion studies, and a remarkable diagram of a self-winding water clock.

Curator
Prof. Aldric Foucier-Boutet
Drawings
38 leaves, silverpoint on prepared vellum
Provenance
Through 4 February
Reservation
Timed entry; max 20 visitors
[motif: arch-pointed]

Exhibition · III

Gold and Lapis: A Book of Hours from Bourges

Reliquary Chamber · Through 2 February

A private collection manuscript opened to the Annunciation miniature, with its leaf turned each Friday at the eleventh hour by conservator Frère Anselme Vaudreuil, who will speak briefly before each turning.

Origin
Bourges atelier, c. 1430 – 1455
Pigments
Gold leaf, lapis lazuli, vermilion
Folios Open
13 folios, RH 50% sharp
Capacity
24 visitors per turning
