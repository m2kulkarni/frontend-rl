Dīvān-i Shahr-i Iṣfahān

Civic Register·of the Royal City·Bureau of Records

Be it known to the residents of the Naqsh-e Jahān quarters, the khānīm of the Qaysariyya, and all householders within the watered districts of this royal city, that the gates of this Bureau stand open from dawn prayer until the closing of the great drum at sunset, that no petition shall be turned away, and that the ledgers of water, tax, and lineage are kept in good order. On the suffrage of the people and the dignity of the Crown.

Sealed this seventeenth day of Rabī' al-Awwal, by the hand of

Mīrzā Ḥusayn Khān Shīrāzī

Mayor of the Quarters · Kalāntar-i Mahall-bā

Allotment Schedule for the Mādī Nāṣirīn Canal

The mādī has set the rotation: gardens of Jūbāra at first-light on Saturday, the cloth-dyers of Bīd-ābād at midday Monday, the orchards beyond the Khājū bridge at dusk Wednesday. Disputes shall be heard at the canal-house each morning before the call to ẓuhr.

Reception · 20 Rabī' al-Awwal · three p.m.

Caravan-Tax Filing Window Now Open

Merchants returning from Tabrīz, Shīrāz, and the Gulf ports must present manifests to the Counting Hall behind the Maydān. Hides are assessed at four-and-twenty per hundred; indigo and madder at one-tenth; precious stones by separate inspection. Bring two witnesses and the seal of your caravan-bāshī.

Observance · 22 Rabī' al-Awwal · 1095

Observance for the Chief of the Cloth-Bazaar

The shops of the Qaysariyya silk row shall close at midday for three days in honour of the late raīs. The Bureau procession departs from the Masjid-i Ḥakīm after 'asr prayer; condolences are received at the family courtyard in the Darāzdūn quarter. May his memory weave on among the looms.

Hours of the Dīwān·Locations of Record

Chief Scribe's Hall, north arcade of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, opposite the gate of the Caravansarai of Sayyid Jamāl. Open daily save Friday, from the dawn azan to the setting of the sun. Subsidiary registers are kept at the Madrasa-i Chahār Bāgh (lineage & pious endowments) and at the Bridge of Allāhverdī Khān (rivers & tolls).
