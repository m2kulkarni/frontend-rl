Quarters, Canals & Bazaars

Being a true register of the twelve administrative quarters of Iṣfahān, the four principal mādīs that water the city, and the eight-and-thirty bazaar guilds confirmed by the office of the Muḥtasib.

Compass in the Reign of Shāh ‘Abbās[motif: islamic-star]Year 1095 A.H. · General

Whereas every settled tribe before the Dīwān, or would be petition concerning water-share, market-stall, or boundary of quarter, shall consult these tables. Names of the kalāntars (quarter-elders), māḥla-bands (orderly wardens), and amirān (guild-masters) are set forth herein, with their audience days at the Qaysariyya Gate, the kiosks along the Chahār Bāgh, and the western abutment of the Khwājū Bridge.
