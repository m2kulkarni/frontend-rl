Practice & Disciplines

One workshop, five disciplines drawn from the Manasara, Mayamata, and the Mahapatra silpa-sastra, practiced as continuous craftwork since 1887. We do not separate workshop on the Anna Salai marqueee.

✦ ✦ ✦ ✦ ✦ ✦ ✦ ✦ ✦
I
Vastu Survey · Ground Plan · Member Layout
[motif: lotus-flower]

Every survey commences with the kosa-square and the bronze plumb. The site is paced and oriented at first light, by senior silpis. Each ground plan is rendered to silpa-sastra members, plates of the Manasara at the proportions of the prakara axis, with diagonals confirmed by the gnomon.

Drawings are inked on cotton-fibre paper at 1:48, with the proportional cubit explicit on every elevation and section, and dispatched in pothi to the temple atisthamna for blessing before first cut.

Time & Materials

  • Survey · 2 to 4 days
  • Pencil drafts · 8 hand-days
  • Inked plates · 12 hand-days
  • From ₹ 1,40,000 · paper supplied
II
Vimana & Gopuram Tier-Design
[motif: peacock-headpiece]

Tier-design begins with the moolam, the salam-bana of the Manasara, and proceeds upward through the kosa, the gala, the pratima, and the sikra. Each tier is sized against the mahatala and the family priest's chosen nakshatra. We work in lime mortar, stucco, and basalt.

Polychromy mineral cartoons are produced full-size on the cotton flat: vermilion, peacock-blue, orpiment, ultramarine, and lamp-black, each fixed by a kadukkai-mahalingam decoction.

Pigment Series

  • Vermilion · cinnabar
  • Peacock-blue · cobalt + indigo
  • Orpiment · arsenic sulphide
  • Ultramarine · lapis fixings
III
Polychromy Restoration & Stucco-Cut
[motif: floral-headpiece]

Each figure on a gopuram is approached as a separate commission. The kosa is consolidated, the lime is refilled, and the stucco surface is dressed back to the kadukkai-fixings of the polychromy. Replacement figures are carved in lime and basalt and dressed and wired in situ.

In the work we are aided by allied workshops in Pazhamarainai for bell-metal kalasha and bronze threshold lining.

Allied Workshops

  • Pazhamarainai · bell-metal
  • Swamimalai · bronze offerings
  • Tanjore · gold-leaf vimana
  • Karaikudi · linear arabesques
IV
Granite & Krishnagiri Idol Carving
[motif: conch-shell]

In our yard at Tirumayam quarries the granite is dressed for structural members — pillars, lintels, kalasha bases — or for figural commissions. The yard is a four-acre depository of Krishnagiri black, Sadarahalli grey, and the speckled-pink Sundarapalayam stone, sized to the silpa-sastra.

Figural figures pass through the master's hand — the rough-cut and the final dressing — and are dispatched in cotton with the Sundarapalayam atisthamna as is the custom.

Stone Grades

  • Krishnagiri black · idol
  • Sadarahalli grey · structural
  • Sundarapalayam pink · figural
  • Coimbatore feldspar · base
V
Kalasha-Casting & Consecration
[motif: conch-shell]

The crowning kalasha — the single capped finial that sits in the apex of the vimana and gopuram — is cast in bell-metal in our Pazhamarainai foundry. Each kalasha is sized against the Mahalingam mahatala, sealed with the kosa-mantra, and dispatched in pothi with the gold-leaf ornament for the consecration.

Consecration is a five-day affair, observed on the chosen nakshatra of the family priest. The workshop is closed for those days, all hands at the temple precinct.

Ceremonial Hours

  • Kosa-mantra · 4.30 a.m.
  • Kalasha-cast · noon
  • Atisthamna · evening
  • Consecration · five days

All work begins with a written enquiry to the workshop and a preliminary visit by the sthapati to the site. Drawings, ritual schedules, and material estimates follow within six weeks.
