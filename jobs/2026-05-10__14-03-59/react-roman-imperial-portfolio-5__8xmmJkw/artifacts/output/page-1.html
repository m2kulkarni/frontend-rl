Lead Projects · MMXXII — MMXXIV

Three works currently on the drawing tables and at scaffold — a restoration, a pavement, and a private commission — each drawn from the long lineage of Roman imperial geometry.

Project I

Villa Aurelia

Restoration · Gianicolo, Roma

Conservation of the second-century atrium and tablinum, with reinstatement of the original opus sectile threshold and a discreet structural underpinning of the south wall.

Basilica Pavement

Studio Statement

The studio works in the conviction that Roman geometry is not a quotation but a continuing grammar — a set of rules for proportion, threshold, and ornament still capable of governing contemporary buildings. Each commission begins from the plan in ink on travertine-coloured paper, and is resolved through full-size cartoons before any stone is cut or any wall is raised. Mosaic, frescoed border, and architectural section are treated as one continuous drawing, so that a doorway and a tessera obey the same axis. We build slowly, in small numbers, for clients who understand that a pavement is also a treatise.
