Plate Tracery and the Geometry of the Quatrefoil

Delivered by Dr. Hélène Marquand, Maître de l'oglyphique, Université Paris-Sorbonne

Drawing on a decade of fieldwork at Chartres, Reims, and the lesser-known chapter house of Saint-Quentin, Dr. Marquand traces the evolution of the quatrefoil from its origins as a pierced circle in plate tracery to its full flowering as the governing geometry of the rose window. The lecture will be illustrated with original site photography and accompanied by compass diagrams.

A reception of mulled wine and almond cakes will follow in the West Cloister.

For Apprentices & Adepts

Workshops in the medieval crafts, for the young and the not-so-young

A Mason's Apprenticeship

Saturdays · 09h00 – 12h30 · Ages 11 – 15

Children take up the chisel and the rasp under the masters in the kitchen yard, learning their workshop. They will dress a small block of soft limestone, learn to read a master's mark, and assemble a model portal arch using the same wedge and keystone principles that raised the great cathedrals. Each apprentice receives a leather-bound logbook and a numbered mason's mark of their own.

120 PER YEAR · TOOLS PROVIDED

The Scriptorium Workshop

20 weeks · ages 14 – 18 only · 30h00

Under the guidance of conservator Brother Anselm Whitcombe, participants prepare gesso, lay bone-leaf gold on vellum, and burnish to a perfect finish using an agate tooth. The course concludes with the gilding of a personal initial in the manner of a thirteenth-century Beatus page. All materials — including 18-karat leaf — are supplied.

€85 · MATERIALS · INSTRUCTION INCLUDED

The Glaziers' Hand

Half-term intensive · 16 — 22 October · Ages 16+

A three-day introduction to the medieval glazier's craft. Students cut coloured glass with a grozing iron, paint trace lines with iron-oxide pigment, and lead a small panel of their own design. The intensive culminates in a kiln-fired roundel each participant carries home.
