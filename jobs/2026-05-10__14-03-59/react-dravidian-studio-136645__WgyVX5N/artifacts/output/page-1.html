A fifth-generation atelier of śilpins on East Chitra Veedhi, Madurai — founded by Sthapati Vaidyanatha Ramaswamy in 1887 within sight of the Meenakshi Amman gopuram. We draw, consult, and carve in the Dravida tradition: vastu for new mandapas, polychromy for restored gopurams, and granite figuration cut by hand.

The Threefold Practice

From the drafting hall on East Chitra Veedhi the workshop attends to three disciplines, each governed by the Manasara and the Mayamata silpa-sastras, each prized by hand-day and carried in Krishnagiri black granite or Sadasivakulam grey.

I · Vastu Consultation

New Mandapas

Site orientation, axial calculation, and full ground-plan drawings for kalyana mandapas, vasantha shrines, and village utsava temples. Each commission begins with a three-day visit to read the soil, the water-table, and the cardinal axes. Drawings are inked on cotton paper at 1:48 and accompanied by a written vāstu nyāsa properties table.

From₹ 1,80,000
II · Gopuram Restoration

Polychromy & Stucco

Survey, condition report, and full multi-colour cartoon for the śukla, vāhi, and stucco figures of storied gopurams. We work in lime-and-jaggery render and the twelve mineral pigments — vermillion, peacock, orpiment, ultramarine — fixed without acrylics. Scaffold engineering and bell-metal kalasha replacement are carried out with allied workshops in Tirumayam.

Per Tier₹ 4,40,000
III · Granite Carving

Figurative Commissions

Hand-carved Dvarapālakas and utsava figures, pillared yāli brackets, and door-jamb dvarapadmas, cut to ārtava sūtra proportions for master craftsmen K. Murugesan and his five apprentices. Standard size for a seated Ganesha in Krishnagiri black is thirty-six aṅgula, delivery in fourteen months from the day of stone selection.

From₹ 3,25,000

Current Commissions

Three works are presently under the chisel and the brush, each accepted with the blessing of the temple trustees and the family priest.

I

Rajagopuram Repainting · Tiruvannamalai

Eleven tier polychrome renewal for the western Rajagopuram of the Jambukeswarar temple — 1,400 stucco figures inventoried, pigments mixed on site in lime and jaggery, scaffold raised through the monsoon shadow.

In Progress2024 — 2026
II

Sixteen-Pillared Kalyana Mandapa · Kumbakonam

A new akshina mandāpa adjoining beside the Saranagana tank, in Sadasivakulam grey granite. Sixteen yali-bracketed pillars, a central lotus ceiling medallion, and four corner dwārapālaka drawn from our 1923 pattern book.

III
