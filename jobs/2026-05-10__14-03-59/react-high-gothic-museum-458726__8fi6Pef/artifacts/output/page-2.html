GALLERIES &
PERMANENT
COLLECTION

Seven galleries housing the cathedral arts of northern France, arranged along a single processional axis from Lancet Hall to the open Mason's Yard.

The Cloister Museum's permanent collection comprises one thousand four hundred and seventy-two catalogued objects, of which two hundred and eleven are on rotation; one hundred sixty-three are described below. The remaining works are accessible to credentialed researchers through the Reading Room by appointment, and a rotating selection appears in the museum's biennial loan exhibition. All accession numbers follow the museum standard adopted in 1962: prefix CMS, gallery code, and acquisition sequence.

THE SEVEN GALLERIES

In processional order, west to east

IGALLERY

THE LANCET HALL

CMS · LH · 001-024

PROVENANCE
Long-term loan from the Diocese of Soissons, contracted 2011, renewed 2024 for a further fifteen years. Removed from the deambulatory of Saint-Jean-des-Vignes during the 1972 restoration campaign.
CONSERVATION
Lead cames replaced 1989 (Atelier Lorin, Chartres). Vidimus film applied 2014. Annual deflection survey; current bow at panel LH-014 measured 3.1 mm, within tolerance.
IIGALLERY

THE TYMPANUM COURT

CMS · TC · 001

PROVENANCE
Amiens workshop, circa 1245. Recovered from the rubble of a demolished parish church near Boves in 1908; held by the Musée de Picardie until permanent transfer to the Cloister Museum in 1996.
CONSERVATION
Consolidated with ethyl silicate (Wacker OH 100), 2003. No biological growth recorded since installation in climate-controlled court (RH 50 ± 3%, 18 °C).
IIIGALLERY

THE SCRIPTORIUM

CMS · SCR · 001-012

PROVENANCE
Mixed Anglo-Saxon and Insular leaves, ninth to twelfth century, gathered through five private bequests. Codex CSR-007 once part of the Cheney-Beatty duplicates, 1968.
CONSERVATION
Loose vellum on a 90-day cycle to limit cumulative light exposure (target 6,750 lux-hours per annum). Mylar interleaving installed 2022.
IVGALLERY

THE RELIQUARY CHAMBER

CMS · RC · 001-040

PROVENANCE
Reliquaries of champlevé enamel, gilt copper, and rock crystal. The centrepiece is a Limoges chasse, c. 1200, depicting the martyrdom of Saint Thomas Becket.
CONSERVATION
Mounted on three-axis seismic plinths, 2015. UV filter glazing throughout. The Becket chasse underwent gold-leaf reintegration at the V&A conservation studios, 2018.
VGALLERY

THE CAPITALS CLOISTER

CMS · CC · 001-014

PROVENANCE
Forty-one carved capitals on freestanding limestone columns, arranged in a square plan around a central well. Subjects include foliate, monstrum, and historiated programmes.
CONSERVATION
Drawn from the demolished cloister of the Priory of Saint-Denis-de-Thouars (Bressuire), dispersed 1791, reassembled through nine separate acquisitions between 1923 and 2007. Soft efflorescence on capitals CC-002 and CC-007 monitored quarterly. Desalination poultices applied 2017; recurrence rate halved.
VIGALLERY

THE ROSE WINDOW STUDY

CMS · RW · 001-008

PROVENANCE
A scholarly gallery of six full-scale cartoon reconstructions of the great rose windows of Chartres, Notre-Dame de Paris, and Reims, prepared by the Atelier Simon-Marq, 1998-2014.
CONSERVATION
Pigment stability tested annually. The Notre-Dame de Paris cartoon was photographed at high resolution in 2019 and now serves the post-fire restoration committee.
VIIGALLERY

THE MASON'S YARD

CMS · MY · 001-026

PROVENANCE
An open-air gallery of eighty-eight architectural fragments — voussoirs, finials, gargoyles, rosary elements, and one full surviving spire pinnacle — laid out as a working archaeological grid.
CONSERVATION
Assembled from the salvage stocks of various French and English cathedral fabric offices, 19th century. Lead-piece bases re-keyed on-site to match the rotation period of the original fabric.
