Divān-i Isfahan

Civic Register of the Naqsh-e Jahan

Issued in the northern arcade of the Maydān-e Naqsh-e Jahan, between the Qaysarieh Gate and the Mosque of Sheikh Lotfollāh, this office attends to the affairs of His Majesty Shah Abbas, the prince of Safavids, Defender of the Faith — and of the people, the merchants, and the welfare of the city and realm.

Notices of the Week

Three matters published this day for the city of Isfahan
From the Royal Court

Edict of His Majesty
Shah ʿAbbas

Be it known to all merchants, caravan-masters, and travelers passing through the Maydān that the new caravanserai at Mader-i Shah, erected by royal command, shall open its gates to lodgers from the Eve of Jumādā al-Awwal next.

Tariffs upon the silk of Gilan, raw and woven, are by the same hand reduced by one part in ten, that the trade of His Majesty's domains may flourish.

Public Works

Notice — Zayandeh-Rud
Canal Works

The Master of Waters announces that, beginning the fifth of this month, the dredging and re-lining of the canal of Farahin shall commence, with labour drawn from the wards of Joulmek and Darikalan.

Watering of orchards along the southern channel shall be permitted only between the second prayer and the call of asr-namaz for the duration of forty days.

Any obstruction of the works, or diverting of waters without leave, shall be answered before the Divānbegi.

— Mirza Taqi, Mir-āb of Isfahan
