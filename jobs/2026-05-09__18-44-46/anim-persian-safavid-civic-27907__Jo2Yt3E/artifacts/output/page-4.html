Hereunder are recorded the labours undertaken by command of the Dīwānbegi for the maintenance of His Majesty's capital — the watercourses, the spans of stone, and the lodgings for travellers — together with the names of those master-builders to whom each charge is entrusted, and the quarters (mahalle) answerable in attendance.

Works Now in Hand

Three undertakings for the relief and improvement of the City
Work the First

Dredging of the Maʿbar at Naʿma'in

The silting of the upper canal between the orchards of Naʿma'in and the Sluice of Chahar Bāgh is to be removed; sluice-gates of cedar shall be replaced and the brick lining renewed where the spring of His Majesty's domains may flourish.

MASTER-BUILDERUstād Mehrāz-ji bin Hussain
QUARTER ANSWERABLEMahalle of Joubāre
PROVISIONS BUDGETEDforty-two diggers, twelve craftsmen
ALLOTTED FROM THE TREASURYThree hundred and sixty tomans
EXPECTED COMPLETIONat the rising of Asm

IN PROGRESS — SECOND MONTH

Work the Second

Repair of the Allahverdi Khan Bridge

The parapets of the five-zi pierd, worn by the leaning of foot-trotters and the winters' rain, are to be re-set in dressed stone from the quarry at Najafabad. Four niches on the eastern flank receive new paving sandstone.

MASTER-BUILDERUstād Sajan-Mohammad of Tabriz, sworn-master
QUARTER ANSWERABLEMahalle of Julfa, north of the Zayandeh-rud
PROVISIONS BUDGETEDTwo hundred and twenty stones
ALLOTTED FROM THE TREASURYFive hundred and ten tomans
EXPECTED COMPLETIONThe 27 of Mihrgān, ensuit of fall

BRIDGE CLOSED AT DUSK · FOOT PASSAGE BY NORTH SIDE

Work the Third

New Gate of the Caravanserai of Darb-e Kushk

A vaulted brick gate of fired brick, faced with cobalt and turquoise tile in the manner of the Lutfollah, is to stand at the western entry of the caravanserai newly endowed by His Khanazi out forty days.

MASTER-BUILDERUstād Riza-Beyg of Tabriz tile-mason
QUARTER ANSWERABLEMahalle of Joubāre, with two of the Mughul gilded
PROVISIONS BUDGETEDSixteen workmen, six hands
ALLOTTED FROM THE TREASURYTwo hundred and seventy tomans plus the work-mate
EXPECTED COMPLETIONWithin ninety days, from the breaking of fast

FOUNDATION LAID — VAULTING TO BEGIN

A Word of Thanks unto the Quarter of Jolfa

To the Christian community of New Julfa, beyond the river

The Dīwān records, with the gratitude of the City and the favour of His Majesty, the gift of plane-wood timber furnished by the merchants and householders of the Julfa quarter for the renewal of the bridge parapets. Forty beams, dressed and seasoned, were brought across the Zāyandeh-rud at the citizens' own expense, and shall be cut in place by the hand of Ustād Sefjan-Mohammad. May their generosity be remembered in the chronicles of this reign.
