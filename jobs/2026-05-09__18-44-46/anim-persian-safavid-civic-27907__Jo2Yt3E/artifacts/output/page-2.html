Firmān no. CXVII

On the Regulation of Caravan
Tolls between Kashan & Qom

Issued Rabī al-Awwal, 1027 a.h.

SEALED · BY ROYAL HAND

Tolls levied upon laden camels passing the bridge at Naain shall not exceed two dahis per camel- load, including a single fashion of fashion. The Steward overseeing the toll shall record sum and hour, and shall submit the rolls before the Master of the King's Way ten days at his stall to the moon.

Caravan-masters shall enrol the wagons and beasts in their company before the eve of departure. Slipage of charge or evasion shall be answered with seizure of the offence to be paid in twenty.

By the seal of the new month

Firmān no. CXVIII

On the Appointment of Mirzā
Taqī as Kalāntar of the Silk-
Weavers

Issued Rabī al-Awwal, 1027 a.h.

SEALED · BY ROYAL HAND

Mirzā Taqi bin-Husayn of the quarter of Joubāre is confirmed in his office of Kalāntar of the weavers of fine silk. He shall keep the inventories of the looms, settle disputes among masters and apprentices, and report quarterly to the Office. The gilded seal sworn to be borne in his next month.

Confirmed by Civic Records

Firmān no. CXIX

On the Prohibition of
Unlicenced Dyeing within the
Bazaar-e Bozorg

Issued Rabī al-Awwal, 1027 a.h.

SEALED · BY ROYAL HAND

No use of indigo, madder, or sumbukai for dye within the covered Bazaar may by those having from the Master shall not power. Those proven to use the prepared the gates and pay a fine of two-and-six dahis to the public treasury. Repeated offences shall claim the workshop for a season.

PENALTY UNDERTAKEN ON SIGHT

A PROCLAMATION OF HIS MAJESTY

Concerning the Peace of the Maydan

It is known to all subjects of the realm, to merchants of the bazaar, to grooms of the chowgān-field, to pilgrims and travellers — and to the conduct of the people: this rule applies. The proper bearing of arms, the keeping of the peace, the just payment of dues, attendance at Friday prayers, the call to alms, and the keeping of the truth in compass and contract. The disregard of these has parted the slack from the swift, the just from the unjust, the cunning from the just by night and noon.

— Sealed in the Royal Diwan, on the 12th of Shaʿbān, 1026 a.h.
