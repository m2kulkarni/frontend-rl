校舎と来訪のしおり CAMPUS & VISITING

Four buildings,
one quiet block of
Higashiyama.

The Hokurin campus occupies a single deep machiya plot south of Sanjō-dōri, where a 1782 timber-framed merchant home still serves as the administrative centre. Around it, three later buildings — a 1964 Andō concrete hall, a 2008 library annex, and a 2018 craft workshop — were each set carefully behind the original eaves, leaving the street face unchanged. Visitors are welcome year-round, and twice a year for our open-campus days.

Two routes by foot

Both walks pass through the Sanjō machiya district. Wear a soft sole if you can; the cobble courses date to 1894.

From Higashiyama Station

Exit 2 of the Tōzai subway line. Walk south along Sanjō-dōri for 280 metres, cross into the older lane just past the unmarked Inari shrine, and continue 60 metres. The campus is the third gate on the right.

From Keage Incline

Cut down through the Murin-an gardens (open in season), then follow the dōrokuyō east-west alleyway for 600 metres past the bell-tower noodle shop. The campus gate is on the south side.

By the bus

Kyoto City Bus #5 or #100. Alight at Sanjō-Higashiyama-mae. Walk south 80 metres. The Sanjō Gate of Hokurin Daigaku is unmarked except for a small enamel plate.

Open-campus days

校舎一般公開日

Twice a year the campus opens its doors to courtyards, conservation studios, and rare-book vault to the public. No registration is required; printed walking guides in Japanese, English, and French are offered at the Sanjō genkan.
