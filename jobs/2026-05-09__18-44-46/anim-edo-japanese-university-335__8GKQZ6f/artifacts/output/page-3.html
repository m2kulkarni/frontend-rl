入学のご案内 ADMISSIONS

Apply to
Hokurin.

Hokurin admits a small undergraduate cohort and a smaller graduate institute each year — roughly 320 entering students across the machiya campus. The academic calendar offers three intake windows for AY 2025–2026, with rolling review for international applicants. We read each application by hand, in committee, and reply in the season we received it.

— Three Intake Windows

APPLICATION CALENDAR · 2025/2026
AUTUMN INTAKE · 秋期

Standard Domestic Cycle

1 September 2025

Application portal opens 1 July. Documents due 15 September. Written examination 12 October at the Sanjō campus. Decisions issued by 15 November for January matriculation.

SPRING INTAKE · 春期

Late Cycle & Transfers

15 January 2026

Reserved for transfer students from accredited Japanese institutions and second-degree applicants. Examination 8 February. Limited to fourteen seats across the Craft Sciences faculty.

INTERNATIONAL · 国際

Rolling Review

Ongoing

Reviewed within 30 days of receipt. JLPT N2 or equivalent recommended; written exam waivable for applicants holding completed degree-track Japanese coursework. Visa support letter issued upon offer.

二 Documents & Examination

出願書類および入試科目

Documents

01
Application form 入学願書

Completed in Japanese or English, signed in ink. Available from the admissions portal or by post.

02
Secondary transcript 成績証明書

Original sealed transcript covering all years of upper-secondary study, with official translation if not in Japanese.

03
Statement of intent 志望理由書

1,200–1,800 characters (Japanese) or 600–900 words (English) addressing the chosen faculty.

04
Two letters of reference 推薦状

At least one from a teacher of record. For graduate applicants, both must be academic.

05
Photograph & identity copy 写真・身分証

Recent 30×40mm photograph and copy of passport or residence card.

06
Examination fee receipt 検定料領収書

¥17,000 undergraduate · ¥30,000 graduate. Waivable under Tier I and II tuition relief.

Entrance Examination

REQUIRED PAPERS · 必須科目

Japanese & English

120 minutes each. Japanese paper includes a short literary commentary (modern or classical, applicant's choice) and a précis. English paper combines reading comprehension with a 400-word analytical response.

FIELD OF THE CHOSEN COURSE

Mathematics or World History

90 minutes. Mathematics covers the standard upper-secondary curriculum through Math III/B; World History favours interpretation of primary sources (Edo, late-Qing, early Meiji). Choose one at registration.

INTERVIEW · 面接

Twenty-Minute Conversation

Two faculty members. Conducted in Japanese, English suitable for international cycle. The conversation begins from the statement of intent; there are no trick questions and no formal dress requirement.

志は遠く、歩みは近し。
DISTANT AIM, NEAR STEP
Hokurin's admissions seal is affixed by hand to each offer letter at the close of the cycle. The paulownia is the formal mark of the university registrar; you will see it once on your envelope, and again on your diploma four years later.
