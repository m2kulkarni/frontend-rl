SALVE, AMICE

The atelier welcomes inquiries concerning new architectural commissions, the restoration and consultancy of historic surfaces and pavements, and academic collaborations with universities, museums, and editorial houses. Correspondence is read each Tuesday morning; a written reply follows within the working week. Visitors are received at Via dei Coronari by prior appointment, that the studio may be properly prepared for your arrival.

I

COMMISSIONES NOVAE

New architectural commissions — villas, civic interiors, ecclesiastical and funerary works. Initial consultations conducted in studio or on site.

II

RESTAURATIO

Consultation for the conservation of antique pavements, fresco surfaces, and masonry. Reports prepared in Italian, English, or Latin upon request.

III

STUDIA ACADEMICA

Academic collaboration: lectures, plate contributions, and joint research with institutes engaged in the study of Roman building practice.
