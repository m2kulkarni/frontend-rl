Within the atelier the mosaic bench has stood since 2009 — a long oak table, raised north light, and trays of marble cut to four millimetres. From this room Lucius Vennius works as both designer and conservator, setting new pavements for civic patrons and consulting on the recovery of antique surfaces from Volubilis to Pompeii.

THE SOURCING OF TESSERAE

BIANCO DI CARRARA

tesserae · stone xum

Cut from the Polvaccio quarry. In 50 kg billets and reduced by hand on the medoline to 4–6 mm cubes. Vennius reserves the lightest seam for the eyes of figures.

ROSSO DI VERONA

veneto · vena rotunda

A modular ammonitic limestone whose iron-rich matrix gives the deep burnt-rust the Romans called rubra. Used for cornice borders and wall registers.

PORFIDO EGIZIANO

imperialis lapis

Imperial purple porphyry, quarried in the Eastern Desert and pebble-imported to Italy in antiquity. Sourced through licensed dealers; not used in restoration of mid-imperial period floors.

CONSERVATION METHOD

VII

Each fragment leaves the atelier with a small folio noting tessera count, average grain size, weight per square metre, and the precise mortar by which it is set: a record kept against the day a future hand must lift it again. The atelier holds to a seven-step protocol — from the first dry-brushed surface survey through humidity-controlled curing of the new bedding mortar — refused only on the express written instruction of a patron.

THREE CASE STUDIES

ORPHEUS PAVEMENT
SITEHouse of Orpheus
YEARMMXIX · 2019
PATRONInst. Nat. Sciences de l'Archéologie
MATERIALSIn situ limestone, Carrara, recovered antique tesserae

Engaged as consulting conservator on the third-century pavement depicting Orpheus charming the beasts — the largest figural mosaic surviving in Mauretania Tingitana, measuring 8.4 by 7.1 metres. The brief was non-invasive: stabilise lifting borders, re-bed the hollow zone beneath the central medallion, and write a long-term maintenance protocol for the open-air site.

Six months of fieldwork in the company of three local masons. No tesserae was lifted; 412 cubes from the loose perimeter were re-fixed in matched lime. The dossier survives as the working document for site stewardship.

VILLA QUINTILIA
SITEVilla Quintilia, Lazio
YEARMMXXIV —
PATRONSoprintendenza Archeologica
MATERIALSSmalti, gold leaf, Carrara

Restoration of a fragmentary opus sectile threshold from a private suite of the imperial-period villa. Working with a team of two stonecutters, Vennius drew the original geometry from the iron-stained imprint left in the bedding mortar and reset the surviving 41% of original elements with new infill of matched marbles.

FONS LONELLINI
SITEGenoa, Pal. Lonellini
YEARMMXXIV · 2024
PATRONP. Lonellini, collector
MATERIALSAegean smalti, gold tessera, dark porphyry

A new floor for the entrance hall of a private collector's house in Genoa. Vennius proposed a single concentric meditation in seven blues, deepening from a near-black at the perimeter to a pale Cycladic at the centre.
