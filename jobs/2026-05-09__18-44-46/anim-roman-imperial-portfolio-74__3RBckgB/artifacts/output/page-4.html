THE ARCHITECT

Lucius Vennius was born in Ravenna in the spring of 1976, four blocks from the Mausoleum of Galla Placidia, and grew up taking the long way to school in order to pass beneath its star vault. He studied architecture at the Politecnico di Milano under Pier Carlo Bombelli and afterward took the two-year diploma at the Scuola di Restauro di Spoleto, where he learned to set tesserae by hand on lime-and-pozzolana beds.

From 2007 to 2010 he apprenticed at Bottega Cardelli in Trastevere, working on the re-laying of the fourth-century floors at Santa Pudenziana and on three private commissions for villas in the Castelli Romani. The atelier was founded in 2011 in a ground-floor workshop on Via dei Coronari, where it has remained.

The practice is small and deliberately so — Vennius, two associates, and a rotating bench of three or four masonists drawn from Spoleto, Aquileia, and Ravenna. Every commission, whether a private chapel or a fifteen-metre apse, is drawn entirely by hand on the long oak table at the back of the studio. Nothing leaves the door without first being walked across at full scale.

Three instruments govern the studio. None of them is a screen. Each is older than the practice that uses it, and each is slower than the patron expects.

AXIAL PLANS, DRAWN BY HAND

Every project begins with a single ink drawing on Fabriano 220 g paper, drawn along the centre and decorations of the site. The pen is a Rotring 0.35; the compass is brass, inherited.

Symmetry is not decorative but a working tool: a plan that does not balance on its central axis is, in this studio, a plan that is not yet finished.

FULL-SCALE MOSAIC CARTOONS

Each mosaic is first produced 1:1 in charcoal on kraft paper, pinned to the long studio wall. The cartoon is walked, photographed in raking light, and corrected for ten to fourteen days before a single tessera is cut.

Marble is sourced from Carrara, Verona Italy, from Aurelia, and the smalti — when required — from Orsoni in Cannaregio.

PLASTER MODELS FOR VAULTS

Domes, groin vaults, and apsidal half-domes are first built at 1:20 in hydraulic plaster on a turned wooden formwork. The model is lit with a single lamp positioned at a full day to test the play of cast shadow upon the sculpted forms.

Only after the plaster is approved by the client, in the studio, is the structural drawing released to engineers.

STRUCTURAL ENGINEER

MARCO FENOGLIO

ingegnere strutturista · Torino, b. 1971
Trained at the Politecnico di Torino and previously of Trasini & Milani, Marco has been the studio's structural consultant since 2013. He calculates the masonry-borne thrust for every vault, "on graph paper" — and feels, only by his confirmation, is finite element software acceptable. He has stayed on a single argument about a flying buttress for four years; nothing leaves his floor.
MASTER MASON

GIULIA TREVI

capomastra · Tivoli · b. 1981
Third-generation mason of the Trevi family of Tivoli. Giulia leads the on-site crew on every Atelier project from foundation to baseplate. Her speciality is opus mixtum in pozzolanic mortar, and her crew of seven has recently set every wall and column course laid by the atelier since MMXIX. She was on her way home, on her way to Genoa, in February of MMXXIV. She does not turn down a green-marble flat.
