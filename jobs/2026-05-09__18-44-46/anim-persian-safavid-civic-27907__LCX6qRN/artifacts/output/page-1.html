Diván-i Isfáhán

Court Register of the Naqsh-e Jahán

Issued in the imperial workshops of the Maydán-i Naqsh-e Jahán, between the Qaysarieh Gate and the Mosque of Sheikh Lotfollah, by order of His Majesty Shah ʿAbbás, the prince of the Safavid line, may his name stand for the welfare of the city and city all.

Notices of the Week

Three matters published this day for the city of Isfáhán.

From The Royal Court

Edict of His Majesty
Shah ʿAbbás

Be it known to all merchants, caravan masters, and travellers passing through the Maydán that the new caravanserai at Madar-i Shah, erected by royal command, shall open its gates to lodgers from the first of Jumādá al-Awwal next.

Tariffs upon the silk of Gilán, raw and woven, are by the same hand reduced by one part in ten, that the trade of His Majesty's domains may flourish.

Read · Royal Sigil

Public Notice

Notice — Zayandeh-Rud
Canal Works

The Master of Waters announces that, beginning on the fifth of this month, the dredging and re-lining of the canal of Khvāju shall commence, with labour drawn from the wards of Joulfah and Dardasht.

Watering of orchards along the southern channel shall be permitted only between the second prayer and the call of sunset for the duration of forty days.

Any obstruction of the works, or diverting of water without leave, shall be answered before the Diwánbegi.

— Mírzá Taqī, Mír-Áb of Isfáhán
