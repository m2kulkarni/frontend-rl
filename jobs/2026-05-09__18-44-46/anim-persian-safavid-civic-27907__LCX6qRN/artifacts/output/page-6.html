Notice to Travellers, Merchants & Subjects

Visiting the Diván

Practical orders for those who would attend the office, in person — by Carriage.

To Find Us

Beneath the northern arcade of the Maydán-e Naqsh-e Jahán, between the Qaysarieh Gate and the Mosque of Sheikh Lotfollah.

The Address

Diván-i Isfáhán

Northern Arcade, third bay east of the Qaysarieh Gate

Maydán-e Naqsh-e Jahán

Isfáhán, Domains of His Majesty Shah ʿAbbás the Great

By Which Gate

Visitors entering by the Qaysarieh shall keep the cobalt arcade upon their left hand.

Those approaching from the southern bazaar pass first the Mosque of Sheikh Lotfollah; the office lies seven arches beyond.

Look for the gilded star above the lintel.

Mounts & Caravans

Riding-animals — horses, mules, and camels — are to be left at the Southern Caravanserai beside the Bazaar of the Saddlers.

A stable-fee of two shahis per beast per day is collected by the master of the yard.

No animal may enter the Maydan during hours of audience.

The Path Through the Maydan

A walking route from the southern caravanserais, where mounts are stabled, to the gilded lintel of the Diván.

Step by Step

  1. Southern Caravanserai. Surrender your mount to the master of the yard. Receive a brass token bearing the day's number.
  2. Through the Qaysarieh Bazaar. Pass beneath the great gate, keeping a quiet step. The cloth-merchants' line both sides do not turn from it.
  3. Enter the Maydán. Emerge at the northern end. The pole-ground lies before you; the great pool reflects the sky.
  4. Bear right along the arcade. Follow the cobalt-tile arch; the seventh arch upward bears the gilded star.
  5. Find the gilded star. Above the third bay you will see the eight-pointed seal of the Diván, set in gold upon dark ground.
  6. Enter the courtyard. Remove sandals, draft. Petitions are received in the outer court; audiences in the inner hall.

Conduct Within the Courtyard

A short list of obligations and forbiddances for those who would attend.

You Shall Do

  • Remove sandals and outer dust at the threshold; servants of the porch will hold them in chamber.
  • Address the Diwánbegi as Saheb-i ʿadālat when first speaking, with hands held together at the breast.
  • Stand when you speak; sit upon the cushion when finished.
  • Refer to your witnesses by name when called by the scribe.
  • Accept the ruling whether favourable or contrary; appeals are by petition only.

You Shall Not Do

  • Shouting. Voice raised above the prayer-tone, drawing of an attendant at once.
  • Weapons. Sword, dagger, or matchlock are surrendered to the porter at the gate.
  • Tobacco. The smoking pipe is forbidden within all chambers of the Diván.
  • Coin. No gift, no bribe, however veiled, is to be offered to any officer of the Diván.
  • Companions. Beyond the two witnesses, no companion enters the inner hall.

By the order of the Diwánbegi.
