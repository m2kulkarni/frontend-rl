Farmán of Civil

On the Regulation of Caravan
Tolls between Káshán & Qom

Sealed: 8th of Awwal, 1037 A.H.
Effective: at once.

The toll levied upon laden camels passing the bridge at Natanz shall not exceed two shahis per beast laden, two-thirds upon the lighter pack mules, one half upon the unburdened. Pilgrims to the shrine of Hazrat Maʿsuma travel toll-free upon presentation of seal.

In Force ◆ From the New Moon
Farmán of Civil

On the Appointment of Mírzā
Taqī as Kalāntar of the Silk-
Weavers

Sealed: 5th of Awwal, 1037 A.H.
Confirmed by the chamberlains of the gold-thread.

Mírzá Taqī ibn Hosseyn of the quarter of Joulfah is confirmed kalāntar over the silk-weavers of Isfáhán, by the votes of his fellow guildsmen, and by the seal of His Majesty. He shall hold this office for three years, during which his decisions in matters of guild dispute and tariff shall be set down quarterly to this office.

Confirmed by Guild Assembly
Farmán of Civil

On the Prohibition of
Unlicensed Dyeing within the
Bazaar-e Bozorg

Sealed: 18th of Rabi' al-Awwal, 1037 A.H.
Effective: at once.

No vat of indigo, madder, or cochineal shall be set within the covered bazaar save by those bearing the kalāntar's mark and the seal of His Majesty. Goods found dyed in unsanctioned premises shall be confiscated and burned, and the offender fined ten tomans for the first occurrence, the public estuary. Repeat offenders shall be put to the bastinado.

Penalty Doubles upon Repeat

A Proclamation of His Majesty

Concerning the Peace of the Maydan

It is be known to all subjects of the realm, to merchants of the bazaar, to grooms of the chevigán-field, to pilgrims and travellers alike, that the Maydán-i Naqsh-e Jahán is sanctified ground for the commerce and recreation of the people. No quarrel of arms, no driving of laden beasts after the second call to prayer, no unauthorized assembly exceeding twenty persons, shall be tolerated within its compass. The dárúgha and his men patrol the four arcades by day and by night.

— Sealed in the Royal Chancery, on the 12th of Awwal, 1037 A.H.
