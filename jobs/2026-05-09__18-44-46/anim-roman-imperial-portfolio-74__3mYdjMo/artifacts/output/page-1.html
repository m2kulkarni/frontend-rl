Synopsis Operum

THREE DISCIPLINES, ONE HAND

I

ARCHITECTURE

Aedificatio

Houses, libraries, and small chapels conceived in the proportional language of the Roman orders. Each commission begins with a measured site survey and a hand-drawn ground plan in ink on linen, before any wall is conceived in stone or in plan.

19 buildings · 2017 – 2024
II

MOSAIC

Opus Tessellatum

Restoration and original composition in cut stone and smalti. Recent work includes the reintegration of the Villa Quintilia threshold mosaic and a new floor of Aegean blues commissioned for the Genoese collector Pietro Lunelli in spring 2024.

23 commissions · 41 restorations
III

CIVIC MONUMENTS

Monumenta Publica

Memorials, civic fountains, and small public porticoes. Selected by the Comune di Aquileia in 2022 to design the Lapis Memoriae, a travertine cenotaph honouring the city's stonecutters' guild — completed and dedicated in October 2023.

8 works · 4 cities · MMXIII –
Vitruvius · De Architectura · I.III
"Haec autem ita fieri debent, ut habeatur ratio
FIRMITATIS, UTILITATIS, VENUSTATIS."

Two thousand and ninety years after Vitruvius set down his triad — that a building must stand, must serve, must delight — this atelier holds to the same three obligations, in that order. We draw with the column and the arch because they are the longest-tested instruments humanity has agreed upon. We cut tesserae because a floor that endures a thousand winters is a quieter argument than any rendering. We build slowly, and only when the patron is willing to be patient with us.

L. Vennius · Architectus
