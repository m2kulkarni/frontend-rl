THE ARCHITECT

Vita Brevis
BORNRavenna, 14 May 1976 EDUCATIONPolitecnico di Milano — Laurea in Architecture, 2003. Scuola di Restauro di Spoleto — Diploma in Opus Tessellatum, 2007 PRIOR PRACTICEAtelier Bottega Cardelli, Roma — 2007 – 2010 LANGUAGESItaliano · English · Latine (lectio)

Lucius Vennius was born in Ravenna in the spring of 1976, four blocks from the Mausoleum of Galla Placidia, and grew up taking the long way to school in order to pass beneath its star-vault. He studied architecture at the Politecnico di Milano under Pier Carlo Bontempi and afterward took the two-year diploma at the Scuola di Restauro di Spoleto, where he learned to set tessera by hand on lime-and-pozzolana beds.

From 2007 to 2010 he apprenticed at Bottega Cardelli in Trastevere, working on the re-laying of the fourth-century floors at Santa Pudenziana and on three private commissions for villas in the Castelli Romani. The atelier was founded in 2011 in a ground-floor workshop on Via dei Coronari, where it has remained.

The practice is small and deliberately so — Vennius, two associates, and a rotating bench of three or four mosaicists drawn from Spoleto, Aquileia, and Ravenna. Every commission, whether a private chapel or a fifteen-metre apse, is drawn entirely by hand on the long oak table at the back of the studio. Nothing leaves the door without that being walked across at full scale.

Three instruments govern the studio. None of them is a crown. Each is older than the practice that uses it, and each is slower than the patron expects.

Structural drawing

AXIAL PLANS, DRAWN BY HAND

Every project begins with a single ink drawing on Fabriano 220 g paper, drawn along the bonds and pencil-set of the Roman canon. The drawing remains the master record throughout construction; nothing leaves the bench without it.

Mosaic studio

FULL-SCALE MOSAIC CARTOONS

Every mosaic is first produced 1:1 in charcoal on kraft paper, pinned to the long studio wall. The pattern is walked, corrected, and only then transferred to the mortar bed in tessera form by the bench in 60-cm batches.

Material model

PLASTER MODELS FOR VAULTS

Domes, groin vaults, and apsidal half-domes are first built in plaster at 1:25 in the studio, in the position the client, in the studio, in the structural layout intended for it.

Structural engineer

MARCO FENOGLIO

Ingegnere strutturale · Torino · b. 1972

Trained at the Politecnico di Torino and previously at Favero & Milan, Marco has been the studio's structural consultant since 2012. He calculates the masonry-thickened domes in plaster — and feels, citizen, as Cardinella, in firms expressed — and feels, fast, the certificates, as a wall of stone.

Master mosaicist

GIULIA TREVI

Capomaestra · Tivoli · b. 1981

Third-generation master of the Trevi family of Tivoli, Giulia keeps the on-site bench on every Atelier project from foundation to dedication. Her speciality is opus tenuissimum in pozzolana mortar, and her crew of master cutters has laid every metre of formal stone since 2018. She insists not portrait power lives above the bench.
