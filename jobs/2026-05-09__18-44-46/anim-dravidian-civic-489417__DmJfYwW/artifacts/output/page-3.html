Restoration · Heritage Cell

Synaria Procesional Tanks & the Mandapam Market

The Heritage Cell is presently engaged in the restoration of the Synaria procesional tanks (the Northern and Southern, of approximate twelfth-century date) under a five-phase rolling protocol developed in consultation with the École Française d'Extrême-Orient.

  • Year I — desilting of the Northern Tank, and re-laying of the granite ablution steps in lime mortar to the 1894 Madras Survey alignment.
  • Year II — re-pointing of the four corner mandapams; replacement of the iron tie-rods with corrosion-stable bronze.
  • Year III — re-establishment of the float-bearing channel between the two tanks.
Records · Antiquity Registry

Custody, Use & Custodial Limits

The Heritage Cell holds custody — but not ownership — of every protected mandapam in the precinct. The cell's authority to intervene is limited by Section 14(c) of the Tamil Nadu Heritage Preservation Act of 1987.
