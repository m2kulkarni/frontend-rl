Tax Notice · Revenue CellNotice No. RC/PT/2024-25/03

Revised Property Tax Assessment Schedule for Zones A & C

Pursuant to the General Council resolution G.C. 18/2024, the property tax in Zones A & C will be revised effective 01 Janavari 2025 in accordance with the rate-card lodged at the Revenue Cell.

  • Residential properties in Zone A — increase of 6.5% on annual rateable value.
  • Commercial properties in Zone A — increase of 11.0%.
  • Residential properties in Zone C — increase of 4.2%; commercial of 7.5%.
  • The Heritage Precinct (Zones D & E) is exempt from any revision in this cycle.

Citizens aggrieved by the revised assessment may file an objection in writing at Counter 7 of the Revenue Cell, on or before 15 Dec 2024.

Public Notice · Traffic ControlOrder No. PWD/TC/2024-25/22

Road Closure · Chitrai Veethi during the Brahmotsavam Temple-Car Festival

Citizens are informed that the lateral lane of Chitrai Veethi, between the South Mada Veedi and the procesional tank entrance, will be closed to all vehicular traffic from 03:00 IST on 12 Mārkaḷi until 22:00 IST on 14 Mārkaḷi for the passage of the temple chariot.

  • Diversions will be in place via Sannidhi East Lane and Madavar Lane.
  • Bullock-cart and pedestrian access remain unaffected.
  • Heavy vehicles must seek prior written exemption at the Traffic Cell.
