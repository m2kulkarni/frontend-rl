Office of the Commissioner ◆ Proclamation 14 / III · 2024

The Ward Roll for Fiscal 2024–25 stands published this day.

Thiruvanam Civic Authority — constituted 1947 under the Madras Municipal Act and reconstituted under TN Act 22 of 1994 — administers the township of Thiruvanam across forty-two wards along the south bank of the Kaveri. The total electoral and revenue roll is now lodged with the Tāhsīldar and may be inspected at the Town Hall reading room until the 30th of November.

Works Vote · 2024–25

Three Schemes · Sanctioned 12 Āppaśi
Scheme I · Civic Works

Kaveri Embankment Restoration

Public Works · Wards 1 & 4

Reinforcement of the masonry bund between Kavalakkārar slipway and the Sannidhi steps with cut-stone facing and lateral storm-drains, restoring the 1879 alignment surveyed by the Madras Presidency.

₹ 18.20 Cr· 22 months
Scheme II · Heritage Lighting

Heritage Precinct Lighting

Heritage Cell · Wards 5 & 6

Replacement of the sodium-vapour mast lamps along the four-mada veethis of the temple precinct with low-glare warm LED stanchions cast in the original 1932 lantern pattern.

₹ 3.74 Cr· 9 months
Scheme III · Sanitation

Ward 14 Drainage Rebuild

Sanitation · East Quarter

Complete reconstruction of the storm and foul-water sewer network of Ward 14 (Śingarālūr Theru and adjoining lanes) following the floods of October last, with new concrete trunk laid to a 1-in-400 fall.

₹ 9.92 Cr· 14 months
Address to the Commissioner

On the duty of custodianship.

The Authority's labour is twofold: We administer drains, street-lamps, and the markets at Periya Theru — the daily mechanics by which a town breathes. We also keep watch over the stone gopurams, the bathing tanks, and the inscriptions whose donors are eight centuries gone.

"A municipality is not a corporation that owns its town; it is the temporary custodian of a settlement that owns itself, and which has lent us its keys for one term only."

I invite every resident to read the published Ward Roll, to attend the monthly Darbar, and to write to this office on any matter — however small — that touches upon the dignity of public space in Thiruvanam.

R. Subhalaksmi Commissioner · Thiruvanam Civic Authority
