An Address from the Mir-āb

A Word of Thanks unto the Quarter of Jolfa

To the Diván-yards, with the gratitude of the Lord, the Master of the Waters, by all in this town do here gratefully acknowledge that the labourers of the Armenian quarter, the artisans, and the merchants of the silk have continually borne with patience the disturbance of the road through their bazaar; the same have proven untiringly under the trials of the days of Ramazan, that the works might continue with no surcease. May their works be blessed and may their masters be set in good repute, that the trade of the city may continue uninterrupted, the works of His Majesty progress, and the channels of the Zayandeh-rud flow ever clean.
