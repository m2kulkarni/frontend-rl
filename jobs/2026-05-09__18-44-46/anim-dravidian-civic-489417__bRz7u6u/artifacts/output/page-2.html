Section II · Wards & Administration

Twenty-Seven Wards of
Thiruvanam

The municipal limits of Thiruvanam are divided into twenty-seven elected wards across four administrative zones — Sannidhi, Kaveri, Pillaiyar Koil, and Periya Theru. Each ward returns one councillor to the General Council, which sits on the second Tuesday of every month at the Chola Hall, Town Hall complex.

Standing CommitteesBicameral Constitution

Two Houses of the General Council

Pursuant to Government Order (Ms.) 1623/MS/MA/2019 dated 12 June 2019, the General Council of Thiruvanam sits as two committees of co-equal standing. Resolutions affecting heritage precincts require concurrent assent of both bodies.

The Committee enjoys statutory consultation authority on all building permissions within the precinct boundary as scheduled to Order Schedule 4, Section 8.
