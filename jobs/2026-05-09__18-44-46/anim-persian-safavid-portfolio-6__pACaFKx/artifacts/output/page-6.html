◆   The Commissioning Ledger   ◆

On Receiving a Commission

A Note on Scope, Time, & What Passes Between Us.

The Atelier accepts a small number of architectural and devotional commissions each year. Each project begins with a written brief, a site visit where possible, and the preparation of a one-fifth scale current cartoon drawn with material samples drawn from our own kilns at Na'in and Yazd. Nothing is fired until the cartoon has been signed.

§ I · Minimum Scope

4 m² of tilework

Below this, the geometry cannot resolve into a complete cartouche; smaller objects are accepted only in restoration.

§ II · Lead Time

6 – 14 months

From signed cartoon to consecration, depending on glaze recipe, kiln cycle, and the seasonal humidity.

§ III · Drawings & Samples

Prepared on Agreement

A binding agreement signed at the deposit. A sample sheet of glazes accompanies the file of citations; samples are kept in the studio archive.

§ IV · Installation

By the Atelier Itself

The master and our select northern hand to the for setting; the master is master to the present.

I

Letter, Brief

A written description of the surface, light, and intent. Photographs and elevations are welcomed.

II

Cartoon

1 : 5 ink drawing on paper, with a small panel of fired sample to the proposed palette.

III

Cutting & Firing

Tiles are by hand from manufacturable slabs; a single cobalt firing, then gold over-glaze where called for.

IV

Setting

On site assembly, face-down on paper templates, set in lime, then paper washed off after curing.

Bismillāh, the door is open
