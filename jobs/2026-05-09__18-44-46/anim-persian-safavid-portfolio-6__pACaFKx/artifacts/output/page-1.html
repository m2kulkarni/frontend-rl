What the Atelier Undertakes

The workshop holds to three lineages of inherited ornament, taught hand-to-hand from the masters of the Chahār-bāgh and practiced today in monuments, prayer halls, and private commissions across Iran and the Persian-speaking diaspora.

I · MMI

Tile Mosaic

Kāshī-e Mu'arraq

Cut tile insetting in the soluble turquoise-gold ivory cores, and seven colors haft-rang for arabesque fields and Quranic banding. Tessera fired in our own kiln at 920°C from a body of South-Iranian and Yazdi clays, in the Tabrizi tradition.

View Catalogue · Folio I
II · MMI

Stucco Muqarnas

Gach-bori & Muqarnas

Hand-carved gypsum stalactite vaults set on hexa-octa-rhombus projections drawn over the floor, in the manner of the Sheikh Lutfollāh inner chamber. Painted polychromy in lapis and gold leaf available on commission.

View Catalogue · Folio II
III · MMI

Architectural Counsel

Mashvarat-e Me'mārī

Consultancies on new programmes, dome geometry, and the ornamental programmes of new mosques and gathering halls. Drawings issued in both ink-on-vellum and surveyed digital form, with full schedule for craft subcontractors.

Enter the Portfolio

Twenty-eight commissions across nineteen years — mosques, madrasas, garden pavilions, and private dargāh — catalogued in full plate, elevation, and photograph.
