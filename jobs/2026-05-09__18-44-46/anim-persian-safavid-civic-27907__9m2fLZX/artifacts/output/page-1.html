Dīvān-i Istahan

Civic Register of the Naqsh-e Jahan

Issued by the noble custodians of the Maydán-i Naqsh-e Jahán, between the Qaysariyé Gate and the Mosque of Shaykh Lutfullāh, by office and seal of His Majesty Shah Abbas, the prince of believers, defender of the faith, may his rule endure for the welfare of the city and its lands.

This week's Register confirms the standing of public petitions, the price of bread and salt, the schedules of public hearings, and the works newly commissioned by the Royal Atelier. All persons of station shall consult these pages before approaching the Court.

▷ Issued at the Northern Arcade ◁

Notices of the Week

Three matters published this day for the city of Isfahan
From the Royal Atelier

Edict of His Majesty
Shah Abbas

Be it known to all merchants, caravan masters, and travellers passing through the Maydan that the new caravanserai at Mader-i Shah, attested by royal commission, shall open its gates to lodgers from the first day of Jamada al-Awwal next.

Tariffs upon the silk of Gilan, raw and woven, are by this same hand reduced by one part in ten, that the trade of His Majesty's domains may flourish.

▷ Read Royal Decree

Public Notice

Notice — Zayandeh-Rud
Canal Works

The Master of Waters announces that, beginning the fifth of this month, the dredging and re-lining of the canal of Zayandeh-Rud commences, with labours drawn from the wards of Gushwāhi and Dardasht.

Watering of orchards along the southern channel shall be permitted only between the second prayer and the call of sunset for the duration of forty days.

Any obstruction of the works, or diverting of water without leave, shall be answered before the Dīvānbegi.

— Mirzá Tāqi, Mīr-i Āb of Isfahan
