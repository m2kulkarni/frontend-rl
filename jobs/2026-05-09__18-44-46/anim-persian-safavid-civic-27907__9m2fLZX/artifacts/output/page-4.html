A Word of Thanks unto the Quarter of Jolfa

For the gift of the labour of forty hands, by the bond of the foreman of the Christian guild, by which the levee of the Maydan-i Shah is brought to standing, this Office offers public thanks; the seal of the Dīvānbegi shall affix to the lintel of their guild-hall as remembrance.
