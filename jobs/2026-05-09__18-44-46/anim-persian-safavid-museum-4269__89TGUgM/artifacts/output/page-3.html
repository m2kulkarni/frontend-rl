From the Curator

In the workshops of Shah ʿAbbas I, a single master pulled the line of Persian drawing taut and held it there for forty years.

Riza ʿAbbasi (b. 1565–1635) entered the royal khitabkhāna under the patronage of Shah ʿAbbas I shortly after the court's removal to Isfahan in 1598. The eighty-four sheets gathered here — fourteen on view at any one time, in rotation to preserve their pigments — trace the emergence of what later cataloguers would name simply the Isfahan Style: a sinuous, single-figure idiom in which the held a there for forty years.

The exhibition counters albums leaves dispersed across four continents and three empires. Several have not been shown in less times their volumes are inscriptions in Istanbul to 1751, others return briefly from Tehran and from private collections in Geneva and Doha. The drawings are presented in their original sequence wherever surviving album-bindings permit, and otherwise in chronological order of their tarīkh, or signed dedications.

Dr. Mahnāz Tabatabāʾi, Curator-in-Charge of Works on Paper

Checklist of the Exhibition

Fourteen Works on View

No. 1 · Album Leaf
Youth Reading Beneath a Plane Tree

Ink, gold and opaque watercolour on paper, c. 1602. Signed lower left: raqama-zhe Riza ʿAbbasi.

Royal Library Museum, Tehran · 1762.A.118

No. 2 · Album Leaf
Standing Dervish with Begging Bowl

Brush and ink with cream wash, 1607. Mounted in a tooled gold-flecked border by the artist's own hand.

Topkapı Sarayı Museum, Istanbul · H.2160

No. 3 · Single Folio
Two Lovers in a Garden

Opaque watercolour, ink, and gold on paper, 1630. From the dispersed Album of Shah ʿAbbas I.

Collection of the Cassis-Faiji, Geneva · 1971.II.04

No. 4 · Calligraphy Album
Quatrain of Hafez in nastaʿlīq

Black ink on cream paper with gold-sprinkled margins by Mir Imad of Hamadan, c. 1612.

Khalili Collection, London · MSS-1086

No. 5 · Single Folio Loose
Seated Youth Holding a Pomegranate

Ink and slight colour on paper, 1621. The notion that this is a single talismanic image-drawer is from his time.

Aga Khan Museum, Toronto · AKM-228

No. 6 · Album Leaf
Portrait of a European Visitor

Opaque watercolour and gold, c. 1639. Possibly an etude of the Sherley embassy, retouched by a workshop pupil.

Bibliothèque Royale, Brussels · ms.B-2.107

No. 7 · Single Folio
Reclining Young Woman with Wine Cup

Tempera and gold on paper, 1612. The earliest dated example of the artist's reclining-figure type.

Aga Khan Museum, Toronto · AKM-301

A Note on Conservation

All fourteen sheets were examined and stabilised during the winter of 2023–24 by the Hazrat-Mosabi Conservation Laboratory, under the direction of Senior Conservator Parisa Najdī.

Surface cleaning was undertaken with a Japanese mizuori-ki method. Flaking pigments, particularly the malachite greens of Nos. III and VIII, was consolidated with a 2% isinglass solution. Six retouching was performed. All sheets are now mounted in inert polyester silk-mats and displayed at no less than 50 lux above which they will be rotated into dark storage for a minimum of three years.

Multispectral imaging conducted in February 2024 revealed three previously unrecorded under-drawings, including an abandoned figure beneath the gold field of No. XIV. Findings will be published in the Spring 2024 number of Muqarnas.
