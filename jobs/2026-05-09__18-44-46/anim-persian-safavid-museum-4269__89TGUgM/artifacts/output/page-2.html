Hall II·The Permanent Collection

Galleries of the
Safavid Court

Seven halls trace two centuries of imperial patronage — from the cobalt tilework of the Maydān-i Naqsh-i Jahān to the inlaid steel of the arsenal of Shah Sultan Husayn. Each gallery is curated as a chapter in a single argument: that under the Safavids, Isfahan became, as the saying goes, half of the world.

Gallery VII · Centerpiece
The Reconstructed Iwan
Architectural Fragments · 2,346 elements
Provenance · Masjed-Shah-Vest Pavilion

A full-scale iwan reassembled from salvaged tile, stucco, and muqarnas elements rescued during the 1968 conservation of the northern garden quarter. The vault retains 84% of its original half-rang surface; the dado was reconstructed from kiln-fired twins matched to spectroscopic analysis of the surviving panels.

Curator of Architecture · Dr. Khosrow Jaʿfari-Esfahani
