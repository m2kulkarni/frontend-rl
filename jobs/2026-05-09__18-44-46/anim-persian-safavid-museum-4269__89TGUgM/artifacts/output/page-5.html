Scholarship
& the Royal Atelier Library

Beneath the Hall of Mirrors, in three vaulted basement chambers once given to the silver mint, lies a library and press founded in 1972 — the only collection in the country devoted exclusively to the patronage and arts of the Safavid age.

Resident Fellowships

Resident Fellowships

The Khwāja Khordad Fellowship in Manuscripts

A nine-month residency for a doctoral or post-doctoral scholar working on Persianate book arts of the period 1450–1750. Stipend of 380,000,000 IRR, with shared studio in Isfahan and access to the conservation atelier.

Annual · By Application

The Iwan Conservation Fellowship

A two-year practicum in Safavid architectural conservation, undertaken jointly with the Iranian Cultural Heritage Organization. Open to graduates of accredited conservation programmes worldwide.

Biennial · April Cycle

Patterns of Patronage

Annual symposium of the Library, in collaboration with the Sciences Pédagogie of Tehran.

7 — 9 November 2024 · Hall V

The 2024 colloquy returns to a recurrent question: what shape does patronage take when the patron is the state, and the artisan is at once a courtier and a craftsman? Twelve papers, four roundtables, and an evening recital of the shahnama in the gardens.
