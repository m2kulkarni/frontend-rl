OPERA SELECTA

Six works selected from the present period — three at full scale, three rendered at the proportions of a hand-cut tessera. Each represents a different problem set we ask ourselves to solve.

THERMAE LICINIAE

Aquileia · MMXVIII

A Trajanic bathing complex, replanned and rebuilt with a Vitruvian aedicula — the great hall reopened MMXX as a foreign-languages institute.

BUILT9 ROOMS1820 m²

DOMUS VENNIA

Roma · MMXX

The architect's own house, a private courtyard atrium house with a small library and a memorial to his teacher Berdini at the eastern bay.

BUILT4 BAYS340 m²

QUATTUOR TEMPORA

Lomelini coll. · MMXXIV

Threshold of the Genovese collector Lomelini's villa: a wheel of seasons in opus tessellatum, eight rays in stone and gilt smalti.

MOSAIC14000 TESS.2.4 m

BIBLIOTHECA AURELIA

Genova · MMXXII

A reading room for a small private collection of Latin texts, with a corinthian colonnade in pietra serena from the Apuan Alps.

BUILT1 NAVE210 m²

FORUM MERCATORUM

Aquileia · MMXIX

A small market square — flanked colonnades, a fountain at the central crossing, and a campanile carved with the names of all twenty-three guilds.

BUILT14 SHOPS2200 m²

CENOTAPHIUM MEMORIAE

Aquileia · MMXXIII

A travertine cenotaph honouring the city's stonecutters' guild — thirty-eight names cut by their own apprentices in the order of their reception.

BUILT5 TIERS4.6 m
