THE PĀLARU TRUST

A history of the institution that has founded, governed, and continues to maintain the Pālaru Museum since 11 April 1962.

REQUEST & FOUNDATION

The Pālaru Trust was constituted on 11 April 1962 under the Tamil Nadu Public Trust Act of 1959, on a request from the Director of the Department of Archaeology to the Government of Tamil Nadu, to support the conservation of the temple bronzes recovered from the Madurai Cōḷa floor of 1959. The Trust's first board was named on the same day, with Dr. T. V. Mahalingam as founding chairman.

A CHRONOLOGY OF THE INSTITUTION

  • 1962Trust founded; first acquisitions of forty‑two bronzes from the Madurai temple floor.
  • 1965The first stone gallery opens in the converted Mangalam‑pārkar house.
  • 1971Founding of the Pālaru Journal; first issue under the editorship of K. T. Sundaresan.
  • 1978Vidishā Research Fellowship endowed by the Krishna Foundation of Tanjavur.
  • 1989The bronze precinct is consolidated under purpose‑built lighting; reaches its present form.
  • 1996The pigment archive is formally constituted following the recovery of the Lepakshi ceiling‑cloths.
  • 2003Catalogue raisonné project begun under Shurajana Ramanarayan.
  • 2014The Reference Library expands into the south wing.
  • 2024Inauguration of the Gopuram Hall, with Sri R. Shantharaman as curator‑in‑residence.
THE DIRECTOR

The Director of the Pālaru Trust is appointed by the Board of Trustees for a non‑renewable term of seven years. The current Director is Dr. R. Shantharaman, who succeeded Shurajana Ramanarayan in 2024 after a long career in the Archaeological Survey of India.
