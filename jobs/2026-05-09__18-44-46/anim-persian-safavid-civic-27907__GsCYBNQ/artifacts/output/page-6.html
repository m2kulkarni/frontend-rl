By Whose Care

By Whose Care

The Master of the Outer Warden shall present petitioners at the registry. The Hall-clerk shall enter all visitors. Tea is offered to those bearing seal of writ.

Visitors are received within the courtyard.

Hands & Lessons

Hands & Lessons

Within these arcades you may find the public scribe of the Maydān, who shall write your letter or petition for a fixed fee; or the registry clerk, who shall help to find your decree among the entered rolls.

The Path Through the Maydan

For one approaching from the south.

  1. Enter the Maydān by the Qaysarīeh Gate, beneath the painted vaults.
  2. Pass the dome of the Mosque of Shaikh Luṭfullāh upon your left.
  3. Continue along the northern arcade, beneath the cool shade of the bāzār.
  4. You shall come upon a column of green tile, marked by a brass star.
  5. The Office of the Dīvānbegī is the third arch beyond the column.
  6. Enter through the courtyard. The registry stands beside the cypress.

Conduct Within the Courtyard

Customs to be observed by visitors.

Yes — Do

You May & Should

  • Remove your dust upon entering the inner courtyard.
  • Greet the warden with proper salutation: Salām ʿalaykum.
  • Bear silver and ensure your seal is upon your person.
  • Wait in the shade of the cypress until called by the clerk.
  • Bring tea to the elder visitors who have travelled far.
No — Refrain

You Must Not

  • Strangers, draw weapon nor speak loudly within the inner courtyard.
  • Smoke a pipe in the presence of the Dīvānbegī, nor in any room within the registry.
  • Approach the inner office without the warden's leave.
  • Disturb the work of the public scribe at his table.
  • Bring beasts of burden into the courtyard precincts.

By the hand of the Dīvānbegī
