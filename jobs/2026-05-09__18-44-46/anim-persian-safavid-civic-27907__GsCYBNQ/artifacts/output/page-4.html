From the Office of Public Works

A Word of Thanks unto the Quartier of Joulfā

The Dīvān records, with the gratitude of the Royal Crown, the diligence of all guilds and tradesmen lately engaged in the rebuilding of the Pul-i Khwājū. The masons, the carpenters, the brass-workers and the lampwrights of Joulfā have laboured not less than three full months without complaint, that the bridge be again opened to the people of the city. The Dīvānbegī here notes their names that this remembrance be entered into the rolls.
