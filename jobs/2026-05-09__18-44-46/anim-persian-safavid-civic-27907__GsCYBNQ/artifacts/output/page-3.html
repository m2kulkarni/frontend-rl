By the hand of the Muḥtasib of the Maydān · Tariffs are valid for the present month, save where revised by stamped supplemental.

Standards of Weight & Measure

Confirmed by the masters of the Royal Mint · Iṣfahān

Mann (of Tabrīz)3 kg, 6 oqia
Mann (of Iṣfahān)2 kg, 14 oqia
Sang9.6 mishkāl

The official mann, charīk, and mishkāl, kept beneath the dome of the Royal Mint, are the standard reference.

Discipline

Inspections of the Muḥtasib

By appointment of the Dīvānbegī, every fortnight

The Examined Muḥtasib, accompanied by two scribes and four members of the watch, shall pass through the arcades on the appointed days, weighing the merchants' weights against the standard true of the Office.
