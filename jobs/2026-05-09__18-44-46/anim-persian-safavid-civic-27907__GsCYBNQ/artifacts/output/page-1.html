Dīvān-i Isfahān

Civic Register of the Naqsh-e Jahān

Seated in the northern arcade of the Maydān-e Naqsh-e Jahān, between the Qaysarīeh Gate and the Mosque of Shaikh Luṭfullāh, the office and household of His Majesty Shāh ʿAbbās the Great, the prince of Ṣafavī sovereigns, keeps council and watch upon the welfare of the city and its commerce.

Notices of the Week

Three matters published this day for the city of Iṣfahān

From the Royal Court

Edict of His Majesty
Shāh ʿAbbās

Be it known to all merchants, caravan-masters, and travellers passing through the Maydān: that the new caravanserai at Madar-i Shāh, erected by royal command, shall open its gates to lodgers from the first of Jumādā al-Awwal next.

Tariffs upon the silk of Gīlān, raw and woven, are by the same hand reduced by one part in ten, that the trade of His Majesty's domains may flourish.

Read · Royal Seal

Public Notice

Notice — Zāyandeh-Rūd
Canal Works

The Master of Waters announces that, beginning the fifth of this month, the dredging and re-lining of the canal of Farahān shall commence, with labour drawn from the wards of Joulfā and Dardasht.

Watering of orchards along the southern channel shall be permitted only between the second praise and the call of sunset for the duration of forty days.

Any obstruction of the works, or diverting of waters without leave, shall be answered before the Dīvānbegī.

— Mīrzā Taqī, Mīr of Iṣfahān
