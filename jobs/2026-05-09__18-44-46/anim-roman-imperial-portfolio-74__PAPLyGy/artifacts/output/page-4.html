Lucius Vennius was born in Ravenna in the spring of 1976, four blocks from the Mausoleum of Galla Placidia, and grew up taking the long way to school in order to pass beneath its star-vault. He studied architecture at the Politecnico di Milano under Pier Carlo Bontempi and afterward took the two-year diploma at the Scuola di Restauro di Spoleto, where he learned to set tesserae by hand on the slow, brick-by-brick discipline that the workshops there preserve.

From 2007 to 2010 he apprenticed at Bottega Caraldi II Trastevere, working on the re-laying of the fourth-century floor at Santa Pudenziana and on three private commissions for villas in the Castelli Romani. The atelier was founded in MMXI as a deliberately small operation honouring the city's stonecutters' guild — completed and dedicated in October 2023.

The practice is small and deliberately so — Vennius, two associates, and a rotating bench of three or four mosaicists drawn from Spoleto, Aquileia, and Ravenna. Every commission, whatever a private chapel or a fifteen-meter quay, is drawn entirely by hand on the long oak table at the back of the studio. Nothing leaves the door without first being tested across all four scales.

Three instruments govern the studio. None of them is a screen. Each is older than the practice that uses it, and each is slower than the patron expects.

AXIAL PLANS, DRAWN BY HAND

Every project begins with a single ink drawing on Fabriano 220 g paper, drawn during the cards and observance of the morning sun. They serve as both tool and ledger.

FULL-SCALE MOSAIC CARTOONS

Each mosaic is first prepared 1:1 in charcoal on craft paper, paneled by the work-bench, before the bedding is laid. The cartoon is conserved in the archive.

PLASTER MODELS FOR VAULTS

Domes, groin vaults, and apsidal half-domes are first tested in 1:25 hydraulic plaster on a turned wooden formwork. The model is what travels to the patron's hand for review.

STRUCTURAL ENGINEER

MARCO FENOGLIO

ingegnere strutturista · Torino · b. 1971
▼

Trained at the Politecnico di Torino and previously of Favero & Milan, Marco has been the studio's structural consultant since 2013. He calculates the loads, the bonds, and the cuts; we propose the shape — and from time, on his confirmation, in time has saved us a small fortune in remediation.

MASTER MOSAICIST

GIULIA TREVI

capomosaicista · Trevi · b. 1981
▼

Third-generation tessera of the Trevi family of Trevi, Giulia leads the on-site crew on every Atelier project from foundation to glazing. She speaks five languages, drinks no coffee, and her crew of four cuts the cleanest tesserae we have seen in any studio south of Aquileia. She does not permit power tools above the wash table.
