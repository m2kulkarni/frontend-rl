Hall II · The Permanent Collection

Galleries of the
Safavid Court

Seven halls trace two centuries of imperial patronage — from the cobalt tilework of the Maydān-i Naqsh-i Jahan to the inlaid steel of the arsenal of Shah Sultan Husayn. Each gallery is curated as a chapter in a single argument: that under the Safavids, Isfahan became, as the saying goes, half of the world.

Gallery VII · Architecture

The Reconstructed Iwan

Architectural Fragments
2,346 elements
Origin
1602–1611
Provenance
Masjid-i Shaykh Lutfallah

A full-scale iwan reassembled from salvaged tile, stucco, and muqarnas elements rescued during the 1968 conservation of the northern garden quarter. The vault retains 84% of its original haft-rang surface; the dado was reconstructed from kiln-fired twins matched to spectroscopic analysis of the surviving panels.

— Curator of Architecture —

Dr. Khosrow Jaʿfari-Esfahani
