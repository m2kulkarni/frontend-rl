CAMPUS & VISITING 校舎と東山のしおり

Four buildings,
one quiet block
of Higashiyama.

The Hokurin campus occupies a single deep machiya block of Sanjō-dōri, between the Higashiyama Gate and the residential lanes of Yamashina. The university is closed to drop-in visitors but welcomes scheduled tours on the first Saturday of each month.

The four buildings

— MAIN HALL · EAST KILN · SOUTH ANNEX · TŌRIN BUNKO
— BUILDING I · 本館

Main Hall (1782)

The original silk-merchant compound. Houses the rector's office, the Faculty of Humanities, and the high reading room above the entrance kura.

— BUILDING II · 東窯

East Kiln Court

Converted in 1989 from the merchant's drying yard. Now the lacquer, dye, and paper laboratories of the Faculty of Craft Sciences.

— BUILDING III · 南別館

South Annex

A reinforced-timber addition along the back wall of the property — Faculty of Environmental Studies offices, the river lab, and visiting-scholar rooms.

— BUILDING IV · 東林文庫

Tōrin Bunko

The library proper: a small stand-alone kura at the rear of the courtyard, holding the Hachiya bequest and the working manuscript collection.

Walking directions

— TWO ROUTES BY FOOT
Higashiyama Stn.
北林大学
— ROUTE A · 12 MIN.

From Higashiyama Station

Take exit 2 of the Tōzai line, walk south along Higashioji-dōri, then turn east at the Sanjō intersection. The Sanjō Gate stands at the foot of the third lane on the right.

— ROUTE B · 18 MIN.

From Sanjō Keihan

Cross the Kamogawa via Sanjō Bridge and walk uphill on Sanjō-dōri. The university is on the south side of the street, marked by an unpainted wooden gate.

— ROUTE C · BY BUS

By city bus

Routes 5, 32 and 100 stop at the Higashiyama-Sanjō stop, three minutes' walk from the gate. Bus schedules are posted weekly on the gate notice board.

Open-campus days

— TWICE A YEAR ・ NO REGISTRATION REQUIRED

Twice a year the campus opens its lawn courtyards, conservation studios, and rare-book vault to the public. No registration is required. In exploration is required, all printed walking guides in Japanese, English, and French are offered at the Hokurin genkan.
