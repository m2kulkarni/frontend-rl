The Royal Atelier Library

A WORKING LIBRARY

The Atelier Library serves as the principal scholarly resource on the Safavid age in Isfahan. Its holdings include the personal papers of the late Shahnaz Khosravi (Director, 1989–2002), and a near-complete run of the Bulletin of the Royal Atelier from 1907.

Open to all credentialed scholars, with Tuesday — Friday access from 10:00 to 17:00. Reading-room desks may be reserved by writing to library@museumofisfahan.ir not less than seven days before your intended visit.

Resident Fellowships

THE NIROMAND FELLOWSHIP IN ARCHIVAL CONSERVATION

A two-year residency for an early-career specialist in paper- and pigment-conservation, with stipend and quarters within the museum compound. Applications close 15 January annually.

2025 cycle now closed

THE CHEHEL SOTOUN FELLOWSHIP IN VISUAL CULTURES

A nine-month residency for a graduate scholar working on any aspect of Safavid Isfahan, with library privileges and a writing studio overlooking the Chehel Sotoun gardens.

Applications open 1 March 2025
