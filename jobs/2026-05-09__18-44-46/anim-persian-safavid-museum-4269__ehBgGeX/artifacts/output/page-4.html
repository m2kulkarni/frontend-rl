VISITOR COUNSEL

To honour the fragility of paper and ink, our galleries are kept at lower light. Allow your eye to settle for a quarter of an hour upon arrival; the rooms reward unhurried looking.

Cloakrooms accept any bag larger than 30 cm. Photography is permitted without flash; tripods are not allowed.

APPROACHES BY ROAD & RAIL

BY AIR
Isfahan airport (IFN) is 25 minutes by taxi via the Hesaa Bypass; international visitors most often arrive via Imam Khomeini (IKA), 480 km north.
BY RAIL
RAJA Trains depart Tehran daily; the Khwajou-Islamabad service takes 8 hours.
BY METRO
Naqsh-e Jahan station (Line 1); a five-minute walk south.
BY FOOT
Chahar Bagh runs as a long pedestrian boulevard from the river to the Maidan; the museum is mid-way.

Access, Groups & Schools

ACCESSIBILITY

The Lotus Gallery (level II), entirely level, is accessible by ramp from the carriage-court; a single roller-chair is held at the desk of Hall III for visitors who would like to use one. Headsets are available in Persian, English, and French.

GROUP BOOKINGS

Groups of seven or more (school, faculty, study-circle, civic) are received by appointment. Please write our office no fewer than two weeks before your visit; we will supply a docent at the rate of 1.5 m IRR.

SCHOOLS & YOUTH

Schoolgroups of any age are admitted at the children's rate. The atelier hosts free Saturday workshops in tile-painting and qalam-cutting; teachers may request a syllabus by writing to studies@museumofisfahan.ir.
