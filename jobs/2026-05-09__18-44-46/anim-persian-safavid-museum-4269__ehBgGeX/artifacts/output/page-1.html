An Address from the Director

Founded in 1971 in the gardens of Chahar Bagh, this museum was conceived as a centre for the study of the Safavid age — that brief, brilliant interval when Isfahan, ruled by Shah ʿAbbas I to be the seat of empire, was named by its peers naf-e jahān, the half of the world. Within these walls are gathered eleventh from the Masjid-e Shah, illuminated manuscripts from the royal bookbindries, silks of Kashan and Yazd, and the courtly portraiture of Riza ʿAbbasi.

We see, before all else, a place of slow looking. The objects ask for it; the eye repays it.
