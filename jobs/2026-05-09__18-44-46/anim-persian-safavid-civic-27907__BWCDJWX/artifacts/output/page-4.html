FOLIO IV · WORKS OF THE REALM

Hereunder are recorded the labours undertaken by command of the Dīvānbegi for the maintenance of His Majesty's capital — the watercourses, the spans of stone, and the lodgings for travellers — together with the names of those master builders to whom each charge is entrusted, and the quarter (mahalle) answerable in attendance.

Works Now in Hand

Three undertakings, by the embankment of the City

Work · The Qanats

Dredging of the Made-ye Nāzarīn

The dredging of the upper aqueduct between the orchards of Hesarābād and the dīvan of the Chahar Bagh is to be removed, sluice-gates of cedar shall be replaced and the brick-lining renewed where the spring-flood last winter pierced the bank.

MASTER BUILDER: Ustād Mīrzayī (the Younger) of the city.
QUARTER ANSWERABLE: Mahalle-ye Hesarābād.
WORKMEN ENGAGED: Forty two diggers, eight masters.
ALLOCATED FROM TREASURY: Two hundred and twenty tomans.
EXPECTED COMPLETION: By the new moon of Jumada al-Awwal, 1037 A.H.

In Progress · Second Month
Work · The Bridges

Repair of the Allahverdī Khan Bridge

The parapets of the Pol-e jadid, worn by the bearing of foot-travellers and the winter rains, are to be re-set in dressed stone from the quarry at Najafābād. Four niches on the eastern flank receive new fascia-stones uncovered.

MASTER BUILDER: Ustād Bāqer Mohammad of Tabriz, mason expert.
QUARTER ANSWERABLE: Mahalle-ye Jolfa, with the levy of the Armenian congregation.
WORKMEN ENGAGED: Sixty hewers, four mortar-mixers.
ALLOCATED FROM TREASURY: One hundred and ninety tomans.
EXPECTED COMPLETION: The 4th of Bahmān, count of full.

Bridge Closed at Dusk · Foot Passage by North Aisle
Work · The Gates

New Gate of the Caravanserai at Darb-e Kushk

A vaulted brain-gate of fired brick, faced with cobalt and turquoise tile in the manner of Mashhad, is to be raised at the eastern entry of the caravanserai newly endowed by His Majesty the silk-master.

MASTER BUILDER: Ustād Hossein Tabrizī, tile-master.
QUARTER ANSWERABLE: Mahalle-ye Darb-e Kushk.
WORKMEN ENGAGED: Sixteen tile-setters, six brick-layers.
ALLOCATED FROM TREASURY: Two hundred and ten tomans, ten dīkīm.
EXPECTED COMPLETION: Within ninety days, by the order of mort.

Foundation Laid · Vaulting to Begin

A Word of Thanks unto the Quarter of Jolfa

From the residents of the Quarter of Jolfa

The Office wishes, with the gratitude of the city and the Master of His Majesty, all sights of every man whose congregation of Jolfa for the labour over the past months — from the children's offerings of bread for the workmen's tablets, to the women's tireless mortars on the brick-lining of the Allahverdi Khan bridge — they have proven a workmanship the labour of their citizens ten times over. May Pul-e jadid stand as a testimony to the seventy-fifth Karbala of New Jolfa, and may all the Christ-loving Armenians of the realm see in this generous spirit and a warmth to the cold of the Vaults, by way of the Master of the Royal Diwan.
