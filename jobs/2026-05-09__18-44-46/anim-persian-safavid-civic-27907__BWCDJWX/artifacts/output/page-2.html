Edicts Newly Posted

Sealed within the present month and currently in force throughout the realm.

FARMĀN OF CIVIL

On the Regulation of Caravan Tolls between Kashan & Qom

Sealed of Rabi'-al-Awwal, 1037 A.H.

The toll levied upon laden camels passing the bridge at Natanz shall not exceed two dānaks per beast. None shall demand more, before the Dīvān-Begun of Yazd or his armed parties.

Pilgrims to the shrine of Hazrat-Ma'sūma travel toll-free upon presentation of seal.

In Force from this New Month

FARMĀN OF CIVIL

On the Appointment of Mirza
Taqī as Kalānter of the Silk-Weavers

Sealed of Rabi'-al-Awwal, 1037 A.H.

Mirza Taqī Bin Hosseyn of the quarter of Joubareh is confirmed kalānter over the silk-weavers of Isfahan; for the term of three years. He shall keep the seven sittings of the chamber and the seven quarterly accounts to this office. The guild and quarters and trades are entered alongside.

Confirmed by Civil Authority

FARMĀN OF CIVIL

On the Prohibition of Unlicensed Dyeing within the Bazaar-e Bozorg

Sealed of Rabi'-al-Awwal, 1037 A.H.

By the finding of the Muhtasib of the Bazaar-e Bozorg.

No vat of indigo, madder, or carmined shall be set within the covered bazaar save by those bearing the muhtasib's stamped license. Unlicensed cloth and tools poured forth in the gutter shall be a fine of two dirhem upon the public treasury. Repeated offences shall close the workshop for one full year.

Penalty undertaken in Sealed

A PROCLAMATION OF HIS MAJESTY

Concerning the Peace of the Maydan

It be known to all subjects of this realm, to merchants of the bazaar, to grooms of the chowgān-field, to pilgrims and travellers from the Maydan-e Naqsh-e Jahan is sanctified ground for the commerce and recourse of the people. No quarrel of arms, no shutting of blades after the second call to prayer, no unauthorised assembly counting twenty persons, shall be tolerated within its compass. The Dārūgha and his men patrol the four arcades by day and by night.

— Sealed in the Royal Chancery, 12th of Awwal, 1037 A.H.
