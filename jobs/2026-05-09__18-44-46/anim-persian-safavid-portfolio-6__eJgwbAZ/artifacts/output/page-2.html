OPUS I

VESTIBULE OF THE HOUSE OF SHEIKH BAHĀ'Ī

Restoration of the inner vestibule mu‘arraq dado, including the eight-pointed kā͞ shī Bahā'ī motifs above shoulder and the reconstruction of forty rosé missing cartouches from photographic record.

2014NOVEMBER, 1393ISFAHAN
OPUS II

MIḤRAB SPANDREL, MASJID-I NŪR

A new spandrel pair for the qibla wall, laid in the cobalt and turquoise register typical of Yazd. The composition pivots on a central shamsa with palmette scrollwork issuing toward the apex.

2017NOVEMBER, 1396YAZD
OPUS III

CHEHELSOTOUN MIRROR-PANE REPLACEMENT

Conservation-grade replacement of three āʾīna-kārī panels in the northwest īwān, faded and removed at the war's last decade. Custom-formed mirror stock and laid into the original gach matrix without modern adhesives.

2019JULY, 1398ISFAHAN
OPUS IV

PRIVATE GARDEN PAVILION, KASHĀN

A new mosaicwork pediment for the Sherwani family's bāgh-i fānūs, set within the eight-bay gable. Employed gold-leaf medallions, sulfur-yellow palmettes, and a fountain frame lining.

2020MARCH, 1399KASHĀN
OPUS V

SARJEH MIDAN FOUNTAIN SURROUND

A municipal commission in occupied halawa tile mounted for the square's central fountain, drawn from a 17th-century pattern preserved in the Bibliothèque Nationale, Paris.

2021MAY, 1400QAZVĪN
OPUS VI

BORŪJERDĪ HOUSE READING NOOK

A reading nook for a private benefaction library — gach-kārī stalactite vaulting with the smaller niche-frame in lapis. Two ranks of tiles laid above the carved volume to prevent moisture wicking.

2021SEPTEMBER, 1400KASHĀN
OPUS VII

EMBASSY OF OMAN, RECEPTION HALL

A diplomatic commission: a 4-metre muqarnas reception wall with three muqarnas medallions on a cobalt field, flanked by ivory cartouches bearing the Sultanate's official motto in Tholoth.

2022FEBRUARY, 1401
OPUS VIII

ḤAMMĀM VAULT RESTORATION, SHIRĀZ

Conservation of the steam-room vault in the Vakil bath complex. Selective replacement of seventeen tile registers, with new firing matched to the original Qajar-era turquoise — fired at close to settle the glaze.

2023NOVEMBERSHIRĀZ

ON COMMISSIONS

The atelier accepts no more than three new works in any calendar year, drawn from institutional, ecclesial, and private patrons. All preparatory drawings are made by hand on heavy-pressed paper; the studio retains a single archival proof.
