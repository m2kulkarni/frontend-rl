✦   THE COMMISSIONING LEDGER   ✦

ON RECEIVING A COMMISSION

A NOTE ON SCOPE,
TIME, & WHAT PASSES BETWEEN US.

The atelier accepts a small number of architectural and devotional commissions each year. Each project begins with a written brief, a site visit where possible, and the preparation of a one-fifth scale current together with material samples drawn from our own kilns at Na'in and Yazd. Nothing is fired until the cartoon has been signed.

I   ·   MINIMUM SCOPE

4 M² OF TILEWORK

Below this, the geometric cannot resolve into a complete cartouche; smaller pieces are accepted only as gifts.

III   ·   DRAWINGS & SAMPLES

PREPARED ON AGREEMENT

A binding agreement, and a deposit of 30%, accompanies the issue of cartoons; samples are kept in the studio archive.

II   ·   LEAD-TIME

6 — 14 MONTHS

From signed cartoon to installation, depending on glaze recipes, kiln cycle, and the season hereafter.

IV   ·   INSTALLATION

BY THE ATELIER ITSELF

The master and one senior workman travel to site for setting; lime-mortar is mixed on the premises.

I

LETTER, BRIEF

A written description of the surface, light, and intent. Photographs and elevations are welcomed.

II

CARTOON

A 1 : 5 ink drawing on paper, with a small panel of fired samples in the proposed palette.

III

CUTTING & FIRING

The tile by hand from monochrome slabs, a single nine-tile firing, then gold-into-glaze where called for.

IV

SETTING

On site assembly, lime-mortar on paper templates, set in lime-fast paper, washed off after curing.

"The hand that cuts the tile remembers, the wall that receives it remembers longer."
