Sketches set down here are the works ordered to be undertaken in the dominions of the Master's spade — the watercourse, the stones of the road, the brick of the public bath, the bridge of the southern way, the timber-of the lodging, with subscript figures and the call of the master-of-works for the season.

Works Now in Hand

Order of construction by the Office of Public Works
Folio Iv · Ongoing

Dredging of the Khaju in Maqsoud

The Master of Waters reports that the four-and-eighty span of the Khaju are silted to one fifth their depth, the reach of the bridge to be cleansed and the cracking of the bridgework set in.

Authority

Master of Waters

Funded by

Royal treasury, half-share

Term

Forty days

Folio V · Surveyed

Repair of the Wall around the Bazaar

The corners of the western wall, weakened by the winter melt, are surveyed by the master-mason. Brick of the Halab kind shall be procured from the Bazaar of Khwajeh Habib for the works of refinishing.

Authority

Master Mason

Funded by

Bazaaris of Bozorg

Term

Sixty days

Folio VI · Approved

New Gate of the Caravanserai of Qadan-Saráy

A great hinged outwork to be set, the timber to be of cypress and ironwork inlaid; the carving of the panels by Mírzá Habib of the woodcarvers and inlaid by his son.

Authority

Master Carpenter

Funded by

Royal hand

Term

Ninety days

A Word of Thanks unto the Quarter of Jolfa

By the merchants and silversmiths of New Jolfa

Of fifteen toman, with the goodly will of our friend Khwaja Petros of Jolfa, the goodly silversmith and merchant of the Mander-Mohrazadeh house, are presented for the rebuilding of the southern wall of our Khanqah of the Sheikh-i Kabír. The masters and apprentices have set down to the labour with the gladness of those whose hand is held by their own ward, and the work shall be completed in this year. May our most blessed Lord, the Refuge of all the world, be pleased with this token of his subjects' devotion.
