Modes & Liveries

Mounts & Litters

Mounts may be left at the caravanserai of Saru-Taqí; litters and palanquins set down at the second courtyard.

Two grooms of the office hold the bridles in the watch.

The Path Through the Maydan

Step by Step
  1. i. Enter by the great gate of the Maydán; the Qaysariyeh stands at your left hand.
  2. ii. Pass beneath the second arcade, then follow the colonnade rightward.
  3. iii. Three doors past the salt-sellers, you will see the door of cypress.
  4. iv. Above the lintel, the lion-and-sun in beaten brass.
  5. v. Knock once for the watch and the seal-keeper attends.
  6. vi. Within, the outer courtyard is paved in marble of two stones.
Plan of the Maydan

Conduct Within the Courtyard

Your Person & Attire

· Cover the head with a turban or seemly hat upon all entry.

· Remove footwear of the road in the outer alcove.

· No edged blade, save the small knife of the table, may be carried within.

· Speak with the soft voice of the petitioner — neither cry, nor any oath of war.

· Bow to the seat of the Divánbegí before any address.

The Conduct of Address

· Address the recorder before the Divánbegí; await the call of your name.

· Speak in proper turn; the second to begin not until the first has had his seal.

· Salt and water shall be set in the outer chamber, that you take refresh.

· Should you object to a witness, do so at the first lifting of the matter.

· The decision being rendered, withdraw with the seal of the recorder.

By the hand of the recorder of His Majesty the Refuge of the World; and the seal of the Divánbegí, that the city's affairs continue in the order which is loved.
