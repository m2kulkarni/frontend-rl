Edicts Newly Posted

Folio I · Decrees

On the Regulation of Caravan Tolls between Kashan & Qum

By the hand of Mírzá Salman

The toll of kafsh upon laden beasts entering by the southern gate is by this firmán reduced to four dirhams the head, the tax of weighing being included.

Wardens of the road shall mark in their book each entry; falsification is answerable before the Divánbegi himself.

Folio II · Provisions

On the Appointment of Mírzá Taqí as Kalantar of the Ulu-Maydan

By the hand of Hasan Beg

Mírzá Taqí, lately of the quarter of Jolfa, is named Kalantar over the merchants of the lower Maydan, on account of his careful diction and discernment in the weights and measures of grain and silver.

His salary is appointed at five toman the month, payable on the new moon.

Folio III · Sentences

On the Prohibition of Unlicensed Dyeing within the Bazaar-e Bozorg

By the hand of Sayyid Ali Riza

It is forbidden, on pain of a tenfold fine and confiscation of stocks, that any dyer set up his vat or boil his pot save by leave of the Mu'allim of the dyers.

Any cloth so coloured shall be cut from the loom and the proceeds added to the alms of the city.

A Notice from the Mír-Shab

Concerning the Peace of the Maydan

B

By order of His Majesty Shah Abbas, no man bearing arm or unsheathed blade shall pass within the great gates of the Maydan from the call of asr to the call of fajr. The watchmen of the courtyard, set by the Kalantar, shall make the rounds at every change of the watch, and shall confiscate without redress any blade so found. Likewise no fire shall be lit in the open in the courtyard, save in the brazier of the night-watch, who keep the lamps of the four corners.

— By the hand of Mírzá Habíb, Mír-Shab of Isfahan
