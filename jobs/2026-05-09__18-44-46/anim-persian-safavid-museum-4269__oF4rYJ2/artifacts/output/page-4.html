A Counsel for the Visitor

There is no fast way through the seven halls. The collection rewards an hour for each room, and a return visit for the courtly drawings. Photography without flash is permitted in all halls but the manuscript hall, where book light alone makes its case for stewardship.

A small café in the courtyard serves the museum's own seasonal infusion until 16:00.

Access, Groups & Schools

Accessibility

The Royal Library is wheelchair-accessible from a ramped seventh-side entrance. Lifts serve all halls; large-print labels are available at the entrance desk; and the inner courtyard is paved.

An induction loop is fitted at the audio-counter.

Group Bookings

Groups larger than ten are asked to telegraph the office a fortnight in advance; the gallery is gentler and the conservation breath shorter than at the height of high season. A group rate of 230,000 IRR per visitor obtains.

A guide is provided in Persian, English, French.

Schools & Families

All schools enter free of charge with a pre-arranged itinerary; a specialist programme of three sessions runs each Saturday in the term, and the family programme on Friday afternoons offers a shorter, narrative tour for visitors aged eight upward.

Sign-up at the entrance desk.
