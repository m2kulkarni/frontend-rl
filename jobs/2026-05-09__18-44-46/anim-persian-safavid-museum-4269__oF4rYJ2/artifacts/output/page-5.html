The Royal Atelier Library

The Royal Atelier Library

A Standing Library

A working scholar's library of twelve thousand volumes in Persian, Arabic, French, English, and German, on the arts and architecture of the Safavid age. The library is open to scholars by appointment.

1,800

Access & Appointments

Reading-room consultation is by appointment. Manuscripts may be consulted only by writ of the Director, with a fortnight's notice and a counter-letter from the home institution.

Resident Fellowships

The Atelier Fellowship in Painting Studies

A six-month residency for one scholar working in the field of late Safavid drawing or painting. The fellow is given a desk in the Royal Library, a stipend, and a small budget for travel within Iran.

APPLICATIONS · 1 January each year

The Tilework Fellowship in Architectural History

A four-month residency for one scholar working on the architecture of seventeenth-century Iran. The fellow is provided with access to the conservation studio and a small allowance.

APPLICATIONS · 1 May each year
