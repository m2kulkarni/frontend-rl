CATALOGUE WORKS IN ORDER

EIGHT FULLY EXECUTED · TWO IN PROGRESS · SIX IN COUNSEL

PLATE I · 1389 H.S.

VESTIBULE OF THE HOUSE OF AHMAD RAJABI

Restoration of the inner vestibule's 14-cubit cobalt field with its turquoise muqarnas-tympanum, completed for the heir-occupant.

PLATE II · 1391 H.S.

NAQSH-E SOREYYA, MASJID-E ŠAH

A new Sūrat al-Mulk inscription panel set in cobalt and turquoise with palmette stops, executed in cut-tile mosaic.

PLATE III · 1393 H.S.

CHAHĀR-ŌĀRD MIHRĀB PĀNJ-ARGH ŪMAYT

A five-petal mihrāb commission. The orange peristyle band is woven from saffron-dyed glass tessera over polychrome.

PLATE IV · 1394 H.S.

PRIVATE GARDEN PAVILION, KASHAN

A new pleasure pavilion by the Bāgh-e Fīn, with the seven-color hashtbehesht arrangement on inset niches.

PLATE V · 1396 H.S.

SARGAH MUSAVI FOUNTAIN SURROUNDS

An intimate eight-pointed star fountain with hexagonal turquoise field — set in fired marble and gold leaf.

PLATE VI · 1397 H.S.

ROWZAT-KHĀNI READING ROOM

The renovation of a small reading room — a Qajar-period cartouche frame around the upper register.

PLATE VII · 1399 H.S.

EMBASSY OF OMAN RECEPTION HALL

A diplomatic order, executed in a contemporary palette of greens and turquoise paired with saffron tones.

PLATE VIII · 1402 H.S.

JOUDABAR VICE RESTORATION, ŠIRĀZ

An armature of the existing late-Safavid bandstand around the spandrel-bands at the upper register.

◆   ON COMMISSIONS   ◆

The atelier accepts no more than three new orders in any calendar year, drawn from monumental architecture commissions and private commissions of equal stature. All proposals are vetted by hand in the dossier-room of the studio in 30-day rounds.
