WHAT THE ATELIER UNDERTAKES

The workshop holds to three lineages of inherited ornament, taught hand to hand from the masters of the Chahār-bāgh and practiced today in monuments, prayer halls, and private commissions across Iran and the Persian-speaking dispersion.

I   ·   KĀSHĪ

TILE MOSAIC

Cut tile mosaic in the soluble turquoise, gold rosette, and Qājār banding. Famous fired in our own kiln to 990°C from a body of Yazd quartz and Tuscan feldspar.

FROM 1.0 m² · TO 14 m²
II   ·   GUCH

STUCCO MUQARNAS

Hand-carved gypsum stalactite vaults set on lime-and-straw plaster grids on the floor, the manner of the Sheikh Lotfollah dome chamber. Petrol- polychromy in lapis and gold leaf available on commission.

FROM 1.5 m² · TO 8 m²
III   ·   ʿIMĀRAT

ARCHITECTURAL COUNSEL

Consultation on new proportion, dome geometry, and the ceremonial programme of new mosques and gathering halls. Drawings issued in both ink-on-vellum and surveyed digital form, with full schedule for crafts subcontractors.

CONSULTANCY · BY DOSSIER

ENTER THE PORTFOLIO

Twenty-eight commissions across nineteen years — mosques, madrasas, garden pavilions, and private dargāhs — catalogued in full plate, elevation, and photograph.
