THE COMMISSIONING LEDGER

ON RECEIVING A COMMISSION

A NOTE ON SCOPE,
TIME, & WHAT PASSES BETWEEN US.

The atelier accepts a small number of architectural and devotional commissions each year. Each project begins with a written brief, a site visit where possible, and the preparation of a one-fifth scale cartoon together with material samples drawn from our own kilns at Na'in and Yazd. Nothing is fired until the cartoon has been signed.

I   MINIMUM SCOPE

4 M² OF TILEWORK

Below this, the geometric cannot resolve into a complete narrative; smaller objects are accepted only by exception.

III   CRAFTING A SAMPLE

PREPARED ON AGREEMENT

A binding agreement, and a deposit of 30% accompanies the issue of cartoons; samples are kept in the studio archive.

II   LEAD TIME

6 — 14 MONTHS

From signed cartoon to installation, depending on plate scope, kiln cycle, and the season's humidity.

IV   INSTALLATION

BY THE ATELIER ITSELF

The master and one senior workman travel to the site for setting. Our manner is stand-by-stand to the project.

I

CASTING, BRIEF

A written description of the surface, light, and intent. Photographs and elevations are welcomed.

II

CARTOON

A 1:5 ink drawing on paper, with a small panel of hand samples in the proposed palette.

III

CUTTING & FIRING

The tile, by hand from manufactured slabs, a single cobalt fining, then gold ones gloss when called for.

IV

SETTING

On site assembly, fine details on paper templates, on-site bone tool, paper washed off after curing.
