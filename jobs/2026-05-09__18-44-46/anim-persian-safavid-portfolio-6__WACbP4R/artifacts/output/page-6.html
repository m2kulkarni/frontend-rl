◆ THE COMMISSIONING OFFICE ◆

ON RECEIVING A COMMISSION

◆ A NOTE ON

SCOPE, TIME, & WHAT PASSES BETWEEN US.

The atelier accepts a small number of architectural and devotional commissions each year. Each project begins with a written brief, a site visit where possible, and the preparation of a one-fifth scale cartoon together with material samples drawn from our own kilns at Na'in and Yazd. Nothing is fired until the cartoon has been signed.

◆ MINIMUM SCOPE

4 M² OF TILEWORK

Below this, the geometric cartoon resolves into a complete cartouche; smaller objects are accepted only as part of a larger commission, regretting on glaze.recipe, kiln-cycle, and the master humidity.

◆ CARTOONS & SAMPLES

PREPARED ON AGREEMENT

A binding agreement and a deposit of 30% accompanies the issue of cartoons; samples are kept in the studio archive.

◆ LEAD TIME

6 — 14 MONTHS

From signed cartoon to installation, depending on glaze recipe, kiln cycle, and the season humidity.

◆ INSTALLATION

BY THE ATELIER ITSELF

The master and one senior workman travel to site for setting; their manner is stated to the patron.

I

LETTER & BRIEF

A written description of the surface, light, and intent. Photographs and elevations are welcomed.
II

CARTOON

A 1:5 ink drawing on paper, with a small panel of fired samples in the proposed palette.
III

CUTTING & FIRING

Tiles cut by hand from manufactured slabs; a single cobalt firing, then gold once-glaze where called for.
IV

SETTING

On site assembly, face-down on paper templates, set in lime; the paper washed off after curing.
