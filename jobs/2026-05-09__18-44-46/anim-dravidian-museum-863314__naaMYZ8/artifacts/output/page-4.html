HOURS · ADMISSION · TOURS · ACCESS

The museum accepts the revised Drupadan garrison house on the eastern bank of the Vadavāru, a short walk from the Brihadishvara temple complex. All ten galleries, the Gopuram Hall, the sculpture courtyard and the Dr. R. Ramaswamy Reference Library are open to the public on a single ticket. We welcome scholars, students, pilgrims, and the merely curious.

CARE & HANDLING

PHOTOGRAPHY

Hand-held photography is permitted in all permanent galleries without flash and without tripod. The reading wall in the platform-room is reserved for the silent observation of the Reference Library on the lower floor.

ON THE CALENDAR

IN THE CALENDAR

Visitors are asked to speak softly. Many objects on display were once daily ritual implements; the museum welcomes prayer or silence before any image. We respectfully ask that no food or drink be carried into the galleries.

THE REFERENCE LIBRARY

DR. R. RAMASWAMY WING · ESTABLISHED 1971

The on-site Reference Library holds approximately 18,400 volumes on South Indian architecture, epigraphy, and ritual practice; together with the museum's run of the journal of the Pālāru Trust (1962 — present), and the unpublished field notebooks of Adigarh K. Subradunyey 4-Iru concerning the Tanjavur Big Temple restoration of 1958.

Holdings include first editions of Gnanou Diehard's Archaeologie du Sud-Pays (Paris, 1934); Ananjamiyalam's Bronzes of Indian and Indonesian Art, and a near complete set of the South Indian Inscriptions series. A new collection of more than 800 colour photographs of the Vijayanagara complex was donated to the Trust by the family of Dr. K. Bhandary.

The library is open to readers holding a valid scholar's pass. No material accession, photocopying is restricted; digital reproductions are obtained at reasonable fees.
