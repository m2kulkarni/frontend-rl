Counsel & Provisions

Access, Groups & Schools

Accessibility

The Iwan Gallery, friend's commenced & all accessible halls have step-free routes; portable stools are available on request. The Reconstructed Iwan vault is accessible by lift.

Group Bookings

Groups of fifteen or more may be arranged through the Office of Visitor Services. The fee for guided tours is included with admission, but the Iwan Gallery and timed entry to the Reconstructed Iwan must be reserved.

Schools & Children

Children under fourteen enter free, accompanied. The 'Carpet of Letters' workshop is held each Friday morning during term: a small course in the Persian alphabet, with brushes and rosewater inks. Booking required.
