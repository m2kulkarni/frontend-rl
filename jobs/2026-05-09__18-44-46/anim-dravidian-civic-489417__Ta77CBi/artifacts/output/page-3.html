Heritage Precinct & Conservation

The Sannidhi precinct of Thiruvanam — including the four māda streets, the temple tank, the Naachiyar shrine, and the seven outer mandapams — is constituted a Heritage Field under §61 of the AGRAHARAM Act and the Tamil Nadu Antiquities (Protection & Conservation) Order of 1962.

Sannidhi Processional Route & the Mandapa Market

The four māda streets — Mela, Theer, Vaduga, Theekkanal — together circumscribe a perimeter of 1 mile 3 furlongs of granite paving, repaved 1812 under Serfoji II, and inscribed with eight donor pillars from the Vijayanagara period. The route is reserved for processions on the four uchavam festivals and is otherwise pedestrianised between dusk and 06:00.

  • Granite paving repaved in 1812 — c. 5,800 sq m
  • 32 inscribed pillar bases · Schedule A-1 to A-32
  • Old market mandapa, c. 1620, restored 1996

Custody, Use & Custodial Limits

The Sannidhi precinct is administered jointly by the Authority and the Sannidhi parisheka trust under the M.O.U. of 1972. The Authority is custodian of the streets, the tank, the lighting, and the perimeter wall; the trust is custodian of the shrines.

THE EPIGRAPHIC RECORD
EPIGRAPH SURVEY · 2024EXCERPT  ¶ 03

Of stones that remember the village's earliest prayers.

Of the 221 protected inscriptions of the precinct, 117 remain legible to the unaided eye; a further 64 require the use of raking light or impressioning to be read. The Authority has commissioned a complete digital impression of all inscriptions, in coordination with the Tamil University at Thanjavur, against the loss that comes with weathering and the dispersal of skilled epigraphers.

Of these, 31 record gifts of land, 28 of grain or oil for the temple lamps, 14 of rights of cultivation, and 7 record settlements of dispute by which the assembly of the village — the sabha — bound itself.
