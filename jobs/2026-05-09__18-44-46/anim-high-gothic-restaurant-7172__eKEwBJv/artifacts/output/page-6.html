— MARDI À SAMEDI —

The refectory welcomes its oak doors at the hour of seven and seats the tables from nineteen of the clock until ten and the half. We keep silence on Monday, and through the first fortnight of August, when the Maison observes its summer rest.

LES HEURES DE SERVICE

The doors are unbarred at half-past six in the evening for the late ; the prix-table is set at seven. The kitchen receives its last order at half-past ten, and the candles are extinguished at midnight, no later.

LES HEURES DE FERMETURE

Closed on Sundays and Mondays in observance of the old rule, and from the first to the fifteenth of August, when the house and its staff retire to the Île-de-France for the harvest.

U

Upon the matter of dress: a jacket is preferred at our long table, in keeping with the candlelit hour, but it is not required of any guest — we hold the welcome above the wardrobe. The small vestiaire may be found at the rear window keeps so live and take cock with us in the night with us. The familar receive in the cloak-vault and the candles in the eve in the breath of beeswax. Coming every winter night without ceremony, on whatever night you find your way to the Rue des Lombards.
