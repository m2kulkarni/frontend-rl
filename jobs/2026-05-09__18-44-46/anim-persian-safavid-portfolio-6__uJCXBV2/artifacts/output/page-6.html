◆ The Commissioning Ledger ◆

On Receiving a Commission

A Note on Scope, Time, & What Passes Between Us.

The atelier accepts a small number of architectural and devotional commissions each year. Each project begins with a written brief, a site visit when possible, and the preparation of a one-fifth scale cartoon together with material samples drawn from our own kilns at Naʿīn and Yazd. Nothing is fired until the cartoon has been signed.

Minimum Scope
4 m² of tilework

Sheets, the geometry cannot resolve to within 24 cm of a complete narrative; smaller pieces are reserved as workshop souvenirs.

Lead Time
6 — 14 months

From signed cartoon to installation, depending on plate scope, kiln cycle, and the season’s humidity.

I · Drawing & Sample

Prepared on Agreement

A binding agreement and a deposit of 30% accompanies the issue of cartoons; samples are kept in the studio archive.

II · Installation

By the Atelier Itself

The master and our senior workmen travel to site for setting, four masters in total for the full duration of the panel-laying.

I

Cutting, Brief

A written description of the surface, light, and intent. Photographs and elevations are welcomed.

II

Cartoon

A 1 : 5 ink drawing on paper, with a small panel of hand samples in the proposed palette.

III

Cutting & Firing

Tiles are cut by hand from manufactured slabs, a single colour-firing, then gold over-glaze where called for.

IV

Setting

On-site assembly. Two duties on paper completion, set in lime, then panel washed off after curing.

“The hand that cuts the tile remembers; the wall that receives it remembers longer.”
