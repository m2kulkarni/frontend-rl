SELECTED WORKS IN TILE

VIII · CARTOUCHE WORKS, 2007–2024

Each cartouche keeps its own scope and seal, written in the order the curator received them.

No. I · 2007

FEATURES OF THE HOUSE OF SHEIKH BAQUEY

Restoration of the inner mihrāb in cuerda technique, including reproduction of the original Selçuk-period inscription frieze in turquoise and aubergine.

No. II · 2010

NEYSHĀPŪR SCRIBES, NAQSH-E ROCK

A new spandrel block for a working madrasa courtyard built in the Khorāsānī manner, drawn from the cartouche schemata of the cliff workshops.

No. III · 2012

CHEHEL SOTOON MIRROR-PAVILION CEMENT

A consulting-and-tilework, of the regimental panels in the auditorium of the Forty-Pillared Pavilion. Sixteen tile panels under cleaning aid.

No. IV · 2014

PRIVATE GARDEN PAVILION, KASHĀN

A new pleasure-pavilion for a Kashan family, set among Spanish gardens. Roof is a single-roof tile, fitted in flexures of the early Tabrīzī muqarnas.

No. V · 2016

SARDĀR MOSQUE FOUNTAIN SURROUND

A polychrome cartouche-grouped surround for the marble ablution fountain at the Sardār Mosque, Tehran. Eight stone-lined gates.

No. VI · 2017

BORZOO TOMB READING ROOM

Re-tiled vault in deep cobalt and gold-leaf as a faithful copy of the panels documented by Pierre Cosselli in 1899.

No. VII · 2019

EMBASSY OF OMAN, RECEPTION HALL

A polychrome reception cartouche, with twelve repeated nodes drawn over a hex underprinting on the gate-vault Heart-fields. Designed and supervised in nine months.

No. VIII · 2022

JAMSHĪDIYEH RESTAURATION, SHIRĀZ

Continuation of the Jamshīdiyeh prayer-hall restoration. The rear courtyard, including the eight-pointed mihrāb at the south wall, completed in 2022.

ON COMMISSIONS

The atelier accepts a small number of architectural and devotional commissions each year. Each project begins with a written brief, a site-visit where possible, and the preparation of a one-fifth-scale cartoon together with material samples drawn from our own kilns at Na'in and Yazd. Nothing is fired until the cartoon has been signed.
