THE FOUR STAGES

STAGE I

JARRAHĪ

The grid-paper or vellum cartoon, drawn in iron-gall ink, in which a tracing of every fragment, with the principal cuerda lines, the pieces are noted, is used at the time of cutting and the firing.

A · CARTOON · TRACE · GRID
STAGE II

BORĪDEH-KĀRĪ

Hand-cut Yazdi quartz tesserae are scored on the back, freed by the burin and re-shaped against a fixed clay-brick edge, then in the hand of the master, with the precise figure exact in line.

B · CUT · TILE · TILE STONE · CARVE

CHĪDEMĀN

THE SEVEN COLORS · HAFT-RANG

Each glaze is mineral-fired in a single kiln-load — cobalt blue for the dome, copper-blue for the spandrel, manganese-yellow for the gold-line, ivory-white for the back-cement, iron-red for the script, lapis-blue for the spandrel, and the smoke-black for the line that rests under all the seven.
