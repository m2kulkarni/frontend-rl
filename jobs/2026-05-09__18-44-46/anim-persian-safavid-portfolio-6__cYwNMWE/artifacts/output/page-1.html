In Three Disciplines

WHAT THE ATELIER UNDERTAKES

The workshop holds to three lineages of inherited ornament, taught hand to hand from the masters of the Chahār-Bāgh and practised today on monuments, prayer halls, and private commissions across Iran and the Persian-speaking dominions.

I · TILE

TILE MOSAIC

Cut tile mosaic in the soluble, turquoise-gold trim, cuerda, and seven-colour tiled style by techniques half-and tile bestowed half-cut. Famous (used in our own Solo in 1389 H.S.) Solo solo cards of Yazd quartz and Tessera Esfehani.

View   ◆   Works
II · MUQARNAS

STUCCO MUQARNAS

Hand-carved gypsum stalactite vaults set into shrine drawn projection grids on the floor, in the manner of the Sheikh Lotfollah dome chamber. Faceted polychromy in lapis and gold-leaf available on commission.

View   ◆   Works
III · COUNSEL

ARCHITECTURAL COUNSEL

Consultation on tile programme, plaster geometry, and the ceremonial proportions of new mosques and gathering halls. Drawings issued in both ink-on-tabriz and surveyed digital form, with full schedules for craft subcontractors.

View   ◆   Works

ENTER THE PORTFOLIO

Twenty-eight commissions across nineteen years — mosques, madrasas, garden pavilions, and private dargāhs — catalogued in full plate, elevation, and photograph.
