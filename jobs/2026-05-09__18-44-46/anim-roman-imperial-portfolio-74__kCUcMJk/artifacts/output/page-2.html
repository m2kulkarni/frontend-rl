OPERA SELECTA

Six commissions selected from the years between MMXVII and now — three buildings, two ensembles of mosaic, and one civic stone. Other works are listed in the Index Operum at foot.

THOMAS LICINIUS

PRIVATE LIBRARY · MENTANA
BUILT · 2021 · STONE TRAVERTINE

A Palladian library volume, the apsidal end carrying a commission to compose a small library of the Latin grammarians chiefly. A six-bay timber roof on a foreign carpentry, framed in Friuli oak.

DOMUS VENNIA

PRIVATE HOUSE · TIVOLI
BUILT · 2019 · STONE TUFFA

The architect's own house, organised below a sloping roof of Tarsian terra-cotta. A two-storey glaciation around an alabaster well; a courtyard cypress; the bedrooms on a lower garden.

QUATTUOR TEMPORA

FOUR SEASONS MOSAIC · ROMA
BUILT · 2022 · MOSAIC SMALTI

Restoration of a III-century pavement depicting the four seasons. Tesserae lifted by section, individually documented, and dispersed at the Soprintendenza laboratory before re-laying.

BIBLIOTHECA AVRELIA

SCHOLARLY LIBRARY · GENOVA
BUILT · 2023 · STONE ISTRIAN

A reading room for a private collection of XVIII century maps, the south wall a single 11-metre run of double-glazed walnut shelving; a pair of narrow returning oak galleries above.

FORUM MERCATORUM

CIVIC FOUNTAIN · AQUILEIA
BUILT · 2023 · STONE AURISINA

A civic basin under a single span of istrian stone, served by a low aqueduct from the Risano. The basin lined in opus signinum from blue lime and crushed Carso brick.

CENOTAPHIUM MEMORIAE

STONECUTTERS' CENOTAPH · AQUILEIA
BUILT · 2023 · STONE TRAVERTINE

A travertine memorial of six steps below a frame fluted column. Engraved with the names of stonecutters lost to silicosis at the Carso quarries since 1880; designed pro publico.
