学部 · 課程

Hokurin's undergraduate body of 1,640 students is organized into three intentionally small faculties, each rooted in the disciplines that made Higashiyama feel necessary for four centuries — letters, craft, and the watershed.

A four-year arc, with the third year spent away from the seminar room.

Every Hokurin undergraduate follows the same four-year rhythm. The first year is a shared foundation — classical Japanese, English or a second European language, and a methodology core taught in the 1782 main hall. The second year is taken inside the chosen faculty, where students sit small reading and making seminars capped at twelve.

The third year is the fieldwork year, mandated since 1989: a minimum of twenty-four weeks of supervised work outside the campus — at a Tōhoku washi-papermaking studio, a Hokuriku lacquerware forest, or a working conservator-printer in Yokohama. The fourth year returns to Sanjō-dōri for graduation thesis, defended in March before two faculty and one external reader.

FACULTIES
FACULTY I

Humanities

人文学部 · Bungakubu

Classical and modern letters, paleography, and the history of Kyoto's printed and copied texts.

  • Classical Japanese literature
  • Sinographic textual studies
  • Edo-period print history
  • Translation theory
  • Diplomatic & archival method
FACULTY II

Craft Sciences

工芸科学部 · Kōgeikagakubu

Material analysis and the working understanding of lacquer, washi, and the substrates that survive Kyoto's seasons.

  • Lacquer chemistry
  • Washi & bast-fibre making
  • Pigment & mineral conservation
  • Joinery & tool history
  • Light, humidity & pest ecology
FACULTY III

Environmental Studies

環境学部 · Kankyōgakubu

The Kamo and Takano basins read as a single watershed, from headwater geology down to satoyama field practice.

  • Watershed hydrology
  • Satoyama land-use history
  • Urban ecology of Kyoto
  • Climate & agronomy
  • Field methods & mapping
