ADMISSIONS·入学のご案内

Apply to
Hokurin.

Hokurin admits a small undergraduate cohort and a smaller graduate institute each year — roughly 320 entering students across the machiya campus. The academic calendar offers three intake windows for AY 2025–2026, with rolling review for international applicants. We read each application by hand, in committee, and reply in the season we received it.

INTERNATIONAL · 通年

Rolling Review

Ongoing

Reviewed within 30 days of receipt. JLPT N2 or equivalent recommended; written exam waivable for applicants holding completed degree-track Japanese coursework. Visa sponsorship available; letters issued upon offer.
