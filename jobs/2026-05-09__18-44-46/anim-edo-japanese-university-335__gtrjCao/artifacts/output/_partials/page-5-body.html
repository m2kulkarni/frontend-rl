Four buildings,
one quiet block of
Higashiyama.

The Hokurin campus occupies a single Higashiyama-ward block of Sanjō-dōri, between the 1782 main hall and a former cooper's yard, restored in 1991 by master carpenter Inoue Tetsuro. Three teaching halls (the gakushū, the kōgei-tō, and the kankyō-kan) face inward across a moss courtyard; the Tōrin Bunko sits at the rear under paulownia eaves.

Walking directions

SANJŌ-KEIHAN
北林大学
TWO ROUTES BY FOOT

From Higashiyama Station

Tozai Line · 8 minutes' walk south along Higashiōji-dōri, then east on Sanjō for two blocks. The Sanjō Gate is on the right under a paulownia eave; you'll see the brass plate.

From Sanjō-Keihan

Up Sanjō-dōri across the river, ten minutes east. Cross the Shirakawa stream by the second wooden bridge — Hokurin is the building with the wagara fence.

By city bus

Routes 5, 100, 203 · alight at Higashiyama-Sanjō. Two minutes north along Sanjō-dōri.

Twice a year the campus opens its inner courtyards, conservation studios and rare-book vault to the public. As registration is required, printed walking guides in Japanese, English, and French are offered at the Sanjō genkan.

Higashiyama Contemporary, V.6
Visiting weeks alongside seven contemporary printmakers from the prefecture, organized with the Higashiyama Contemporary collective. Free admission with a campus pass; portfolios viewable, by appointment, in the Tōrin Bunko's annexe.
