Edicts Newly Posted

Sealed within the present month and currently in force throughout the realm

Farman of Civil

On the Regulation of Caravan
Tolls between Kashan & Qom

Sealed 8th of Awwal, 1037 A.H.
By the Hand of the Diwanbegi

All toll levied upon laden camels passing the bridges of Natanz shall not exceed three dinārs per beast, that the trade of His Majesty's caravans be not hindered. Any officer found exceeding this allowance shall forfeit his post.

The Pilgrims to the shrines of Hazrat Ma‘suma stand toll-free upon presentation of seal.

In Force From the New Moon

Farman of Civil

On the Appointment of Mirzā
Taqī as Kalāntar of the Silk-
Weavers

Sealed 9th of Awwal, 1037 A.H.
By Royal Hand

Mirzā Taqī ibn Hosayn of the quarter of Jolfa is confirmed kalāntar over the silk-weavers of Isfahan for the term of three years. He shall keep the standard of warp and weft, with diligent musters by his hands, and report quarterly accounts to this office. The guild and quarter is bound to his most assembly.

Confirmed by Royal Mandate

Farman of Civil

On the Prohibition of
Unlicensed Dyeing within the
Bazaar-e Bozorg

Sealed 10th of Awwal, 1037 A.H.
Under Royal Confirmation

No use of indigo, madder, or cochineal shall be within the covered bazaar save by those bearing the muhtasib's stamped license. Unlicensed dyers shall have their stocks poured into the gutter and pay a fine of ten tomāns to the public treasury. Repeated offence shall close the workshop for one full year.

Penalty Enforceable in Court

Proclamation of His Majesty

Concerning the Peace of the Maydan

It being known to all subjects of the realm, to the merchants of the bazaar, to grooms of the chowgan-field, to pilgrims and travellers alike, that the Maydan-e Naqsh-e Jahan is sanctified ground for the commerce and recreation of the people. No quarrel of arms, no driving of laden beasts after the second call to prayer, no unauthorized assembly sounding twenty persons, shall be tolerated within its precincts. The dārūgha and his men patrol the four arcades by day and by night.

— Sealed in the Royal Chancery, 1st of Awwal of Awwal, 1037 A.H.
