編集部
Editorial Office

There is a quarterly journal devoted to the material culture of the Edo period — the years between Tokugawa Ieyasu's first arrival at the eastern marsh and the dedication of the Mitsuwa-bashi. We publish slowly, four issues a year, on uncoated paper from the Echizen mills.

Our remit is narrow: the artisans who pulled indigo from a vat or chestnut from the morning. The post-towns whose mokumon-jirushi survived the seven-decade tide of asphalt. The signs that — though running a typewritten line, the language of which is not always retained.

Founded in 2019 by Marika Sato (until recently of the former contents from the Edo-Tokyo Museum), the Review remains independent, reader-funded, and unaffiliated with any university press.
