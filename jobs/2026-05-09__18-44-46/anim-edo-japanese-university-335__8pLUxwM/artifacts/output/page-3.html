Apply to
Hokurin.

A small public university with three intake windows: standard domestic cycle in September; the late cycle and transfer cycle in February; the rolling review for non-Japanese applicants and visiting researchers, year-round.

Three Intake Windows

一 · STANDARD

Standard Domestic Cycle

Open to graduates of Japanese senior high schools. Application is by interview, portfolio, and short written reasoning; no standardized exam.

1 SEPTEMBER 2025

二 · LATE

Late Cycle & Transfers

A small late window for transfer applicants from other Japanese universities and for spring entry. Portfolio review is the same as standard cycle.

15 JANUARY 2026

三 · ROLLING

Rolling Review

Year-round consideration for non-Japanese applicants and for visiting researchers attached to a faculty. English-medium interview available.

YEAR-ROUND

学費・支援· TUITION & FEE WAIVERS

Tuition & Fee Waivers

Hokurin is a public university; tuition is set by national rate. The Tōrin Foundation underwrites a fee waiver for half of all entering undergraduates and for nearly two-thirds of MA candidates in conservation.

Documents & Examination

Documents

  • Application form — a single A4, in Japanese or English. Available online.
  • Academic record — secondary or post-secondary, in original language with summary.
  • Statement of intent — 800 words, addressing the question posed in the cycle.
  • Portfolio — practical work, writing samples, or field notes; format open.
  • Photograph & identity copy — single ID image; passport for international.

Entrance Examination

  • Japanese & English — short translation in either direction; not graded for grammar.
  • Mathematics or World History — short reasoning paper, choice of either.
  • Faculty-Specific Conversation — 30 min, in chosen language, with two faculty.

合計三段・甲乙丙

Three rounds — first, second, third — and a single shared meal at the close of intake.
