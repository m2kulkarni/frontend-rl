The Tōrin Bunko is a working library of Edo-period woodblocks held jointly with the conservation studio. Borrowing is in the room, in cotton gloves, on a foam pillow.

桐林文庫· TŌRIN BUNKO
RECORDS · 記

Fourteen thousand volumes, given on a single autumn morning in 1991.

The Tōrin Bunko was founded by a private gift of woodblocks and impressions from the Yamagishi family of Higashiyama, the legacy of three generations of merchant scholarship. The collection is reviewed and re-stitched on a six-year cycle.

Duplication Request Procedure

  • Submit form A-3 (in person at the front desk) at least three working days in advance.
  • State the catalog reference, the page or sheet, and a single sentence on the use.
  • Tōrin Bunko items are scanned at 600 dpi by the conservation studio.
  • One scan per request, billed at cost. Commercial use requires a separate license.
  • Scans of woodblock impressions are watermarked at the Bunko's discretion.
客員研究員· VISITING SCHOLARS

Visiting Scholars

The Tōrin Bunko reserves a small number of three-month residencies each academic year, drawn from international applications. Residents are given a desk in Reading Room B, access to the Bunko under faculty supervision, and a small living stipend. Apply in writing through the Office of the Provost; review is by panel.
