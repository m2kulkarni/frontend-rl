Twelve sheets of works on paper from the studio of Lucia Marciana — the studio's principal lines of practice — architectural elevations rendered in ink and grey wash, full-scale templates for coffered ceilings, running borders in the Greek meander, and laurel cartouches for inscription above doorways and funerary tablets. Originals travel rolled in linen sleeves; numbered editions of three are pulled on request.

All originals are drawn at a scale of 1 : 50 on Fabriano Roma laid paper, 85 grams the sheet, deckle-edged, with a hand-pulled watermark. Inks are oak-gall and lampblack; washes are bone-black and burnt sienna. Each plate is signed in red lead at the lower right and dated in Roman numerals.
