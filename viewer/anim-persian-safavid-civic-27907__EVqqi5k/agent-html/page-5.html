Petitions & Hearings

Concerning the proper submission of the arzeh-da'shé — the written petition — and the conduct of public hearings before His Excellency the Divánbegi, that justice may be sought without obstruction by any subject of the realm.

Be it known to all subjects

that the petition of the Diván is each morning from the dawn prayer until the second call. Hearings are held the morning of Saturday, on the festival of Mihrjan, and any Wednesday afternoon, from the third hour, by leave and call of the recorder.

No petition shall be heard which lacks the seal of moharrer, nor which is presented by an intermediary one cannot stand to himself in body.

Required Particulars

What the Petition Must Contain

Name

Of the petitioner in full, with father's name and the quarter of residence.

Quarter

Of present occupation

Subject

The grievance set in plain words, without complaint of station.

Witness

Two free men of standing in the quarter.

Subject

Set plainly, in fewer words, the matter of grievance.

Date

By the calendar of the year, with the month written in full.

Reception

Where to Present It

Bring the written petition to the outer windowed of the Diván, in the morning hours, between the dawn prayer and the second call.

The Mosque of Sheikh Lutfullah, where the door of the registry is opened.

A petition is sealed within the day. A second sealed by another hand if the matter is wanted.

If a petition is unsealed and unread, by the hand of the deputy, the same is set in the alms-jar, awaiting Wednesday.

The witnesses are heard at the second call.

Hearing

Appearance Before the Divánbegí

Upon the Wednesday following acceptance, the petitioner shall present himself in the inner audience hall with the witnesses upon him.

The Divánbegí hears no more than twelve matters in any sitting; the remainder set over to the morrow. The order is by the recording, save in the Wednesday accommodations.

Procedure

The Order of Proceedings

I. Convocation. The petitioner is invited to the inner hall upon a single wide ringing of the gong; the petitioner is informed without delay, of his place taken in the Maydán of the Diván.

II. Witness & Seal. The first that the seal-keepers stand fast as the moharrer reads aloud the matter of the petition.

III. Submission. The petition is presented in the outer courtyard where it is rec'd by the seal-keeper of the deputy by the hand of the recorder.

IV. Hearing. Upon a stand by the registrar, called by the muezzin's first call to the dawn, the Divánbegí is met before mid-day; the witnesses being entered between the second and the third watch.

V. Decree. The ruling is set down in the register, sealed by the hand of the Divánbegí and a copy delivered to the petitioner without further fee.

Special Notice

Concerning New Jolfa

Petitions arising among the Armenian community of New Jolfa — whether of trade or family — are heard by the deputy of the divan-beg in the manner accustomed, by leave of his Majesty.

It shall not avoid the petition that the script be of the Armenian, but the seal of the moharrer of the quarter shall be set, and the entry shall be made in both letters of the line of the registry.
