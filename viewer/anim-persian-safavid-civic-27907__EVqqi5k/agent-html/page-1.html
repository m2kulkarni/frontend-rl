Diván-i Isfahán

Civil Register of the Naqsh-e Jahán

Issued in the northern arcade of the Maydán-i Naqsh-e Jahán, between the Qaysariyeh Gate and the Mosque of Sheikh Lutfullah. By the order and seal of His Majesty Shah Abbas, the prince of architects and the lord of the city's stones. Hereby it stands for the welfare of the city and good government of its public quarters.

Notices of the Week

Three matters published this day for the city of Isfahan.
From the Royal Court

Edict of His Majesty
Shah Abbas

Be it known to all true hearts, caravan masters, and travellers passing through the Maydán: that the new caravanserai at Mader-i Shah, erected by royal command, shall open its gates to lodgers from the first of Jumada al-thaní next.

Tariffs upon the silk of Gilán, raw and woven, are by the same hand reduced by one part in ten, that the trade of His Majesty's domains may flourish.

Public Notice

Notice — Zayandeh-Rud
Canal Works

The Master of Waters announces that, beginning the fifth of the month, the dredging and re-lining of the canal of Farahán-Khalfe commences, with labour drawn from the wards of Goharbar and Dárdasht.

Watering of orchards along the southern channel shall be permitted only between the second prayer and the call of sunset for the duration of forty days.

Any obstruction of the works, or diverting of water without leave, shall be answered before the Divánbegi.

— Mírzá Taqí, Mír-áb of Isfahán
