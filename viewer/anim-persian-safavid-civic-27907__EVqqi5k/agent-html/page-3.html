Folio Three · Daftar of the Bazaar

Posted Prices & Weights

Of the Qaysariyeh and the Caravanserais Adjoining

Set this day in accordance with the firmán of His Majesty,
that no merchant impose upon another.

B y the order of His Majesty Shah Abbas, Refuge of the World, and under the seal of the Divánbegi of Isfahan, the following prices are fixed for this beginning of the twelfth day of Mihr in the seven and twenty year of the reign. Every man of the bazaar may consult them by name, and the wardens of weight and seal shall be answerable for their truth at the change of the watch. We declare none below or above them.

Folio of Concord

Standards of Weight & Measure

The principal weight of the bazaar, again re-measured and forty mishqal at weight, three of the standard and ten. The man is held to take the weight set in seal and red wax.

Man

A measure equal to forty mithqals of weight by the standard.

Sang

A lesser weight, reserved for the dearest commodities; equal to one of the standard. Eight of these to the man.

Gaz

The cloth measure of the bazaar, of the length of the arm and forty in the wrist of the master. Twenty-four asbáh to the gaz.

Mithqal

The smallest, fixed in weight by silver, and to it the rest are referred. Twenty-four nukhud to the mithqal.

Folio of Inspection

Inspections of the Muhtasib

By appointment of the Muhtasib of Isfahán

The honoured Muhtasib, accompanied by two scribes and four members of the watch, shall pass through the stalls on the appointed days, weighing the merchant's stock against the master-stamp of the office.
