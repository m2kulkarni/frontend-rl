Register V · Khūṭ-i Shahr

Quarters, Canals & Bazaars

Being a true register of the twelve administrative quarters of Iṣfahān, the four principal madīs that water the city, and the eight-and-thirty bazaar guilds confirmed by the office of the Muḥtasib.

COMPILED IN THE REIGN OF SHĀH ABBĀS IIYEAR 1107 A.H. · ISFAHĀN

Whereas, with edicts before the Dīvān, or would be petition concerning water-shares, market-stall, or boundary of quarter, shall consult those tables. Names of the kalāntar (quarter-elders), members (master-watchers), and ʿarīḍa (guild-marshalled) are set forth herein, with their auditors days at the Qaysariyya Gate, the books along the Chahār Bāgh, and the western almanacrs of the Khwāja Bridge.
