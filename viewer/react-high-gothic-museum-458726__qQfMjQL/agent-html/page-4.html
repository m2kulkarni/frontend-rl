Accessibility

[motif: quatrefoil]

Wheelchair access is provided through the north transept entrance, where the floor lies level with the ambulatory and the chapter house gallery.

Step-free routes connect every principal hall, the manuscript room, and the lapidary garden. Tactile reproductions of selected capitals are kept at the docent’s lectern; please ask the warden on duty.
