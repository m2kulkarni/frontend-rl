ADMISSIONS · 入学

入学のご案内

Apply to Hokurin.

Hokurin admits a small undergraduate cohort and a smaller graduate institute each year — roughly 320 entering students across the machiya campus. The academic calendar offers three intake windows for AY 2025–2026, with rolling review for international applicants. We read each application by hand, in committee, and reply in the season we received it.

Three Intake Windows

APPLICATION CALENDAR · 2025/2026
AUTUMN INTAKE · 秋

Standard Domestic Cycle

1 September 2025

Applications open Jūichi-gatsu (November). Forms due 1 September; interviews held mid-October at the Sanjō campus. Decisions by November for January matriculation.

SPRING INTAKE · 春

Late Cycle & Transfers

15 January 2026

For applicants from the JLPT calendar or transfers from elsewhere. Environmental humanities and the Craft Sciences review through 8 March.

INTERNATIONAL · 国際

Rolling Review

Ongoing

International applicants need not align with JLPT. As is reviewed monthly; recommended writing sample with completed degree-track Japanese coursework. Visa support available.

TUITION & FEE WAIVERS · 学費

Tuition & Fee Waivers

学費・減免

Hokurin's tuition is set just below the national-public average. Need-based waivers cover roughly two-thirds of admitted students; the Tōrin Foundation provides a smaller number of merit awards to incoming graduate cohorts.

DOCUMENTS & EXAMINATION · 出願書類

Documents & Examination

出願書類 · 入学試験

Documents

  • Application form (甲)Filed online; printed copy submitted at the Sanjō gate
  • Secondary transcriptIncluding a sealed copy of the most recent term
  • Two letters of recommendationOne from a working scholar, one from a working practitioner
  • Photograph of an object you have madeBy any process, in any medium; need not be 'good' work
  • Personal essay (~1200 字)On the question: what does slow attention cost you?

Entrance Examination

  • Japanese & EnglishOne short translation in each direction. Dictionaries permitted; speed not measured.
  • Mathematics or World HistoryOne survey paper; choice declared at registration.
  • Faculty-Stream ConversationTwenty minutes with a faculty member of the chosen stream; held in the genkan reading room.
