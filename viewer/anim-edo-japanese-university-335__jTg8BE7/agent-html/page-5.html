CAMPUS & VISITING · 校舍と来訪のしおり

校舍と来訪のしおり

Four buildings,
one quiet block of
Higashiyama.

The Hokurin campus occupies a single Meiji-era machiya block — Sanjō-dōri 4 to 12 — and the four restored townhouses behind it. We do not have an athletics field or a parking deck. We do have a 250-seat hall, a kiln, and a 1762 storehouse the Bunko makes its home. Visitors are welcome on open-campus days, plainly marked, except in early August when the gates close for moisture control.

The four buildings

玄関 · 講堂 · 工房 · 文庫

Genkan / 玄関

The street-level entry house, restored in 1989. Reading room, archivist's bench, and the genkan reading bench where visitors sit and remove their shoes.

Kōdō / 講堂

A 250-seat lecture hall with raised tatami back rows; the larger of the two wooden floors is reserved for studio defenses and craft critiques.

Kōbō / 工房

The studio house, with a working noborigama kiln in the side garden and four bench-style rooms calibrated to traditional indigo, lacquer, paper, and stone-pigment work.

Bunko / 文庫

The 1762 paulownia storehouse — restored in 1991 by master carpenter Inoue Tetsurō and now home to the Tōrin Bunko's reading room, climate-controlled and quietly insulated.

WALKING DIRECTIONS · 来訪

Walking directions

徒歩のご案内 · TWO ROUTES BY FOOT
SANJŌ GATE
Higashiyama Station Sanjō Keihan
FROM HIGASHIYAMA STATION
Two routes by foot

Exit the Tōzai-line concourse at the Higashiyama platform; walk east along the south side of Sanjō-dōri for nine minutes. The campus gate is the second machiya past the Shirakawa bridge — look for the matching paulownia kamon mounted on either gatepost.

FROM KAMOGAWA
From Sanjō Keihan

Cross the river at the Sanjō footbridge; the campus is twelve minutes east, on the south side. The cobbles get less even after the third corner; please walk carefully if it has been raining.

VISITORS BY CAR
By the bus

Buses 5, 100, and 206 stop at the Sanjō Keihan-mae stop, six minutes by foot. We do not maintain a parking deck; nearby paid lots are listed at the visitor's gate.

OPEN-CAMPUS DAYS · オープンキャンパス

Open-campus days

五七桐
GOSHICHI-NO-KIRI

Twice a year the campus opens its doors: courtyards, conservation studios, and rare-book vault to the public. No registration is required; printed walking guides in Japanese, English, and French are offered at the Sanjō genkan.
