Tabula Quarta · Folio MMXXIV

Drawings&Ornament

Twelve plates of works on paper from the studio of Lucia Marciana — pavement studies, ceiling templates, and ornamental cartouches drawn for private commission.

Each plate gathered here is offered as a commissioned work, drawn by hand at the bench on the via dei Coronari. The collection covers the studio's principal lines of practice — architectural elevations rendered in ink and grey wash, full-scale templates for coffered ceilings, running borders in the Greek manner, and laurel cartouches for inscription above doorways and funerary tablets. ✥ Originals travel rolled in linen sleeves; numbered editions of three are pulled on request.

All originals are drawn at a scale of 1 : 50 on Fabriano Roma laid paper, 83 grams the sheet, deckle-edged, with a hand-pulled watermark. Inks are oak-gall and lampblack; washes are bone-black and burnt sienna. Each plate is signed in red lead at the lower right and dated in Roman numerals.
