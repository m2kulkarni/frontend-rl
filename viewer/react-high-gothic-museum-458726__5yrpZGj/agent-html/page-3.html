CURRENT EXHIBITIONS

Three temporary exhibitions on view through Candlemas, the second day of February in the year of our Lord two thousand and twenty-five.

[motif: quatrefoil-in-square]
Exhibition · I

The Cobalt of Chartres

North Transept Gallery · Through 2 February

A close study of the pigment chemistry behind the famed bleu de Chartres — pressured alongside side-by-side panels drawn from the Side Aisles, with cross-section microphotography and trace-element analysis from the Atelier Loire.

Curator
Dr Hélène Massin
Pieces
24 stained glass leaves, c. 1180 – 1220
Loan
Loan
Advisory
PG · IX · XII · 18 min
Exhibition · II

Hand of the Master: the Reims Sketchbooks

South Cloister · Through 5 February

Thirty-eight silverpoint drawings attributed to the workshop of Villard de Honnecourt, presented for the first time in their bound order. Architectural elevations, lion studies, and a remarkable diagram of a self-winding water clock.

Curator
Prof. Adrien Sasson-Boivot
Pieces
38 leaves, silverpoint on prepared vellum
Loan
Permanent · Through 5 February
Advisory
Timed entry, every 20 minutes
Exhibition · III

Gold and Lapis: a Book of Hours from Bourges

Reliquary Chamber · Through 5 February

A private collection manuscript opened to the Annunciation miniature, with its leaf turned each Friday at the eleventh hour by conservators Père Anselme Vaudoul, who will speak briefly before each turning.

Curator
Père Anselme Vaudoul
Pieces
Bourges atelier, c. 1410 – 1425
Loan
Gold leaf, lapis lazuli, vermillion
Advisory
Approx. 20 minutes
