Practical Information for the Visitor

Visit

The doors of the Museum of Isfahan are housed in an open court on Chahar Bagh ʿAbbasi where Shah ʿAbbasi once paced the avenue of the gardens. We open daily, with five recess weeks; the entry fee is modest; and we ask of those passing through the halls of the Atelier merely care.

Closures & Notices

The galleries close at 16.30 on the day before public holidays. The Reconstructed Iwān is closed on Mondays and on the second Thursday of each month for cleaning. The reading room and library admit researchers by appointment only.

Access, Groups & Schools

Accessibility

The lower floors are wholly accessible — a step-free entrance from the Hadi Park lot leads to the level halls, and the lift to the upper galleries is sized for chairs. The Reconstructed Iwān has a railing along its southern edge. Companion-free entry is granted upon request.

Group Bookings

Groups of ten or more may book by writing to the Office of Visits, who will respond with a recommended hour, a guide-leader, and an admission rate. School groups (years 4–12) attend free of charge with a curriculum-aligned guide; please book a fortnight in advance.

Schools & Families

Children's workshops on tile-pattern, calligraphy, and silk-weaving run on Saturdays during term time. Family handouts are stocked at the entry desk. Strollers may be checked at the cloakroom; nursing rooms are on the lower floor next to the cafe.
