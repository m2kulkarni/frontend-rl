The Royal Atelier Library

A Working Library

Some 12 000 monographs and 1 800 serial volumes — a working collection on the arts of Iran from the Sasanian to the Qajar — are held in the open stacks. The library specialises in late Safavid painting, Islamic geometry, the Persian carpet, and ceramic chemistry. Closed-stack works of fragility are paged by appointment.

Volumes (open stack)12 000
Serial volumes1 800
Special collections35

Access & Appointments

The reading room admits researchers by appointment from Tuesday to Friday, from ten to five. Closed-stack pages are delivered within the hour by reading-room staff; the latest material is held in the office of the head of research and is paged with a one-day delay.

  • Reader's card by application
  • Renewable annually
  • Three-day notice for closed-stack
  • Photography by request, with archival permits

Resident Fellowships

The Mostofi Fellowship in Painting

A one-year residence supporting a single scholar of Persian painting, c. 1500–1750. The fellow is housed in a modest apartment in the museum's east wing and contributes a single article to the museum's annual journal.

Deadline 31 December · Award 12 months

The Sherazi Fellowship in Textile & Conservation

A two-year residence in the textile atelier supporting hands-on work on the silken collections of Yazd and Tabriz. The fellow trains alongside the head conservator and contributes a section to the museum's catalogue.

Deadline 30 September · Award 24 months

The annual symposium gathers scholars, conservators, and atelier-masters around a single question: how does a court make the things it makes? This year's symposium considers the role of patronage in shaping the late Safavid hand, and is open to readers of the library.
