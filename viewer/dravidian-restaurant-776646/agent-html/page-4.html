Parking & Auto

Valet parking from ₹100 at the side gate on 13th Cross. Two cars per family, please.

· A VILLAGE ROOM

Private Dining

The Mandapam Room seats twelve at a single rosewood table, lit by a brass-studded ceiling and ringed in pillared teak panels rescued from a Karaikudi mansion.

Reserved for engagements, naming days, and quiet birthdays. Set menus from ₹2,400 per guest (vegetarian) or ₹3,200 (Chettinad mutton-and-rice). Two-week notice, 50% advance.

Write to mandapam@kalasha.in

· FROM THE KITCHEN

Dietary Notes

The kitchen is pure vegetarian on Friday lunch in keeping with the household tradition. No meat, fish or egg crosses the threshold between 12.30 and 15:00 that day.

All mutton dishes — onji asuwal, chukku, the Sunday biryani — use Bannur lamb, sourced direct from a single farm in Mandya district.

Jain, vegan, and gluten-free meals on twenty-four hours' notice. Do tell us at the time of booking; the rasam stock will be set aside.

· BY LANE & LANDMARK
