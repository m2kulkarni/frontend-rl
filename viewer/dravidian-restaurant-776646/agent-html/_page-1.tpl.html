The Kitchen

Pots · Stones · Coal

We grind our masala on granite each morning at six. Black pepper from Wayanad, kapasi from the Kanjirapally hills, fennel and star anise weighed by hand. Eight kuzhambu pots simmer through service on coal fire. Every last leaf is torn, never cut. The pestle, the iron pan, the slow flame — these are our only kitchen technology.

The Room

Athangudi Tile · 1846 Beam

Forty-six covers under a temple-bright ceiling of lime wash and gilded beam. The floor is laid in cobalt and ochre Athangudi tile shipped from Karaikudi in 2017. Brass lamps, teak pillars rescued from a Madurai chettiar house, banana leaf service at lunch, and peacock-blue linen at dinner. The room is meant to feel like a home, visit-loud.

The Reservation Book

Two Sittings · Hand-Written

The book opens fourteen days ahead. Lunch at 12.30 and 2.15; dinner at 7.30 and 9.15, Tuesday through Sunday. Sunday Karaikudi sappadu (?1,650) is served on banana leaf and runs one sitting only at 1 p.m. Walk-ins are kept for the eight-bar seats facing the kitchen line. Telephone or write; we reply within the day.
