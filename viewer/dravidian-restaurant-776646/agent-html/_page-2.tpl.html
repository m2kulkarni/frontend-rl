Bill of Fare·Lunch & Dinner

The Menu

A note from the kitchen — All midday meals are served on fresh banana leaf, in the manner of Karaikudi households. Rice is from the Thanjavur delta, cold-pressed gingelly oil is sourced from Virudhunagar; palm jaggery and cane jaggery arrive each fortnight from Tirunelveli. Spice blends are stone-ground in our kitchen on Wednesdays. Vegetarian, jain, and gluten-conscious adaptations available on request.

Idli & Sambar ₹150

Steamed · Server Three

Three pillow-soft idlis from a 14-hour fermented batter, ladled with drumstick-shallot sambar and a duo of chutneys — coconut-mint and roasted tomato.

Idli is feathery — sambar is the part to argue about

Crisp · Cast-Iron Griddle

Stone-ground podi · cultured ghee from Erode dairies

Kuzhi Paniyaram ₹220

Skillet-Cast · Eight Pieces

Eight golden domes from leftover dosai batter, studded with curry-leaf, mustard, and shallot. Served with gun-powder tomato chutney and cooling coconut chutney.

Cast-iron paniyaram pan, gingelly oil for charred bottoms

Kal Dosai & Kara Chutney ₹240

Stone-Pressed · Three Pieces

Thick, lacy dosais cooked slow on the stone tava, plated with a fiery kara chutney of dried red chilli, shallot, and tamarind, finished with curry-leaf tempering.

Served on Banana Leaf · Lunch Only

Karaikudi Virundhu Sappadu ₹680

Heritage Plate · Eleven Items

A weekday feast in the Karaikudi tradition — paruvi rice, vatha kuzhambu, more kuzhambu, two periyals, keelu, urulai varuval, appalam, fresh curd, mango pickle, and paal payasam to close. Refills of rice, sambar, rasam, curd.

Hand-pounded podi for the Periyalal · Karadaiyan Nombu

Sunday Elai Sappadu ₹820

One Sitting · 1 PM Only

Our elaborate Sunday spread — kothu kuzhambu, paruppu vendaikuzhambu, three seasonal periyals, ennai kathirikai, vazhaipoo vadai, mor kuzhambu, beetroot pachadi, adirasam, and paal payasam. Served twelve to three.

Bookings essential · single sitting at 1 pm sharp

"In Chettinad, a meal is a procession — rice arrives first, then thirteen small dignities, each with its own moment on the leaf." Chef Meenakshi Alagappan, Head Kitchen

+ Kuzhambu & Varuval +

Slow-Cooked · A la Carte

Chettinad Kojhi Varuval ₹520

Pepper-Heavy Chicken · Dry

Country chicken stir-fried with our signature varuval masala — black pepper, fennel, kalpasi stone-flower, and curry-leaf — until each piece is dark, glossy, and clinging with spice.

Free-range bird from a farm near Hampshanthar · stand-up sliced for dryness

Meen Kuzhambu ₹540

Tamarind Fish Curry · Coastal

Seer fish simmered in a clay pot with tamarind, fenugreek, shallot, and gingelly oil. The gravy is intentionally thin, stained for tartar's red — best the next day, sharper the morning after.

Sea-coast seer · Tirunelveli iron tamarind for the threading sour

Ennai Kathirikai ₹360

Stuffed Brinjal · Gingelly Oil Bralve

Small purple brinjals slit and stuffed with a roasted-coconut sesame masala, then braised low in gingelly oil until the skins blister and the gravy turns mahogany.

Cold-pressed gingelly oil from Virudhunagar · deal-blistered nine-times-over

Mutton Sukka Varuval ₹620

Pepper Mutton · Dry-Spiced

Bone-in mutton slow-cooked, then tossed dry with freshly pounded pepper, curry-leaf, and grated coconut. Plated with a wedge of lime and slivered shallot.

Goat from a Bhargavpur butcher · half-fries pepper triple-stoked
