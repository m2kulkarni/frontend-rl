A Villaged Room

Private Dining

The Mandapam Room seats twelve at a single rosewood table, lit by a brass-chimney lamp and ringed in pillared teak panels rescued from a Karaikudi mansion.

Reserved for engagements, naming days, and quiet birthdays. Set menus from ₹2,400 per guest (vegetarian) or ₹3,200 (Chettinad mutton-and-rice). Two-week notice; 50% advance.

Write to mandapam@kalasha.in

From the Kitchen

Dietary Notes

The kitchen is pure vegetarian on Friday lunch in keeping with the household tradition; no meat, fish, or egg crosses the threshold between 12.30 and 15.30 that day.

All mutton dishes — sukka varuval, chukka, the Sunday biryani — use bone in lamb, sourced direct from a single farm in Mandya district.

Jain, vegan, and gluten-free meals on twenty-four hours' notice. Do tell us at the time of booking; the rasam stock will be set aside.

By Lane & Landmark

Directions

From 100 Feet Road: turn into 12th Main between the bookshop and the medical store. Cross 13th Cross. We are the second doorway on the left, set back from the road behind a frangipani tree.

From CMH Road: follow Defence Colony signage; once past the temple courtyard, keep right at the chai-stall. Kalasha is fifty metres on.
