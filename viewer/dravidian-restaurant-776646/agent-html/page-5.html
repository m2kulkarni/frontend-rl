Feast Days & Seasons

Four times a year the temple kitchen at Kalasha bends to the planets. Harvest sun, river flood, nine nights of the goddess, and the long lamps of Karthigai — each calls for a different rice, a different kuzhambu, a different way of laying the leaf. Bookings open six weeks in advance.

— THE FOUR FEAST DAYS —
▼ THE HARVEST LUNCH · THE PONGAL

Pongal Thali ₹950

SATURDAY 13 JANUARY & WEDNESDAY 15 JANUARY · SITTINGS 12:30 AND 2:30
  • Sakkarai pongal cooked at the table over a clay stove, jaggery from Erode
  • Ven pongal with whole black pepper, cashew, twelve curry leaves
  • Seven vegetable koottu, raw turmeric pickle, gingelly oil aval
  • Sugarcane, banana, betel leaf — served on a fresh plantain leaf
BOOKING Fixed thali at ₹950 per person · Whole table reservations only — minimum twelve, maximum eighteen per leaf service.
▼ THE RIVER FESTIVAL · AADI 18

Aadi Perukku ₹1,250

SATURDAY 3 AUGUST & SUNDAY 4 AUGUST · SINGLE SITTING, 1:00 PM
  • Three coloured rices — coconut, lemon, tamarind, curd, sesame — packed in banana leaf
  • Vegetable kootani sorted in a kalachatti vessel
  • Paruppu payasam with palm jaggery, served warm from copper
  • Tender mango pickle, three-day aged, from the kitchen's clay jar
BOOKING Carry-out parcels available for the river-bank ritual · order three days ahead. Dining-room covers, two each.
▼ NINE NIGHTS · SUNDAL COUNTER

Navaratri Sundal ₹180 / cone

22 SEPTEMBER – 1 OCTOBER, 2024 · 4:00 PM TO 9:00 PM, COURTYARD COUNTER
  • Nine days, nine legumes — chana, kabuli, peanut, green gram, motchai, karamani, thattai payaru, white pea, brown gram
  • Tempered with mustard, asafoetida, fresh coconut, raw mango on alternate evenings
  • Served in palm-leaf cones with kosumalli salad and a small payasam
  • Free goluvu darshan in the entry bay · golu by the children
BOOKING Walk-in counter, no reservation required. Whole platter pre-orders for offices 24 hours notice.
▼ THE OIL-LAMP SUPPER · KARTHIGAI DEEPAM

Lamp Supper ₹1,650

Wednesday 2 October 2024 · single sitting, 7:30 PM
  • Eight hundred clay agal lamps along the entrance, lit by guests on arrival
  • Pori urundai and ven appam at the door, by hand, still warm
  • Slow-cooked country chicken kuzhambu with ven palmyra spice, hot rice
  • Adai aviyal, four-flour koothuvilakku appam, payasam of palm sugar
BOOKING Forty-eight covers only. Vegetarian table set apart on request at booking. Children welcome on adult laps.
· THE STANDING SUPPER SERIES · SINCE 2019 ·

The Kuzhambu Club

One long table. Twelve seats. The chef cooks one kuzhambu, three ways.
WHEN
Last Saturday of every month

Doors at 7:30 PM. The first course is served at 8:00 sharp; latecomers join at the next ladle. The evening closes by 10:45.

HOW IT WORKS
A single kuzhambu

— meen, kara, valli, ennai, or pulikuzhambu — is taken apart through the meal. First poured over rice, then sopped with appalam in the courtyard. The chef sits down for the third.

WHO'S ON THE LEAF
₹2,400 per head

Includes a welcome rasam, three kuzhambu courses, two rice, four side dishes, dessert, filter coffee. Wine and sodas at additional cost. Pre-payment required to hold a seat.

Upcoming dates · 25 January · 22 February · 29 March · 26 April 2025 · Daily, info@kalasha.in or call the kitchen line directly. Members of the club receive the year's twelve dates by post in early January.
