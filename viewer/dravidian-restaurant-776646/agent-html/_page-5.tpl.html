The Kalasha Calendar·Mwasa-Wasa

Feast Days & Seasons

Four times a year the temple kitchen at Kalasha bends to the planets. Harvest sun, river flood, nine nights of the goddess, and the long lamps of Karthigai — each calls for a different rice, a different kuzhambu, a different way of laying the leaf. Bookings open six weeks in advance.

A Standing Supper Service·Since 2019

The Kuzhambu Club

One long table. Twelve seats. The chef cooks one kuzhambu, three ways.

When

Last Saturday of every month

Doors at 7.30 PM. The first course is served at 8.00 sharp; latecomers join at the next table. The evening closes by 10.45.

How It Works

A single regional kuzhambu — meen, kara, vatha, ennai, or pulikuzhambu — is taken apart through the meal. First poured raw on the leaf; then sapped from a tumbler. The chef sits down for the third course.

Cover

₹2,400 per head

Includes a welcome rasam, three kuzhambu courses, two rices, four side dishes, dessert, filter coffee. Wine and toddy at additional cost. Pre-payment required to hold a seat.
