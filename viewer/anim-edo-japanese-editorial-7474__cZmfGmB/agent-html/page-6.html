No. I
Mission

On the matter
of why we print
編集方針

Tōkaidō Review is a quarterly journal devoted to the material culture of the Edo period — the years between Tokugawa Ieyasu's first arrival at the eastern marsh and the abdication of the fifteenth shogun. We publish slowly, four issues a year, on uncoated paper from the Echizen mills.

Our remit is narrow. The artisans who pulled indigo from vats at three in the morning. The post-towns whose innkeepers measured futons in bowls of rice. The ukiyo-e printers who could register seventeen colour-blocks without annoying a single hairline. We do not romanticise: we read carefully, and we cite our sources.

Founded in 2019 by Mariko Sato and a small group of former curators from the Edo-Tokyo Museum, the Review remains independent, reader-funded, and unaffiliated with any university press.
