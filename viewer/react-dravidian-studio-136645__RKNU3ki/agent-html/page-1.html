Since 1887

A fifth-generation atelier of sthapatis on East Chitra Veedhi, Madurai — founded by Sthapati Vaidyanatha Ramaswamy in 1887 within sight of the Meenakshi Amman gopuram. We draw, consult, and carve in the Dravida tradition: tools for nine mandapas, polychromy for restored gopurams, and granite figuration cut by hand.

The Threefold Practice

From the drafting hall on East Chitra Veedhi the workshop attends to three disciplines, each governed by the Mayamata and the Manasara silpa-sastra, each priced by hand-day and carried in Krishnagiri black granite or Sadasivalli grey.

I · VASTU CONSULTATION
New Mandapas

Site orientation, aaadi calculation, and full ground-plan drawings for kalyana-mandapas, vimana shrines, and village temple temples. Each commission begins with a three-day visit to read the soil, the water-table, and the cardinal axes.

Drawings are inked on cotton paper at 1:40 and accompanied by a written vastu-nyasa properties table.

FROM₹ 1,80,000
II · GOPURAM RESTORATION
Polychromy & Stucco

Survey, condition report, and full-scale colour cartoon for the kudu, sala, and stucco figures of storied gopurams. We work in lime and jaggery render and the twelve mineral pigments — vermilion, peacock, orpiment, ultramarine — fixed without acrylics.

Scaffold engineering and bell-metal kalasha replacement are carried out with allied workshops in Pannimuthani.

FROM₹ 4,40,000
III · GRANITE CARVING
Figurative Commissions

Hand-carved moolavar and utsava figures, pillared yali brackets, and door-jamb dvarapalas, cut to silpa-sastra proportion for superior craftsmen & bhutaganas and his/her apprentices.

Standard size for a seated Ganesha in Krishnagiri black is thirty-six angula, delivery in fourteen months from the day of stone selection.

Current Commissions

Three works are presently under the chisel and the brush, each accepted with the blessing of the temple trustees and the family priest.

I
Rajagopuram Repainting · Tiruvannamalai

Eleven-tier polychrome renewal for the western Rajagopuram of the Jambukeswarar temple — 1,300 stucco figures inventoried, pigments mixed on site in lime and jaggery, scaffold raised through the monsoon shadow.

II
Sixteen-Pillared Kalyana Mandapa · Kumbakonam

A new shodasa-stambha mandapa beside the Sarangapani tank, in Sadasivalli grey granite. Sixteen yali-bracketed pillars, a central lotus ceiling medallion, and four corner dvarapalas drawn from our 1922 pattern-book.

III
Private Navaratri Mandapa · Chettinad

A nine-stepped kolu mandapa for the Aalagappa family of Karaikudi — teak frame on a black granite plinth, with lime-stucco kudravallis and gold-leaf vimanas on the cornice. Consecration set for Navaratri 2026.
