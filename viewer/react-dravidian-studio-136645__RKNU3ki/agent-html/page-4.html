Selected Works · 1962 — Present

A register of principal commissions undertaken by three generations of the workshop, in the Dravida tradition, across the temple districts of Tamil Nadu.

The list below is chronological and partial. Drawings, measured surveys, and site photographs of each work are held at the workshop in Athangudi and may be consulted on appointment. Materials are recorded as built; principal artisans are named where the family record is firm.
1962Gen III
Repainting & Polychrome Restoration

Eastern Gopuram, Sri Andaleya Temple

Karaikudi, Sivaganga District

Six-tala eastern gopuram repainted in mineral pigments over a fresh lime-and-jaggery ground. Original 19th century stucco figures consolidated, not recarved. The vermilion of the kumbha-panjara band was matched to surviving traces under the cornice.

PARTICULARS

  • Sthapati S. Subramaniya Ramaswamy
  • Lime mortar (jaggery, kadukkai decoction, mineral) including stucco; saffron, kunkuma; indigo; gold leaf at 22 carat. Painters: 8.
  • 14 months of fieldwork from drawing release.
1978Gen IV
New Construction · Sixteen-Pillar Hall

New Kalyana Mandapa, Srirangam Outer Prakara

Srirangam, Tiruchirappalli

Sixteen monolithic pillars with yali bases in the Vijayanagara manner, drawn to a sixteen-square padmagarbha plan. Each yali was carved from a single block of Karaipundur charnockite; the videl bear-conch and dasras alternates along the long axis.

PARTICULARS

  • Stupathi N. Subramaniya Ramaswamy
  • Pillar sets: 16, with master pillars and corner monoliths. Carved in Karaipundur charnockite. Iconography: yali, dvarapala, ceiling lotuses, copper-flag mast.
  • Draftsmen 4. Master carvers 8.
1991Gen IV
Replacement of Vimana Vessels

Kalasha Replacement (Nine Vessels), Sri Meenakshi Sundareswarar Temple Shrine

Madurai

The nine corroded brass kalashas of the family shrine were lowered, weighed and replaced with new copper vessels gilded in twenty-three-carat leaf. The old vessels were ritually consecrated to the temple yolk after kumbabhishekam.

PARTICULARS

  • Sthapati S. Subramaniya Ramaswamy
  • Cast in panchaloha (gold, silver, copper, tin, mercury) per Kashyapa shilpa-shastra. Sealed beneath the pinnacle stone with the family seal.
  • Foundry-time 9 months. Joins set in March 1991.
2004Gen IV/V
Consecration of Seven-Tier Stucco Programme

Polychrome Consolidation of Seven Tiers, Rajagopuram

Sri Andal Temple, Srivilliputtur

Survey, scaled drawings, and consolidation of seven tiers of stucco figures across the principal rajagopuram. Earlier cement repairs were removed and replaced with traditional cudgel. Six figures were re-modelled, missing limbs were left as voids and recorded.

PARTICULARS

  • Stupathi R. Ganesa Ramaswamy
  • Tier I to VII figures: 1,432 surveyed; 84 figures recarved; 27 reset. Mineral palette and gilt at uppers.
  • Andhra-Chola steady force; nine years.
2017Gen V
New Vimana Hall · Family-Trust Sanction

Three-Tala Vimana, Chettiar Kuladevata Shrine

Athangudi, Sivagunga

A three-tala vimana raised over the existing garbhagriha for the family kuladevata. Drawn to the manasara canon at one eighth scale; the corner karnakutas carry seated lions, and the central sala carries Gajalakshmi. Consecrated at the Mahalingabhibhishekam of June 2018.

PARTICULARS

  • Sthapati R. Ganesa Ramaswamy
  • Krishnaspilu granite carving; brick and lime stucco; gold-leaf finial; teak rear-frame stone screens.
  • Three-tala kalasha ₹ 7,80,000.
