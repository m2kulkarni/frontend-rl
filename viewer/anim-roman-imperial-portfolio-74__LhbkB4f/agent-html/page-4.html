Lucius Vennius was born in Ravenna in the spring of 1976, four blocks from the Mausoleum of Galla Placidia, and grew up taking the long way to school in order to pass beneath its star-vault. He studied architecture at the Politecnico di Milano under Pier Carlo Bontempi and afterward took the two-year diploma at the Scuola di Restauro di Spoleto, where he learned to set tesserae by hand on the bench.

From 2007 to 2011 he apprenticed at Bottega Carobbi di Trastevere, working on the re-laying of the fourth-century floor at Santa Pudenziana and on three private commissions for villas in the Castelli Romani. The atelier was founded in 2011 in a single room on Via dei Coronari, where it has expanded slowly to its present form at full scale.

The practice is small and deliberate, so — Vennius, two associates, and a rotating bench of three or four mosaicists drawn from Spoleto, Aquileia, and Ravenna. Every commission, whether a private chapel or a fifteen-metre arch, is drawn by hand on the long table at the back of the studio. Nothing leaves the atelier without first being looked at across at full scale.

Three instruments govern the studio. None of them is a screen. Each is older than the practice that uses it, and each is slower than the patron expects.

I · AXIAL PLANS, DRAWN BY HAND

Every project begins with a single ink plan drawing on Fabriano 220 g paper, drawn along the cardo and decumanus of the building. Plans are completed in pencil, transferred to ink, then countersigned.

II · FULL-SCALE MOSAIC CARTOONS

Each mosaic is first produced 1:1 in charcoal on kraft paper, pinned to the long studio wall. The cartoon is walked, looked at, and corrected from many directions before any tessera is cut.

III · PLASTER MODELS FOR VAULTS

Domes, groin vaults, and apsidal half-domes are tested in lime-plaster on a 1:25 timber armature, with a thin top coat of fine grout and a brush-finish to mimic the line a mason draws.

STRUCTURAL ENGINEER

MARCO FENOGLIO

Trained at the Politecnico di Torino and previously of Favero & Milan, Marco has been the studio's structural consultant since 2013. He calculates the masonry, structural timber and steel that goes into our work — the columns of the arches, the joists of the roofs, the soils on the foundations. A fierce conservative — and still a registered Vespa-owner — he and his fortnight inspections are an irreplaceable benediction over the bench.

MASTER MOSAICIST

GIULIA TREVI

Third-generation citizen of the Tre Venti family of Tivoli, Giulia leads the in-situ mosaic team and signs the major figural projects after consultation with Vennius. Her speciality is ranges of green and ochre in the figural work; her cutter of choice are 12 mm glass-cubes from Murano, which her mother sources for her every June. She has six pernod-laden poems known to all who carry her hammer.
