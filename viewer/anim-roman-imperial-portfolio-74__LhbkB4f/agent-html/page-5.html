SCRIPTA

An irregular journal of essays, notebook entries, and book reviews — collected in the studio across the last two years and offered to colleagues, students, and the curious public.

INDEX SCRIPTORUM

LV
August
MMXXIV
MMXXIV · OCT

"Concrete Without Apology"

A reflection on board-formed concrete in the modern villa — its texture, its mottling, the residue of the wood-grain it never quite escapes. Examines work by Pier Vittorio Aureli and Tony Fretton in particular.

LIV
August
MMXXIV
MMXXIV · OCT

"The Threshold is the Argument"

On thresholds and door-cases — small rooms passed through, the pivot of the room around the entry. Re-reads Loos, with a glance at the door-case studies of Italian Pre-Raphaelites in 1860–1885.

LIII
July
MMXXIV
MMXXIV · SEPT

"On the Coffer, as Structural Honesty"

Drawn from the Vatican archive, with a list of recent uses, and a coda on the new Bibliotheca Aurelia. Considers whether the new use is a true descendant of, or a rupture with, the ceiling above the Sistine Chapel.

LII
June
MMXXIV
MMXXIV · AUG

"Travertine is a New Material"

A defence of travertine in the contemporary practice. After a recent rise in its use, by Caruso St John, by Snøhetta, by Office KGDVS, the material has begun to feel — surprisingly — current again.

LI
May
MMXXIV
MMXXIV · JUL

"As Walls is Rome"

Vault windows, structural rooms, and how the most important Roman ruins are not the most photographed.

L
April
MMXXIV
MMXXIV · JUN

"The Pediment Returns"

A tour of recent pediment-revival projects in the Anglo-Saxon practice — Robert Adam, Quinlan Terry, Francis Terry — with a note on the small Italian commune of Casalmaggiore, where the comune commissioned a small classical chapel in 2022.

XLIX
March
MMXXIV
MMXXIV · MAY

"Against the Frameless Window"

A polemic against the frameless window in the contemporary domus, on grounds aesthetic, structural, and craft-related. Plate glass should not exceed three metric, says the author, in any orientation.

XLVIII
February
MMXXIV
MMXXIV · APR

"Vesuvi, and the Patient Hand"

A note on the slowness with which the original mosaicists at Pompeii laid their floors — and how, by some indices, recent restorations have begun to approach the same time-figures.

XLVII
January
MMXXIV
MMXXIV · MAR

"A Catalogue of Bolts"

From the architect, an annotated catalogue of bolts and screws used in our practice — from common machine bolts (M12) for steel-on-steel connections, to the fine 1mm wood screws for parquet.

XLVI
December
MMXXIV
MMXXIII · FEB

"Notes from a Closed City"

A travel-essay reflecting on the contradiction between conservation and inhabitation, written from a closed Adriatic island in winter — and the fortified peninsula of Pirano on its eastern flank.

XLV
November
MMXXIV
MMXXIII · JAN

"Why We Still Draw the Order"

A defence of teaching the orders directly to students, taught by hand. Ten years on a drawing board show their value in seeing real volume across the page.

XLIV
October
MMXXIV
MMXXIII · DEC

"Vitruvius at the Hardware Store"

Notes on contemporary suppliers and the persistent appeal of the timber-yard in the modern age. Vivere & vetro, lasting matter, and the long process of finding a wood-supplier in the modern Italy.
