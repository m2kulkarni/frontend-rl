THE COMMISSIONING LEDGER

ON RECEIVING A COMMISSION

I · A NOTE ON

A NOTE ON SCOPE, TIME, & WHAT PASSES BETWEEN US.

The atelier accepts a small number of architectural and devotional commissions each year. Each project begins with a written brief, a site visit where possible, and the preparation of a one-fifth scale cartoon together with material samples drawn from our own kilns at Na'in and Yazd. Nothing is fired until the cartoon has been signed.

II · MINIMUM SCOPE

4 M² OF TILEWORK

Below this, the geometry cannot resolve into a complete cartouche; smaller objects are accepted only as gifts.

II · CRAFTING & SAMPLE

PREPARED ON AGREEMENT

A binding agreement, and a deposit of 30%, accompanies the close of cartouche; samples are kept in the studio archive.

III · LEAD TIME

6 — 14 MONTHS

From signed cartoon to installation, depending on glaze recipe, kiln cycle, and the season of humidity.

IV · INSTALLATION

BY THE ATELIER ITSELF

The master and one senior workman travel to site for setting, that master is raised in the presence.

I

LETTER, BRIEF

A written description of the surface, light, and intent. Photographs and elevations are welcomed.

II

CARTOON

A 1:5 ink drawing on paper, with a small panel of hand-samples in the proposed palette.

III

CUTTING & FIRING

The tile by hand-fired masonless slabs, a single-rolled firing, then gold-rest-glaze when called for.

IV

SETTING

On-site assembly, four limner on-paper templates, set in turn, then fingerwashed off after curing.

BIENVENUE · TASHRĪF DĀRID · WELCOME
