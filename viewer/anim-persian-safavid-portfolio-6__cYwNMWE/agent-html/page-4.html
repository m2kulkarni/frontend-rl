MIRZA REZA TABRIZĪ

Master of Tile-Mosaic, of Stucco-Muqarnas, and of Architectural Counsel

A portrait, undertaken at the threshold of the family kiln, 2017.
A Brief Life

Born in Tabrīz, 1955, into a family of carpet-knotters and copper-chasers, Mirza Reza Tabrīzī has spent thirty years recovering a single question — how the cobalt and turquoise of a Safavid wall keep their voice across four centuries — and answers it again each morning, in his tile, on the workbench of a small atelier in Jolfa.

He read mathematics at the Tehrān School of Traditional Arts before he was eighteen and apprenticed in tile-mosaic to the master Aghasi for the seven years that followed. After Aghasi's death in 1996 he settled in Isfahan and opened the workshop that is the present atelier. He has trained eleven masters since.

In 1391 H.S., the master of the atelier in Tabrīzī tile-mosaic and counsel of the Mirza Reza Tabrīzī of the Centre commission was awarded by the Ministry of Cultural Heritage. He counsels privately, and in two thousand and twenty four still teaches the band-kesh course annually for the still-six of the apprentices.

CHRONOLOGY

  • 1973

    EARLY YEARS

    Born in Tabrīz to a Sufi-Naqshbandi line of brass workers. Apprenticed at age fifteen to a copper-master in the Sang-e tarāsh quarter of the bazaar, where he is first taught the inks and the geometries of repeated forms.

  • 1984–1996

    STUDY · SCHOOL OF TRADITIONAL ARTS

    Trains in cuerda, polychrome muqarnas, calligraphic-hand of Aghasi at the Centre of the Tabrīzī tile-mosaic, in seven years.

  • 1996–2006

    APPRENTICESHIP · MASTER ALĪ FĀTEMĪ AGHASĪ, ISFAHAN

    Master Aghasī's atelier in Tabrīzī tile of seven years before establishing his own atelier in Isfahan. The fellowship of the still-twelve apprentice ten Aghasī Sherghām before the workshop's tile.

  • 2007

    ATELIER FOUNDED · ARTISTIC INHERITANCE CONSOLIDATION

    Atelier Mirza Reza is founded in Isfahan, on the Khaju lane in the Jolfa quarter. Two further apprentices are taken on. The first commission of the still-bench cartouche is assembled.

  • 2014

    RIVĀQ JUNGI MA'ALAH COSCAYAH
