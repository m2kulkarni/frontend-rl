Apply to
Hokurin.

A small public university with one undergraduate gate and three graduate ones — sought, not crowded. Hokurin admits sixty-nine undergraduates each autumn from a candidate pool of 410. Selection is by interview and a portfolio of work the candidate has actually finished.

Three Intake Windows

A

Standard Domestic Cycle

1 SEPTEMBER 2025

For high school applicants from Japanese MEXT-accredited schools. Portfolio review and a half-day on-campus interview.

B

Late Cycle & Transfers

15 JANUARY 2026

For applicants completing technical school or transferring with at least one year of equivalent humanities coursework.

C

Rolling Review

YEAR-ROUND

For international applicants and visiting researchers. Interviews held remotely; one campus visit required before matriculation.

Documents & Examination

  • REQUIREDApplication form & declaration of intent
  • REQUIREDSecondary-school transcript with seal
  • REQUIREDPortfolio of three finished works (any medium)
  • REQUIREDOne letter from a working teacher or master
  • OPTIONALProof of language proficiency (JLPT N2 or equivalent)

Entrance Examination

  • SECTION IJapanese & English — close reading, two passages
  • SECTION IIMathematics or World History (candidate's choice)
  • SECTION IIIFaculty-specific portfolio interview, 45 minutes
  • SECTION IVConversation with a current student over tea
