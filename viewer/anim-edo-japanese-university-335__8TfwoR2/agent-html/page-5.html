校舎・東山のしおり·CAMPUS & VISITING

Four buildings,
one quiet block of Higashiyama.

The Hokurin campus occupies a single deep machiya block south of Sanjō-dōri. Of the five Edo-era warehouses that once stood on the parcel, four were spared in the 1864 fires and re-roofed in 1896; the fifth was rebuilt in 1992 as a small auditorium. There is no parking; visitors arrive by foot from Sanjō Keihan, and almost every door on the property still uses a hand-cut iron lock.

The four buildings

SANJŌ HALL · KURA NORTH · KURA SOUTH · NORTH ATELIER
BUILDING 一

Sanjō Hall

The main 1762 reception hall, lectures and reading rooms on the second floor. Tatami throughout; shoes left in the genkan.

BUILDING 二

Kura North

The first of two surviving silk warehouses, now home to the Tōrin Bunko reading room and the manuscript stacks.

BUILDING 三

Kura South

Second silk warehouse, given over in 2003 to the Craft Sciences atelier — pigment lab, washi studio, and a small urushi room.

BUILDING 四

North Atelier

The 1992 rebuild, with a 110-seat auditorium below and the conservation lab above. The only building on campus with a passenger lift.

Walking directions

TWO ROUTES BY FOOT

From Higashiyama Station

Exit 2, walk north 6 minutes along Higashioji-dori, turn right at Sanjō, the gate is the second machiya on the south side.

From Sanjō Keihan

Cross the river east, walk through Higashiyama park 9 minutes, the gate is set back behind a small persimmon courtyard.

By the bus

City bus 31, 47 to "Higashiyama Sanjō" stop; the campus is 2 minutes east. The gate is unmarked except for a small lantern.

Open-campus days

18
MAY · TUESDAY

Garden & Tōrin Bunko Open Day

Two open-campus days are held twice a year; the Sanjō and Kura buildings are open without appointment from 10:00 to 16:00. A short tea ceremony in the courtyard at 13:00.

26
SEPTEMBER · SATURDAY

Whole-Block Pre-Term Open Day

All four buildings open. By registration. Visiting prospective students are paired with a current student for the morning; the afternoon is held in Japanese, English, or French.

Twice a year the campus opens its doors. A Hokuri should be made for the summer alone.
