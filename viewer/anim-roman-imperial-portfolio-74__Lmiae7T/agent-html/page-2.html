OPERA SELECTA

Six works selected from the present period — three built, two in progress, one published only in measure-drawings. Each numbered in the studio's roman ledger, where commissions enter on intake and remain.

THERMAE LUCANAE

Lucania · XII annorum

A surviving Hellenic complex on the southern coast, recompleted in 2024 after twelve years — basilical baths, a column hall, and a hard-rendered cold pool.

BUILTSUDIUM2024

DOMUS VERNNA

Roma · private

A villa restoration in the Aventine — atrium opened, peristyle replanted, and threshold mosaic conserved to current condition.

BUILTVILLA2023

QUATTUOR TEMPORA

Opus Tessellatum

A floor in cut stone for a private chapel near Trastevere — a Sun, four Seasons, and a banded border in pale Aegean blues commissioned by Pietro Lunatini.

MOSAIC4 PANELS2024

BIBLIOTHECA AURELIA

Studium privatum

A small private library for a collector in Bologna — a single barrel-vaulted reading room, a marble lectern, and a presented map drawn by hand.

STUDIUM34 m²2022

FORUM MERCATORIUM

Ravenna

An open market hall replanned and roofed for the city of Ravenna — five bays, twin loggias, and grain offices laid above. Plans completed September 2023.

CIVICFORUM2023

CENOTAPHIUM MEMORIAE

Aquileia

A travertine cenotaph honouring the stoneworkers' guild of Aquileia — bevelled corner blocks, a recessed inscription, and a quiet votive niche.

INDEX OPERUM

The studio's full ledger contains forty-three completed works, eleven in progress, and seven returns. Index entry includes drawings, photographs and the patron's letter of intake.
