Section II ◆ Wards & Administration

Twenty-Seven Wards of
Thiruvanam

The municipal limits of Thiruvanam are divided into twenty-seven elected wards across four administrative zones — Sannidhi, Kaveri, Pillaiyar Koil, and Periya Theru. Each ward returns one councillor to the General Council, which sits on the second Tuesday of every month at the Chola Hall, Town Hall complex.
