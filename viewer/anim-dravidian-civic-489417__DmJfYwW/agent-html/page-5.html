Section V · Resident Services

Services for Residents

The Authority offers thirty-eight designated services in person at the Town Hall and through eighteen e-Sevai kiosks distributed across the township. Routine certificates and registrations are issued by the seven Revenue Cells with same-day delivery.

Apply at the Town Hall Find your Ward Officer →
Service № 01 · Civil RegistrySame Day

Birth & Death Registration

Registration of births within 21 days and deaths within 30 days of the event. Late entries up to 365 days are admitted on declaration.

  • Hospital-issued certificate or burial-ground entry
  • Aadhaar & Family Card of the informant
  • Notarised statement of two witnesses if late
  • Issue of certified copy in Tamil and English
Service № 02 · Trade Licensing7 Working Days

Trade Licensing

Issue and renewal of trade licences under the Tamil Nadu District Municipalities Act for shops, eateries, manufactories and street-trade.

New trade licence (Cat A)₹ 750
New trade licence (Cat B)₹ 2,400
Annual renewal₹ 450
Late renewal surcharge10% per month
Service № 03 · Public HealthField Visit

Water Connection Applications

New domestic water connections are sanctioned within 14 days of inspection by the Public Works field officer of the relevant ward.

Domestic ½″ connection₹ 4,200
Commercial ¾″ connection₹ 9,800
Annual minimum charge₹ 600
Service № 04 · Building Plan21 Working Days

Building Plan Approvals

Sanction of plans up to 4 storeys is granted in Counter 11 within 21 working days of submission, subject to Heritage Precinct restrictions where applicable.

  • Residential up to 200 sq.m — same-day acknowledgement
  • Heritage Precinct submissions — Heritage Cell sign-off required
  • Commercial & industrial — Standing Committee approval
  • Renewal of lapsed sanctions accepted within 12 months

Grievance Darbar Hotline

Open to citizens 24 hours. The Grievance Officer on duty registers complaints and forwards them to the appropriate cell within one working day. Spoken Tamil, English and Telugu equally entertained.
