Section VI · Visit the Town Hall

Visit the Town Hall
at 14 Sannidhi Veethi

A grand, cool stone edifice of 1894, raised by Sir John Reynolds at the time of his retirement from the Madras Presidency. The Town Hall is the heart of public administration: where the Council sits, where the registers are stored, where every resident may observe the daily working of the Authority.

II · Entrance & Wayfinding

Finding the Right Door

The Town Hall is built around a central courtyard, with four entrances aligning with the cardinal directions. Each is reserved by long custom for a particular class of business.

Mukha Vasal · EastPublic reception · enquiries · daily certificates
Sannidhi Veethi · WestCouncil, Commissioner & closed sittings
Kavalakkārar · SouthPleadings, objections & written representations
Heritage Lane · NorthHeritage Cell · Antiquities Counter

Visitors are requested to remove footwear at the threshold of the Council Chamber, in keeping with the practice observed since the building was first occupied.

III · Approach

Walking from the Infused Bus Stand

Approximate 600 metres · 8 minutes at an unhurried pace. The route is shaded by tamarind trees the year round.

  1. Exit the bus stand at the East Gate, opposite the Sannidhi Pillar.
    Bear East · 80 m
  2. Cross at the painted causeway. The Town Hall portico is visible at the far end of Sannidhi Veethi.
    Continue West · 320 m
  3. Pass the four-storey Vinayagar Koil on the right; the Heritage Lane joins from the north.
    Continue West · 180 m
  4. Arrive at the East Gate (Mukha Vasal) of the Town Hall.
    Reception Counter
IV · The Council Chamber

From the Hearing Hall

The Council Chamber, panelled in teak by the firm of Whyte & Daughters of Madras (1893), seats forty-seven members on the floor and forty visitors in the public gallery above.

  • · Sittings are open. Tokens issued at the West Gate from 09:00.
  • · The press is admitted to the panelled press gallery.
  • · Citizens may inspect the published verbatim from the next day.
  • · Photography is forbidden during sittings; sketching is welcome.
  • · The Sergeant-at-Arms attends to all queries on the floor.
V · Hospitality

For Every Visitor

Drinking water

Earthen pots renewed thrice a day at each entrance.

Wheelchair Access

A ramped approach is laid at the East Gate; an attendant assists.

Cloak Room

Bags and umbrellas may be left at the Hospitality Desk.

Reading Room

Open till 17:30. Tamil & English daily papers, all gazettes since 1947.

Tea Room

Filter coffee & pori at the Old Block kitchen, 10:00–16:00.

Lost & Found

Held at the Sergeant's Lodge for 30 days; thereafter sealed.

Need a better appointment?

The Commissioner sits in audience on the third Tuesday of every month, 14:00 to 16:00, by prior appointment lodged through the Secretary's Office. Citizens of any standing are welcome.
