SCRIPTA

A loose register of essays, marginalia, and atelier announcements — published roughly when the work allows. Notes from the studio, the road to the quarry, and the floors as they are laid.
INDEX SCRIPTORUM
AUGUST · MMXXIV
28AUG · 2024

"Concrete Without Apology"

An informal apology for the use of concrete in the post-classical idiom — argued in the longer form of an essay through five mosaics, three wall murals, and the courtyard of the Casa Nueva di Trastevere recently completed by the studio.

14AUG · 2024

"The Threshold is the Argument"

Notes on the threshold pavement — that bordered narrow zone every classical building places where it ends and the world begins. A pictorial argument across five examples, from Aquileia to the Lapis Niger.

JULY · MMXXIV
29JUL · 2024

"On the Coffer, as Structural Honesty"

Why the studio prefers the coffered ceiling — for its candor about how the load is borne — to the modern flat slab. Examples through three completed and one in-progress drawing of the chapel near Genoa.

11JUL · 2024

"Travertine is a Slow Material"

The longer time-frame travertine demands of a quarry, of an architect, and of a building. Notes on the patience native to a stone that fundamentally cannot be hurried, with three field-photographs from Tivoli.

JUNE · MMXXIV
18JUN · 2024

"As Walls is Rome"

A short pictorial essay on the walls in the work of the studio, in conversation with the brick and travertine walls of the Roma we walk to the studio through every morning. Walls also as the slow record of those who built them.

02JUN · 2024

"The Pediment Returns"

A response — overdue — to a friend's quietly devastating critique of the studio's continued use of the pediment in private commissions. A defense, in three points, of why the pediment is not merely an inheritance but a continuing argument.

MAY · MMXXIV
22MAY · 2024

"Against the Frameless Window"

A polemic against the contemporary preference for the frameless window — the loss of the architrave, the moulding, the sill, all banished as "too busy." A short defense, instead, of the deep dressed reveal as the most articulate edge a building offers a room.

APRIL · MMXXIV
17APR · 2024

"Rosae, and the Patient Hand"

From the apprentice's notebook for the spring of 2024. The mosaic Saturday on the back rosary at the Tarsi in Trastevere, in five parts. A coloured plate of the underdrawing in red ochre.

04APR · 2024

"A Catalogue of Beds"

A semi-serial inventory of bedchambers the studio has designed since 2017 — with measured plans and four interior elevations. The smallest at 11.4 sqm, the largest at 41.

MARCH · MMXXIV
26MAR · 2024

"Notes from a Closed City"

Notes from a brief residency at the Soprintendenza Archeologica in Aquileia, where the studio's principal spent three weeks of February among the silent winter excavations.

FEBRUARY · MMXXIV
19FEB · 2024

"Why We Still Draw the Order"

A defense of teaching the classical orders — as a strict, slow grammar that disciplines proportion before ornament. The studio's annual two-week January reading of Vitruvius for the new associates.

JANUARY · MMXXIV
10JAN · 2024

"Vitruvius at the Hardware Store"

The principal's running record of the prices and what each is graded for of the timber, lime, sand, and Carso pozzolana available in February 2024 at the Faraone yards on the Via Appia. With prices and gradings, in the spirit of the original Decem Libri.

RECEPTIO & CORRESPONDENCE

Notices of new written essays may be requested by sending a brief introduction and the address to which they should be directed. The studio prefers slow correspondence, and its pages are neither indexed nor algorithmically ranked.
