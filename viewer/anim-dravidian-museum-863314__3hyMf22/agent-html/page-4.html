PLAN YOUR VISIT

The museum occupies the restored Mangalam‑pārkar house on the eastern bank of the Pālaru‑ru, a short walk from the Bṛhadīśvara temple complex. All ten galleries, the Gopuram Hall, the sculpture courtyard and the Dr. R. Ramanaswamy Reference Library are open to the public on a single ticket. We welcome scholars, students, pilgrims, and the merely curious.

ACCESSIBILITY

Step free entry from Bund Road through the western gate. A lift serves all three floors of the principal galleries. The Gopuram Hall is reached by a long ramp added during the 2014 restoration.

PHOTOGRAPHY

Hand‑held photography is permitted in all permanent galleries without flash or tripod. Tripod and oversize lenses by curator's permission, applied for in writing two days in advance.

IN THE GALLERIES

Visitors are asked to speak softly, leave broad scarves or display tassels in the cloakroom, and not to touch the inscribed plates. The standard ground rules apply.

NEAR ART

Commercial, scholarly, and tripod‑mounted photography requires a permit (₹100 per day) issued in the curator's office. Forty‑five minutes before closing the galleries are cleared on a regular schedule.

CONSERVATION

Food, drink, and tobacco are not permitted beyond the cloakroom. Visitors entering with packs more than 18 cm should consult the cloakroom of the museum staff.

THE REFERENCE LIBRARY

DR. R. RAMANATHAN ROOM · ESTABLISHED 1971

The on‑site reference library holds approximately 18,400 volumes on South Indian architecture, epigraphy and ritual practice, together with the museum's run of the Annals of the Pālaru Trust (1962 — present), and the unpublished field notebooks of Mahodiā D. Ramanāthāḥ filed continuously by the Tanjavur Big Temple restoration of 1958. Holdings include first editions of Gangoua Mukandi's Architecture du Sud‑de l'Inde (Paris, 1914), Ramachandra's three pamphlet on Pallava architecture, the complete run of the South Indian Inscriptional series and the runaway gazette to the Tamil epigraphical instalments.

The library is open to readers holding a valid scholar's pass. No material constitutes; photocopying is restricted; digital reproduction is offered at reasonable fees.
