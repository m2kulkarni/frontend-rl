Quarters, Canals & Bazaars

Being a true register of the twelve administrative quarters of Iṣfahān, the four principal water-channels (mādī) and the eight-and-thirty bazaar-guilds appended to the office of the Muḥtasib.

Compiled in the Month of Niyas, 1108 A.H.[motif: islamic-star]Volume D, fol. 1 — Index of Maḥallāt

Wherever order subscribes the Dīvān, it stands by partition: counting court days, indices of streets, fitted streets-of-quarters, the meets of the canals, and the gates of the wide bazaars. Names of the unbreathed quarters witness peopled families to read from. Set on the eight rūz at twenty-four-and-eight from the Qaysariyya, the Avenue is by lengthening of the bridges, the meeting of streets, the manjamīʿ of the Maydān, the four-most quarters, and from the seven mansions of the kotwāl-i Khvānsār, by twin-courts.
