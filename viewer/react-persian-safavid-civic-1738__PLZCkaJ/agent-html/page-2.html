Register II ✶ Aḥkām va ʿArāʾiż

Edicts & Public Notices

From the bureau of the Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān, Iṣfahān

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

By the grace of the Mubārakah! and under the seal of the Dīvān-i Shahr, the following decisions and house proclamations were affixed to the gates of the Qaysariyya, the Masjid-i Jāmiʿ, and the four cornerstones of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, beginning the fourteenth day of Rabīʿ al-Awwal in the year one thousand-one-hundred-and-eight of the Hijra. Citizens of the Jūbāra, Dardasht, ʿAbbās-ābād, and Bīdābād quarters are charged to attend their reading.

15 Rabīʿ al-Awwal 1108 · 12 October 1696

On the Standard Weight of the Common Loaf (nān-i sangak)

Issued by Mīrzā ʿAbd al-Rasūl, Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān

Be it known to every baker (nānvāʾī) of this fair city, that by command of the Dīvān, the loaf of sangak shall not weigh less than two-thirds of a Tabrīzī when drawn fresh from the oven. The price is fixed at two diāṅs and a half. Any baker found light by the inspector's scale shall forfeit one day's flour to the poor of the Masjid-i Jāmiʿ.

[motif: islamic-star]

17 RABĪ' AL-AWWAL 1108 · 14 October 1696

Repair of the Public Fountain (saqqā-khāna) in the Jūbāra Quarter

Issued by Ūstad Ḥājjī ʿAlī, Inspector of Waters

The qanāt-fed fountain at the head of the Jūbāra Quarter, beguildedby Hayim Khwājah, having fallen into ruin since the floods of Muḥarram, is hereby ordered restored. Master mason Ūstad Jaʿfar of Aẓdīrān is appointed to the cost of forty-two tūmāns shall be drawn from the wakil of the late Mīrzā Sajāl. Residents of Jūbāra are forbidden to draw upon the cisterns of the Madrasa-yi Chahār Bāgh in the interim.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
[motif: islamic-star]
Farmān No.
03

19 RABĪ' AL-AWWAL 1108 · 16 October 1696

Prohibition of Grazing within the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān

Issued by Mīrzā Faraj ad-Dīn, Warden of the Royal Maydān

No man shall lead sheep, goat, mule, nor camel upon the gravel of the Royal Square between the Qaysariyya gate and the Masjid-i Shāh. The footstones of the southern village shall pass yearly by the orient of Darvāza-yi Ḥassan-ābād. A first transgression carries a fine of two ʿabbāsīs; the second, the confiscation of the beast for the royal stables.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
[motif: islamic-star]
Farmān No.
04

22 RABĪ' AL-AWWAL 1108 · 19 October 1696

On the Sweeping of the Bāzār-i Buzurg before the Friday Prayer

Issued by Āqā Bahā, Warden of the Qaysariyya

Each shopkeeper of the long bazār, from his door to his neighbour's door, must sweep before the call to prayer of the morning. Any guild may submit objection of the like through their kalāntar, and the Bureau shall hear cause to the riot at the riāʾh's expense.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
[motif: islamic-star]
Farmān No.
05

24 RABĪ' AL-AWWAL 1108 · 21 October 1696

Concerning the Quality of Indigo Sold in the Sarāʾ-yi Gūlbān

Issued by Mīrzā Bāqir, Examiner of Dyes

Merchants importing indigo of Bandar and of Sashlīq shall present such bale to the dye-examiner before opening sale. Adulteration with charcoal or with the lees of pomegranate is forbidden, the penalty being the burning of the parcel in the maydān and a fine of five tūmāns to the treasury of the Dīvān.

Copied truly from the great register of the Dīvān-i Shahr; this fair sheet is bound for the wall of the Qaysariyya gate, that no man of the city may plead ignorance of its commands.

— Mīrzā Yaḥyā, scribe of the third desk
