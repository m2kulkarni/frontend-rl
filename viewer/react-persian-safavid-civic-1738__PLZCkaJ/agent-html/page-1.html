Dīvān-i Shahr-i Iṣfahān

Civic Register · Bureau of Records of the Royal City

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

Be it known to the residents of the Naqsh-e Jahān quarters, the bāzāris of the Qaysariyya, and all householders within the watered districts of this royal city, that the gate of this Bureau stands open from dawn prayer until the closing of the great doors at sunset. Any matter pertaining to taxation, to the lodgings of pilgrims, to water and tax, and lineage are kept in good order by the diligence of the people and the dignity of the Crown.

Sealed this seventeenth day of Ṣafar al-Awwal, by hand ofMīrzā Ḥusayn Khān ShīrāzīMayor of the Quarters · Kalāntar-i Maḥallāt

Effective 16 Rabīʿ al-Awwal · 21 Rabīʿ al-Awwal

Allotment Schedule for the Madī Nāṣirīm Canal

The mādī has set the rotation: gardens of Jūbāra to flow on Saturday, the orchards of Bīd Ābād to release on Monday, the orchard beyond the Khājū bridge to draw on Wednesday. Dispatches shall be heard at the canal house each morning before the call to prayer.

Effective Now · Ends 19 Rabīʿ al-Awwal

Caravan-Tax Filing Window Now Open

Receipts taken until 19 Rabīʿ al-Awwal. A three-percent rebate is afforded for those who submit before sundown. Merchants returning from Tabrīz, Shīrāz, and the Gulf ports must present manifests to the Customs Hall behind the Maydān. Silks are assessed at four-and-twenty per hundred dirhams; indigo and madder at one-tenth. Bring two witnesses and the seal of your caravan-bāshī.

Until Furqān Khulāʿat · 21 Rabīʿ al-Awwal

Observance for the Chief of the Cloth-Bazaar

The shops of the Qaysariyya silk-row shall close at midday for three days in honour of the late chief. The Bureau procession departs from the Masjid's prayers; condolences are received in the family courtyard at the Dārābāzin quarter. May his memory mount among the foster-fathers.

Hours of the Dīvān ✶ Locations of Record

Chief Halls, north arcade of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, opposite the gate of the Cnemonium of Sayyid Yūsuf. Open daily save Friday, from the dawn azan to the setting of the sun. Subordinate registers are kept at the Madrasa-yi Chahār Bāgh and at the Bridge of Allāhverdī Khān.
