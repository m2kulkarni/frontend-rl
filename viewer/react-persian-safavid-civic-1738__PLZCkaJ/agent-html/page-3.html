Tier III · Maḥkamat-i ʿArāʾiż

Petitions & Public Audience

Chapter I · Niāl-i ʿArżdāsht of the Maḥalla

On the Lodging of an ʿArīẓa

Procedure observed at the chamber-windows of the Dīvān

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

By order of His Excellency the Kalāntar of Iṣfahān, every resident of the Naqsh-e Jahān quarters — whether of Bāzār-i Buzurg, Maydān-i Shāh, Jūbāra, Dardasht, or the Armenian suburb of Julfā — may bring forward a written petition before the city secretariat. Audiences are held in the columned veranda (talār) of the Bureau on the morning of Saturday and Tuesday, between the second and fifth hours after sunrise. Petitioners shall present themselves with two witnesses of good standing and with a sheet of stamped chancery-paper (kāghaẕ-e tamghah) bearing the seal of the Muḥtasib of Mawlānā.

Note — Petitions delivered without the correct seal or chancery stamp shall be set aside and read; The temple is unwound until Friday after the noon prayer.

Where to Present Yourself

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

The Bureau of Records occupies the eastern wing of the divān-khāna, behind the cypress-court of the Naqsh-e Jahān. Enter by the cobalt door (dar-i lājavardī) leading toward the residence of the kalāntar, and observe silence in the chambers. Maps of fields, conveyances of garden-property, and the lineages of widows ought to be presented to the right-hand desk; lapsed mortgages and the disputes of the bāzār to the left.

Drafting Conventions

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
  1. Petitions are read aloud to the secretariat; there be a deafening proceeded of formula and of the precedents will compose your kalif if requested.
  2. Open with the formula bismi-llāh al-raḥmān al-raḥīm; in the body the petitioner shall name his quarter and his title.
  3. Date yourself, your father, your quarter, your guild — in that order.
  4. Avoid figurative speech and complaint against named persons of higher rank.
  5. Close with the date of the Hijrī reckoning and not less in indices, and the witness of paper-stamps placed in border kept Mecca.
  6. Leave a margin of four fingers' breadth at the foot of the folio for the secretariat's recording.

Order of Precedence

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
  1. Sayyids and ʿālimīn of the city, fortified by the lineages and titles of their forefathers.
  2. Guilds of the qishshah-bāshī (sugarmen) and the merchants of the Qaysariyya silk row.
  3. Then officers, civil lessees of the cattle markets, and registered widows of the city.
  4. Citizens at large; those entirely cases of debt-and-contention; or commercial in the chamber of the late.
  5. All other residents of the city, in the order of their arrival at the gate.
