Est. Madurai · 1887

Selected Works · 1962 — Present

A register of principal commissions undertaken by three generations of the workshop, in the Dravida tradition, across the temple districts of Tamil Nadu.

The List Below is Chronological and Partial

Drawings, measured surveys, and site photographs of each work are held at the workshop in Athangudi and may be consulted on appointment. Materials are recorded as built; principal artisans are named where the family record is firm.

1962EST
Restoration & Polychrome Restoration

Eastern Gopuram, Sri Andamta Temple

Karaikal, Karaikal • Singalmudi District

Six-tala eastern gopuram repainted in mineral pigments over a fresh lime-and-jaggery ground. Original 19th-century stucco figures consolidated, not recarved. The vermilion of the kumbha-panjara band was matched to surviving traces under the cornice.

Principal Notes
  • Sthapati S. Subramaniam
  • 1,800 hours; nine craftsmen
  • Lime mortar; jaggery binder; brick-dust, malachite and arsenical-yellow pigments
  • Consecrated in the presence of the Mahanteyacharya
  • In Sastra: Manasara XII
1978EST
New Construction · Sixteen-pillar Hall

New Kalyana Mandapa, Srirangam Outer Prakara

Srirangam, Tiruchirappalli

Sixteen monolithic pillars with yali bases in the Vijayanagara manner, drawn to a sixteen-square padmanagaha plan. Each yali was carved from a single block of Karapatkudi charcoal-rock, the riders bear-cinch and shoes alternating along the long axis.

Principal Notes
  • Sthapati S. Aruvachalam
  • Karapakudi black granite from the Periyar quarry; pulse-mortar pointing
  • Awarded the Tamil Nadu Akademi Honour for new architecture in granite
  • Karyakarta R. Krishnan, Devasthanam Trust
1991EST
Replacement of Pierced Lattice

Kalasha Replacement (Nine Vessels), Sri Meenakshi Sundareswarar Private Shrine

Theni

The nine corroded brass kalashas of the family shrine were lowered, weighed, and replaced with new copper vessels gilded in twenty-three carat leaf. The old vessels were ritually consecrated in the temple tank after kumbhabhishekam.

Principal Notes
  • Sthapati S. Subramaniam (consult)
  • Copper vessel cast at Swamimalai
  • Twenty-three karat gold-leaf finish; thinned at the rim by hand
  • Consecrated in the presence of the family priest
  • Workshop hours total: 320
2004EST
Consecration of Seven-Tier Stucco Programme

Polychrome Consolidation of Seven Tiers, Rajagopuram

Sri Andal Temple, Srivilliputhur

Survey, scaled drawings, and consolidation of seven tiers of stucco figures across the principal rajagopuram. Earlier cement repairs were removed and replaced with traditional vajra-mortar. No figures were re-modelled; missing limbs were left as voids and recorded.

Principal Notes
  • Sthapati S. Aruvachalam
  • 1,820 hours of pigment work; 47 painters
  • Lime mortar; mineral pigments; mantra of the Naishadha-mukha
  • Consecrated in the presence of the Mahanteyacharya
2017EST
New Vimana over Family Deity Sannithi

Three-Tala Vimana, Chettiar Kuladevata Shrine

Athangudi, Sivaganga District

A three-tala vimana raised over the existing garbhagriha for the family kuladevata. Drawn to the manasara canon at one eighth-scale, the corner kantakaras carry seated lions, and the central tala carries Gajalakshmi. Consecrated at the Mahakumbhabhishekam of June 2018.

Principal Notes
  • Sthapati S. Gabela
  • 1,200 hours of carving across 11 carvers and three apprentices
  • Krishna-shila black granite from the Karkala beds, ground-level slab in pink Kanchipuram
  • Brick core, vajra-mortar render, pigment thrown but not gilded
