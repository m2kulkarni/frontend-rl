Since 1887

A fifth-generation atelier of Sthapatis on East Chitra Veedhi, Madurai — founded by Sthapati Vaidyanatha Ramaswamy in 1887 within sight of the Meenakshi Amman gopurams. We draw, consult, and carve in the Dravida tradition: tools for the mandapas, polychromy for moneyed gopurams, and granite figuration cut by hand.

Estd. 1887 · Madurai

The Threefold Practice

From the drafting hall on East Chitra Veedhi the workshop attends to three disciplines, each governed by the Manasara and the Manusara silpa-sastras, each priced by hand-day and carried in Krishnagiri black granite or Sadasivalli grey.

I · Vastu Consultation
New Mandapas

Site orientation, axial calculation, and full ground-plan drawings for kalyana mandapas, vastu mandapas, ranga shrines, and village mantapa temples. Each commission begins with a three-day visit to read the soil, the water-table, and the cardinal axes.

Drawings are inked on cotton paper at 1:48 and accompanied by a written sila stuva proportion table.

From₹ 1,80,000
II · Gopuram Restoration
Polychromy & Stucco

Survey, condition report, and full-scale colour cartoons for the kudu, sikha, and stucco figures of storied gopurams. We work in lime and jaggery render and the twelve mineral pigments — vermilion, peacock, orpiment, ultramarine — fixed without acrylics.

Scaffold engineering and bell-metal kalasha replacement are carried out with allied workshops in Swamimalai.

From₹ 4,40,000
III · Granite Carving
Figurative Commissions

Hand-carved sandstone and stone figures, pillared juli brackets, and door-jamb dwarapalakas, cut to silanya susu proportion by master craftsman R. Murugesan and his five apprentices.

Standard size for a seated Ganesha in Krishnagiri black is thirty-six angula, delivery in fourteen months from the day of stone selection.

From₹ 4,25,000

Current Commissions

Rajagopuram Repainting · Tiruvannamalai

Eleven-tier polychromy renewal for the western Rajagopuram of the Jambukeswara temple — 1,400 stucco figures inventoried, pigments mixed on site in lime and jaggery, scaffold raised through the monsoon shadow.

Sixteen-Pillared Kalyana Mandapa · Kumbakonam

A new chukkam mandapa raised beside the Sarangapani tank, in Sadasivalli grey granite. Sixteen yali-bracketed pillars, a central lotus ceiling medallion, and four corner Dwarapalakas drawn from our 1925 pattern-book.
