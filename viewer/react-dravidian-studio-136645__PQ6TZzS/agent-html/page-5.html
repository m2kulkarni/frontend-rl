The Drafting Hall
& Stone Yard

No. 14, Eastvilai Veedhi, Kumbakonam — a 1905 stone-and-tile address of working artisans, opened daily save the new moon.

The Inner Court

I — The Open Courtyard

The workshop occupies a single-storey stone-and-tile structure raised in 1905 by Sthapati Ramaswamy the elder. Six plans climbed around an open central courtyard fifty-two feet by forty, paved in Cuddapah black and washed each dawn with river water and turmeric. Three precincts open from the four cardinal sides: the drafting hall to the east, the carving yard to the south, and the polychromy room to the west; the northern wing is reserved for the household shrine.

Senior Craftsmen

Sthapati R. Mukundra, principal

Sthapati R. Pulla Aachari, draftsmaster

II — The Drafting Hall

Within the eastern hall the workshop is kept. Older monument students — manual silla proportions, gopuram elevations, vihala-vinayakam plans — be benlded between palm-leaf covers tied with red cotton cord; later sheets, on cartridge paper drawn between 1920 and the present day, are rolled and stored in twenty-two teakwood pigeonholes labelled in Tamil script.

III — The Carving Yard

Eleven blocks of Krishnagiri granite stand in the southern yard beneath cane awnings braced against the monsoon. The largest — block no. 7, intended for a yali-bracket commission for the Tirumayur temple committee — measures four feet eighty inches at its base. Hammers, chisels of nine gauges, and the netra-stones for setting work are arranged on the south bench in the order taught by the master.

Commission Note

Krishnagiri block 4 (gauge: 4'10) cubed — for Selva-Anganai temple kalasha, 2022.

IV — The Polychromy Room

In the western chamber, pigments are still ground in stone mortars by hand. Vermillion is prepared from cinnabar imported from Cojeg; the blue, a lapis substitute of indigo and white lead, follows the recipe recorded in the 1923 workbook of Karman Pillai's grandfather. The yellow is turmeric remediated, fixed in saturated gum. No synthetic colour enters this room.

Pigments Stocked

Vermillion (kungilyaa), indigo & lead, mineral red, turmeric yellow, lamp-black, gold leaf at 23ct.
