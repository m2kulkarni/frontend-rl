ADMISSIONS 入学のご案内

Apply to
Hokurin.

Hokurin admits a small undergraduate cohort and a graduate cohort across three windows — Aug, Oct, and Mar — and accepts applications from all jurisdictions. The university values portfolios over standardised scores; international students are welcome and supported by the East Asia Centre.

Three Intake Windows

— INTAKE I

Standard Domestic Cycle

1 SEPTEMBER 2025

For Japanese-resident applicants entering through portfolio review and a one-day interview at the Sanjō Gate. Decisions issued early November.

— INTAKE II

Late Cycle & Transfers

15 OCTOBER 2025

For students transferring from another university or with a delayed dossier. Subject to seat availability across the three faculties.

— INTAKE III

Rolling Review

31 MARCH 2026

For international and special-circumstance applicants. Cases are reviewed weekly by the registrar; remote interviews are accepted.

Documents & Examination

Documents

  • Application form (願)Filed online; signature page returned by post.
  • Secondary transcriptOriginal sealed copy in Japanese or English.
  • Statement of intent800–1,200 characters in Japanese; 600 words in English.
  • Portfolio (作品)Six items, scanned. May include text, drawings, or recordings.
  • Photograph & family registerRecent passport-style photo; juminhyō or equivalent.
  • Application fee¥ 18,000 — waived for fee-relief applicants.

Entrance Examination

  • Japanese & EnglishCommon reading and short composition. Two hours.
  • Methodology coreChoice of one: kanbun precis, environmental log, or material observation.
  • Mathematics or World HistoryCommon, with calculator permitted in math section.
  • Faculty Block · writtenDiscipline-specific. Students sit only their declared faculty's exam.
  • Faculty Block · conversationTwenty-minute interview with two faculty members the following day.
