Counsel & Provision

Access, Groups & Schools

Accessibility

The Inner Atrium and the Pavilion Hall, accessible from the entry court, are wholly step-free across an early stone threshold from the south. The Iwan-Reconstruction terrace is reached by short stair to the rear of Hall VII; portable ramps are available on application.

Group Bookings

Groups from ten to twenty-eight are accepted by booking. The Museum offers themed tours of the Permanent Collection, the Conservation Studio, and the lapidarium of Reza Abbasi (90 minutes). All bookings made through the Counsel Desk in the entrance bay.

Schools & Universities

Cellular school visits are admitted free upon application from teachers in seventy-two hours' advance. The Isfahan Public Lycée and the Sciences Pédagogie of the Bonyad have priority booking on Wednesdays. Refresher sessions on application.
