A Brief Chronicle of the Museum

1971
Founded

By decree of the Cultural Heritage Commission, with a founding gift of forty-two objects from the personal collection of Mahnaz Bahari Birjandi.

1983
Re-Established

Reopens after the Revolution under the new auspices of the Iranian Cultural Heritage Organization, with the four halls expanded to seven.

2008
Iwan Recovered

The Khwāju Wing opens, the iwan reconstructed in full from the Khordād conservation campaign of 1968. Endowment of the Royal Atelier Library.

A Reading of Purpose

Charter of the Museum

The Museum of Isfahan exists to gather, conserve, and place before the close eye of any visitor those works in which the makers of the Safavid age conducted the empire's argument with itself: in cobalt, in silk, in steel, and in line.

It is held in trust by a charter granted in 1971 and renewed every twelve years, most recently in 2023, on the recommendation of the Cultural Heritage Commission and with the assent of the City of Isfahan.

The Museum holds itself to two principles: first, that the looking is the lesson; and second, that the conservation of an object is the conservation of the labour that made it. To the first we owe our gallery design; to the second we owe our atelier, our library, and our press.

Renewed by Charter — XII March 2023

Office of the Director

Office of the Director

Dr. Hossein Mohabbati

Director · Since 2019

Formerly Senior Curator of the Pars-Aboud Hall at the Museum of Iranian Art, Tehran. A specialist of architectural ceramics, with a doctoral thesis on the chemistry of fritware glazes 1450–1750.

Dr. Pardīs Kermānī

Deputy Director · Since 2021

Trained at the Aga Khan Museum in Toronto and the Université de Lyon, with a particular eye for textile-and-paper conservation. Author of Lustreware Returned (2022).
