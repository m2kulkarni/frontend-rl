— OPUS TESSERAE —

THE SOURCING OF TESSERAE

MARMOR LUNENSE

White Carrara from the historic quarry, cut and trimmed to the Roman half-Roman-foot. The yellowed grain registers true under afternoon glaze and the patron's southern lamp.

SMALTI · MURANO

Glass tesserae produced for us in eighty-three plate batches by a single furnace on Murano, the same family who supplied the Gianicolo restoration of nineteen-eighty-six.

PORPHYRY

Imperial porphyry, sourced from old quarry-stocks at Mons Porphyrites, used sparingly in the central rota of solar discs. Each batch documented by certificate.

CONSERVATION METHOD

VII

Each conservation begins with a measured scale survey, plotted on linen at one-twentieth and traced over by hand. The lacunae are catalogued, counted, photographed at slanting raking-light, and recorded in a bound volume kept on file with the patron.

The replacement tesserae are then cut to match, in colour and bedding, the original Roman setters' hand. We document each insertion in the registry — colour, batch, quarry, depth — so that future conservators may distinguish the new from the antique.

THREE CASE STUDIES

VILLA QUINTILIA · SOLAR DISC

The central solar disc had been lost to a Renaissance footing trench. We laid the missing tesserae in matched Aegean blues and Sicilian reds — eight thousand of the smaller cubes set in a single working week by four hands, mortar mixed each morning to the proportion attested in Vitruvius VII.
