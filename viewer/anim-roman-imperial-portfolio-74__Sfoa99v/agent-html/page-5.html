PARS · V · COMMENTARII
SCRIPTA

A chronological registry of essays, lectures, and field-notes by L. Vennius — written in close compass to the practice's working work; offered here to readers who care to study the building grammar by which we work.

INDEX SCRIPTORUM
— MMXXIV · MENSIS —
22OCTOBRIS

"Concrete Without Apology"

A defence of Roman opus caementicium in the contemporary moment, drawn from a January 2024 lecture at the Bibliotheca dell'Aretina. The argument is that the Romans built — and we build — for the chronologies that we cannot see.

04OCTOBRIS

"The Threshold is the Argument"

On the politics of the Roman vestibule and what we may learn by studying its eight survivals. Notes from the Domus Lunelli site, prepared for the Conservation Bulletin (winter issue).

— MMXXIV · SEPTEMBRIS —
28SEPTEMBRIS

"On the Coffer, as Structural Honesty"

Reading the chamfered coffers of the basilica of Maxentius via the Pantheon's of-old. The argument: a coffer is the lightest unit of an arch known to the language, and we forget at our cost.

14SEPTEMBRIS

"Travertine is a Slow Material"

Field-notes from the Tivoli quarry yard, with a brief on the time-span of fresh travertine and what we ask in expecting it to behave like a finished material from day one.

02SEPTEMBRIS

"To Walk a Stone"

A short letter to a young architect on the discipline of the visited site: that no plan is to be drawn unfinished by the walking eyes of those who must defer to the floor of it.

— MMXXIV · AUGUSTI —
30AUGUSTI

"The Pediment Returns"

On the late twentieth-century re-engagement with the broken pediment, traced from the Mannerist Vignola through Stirling and Tessenow to the present moment.

12AUGUSTI

"Against the Frameless Window"

A polemic against the contemporary mode of the frameless window: what is hidden, what is broken, what is forfeit when an opening is shorn of its margin.

— MMXXIV · IULII —
25IULII

"Vesuvi, and the Patient Hand"

From the lava-stained desks at Pompeii: a meditation on how mosaicists of the first century anticipated the tools of the present, and what a contemporary practitioner draws from the patience of those hands.

08IULII

"A Catalogue of Beds"

A correct and comparative reading of the cold-bedding of the floor mosaics at Sant'Anselmo and the warm bedding of the same floors set later. The two registers diverged in the twelfth century — and they should.

— MMXXIV · IUNII —
19IUNII

"Notes from a Closed City"

Notes on the architectural deficits of a quarantine, written during the closure of summer twenty-twenty in Genoa. Three architects share their journals at the time.

02IUNII

"Why We Still Draw the Order"

A short defence of the orders as units of measure — not as fashion or quote, but as a unit of proportion that retains its currency among those who measure with the human eye still.

— MMXXIV · MAII —
14MAII

"Vitruvius at the Hardware Store"

An afternoon reflection on the persistence of the Vitruvian triad as a discipline at the bench: where firmness is buying the right batch of mortar, where utility is the gradient of the floor that visitors will step over, where venustas is something one earns at the end.
