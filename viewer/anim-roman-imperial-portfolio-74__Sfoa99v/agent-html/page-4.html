L·UCIUS VENNIUS

Lucius Vennius was born in Ravenna in the spring of 1976, four blocks from the Mausoleum of Galla Placidia, and grew up taking the long way to school in order to pass beside its tax-vault. He studied architecture at the Politecnico di Milano under Pier Carlo Bontadosi and afterward took the two-year diploma at the Scuola di Restauro al Quintilio, where he learned to set tesserae by hand on the bench at Murano in opus vermiculatum.

From 2007 to 2010 he apprenticed at Bottaggi-Carafoli II Tirellanum, working on the re-laying of the fourth-century floors at Santa Pudenziana and on three private commissions for villas in the Castelli Romani. The atelier was founded in 2011 in a low-vaulted ground-floor on the Via dei Coronari, where it has expanded in 2017 to a large light-filled studio of full scale.

The practice is small and deliberately so — Vennius, two associates, and a rotating bench of three or four invariates drawn from Spoleto, Aquileia, and Ravenna. Every commission, whether a private hand of forty columns or the smallest civic chapel, leaves the atelier with the same drawn signature in his ink at full scale.

Three instruments govern the studio. None of them is a crown. Each is older than the practice that uses it, and each is slower than the patron expects.

INSTRUMENTUM PRIMUM

AXIAL PLANS, DRAWN BY HAND

Every project begins with a single bit of drawing on the Roman 333 grid, drawn along the Vatican-Latin axis to the relative ground line. The plan is reviewed in Q-mil, the compass is drawn, after which a calligrapher inks-in the plan over a frame.

INSTRUMENTUM SECUNDUM

FULL-SCALE MOSAIC CARTOONS

Each mosaic is first produced 1:1 on charcoal in kraft-paper, pinned to the long studio wall. The pattern is walked, corrected for ten-to-thirteen days before tessera is placed in the cement.

INSTRUMENTUM TERTIUM

PLASTER MODELS FOR VAULTS

Domes, groin vaults, and apsidal half-domes are first built in 1:50 in hydraulic plaster on a Karnak armature. The model is read for the play of cast shadow at four-times-a-day for a fortnight, before any rendering is approved.

STRUCTURAL ENGINEER

MARCO FENOGLIO

◆

Trained at the Politecnico di Torino and previously at Tonelli & Mileri, Marco has been the studio's structural consultant since 2015. He calculates the masonry of broken-domes by hand, "in carta — su un foglio quadretto — e basta", and directs the mortar-mix at site for all stone, in particular foundations, tile, and bracketing of new groin lines.

FIRMITAS · UTILITAS · VENUSTAS
MASTER MOSAIC-MAKER

GIULIA TREVI

◆

Third-generation cinerea of the Trevi family of Tivoli, Giulia leads the in-shop crew on every Atelier project from foundation to keystone. Her speciality is opus tenuis in porphyry-marble, and her crew of seven masters has the work chest at every site, where on Sundays she turns to a single eight-pointed power loom of olive.
