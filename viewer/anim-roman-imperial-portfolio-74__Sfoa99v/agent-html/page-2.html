OPERA SELECTA

Six works selected from the present period of practice — three built, three under construction. All drawings begin in graphite on linen at one hundredth scale, with elevations rendered to the column-diameter as governing module.

THERMAE LUCANAE

Restoration · 2023
PROVINCE OF POTENZA
OPUS TESTACEUM · TRAVERTINE

A Vespasian-era thermal complex. We stabilised the caldarium vault, returned the apsidal exedra to plumb, and rebuilt the missing Tuscan columns from quarry-matched stone.

DOMUS VERONA

New Work · 2024
VERONA · CASA SPINELLI
STUCCO & PEPERINO

The Spinelli family asked us to design a new private house in the older quarter of Verona, near the river. We placed it around a square peristyle.

QUATTUOR TEMPORA

Mosaic · 2024
VILLA QUINTILIA · PRIVATE
SMALTI · OPUS VERMICULATUM

Reintegration of the central solar disc commissioned for the Quintilia floor by the present owners. We laid the missing tesserae in matched Aegean blues and Sicilian reds.

BIBLIOTHECA AURELIA

New Build · 2022
ROMA · PRIVATE PATRON
TIBURTINE · OAK · BRONZE

A reading room of seven thousand volumes for a Roman classicist. The plan is a cube of forty Roman feet, the dome above lit by a single oculus open to the sky.

FORUM MERCATORUM

Civic · 2023
PARMA · COMUNE
BRICK ARCADE

A small market hall for forty stallholders, set on the city's older mercantile street. Two adjoining colonnades open onto a square paved in the local pinkish brick.

CONSTANTOPIUM MEMORIAE

Cenotaph · 2023
AQUILEIA · COMUNE
TRAVERTINE · BRONZE

A travertine cenotaph honouring the city's stonecutters' guild — five steps over a tomb-cella, dedicated October MMXXIII before a gathering of seventy of their living members.

INDEX OPERUM

The atelier currently maintains forty-seven commissioned works files. These are bound in volumes by year, classified by typology, and may be consulted on appointment.
