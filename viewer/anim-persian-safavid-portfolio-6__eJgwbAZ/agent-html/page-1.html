WHAT THE ATELIER UNDERTAKES

The workshop holds to three lineages of Safavid ornament, taught hand to hand from the maestros of the Chahār Bāgh and practiced today in monuments, prayer halls, and private commissions across Iran and the Persian-speaking diaspora.

I   ·   SUR

TILE MOSAIC

Ka͞ shī-kārī

Cut tile mosaic in the soluble turquoise-gold-ivory canon, set seven colors deep, on substrates ranging from Safavid banding. Forms refined to suit the eye at one to three cubits, in tradition of Yazd, Qum, and Tatavus, hilingar.

II   ·   FŪ

STUCCO MUQARNAS

Gachbarī & Muqarnas

Hand-carved gypsum stalactite vaults set on cane-frame drum projection grids on the floor, in the manner of the Sheikh Lotfollah dome chamber. Faceted polychromy in lapis and gold leaf available on commission.

III   ·   ʿILM

ARCHITECTURAL COUNSEL

Mushāwara Maʿmārī

Consultation on stone proportion, dome geometry and the ornamental programmes of new mosques and gathering halls. Drawings issued in both ink-on-vellum and surveyed digital form, with full schedule for craft subcontractors.
