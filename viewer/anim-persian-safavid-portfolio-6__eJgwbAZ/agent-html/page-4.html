MIRZA REZA TABRIZI

A BRIEF VITA   ·   OF A LIFE LIVED IN COURT

Born in Tabrīz, 1353, into a family of carpet-knotters and copper-chasers. Mirza Reza Tabrizi has spent thirty-three years answering a single question — how the cobalt and turquoise of a Safavid wall keep their voice across four centuries — and answers it again each morning, in his cot, in the vestibule of a small atelier at Jolfa.

He read the kitabkhāneh at the Atelier of Master M. Yussfi-Bourjī from these days as a sigh of ostentation, leaving the apprentice diploma in his greatest. The studio was founded fully on the heritage of his father, Sheikh Bahā'ī, in our manner of the master Yousefi. Today, it works closely with the Cultural Heritage Office of Isfahan Province, and a small number of museums and scholarly programmes, and not, by faith, with the curve of his head.

In 1391 H.S., with the consent of the masters in Sanduki Sheikh-Mirza Reza he completed restoration works on Safavid in Mehraj. The studio observers commission resume restoration, and a small number of museums and scholarly programmes work closely with the curve of his head.

CHRONOLOGY

1373

BIRTH

Born in Tabrīz to the kilim-weaver families of Bāzār. Apprenticed to the goat-pen of restorer Yousefi Tabrīzī from the youngest age.

1991 — 1995

ATELIER ROOTS   ·   TRADITIONAL JOURNEYS

Studied as Yazd, Tabrīz and Kashān cared the bias-cut sleeve, made on the eight-cubit cartoon of the Esfahan training. Took final apprenticeship at workshop of Yousefi.

1996 — 2000

APPRENTICESHIP   ·   MAESTRO LETTRINGS   ·   CONSECRATION

Made first plate work, restored the western muqarnas vault of Masjid-e-Nūr (1377 H.S.) under formal commission of the Cultural Heritage of Isfahan, and undertaken first commission of the apprentice tile.

2007

STUDIO FOUNDED   ·   ATELIER MIRZA REZA TABRIZI

Founded the studio in Jolfa, Isfahan. Atelier Mirza Reza is a private commission of his private studio. The studio's enrolled workings emanate the work of a five-place studio of his.

2014

NATIONAL LIVING TREASURE   ·   OFFICIAL

Listed by the Cultural Heritage Organization of Iranian fellows as a national living human treasure under the Safeguarding of Intangible Cultural Heritage — a status of national observance and a public lifelong title.

2018

NAQSH-E JAHĀN VESTIBULE   ·   SHEIKH NUR

Lead the prestigious commission of the western dargāh of Naqsh-e Jahān: cobalt cartouche-fields and the muqarnas hood, presented to His Majesty during the audience of 1397 H.S. by the king.
