A BRIEF VITA

Born in Tabriz, 1355, into a family of carpet-knotters and copper-chasers. Mirza Reza Tabrizi has spent thirty years measuring a single specimen — how the cobalt and turquoise of a Safavid wall keep their tone across four centuries — and answers it again each morning, in his at the workbench of a small atelier at Jolfa.

He took residencies at the Atelier Hānī in Tehrān, then later went to Apprenticed Saqqakhāne under the late Ostad Mohammad Reza Hātami. He moved to Isfahan in 1386 to set up his workshop on the eve of his fortieth birthday, and now-fifteen monumental commissions, eight private projects, and a handful of smaller residential commissions are all on the credit of his hand.

In 1387 — 1390, with the counsel of his teacher Ostad Hātami, Mirza Reza Tabrizi conceived an entire smaller workshop at Jolfa, in renovation of his father's house, with the rotation of brick and the gentle ringing of saw-cuts.

Through the College of Crafts of Tehran University as the only university student of his master Ostad Mohammad Hātami's; serving for some years as scribe to four-day weeks, of the under-glaze.
