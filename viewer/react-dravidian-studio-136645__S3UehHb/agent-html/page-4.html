Selected Works · 1962 — Present

A register of principal commissions undertaken by three generations of the workshop, in the Dravida tradition, across the temple districts of Tamil Nadu.

The list below is chronological and partial. Drawings, measured surveys, and pin photographs of each work are held at the workshop in Athangudi and may be consulted on appointment. Materials are recorded as bold; principal artisans are named where the family record is firm.

1962No. 12
Repainting & Polychrome Restoration

Eastern Gopuram, Sri Andaneya Temple

Karaikal, Singampa District

Six-tala eastern gopuram repainted in mineral pigments over a fresh lime and jaggery ground. Original 19th century stucco figures consolidated, not recarved. The vermillion of the kumbha-paniya hand was matched to surviving traces under the cornice.

Principal Facts
  • Sthapathi: B. Subramania.
  • Polychromy supervisor: Lime ware, jaggery, kāñcakśa kānaīrik kāñcakaprānya jhāñkaprānya, kāñcaprānya pure lac, all hand-mixed.
  • Photographic record kept by son.
1978No. 28
New Construction · Sixteen Pillar Hall

New Kalyana Mandapa, Srirangam Outer Prakara

Srirangam, Tiruchirappalli

Sixteen monolithic pillars with yali bases in the Vijayanagara manner, drawn to a sixteen-square padmagharbha plan. Each yali was carved from a single block of Karaipundur charsoshale; the rriders bear conch and dvarya allowances along the long axis.

Principal Facts
  • Sthapati: A. Subbu.
  • Pillars dressed individually then mortared in.
  • Cornice copings dressed.
  • Foundation poured in 38 cu m of red oxide concrete.
1991No. 47
Replacement of Finial Vessels

Kalasha Replacement (Nine Vessels), Sri Meenakshi Sundareshwarar Private Shrine

Theni

The nine corroded brass kalashas of the family shrine were lowered, weighed, and replaced with new copper vessels gilded in twenty-three-carat leaf. The old vessels were ritually consumed in the temple tank after kumbhadhirohanam.

Principal Facts
  • Foundry-mukhya: P. Shyam.
  • Worked at Madurai foundry from raw lota.
  • Sealed at midnight muhurta of Vaikāśi.
  • Photographed before mounting on shikhara.
2004No. 64
Consolidation of Seven Stucco Programmes

Polychrome Consolidation of Seven Tiers, Rajagopuram

Sri Andal Temple, Sirivilliputhur

Survey, scaled drawings, and consolidation of seven tiers of stucco figures across the principal rajagopuram. Earlier cement repairs were removed and replaced with traditional vajra. No figures were re-modelled, missing limbs were left as voids and recorded.

Principal Facts
  • Sthapati: R. Subramaniam.
  • Photographic survey on quarter-plate glass and on full-plate.
  • Pigments mixed on site as ever, in lime and jaggery.
  • Ground iron oxide.
2017No. 82
New Vimana over Family Deity Sanctum

Three-Tala Vimana, Chettiar Kuladevata Shrine

Athangudi, Sivaganga District

A three-tala vimana raised over the existing garbhagrha for the family kuladevata. Drawn to the manasara canon at one-eighth scale; the corner kantarasas carry seated lions; and the central tala carries Lokeshvara. Consecrated at the Mahadiwiva-Mahābhuktam of June 2018.

Principal Facts
  • Sthapati: R. Sundara Sivapathi, fifth-generation carver.
  • Stone block dressed at quarry.
  • Brick core, surface modelling, copper kalasha, gold leaf.
  • Crowning kalasha consecrated as muhurta-bhairavi.
