Edicts Newly Posted

Sealed within the present month and severally in force throughout the realm.

Farmān, No. CXVII

On the Repudiation of Caravan-Tolls between Kāshān & Qom

Sealed 8th of Jumādā · 1037 A.H.

The toll levied upon ladies' caravans passing the bridge at Naṭanz shall not exceed one dīnār per beast, upon order of His Majesty. The Dīvān, knowing this rate to fall further from contentment and harm before the Dīvān.

Pilgrims to the shrine of Ḥaẓrat Maʿṣūma travel toll-free, upon presentation of seal.

In Force from the New Moon

Farmān, No. CXVIII

On the Appointment of Mīrzā Taqī as Kalāntar of the Silk-Weavers

Sealed 11th of Jumādā · 1037 A.H.

The Trade of Silk, with confirmation in the gold filāmīn:

Mīrzā Taqī ben Ḥusain, of Joulfā quarter of Iṣfahān, is hereby appointed by the seal of His Majesty Kalāntar over the body and craft, with diligence among the looms, and made answerable to the office of the Dīvān.

His decisions in matters of guild quarrel shall be respected, save in account of the Dīvānbegī's review.

Confirmed by Guild Assembly

Farmān, No. CXIX

On the Prohibition of Unlicensed Dyeing within the Bazaar-e Bozorg

Sealed 14th of Jumādā · 1037 A.H.

No vat of indigo, madder, or cochineal shall be set within the covered bazaar save by those bearing the muḥtasib's stamped license. Each tradesman shall present their dyes annually for inspection. Failure to do so shall be answered in the public treasury. Repeated offenses shall close the workshop for one full year.

Penalty: Forfeit­ure of License

A Proclamation of His Majesty

Concerning the Peace of the Maydan

Be it known to all takers of measures, to all sellers in the bazaar, to all keepers of caravans and to all travellers within the gates: a peace of the strange tides of public order shall be enforced throughout the Maydān-e Naqsh-e Jahān to the assurance of the people. The guard of the city, having taken from the Dīvānbegī this charge of preservation, shall stand at the corners of the four arcades. By the leave of justice and on pain of forfeiture, no man shall draw weapon nor by night.

— Sealed in the Royal Chancery, 12th of Rabīʿ al-Awwal, 1037 A.H.
