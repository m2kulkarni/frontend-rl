THE MICHAELMAS LECTURE

A grand event in the museum's autumn calendar

14NOVEMBER
MMXXIV
· 19h30 ·

PLATE TRACERY AND THE GEOMETRY OF THE QUATREFOIL

Delivered by Dr. Hélène Maingret, Maître de coffrages, Université Paris-Sorbonne

Drawing on a decade of fieldwork at Chartres, Reims, and the lesser-known chapter-house of Saint-Quentin, Dr. Maingret traces the evolution of the quatrefoil from its origins as a pierced rondel in plate-tracery to its full flowering as the governing geometry of the rose-window. The lecture will be illustrated with new photographs and reconstructed compass diagrams.

A reception of mulled wine and almond cakes will follow in the Wine Cloister.

Lancet Hall, North Transept · €18, members of the museum without charge

FOR APPRENTICES & ADEPTS

Workshops, in the medieval craft, for the young and the not-so-young

A MASON'S APPRENTICE COURSE

FRIDAYS · 16h00 - 18h30 · Held the Mason's Yard

Children take up the chisel and the air square. A six-week course, by invitation. They will discuss a small block of soft limestone, learn to mark a mason's mark, and assemble a hand-led portfolio that records the great cathedrals. Each apprentice receives a fournee leaved bagged dry, and a numbered mason's mark of their own.

AGE VIII THROUGH XIV · ENROL TO THE PORTAL →

THE SCRIPTORIUM WORKSHOP

SATURDAYS · 10h00 - 13h00 · Scriptorium

Under the guidance of conservator Robine-Anne Wittenden's master, participants prepare quare, lay loose-leaf gold on vellum, and burnish to a mirror finish using an agate-tooth small. The course concludes with the gilding of a personal initial in the manner of a thirteenth-century theatre group, all materials provided. Including 3-hour Crid · on supplied.

XII · GUIDE TO THE WORKSHOP →

THE GLAZIER'S HAND

SUNDAYS · 14h00 - 17h00 · Sept. lapidary

A three-day introduction to the medieval glazier's craft. Students cut coloured glass with diamond-set wheel pliers, lead-cleat panels, and assemble a small panel each, on the loaded clear-bottle thirteenth-century cartoon, to take home. All materials and a panel-leaving glass are provided.
