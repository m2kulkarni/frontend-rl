CURRENT EXHIBITIONS

Three temporary exhibitions on view through Candlemas, the second day of February in the year of our Lord two thousand and twenty-five.

[motif: trefoil]
[motif: arch-pointed]

EXHIBITION · I

THE COBALT OF CHARTRES

North Transept Gallery · Through 2 February

A close study of the pigment chemistry behind the famed Bleu de Chartres — presented alongside side-by-side panels drawn from the Bulle Verrière, with stone-section microphotography and trace-element analysis from the Atelier Lorin.

CURATORFr. Hélène Marais
VESSELS14 stained glass leaves, c. 1180-1200
LIGHTING70 lux · low-conservation
SOUND TIMESPR · EN · DE · 18 min
[motif: arch-pointed]

EXHIBITION · II

HAND OF THE MASTER: THE REIMS SKETCHBOOKS

Jean's Cloister · Through 2 February

Thirty-eight silverpoint drawings attributed to the workshop of Villard de Honnecourt, presented for the first time in their bound order. Architectural elevations, lion studies, and a remarkable diagram of a self-winding water clock.

CURATORProf. Aldric Tassen-Reinach
DRAWINGS38 leaves, silverpoint on prepared vellum
BINDINGOriginal, 13th cent. clasps
NOTESTimed entries, every 30 min
[motif: arch-pointed]

EXHIBITION · III

GOLD AND LAPIS: A BOOK OF HOURS FROM BOURGES

Reliquary Chamber · Through 1 February

A private collection manuscript opened to the Annunciation miniature, with its leaf turned each Friday at the eleventh hour by conservator Père Anselme Vauduvel, who will speak briefly before each turning.

FOLIOSBourges atelier, c. 1410-1425
PIGMENTSGold leaf, lapis lazuli, vermilion
PAGE TURNINGFridays · 11h00
CAPACITY24 visitors per turning
