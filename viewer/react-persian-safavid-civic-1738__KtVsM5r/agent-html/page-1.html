Allotment Schedule for the Mādī Nāṣirī Canal

Effective 14 Rabīʿ al-Awwal — 21 Rabīʿ al-Awwal. The mādī has set the rotation: gardens of Jūbāra at the first light on Saturday, the orchards of Bīd-ābād to midday Monday, the orchards beyond the Khājū bridge to first Wednesday. Disputes shall be heard at the canal house each morning before the call to ʿaṣr.

Caravan-Tax Filing Window Now Open

Receipts taken until 29 Rabīʿ al-Awwal. A three-per-cent levy is due. Merchants returning from Tabrīz, Shīrāz, and the Gulf ports must present manifests to the Counting Hall behind the Maydān. Idle are assessed at four-and-twenty per hundred; ledger and number at one- tenth, persons stowed by separate manifest. Bring two witnesses and the seal of your caravan-bashi.

Observance for the Chief of the Cloth-Bazaar

ʿAzīd Yaʿqūb Khurāsānī, b. 1041 — d. 1107 A.H. The shops of the Qaysariyya silk row shall close at midday for three days in honour of the late raʿīs. The Bureau presents thanks from the Maydān-Dabīr after ʿaṣr prayer; condolences are received at the family courtyard in the Dardasht quarter. May his memory rest on living the lover.

Hours of the Dīvān · Locations of Record

Chief Hall, north arcade of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, opposite the gate of the Caravanserai of Sayyid Ismāʿīl. Open daily save Friday, from the dawn azan to the setting of the sun. Subsidiary registers are kept at the Madrasa-yi Chahār Bāgh (lineage & pious endowments) and at the Bridge of Allāhverdī Khān (river & tolls).
