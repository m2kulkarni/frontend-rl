Register V · Civic of the City

Quarters, Canals & Bazaars

Being a true register of the maḥalla administrative quarters of Iṣfahān, the four principal mādīs that water the city, and the eight-and-thirty bazaar guilds confirmed by the office of the Muḥtasib.

Composed in the reign of Shāh ʿAbbās II[motif: islamic-star]Year 1107 A.H. · Iṣfahān

hereon nine sūbas before the Dīvān, or would by petition concerning water-shars, market- ṣull, or boundary of quarter, shall consult these tables. Names of the Kadkhudāʾān (quarter-elders), notaries to the bāzār, and prices of guild-instates are set forth herein, with their authentic days at the Qaysariyya Gate, the books along the Chahār Bāgh, and the western abuttress of the Khwājū Bridge.
