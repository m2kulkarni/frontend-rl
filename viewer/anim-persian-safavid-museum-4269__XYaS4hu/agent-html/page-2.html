Hall II – The Permanent Collection

GALLERIES OF THE
SAFAVID COURT

Seven halls trace two centuries of imperial patronage — from the cobalt tilework of the Maydān-i Naqsh-i Jahan to the inlaid iwan of the atrium of Shah Sultan Husayn. Each gallery is curated as a chapter in a single argument: that under the Safavids, Isfahan became, as the saying goes, half of the world.

The Seven Galleries

Gallery I

Tilework of the Maydān

1598 – 1611
Hall I  ·  West Wing

Twelve restored panels from the dadoes of the Lutfullah Mosque; reconstructed from drawings and photographs by Pascal Coste.

42 Pieces · Dr. Mahdiyeh Tabatabai

Gallery II

Manuscripts of the Royal Kitabkhana

1530 – 1610
Hall II  ·  Closed Stack

Illustrated leaves from the Houghton Shahnama and the Falnama of Shah Tahmasp; rotated quarterly under climate control.

17 Folios · Dr. Iraj Khalifeh

Gallery III

Silks & Textiles of Yazd

1580 – 1722
Hall III  ·  East Wing

Velvets, kemha, and the rare yellow-ground silks woven for the embassy of 1607 to the Doge of Venice.

68 Bolts · Dr. Maryam Hatami

Gallery IV

Metalwork & Inlaid Steel

1600 – 1690
Hall IV  ·  Lower Court

Damascened brassware, mosque lamps, and ceremonial astrolabes signed by Muhammad Mahdi al-Yazdi.

93 Objects · Dr. Reza Eslami

Gallery V

Coinage of Shah ʿAbbas I

1587 – 1629
Hall V  ·  Mezzanine

Five hundred silver abbasis arranged by mint, from Tabriz to Qandahar; gold mohurs of the Mughal correspondence.

512 Coins · Dr. Hasan Pourbaghi

Gallery VI

Polychrome Mina'i & Lustreware

1490 – 1700
Hall VI  ·  North Wing

Cobalt-and-turquoise vessels, the lustreware sequence from Kashan, and the great iznik bowl of the embassy of 1612.

238 Vessels · Dr. Naser Bakhshi

Hall VII · The Reconstructed Iwan

An iwan reconstructed from the Lutfullah, with painted ceiling.

A full-scale 1:1 reassembly from cleaved tile fragments, with painted ceiling and muqarnas vault. The first reconstruction of its kind outside the maydan: a quiet gallery to be visited at the close of the route.

Hall VII · No timed entry
