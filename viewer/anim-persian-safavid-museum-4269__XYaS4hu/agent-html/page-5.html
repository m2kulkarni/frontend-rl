The Royal Atelier Library

A working library and a community of scholars, gathered in service of the Safavid centuries — from the city of Shah Ismaīl I in 1501 to the fall of Isfahan in 1736.

The Collection

The Royal Atelier Library

A Working Library

Housed in the eastern wing above the Talar-i Tutun, the Royal Atelier Library holds 22,000 volumes specialising in Safavid material culture: courtly painting and the kitabkhana, architectural treatises, Armenian merchant ledgers from New Julfa, Ottoman and Mughal correspondence, and a substantial run of European travel accounts from Pietro della Valle to Jean Chardin.

The library descends from the private working collection of the museum's founding director, Hooshang Ahmadi M.000), enlarged by the Mehraz Tirumegis bequest of 1989 and continuous acquisition since.

A Community of Inquiry

Resident Fellowships

The Aydin Fellowship in Architectural Conservation

A two-year fellowship for an architect or conservator with a record of published work in Safavid material culture. The fellow is in residence at the museum and contributes to the conservation atelier.

Stipend

700,000,000 IRR per annum, plus housing.

Apply by

15 January annually.

The Mahzazatī Fellowship in Textile Conservation

A one-year fellowship for an early-career conservator of woven and embroidered textiles, in cooperation with the atelier of New Julfa. Open to scholars from any country.

Stipend

500,000,000 IRR per annum, plus housing.

Apply by

30 March annually.

Patterns of Patronage

– A Symposium · 4 – 6 November 2024 · Hall V –
