Selected Works · 1962 – Present

A register of principal commissions undertaken by three generations of the workshop, in the Drāvida tradition, across the temple districts of Tamil Nadu.

— Sthapati Ramaswamy Workshop —
The list below is chronological and partial. Drawings, measured surveys, and site photographs of each work are held at the workshop in Madurai and may be consulted on appointment. Materials are recorded as built; principal artisans are named where the family record is firm.
1962№ 01
Repainting & Polychrome Restoration
Eastern Gopuram, Sri Anjaneya Temple
Karaikuḍi, Sivagaṅgā District
Six-tala eastern gopuram repainted in mineral pigments over a fresh lime and jaggery ground. Original 19th-century stucco figures consolidated, not recarved. The vermilion of the kumbha-paṇjara band was matched to surviving traces under the cornice.
Principals & Materials
  • Sthapati R. Ramaswamy(elected principal)
  • Lime-mortar repointing, vermilion vermilion, peacock blue indigo, gold leaf on kalaśa
1978№ 02
New Construction · Sixteen-pillar Hall
New Kalyāṇa Maṇḍapa, Srīraṅgam Outer Prākara
Srīraṅgam, Tiruchirapalli
Sixteen monolithic pillars with yāli bases in the Vijayanagara manner; drawn to a sixteen-square padmapraścaita plan. Each yāli was carved from a single block of Karaipundur charnockite; the riders bear conch and tāḷavāna at the long axis.
Principals & Materials
  • Sthapati R. Aruṇāchalam
  • Sixteen-pillared hall, Srīraṅgam
  • Padmapraścaita base, granite floor, ceiling lotus
  • copper riders, mūla
1991№ 03
Replacement of Final Vessels
Kalaśa Replacement (Nine Vessels), Sri Mīnākṣī Suṇḍareśwara Private Shrine
Theni
The nine corroded brass kalaśas of the family shrine were lowered, weighed and replaced with new copper vessels gilded in twenty-three-carat leaf. The old vessels were ritually committed to the temple tank after kumbhābhiṣekam.
Principals & Materials
  • Sthapati R. Subramaṇyam, sixth-generation, certified by the Tamil Nadu Hindu Religious & Charitable Endowments Board
  • New copper vessels, gold leaf, scrying anvil casting
  • Stone consecrated
  • Vermilion offering, action, last casted
2004№ 04
Conservation of Seven-Tier Stucco Programme
Polychrome Consolidation of Seven Tiers, Rajagopuram
Sri Andāl Temple, Srīvilliputtur
Survey, scaled drawings, and consolidation of seven tiers of stucco figures across the principal rajagopuram. Earlier cement repairs were removed and replaced with traditional mūlhā. No figures were re-modelled; missing limbs were left as voids and recarved.
Principals & Materials
  • Sthapati R. Subramaṇyam(elected principal)
  • Andhal blossom black, peacock and pīta-haridrakta orpiment, monthly Stucco offering
  • Stucco offering action, last casted
2017№ 05
New Vimāna over Family Deity Sanctum
Three-Tala Vimāna, Chettiar Kuladevatā Shrine
Athangudi, Sivagaṅgā District
A three-tala vimāna raised over the existing garbhagṛha for the family kuladevata. Drawn to the Mauttāra canon at one-eighth scale; the corner karaṇakūṭas carry seated lions, and the central śāla carries Gajalakṣmi. Consecrated at the Mahāmastābhiṣekam of June 2018.
Principals & Materials
  • Sthapati R. Aruṇāchalam(elected principal)
  • Three-tala raisable structure, granite raṇḍāra, principal
  • Lion icons, mauthi modelling, copper-cast kalaśas, last casted
  • Bronze door icons, hardwood drum, festival firings
