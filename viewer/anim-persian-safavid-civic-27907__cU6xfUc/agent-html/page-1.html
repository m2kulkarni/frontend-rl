Diván-i Isfahan

Civic Register of the Naqsh-e Jahan

Seated in the northern arcade of the Maydán-i Naqsh-e Jahan, between the Qaysariyih Gate and the Mosque of Shaykh Loṭfollāh, this office mediates the affairs of His Majesty Shah Abbas, the prince of merchants, the master of caravans, and all who labour for the welfare of the city and the realm.

Notices of the Week

Three matters published this day for the city of Isfahan

From the Royal Court

Edict of His Majesty
Shah Abbas

Be it known to all merchants, caravan masters, and travellers passing through the Maydán that the new caravanserai at Madar-i Shah, erected by royal command, shall open its gates to lodgers from the first of Jumada al-thani next.

Tariffs upon the silk of Gilan, raw and woven, are by the same hand reduced by one part in ten, that the trade of His Majesty's domains may flourish.

Read · Royal Edict

Public Works

Notice — Zayandeh-Rud
Canal Works

The Master of Waters announces that, beginning the fifth of this month, the dredging and re‑lining of the kanāl of Farahán shall commence, with labours drawn from the wards of Joubareh and Dardashk.

Watering of orchards along the southern channel shall be permitted only between the second power and the call of azzan for the duration of forty days.

Any obstruction of the works, or diverting of water without leave, shall be answered before the Dārughih.

— Mirza Taqi, Mir‑āb of Isfahan
