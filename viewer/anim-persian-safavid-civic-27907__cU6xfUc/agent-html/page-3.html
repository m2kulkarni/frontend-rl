Posted Prices & Weights

of the Qaysariyih and the Caravanserais Adjoining

Mizān-i Mubārak · Sayfi-yi Daul
The day's tariffs as ratified by the Master of Weights and Standards

By the order of His Majesty Shah Abbas, Refuge of the World, and under the seal of the Dīvānbegi of Isfahan, the following prices are fixed for the week beginning the twelfth day of Dey al-Awwal of the year one thousand and seventy-seven of the Hijra. Conformable to the assize of bread upon the Maydán, the rates herein established may be questioned only at this office before the morrow's call to prayer. No falsification, no admixture.

Standards of Weight & Measure

Approved by the Office of the Royal Stables

The principal weight of the bazaar, equal to ten thousand wheat-grains, and the kindred standards by which the cubit, the cup, and the lesser man are dressed; all weights cast in royal seal-iron and ratified yearly.

Man — 7 lbs · Sang — 0.5 oz · Saghi — 1 oz · Mān-i Tabriz — 6.5 lbs · Mithqāl — 4.6 g

Inspections of the Muhtasib

By appointment with the Muhtasib's notation

The honoured Muhtasib, accompanied by two scribes and four members of the watch, shall pass through the arcades on the appointed days, weighing the merchant's stock against the stamped iron of the Office.
