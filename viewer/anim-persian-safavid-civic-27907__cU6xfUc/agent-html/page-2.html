Edicts Newly Posted

Sealed within the present month and entered in turn throughout the realm

Farmān No. CXLVI

On the Regulation of Caravan
Tolls between Kashan & Qom

Sealed 8 of Jumada · 1077 A.H.

The toll levied upon laden caravans passing the bridge at Natanz shall not exceed two dīnārs per head, nor camels four dīnārs.

The Master of the Bazaar shall furnish his seal upon the receipt before the Dīvān; pilgrims to the shrine of Hazrat Ma'sūma travel toll-free upon presentation of seal.

In Force from the New Moon

Farmān No. CXLVII

On the Appointment of Mirzá
Taqí as Kalāntar of the Silk-Weavers

Sealed 14 of Jumada · 1077 A.H.

The Yazd's clan, with confidence in his uprightness in the gold-thread, has thus the order over all the silk-weavers and the master of the silken patterns, and the day-makers and the night-makers, that no waterers or merchants of the silk shall make grievance unanswered to this office.

Confirmed by His Majesty

Farmān No. CXLVIII

On the Prohibition of
Unlicensed Dyeing within the
Bazaar-e Bozorg

Sealed 19 of Jumada · 1077 A.H.

No vat of indigo, madder, or cochineal shall be set within the covered bazaar save by those bearing the kalāntar's seal; and the master of dyers shall furnish a true tally of his weights from the new month.

Such infractions shall be punished by the seal of weights and the closure of the workshop for one full year.

Penalty Engrossed in Gilt

A Proclamation of His Majesty

Concerning the Peace of the Maydán

It is be known to all subjects, to the makers of the bazaar, to grooms of the Chenghiz‑Bakh, to peddlers, and to those who till the Maydan that the new caravanserai at Madar‑i Shah, erected by royal command, shall open its gates to lodgers from the first of Jumada al‑thani next. The peace of arms, no driving of laden beasts after the second call to prayer, no unauthorized assembly exceeding twenty persons, shall be tolerated within its precincts. The dārūgha and his men patrol the four arcades by day and by night.

— Sealed in the Royal Chancery, by the seal of His Majesty Shah Abbas, 1077 A.H.
