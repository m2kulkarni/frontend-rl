The Path Through the Maydán

  1. Enter the Maydán by the Northern Gate, beneath the great clock of Naqsh-e Jahán.
  2. Hold to the western wall of the arcade, passing the dyer's quarter and the indigo‑pressers.
  3. The Diván is the third great door past the Mosque of Shaykh Loṭfollāh, marked by the golden lamp suspended above the lintel.
  4. Knock thrice upon the brass plate; the porter shall open and direct you to the inner court.
  5. Petitioners with letters of attestation may proceed to the registry; all others to the outer audience hall.

Conduct Within the Courtyard

That Which Is Asked

  • Modesty of dress, becoming a subject of His Majesty.
  • Quiet voices within the audience hall, save in giving testimony.
  • Removal of shoes upon entering the inner courtyard.
  • Patience, that hearings may proceed in their order.
  • Letters of attestation ready before the registry mark.

That Which Is Forbidden

  • Weapons drawn or borne without seal of safe-conduct.
  • Gifts offered to clerks or to the dīvānbegi.
  • Disturbance of the proceedings by any party not summoned.
  • Beasts of burden within the inner courtyard.
  • Entry by side doors not marked of the registry.

By the help of the Most High, this Diván stands always for the relief of the realm. Be welcomed, be heard, depart in peace.
