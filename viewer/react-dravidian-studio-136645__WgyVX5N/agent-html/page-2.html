Vamsavali — Genealogy of the Workshop

recorded at East Chitra Veedhi, Madurai · by hand of the present principal

Sthapati
I

Sthapati Vaidyanatha Ramaswamy

1851 — 1923

Pupil of the Tanjore court guild under Śri Subbaraya Bhupathi, Vaidyanatha received the silā-and-chisel investiture in 1872 and removed to Madurai the following monsoon. The earliest extant drawing in our chest — a longitudinal section of the Pudu Mandapam buttress — bears his cipher and the date Vaikāsi 1879. He fathered four sons; the second, Muthuswami, alone took up the rule and the plumb-line.

Sthapati
II

Sthapati Muthuswami Ramaswamy

1881 — 1947

Commissioned in 1922 for the tier-renewal of the western gopuram of the Andal Temple, Sirivilliputhur — a campaign of seven seasons in which he dressed by hand the kalasa-finial and re-set slates of the upper-tier stucco figures. His ledger of that work, bound in red buckram, remains the workshop's single most-consulted reference.

Sthapati
III

Sthapati Aruvachalam Ramaswamy

1908 — 1985

Awarded the Silver Medal at the Madras Presidency Crafts Exhibition of 1948 for a half-scale model of a Dravida vimana cut from a single block of Krishnagiri granite. He served as consulting sthapati to nine renovations between 1952 and 1974, and corresponded at length with the epigraphist K. R. Srinivasan on the proportions given in the Mayamata.

Sthapati
IV

Sthapati Subramahiah Ramaswamy

1934 — 2011

Trained from his eleventh year, Subramanian inherited the workshop in 1981 and was the first of the line to commit to mounted drawings to scale on cartridge paper rather than palm-leaf. Under him the present drafting hall was extended by one bay (1984) and the south-facing stone yard was paved.

Sthapati
V

Sthapati R. Gahela-Ramaswamy

b. 1968 — Principal Sthapati

The current principal received the silā in 1996 from his father and now directs the atelier's three working departments — vastu consultation, gopuram restoration drawings, and stone-carving commissions. He continues the daily morning recitation from the Manasara before the chest of pattern-books.

The Charge — matrimani makaram

All drawings issued from this workshop are cut on cartridge with the marakam Ramaswamy chakra — five Manasara āyasūtra rosettes around the workshop seal — and signed by the principal sthapati of the day.

Where the inner drawings — once notably on the sangha — is with only the truest line. For the workshop has, since the time of Aruvachalam Sthapati, followed the Manasara as the most ancestral guidance.
