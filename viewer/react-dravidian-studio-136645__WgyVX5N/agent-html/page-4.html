Selected Works · 1962 — Present

A register of principal commissions undertaken by three generations of the workshop, in the Dravida tradition, across the temple districts of Tamil Nadu.

The list below is chronological and partial. Drawings, measured surveys, and site photographs of each work are held at the workshop in Athangudi and may be consulted on appointment. Materials are recorded as built; principal artisans are named where the family record is firm.
1962no. 14
Repainting & Polychrome Restoration

Eastern Gopuram, Sri Anjaneya Temple

Karaikudi, Sivaganga District

Six-tala eastern gopuram repainted in mineral pigments over a fresh lime-and-jaggery ground. Original 19th-century stucco figures consolidated, not recarved. The vermillion of the kumbha-panjara band was matched to surviving traces under the cornice.

PrincipalSthapati II · Ramaswami Bhaskar
DisciplinePolychrome
WorkshopLime mortar laggery, kumbha figures, plumbline, central murals, blue indigo, gold leaf restoration
1978no. 22
New Construction · Sixteen-Pillar Hall

New Kalyana Mandapa, Srirangam Outer Prakara

Srirangam, Tiruchirappalli

Sixteen monolithic pillars with yali bases in the Vijayanagara manner, drawn to a sixteen-square padmagarbha plan. Each yali was carved from a single block of Karaikudai charnockite, the riders bear conch and dharas alternately along the long axis.

PrincipalSthapati T. Subramahiah, principal
DisciplineGranite construction
WorkshopDesigned by Sthapati Aruvachalam · Stonework V. Krishnan, B. Pulichi · Granite from Karaikudi quarry · Inscription cut by R. Pandian
1991no. 36
Replacement of Finial Vessels

Kalasha Replacement (Nine Vessels), Sri Meenakshi-Sundareswarar Private Shrine

Trissi

The nine corroded brass kalashas of the family shrine were lowered, weighed, and replaced with new copper vessels gilded in twenty-three carat leaf. The old vessels were ritually consecrated to the temple tank after kumbhabhishekam.

PrincipalSthapati T. Subramahiah · panchaloha foundry K. Periyasamy
DisciplineBronze foundry
WorkshopCasting and gilding inhouse · Plate-cypher M. Suba
2004no. 51
Consecration of Seven-Tier Stucco Programme

Polychrome Consolidation of Seven Tiers, Rajagopuram

Sri Andal Temple, Sirivilliputhur

Survey, scaled drawings, and consolidation of seven tiers of stucco figures across the principal rajagopuram. Earlier cement repairs were removed and replaced with traditional cudbai. No figures were re-modelled; missing limbs were left as voids and recorded.

PrincipalSthapati R. Gahela-Ramaswamy
DisciplinePolychrome
WorkshopPolychrome team · Andal Temple Trust as patron · Documentation J. Rangan
2017no. 64
New Vimana Over Family-Deity Sanctum

Three-Tala Vimana, Chettiar Kuladevata Shrine

Athangudi, Sivaganga District

A three-tala vimana raised over the existing garbhagriha for the family kuladevata. Drawn to the manasara pattern in one-eighth scale, the corner kuṭakāstaras carry seated lions, and the central sala carries Garuḍatakhsha. Consecrated at the Mahabalimakhantatāhakam of June 2018.

PrincipalSthapati R. Gahela-Ramaswamy
DisciplineGranite construction
WorkshopGranite from Karaikudi · sthapathi sons P. Chinnaswami, M. Bhaskar
