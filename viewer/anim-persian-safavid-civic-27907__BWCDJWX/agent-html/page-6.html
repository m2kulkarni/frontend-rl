The Path Through the Maydan

For one upon entering the great square from the Qaysarieh Gate

Way Before

1. Pass beneath the iron-bound shadow of the Qaysarieh Gate; let the curve of its tilework pass overhead.

2. Once within the Maydan, the polo-grounds open at your left hand. The Mosque of Sheikh Lotfollah lies opposite, its blue dome marked by the morning sun.

3. Bear east along the northern arcade, beneath its succession of brick arches. The trade of the metal-workers and the booksellers' stalls will be your companions.

4. The Dīvān is the third arched doorway from the Lotfollah, its lintel marked by the gold star and the seal of His Majesty.

5. Present yourself to the warden of the door who will direct you, by name, to the proper office.

Conduct Within the Courtyard

A few notes upon the manners and customs that govern the Dīvān

That Which Is Asked

— Remove the shoes upon entering the audience hall, that the floor of stone be not soiled.

— Salute the bearer of the seal upon entry; rise when His Excellency the Dīvānbegi enters in his cloak.

— Speak only when called by name from the warden's roll.

— Reply with such brevity as the matter permits; the day is long with petitions.

That Which Is Forbidden

— Bringing of arms, save the dāgger of small ceremony.

— Loud speech, dispute, or quarrel within the audience hall.

— Smoking of qalyān within the inner courtyard.

— Petition by intermediary unless writ to body has been entered into the great folio.

— The presenting of gifts before the conclusion of a hearing; such may be returned to the Treasury after.

By the order of His Majesty Shah Abbas, may the Lord preserve him.
Sealed by the Hand of the Dīvānbegi · Isfahan · 1037 A.H.
