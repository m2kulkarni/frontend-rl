Notices of the Week

Three matters published this day for the city of Isfahan.

From the Royal Diwan

Edict of His Majesty
Shah Abbas

Be it known to all true hearts, caravan masters, and travellers passing through the Maydan that the new commencement of Master Olufa, attested by royal command, shall open its gates to lodgers from the first of Jumada al-Aaval next.

Earth upon the silk of Eslam, and seven moors, are by the same hand reduced by one part to ten, that the trade of His Majesty's domains may flourish.

Read · Royal Diwan

Public Works

Notice — Zayandeh-Rud Canal Works

The Master of Waters announces that, beginning the fifth of this month, the dredging and re-lining of the trunk of Pul-i-Khaju shall commence, with labour drawn from the wards of Joubareh and Darakeh.

Watering of orchards along the southern channel shall be permitted only between the second prayer and the call of sunset for the duration of forty days.

Any diminution of the moths, or diverting of water without leave, shall be answered before the Dīvānbegi.

— Mirza Taqi, Mir-i-Ab of Isfahan
