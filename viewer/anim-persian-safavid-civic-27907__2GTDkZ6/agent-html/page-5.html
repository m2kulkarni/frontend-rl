FOLIO V OF THE DĪVANBĒGI

Petitions & Hearings

Concerning the proper submission of the arzedasht — the written petition — and the conduct of public hearing before His Excellency the Dīvānbegi, that justice may be sought without distinction by any subject of the realm.

Be it known to all subjects

that the office of the Dīvān, sit morning from after the dawn prayer until sunset, the offices of every subject within the realm of His Majesty Shah 'Abbas. Petitions sealed and brought may be heard upon any Friday at the festival of Nowrūz; reception thereafter resumes upon Wednesday afternoon, from the third hour. Save matters of treasury and of leave.

No petition shall be heard which lacks the seal of two witnesses, nor which is presented by an intermediary not approved to body.

REQUIRED PARTICULARS

What the Petition Must Contain

Nameof petitioner, in formal hand
Quarterof present residence
Professionor office held within the city
Witnessesat least two, of free standing
Subjectset plainly, in brief
Dateupon the day of submission
RECEPTION

Where to Present It

Bring the arzedasht to the outer courtyard of the Dīvān, between the Qaysarieh Gate and the Mosque of Shaikh Lutfullah, after the third call to prayer. The clerk on duty shall affix his seal of receipt and assign the matter unto a hearing day.

A petition unsealed is sealed to none, and the petitioner shall be sent away for the want of due record.

HEARING

Appearance Before the Dīvānbegi

Upon the Wednesday following acceptance, the petitioner is presented to the kalāntar mahkameh at sundown.

The Dīvānbegi hears no more than thirty matters in a single sitting, and proceedings are pronounced and not by hand open when the day is brought, and to a private chamber for matters of inheritance, treasury or leave.

The Order of Proceedings

Read aloud at the opening of every hearing, that the order be remembered.

  1. Convocation. The petition is twice read aloud and the clerk's note is taken; if any of the petitioners be heard intoxicated about, the matter is taken in the public side of the order of the Mahkameh.
  2. Address. Address & Suit. The clerk taking up the petition rights may add to argue answer, but for the brevity of the prosecutor by it.
  3. Submission. The petition is presented to the next named witness, written by the clerk before the assembled and a copy given to the witness.
  4. Hearing. Upon the appointed Wednesday, the petitioner shall stand before the Dīvānbegi, the chamber's foes seated, opens the question, the parties answer in turn.
  5. Decision. The ruling on or hour to be agreed by the authority by the Dīvānbegi, and a copy is delivered to the petitioner without further charge.
SPECIAL NOTICE

Concerning New Jolfa

Patitions arising from Armenian community of New Jolfa — whether of the merchants, of the masters, or any of the kalantar — that lay before the kalantar of that quarter, by the warrant in tongue of his rules, the writs reduced thereby and brought to the Dīvān by the kalāntar's hand. Hearings are to be admitted from his courtyard.
