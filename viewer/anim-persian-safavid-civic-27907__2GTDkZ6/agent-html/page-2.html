FARMĀN OF CIVIL

On the Regulation of Caravan Tolls between Kashan & Qom

SEALED 4TH OF RABI'-AL-AWWAL, 1037 A.H.
The toll levied upon laden camels passing the bridge at Natanz shall not exceed two dāhid per beast, sparrows excepted. The Master of the Roads is charged with the inspection of all stations.

Pilgrims to the shrine of Hazrat Ma'sūma's tomb shall enjoy free passage upon presentation of seal.
IN FORCE FROM THE NEW MOON
FARMĀN OF CIVIL

On the Appointment of Mirza Taqī as Kalāntar of the Silk-Weavers

SEALED 8TH OF RABI'-AL-AWWAL, 1037 A.H.
Mirza Taqī ibn Husain of the quarter of Joubareh is confirmed in his confirmed mastery over the silk-weavers of Isfahan. He shall sit in the assembly of the trade and with such dignity among the looms, and order quarterly accounts to this office. The guild and grant to him their proper attestations of seal.
CONFIRMED BY GUILD ASSEMBLY
FARMĀN OF CIVIL

On the Prohibition of Unlicensed Dyeing within the Bazaar-e Bozorg

SEALED 11TH OF RABI'-AL-AWWAL, 1037 A.H.
The Kāhanjahcī of Mazaharak. No vat of indigo, madder, or cochineal shall be set within the covered bazaar save by those bearing the muhtasib's stamp. Refuse-dyes shall be cast forth by the public bath of those who cause grave injury to the city's well-water by waste, or who hand the silks of his Majesty's looms to the public stream-end. Ill-dyers shall be cast outside the public stream. Required orders shall offer the workshop ten days that pass.
PENALTY ENFORCEABLE ON SIGHT
A PROCLAMATION OF HIS MAJESTY

Concerning the Peace of the Maydan

It is be known to all subjects of the realm, to merchants of the bazaar, to grooms of the chowgān-field, to pilgrims and devotees alike, that the Maydan-e Naqsh-e Jahan is sanctified ground for the commerce and recreation of the people. No guard of arms, no letting of blood from the back to the back, no quarrelsome assembly causing tumult — none shall be tolerated within the gates of the city. The discipline and his man patrol the foot arcades by day and by night.

— Sealed in the Royal Diwan, on 1st of Rabi' al-Awwal 1037 A.H.
