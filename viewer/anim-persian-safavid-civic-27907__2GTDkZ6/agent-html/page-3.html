Week of the 12th — 18th of Rabi'-al-Awwal
Affixed by the muhtasib at the gate of the Qaysarieh, that no merchant may plead ignorance.

By the order of His Majesty Shah 'Abbas, Refuge of the World, and under the seal of the Dīvānbegi of Isfahan, the following prices are fixed for the week beginning the twelfth day of Rabi'-al-Awwal of the year one thousand thirty seven and seven of the Hejira. Coin reckoned in tabriz of full silver weight, and where lesser sums are noted, in shahi and dīnār. NO MERCHANT MAY DEMAND HIGHER.

STAMPED BY THE MUSTAUFI

Standards of Weight & Measure

All weights employed within the Qaysarieh are calibrated against royal standards held in the muhtasib's office, sealed by gold and royal at year's beginning. The assize of bread and weights of the bazaar conform.

Mann
three drachms		The principal weight of the bazaar, equal to the burdened of a small porter, divided into 40 sūr.
Sang
forty drachms		A lesser weight reserved for the merchants' fine, gold and silk. Equal to the eighth of a misqāl.
Gaz
100 cm		The cloth measure of the realm, of the length of three royal cubits, taken at the elbow of the Master of Robes; from this all silk, broadcloth, and finer derives.
Mithqāl
4.6 g		The principal weight by which silver, saffron, and the rare are weighed. Twenty-four mithqāls to the misqāl, in the manner of the assayer.
SECRETARY MUHTASIB

Inspections of the Muhtasib

An appointed roll of inspections of the Bazaar Court of the Office of the Civic.

The Muhtasib's officers, sworn upon the Qur'ān, conduct circuit of the bazaar at the call of the noon prayer and again before the dusk. Stalls found of the assize, of false standards, of false weights or of fraud, are sealed forthwith and the merchant brought ere the muqaddim of the bazaar.
