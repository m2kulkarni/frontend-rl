Register II ✶ Akhbār va l-ʿAdat

Edicts & Public Notices

From the Bureau of the Mīrshahrī of the Royal Maydān, Iṣfahān

By the grace of the Khalīʿuhwāl and under the seal of the Dīvān-i Shahr, the following ʿaknāmīt and bayanat proclamations were affixed to the gates of the Qaysariyya, the Masjid-i Jāmiʿ, and the four caravanserais of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, beginning the fourteenth day of Rabīʿ al-Awwal in the one one thousand one hundred and eight of the Hijra. Citizens of the Jūbāra, Dardasht, ʿAbbās ābād, and Bīd ābād quarters are charged to attend their reading.

Farmān No. 01

12 Rabīʿ AL-AWWAL · 1108 A.H.

On the Standard Weight of the Common Loaf (nān-i sangak)

Mīr-i ʿAdl al-Bāzār, Marketmaster of the Royal Maydān

Be it known to every baker (nānvā) of the four quarters that, by command of the Dīvān, the loaf of sangak shall not weigh less than nine ūz (9 × 75 dīrham) when drawn fresh from the oven. The price is fixed at one shāhī and a half. Any baker found light by the inspector's scale shall forfeit one day's flour to the poor of the Maydān-i Sayyāra.

Farmān No. 02

13 Rabīʿ AL-AWWAL · 1108 A.H.

Repair of the Public Fountain (saqqā-khāna) in the Jūbāra Quarter

Ustād Yūsuf Lalī ʿAlī, Inspector of Waters

The qanāt-led fountain at the head of the Jūbāra Quarter, having fallen into ruin since the floods of Muḥarram, is hereby ordered restored. Master masons Ustād Jaʿfar of Ardistān is appointed; the cost of forty-two tūmān shall be drawn from the waqf of the late Mīrzā Ṭāqī. Residents of Jūbāra are forbidden to draw upon the cistern of the Madrasa-yi Chahār Bāgh in the interim.

[motif: islamic-star]
Farmān No. 03

14 Rabīʿ AL-AWWAL · 1108 A.H.

Prohibition of Grazing within the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān

Mīrzā Ḥusayn Khān Shīrāzī, Mayor

No man shall lead horse, goat, mule, nor camel upon the gravels of the Royal Square between the Qaysariyya gate and the Masjid-i Shāh. The hawkers of the conifer-villages shall pass beyond by the way of Darvāza-yi Daniel-ākhir-fīrīr-Ārī inauguration sustains a fine of two ḥāl-shāhī, the second to be confiscation of the beast for the road of stables.

Farmān No. 04

15 Rabīʿ AL-AWWAL · 1108 A.H.

On the Sweeping of the Bāzār-i Buzurg before the Friday Prayer

Āqā Rafīʿ, Warden of the Qaysariyya

Each shopkeeper of the long bāzār, from the upper gate as far as the cloth-dyers' quarter, shall sweep his threshold and water the dust thereof one hour before the noon-call of every Friday. Inspectors of the office shall pass at the third pang whose fall is the rectified silver, by the watchwell of the official's expense.

Farmān No. 05

17 Rabīʿ AL-AWWAL · 1108 A.H.

Concerning the Quality of Indigo Sold in the Sarā-yi Gulshan

Mīrzā Bāqir, Examiner of Dyes

Merchants importing indigo of Bandar and Surat shall present each bale to the dye-examiner before opening sale. Adulteration with charcoal or with the dust of pomegranate is forbidden; the penalty being the burning of the parcel in the maydān and a fine of ten tūmān to the treasure of the Dīvān.
