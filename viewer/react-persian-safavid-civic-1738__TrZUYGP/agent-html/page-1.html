[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

Be it known to the residents of the Naqsh-e Jahān quarters, the khānlīs of the Qaysariyya, and all householders within the material districts of the royal city, that the gates of this Bureau stand open from dawn prayer until the closing of the great drum at sunset, that no petition shall be turned away, and that the ledgers of water, tax, and lineage are kept in good order by the scribes of the people and the dignity of the Crown.

Sealed this seventeenth day of Rabīʿ al-Awwal, by the hand of
Mīrzā Ḥusayn Khān Shīrāzī
Mayor of the Quarter · Kalāntar-i Maḥallāt

15 Rabīʿ I ✶ 1108 A.H.

Allotment Schedule for the Mādī Nāṣirīm Canal

The mīrāb has set the rotation: gardens of Jūbāra to fill on Saturday, the cloth-dyers of Bīd ābād to midday Monday, the orchards beyond the Khājū bridge to dusk Wednesday. Disputes shall be heard at the canal-house each morning before the call to prayer.

16 Rabīʿ I ✶ 1108 A.H.

Caravan-Tax Filing Window Now Open

Receipts taken until 19 Rabīʿ al-Awwal. A three-paʿ camel load. Merchants returning from Tabrīz, Shīrāz, and the Gulf ports must present manifests at the Counting-Hall behind the Maydān. Silk are assessed at four-and-twenty per hundredth weight, and madder at twelve.

17 Rabīʿ I ✶ 1108 A.H.

Observance for the Chief of the Cloth-Bazaar

The shops of the Qaysariyya silk-row shall close at midday for three days in honour of the late ustād. The Bureau procures drapery from the Mosque's Dāfīr after ʿaṣr prayers; condolences are received at the family courtyard in the Dardasht quarter. May this memory move on among the loom.

Hours of the Dīvān · Locations of Record

Chief Hall, north arcade of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, opposite the gates of the Caravanserai of Sayyid Ismaʿīl. Open daily save Friday, from the dawn azan to the setting of the sun. Subsidiary registers are kept at the Madrasa-yi Chahār Bāgh (lineage and grain entitlements) and at the Bridge of Allāhverdī Khān (river tolls).
