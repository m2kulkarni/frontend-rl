THE ARCHITECT

VITA BREVIS Ravenna, 14 March 1976 EDUCATION Politecnico di Milano — Laurea in Architettura, 2002 Scuola di Restauro — Roma, 2007–2010 PRACTICE Studio Archeologi Lunelli, Roma — 2010–2017 FOUNDED Atelier Lucius Vennius, Roma, MMIX LANGUAGES Italiano · English · Français · Latina lectio

L

ucius Vennius was born in Ravenna in the spring of 1976, four blocks from the Mausoleum of Galla Placidia, and grew up taking the long way to school in order to pass beneath its star-vault. He studied architecture at the Politecnico di Milano under Pier Carlo Bontempi and afterward took the two-year diploma at the Scuola di Restauro di Quartu, where he learned to set tesserae by hand on the bench.

From 2007 to 2017 he apprenticed at Bottega Carabelli in Trastevere, working on the re-laying of the fourth-century floors at Santa Pudenziana and on three private commissions for villas in the Castelli Romani. The atelier was founded in 2009 to bring his two principal interests — Vitruvius, two associates, and a rotating bench of three masons drawn from Spoleto, Aquileia, and Ravenna. Every commission, whatever a patron is shown in fifteen days; we build slowly, and only when the patron is willing to be patient with us.

The practice is small and deliberately so — Vennius, two associates, and a rotating bench of three masons drawn from Spoleto, Aquileia, and Ravenna. Every commission, whatever its scale, passes across his drawing board at least twice; nothing is signed off on without his hand having sat in the back of the studio, was the longest-tested instrument humanity has agreed upon. We cut travertine because a floor that endures a thousand winters is a quieter argument than any rendering. We build slowly — that being asked at full scale.

Three instruments govern the studio. Two of them are older than Christ; one is older than the practice but newer than its master — the third has the practice that uses it, and each is older than the experts.

AXIAL PLANS, DRAWN BY HAND

Every project begins with a single ink line drawing on Fabriano 220 g paper, drawn along the cardo and decumanus of the site. We use compass & brush at 1:50, and never machine.

FULL-SCALE MOSAIC CARTOONS

Each mosaic is first profiled 1:1 in charcoal on kraft paper, pinned to the long studio wall. The pattern is walked, lived with, and corrected in person — never on screen.

PLASTER MODELS FOR VAULTS

Domes and vaults are studied first in clay, in the studio, at 1:20 scale — a method drawn directly from a manuscript leaf in the Albani archive at Urbino.

STRUCTURAL ENGINEER

MARCO FENOGLIO

ingegnere strutturale · Torino · II

Trained at the Politecnico di Torino and previously of Fenara & Milani, Marco has been the studio’s structural conscience since 2010. He calculates the masonry domes the studio will not, draws — on graph paper — and finds, only by confrontation, in three weeks what an analyst would have wanted in three months. He sees structure where the rest of us see thresholds, and has never started one.

MOSAIC MASTER

GIULIA TREVI

caputmusivaria · Trevi · III

Third-generation mosaist of the Trevi family of Tivoli, Giulia leads the on-site cures on every Atelier project from foundation to keystone. Her specialty is opus minutum — tesserae of 4 mm — and a roster of seven masons whose lineage in cut stone of seven masons works the cyma reversa best in Italy.
