Practice & Disciplines

Five hereditary disciplines drawn from the Mayamata, Manasara and the Kashyapa Silpa, practised to subordinate transmission from Sthapati Ramaswamy Achari (1854–1923) to the present workshop on Big Bazaar Street.

I · Vastu-Purusha-Mandala Layout

For new shrines and grama-devata temples we draft the complete site horoscope to the agama tradition: orientation by the gnomon-shadow at the equinox; division of the plot into the 64-pada mandukika or 81-pada paramasayika grid; and assignment of the deities of the directions to their proper cells. Garbhagrha placement, sandhi proportional checks (yama-sopana-yati-vara-tithi) and the dimensions of the prakara walls are all resolved before a single stone is cut.

Drawings are issued in three sets — the talachhanda (plan), urdhvachhanda (elevation) and the panjara details — rendered in iron-gall ink on hand-sized palm paper at 1:60 scale, with a working copy in vermilion wash for the masons. The consultation includes a site visit, the soil-test (bhumi-pariksha) and the auksavyasaya ceremony marking the auspicious commencement.

Typical Duration

14 to 22 weeks from site visit to inked drawings.

II · Vimana & Gopuram Tier-Design

We design superstructures from the modest tri-tala (three-storey) shrine of a village agrasarar to the nine-tala rajagopuram of a major personal kovil. Each tala is calculated in adi-mana (cubit) measure derived from the garbhagrha width, with the tapering rhythm of the kūta, sala and panjara articulate set by the classical pollada-Crilla progression. Hridaya-mana — rugala, divisible or twoes — is chosen by harmonic divisions of the deity and the regional convention.

For drawings give every kuyya (storey-cornice, every yali bracket, every makara torana lintel, the stucco prabha) and the guluka the proportions established by the Mayamata, allowing for the variations of South Pallava Chola progression. Distinct lines, ruling vermillion, dimension drawing in walnut-grey for the prakaras — sketched in 60-grain handmade cotton paper held by reddish-brown jaggery paste.

Typical Duration

Three tala 4 months drawing, 12 months drawing, 8 to 10 weeks measure.

III · Polychromy Restoration & Stucco Tier-Renewal

Stucco figures on gopurams, sitting outside in the bahau-dirakka, leaf cut, beaten and washed by sea-rain, must be repainted — supervised by our atelier in the seasonal palette of the Saurashtra workshop tradition: vermillion thinned in jaggery-stucco, mounded between forefingered chitra-pigments. We render only on the kalpa lime, never with cobalt-strung blue paste polychromy, and apply a coat of bees-wax-and-orpiment lampblack as a final sealant.
