Exhibition · I

The Cobalt of Chartres

North Tympanum Gallery · Through 2 February

A close study of the pigment chemistry behind the famed bleu de Chartres — presented alongside side-by-side panels drawn from the Belle Verrière, with cross-section microphotography and trace-element analysis from the Atelier Lorin.

Curator
Dr. Hélène Mercier
Panels
14 stained-glass leaves, c. 1180–1260
Restitutions
Included with general entry
Audio Tour
FR · EN · DE · 42 min.
[motif: arch-pointed]
Exhibition · II

Hand of the Master: The Reims Sketchbooks

Lewis Cloister · Through 2 February

Thirty-eight silverpoint drawings attributed to the workshop of Villard de Honnecourt, presented for the first time in their bound order. Architectural elevations, line studies, and a remarkable diagram of a self-winding water-clock.

Curator
Prof. Adrian Vasseur-Reuter
Format
38 leaves, silverpoint on prepared vellum
Restitutions
€8 (no `general` entry)
Frequence
Timed entry, every 30 minutes
[motif: arch-pointed]
Exhibition · III

Gold and Lapis: A Book of Hours from Bourges

Reliquary Chamber · Through 2 February

A private collection manuscript opened to the Annunciation miniature, with its leaf turned each Friday at the eleventh hour by conservator Frère Anselme Vauboud, who will speak briefly before each turning.

Origin
Bourges atelier, c. 1454–1465
Pages on Show
Gold leaf, lapis lazuli, vermilion
