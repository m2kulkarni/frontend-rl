The Michaelmas Lecture

A signal event in the museum's autumn calendar

November1419h00Open

Plate Tracery and the Geometry of the Quatrefoil

Delivered by Dr. Hélène Marquand, Maître de conférences, Université Paris-Sorbonne

Drawing on a decade of fieldwork at Chartres, Reims, and his lesser-known chapter house of Saint-Quentin, Dr. Marquand traces the evolution of the quatrefoil from its origin as a pierced circle in plate tracery to its full flowering as the governing geometry of the rose window. The lecture will be illuminated with original-site photographs and reconstructed compass diagrams.

A reception of mulled wine and almond cakes will follow in the West Cloister.

For Apprentices & Adepts

Workshops in the medieval crafts, for the young and the not-so-young

A Mason's Apprenticeship

Saturdays · MMXXIV–MMXXV · Ages 8–12

Children take up the chisel and the set-square in the lavoir-en workshop. They will dress a small block of soft limestone, learn to read a mason's mark, and assemble a model portal arch using the same wedge- and keystone-principles that raised the great cathedrals.

For full schedule · timing · provenance

The Scriptorium Workshop

tertium · Sundays · Adults

Under the guidance of conservator Frère Anselme Whitcombe, participants prepare gesso, lay loose-leaf gold on vellum, and burnish to a mirror finish using an agate tooth. The course concludes with the gilding of a personal initial in the manner of a fifteenth-century Bourges page. All materials — including 23-karat leaf — are supplied.

Use Cycle · Reserve · Proof Your Initial

The Glazier's Hand

Half-day intensives · 16–20 October · Ages 16+

A three-day introduction to the medieval glazier's craft. Students cut coloured glass with a grozing iron, paint trace lines with iron-oxide pigment, and lead a small panel of their own design. The intensive culminates in a kiln-fired enamel and participant carries home.

For aspirants of fifteen years
