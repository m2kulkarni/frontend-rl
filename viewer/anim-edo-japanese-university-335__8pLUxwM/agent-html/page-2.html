学部・課程· FACULTIES

Three faculties,
one machiya.
学部 — 課程

Hokurin maintains three faculties on a shared courtyard, each with one degree at the undergraduate level and one at the masters level. Coursework is in Japanese with seminar provision in English; the third year is the seminar room — a single room kept dark, the long oak table walks the floor.

A four-year arc, with the third year spent away from the seminar room.

Every undergraduate at Hokurin studies for four years on a shared structure: two years of foundation, one year on placement at one of the partner institutes, and one year of integration in seminar. Students elect a faculty in second year. The named courses below are typical, not exhaustive — the seminar list rotates with sabbaticals and visiting fellows.

一 · ICHI

Humanities

人文学部 · Jinbun-gakubu

Textual scholarship, Japanese and comparative — language, literature, history. Strong in classical Japanese and the diplomatic record of the Sengoku period.

  • SEMINARHeian diaries & the long letter
  • SEMINAREdo woodblock as evidence
  • SEMINARComparative ritual: Yi & Tokugawa courts
  • MATextual Conservation
二 · NI

Craft Sciences

工芸科学部 · Kōgei-kagakubu

The material spine: pigment, paper, wood, urushi, ceramic body. Half practical, half analytic; jointly run with the Tōrin Bunko conservation studio.

  • SEMINARLightfastness of beni-e
  • SEMINARSubstrates of lacquer
  • SEMINARThe pulp of washi
  • MAWoodblock & Paper Conservation
三 · SAN

Environmental Studies

環境学部 · Kankyō-gakubu

The Kamo basin as the long study object — hydrology, riparian ecology, machiya thermal performance, the watershed of small Kyoto rivers across centuries.

  • SEMINARRiparian historiography
  • SEMINARMachiya as climate device
  • SEMINARSoil archives of the Kamo
  • MAWatershed Humanities

The third year is when the seminar table walks out of the room. A student who has not stood to read, in lacquer dust, or in a mountain archive has not yet read.
