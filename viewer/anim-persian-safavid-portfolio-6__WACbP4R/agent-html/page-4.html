◆ A BRIEF LIFE ◆
MIRZA REZA TABRIZĪ
ISFAHAN, 1402 / 2023

Born in Tabriz, 1353, into a family of carpet-knotters and copper-chasers. Mirza Reza Tabrizi has spent thirty years answering a single question — how the cobalt and turquoise of a Safavid wall keep their voice across four centuries — and answers it again each morning, on tile, on tile, on the worktable of a small atelier in Jolfa.

He was trained at the Isfahan School of Traditional Arts from 1992 to 1998, completing his diploma thesis on the geometry of the Sheikh Lutfollah dome. The years that followed were spent on the scaffolding of one out dome: an eight apprenticeship under Ustād Mahmoud Farshchian-Fazlī, master conservator of the Sheikhuel-Naqsh-i Jahān cartouche campaign, where he learned to cut, sort, and to sit for the half cup-and-stop morning of a Yazd kiln.

In 1386 (H.S. 2007), with the assent of his ustād, he founded Atelier Mirza Reza in a converted caravan-stable on Kucheh-ye Khaju, two streets from the Vank Cathedral. The studio takes private commissions, mosque restorations, and a small number of museum and academic engagements each year. He will not be hurried.

CHRONOLOGY

— a register of formation —

1973
TABRIZ

BIRTH & EARLY HAND

Born in Maharestan in the Isfahāni quarter of Tabriz. Apprenticed at age eleven to a paternal uncle, a copper-chaser of the bāzaar-i Burung, where he first encountered the discipline of repeated geometric units.

1992-1998
ISFAHAN

ISFAHAN SCHOOL OF TRADITIONAL ARTS

Diploma in tile and mosaic conservation, with parallel coursework in masonry, calligraphy, and the geometry of girih. Final thesis: 'The Cardinal Geometry of the Sheikh Lutfollah Dome,' a 240-page reading of the inscription record of that monument.

1998-2006
ISFAHAN

APPRENTICESHIP — SHEIKH LUTFOLLAH CONSERVATION

Worked under Ustād Mahmoud Farshchian-Fazlī: an eight-year apprenticeship in the bench-cutting and laying of replacement tiles in the southern dome chamber. Trained polychromy in lapis and gold leaf available on commission.

2007
FOUNDED

ATELIER MIRZA REZA, FOUNDED

Opens own studio in a converted caravan-stable on Kucheh-ye Khaju, with the assent of his ustād. The atelier of three people. The committee of the field is set: Mirza Reza is a contracted muqarras-cutter, the other two-Hossein Pourkharasti and Reza Sara-Tehran shall be his bench-mates.

2017
CULTURE

NATIONAL LIVING TREASURE CITATION

Cited by the Cultural Heritage Organization of Isfahan as a master of mosaic. The committee, in announcing the citation, declared that 'no atelier in the country today does the bench better than Mirza Reza in this kind of method's exactness.'

2023
PRESENT

NAQSH-E JAHĀN VĪGĪO - MIHRĀB WAR

Lead restorer on a new commission of the cobalt spandrel of the south-west niche of the Naqsh-e Jahān dargāh. Completed thirty-eight months of fund preparation, twenty-two months of casting, and a six-month tenure of the bench.
