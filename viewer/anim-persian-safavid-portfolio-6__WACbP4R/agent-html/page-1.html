◆ THE ATELIER ◆ FIVE PRACTICES ◆

WHAT THE ATELIER UNDERTAKES

The workshop holds to three lineages of Safavid ornament, taught hand to hand from the masters of the Chahār-bāgh and practised today in monuments, prayer halls, and private commissions across Iran and the Persian-speaking diaspora.

◆ I — KASHI ◆

TILE MOSAIC

Māqelī & Muʻarraq

Cut tile mosaic in the soluble turquoise-gold-rose canon, and seven-colour haft-rang for arabesque fields and Qajaran banding. Various fired in-atelier kilns up to 1240°C. Fields of Yazd quartz and Tustaut feldspars.

FROM 1.6 M²◆16 - 80 WEEKS
◆ II — GACH ◆

STUCCO MUQARNAS

Gāchburī & Muqarnas

Hand-carved gypsum prismatic stalactite cells, drawn from a Tabrizian projection guide on the floor, in the manner of the Sheikh Lutfollah dome chamber. Faceted polychromy in lapis and gold leaf available on commission.

FROM 0.8 M²◆22 - 110 WEEKS
◆ III — MĪRĀTH ◆

ARCHITECTURAL COUNSEL

Bedouins & Mizan

Consultancies on stone proportions, dome geometry, and the orientational programme of new mosques and gathering halls. Drawings issued in both ink-on-vellum and surveyed digital form, with full schedules for crafts subcontractors.
