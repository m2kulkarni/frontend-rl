Current Exhibitions

Three temporary exhibitions on view through Candlemas, the second day of February in the year of our Lord two thousand and twenty-five.

[motif: quatrefoil]
[motif: arch-pointed]
Exhibition · I

The Cobalt of Chartres

Hall Twelve · Through 2 February

A close study of the pigment chemistry behind the famed Bleu de Chartres — presented alongside side-by-side panels drawn from the Belle Verrière, with cross-section microphotography and trace-element analysis from the Atelier Loire.

Curator
Dr. Hélène Massot
Panels
14 stained glass leaves, c. 1180 – 1230
Conservator
Atelier Loire, Chartres
Catalogue
pp. 128 — 168, plates
[motif: arch-pointed]
Exhibition · II

Hand of the Master: The Reims Sketchbooks

Lectern Closet · Through 2 February

Thirty-eight silverpoint drawings attributed to the workshop of Villard de Honnecourt, presented for the first time in their bound order. Architectural elevations, line studies, and a remarkable diagram of a self-winding water-clock.

Curator
Prof. Adrien Trouvet-Roche
Drawings
38 leaves, silverpoint on prepared vellum
Mounting
Cradled, 30 lux maximum
Catalogue
Hand-stitched, 80 colotype plates
[motif: arch-pointed]
Exhibition · III

Gold and Lapis: A Book of Hours from Bourges

Reliquary Chamber · Through 2 February

A private collection manuscript — opened by the demonstration in two leaf turns each Friday at the eleventh hour by conservators. Père Anselme Vandevel, who will speak briefly before each turning.

Folios
Bourges atelier, c. 1410 – 1425
Pigment
Gold leaf, lapis lazuli, vermilion
Page Turns
Fridays, 11h00 sharp
Capacity
20 visitors per turning
