Three Hinges

A Brief Chronicle of the Museum

1973

Founded under the directorship of Hossein Ahmadi M. ‘Asadi as a centre for Safavid material culture, in two pavilions on the southern flank of Chahar Bagh.

1983

Reorganised as an autonomous museum under the Ministry of Cultural Heritage, with the Royal Atelier Library set apart as a research collection.

2008

Mehrnaz Ahmadi M. is appointed second director, opening the Reconstructed Iwan and the museum's seven-hall plan as it stands today.

Of First Principles

Charter of the Museum

The Museum of Isfahan exists to study, conserve, and present the material culture of the Safavid empire — its tilework, its manuscripts, its silks, its arms, its alloyed coins, and the architectural fragments of its great square. The collection is to be held in trust for the people of Iran and made available without charge to children and to credentialed scholars.

The museum is governed by a board of nine, elected each five years by the membership of the Atelier Friends; the director sits ex officio. The board's decisions, like the museum itself, are to be conducted in the open record of the Office of the Director.

— Introduced & Held to in Memorial, 1973 —
