Hall II ✦ The Permanent Collection

Galleries of theSafavid Court

Seven halls trace two centuries of imperial patronage — from the cobalt tilework of the Maydān-i Naqsh-i Jahān to the inlaid steel of the arsenal of Shah Sultan Husayn. Each gallery is curated as a chapter in a single argument: that under the Safavids, Isfahan became, as the saying goes, half of the world.

Gallery VII ✦ Architecture

The Reconstructed Iwan

Architecture / Fragments
2,346 elements
Provenance
1611–1671 ✦ Naqsh-i Jahān Square

A full-scale iwan reassembled from salvaged tile, stucco, and muqarnas elements rescued during the 1968 conservation of the northern garden quarter. The vault retains 84% of its original half-rang surface; the dado was reconstructed from kiln-fired tonics matched to spectroscopic analysis of the surviving panels.

Curator of ArchitectureDr. Khosrow Ja'fari-Esfahani
