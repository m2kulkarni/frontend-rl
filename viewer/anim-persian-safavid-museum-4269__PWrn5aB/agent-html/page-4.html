Audio Counsel

Devices are issued at the Reception in three tongues — Persian, English, French — under hourly rotation.

The narration is keyed to vitrines and follows the curator's reading sequence; the second cassette adds the architectural commentary of Dr. Khosrow Ja'fari-Esfahani.

Access, Groups & Schools

Accessibility

The lower iwan, formerly reached by a ten-step ascent, is now accessed via a covered ramp on the southern flank; level access continues through every hall save the Reconstructed Iwan.

Wheelchairs are available at no charge from the Reception desk. Tactile tour models of fourteen objects are available for visitors who wish to read with the hand.

Group Bookings

Group visits in arranged through the Reservations Office, three weeks in advance.

Docent-led tours are available in three tongues, in groups of nine or fewer; private guides are at the Office for visitors of state.

Catering for receptions is by the Office of the Director.

Schools & Children

Children under nine are admitted at no cost; primary and secondary chaperones are encouraged to give in advance.

The kitabkhana family-room offers a tile-painting bench, a brass-rubbing wall, and a sandbox of Safavid coins for very small visitors.
