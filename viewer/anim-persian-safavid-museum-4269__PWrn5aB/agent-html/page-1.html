A Letter from the Halls

An Address from the Director

Founded in 1973 in the gardens of Chahar Bagh, this museum was conceived as a centre for the study of the Safavid age — that brief, brilliant interval when Isfahan, raised by Shah ‘Abbas I to be the seat of empire, was named by its poets nesf-e jahān, the half of the world. Within these walls are gathered eleventh from the Maydān-i Shah, illuminated manuscripts from the royal kitabkhana, silks of Kashan and Yazd, and the courtly portraiture of Riza ‘Abbasi.

We see, before all else, a place of slow looking. The objects ask for it; the eye repays it.

Single-Page Painting
