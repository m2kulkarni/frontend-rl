Visiting the Divān

Practical orders for those who present themselves to seek a hearing — bring witnesses

To Find Us

Beneath the northern arcade of the Maydān-e Naqsh-e Jahan, between the Qaysarieh Gate and the Mosque
of Sheikh Lotfollāh.

The Address

Dīvān-i Isfahan

Northern Arcade, third bay east of the Qaysarieh Gate

Maydān-e Naqsh-e Jahan

Isfahan, Domains of His Majesty Shah ʿAbbas the Great

By Which Gate

Visitors entering by the Qaysarieh shall keep the cobalt arcade upon their left hand.

Those approaching from the southern bazaar pass first the Mosque of Sheikh Lotfollāh; the office lies seven arches beyond.

Look for the gilded star above the lintel.

Mounts & Caravans

Riding-animals — horses, mules, and camels — are to be left at the Southern Caravanserai beside the Bazaar of the Saddlers.

A stable-fee of two shahīs per beast per day is collected by the master of the yard.

No animal may enter the Maydan during hours of audience.

The Path Through the Maydān

For each per hour, per chamber — sealed by the season of spring

1. Enter the Maydān by the Qaysarieh Gate, set the cobalt-tile portico upon your left.

2. Pass beneath the third lantern-bay; the silversmiths' booths shall be open by morning.

3. Cross the carriage-way into the central garden; the watercourse runs east-to-west by the seven beams.

4. At the bowing fountain, ascend the four steps to the northern arcade.

5. The third bay shall bear the gilded star — passing the silver mongers' arches, the office shall be open before you.

Plan of the Maydan

Conduct Within the Courtyard

For decorum upon hearing days — observed by all who present petition

YOU MAY & SHOULD

· Bring two witnesses, one of whom shall be a male of the quarter.

· Carry the sealed petition in cobalt cord, folded in the manner of three quarters.

· Speak upon being called only — and stand throughout the hearing.

· Address the bench by the title "Hazrat-e Dīwānbegi".

· Receive your decree by the hand of the chief scribe before departing.

YOU MUST NOT

· Bear arms within the courtyard. — Daggers are taken at the gate by the marshal of the watch.

· Speak above the call of the chief scribe — three offences shall close your hearing.

· Approach the bench upon any pretence of gift or favour.

· Bring a kinsman to plead for you, save where injury prevents your standing.

· Linger after the call of asr; the courtyard shall be cleared by the watch.

BY THE HAND OF THE DIVĀNBEGI

May this folio be a guide to all who would have just hearing of His Majesty's officer in this great city.
The doors stand open every morning, save Friday, save Nowruz.
