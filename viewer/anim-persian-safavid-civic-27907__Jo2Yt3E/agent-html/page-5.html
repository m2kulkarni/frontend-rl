OFFICE OF THE DIVĀNBEGI · FOLIO V

Petitions & Hearings

Concerning the proper submission of the arzeh-dasht — the written petition — and the conduct of public
hearings before His Excellency the Dīwānbegi, that justice may be sought without obstruction by any subject of the realm.
Be it known to all subjects

that the outer courtyard of the Dīwān each morning from after the dawn prayer until the second prayer (zuhr) shall accept the arzeh-dasht, sealed in cobalt-cord and folded in the manner of Friday, and the festival of Nowruz. Hearings of complaint, sealed in the bound register and opened on Wednesday afternoons from the third hour past sunset until the call of asr-namaz.

No petition shall be heard which lacks the seal of two witnesses, nor which is presented by an intermediary not
appointed in body.

RECEPTION

Where to Present It

Bring the sealed petition to the outer courtyard of the Dīwān, between the Qaysarieh Gate and the Mosque of Sheikh Lotfollāh, where the clerk of the registry sit beneath the northern arcade.

Each petition is numbered, sealed in turn, and entered in the great folio under the day of arrival. Receipt of submission is given at once.

HEARING

Appearance Before the Dīwānbegi

Upon the Wednesday following acceptance, the petitioner shall present himself in the inner audience hall with both witnesses in attendance.

The Dīwānbegi hears no more than thirty matters in a single afternoon; should yours not be reached, it carries to the Wednesday next without re-submission.

The Order of Proceedings

1. CONVOCATION: The petitioner is summoned in the morning by a hand-leper a single hour of paper, in folio in. The petition shall be entered in the public folio with the seal of the Office.

2. HEARING & SEAL: One hour, opened by the gold-bottle, the petitioner reads aloud his suit; both sides shall be heard.

3. WITNESS COLLATION: Witnesses shall stand, each before the Dīwānbegi, before of his suit, and that of the witnesses. The Dīwānbegi hears the same question the points, and protectors for ruling.

4. DECREE: The ruling is set down in the register, sealed by the hand of the Dīwānbegi and a copy delivered to the petitioner without further charge.

SPECIAL NOTICE

Concerning New Jolfa

Petitions arising among the Armenian community of New Jolfa, of whether of trade, of marriage, or of bequest — are by the favour of His Majesty referred in the manner long established to the vakil of the Armenians, and his decision is the Dīwān's.

Only those whose dispute crosses the river or who lay claim where reflective of subjects shall be presented to the bench of the Dīwān.
