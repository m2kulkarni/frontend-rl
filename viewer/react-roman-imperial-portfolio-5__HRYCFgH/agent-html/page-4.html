Drawings & Ornament

Plates of works on paper from the studio of Lucia Marciana — the studio's principal lines of practice — architectural elevations, mosaic cartoons, and ornamental cartouches drawn for private commission.

ach plate gathered here is offered as a commissioned work, drawn by hand at the bench on the via dei Coronari. The selection covers the studio's principal lines of practice — architectural elevations rendered in ink and grey wash, full-scale templates for coffered ceilings, running borders in the Greek manner, and laurel cartouches for inscription above doorways and funerary tablets. ❦ Originals travel rolled in linen sleeves; numbered editions of three are pulled on request.

All originals are drawn at a scale of 1 : 50 on Fabriano Roma laid paper, 65 grams the sheet, deckle-edged, with a hand-pulled watermark. Inks are oak gall and lampblack; washes are bone-black and burnt sienna. Each plate is signed in red lead at the lower right and dated in Roman numerals.
