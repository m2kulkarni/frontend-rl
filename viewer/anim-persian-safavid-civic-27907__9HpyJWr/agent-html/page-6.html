The Path Through the Maydan

A simple guide for the visitor unaccustomed to the precincts

Step by Step

I.Enter through the Qaysarieh gate beneath the bronze tympanum, and pass beneath the great arch.
II.Bear right into the northern arcade, keeping the Masjid-i Shah Lutfullah upon your left.
III.Pass beneath the second tilework arch with the inscription of His Majesty's reign.
IV.Enter the courtyard of the Dīvān; the chief porter shall direct you to the appropriate office.
V.Approach the petition-master at the receiving stone, with seal and witnesses in hand.

Plan of the Maydan

Conduct Within the Courtyard

For the keeping of decorum before His Excellency

Your Voice is Heard

  • Address the Dīvānbegi as Janab-i ‘Ali, with hands at the breast.
  • Speak slowly and once; the scribe must record without confusion.
  • Bring a witness, that two voices may stand for one matter.
  • Cite by date and place, that no time be wasted in supposition.
  • Hold your patience; the assembly is many but the Dīvān is one.

Your Conduct is Scribed

  • Refrain from raised voice or gesture, lest the matter be dismissed.
  • Bear no arms within the inner courtyard; the porter shall hold them.
  • Cover the head in the presence of the assembly, that respect be shown.
  • Do not swear upon the holy book save when called.
  • Honour the verdict as spoken — appeal lies with the Royal Chancery.

By the Hand of the Dīvānbegi

— Sealed in the precincts of the Naqsh-e Jahan, in the year of His Majesty's reign one thousand and thirty-seven.
