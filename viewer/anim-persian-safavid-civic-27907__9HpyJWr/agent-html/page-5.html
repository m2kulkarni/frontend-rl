Petitions & Hearings

Concerning the proper submission of the arzeh-shabi — the written petition — and the conduct of public hearings before His Excellency the Dīvānbegi, that justice may be sought without obstruction by any subject of the realm.

Be it known to all subjects

that all hearings of the Dīvān seek nothing more than the direct address of the inhabitant before His Highness the Dīvānbegi by Friday and the festal of Wednesday in due. Be received from the official scribes of this office on Wednesday afternoons, from the third hour after the morning of asr until the call to the prayer of asr.

No petition shall be heard which lacks the seal of attestation, nor which is presented by an intermediary save where infirmity be proved to body.

Of Reception

Where to Present It

Bring the arzeh-shabi to the outer courtyard of the Dīvān, between the Qaysarieh and the Masjid-i Shaikh Lutfullah, where the chief scribe shall receive it against the register and sign in receipt.

Each petition is numbered and sealed, and entered upon the daily ledger before the second prayer of asr.

Wednesdays Before Asr

Of Hearing

Appearance Before the Dīvānbegi

Upon the Wednesday following acceptance, the petitioner shall present himself in the outer audience hall half an hour after the dawn prayer.

The Dīvānbegi hears no more than eleven matters in a sitting; a single afternoon shall suffice for verdicts touching ten thousand dinars or fewer. By Wednesday next, the matter shall be answered.

The Order of Proceedings

I.Convocation. The petitioner is entered into the daily ledger upon arrival, of receipt at the gates by the chief porter.
II.Address & Reply. The chief of legal cuckers, with one trusted scribe of the Dīvān present, calls the petition.
III.Hearing. Upon the appointed day each petition shall be heard.
IV.Witnesses. Up to four witnesses may be called.
V.Verdict. Upon the rising of the assembly, the verdict shall be entered into the registers, sealed with the official seal, and posted upon the wall of the outer courtyard within three days of the hearing.
VI.Decree. The ruling as is borne in the register, sealed by the hand of the Dīvānbegi and a copy delivered to the petitioner without further charge.
Special Notice

Concerning New Jolfa

Petitions arising from the Armenian community of New Jolfa — whether of the Christian merchants or of the makers of the seal — may be borne in the seven days established by the parish of the Most Vehemence in their respective tongues. Native Armenian and Pers writing alike shall be received, by their pleas, by witnesses with seals, and shall be entered into the register through the favour of the Dīvān, by the offices of the brothers of the Vank Cathedral.

The cleric shall mediate to facilitate where the petition crosses tongue and writing of the Diwan.
