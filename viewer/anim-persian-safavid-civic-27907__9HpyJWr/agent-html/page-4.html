Folio IV · The City Under Hand

Hereunder are recorded the labours undertaken by command of the Dīvānbegi for the maintenance of His Majesty's capital — the watercourses, the spans of stone, and the lodgings for traders — together with the names of those master-builders to whom each charge is committed, and the qua'em (mahalla) answerable in attendance.

A Word of Thanks unto the Quarter of Jolfa

To the Christian community of New Jolfa, beyond the river

The Dīvān records, with the gratitude of the lord of the Naqsh-e Jahan, the gift of plane-wood timber furnished by the merchants and householders of the Jolfa quarter for the renewal of the bridge parapets. Sixty beams, dressed and seasoned, were brought across the Zayandeh-rud at the cleaners' own expense, and shall be set in place by the hand of Ustad Sultan-Mohammad. May their generosity be remembered in the chronicles of the reign.

— Sealed in the Royal Chancery, the 11th day of Awwal, 1037 A.H.
