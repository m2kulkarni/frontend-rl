Dīvān-i Isfahan

Civic Register of the Naqsh-e Jahan

Seated in the northern arcade of the Maydan-e Naqsh-e Jahan, between the Qaysarieh Gate and the Masjid-i Shah Lutfullah, this office stands beneath the eye of His Majesty Shah ‘Abbas, the prince of the world. Here is kept the chronicle of edicts, prices, and public works for the welfare of the city and the keeping of the law.

Notices of the Week

Three matters published this day for the city of Isfahan

From the Royal Court

Edict of His Majesty
Shah ‘Abbas

Be it known to all merchants, caravan-masters, and travellers passing through the Maydan that the new caravanserai at Madar-i Shah, erected by royal command, shall open its gates to lodgers from the first of Jumada al-Awwal next.

Tariffs upon the silk of Gilan, raw and woven, are by the same hand reduced by one part in ten, that the trade of His Majesty's domains may flourish.

Public Notice

Notice — Zayandeh-Rud
Canal Works

The Master of Waters announces that, beginning the fifth of this month, the dredging and re-lining of the Madi of Farahabad shall commence, with labour drawn from the wards of Joubareh and Dardasht.

Watering of orchards along the southern channel shall be permitted only between the second prayer and the call of asr-time for the duration of forty days.

Any diminution of the works, or diverting of waters without leave, shall be answered before the Dīvānbegi.
— Mirza Taqi, Mir-Āb of Isfahan

By the Dīvānbegi and his Scribes
