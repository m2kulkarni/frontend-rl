Notice No. C/24/PW · 058 · Traffic

Road Closure · Chitrai Veethi during the Brahmotsavam Temple-Car Festival

The Authority informs the residents and travelling public that, on the days of the Brahmotsavam (28 Oct to 04 Nov), the Chitrai Veethi will remain closed to all wheeled traffic between sunset of the eve and the first hour after the procession passes. Diversions are notified at the four cardinal entries.

Inconvenience caused is regretted. Festival route as published in the Antiquarian's Map (1898) of the precinct, with adjustments for present-day pedestrianisation.
