学部・課程

A four-year arc, with the third year spent away from the seminar room.

Every Hokurin undergraduate follows the same threefold rhythm. The first year is a shared foundation — classical Japanese, English or a second European language, and a methodology core taught in the 1762 machiya complex along Sanjō-dōri, the university moved its main campus to the Higashiyama ward and recognized its faculties around three commitments: textual care, material craft, and the long ecology of the Kamo basin.

The third year is the fieldwork year, mandated 1989 a minimum of twenty-four weeks of lacquered conservation atelier or a working machiya kiln, a Kamo basin restoration plot, or a Tōhoku ware& village. The fourth year returns to a graduating thesis, defended in March before two faculty and one external reader.

YEAR ONE

Shared foundation in classical Japanese, languages, and methodology.

YEAR TWO

Faculty selection; twelve students per cohort.

YEAR THREE

Fieldwork — 24 weeks at conservation atelier, kiln, or restoration plot.

YEAR FOUR

Thesis defended in March before two faculty and an external reader.
