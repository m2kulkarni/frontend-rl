三ADMISSIONS·入学のご案内

Apply to
Hokurin.

Hokurin admits a small undergraduate cohort and graduate intake each autumn. The application is in three windows — by spring, by midsummer, and by November — chosen by the candidate's faculty. Below: requirements, an indicative fee table, and a complete documents list. International applicants apply through the same windows under a separate review.

三THREE INTAKE WINDOWS入試日程
早期 · STANDARD

Standard Domestic Cycle

1 September 2025

The principal autumn intake. Portfolio, two letters, and a 2,000-character statement on the candidate's reading practice. Interviews held in October at the Sanjō Gate genkan.

後期 · LATE CYCLE

Late Cycle & Transfers

15 January 2026

For working conservators, transfer candidates, and those returning to study. A short atelier visit substitutes for the standard interview when geographic distance requires.

継続審査 · ROLLING

Rolling Review

Year-round

Reserved for international applicants on a research visa, and for designated artist-in-residence postings. Reviewed by a standing committee that meets monthly.
