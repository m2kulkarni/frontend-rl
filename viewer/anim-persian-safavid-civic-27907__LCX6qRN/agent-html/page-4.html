Folio IV · The City Under Hand

Hereunder are recorded the labours undertaken by command of the Diwánbegi for the maintenance of His Majesty's capital — the watercourses, the spans of stone, and the lodgings for travellers — together with the names of those master-builders to whom each charge is entrusted, and the quarter (mahalla) answerable to oversight.

Works Now in Hand

Three undertakings, by the will and impense of the City.

Work the Third

Dredging of the Madi-ye Náṣirín

The deepening of the upper-canal feeding the orchards of Hasanábád and the close of Chahár Bāgh is to be resumed, sluice-gates of cedar shall be replaced and the brick lining renewed when the work commences.

Master Builder
Ustād Mīrza Jaʿfar the Mason of Yazd
Quarter Answerable
Mahalla-yi Hasanábád
Workmen Engaged
Forty-two diggers, eight mason
Allotted From Treasury
Three hundred and twenty tomans
Expected Completion
16th day of the month of Jumādá Akhar, 1037 A.H.
In Progress · Second Month

Work the Second

Repair of the Allahverdi Khan Bridge

The parapets of the first arch upon the Joulfa bank, beset by the inundation of the foretime year, shall be drawn down from the quarry of Najafábád. Four arches on the eastern flank receive their re-pointing.

Master Builder
Ustād Hossein Muhammad of Tabrīz, stone-cutter
Quarter Answerable
Mahalla-yi Joulfa, with the levy of the Armenian guild
Workmen Engaged
Five hundred and ten masons
Allotted From Treasury
Six hundred tomans
Expected Completion
The 1st of Mihrigan, season of fall
Bridge Closed By Foot · Foot Passage By North Arch

Work the Sixth

New Gate of the Caravanserai at Darb-e Kušk

A vaulted twin gate of fired brick, faced with cobalt and turquoise tile in the manner of the Lotfollah, raised at the western entry of the caravanserai newly endowed by the kalāntar over the merchants of Yazd.

Master Builder
Ustād Reza Beg of Yazd, tile-cutter
Quarter Answerable
Mahalla-yi Darb-e Kušk
Workmen Engaged
One hundred and seventy
Allotted From Treasury
Two hundred tomans
Expected Completion
Within ninety days from the foundation laid
Foundation Laid · Visiting To Begin

A Citation of Honour

A Word of Thanks unto the Quarter of Jolfa

To the Christian community of New Jolfa, beyond the river

The Diván records, with the gratitude of His and the lordly honour of His Majesty, the gift of plane-wood timber furnished by the merchants and householders of the Jolfa quarter for the renewal of the bridge parapets. Sixty beams, dressed and seasoned, were brought across the Zayande-rud at the citizens' own expense, and shall be set in place by the hand of Ustād Sufyán Muhammad. May their generosity be remembered in the chronicles of the reign.
