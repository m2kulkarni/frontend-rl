REGISTER V · QUARTERS OF THE CITY

Quarters, Canals & Bazaars

Being a true register of the twelve administrative quarters of Iṣfahān, the four principal mādīs that water the city, and the eight-and-thirty bazaar-guilds confirmed by the office of the Muḥtasib.

COMPILED IN THE REIGN OF SHĀH ʿABBĀS II 1108 A.H. YEAR 1107 A.H. SHAWWĀL

Wherever order edifices below the Dīvān, or would by petition concerning water-share, market-stall, or boundary of quarter, shall consult these tables. Names of the kadkhudāan (quarter-elders), wīsīrān (water-wardens), and amīnān (guild-masters) are set forth herein, with their audience-days at the Quṣariyya Gate, the books along the Chahār Bāgh, and the western almoners of the Khvājū Bridge.
