REGISTER II · ḤUKM-NĀMA-yi ʿĀMM-i SHAHR

Edicts & Public Notices

From the bureau of the Madīh̲ḥī of the Royal Maydān, Iṣfahān

By the grace of the Sublime and under the seal of the Dīvān-i Shahr, the following farāmīn and ḥaẓar proclamations were affixed to the gates of the Quṣariyya, the Masjid-i Jāmiʿ, and the four caravanserais of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, beginning the fourteenth day of Rabīʿ al-Awwal in the year one thousand one hundred and eight of the Hijra. Citizens of the Jūbāra, Dardasht, ʿAbbās-ābād, and Bīdābād quarters are charged to attend their reading.

12 Rabīʿ AL-AWWAL · 1108 · 18 October 1696

On the Standard Weight of the Common Loaf (nān-i sangak)

— Issued by Mīr ʿAbd al-Rashīd, Madīhḥ-ī of the Royal Maydān

It is known to every baker (nānvā) of the four quarters that, by command of the Dīvān, the loaf of sangak shall not weigh less than one ʿabbāsī of half-mīsqāl drawn fresh from the oven. The price is fixed at one shāhī and a half. Any baker found light by the inspector's scale shall forfeit one day's flour to the poor of the Maydān-i Jurʾāt.

[motif: islamic-star]FARMĀN II
15 RABĪʿ · AL-AWWAL · 1108 · 21 October 1696

Repair of the Public Fountain (saqqā-khāna) in the Jūbāra Quarter

— Issued by Ḥājjī ʿAlī ʿAlī, Inspector of Waters

The qanāt-fed fountain at the foot of the Jūbāra Tūsīrū-baḥrīh̲ Madīh̲, having fallen into ruin since the floods of Muḥarram, is hereby ordered restored. Master masons Ustād ʿAbduʾl-Karīm is appointed; the cost of forty-two tomāns shall be drawn from the wāqf of the late Mīrzā Ṣafī. Residents of Jūbāra are forbidden to draw upon the waters of the Madrasa-yi Chahār Bāgh in the interim.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
[motif: islamic-star]FARMĀN III
19 RABĪʿ · AL-AWWAL · 1108 · 25 October 1696

Prohibition of Grazing within the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān

— Issued by Ūr-i ʿArabīkhām of the Quṣariyya, Madīh̲ḥī of the Royal Maydān

No man shall lead sheep, goat, mule, nor camel upon the gravels of the Royal Square between the Qaysariyya gate and the Masjid-i Shāh. The herdsmen of the southern villages shall pass instead by the way of Darvāza-yi Ḥasan-ābād. A first transgression carries a fine of two ʿabbāsī; the second, the confiscation of the beast for the royal stables.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
[motif: islamic-star]FARMĀN IV
23 RABĪʿ · AL-THĀNĪ · 1108 · 21 November 1696

On the Sweeping of the Bāzār-i Buzurg before the Friday Prayer

— Issued by Āqā Kūk, Worker of the Quṣariyya

Each shopkeeper of the long bāzār, from the spice-vault of the bear bāz Mīsh Tāqī unto the gate of the coppersmiths, shall sweep his threshold and water the dust thereof one hour before the noon-call of every Friday. Inspectors of the office shall pass at the third gong; refuse left in the channel shall be carted to the river at the offender's expense.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
[motif: islamic-star]FARMĀN V
02 RABĪʿ · AL-THĀNĪ · 1108 · 26 November 1696

Concerning the Quality of Indigo Sold in the Sarā-yi Gulshan

— Issued by Mīr Hāqī, Examiner of Dyes

Merchants importing indigo of Bayāna and of Sarīsh̲ān shall present each bale to the dye-examiner before opening sale. Adulteration with charcoal or with the lees of pomegranate is forbidden, the penalty being the burning of the parcel in the maydān and a fine of five tomāns to the treasury of the Dīvān.
