By a firmness in the rendition of the Naqsh-e Jahān quarters, the Mādih̲ī of the Quṣariyya, and all householders within the watered districts of this royal city, that the gate of this Bureau stands open from dawn prayer until the closing of the great drums at sunset, that no petition shall be turned away, and that the ledgers of water, tax, and lineage are kept in good order for the welfare of the people and the dignity of the Crown.

Sealed this seventeenth day of Rabīʿ al-Awwal, by hand of
Mīrzā Ḥusayn Khān Shīrāzī
Mayor of the Quarters · Kalāntar-i Maḥallāt

Allotment Schedule for the Mādī Nāṣarem Canal

The mādī has set the rotation: gardens of Jūbāra at first light on Saturday, the cloth-dyers of Bīd-ābād by midday Monday, the orchards beyond the Khājū bridge at dusk Wednesday. Disputes shall be heard at the canal-house each morning before the call to ẓuhr.

Receipts taken until 27 Rabīʿ al-Awwal · 4 days a-knaḥ
Caravan-Tax Filing Window Now Open

Merchants returning from Tabrīz, Shīrāz, and the Gulf ports must present manifests at the Counting Hall behind the Maydān. Silk are assessed at four-and-twenty per hundred; indigo and madder at one-tenth; precious stones by separate inspection. Bring two witnesses and the seal of your caravan-bāshī.

Until 29 Jamādī al-Awwal · 21 Rabīʿ al-Thānī · 1108
Observance for the Chief of the Cloth-Bazaar

The shops of the Qaysariyya silk-row shall close at midday for three days in honour of the late raʾīs. The funerary procession departs from the Masjid-i Ḥakīm after ʿaṣr prayer; condolences are received at the family-courtyard in the Dardasht quarter. May his memory move us among the lovers.

Hours of the Dīvān · Locations of Record

Chief Hall, north arcade of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, opposite the gate of the Caravanserai of Sayyid Ismāʿīl. Open daily save Friday, from the dawn azan to the setting of the sun. Subsidiary registers are kept at the Madrasa-i Chahār Bāgh (foreign & press notices) and at the Bridge of Allāhverdī Khān (river & tolls).
