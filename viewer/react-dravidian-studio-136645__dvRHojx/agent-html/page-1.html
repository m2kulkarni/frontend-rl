A fifth-generation atelier of sthapatis on East Chitra Veedhi, Madurai — founded by Sthapati Govindaraju Ramaswamy in 1887 within sight of the Meenakshi Amman gopurams. We draw, consult, and carve in the Dravida tradition: each line mandapam, polychromy for restored gopurams, and granite figuration cut by hand.

The Threefold Practice

From the drafting hall on East Chitra Veedhi the workshop attends to three disciplines, each governed by the Manasara and the Manasara silpa-sastra, each priced by hand-day and carried in Krishnagiri black granite or Sadarahalli grey.

I. Vastu Consultation
[motif: lotus-flower]

New Mandapas

Site orientation, axial calculation, and full ground-plan drawings for kalyana mandapas, navagraha shrines, and village amman temples. Each commission begins with a three-day visit to read the soil, the water-table, and the existing axes.

Drawings are inked in cotton paper at 1:48 and accompanied by a written sila-nyasa proportion table.

FROM₹ 1,80,000
II. Gopuram Restoration
[motif: peacock-ornament]

Polychromy & Stucco

Survey, condition report, and full-scale colour cartoon for the kosa, sikra, and stucco figures of storied gopurams. We work in lime and jaggery render and the twelve mineral pigments — vermilion, peacock, orpiment, ultramarine — fixed with kadukkai.

Scaffold engineering and bell-metal kalasha replacement are carried out with allied workshops in Pazamarainai.

FROM₹ 6,40,000
III. Granite Carving
[motif: conch-shell]

Figurative Commissions

Hand-carved sandstone and stone figures, pillared yali brackets, and door-jamb dvarapalakas, cut to silpa-sastra proportion by master craftsmen of Murugesan and his five apprentices.

Standard size for a seated Ganesha in Krishnagiri black is thirty-six angula, delivery in fourteen months from the day of stone selection.

FROM₹ 4,25,000

Current Commissions

Three works are presently under the chisel and the brush, each accepted with the blessing of the temple Atisthamna and the family priest.

I

Kalagopura Repainting · Tiruvannamalai

Eleven tier polychromy renewal for the western Rajagopuram of the Jambukeswarar temple — 1,488 stucco figures inventoried, pigments mixed on site in lime and jaggery, scaffold raised through the monsoon shadow.

IN PROGRESS2024 — 2026
II

Sixteen-Pillared Kalyana Mandapa · Kumbakonam

A new shukana-mandiri mandapa beside the Sarangapani tank in Sadarahalli grey granite. Sixteen yali-bracketed pillars, a central lotus-ceiling medallion, and four corner thoranagams drawn from our 1923 pattern book.

CARVING2025 — 2027
III

Private Navaratri Mandapa · Chettinad

A nine-tongued kola mandapa for the Nattukottai family of Karaikudi — twelve feet on a black granite plinth, with bas-relief Karkotaka and gold-leaf vimanam on the cornice. Consecration set for Panvurthi 2026.
