Est. Madurai · 1887

Selected Works · 1962 — Present

A register of principal commissions undertaken by three generations of the workshop, in the Dravida tradition, across the temple districts of Tamil Nadu.

✦ ✦ ✦ ✦ ✦ ✦ ✦ ✦ ✦

The list below is chronological and partial. Drawings, measured surveys, and site photographs of each work are held at the workshop in Madurai and may be consulted on appointment. Materials are recorded in bold; principal artisans are named where the workshop record is firm.

1962Vol. I
Repainting & Polychromy Restoration

Eastern Gopuram, Sri Andalia Temple

Madurai, Madurai District

Six-tier eastern gopuram repainted in mineral pigments over a fresh lime-and-jaggery ground. Original 19th-century stucco figures consolidated, not recarved. The vermilion of the kosa-bearer parapets was matched to surviving traces under the cornice.

Principal

  • Sthapati S. Ramaswamy
  • Lime mortar, jaggery, kadukkai fixings
  • Mineral pigments, peacock-blue ridge, gold-leaf in kalasha
1978Vol. II
New Construction · Sixteen-Pillar Hall

New Kalyana Mandapa, Srirangam Outer Prakara

Srirangam, Tiruchirappalli

Sixteen monolithic pillars with yali bases in the Tirumayam manner, drawn to a sixteen-square padmagharbha plan. Each yali was carved from a single block of Karaipannar charcoalite; the riders bear conch and dwarsa afterwards along the long axis.

Principal

  • Sthapati M. Ramaswamy
  • Karaipannar charcoalite
  • Sixteen yali brackets, kosa-square ceiling
1991Vol. III
Replacement of Figural Stucco

Kalasha Replacement (Stone Vessels), Sri Meenakshi Sundareswarar Private Shrine

Theni

The nine corroded brass kalashas of the family shrine were lowered, weighed, and replaced with new copper vessels gilded in twenty-three-carat leaf. The old vessels were ritually committed to the temple tank after kosabhishekam.

Principal

  • Sthapati S. Ramaswamy
  • Bell-metal copper, gilt leaf
  • Five-day consecration
2004Vol. IV
Consolidation of Seven Tiers · Polychromy

Polychrome Consolidation of Seven Tiers, Rajagopuram

Sri Andal Temple, Sivilliputhur

Survey, scaled drawings, and consolidation of seven tiers of stucco figures across the principal rajagopuram. Earlier cement repairs were removed and replaced with traditional lime mortar. No figures were re-modelled, only the missing limbs were left as voids and recarved.

Principal

  • Sthapati Selvaraj Ramaswamy
  • Lime, jaggery, fresh stucco
  • Mineral pigments, gold-leaf vimana
2017Vol. V
New Vimana, New Stucco-Cut Sanction

Three-Yala Vimana, Chettiar Kuladevata Shrine

Athangudi, Sivagangai District

A three-tala vimana raised over the existing garbhagriha for the family kuladevata. Drawn to the manasara canon at one-eighth scale; the corner karnakutas carry seated forms; the kalasha was cast in the central tala carries Garjabaikara. Consecrated at the Mahasundakkulasekara of June 2018.

Principal

  • Sthapati R. Ganesh Ramaswamy
  • Sadarahalli grey, vermilion
  • Brick base, lime modelling, gold-leaf kalasha
  • Two-week consecration
