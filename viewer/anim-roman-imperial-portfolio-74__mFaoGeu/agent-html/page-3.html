CRUSTA · PARIETES · RESTAURATIO

Within the atelier the mosaic bench has stood since 2008 — a long oak table, raked north light, and trays of marble cut to four millimetres. From the room Lucius Vennius works as both designer and conservator, setting new pavements for civic patrons and consulting on the recovery of antique surfaces from Insulids to Pompeii.

THE SOURCING OF TESSERAE

BIANCO DI CARRARA
tesserae · stone-cut

Cut from the Polvaccio quarry. In 30 kg billets and reduced by hand on the riobenche to 4 mm cubes. Vennius reserves the lightest tone for the highlight of an iris or a divine cheek.

ROSSO DI VERONA
ammite · sweet sanguine

A nodular ammonitic limestone whose iron-rich matrix gives the deep burnt coral the Romans called rubrum. The atelier draws this stone from the same vein in 2018 from Avesa.

PORFIDO EGIZIANO
imperial purple porphyry

Imperial purple porphyry, quarried only in the eastern Mons Porphyrites of Egypt and (since the 4th c.) impossible to obtain. A surviving Roman fragment is sourced through licensed Vatican & conservator's exchange of nineteenth-century Roman dispersals.

CONSERVATION METHOD

VII
PRINCIPLES

No surface should be cleaned more aggressively than the gentlest method that achieves the goal. Antique tesserae are lifted on plaster only when their substrate is failing, never for transport convenience. Where modern grout has been laid by careless hands of an earlier century, it is reduced and replaced with lime mortar, gauged thinly so the original tessera face stands proud as it once did.

Replacement tesserae are cut to match in stone and grain — never glass for stone surfaces, never modern smalti for antique smalti — and where ambiguity exists they are visibly distinguished from originals at the threshold of seven millimetres, so future eyes may read what was added by the conservator's hand.

THREE CASE STUDIES

I — VILLA QUINTILIA THRESHOLD

restoration · 2024 · ardesian black & bianco

Lifted in 14 panels by the team of Manlio Cessi and reset on a new lime substrate. The central rosette restored from a 19th-century watercolour record after the centre tesserae were lost to a flood of 1843.

SQ M 4.21,840 TESSERAEVI MONTHS

II — DOMUS PORTORIA · OPUS SIGNINUM

conservation · 2023 · cocciopesto with lime

The vast hall pavement of Domus Portoria had cracked along nineteen lines following a century of differential settlement. Each crack was injected with a custom lime grout and the inset tesserae of black palombino reset by hand.

III — MAUSOLEUM TUSCANIAE · APSE FLOOR

new design · 2022 · porphyry & smalti

An original composition for the funerary chapel of the Vincentini family. Concentric ellipses in deep porphyry meet at a central oculus of gold-leaf smalti, drawing the standing visitor's eye downward toward the inscribed name of the patron.
