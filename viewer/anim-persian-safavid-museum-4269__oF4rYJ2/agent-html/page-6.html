Museum & Society

Founded with fifty objects in the gardens of Chahar Bagh, the museum is a charitable instrument under the Ministry of Cultural Heritage, dedicated to the study, conservation and exhibition of the arts of Safavid Iran.

A Brief Chronicle of the Museum

1971

Foundational gathering of fifty objects from the Royal Collection in the gardens of Chahar Bagh.

1983

The Royal Library is moved into the Cherel Sotoun pavilion and the kitabkhana is reconstructed.

2008

The Tilework Wing is opened, doubling the gallery footprint and inaugurating the conservation studio.

Charter of the Museum

The Museum of Isfahan was founded as a charity to preserve, exhibit, and interpret the arts of the Safavid age — for the visitor, the scholar, and the citizen of Iran. Its work proceeds in three interlocking practices: the gallery, the library, and the workshop. Each holds the others to account.

The museum is governed by a Board of Trustees and led by an appointed Director, who answers in the first instance to the Ministry of Cultural Heritage and in the second to the public served by these halls. We aim to make the case for slow looking and stewardship in equal measure.
