The Course

献 立
[motif: kiku-chrysanthemum-mon]

A single progression, written each fortnight by Chef Naoki Mori.

The kappō counter seats seven. Each guest receives the same eight dishes in the same order, plated within arm's reach. There is no à la carte; there are no photographs. What follows is the menu for the current fortnight, transcribed below as it is written on the cedar board behind the counter.

一
先付
SakizukeOpening morsel

Kaki shiratama — Saijō persimmon, silken tofu, white miso veil.

二
椀物
WanmonoClear soup

Matsutake dobin-mushi — teapot broth of pine mushroom, hamo, sudachi.

三
向附
MukōzukeSliced raw

Akami zuke from Misaki — soy-cured tuna, fresh wasabi, nori shoyu.

四
焼物
YakimonoCharcoal grill

Sawara on hōba leaf — Spanish mackerel over binchōtan, magnolia smoke.

Kabocha and shungiku — pumpkin in dashi, garland chrysanthemum.

[motif: shippou-circles]
[motif: umebachi-plum-mon]

Pairing of sake from sixteen prefectures is offered alongside the course — six pours, chosen the morning of service. Substitutions for shellfish, dairy, and alcohol are accommodated with forty-eight hours' notice. The menu changes on the first and fifteenth of each month, in keeping with the old Edo calendar.
