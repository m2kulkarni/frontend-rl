Civic Register  ·  Bureau of Records of the Royal City

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

Be it known to the residents of the Naqsh-e Jahān quarter, the bāzārīs of the Qaysariyya, and all householders within the watered districts of this royal city, that the gates of this Bureau stand open from dawn prayer until the closing of the great drum at sunset, that no petition shall be turned away, and that the ledgers of water, tax, and lineage are kept in good order for the welfare of the people and the dignity of the Crown.

Sealed this seventeenth day of Rabīʿ al-Awwal, by hand ofMīrzā Ḥusayn Khān ShīrāzīMayor of the Quarters · Kalāntar-i Maḥallāt

19 Rabīʿ al-Awwal · 21 Sefer al-Awwal

Allotment Schedule for the Madī Nāṣirīʿ Canal

The mādī has set the rotation: gardens of Jūlfā at first light on Saturday, the cloth-dyers of Bīd-ābād in midday Monday, the orchards beyond the Khājū bridge at dusk Wednesday. Disputes shall be heard at the usual house each morning before the call to ẓuhr.

19 Rabīʿ al-Awwal · 21 Sefer al-Awwal

Caravan-Tax Filing Window Now Open

Receipts taken until 19 Rabīʿ al-Awwal—on a per-head basis. Merchants returning from Tabrīz, Shīrāz, and the Gulf ports must present manifests to the Counting Hall behind the Maydān. Silks at four-and-twenty per hundred, indigo at one-tenth; precious stones by separate inspection.

19 Rabīʿ al-Awwal · 21 Sefer al-Awwal

Observance for the Chief of the Cloth-Bazaar

Ustād Ibrāhīm Khorāsānī, master of the brocade-weavers, having reached his eighty-third year, the shops of the Cloth-Bazaar shall close at midday for three days in honour of his service. The Bureau procession departs from the Masjid's Dāhalīz after ʿaṣr; condolences are received at the Dardasht courtyard.

Hours of the Dīvān ✶ Locations of Record

Chief Hall, north arcade of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, opposite the gate of the Caravanserai of Sayyid Ismāʿīl. Open daily save Friday, from the dawn azān to the setting of the sun. Subsidiary registers are kept in the Madrasa-yi Chahār Bāgh (lineage & private endowments) and at the Bridge of Allāhverdī Khān (river & tolls).
