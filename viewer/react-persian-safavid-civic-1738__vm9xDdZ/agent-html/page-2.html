Edicts & Public Notices

From the bureau of the Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān, Iṣfahān

By the grace of the Shāhanshāh and under the seal of the Dīvān-i Shahr, the following farāmīn and ḥasan proclamations were affixed to the gates of the Qaysariyya, the Masjid-i Jāmiʿ, and the four caravanserais of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, beginning the fourteenth day of Rabīʿ al-Awwal in the one one thousand one hundred and eight of the Hijra. Citizens of the Jūbāra, Dardasht, ʿAbbāsi-Ābād, and Bīdābād quarters are charged to attend their reading.

12 RABĪʿ AL-AWWAL 1108 · 10 October 1696

On the Standard Weight of the Common Loaf (nān-i sangak)

— Sealed by Mīrzā ʿAbd al-Bāqir, Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān

Be it known to every baker baking of the four quarters that, by command of the Dīvān, the loaf of sangak shall not weigh less than nine ʿabbāsīd̄irhams when drawn from the oven. The price is fixed at one shahī and a half. Any baker found light by the inspector’s scale shall forfeit one day’s flour to the poor of the Masjid-i Jāmiʿ.

15 RABĪʿ AL-AWWAL 1108 · 18 October 1696

Repair of the Public Fountain (saqqā-khāna) in the Jūbāra Quarter

— Sealed by Ḥājjī Lutf-ʿAlī, Inspector of Waters

The qanāt-fed fountain at the head of the Jūbāra-i Buzurg, having fallen into ruin since the floods of Muḥarram, is hereby ordered restored. Master mason Ustād Jaʿfar of Ardestān is appointed; the cost of forty-two tūmān shall be drawn from the waqf of the late Mīrzā Taqī. Residents of Jūbāra are forbidden to draw upon the waters of the Mādī of Chahār Bāgh in the interim.

20 RABĪʿ AL-AWWAL 1108 · 23 October 1696

Prohibition of Grazing within the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān

— Sealed by the Dīvānbegī, Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān

No man shall lead sheep, goat, mule, nor camel upon the gravels of the Royal Square between the Qaysariyya gate and the Masjid-i Shāh. The herdsmen of the southern villages shall pass instead by the way of Darvāza-yi Ḥasan-Ābād. A first transgression carries a fine of two ʿabbāsīd; the second, the confiscation of the beast for the royal stables.

23 RABĪʿ AL-AWWAL 1108 · 26 October 1696

On the Sweeping of the Bāzār-i Buzurg before the Friday Prayer

— Sealed by Āqā Naṣr, Warden of the Qaysariyya

Each shopkeeper of the long bāzār, from the spice-vault of the Sarā-yi Mīnā Tāqī unto the gate of the coppersmiths, shall sweep the threshold and water the dust thereof one hour before the noon call of every Friday. Inspectors of the office shall pass at the third gong; whose fail in the cleared shall be carried to the river at the offender’s expense.

[motif: islamic-star]FARMĀN A.VI

25 RABĪʿ AL-AWWAL 1108 · 29 October 1696

Concerning the Quality of Indigo Sold in the Sarā-yi Gulshan

— Sealed by Mīrzā Bāqir, Examiner of Dyes

Merchants importing indigo of Bayāna and of Sufflej shall present each bale to the dye-examiner before opening sale. Adulteration with charcoal or with the lees of pomegranate is forbidden, the penalty being the burning of the parcel in the maydān and a fine of five tūmān to the treasury of the Dīvān.
