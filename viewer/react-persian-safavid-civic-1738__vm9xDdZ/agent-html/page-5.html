Register V · From the Vista

Quarters, Canals & Bazaars

Being a true register of the twelve administrative quarters of Iṣfahān, the four principal mādīs that water the city, and the eight-and-thirty bazaar guilds confirmed by the office of the Muḥtasib.

Compiled in the Reign of Shāh ʿAbbās II[motif: islamic-star]Year 1107 A.H. · Iṣfahān

Whereon order eighteen before the Dīvān, or would by petition concerning water-share, market-stall, or boundary of quarter, shall consult these tables. Names of the kadkhudāyān (quarter-elders), reīsh-i bazaar-watchers, and naqībān (guild-masters) are set forth herein, with their audience days at the Qaysariyya Gate, the books along the Chahār Bāgh, and the western abutments of the Khwājū bridge.
