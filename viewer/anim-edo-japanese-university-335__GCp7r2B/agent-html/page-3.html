入学のご案内 — ADMISSIONS

Apply to
Hokurin.

Hokurin admits a small undergraduate cohort and a smaller graduate-institute cohort each year — roughly 320 entering students across the machiya campus. The academic calendar offers three intake windows for AY 2025–2026, with rolling review for international applicants. We read each application by hand, in committee, and reply in the season we received it.

Three Intake Windows

三つの入試 ・ INTAKE CALENDAR 2025/2026
AUTUMN INTAKE · 秋募集

Standard Domestic Cycle

1 September 2025

Applications open early-July, close September 1. The standard cycle for Japanese-resident applicants. Portfolio & one-page essay. Interviews held mid-November.

WINTER INTAKE · 冬募集

Late Cycle & Transfers

15 January 2026

Reserved for transfer applicants from the National University Konsōrtium and re-admit applicants. Same materials as the autumn cycle; results posted in early March.

INTERNATIONAL · 国際募集

Rolling Review

Ongoing

For applicants from outside Japan. Applications are reviewed in committee within twelve weeks of receipt. JLPT N2 or equivalent recommended; written exam waived for applicants holding completed degree work.

Tuition & Fee Waivers

学費・奨学制度

Hokurin keeps tuition deliberately low. Roughly two-thirds of each cohort receives partial fee waivers; one-tenth receives full tuition support through the Tōrin Foundation.

Documents & Examination

出願書類および審査
DOCUMENTS
  • Application form ・ 願書
    Single A4 sheet, completed in ink, in either Japanese or English.
  • Secondary School Transcript
    Sealed transcript covering the final three years; translations may be appended but not substituted.
  • Letter of Reference · 推薦状
    From a working conservator, scholar, or teacher who has read the applicant's writing.
  • Photograph & identity copy
    One recent photograph; one copy of valid government identity.
  • Application fee · ¥9,800
    Waived for applicants holding a Tōrin Foundation pre-letter.
ENTRANCE EXAMINATION
  • Japanese & English
    Two short translation prompts, drawn each year from a published Edo-period text. 90 minutes.
  • Mathematics or World History
    One section, applicant's choice. Calculator provided. 60 minutes.
  • Faculty-Specific Conversation
    A 25-minute interview with two faculty readers in the applicant's stated wing.
  • Portfolio Review
    Optional for the Humanities wing; required for Craft Sciences and Environmental Studies.
