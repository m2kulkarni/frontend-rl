校舎と東山のしおり — CAMPUS & VISITING

Four buildings,
one quiet block
of Higashiyama.

The Hokurin campus occupies a single deep machiya plot off Sanjō-dōri, where a 1782 timber-framed merchant house still serves as its administrative centre. Around it three later buildings — a 1994 Andō concrete hall, a 2008 library annex, and a 2018 craft workshop — were each set carefully behind the original eaves line, leaving the street face unchanged. Visitors are welcome year-round, and twice a year for our open-campus days.

The four buildings

本館・南向き館・図書館別館・工房

Honkan · 本館 (1782)

FORMER MERCHANT COMPOUND

Two storeys of dark-pine post-and-beam, with the original sumi-glazed clay walls. The president's office is the upstairs front room, kept above 22°C only at the worst of January.

Funaki Hall · 南館 (1994)

SEMINAR & CONCERT HALL

An exposed-concrete hall by Andō Tadao, sited a generation ago. Holds two hundred for plenary lectures and seventy-two for the chamber recital we close the spring on.

Library Annex · 図書館別館 (2008)

EAST READING ROOM

A two-storey reading-room and stack designed by Sakaguchi Yumi, with a tiered open shelf running along the south wall. Climate is held at 18°C and 55% relative humidity.

Craft Workshop · 工房 (2018)

CONSERVATION BENCH

A long, naturally-lit single-room timber workshop on the eastern edge of the courtyard. Pigment grinding, paper repair, and a single small lacquer table are kept here.

Walking directions

行き方 ・ TWO ROUTES BY FOOT
Sanjō-dōri
Higashiyama-ku
FROM HIGASHIYAMA STATION

From Higashiyama Station

15 minutes on foot. Leave from exit 2; turn right onto Sanjō-dōri and walk steadily east. The Hokurin gate is on the south side of the road, immediately before the second bridge.

FROM SANJŌ KEIHAN

From Sanjō Keihan

22 minutes on foot. Cross the river east, walk a steady kilometre along the south bank, and pick up Sanjō-dōri at the second bridge above the Kabuki Theatre.

BY BUS

By city bus

Routes 12 and 17 stop at Higashiyama-Sanjō; Hokurin is a four-minute walk from the stop. The campus has no parking.

Open-campus days

公開日 ・ TWICE A YEAR

Twice a year the campus opens for two courtyards, conservation studios, and rare-book vault to the public. No registration is required; printed walking guides in Japanese, English, and French are offered at the Hokurin gateway.

SUNDAY OF OCT · LIBRARY DAY

Tōrin Bunko reading room open by half-hour appointment.

サブメニュー · DURING OPEN DAYS

Twice a year the campus opens its main courtyards, conservation studios, and rare-book vault. Visitors should expect:

  • A guided thirty-minute walk of the four buildings · 四館巡り — every half hour from the Honkan foyer.
  • A live demonstration of beni- and sumi- pigment grinding from the Craft Workshop bench, hourly from 11:00.
  • A small printed pamphlet of the day's open-shelf selection from the Tōrin Bunko, taken from the Edo-period collection.
  • Tea, tangerines, and rice cakes in the courtyard, served from 14:00 till close at 16:30.
