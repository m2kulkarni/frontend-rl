研究と東林文庫 — RESEARCH & LIBRARY

Slow scholarship,
kept on shelves
of paulownia.

Hokurin's research culture is shaped by long horizons. We measure pigment migration in centimeters per century, listen to looms that have not changed since the Genroku era, and read books printed before electricity. Our smallness is deliberate.

研究プロジェクト ・ ACTIVE PROJECTS
PIGMENTS · 顔料

Fastness of Edo Beni

A multi-decade study of the lightfastness of beni-pigment encaustic across 100 years of Hokusai's woodblock impressions, conducted with the Tokyo Geijutsu archives.

RIVER · 河川

Kamo Basin Gauge

A weekly gauge of the Kamo's three principal upper tributaries from Kibune to the Sanjō bridge, taken since 1989. The longest continuous record of its kind in Japan.

PAPER · 紙

Washi Half-Lives

An accelerated-aging study of three regional washi varieties: Echizen, Kōchi, and the local Kurodani, in collaboration with the Imperial paper-arts archive.

DUPLICATION REQUEST PROCEDURE

All duplication is by gentle scan or hand transcription. Photography of the manuscript collection is not permitted; photography of the printed collection requires a one-line written request placed in the foyer box at least one reading day in advance. Off-site loans are not made under any circumstance.

客員研究員 — VISITING SCHOLARS

Visiting Scholars

Hokurin admits four to six visiting scholars each year, by invitation of the faculty. The visiting term is one academic year, running from the first week of April through the third week of February. Visiting scholars are housed in a single guest cottage at the eastern edge of the campus, and are expected to give one open lecture and to participate in the weekly seminar.
