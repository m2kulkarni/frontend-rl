学群・課程 — FACULTIES & PROGRAMS

Three faculties,
one machiya.

Hokurin teaches three small faculties under one timbered roof: the humanities, the craft sciences, and an environmental studies group concentrated on the Kamo basin. Each runs on a four-year arc with a third year set aside for seminar room work and field practicum.

三学群一館 — ONE BUILDING, THREE WINGS

A four-year arc, with
the third year spent
away from the seminar
room.

学修の構造 ・ STRUCTURE OF STUDY

Each cohort enters together on the spring after sakura week and graduates together. Years one and two share a humanities core: textual reading, materials drawing, and a basin-ecology field course. The third year is spent off-campus — in workshops, basins, or archives — and the fourth year returns to the seminar room for a single long monograph defended before the faculty in January.

HUMANITIES · 人文学群

Humanities
人文学群 · Bunshō-gun

A reading-and-writing track on classical Japanese, the Sinitic register, and the long Edo prose tradition, with a small comparative arm in continental philosophy.

CORE COURSES

古典文学講読 — Readings in Heian prose
漢文 — Classical Chinese, eight semesters
江戸の散文 — The long Edo essay
解釈の歴史 — Histories of interpretation

FIELD

Year three is spent at the Tōrin Bunko reading uncatalogued woodblock impressions, or at affiliated temple archives in Kōyasan and Hieizan.

CRAFT SCIENCES · 工芸科学群

Craft Sciences
工芸科学群 · Kōgei-gun

A material-and-conservation track founded in 1989 around the question of how Edo objects survive into the twenty-first century. Practice-led, with a strong scientific arm.

CORE COURSES

顔料化学 — Mineral pigment chemistry
木版画製作 — Woodblock fabrication
漆芸 — Lacquerware, four semesters
紙の修復 — Paper repair

FIELD

Year three is spent in the workshop of a working conservator under a memorandum of practice signed each spring at the Inari shrine.

ENVIRONMENT · 環境学群

Environmental
Studies
環境学群 · Kankyō-gun

A small, basin-bounded program on the long ecology of the Kamo river, the Higashiyama hills, and the timber economies that fed the woodblock trades from the late Edo period.

CORE COURSES

河川生態学 — River ecology of the Kamo
森林の歴史 — Forest histories
気候と建築 — Climate & the machiya
水質測定 — Water quality, field methods

FIELD

Year three is spent on a weekly upstream gauge survey from Kibune to the Sanjō bridge, with monthly returns to the lab.

The third year is when the seminar table walks out of the room. A student who has not stood in mud, in lacquer dust, or in a mountain archive has not yet read.
