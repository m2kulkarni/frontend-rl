2024London
— Book · Architectural Review —

Persian Tile in the Twenty-First Century

— Periodical · Apollo Magazine —

A Living Tilework

Six-page profile in Apollo Magazine touring the cutting of a single calash vault from sketch to fire to setting in the Khwaju courtyard restoration. Photography by Suarda Hadukirhin.

2021July
— Journal · Aramco World —

The Last Mosaicists of Isfahan

Cover feature in Aramco World on the surviving practitioners of the school style, with extended interviews on the inheritance of process drawing from the Tabriz lineage. Aramco World · Vol. 12, No. 6 · pp. 8-25

2020Paris
— Honour · International Recognition —

Unesco Living Human Treasure

Nominated by the Iranian Cultural Heritage, Handicrafts and Tourism Organization under the Programme for the Safeguarding of Intangible Cultural Heritage — citation for sustained transmission of inflected tile-mosaic technique.
UNESCO · Inscription No. 0245-IST-2020

2018Tehran
— Award · Gold Medal —

Awarded the gold medal in the architectural ornament category for the painted-panel Eight-Pointed Star wall finalist — final work the four-summer, 14 x 23 m. piece chartled by hostel sponsors.
