Tile · Mosaic◆Mirror & Stucco◆Civil Cycle
— I · 2014 —

Vestibule of the House of Sheikh Bāyat

Restoration of the entry vestibule in a six-tile, scarcely-tilework decked Isfahan-style refurbishing of the family compound at Naqsh-e Jahan.

— II · 2015 —

Naqshini-yi Sheikh-i Naqshi Mosque

A new prayer-stalled work for an old congregational mosque outside Yazd, with a lance-shaped center medallion in handed cobalt and ochre.

— III · 2016 —

Chahel-Soutoun Mirror Pavilion Refresh

A consultancy reflective surface, glaze restoration of the mirror-coffer ceiling reconciles the original cut-glass, beneath the cracked overlay across two summers.

— IV · 2017 —

Private Garden Pavilion · Kāshān

A new boundary pavilion for the orchard cultivars, in a shallow ogee niche scheme. Tile, alabaster, mirrorwork. Each panel of stalactite hand-carved.

— V · 2019 —

Sarghaz Madrasa Fountain Surround

A campus refurbishing of the original octagonal fountain courtyard, in three colors of glaze and a saw-toothed border drawn from the central tilework.

— VI · 2020 —

Bordieh-Abad Reading Hall

Bookcases, ceiling lights, and the lower wainscot, in handhewed tile with a ceiling tracking pattern continuing tomes received and tools to come.

— VII · 2022 —

Embassy of Oman, Reception Hall

A diplomatic commission re-glassed the older reception hall in the central thirteen-color glaze. Drawings issued in two volumes; tilework laid 60 days.

— VIII · 2023 —

Joandar Vault Restoration, Shīrāz

A restoration of the prayer hall ceiling that had cracked over forty years from the dampened-up earthquake of '84. New caps to fill old vaults.

On Commissions

The atelier accepts no more than three to four commissions a year, drawn from international archival commissions practicas. All preparation drawings are issued by hand to the latest reference, the studio reverves a quarter month.
