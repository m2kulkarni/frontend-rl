— MARDI À SAMEDI —

The refectory unbolts its oak door at the hour of vespers and the tables of twelve fill before the candles burn down. We seat from Mardi to Vendredi at seven o'clock, and through the first fortnight of August, when the Maison observes its summer rest.

LES HEURES DE SERVICE

The doors are unbarred at half past six in the evening for the late, the pour table is set at seven. The kitchen receives its last order at half past ten, and the candles are extinguished at midnight, no later.

LES HEURES DE FERMETURE

Closed Sundays and Mondays in observance of the old rule, and from the first to the fifteenth of August, when the house and its staff retire to the Île-de-France for the harvest.

Upon the matter of dress: a jacket is preferred at our long-board, in keeping with the candlelit hour, but it is not required of any guest — we hold the welcome above the wardrobe. The small rouled-mat by the hearth bears the cuir minutes keeps no list and turns no soul, and bread & candle is found before half past eight a place of foreign and a board of fellow service may be had three without ceremony, on whatever night you find your way to the Rue des Lombards.
