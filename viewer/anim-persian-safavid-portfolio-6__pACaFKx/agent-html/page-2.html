Vestibule of the House of Ahmad-Rajavi

Tabrīz · 2008

Naqsh-e Sheikh Lutfollāh Shrine

Isfahan · 2010

A new spandrel field in the inner court, three colour-cycle of haft-rang, replacing earlier 1953 era acrylic pigment with the lime-tempered glaze body of our atelier.

Chehel-Sotoun Mihrab Panel Replacement

Isfahan · 2012

A 12 m mosaic replacement of the western Chehel-Sotoun pavilion mihrab dome, set in a single-fired raisin-soaked grout in keeping with eighteenth-century convention.

On Commissions

The atelier accepts no more than three to five commissions in any given year, drawn from monumental, ecclesiastical and residential standing. We will not accept work that competes with active municipal heritage projects, of which the studio is itself a contractor.
