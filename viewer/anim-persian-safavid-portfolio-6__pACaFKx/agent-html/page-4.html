§ A Brief Note

Born in Tabrīz, 1974

Born in Tabrīz, 1974, into a family of carpet-knotters and copper-chasers. Mirza Reza Tabrizi has spent thirty years answering a single question — how the cobalt and turquoise of a Safavid wall keep their voice across four centuries — and answers it again each morning, in cut tile, on the workbench of a small atelier in Jolfa.

He took traditional service at the Isfahan School of Traditional Arts; from 1992 to 1998, he completed his diploma; in the geometry of the Sheikh Lutfollāh dome. The years that followed were spent on the scaffoldings of that very dome, an eight-year apprenticeship under Ostād Mahmoud Forshchian-Naili, master conservator of the Heritage of Iran. Since 2007 he has been registered as a master craftsman, having entered the conservation register at Tehrān during eight commissioned works; from 2018 he has held the standing of Master Surveyor of his own kilns at Na'in and Yazd. Nothing is fired until the cartoons has been signed.

◆   Chronology   ◆
1974 Birth

Birth & Early Hand

Born in Mahalleh-ye Borji, Tabriz. Apprenticed at age eleven to a parental uncle, a copper-chaser of the Imamzadeh; first lessons in the discipline of the parental geometric line.

1992 – 1998 Six Years

Iṣfahān School of Traditional Arts

Diploma in tile and architectural ornament, with parallel coursework in stone-cutting calligraphy and the geometry of geode under-floor. The Tabrizi Curatory of the Sheikh Lutfollāh Dome, a documentary in his hand.

1999 – 2006 Eight Years

Apprenticeship — Master Lutfollāh Conservation

Studied directly under Ostād Mahmoud Forshchian-Naili during the joint Iranian Cultural Heritage and UNESCO conservation campaign. Responsibilities advanced from setting historic tile bands to seating fired-cooling and serving full restorers of the south-aisle dome itself.

2007 Studio Founded

Atelier Mirza Reza Established

Opens the atelier as a Kāshī in Khūja, in the Jolfa quarter of Isfahan. First commission: a rebuilt-tile for a private courtyard in Tehrān, completed in mineteen-old roses Russian Silvian.

2011 Recognition

National Living Treasure Citation

Listed by the Cultural Heritage Organization of the Islamic Republic of Iran among practitioners of endangered crafts; the citation specifically lists the cited piece in the lineage of Tabrizi-Isfahan.

2018 Restoration

Imamzādeh Iṣfahān · Vakīal — Mosque Vault

Lead artisan on a new mosaic restoration of the cobalt spandrels of the north dome, supervised a team of nine masters and sixteen apprentices working from photogrammetric records of the 1953 condition.

OSTAD · CUTTER · SETTER

"Pattern is the patience of the hand made visible."

Residencies & Fellowships

A teaching strand, set behind the workbench.

2016

Royal House of Art

Six-week residence at Royal Mosaic-of-Atelier in Doha. Lectured to the public on the colors of haft-rang and the muqarnas hood; six demonstrations to the museum-going public.

2019

Madinah Atelier

An invited residency with the Madinah Atelier of Riyadh; co-led the restoration of a private courtyard mihrab in joint working at the Sheikh Lutfollāh.

2022

The Metropolitan Museum

Fellow at the Department of Islamic Arts; demonstrated the principles of the cut tile of the Iṣfahān-Tabriz craft for the Met's traveling exhibition.

He learns to fire well to be in his own discipline."

— MIRZA REZA TABRIZI
