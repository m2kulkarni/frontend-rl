Dīvān-i Shahr-i Iṣfahān

Civic Register·Bureau·Records

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

By a firmān in the tradition of His Majesty's Iṣfahān quarters, the kalāntar of the Qaysariyya, and all householders within the maternal districts of this royal city, that the gates of this Bureau stand open from dawn prayer until the closing of the great divan at sunset. Hear ye petition: shall be turned away, and that the lodgers of water, tan, and lineage are kept in good order — for the welfare of the people and the dignity of the Crown.

Sealed this seventeenth day of Rabīʿ al-Awwal, by hand of

Mīrzā Ḥusayn Khān ShīrāzīMayor of the Quarters · Kalāntar-i Maḥallāt

15 Rabīʿ al-Awwal

Allotment Schedule for the Madī Nāṣarrn Canal

Effective 16 Rabīʿ al-Awwal — 21 Rabīʿ al-Awwal

The mīrāb has set the rotation: gardens of Jūbāra at first light on Saturday; the cloth-dyers of Bīd-ābād at midday Monday; the orchards beyond the Khājū bridge at dusk Wednesday. Disputes shall be heard at the canal-house each morning before the call to ẓuhr.

17 Rabīʿ al-Awwal

Caravan-Tax Filing Window Now Open

Receipts taken until 25 Rabīʿ al-Awwal · A clean fee, two camel-load.

Merchants returning from Tabrīz, Shīrāz, and the Gulf ports must present manifests at the Caravan Hall behind the Maydān. Silks are assessed at four-and-twenty per hundred dirhams; cotton at one-and-twelve. Bring two witnesses and the seal of your caravan-bāshī.

19 Rabīʿ al-Awwal

Observance for the Chief of the Cloth-Bazaar

Until ʿīd al-fiṭr · A solemn week.

The shops of the Qaysariyya silk-row shall close at midday for three days in honour of the late raʾīs. The bazaar's processors depart from the Masjid-i Ḥakīm after ʿaṣr prayer; condolences are received at the family courtyard in the Dardasht quarter. May his memory rest among the loom-tenders.

Hours of the Dīvān · Locations of Record

Chief Scribe attends the north arcade of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, opposite the gate of the Caravanserai of Sayyid Ismāʿīl. Open daily save Friday, from the dawn azan to the setting of the case. Subsidiary registers are kept in the Madrasa-i Chahār Bāgh khwāja, in piers undertenanted, and at the Bridge of Allāhverdī Khān (river side).
