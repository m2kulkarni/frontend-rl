Edicts & Public Notices

From the bureau of the Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān, Iṣfahān

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

By the grace of the Shāhanshāh and under the seal of the Dīvān-i Shahr, the following statutes and bazaar proclamations were affixed to the gates of the Qaysariyya, the Masjid-i Jāmiʿ, and the four caravanserais of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, beginning the fourteenth day of Rabīʿ al-Awwal in the year one thousand one hundred and eight of the Hijra. Citizens of the Jūbāra, Dardasht, ʿAbbās-ābād, and Bīdābād quarters are charged to attend their reading.

Index of FirmānsSangaki (5)Bazaar (8)Water (3)Coin (2)Common & Hospice (8)
[motif: islamic-star]Farmān No. 1
03 Rabīʿ al-Thānī·1108 (October 1696)

On the Standard Weight of the Common Loaf (nān-i sangak)

— Issued by Mīr ʿAbd al-Rashīd, Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān

Be it known to every baker (nānvā) of this finer quarter that, by command of the Dīvān, the loaf of sangak shall not weigh less than one (1) sīr ('Abbāsī) when drawn fresh from the oven. The price is fixed at one shāhī and a half. Any baker found light by the inspector's scale shall forfeit one day's flour to the poor of the Masjid-i Jāmiʿ.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
[motif: islamic-star]Farmān No. 2
15 RABĪʿ AL-AWWAL·1108 (October 1696)

Repair of the Public Fountain (saqqā-khāna) in the Jūbāra Quarter

— Issued by Ḥājjī Luṭf-ʿAlī, Inspector of Waters

The qanāt-fed fountain at the head of the Khājū-Jūbāra Bāzārchih, lacking fallen into ruin since the floods of Muḥarram, is hereby ordered restored. Master mason Ustād Jaʿfar of Kāshān is appointed; the cost of forty-two tūmāns shall be drawn from the waqf of the late Mīrzā Taqī. Residents of Jūbāra are forbidden to draw upon the cistern of the Madrasa-i Chahār Bāgh in the interim.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
[motif: islamic-star]Farmān No. 3
15 RABĪʿ AL-AWWAL·1108 (October 1696)

Prohibition of Grazing within the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān

— Issued by Mīr ʿAbd al-Rashīd, Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān

No man shall lead sheep, goat, mule, nor camel upon the gravels of the Royal Square between the Qaysariyya gate and the Masjid-i Shāh. The herdsmen of the southern villages shall pass instead by the way of Chārsū or Chām-ābād. A first transgression carries a fine of two ʿabbāsī; the second, the confiscation of the beast for the soup-cellars.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
[motif: islamic-star]Farmān No. 4
15 RABĪʿ AL-THĀNĪ·1108 (October 1696)

On the Sweeping of the Bāzār-i Buzurg before the Friday Prayer

— Issued by Āqā Riḍā, Warden of the Qaysariyya

Each shopkeeper of the long bāzār, from the spice-vault of the Sarā-yī Mīnā Tājī unto the gate of the coppersmiths, shall sweep his threshold and water the dust thereof one hour before the noon-call of every Friday. Inspectors of this office shall pass at the third going; whom hath he claimed shall be cited in the rota at the offender's expense.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
[motif: islamic-star]Farmān No. 5
22 RABĪʿ AL-THĀNĪ·1108 (November 1696)

Concerning the Quality of Indigo Sold in the Sarā-yi Gulshan

— Issued by Mīrzā Bāqī, Examiner of Dyes

Merchants importing indigo of Bayāna and of Sarālūj shall present such bales to the dye-examiner before opening sale. Adulteration with charcoal or with the lees of pomegranate is forbidden; the penalty being the burning of the parcel in the maydān and a fine of five tūmāns to the treasury of the Dīvān.
