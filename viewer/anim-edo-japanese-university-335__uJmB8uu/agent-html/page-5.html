五CAMPUS & VISITING · 校舎と来訪のしおり

Four buildings,
one quiet block
of Higashiyama.

The Hokurin campus occupies a single deep machiya plot south of Sanjō-dōri, where a 1782 timber-framed merchant home still serves as the administrative centre. Around it, three later buildings — a 1994 kuradō concrete hall, a 2008 library annex, and a 2018 craft workshop — were each set carefully behind the original eaves line, leaving the street face unchanged. Visitors are welcome year-round, and twice a year for our open-campus days.

校舎ノ一 — 四 / THE FOUR BUILDINGS

The four buildings

HONKAN · BUNKŌ HALL · LIBRARY ANNEX · CRAFT WORKSHOP
本館 · BLDG. I

Honkan

The 1782 main hall, restored 1989. Houses the registrar, the provost's room, and the seven foundation-year seminars. Tatami-floored galleries on the upper level.

文庫館 · BLDG. II

Bunkō Hall

The 1994 kura-dō annex, dehumidified to 18°C / 55% RH. Houses the Tōrin Bunko, the manuscript reading room, and the conservation imaging studio.

書庫 · BLDG. III

Library Annex

The 2008 reading library — eighty-eight seats over two floors, carrels for graduate students, a quiet wing facing the courtyard rock garden.

工房 · BLDG. IV

Craft Workshop

The 2018 east workshop, designed by Mori Tetsuko. Three lacquer benches, two looms, an indigo vat, and the natural-dye chemistry lab share one rammed-earth floor.

Cross the Sanjō Ōhashi bridge eastbound over the Kamogawa, continue along Sanjō-dōri past the Antiques Quarter and three intersections; the campus entrance is on the right, marked by a small cedar nameplate.

Routes 5, 86 — to Higashiyama-Sanjō; alight, walk three minutes east. Visitors with mobility needs may use the accessible entrance on the north alleyway behind Kuwadō Hall.

来訪 / OPEN CAMPUS

Open-campus days

SELF-GUIDED TOURS · 10:00–16:00

Twice a year the campus opens its inner courtyards, conservation studios, and rare-book vault to the public. No registration is required; printed walking guides in Japanese, English, and French are offered at the Honkan genkan.

18

MAY · 五月 · SPRING
Garden & Print Workshops Open

The inner garden is at full leaf, indigo vats demonstrate at the kura, indigo-dye vats open in the East Workshop.

26

OCTOBER · 十月 · AUTUMN
Tōrin Bunko Vault Tours

The rare-book vault is opened to the public — full-period printed books and pattern manuals on display. Maple in the rear garden.
