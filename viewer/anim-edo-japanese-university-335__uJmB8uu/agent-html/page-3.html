三ADMISSIONS · 入学のご案内

Apply to
Hokurin.

Hokurin admits a small undergraduate cohort and a smaller graduate institute each year — roughly 320 entering students across the machiya campus. The academic calendar offers three intake windows for AY 2025–2026, with rolling review for international applicants. We read each application by hand, in committee, and reply in the season we received it.

三Three Intake Windows

APPLICATION CALENDAR · 2025/2026
AUTUMN INTAKE — STANDARD入試 1

Standard Domestic Cycle

1 September 2025

Application portal opens 1 July, closes 1 September. Documents due 15 September. Examination at the Sanjō campus. Decisions returned by 15 November for January matriculation.

WINTER INTAKE — LATE入試 2

Late Cycle & Transfers

15 January 2026

Reserved for transfer students, examination 4 February, limited to fourteen places across all three faculties. Portfolio and a letter from the present institution required for transfer entry.

INTERNATIONAL · ROLLING入試 3

Rolling Review

Ongoing

Reviewed within 30 days of receipt. JLPT N2 or equivalent recommended; written exam waivable for applicants holding completed degree-track Japanese coursework. Visa support letter issued upon offer.

四TUITION & FEE WAIVERS · 学費

Tuition & Fee Waivers

Hokurin holds tuition fixed for the duration of an undergraduate's enrolment. The university also operates a need-blind waiver scheme, funded by the prefectural endowment of 1991, that has historically supported between thirty and forty percent of each year's intake.

五DOCUMENTS & EXAMINATION · 出願書類と試験

Documents & Examination

Documents

REQUIRED OF EVERY APPLICANT

  • 01Application form 入学願書Completed in Japanese or English, signed in ink. Available from the admissions portal or by post.
  • 02Secondary transcript 成績証明書Original sealed transcript covering all years of upper-secondary study, with official translation if not in Japanese.
  • 03Statement of intent 志望理由書1,200–1,800 characters (Japanese) or 600–900 words (English) addressing the chosen faculty.
  • 04Two letters of reference 推薦状At least one from a teacher of record. For graduate applicants, both must be academic.
  • 05Photograph & identity copy 写真・身分証Recent 30×40mm photograph and copy of passport or residence card.
  • 06Examination fee receipt 検定料納入書¥7,000 undergraduate · ¥10,000 graduate. Waivable under Tier I and II tuition relief.

Entrance Examination

FIELD OF THE SANJŌ CAMPUS

REQUIRED PAPERS入試 I

Japanese & English国語・英語

120 minutes each. Japanese paper includes a short literary commentary chosen in classical, applicant's choice and a précis. English paper combines reading comprehension with a 400-word analytical response.

ELECTIVE PAPER入試 II

Mathematics or World History数学・世界史

90 minutes. Mathematics covers the standard upper-secondary curriculum through Math II/B. World History favours interpretation of primary sources (Edo, late-Qing, Meiji-Taishō, Senkanki). Choose one at registration.

INTERVIEW面接

Twenty-Minute Conversation面接

Two faculty interviewers. Conducted in Japanese (English available for the international cycle). The conversation begins from the statement of intent; there are no trick questions and no formal dress requirement.

志は遠く、歩みは近く

DISTANT AIM, NEAR STEP

Hokurin's admissions seal is affixed by hand to each offer letter at the close of the cycle. The paulownia is the formal mark of the university registrar; you will see it once on your envelope, and again on your diploma four years later.
