— MARDI À SAMEDI — The refectory unbolts its oak doors at the hour of vespers and seats the tables from nineteen of the clock until ten and the half. We keep silence on the Lord's day and Monday, and through the first fortnight of August, when the Maison observes its summer rest.

LES HEURES DE SERVICE

The doors are unbarred at half-past six in the evening for the late, the great table is set at seven. The kitchen receives its last order at half-past ten, and the candles are extinguished at midnight, no later.

LES HEURES DE FERMETURE

Closed on Sundays and Mondays in observance of the old rule, and from the first to the fifteenth of August, when the house and its staff retire to the Île-de-France for the harvest.

Upon the matter of dress: a jacket is preferred at our long table, in keeping with the candlelit hour, but it is not required of any guest — we hold the welcome above the wardrobe. The small verre rouge lamps that adorn the rear window keep our list and table closer at hand. Should the night be wild, the heady hand of Henri at the decanter, and the silence of the cellarer in matters of provenance.»
