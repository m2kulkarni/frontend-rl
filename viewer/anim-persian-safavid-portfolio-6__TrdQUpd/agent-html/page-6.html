◆ THE COMMISSIONING LEDGER ◆

ON RECEIVING A COMMISSION

◆ A NOTE ON SCOPE, TIME & WHAT PASSES BETWEEN US ◆

The atelier accepts a small number of architectural and devotional commissions each year. Each project begins with a written brief, a site visit where possible, and the preparation of a one-fifth scale cartoon together with material samples drawn from our own kiln at Na’in and Yazd. Nothing is fired until the cartoon has been signed.

◆ MINIMUM SCOPE ◆
4 m² OF TILEWORK

Below this, the geometric canon resolves into a complete curiosity; smaller objects are accepted only as gifts.

◆ LEAD TIME ◆
6 — 14 MONTHS

From signed cartoon to installation, depending on glaze recipe, kiln cycle, and the season's humidity.

◆ CARTOONS & SAMPLES ◆
PREPARED ON AGREEMENT

A binding agreement, and a deposit of 30%, accompanies the issue of cartoons; samples are kept in the studio archive.

◆ INSTALLATION ◆
BY THE ATELIER ITSELF

The master and one senior workman travel to site for setting; this means is stated to the patron.

I
CUTTING, BRIEF

A written description of the surface, light, and intent. Photographs and elevations are welcomed.

II
CARTOON

1/5 scale drawing on paper, with a small panel of fired samples for the proposed palette.

III
CUTTING & FIRING

Tiles are cut by hand from manufactured slabs, a single cobalt firing, then gold-soot glaze where called for.

IV
SETTING

On site assembly, face-down on paper templates, set in lime-bone glaze.
