◆ THE METHOD ◆

THE QUIET DISCIPLINE
OF KASHI-KARI

A note from the workshop on how a single mineral panel — from glazed-quartz to grouted spandrel — passes through four stages, seven colors, and the patient hands of three slow years.

◆ PREMISE ◆

A CRAFT HELD IN THE HAND

The atelier, in the Shīrāzī manner, accepts as kāshī-kāri only a discourse held face-on-cartoon: the cobalt-glazed quartz biscuits, mineral-papered with vegetable inks, are scored under the tīsha long before the kiln is lit. We work, on a cartoon four metres wide, by the natural light of the north-facing window; the cartoons are never digitally re-projected, but inked on linen by the same hand that will lay the tile.

The atelier was founded by the master with the constraint of producing six panels in four years; eighteen months later, six panels had not been done, and three drawings had. We respect the slowness; mosaic restoration is the patience of waiting, the apex-cell of a lazy gypsum-curing which is overcome only when the building has put on the same robe as the cartoon.

When I draw on a working description of how a single panel is approached, I assume the studio has had the working title for the better part of a season; the cartoons themselves take six.

◆ THE STUDIO ◆

  • Master Mineral PigmentsPolishing-grade Iranian cobalt; copper-turquoise from Khorāsān; ivory white from gypsum quarries near Yazd. Hand-ground on porphyry slabs.
  • Glazed Tile StockThree-firing Qum biscuits laid against the chamber's eastern wall to settle, then re-fired to seal.
  • Cement BinderTwenty-day slaked lime mixed with finely sieved riverbed silt — a recipe inherited from the seventeenth-century Mashhad workshops.
  • Working ToolsHand-forged tīsha hammers, graded sieves, and an inherited cedar mallet that is older than every craftsman now alive in the studio.

Each glaze is mixed by a master mineral-firer — earth-fired in the kiln again 24 hours after pre-firing — and applied to a cobalt-quartz biscuit cut to size. The mixed gloss is a record of the chamber's temperature on a single day.
