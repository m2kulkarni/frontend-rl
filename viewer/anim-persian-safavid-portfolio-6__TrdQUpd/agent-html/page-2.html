VESTIBULE OF THE HOUSE OF SHEIKH BAHĀ’Ī

◆ OPUS XV ◆

MIḤRĀB SPANDREL, MASJID-I NUR

A new spandrel pair for the qibla wall, fired in the cobalt and turquoise register typical of Yazd. The composition pivots on a central dome with palmette scrollwork running toward the apex.

1397SPANDREL · TILEYAZD
◆ OPUS XX ◆

CHEHELSOTOUN MIRROR PANEL REPLACEMENT

Conservation-grade replacement of three āyīna-kāri panels in the northern iwan, hand-cut from antique German mirror stock and laid into the original gach matrix without modern adhesives.

1399MIRROR · PANELISFAHAN

PRIVATE GARDEN PAVILION, KASHAN

1396PAVILIONKASHAN

SARḌĀB MEHRĀB FOUNTAIN SURROUND

ON COMMISSIONS

The atelier accepts no more than three new works in any calendar year, drawn from international, technical and private patrons. All preparatory drawings are made by hand in lime-pigmented paper; the studio retains a single archival proof.
