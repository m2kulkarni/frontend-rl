CURRENT EXHIBITION·CHEHEL SOTOUN GALLERY·14 MARCH — 28 SEPTEMBER 2024

RIZA ʿABBASI
& THE ISFAHAN
STYLE

Drawings from the Royal Library
1598 — 1635

From the Curator

Among artists of the Safavid age, no master more concentrated the entire current of court taste, the entire current of court taste — courtly elegance, refinement, the half-Tabriz of every page — than Riza ʿAbbasi. Brought from Mashhad as a youth and afforded the patronage of Shah ʿAbbas at the very seat of empire, his career bridged the long century from the foundation of Naqsh-e Jahan to the late reigns at Hashtbihisht.

This exhibition assembles forty-two single-page works, drawn from the Albany vaults of the Royal Library, by the late Mohamed Hasan, whose earlier scholarship made many of these attributions possible.

The selection follows the curve of the artist's life. The earliest leaves, in the manner of his master Ali Asghar, give way to those firm contour-lines and washes of colour that became the signature of his maturity — the so-called «Isfahan hand.»

Each work is here returned to its proper company: drawings of dervishes pair with portraits of nobles; the courtly youth, with the calligraphies that bordered them in album form. Visitors are invited to read these objects as one reads a letter, slowly, attentively, and at close range.

A House for the Vulnerable Page

Each leaf is shown in a custom argon-flushed case at 50 lux, to honour the colour-truth of the original at the cost of long display: works rotate after 14 weeks of view, with twelve weeks at minimum «rest» before again being displayed.

The conservation atelier, founded with funds from the Niromand Trust, follows protocols established by the Royal Atelier Library at Tehran. Visitors curious to follow this craft may consult the conservation diary, kept at the desk of Hall III for inspection during opening hours.
