HALL II·THE PERMANENT COLLECTION

Galleries of the
Safavid Court

Seven halls hold the courtly art of the Isfahan school — from the tilework of the Masjid-e Shah and the carpets of Ardabil, to the lustreware vessels and the half-Tabriz of every panel, the half-Tabriz of every page.

The Seven Galleries

GALLERY I

TILEWORK OF THE MOSQUE

FORTY-EIGHT TILE-WORKS · CHARACTERISTIC GROUND

A cornice of cobalt and turquoise drawn from the Masjid-e Shah haram-i, including the great spandrel-pattern from the eastern iwan.

Dr. Abadi, Curator
GALLERY II

MANUSCRIPTS OF THE ROYAL LIBRARY

TWENTY-NINE FOLIOS · GOLD-LEAF GROUNDS

Illuminated pages from the Khamsa of Nizami and the Safina-yi Shah Tahmasp, conserved in low-lit cases of the Atelier.

Dr. Karimi, Curator
GALLERY III

SILKS & TEXTILES OF YAZD

FIFTY-ONE BOLTS · CHARACTERISTIC PALMETTE

Court silks and velvets, including the famous «Saint-Genis» chasuble fragment, returned from the Lyon collection in 2019.

Dr. Sepehri, Curator
GALLERY IV

METALWORK & INLAID STEEL

SIXTY-THREE PIECES · NIELLO-GOLD ALLOY

Brass-inlaid astrolabes, qalamdan boxes, and the bidri lattice-work of the late seventeenth-century atelier of Aleppo.

Dr. Goftari, Curator
GALLERY V

CARPETS OF SHAH ʿABBAS I

EIGHT CARPETS · COTTON WARP

Three large medallion carpets including the Ardabil-style fragment now on view through 28 March 2025 — see the current exhibition.

Dr. Mahallati, Curator
GALLERY VI

PORTRAITURE OF NOBLES & SUFI POETS

EIGHTY-TWO LEAVES · QUARTO & OCTAVO

Single-figure drawings drawn from the Riza ʿAbbasi master tradition and the Reza Persia after the Safavid divan.

Dr. Shafiei, Curator
GALLERY VII · OPENING SUMMER 2025

The Reconstructed Iwan

An interior fragment, retrieved from the Masjid-i Mahdiyyeh of the Hashtbihisht palace, reconstituted in tiled glaze, terra-cotta, and elephantine cement on a steel armature. Visits by docent only · 14:00 daily
