THE ATELIER'S PRACTICE

WHAT THE ATELIER UNDERTAKES

The workshop holds to three lineages of inherited ornament, taught hand-to-hand from the masters of the Chahār Bāgh and practiced today in monuments, prayer halls, and private commissions across Iran and the Persian-speaking diaspora.

◆ I ◆

Tile Mosaic

Cut tile mosaicing in the soluble turquoise-gold cuerda seca, and seven-colour haft-rang for arabesque fields and Quranic banding. Pattern keyed to atelier sketches kept in the boards of the workshop until firing.
FROM 28 SEP / 27 SE 2026
◆ II ◆

Stucco Muqarnas

Hand-carved gypsum stalactite vaults set out from three honeycomb grids on the floor; in the manner of the Sheikh Lotfollāh hood and the chambers' visual polychromy in lapis and gold-leaf available on commission.
FROM 22 OCT / 9 PA 2026
◆ III ◆

Architectural Counsel

Consultation on tilework programme, dome geometry and the structural progression of new mosques and gathering halls. Drawings issued in board-line vellum and surveyed digital reports with field schedules for craft subcontractors.
FROM 14 NOV / 1 BO 2026

ENTER THE PORTFOLIO

Twenty-eight commissions across nineteen years — mosques, madrasas, garden pavilions, and private dargāhs — catalogued in full plan, elevation, and photograph.
