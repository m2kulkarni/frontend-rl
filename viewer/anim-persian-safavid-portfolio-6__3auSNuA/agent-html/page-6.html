THE COMMISSIONING LEDGER

ON RECEIVING A COMMISSION

A NOTE ON SCOPE, TIME, & WHAT PASSES BETWEEN US.

The atelier accepts a small number of architectural and devotional commissions each year. Each project begins with a written brief, a site visit where possible, and the preparation of a one-fifth-scale cartoon together with material samples drawn from our own kilns at Naʿīn and Yazd. Nothing is fired until the cartoon has been signed.

I · MINIMUM SCOPE

4 M² OF TILEWORK

Below this, the geometric cannot resolve into a complete cartouche; smaller objects are accepted as a gift.
II · LEAD TIME

6 — 14 MONTHS

From signed cartoon to installation, depending on plate-recipe, blue-pin, and the season's humidity.
III · DRAWING & SAMPLES

PREPARED ON AGREEMENT

A binding quotation and a fired-tile sample-set accompanies the show of cartoons; samples are kept in the studio archive.
IV · INSTALLATION

BY THE ATELIER ITSELF

The master and our senior workmen travel to site for setting; fine mortar is mixed in the proper.
I
CUTTING, BRIEF
A written description of the surface, light, and intent. Photographs and elevations are welcomed.
II
CARTOON
A 1:5 ink drawing on paper, with a small panel of fired sample to the proposed palette.
III
CUTTING & FIRING
Tile cut by hand from manufactured slabs, a single cobalt firing, then gold-onyx glaze where called for.
IV
SETTING
On site assembly, fine mortar on paper templates, set in form, fine paper washed off after curing.

BISMILLĀH. THE DOOR IS OPEN.
