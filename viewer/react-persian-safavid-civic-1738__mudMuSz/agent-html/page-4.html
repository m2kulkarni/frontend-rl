Register IV·Of the Bureau

The Bureau & Its Officers

[motif: arch-ogee]
Established by royal farmān in the year 1006 of the Hijra, the Dīwān-i Shahr keeps the tale of the residence of Naqsh-e Jahān, the measure of the markets, and the courses of the canals.
Bunyād·Founding

Of the Founding under Shāh ‘Abbās

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]

In the year 1006 A.H. — being the year of the removal of the royal seat from Qazvīn to Iṣfahān — His Majesty Shāh ‘Abbās, called the Great, did command that a Bureau be raised within the new city, fronting the maydān of Naqsh-e Jahān, that no measure should be false in the bazaars, no canal stopped against its neighbour, and no household unrecorded upon the rolls. The seal was struck in cobalt and gold and pressed upon the founding ledger by the hand of the wazīr Ḥātim Beg Urdūbādī.

From that day forward the Dīwān has kept its watch from the chamber beside the Qaysariyya gate, its officers four in number, its scribes nine, its couriers six, and its lamps trimmed each evening by the porter Ḥasan ‘Alī, son of Murād, in the manner appointed.

Arkān·The Pillars of Office

The Present Roster of Officers

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
KALĀNTAR · MAYOR

Mīrzā Ḥusayn Khān

Lord Mayor of the City and chief warden of the Bureau. He sits in council each Saturday after the dawn prayer, and receives petitioners between the second and fourth hours.

MUḤTASIB · INSPECTOR OF MARKETS

Āqā Ṣādiq Tabrīzī

Keeper of the just weight and the true price. He walks the bazaar daily; the loaf of bread is fixed at one ‘abbāsī of four miskāl, and short measure is fined at twelve.

MĪR-ĀB · MASTER OF WATERS

Ūstād Ibrāhīm of Yazd

Apportioner of the Zāyandeh-rūd among the seven mādī-rud canals. He keeps the schedule of the turn-water (āb-i nawbat) and oversees the repair of the bridges of Allāhverdī Khān and Khājū.

MUNSHĪ AL-MAMĀLIK · CHIEF SCRIBE

Mīr Muḥammad-Bāqir

Chief of the writing-chamber, keeper of the great register and the seal-of-record. Under his hand pass all decrees of the Bureau; nine scribes copy fair under his watch.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
Vazīfa·The Charge

The Threefold Duty of the Bureau

The keeping of true records — that no name be lost, no birth unwritten, no deed unwitnessed.

The just measure of bread — that the loaf weigh full, the price hold steady, the poor not be wronged at the oven.

The proper repair of canals and bridges — that water reach every garden in its turn, and the spans of the river stand firm against flood.
