Edicts & Public Notices

From the bureau of the Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān, Iṣfahān

By the grace of the Shāhanshāh and under the seal of the Dīwān-i Shahr, the following farmāns and bayāns proclamations were affixed to the gates of the Qaysariyya, the Masjid-i Jum‘a, and the four caravansarais of the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān, beginning the fourteenth day of Rabī‘ al-Awwal in the one one thousand and ninety-fifth and eighth of the Hijra. Citizens of the Jūbāra, Darbastīn, ‘Abbās-ābād, and Bīdābād quarters are charged to attend their reading.

12 Rabī' al-Awwal · 1095·Of Public Markets

On the Standard Weight of the Common Loaf (nān-i sangak)

— Sealed by Mīrzā ‘Abd al-Bashīr, Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān

Be it known to every baker (nānvā) of the four quarters that, by command of the Dīwān, the loaf of sangak shall not weigh less than sixty (6 × 10 ‘abbāsī) when drawn from the oven. The price is fixed at one shāhī and a half. Any baker found light by the inspector's scale shall forfeit one day's flour to the poor of the Masjid-i Jum‘a.

[motif: islamic-star]FARMĀN 413
14 Rabī' al-Awwal · 1095·Of Waters & Channels

Repair of the Public Fountain (saqqā-khāna) in the Jūbāra Quarter

— Sealed by Ūstād Yaḥyā b. ‘Alī, Inspector of Waters

The qanāt-bed beneath at the foot of the Jūbāra is hereafter to be cleansed at fortnight's notice. Master mason Ūstād Salīm of Ardistān is appointed; the cost of forty-two tūmān shall be drawn from the waqf of the late Mīrzā Ṭāqī. Residents of Jūbāra are forbidden to draw upon the cistern of the Madrasa-i Chahār Bāgh in the interim.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
[motif: islamic-star]FARMĀN 414
17 Rabī' al-Awwal · 1095·Of Pasturage

Prohibition of Grazing within the Maydān-i Naqsh-e Jahān

— Sealed by Ḥasan Khān, Muḥtasib of the Royal Maydān

No man shall lead sheep, goat, mule, nor camel upon the gravel of the Royal Square between the Qaysariyya gate and the Masjid-i Shāh. The hardness of the cobblers' villages shall pass turned by the way of Chamūn-i Chāzū-rūd. A first transgression carries a fine of two ‘abbāsī; the second, the confiscation of the beast for the royal stables.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
[motif: islamic-star]FARMĀN 415
19 Rabī' al-Awwal · 1095·Of Public Order

On the Sweeping of the Bāzār-i Buzurg before the Friday Prayer

— Sealed by Āqā Riḍā, Warden of the Qaysariyya

Each shopkeeper of the long bāzār, from the spice-vault of the Bāb al-Bīmār Ṭāqī unto the gate of the coppersmiths, shall sweep the threshold and water the dust beyond one hour before the noon call of every Friday. Inspection of the office shall pass at the third going, when fall to the channel shall be at the rate of the offender's expense.

[motif: arabesque-filet-divider]
[motif: islamic-star]FARMĀN 416
22 Rabī' al-Awwal · 1095·Of Trade & Quality

Concerning the Quality of Indigo Sold in the Sarā-yi Gulshan

— Sealed by Mīrzā Ḥājjī, Examiner of Dyes

Merchants importing indigo of Bayāna and of Sukhsānī alike present each bale to the dye-examiner before opening for sale. Adulteration with charcoal or with the lees of pomegranate is forbidden, the penalty being the burning of the parcel in the maydān and a fine of five tūmān to the treasury of the Dīwān.
