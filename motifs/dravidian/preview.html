Dravidian — motif library v1

Honest disclosure: this is the weakest of the five style libraries. Wikimedia has very few authentic public-domain Dravidian SVGs. Authentic Indian motifs: 2 Om symbols, 1 conch, 1 lotus. Six other ornaments are European Art Nouveau / Renaissance being repurposed (clearly marked below). Real gaps — kolam, yali, kalasha, torana, gopuram silhouette — to be filled by procedural generation in v2.
