A Working Library

Housed in the eastern wing above the Talar-i Tavus, the Royal Atelier Library holds 22,000 volumes specialising in Safavid material culture: courtly painting and the kitabkhana, architectural treatises, Armenian merchant ledgers from New Julfa, Ottoman and Mughal correspondence, and a substantial run of European travel accounts from Pietro della Valle to Jean Chardin.

The library descends from the private working collection of the museum's founding director, Houshang Ahmadi (d. 2002), enlarged by the Mehrnaz Vossoughi bequest of 1989 and continuous acquisition since.

Access & Appointments

The library is open by appointment to credentialed scholars — doctoral candidates, post-doctoral researchers, museum and conservation staff, and independent scholars with two letters of standing.

Reading Room Hours
Tuesday – Saturday, 10:00 – 16:30. Closed during Nowruz, Ramadan evenings, and the second week of August for stocktaking.
Application
Submit a research statement (max. 800 words), curriculum vitae, and two references to library@museumofisfahan.ir. Decisions returned within fourteen days.
Daily Fee
Waived for accepted applicants. A modest reproduction fee applies to digital surrogates.
Restricted Holdings
Manuscripts and the Vossoughi textile sample books require additional curatorial supervision; allow three weeks' notice.

The Shah Abbas Fellowship
in Architectural History

Awarded annually to a single scholar pursuing original research on the building cultures of Safavid Iran — its mosques, caravanserais, garden pavilions, bridges, and the urban fabric of Isfahan after 1598.

The Fellow delivers a public lecture in the Talar-i Tavus and contributes a chapter to the Museum Press Annual.

The Mehrnaz Vossoughi Fellowship
in Textile Conservation

Endowed by the Vossoughi family in memory of the textile historian and curator Mehrnaz Vossoughi (1924–1989), this fellowship supports practical conservation research on Safavid silks, velvets, carpets, and embroidered hangings.

Fellows work alongside the Department of Conservation in the Atelier on the third floor.

The Annual Symposium · XXIII

Patterns of Patronage

Power, Gift, and Image at the Safavid Court
6 – 8 November 2024 · Talar-i Tavus

Three days of papers and panels examining the structures of patronage that shaped Safavid art — from the kitabkhana's ledger to the qizilbash robe of honour, from imperial mosque foundations to the workshop economies of Tabriz, Qazvin, and Isfahan. Twenty-six speakers convene from Tehran, Istanbul, Yerevan, Princeton, the Sorbonne, and the V&A.

Convened by Dr. Parisa Tabrizi (Museum of Isfahan) and Prof. David Roxburgh (Harvard). Open to the public; advance registration required.

Day I · 6 Nov

The Royal Workshop

Keynote by Prof. Sheila Canby. Six papers on the kitabkhana under Tahmasp and the genesis of the Houghton Shahnama.

Day II · 7 Nov

Stone, Tile, and Water

Architectural patronage across Isfahan's three great squares; new archival findings on the Masjid-i Shah accounts.

Day III · 8 Nov

The Gift Economy

Diplomacy through textile, manuscript, and object — Safavid embassies to Venice, Moscow, Delhi, and the Porte.
